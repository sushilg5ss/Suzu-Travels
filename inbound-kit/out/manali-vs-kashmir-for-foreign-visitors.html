Should a foreign visitor choose Manali or Kashmir?

For most visitors from the UK, US, Europe and Australia, Manali (Himachal) is the easier choice: the UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu & Kashmir, the US rates it Level 4, and going against that advice can void your travel insurance. Start here: what governments advise

The UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) advises against all travel to Jammu & Kashmir, including Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg (checked 16 Nov 2026). The US State Department rates Jammu & Kashmir Level 4: Do not travel (checked 14 Nov 2026). Read your own government's advice before you book. Travelling against your government's advice can invalidate your travel insurance. The FCDO's list of areas it advises against in India is the India–Pakistan border area, Jammu & Kashmir and Manipur — Himachal Pradesh, where Manali is, is not on it.

We will tell you honestly if we don't recommend travelling on your dates. The FCDO also notes that consular help in Jammu & Kashmir is severely limited. Advice changes — check it again before you pay and before you fly.

Which suits you?

Choose Manali (Himachal) if…

Your government advises against Kashmir or your insurer won't cover it; you want snow at Solang in winter; you'd like to combine the mountains with Shimla, the UNESCO toy train or the Golden Triangle; or you want to drive on to Lahaul through the Atal Tunnel.

Kashmir may still be on your list if…

Your own government's advice allows it and your insurer confirms cover in writing. Then the draws are Dal Lake and its shikaras, the Mughal gardens and tulip garden in spring, and Gulmarg's gondola and ski slopes in winter. We tell you honestly if we don't recommend your dates.

Month by month: when each one works

Both valleys have four clear seasons. What changes is rain, snow and which roads are open:

December to February

Snow season in both. Shimla, on the way to Manali, averages about 40 cm of snow in January and February (IMD). Manali can drop below −5 °C (23 °F); Solang turns into a ski area. In Kashmir, Srinagar averages 2.5 °C (37 °F) in January and Gulmarg's ski season runs.

March to May

Spring. Srinagar's tulip garden usually opens in March–April (March is also its wettest month, 104.6 mm). In Himachal the snow line climbs; Rohtang Pass reopens by May.

June to September

Summer in Kashmir: Srinagar averages 24.2 °C (76 °F) in July and stays fairly dry, but July–August is the Amarnath pilgrimage. Himachal gets the monsoon — the FCDO warns of flash floods and landslides with little warning, and advises against driving at night or in fog.

October and November

Clear, dry and quieter in both — October is Srinagar's driest month. Rohtang closes with heavy snow from late November; the Atal Tunnel keeps the road to Lahaul open.

Cold and altitude affect people differently — consult your doctor and see CDC or NHS Fit for Travel. Gulmarg's gondola climbs to about 3,980 m (13,000 ft); the Atal Tunnel is at about 3,000 m (10,000 ft).

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Which is better for a foreign tourist, Manali or Kashmir?

It depends on your government's advice more than on the scenery. The UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu & Kashmir and the US State Department rates it Level 4: Do not travel; Himachal Pradesh, where Manali is, is not on the FCDO's list of areas to avoid. For most visitors from the UK, US, Europe and Australia, Manali is the easier choice.

Is Kashmir safe for foreign tourists?

We don't make safety promises. The UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu & Kashmir, including Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg (checked 16 Nov 2026), and the US State Department rates it Level 4: Do not travel. Read your own government's advice before you book, and tell us — we will say honestly if we don't recommend your dates.

Will my travel insurance cover Kashmir?

Possibly not. The FCDO says your travel insurance could be invalidated if you travel against its advice. Ask your insurer in writing before you book, and see our India travel insurance guide.

Where is there more snow in winter, Manali or Kashmir?

Both get snow from about December to February; neither is guaranteed on a given day. Near Manali, Solang becomes a ski area and Manali can drop below −5 °C (23 °F); in Kashmir, Gulmarg's ski season usually runs from mid-December to mid-April.

What is the best month to visit Manali?

For snow, December to February. For green valleys and the high roads, May to June and September to October. July and August bring heavy monsoon rain to Himachal, with landslide risk.

Can I visit Manali and Kashmir on one trip?

We plan them as separate trips, and Kashmir's travel advice applies to the whole of Jammu & Kashmir. Manali combines easily with Shimla, Dharamshala, Amritsar or the Golden Triangle — see the routes under Prices above.

How do I get to Manali from Delhi?

By road it is about 530 km (330 mi), a long drive — or break it at Shimla, 370 km (230 mi) from Delhi. Or fly to Bhuntar airport, 50 km (31 mi) from Manali, and continue by car. We meet you with a private car and driver.
