Is India expensive to visit? It depends on six choices

India can be a modest or a lavish trip. These are the choices that move the total most — ask us to price two versions side by side.

  1. SeasonOctober to March is the peak season for Delhi, Agra and Rajasthan, so book early. The UK FCDO notes the monsoon usually runs from June to October — fewer visitors, but rain and landslides in the hills. See the best time to visit India.
  2. Number of daysEvery extra day adds a hotel night and a day of car and driver. Our routes run from 4 to 12 days; longer trips are quoted on request.
  3. Hotel levelOur “from” prices use comfortable 3-star hotels. 4-star, 5-star and heritage palace hotels cost more — we quote them in US dollars.
  4. How many of youPrices are per person for two sharing a room. Solo travellers and families get their own quote.
  5. Car or trainMost of our trips use your own car. Some legs work well by train: foreign tourists can reserve seats up to 365 days ahead under the Foreign Tourist Quota (the general window is 60 days). Compare in trains vs flights in India.
  6. Festivals and big datesBig festivals such as Holi and Diwali are busy times. Plan around them with our India festivals calendar.

The fixed costs outside any tour: visa, insurance and tickets

These you pay yourself, whoever you travel with.

e-Visa fee

The fee depends on your nationality and the visa length, and for the 30-day e-Tourist visa it is lower for April–June arrivals than July–March. The official fee table on indianvisaonline.gov.in lists it; a small bank charge is added, the fee is non-refundable and there is no express fee. Pay the e-Visa fee only on indianvisaonline.gov.in — look-alike sites charge extra. We are not a visa agency. Step by step: our India e-Visa guide.

Travel insurance

The US CDC describes three kinds: travel disruption, travel health and medical evacuation — and notes that an evacuation can be very expensive. The US State Department says Medicare and Medicaid don't pay for care abroad. We don't sell insurance and this is not advice — compare policies yourself; see CDC/NHS Fit for Travel. More in our India travel insurance guide.

Monument tickets

At the Taj Mahal and Humayun's Tomb, foreign visitors buy a separate, higher ticket than Indian visitors; prices are on the official sites. The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays.

Money on the ground

Cash, cards and UPI — how visitors can pay day to day: UPI and money in India.
