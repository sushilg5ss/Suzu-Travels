When does it snow in India? The official figures

January and February are the snowiest months in the Himachal hills. December can bring snow but usually less of it. The India Meteorological Department's long-term figures for Shimla:

Shimla's district administration says nights in December–January fall to about 0 °C (32 °F) and that warm clothing and a jacket are a must. March can still bring a little snow (about 7 cm on average). Manali, higher up, is colder. More detail in our best time to visit Himachal guide.

Skiing and snow play: Solang, Auli and Gulmarg

India has a few real ski areas. They suit beginners and a holiday day on the snow more than a European-style ski week:

Solang valley, near Manali

Himachal Pradesh Tourism says that in winter the snow-packed slopes become a ski arena where state and national championships are held. There is a gondola (ropeway). It is a day trip from Manali, 13 km (8 mi) away.

Auli, Uttarakhand

Run by the state's GMVN: a chair lift and a ski lift, with the ski season starting in December and snow until March. Auli lies at 2,500–3,050 m (8,200–10,000 ft) — a long mountain drive from Dehradun airport.

Gulmarg, Kashmir

India's best-known ski area: a gondola up to about 3,980 m and a government-run ski shop. Read the government advice first — see our Gulmarg guide.

Just want to touch the snow?

Shimla, Manali and McLeod Ganj in January and February are the easy answer: snow-covered towns and forests, cosy hotels and mountain views, without needing to ski.

Winter roads: why we drive in daylight only

The UK Foreign Office advises: "Avoid travelling at night and in fog as accidents are common due to low visibility." Snow can close mountain roads for a day or more, and Rohtang Pass stays shut from late November until about May. So we drive the hills only in daylight, keep a spare day in every snow trip, and re-route when a road closes.

Before you go

Kashmir and government advice

The UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir except Jammu city, and the US State Department says 'Do not travel' to J&K except eastern Ladakh and Leh (advice checked 23 Oct 2026). The FCDO warns that your travel insurance could be invalidated if you travel against its advice. Check the latest before you book.

Cold, altitude and health

Cold and altitude affect people differently. If you have heart or breathing conditions, consult your doctor; see CDC or NHS Fit for Travel. Pack proper layers, a warm jacket, gloves, a hat and shoes with grip.

Visa

Apply yourself only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware look-alike sites that charge extra. We are not a visa agency. Our e-Visa guide explains the steps.

Christmas and New Year

The hill towns are very busy over Christmas and New Year — Shimla's district administration advises booking early. January and February are quieter and snowier.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Where can I see snow in India?

In the Himalayan north. The easiest places for a visitor are the Himachal hill towns — Shimla, Manali with Solang valley, and Dharamshala/McLeod Ganj — all reachable by car from Delhi. Auli in Uttarakhand and Gulmarg in Kashmir are ski areas.

Which month does it snow in India?

Mostly December to February. In Shimla the India Meteorological Department's averages are about 7 cm in December and about 40 cm in each of January and February, with roughly four snow days a month. Snow is never guaranteed on a given date.

Does it snow in Shimla and Manali in December?

It can. December snowfall in Shimla averages about 7 cm, so a white Christmas is possible but not certain. January and February are the better bet. Manali is colder, and in winter the Solang slopes near Manali become a ski area.

Can you ski in India?

Yes — at Solang near Manali, at Auli in Uttarakhand (chair lift and ski lift, season from December) and at Gulmarg in Kashmir. They suit beginners and snow days more than a long ski holiday. For Gulmarg, read the UK and US government advice first.

Is it safe to go to Gulmarg for snow?

The UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir except Jammu city, and the US State Department says 'Do not travel' to J&K except eastern Ladakh and Leh (checked 23 Oct 2026). Travel insurance may not cover you against FCDO advice. Himachal is our suggested alternative.

Is Rohtang Pass open in winter?

No. Heavy snow closes Rohtang Pass from late November until about May, according to Himachal Pradesh Tourism. In winter the Atal Tunnel keeps the road from Manali to Lahaul open, and Solang is the usual snow day.

How cold is it, and what should I pack?

Shimla averages 2.5–10.3 °C (37–51 °F) in January and nights fall to about 0 °C (32 °F); Manali can drop below −5 °C (23 °F). Bring warm layers, a jacket, gloves, a hat and shoes with grip. If you have health concerns, consult your doctor.
