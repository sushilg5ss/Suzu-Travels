In short

When is the best time to visit India?

For most first trips, the best time to visit India is October to March: dry, mild days in Delhi, Agra, Jaipur and Rajasthan, and the peak season in Kerala and Goa. The Himalayan hill towns are best in spring and autumn (snow from December to February), Ladakh is a summer trip, and the monsoon usually runs June to October. The best time for each region

If you already know where you want to go, start here.

Delhi, Agra, Jaipur and Rajasthan

October to March. Incredible India gives Jaisalmer's best time as November to March and Udaipur's as September to March. Nights in December–January can be cold: Jodhpur averages 5–30 °C (41–86 °F) in January. Remember the Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays. See our 12-day Rajasthan tour.

Himachal hill towns: Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala

Our home region. In our planning experience March–June and September–November are the easiest months; December–February brings snow, which many guests come for. Rohtang Pass closes from late November until about May, but the Atal Tunnel keeps Manali–Lahaul open all year.

Ladakh (Leh)

A summer trip. The roads in from Manali and Srinagar are seasonal, so we confirm them when we quote. Leh is at high altitude — consult your doctor before you go.

Kerala

Kerala Tourism names November–February as peak season and October–February as the best time for houseboat cruises. The south-west monsoon arrives around 1 June; June–August is the wettest time. See our 8-day Kerala tour.

Goa

Winter is the season for foreign visitors — roughly November to March, with Palolem at its best November–February. In the monsoon, swimming in the sea and water sports stop. See our Goa holiday guide.

Rishikesh and Varanasi

Rishikesh suits October to April; the yoga festival is in February–March and rafting stops in July–August. Varanasi is best October to March — Dev Deepavali, 15 days after Diwali, lights the ghats with thousands of lamps.

The monsoon: when it comes and what it means for your trip

The UK FCDO says the monsoon season in India usually runs from June to October, with flash floods and landslides that can come with little warning. It does not arrive everywhere at once — these are the India Meteorological Department's normal dates:

1 Junmonsoon onset in Kerala
11 Junonset in Mumbai
27 Junonset in New Delhi
~15 Octfinal withdrawal over south India

Normal dates, not forecasts — each year's monsoon is early or late by days or weeks. In our experience, July and August are the hardest months on mountain roads in Himachal and Uttarakhand, so we build spare days into monsoon itineraries.

Festivals and wildlife seasons worth planning around

Some trips are built around a date rather than the weather:

Holi — 23 March 2027

The festival of colours, Tuesday 23 March 2027, with Holika Dahan bonfires the evening before (Government of India holiday list). UP Tourism's Rangotsav in Mathura and Vrindavan runs 16–30 March 2027. Read our Holi festival guide.

Tiger safaris — October to June

Corbett's zones open from 15 October or 15 November (Dhikala) until June; Madhya Pradesh's reserves are best October–June, with core zones closed July–September. See our tiger safari guide.

Kerala's snake-boat races — August

The Nehru Trophy race is held every August on Punnamada Lake near Alappuzha — a monsoon-season reason to visit Kerala.

Dev Deepavali in Varanasi — November

Fifteen days after Diwali, thousands of lamps line the ghats on the Ganga. Dates move each year — we confirm when we quote.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

What is the best month to visit India?

For a first trip covering Delhi, Agra, Jaipur and Rajasthan, November to February are the most comfortable months, with October and March close behind. Kerala and Goa peak from November to February. For the Himalayan hill towns, choose spring or autumn — or December to February if you want snow.

Which months should I avoid in India?

It depends on the region. The plains and Rajasthan are very hot in May and June (Jaisalmer averages up to 46 °C / 115 °F in May), and the monsoon usually runs June to October, with the heaviest rain in July and August. Even then, Ladakh and the Himachal hills can work — tell us your dates.

When is monsoon season in India?

The UK FCDO says it usually runs from June to October. The India Meteorological Department's normal onset dates are 1 June in Kerala, 11 June in Mumbai and 27 June in New Delhi; it withdraws from Delhi around 25 September and from the south by mid-October.

Is December a good time to visit India?

Yes, for most of the country: the Golden Triangle, Rajasthan, Varanasi, Kerala and Goa are all in season, and the Himachal hills get snow. Nights in the north are cold and fog can delay early starts, and Christmas–New Year is busy, so book early.

When is the best time to visit the Himalayas and Ladakh?

For Shimla, Manali and Dharamshala, March–June and September–November are easiest in our experience; December–February is the snow season. Ladakh is a summer trip because the roads in are seasonal — we confirm them when we quote. Both are at altitude: consult your doctor.

When is the best time to visit Kerala and Goa?

Kerala Tourism names November–February as peak season and October–February as the best time for houseboats. Goa's season for foreign visitors is winter, roughly November to March; swimming in the sea stops during the monsoon.

When is the best time to see tigers in India?

Most parks are open from October to June. Corbett's zones open from 15 October or 15 November until June, and Madhya Pradesh's tiger reserves close their core zones from July to September. Sightings are never guaranteed.
