What you see on a Delhi tour

Red Fort (Lal Qila)

Shah Jahan's palace fort of Shahjahanabad, built in 1638, with red sandstone walls 33 m (108 ft) high. A UNESCO World Heritage Site; its gardens and pavilions inspired buildings across Rajasthan, Delhi and Agra. Closed on Mondays.

Jama Masjid

Begun by Shah Jahan in 1644; the courtyard holds about 25,000 worshippers. Foreign visitors can enter from 8 am to sunset outside prayer times; robes can be hired at the northern gate. Open every day.

Humayun's Tomb

Built in the 1560s under Emperor Akbar and finished in 1570 — the first garden-tomb in the Indian subcontinent and the model the Taj Mahal perfected about 80 years later. UNESCO-listed since 1993; open sunrise to sunset, every day.

Qutb Minar complex

The 72.5 m (238 ft) tower begun in 1199, the Alai Darwaza gate of 1311, the oldest mosque in northern India and the Iron Pillar. A UNESCO World Heritage Site, open from sunrise to 8 pm.

India Gate and New Delhi

The broad central avenue with India Gate at one end and Rashtrapati Bhavan at the other, best at dusk. Delhi Tourism's itinerary suggests lunch around Connaught Place.

Lotus Temple and Akshardham

The Bahá'í House of Worship (completed 1986; free; 9 am–5:30 pm) and Swaminarayan Akshardham (free entry, first entry 10 am, last 6:30 pm). Both are closed on Mondays.

What to wear and what to leave in the car

Akshardham does not allow mobile phones, cameras or any electronics, and no bags or food — there is a free cloakroom, or leave them in the car with your driver. Shoulders, upper arms and knees must be covered there. At the Jama Masjid, dress modestly and take off your shoes; robes can be hired at the northern gate, and tourists are not allowed in during prayers.

Foreign visitors pay a separate, higher ticket than Indian visitors at the ASI monuments (Red Fort, Humayun's Tomb, Qutb Minar). We can buy them for you in the same quote, or you buy them on the spot. Photography for personal use is free at Humayun's Tomb.

On a layover, and in the winter haze

Long layover: the Airport Express metro links IGI Airport and New Delhi station in about 15 minutes (22.7 km / 14.1 mi), but a car from the terminal saves the changes and the luggage. Leaving the airport needs a valid Indian visa. Apply only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware of look-alike sites that charge extra. We are not a visa agency; our e-Visa guide walks you through it.

Winter air: the UK Foreign Office says severe air pollution is a major health hazard, especially from October to February, and North Indian cities are most affected. If you have asthma or a heart or lung condition, ask your doctor before a winter trip and see NHS Fit for Travel or the CDC. On bad days we keep more of the day indoors (museums, Akshardham) and less time walking by the roads.

Fog and night driving: the Foreign Office also advises against travelling at night and in fog. That is one reason we suggest the Taj Mahal from Delhi as a separate day or a night in Agra, not squeezed onto the end of a Delhi tour.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

How many days do you need in Delhi?

Two days let you see Old Delhi, New Delhi and the southern monuments without rushing. One long day by private car covers the highlights — Jama Masjid, the Red Fort, Humayun's Tomb, the Qutb Minar and India Gate — if you start early.

Which Delhi monuments are closed on Monday?

The Red Fort, Swaminarayan Akshardham and the Lotus Temple are closed on Mondays. Humayun's Tomb, the Qutb Minar complex, the Jama Masjid and India Gate are open every day, so a Monday tour still works.

Can I visit Delhi on a long layover?

Yes, if you hold a valid Indian visa — you need one to leave the airport. Apply only at indianvisaonline.gov.in. The Airport Express metro reaches New Delhi in about 15 minutes, or our car collects you at the terminal.

What should I wear on a Delhi tour?

Clothes that cover shoulders and knees work everywhere and are required at Akshardham. At the Jama Masjid you take off your shoes, and robes can be hired at the northern gate. Comfortable shoes matter more than anything else.

Can I take my phone into Akshardham?

No. Mobile phones, cameras, electronics, bags and food are not allowed. Use the free cloakroom or leave them in the car with your driver.

Is Delhi's air bad in winter?

Often, yes. The UK Foreign Office says severe air pollution is a major health hazard from October to February, worst in North Indian cities. If you have a heart or lung condition, ask your doctor before you travel; we adjust the day on bad days.

Can I add the Taj Mahal to a Delhi tour?

As a separate day. Agra is 204 km (127 mi) from Delhi by road, and the Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays. See our Taj Mahal tour from Delhi page, or the Golden Triangle with Jaipur.

Do I need a guide as well as a driver?

Not necessarily — your driver handles the car and the route. A licensed guide brings the Red Fort, Humayun's Tomb and the Qutb Minar to life; hire only approved guides who show an ID card, or ask us to add one.
