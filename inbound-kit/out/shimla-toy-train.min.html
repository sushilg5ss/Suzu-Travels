What is the Shimla toy train, and how do you ride it?

The Shimla “toy train” is the Kalka–Shimla Railway, a 96 km (60 mile) narrow-gauge line, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2008. It climbs from Kalka (656 m) to Shimla (2,075 m) through 102 tunnels in about 4½ to 6 hours. Book seats on IRCTC; landslides can suspend it in the monsoon. Which toy train should you take?

District Shimla lists several trains a day in each direction. Their names, numbers and timings change from season to season, so check the current list on IRCTC — but these are the types you will see:

Vistadome

Coaches with a glass roof and large windows for an all-round view. The pick for most first-time visitors who come for the scenery.

Rail Motor

A small heritage railcar. District Shimla gives the journey as about 4 hours 20 minutes, among the quicker options.

Himalayan Queen

A regular named train; District Shimla gives about 5½ hours from Kalka to Shimla.

Mail and Shivalik Express

Other daily services. The Shivalik Express is listed with one halt, at Barog.

Steam?

UNESCO noted in 2008 that a restored steam locomotive could be chartered. We cannot confirm current steam runs, so please don't plan a trip around one.

Which way?

You can ride uphill, Kalka to Shimla, and continue by car into the hills — or ride down at the end of a Himachal trip. Either way our car carries your bags by road.

How to book the toy train from outside India

You book Indian train seats yourself on IRCTC, the Indian Railways booking site. Foreign passport holders have their own quota:

  1. Look for Foreign Tourist (FT) quotaIRCTC's Foreign Tourist quota lets foreign passport holders book online up to 365 days ahead, paying 1.5 times the normal fare with an international debit or credit card. It is not confirmed that FT seats are offered on these narrow-gauge trains — check your train.
  2. If there is no FT quota, use the general quotaGeneral bookings open 60 days before the journey (Indian Railways rule since 1 November 2024).
  3. Carry your original passportFT tickets are checked against the original passport on board.
  4. Keep a plan BWe cannot book Foreign Tourist quota seats for you — IRCTC allows only the traveller to book them. We help you choose the train and plan the car on either side.

Getting to Kalka — and on from Shimla

Kalka sits at the foot of the hills, close to Chandigarh. By road, Shimla is about 370 km (230 miles) from Delhi and 119 km (74 miles) from Chandigarh; by rail you change to the narrow gauge at Kalka. You can reach Kalka by train from Delhi, or by private car.

Many toy trains leave early, so it is easier to spend the night before near Kalka or in Chandigarh than to travel from Delhi the same morning. Our usual plan: your private car takes you to Kalka, carries your big bags up to Shimla by road while you ride the train, and meets you at the station. From Shimla the same car continues to Manali, Dharamshala or wherever your trip goes next — see the best time to visit Himachal.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Is the Shimla toy train worth it?

For most visitors, yes — if you enjoy a slow scenic day. The 96 km line takes about 4½ to 6 hours through 102 tunnels and pine-covered hills. If a long train ride sounds tiring, ride one way and go the other way by car: Shimla is 119 km (74 miles) from Chandigarh by road.

How long does the Kalka to Shimla toy train take?

About 4½ to 6 hours, depending on the train. District Shimla gives around 4 hours 20 minutes for the Rail Motor, about 5½ hours for the Himalayan Queen and up to about 6 hours for other trains. Check current timings on IRCTC.

How do I book the toy train from outside India?

On IRCTC. Foreign passport holders can book Foreign Tourist (FT) quota seats up to 365 days ahead with an international card, at 1.5 times the normal fare, where the train offers FT seats. If yours does not, book the general quota, which opens 60 days ahead. Only you can book FT seats — travel agents cannot.

Which toy train is best for views?

The Vistadome, with its glass roof and large windows, is the usual choice for scenery. The Rail Motor heritage railcar is among the quicker options. Train names and timings change, so check the current list on IRCTC.

Does the toy train run in winter and in snow?

It runs through the winter, and a snowy ride can be beautiful — Shimla averages about 40 cm (16 in) of snow in each of January and February. Snow and weather can delay or stop trains, so keep a buffer day and warm clothes.

Does the toy train run in the monsoon?

Usually, but in the monsoon (roughly July–September) landslides have suspended the line for days at a time — for example from 31 August to 6 September 2025. We keep a road plan ready so your trip continues by car.

How do I get from Delhi to Kalka?

By train from Delhi to Kalka, or by private car. Many toy trains leave early, so we suggest a night near Kalka or in Chandigarh first. Our car can drive you there, carry your bags up to Shimla by road and meet you at Shimla station.

How many tunnels are there on the Kalka–Shimla line?

102. The line was built with 107 tunnels, and landslides have reduced the number since. The longest, tunnel no. 33 at Barog, is just over 1 km long. There are also 988 bridges and viaducts and 917 curves (UNESCO/ICOMOS, 2008).
