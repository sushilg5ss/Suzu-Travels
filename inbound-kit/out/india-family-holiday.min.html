When to go with children

October to March

The best months for Delhi, Agra and Rajasthan. The monsoon runs roughly June to September.

Snow in the hills

December to February in Shimla and Manali. Shimla averages about 40 cm (16 in) of snow in January and in February (IMD normals). Snow is never guaranteed on a given day.

School holidays

Christmas–New Year and the February half-term fall in the best months — book early for those weeks.

Winter air in the plains

The UK FCDO says: “Severe air pollution is a major health hazard, especially during the winter months from October to February,” and “children, the elderly and those with pre-existing medical conditions may be especially affected.” Many families spend fewer days in Delhi and more in the hills.

Visas, health and safety: what the official sources say

Each child needs their own passport and their own visa. Apply only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — look-alike sites charge extra. We are not a visa agency. The official e-Visa page says it is not available to people endorsed on a parent's passport, and passports should be valid for at least six months from arrival. In November 2026 the Government of India announced that the Tourist Visa is being renamed the Visitor Visa (PIB, 11 Nov 2026); until the official site changes, apply for the visa it shows there. We're a travel company, not doctors: see your GP or a travel clinic 6–8 weeks before you go, and read NHS Fit for Travel / TravelHealthPro or CDC for India. TravelHealthPro (NaTHNaC) asks parents to consider early MMR vaccination for infants and children, and notes that children are at higher risk of rabies because they are less likely to avoid animals. On safety, the UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu & Kashmir (except air travel to and within Jammu city), within 10 km of the India–Pakistan border, and against all but essential travel to Manipur (still current at 15 Nov 2026); Delhi, Agra, Rajasthan and Himachal are not on that list. Children under 15 enter ASI World Heritage monuments free. Rules change — check the official sites before you travel (as of Nov 2026).

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Is India a good family holiday destination?

Yes, for families who travel by private car at a slower pace. The Golden Triangle suits a first trip; adding Shimla and Manali gives children the toy train and, in winter, snow. Our full India with kids guide covers regions by age, food and packing.

Do children need their own visa for India?

Yes. Each child needs their own passport and their own visa — the official e-Visa page says it is not available to people endorsed on a parent's passport. Apply only at indianvisaonline.gov.in; look-alike sites charge extra. We are not a visa agency.

What vaccinations do children need for India?

We're a travel company, not doctors: see your GP or a travel clinic 6–8 weeks before you go. TravelHealthPro (NaTHNaC) suggests considering early MMR vaccination for infants and children; NHS Fit for Travel and CDC also have India pages.

Is the air pollution a problem for children in winter?

The UK FCDO says severe air pollution is a major health hazard especially from October to February, and that children may be especially affected. Many families keep Delhi short and spend more days in the hills.

What is the best time for a family holiday in India?

October to March for Delhi, Agra and Rajasthan. For snow in Shimla and Manali, December to February. Avoid the June–September monsoon for a first trip.

How long should a family trip to India be?

Ten to fourteen days works well: fewer hotel changes and time to rest. Our 12-day Golden Triangle + Himalayas route is built that way; the 4- and 6-day Golden Triangle routes suit shorter school breaks.

Is a car or the train better with kids?

For most families, a private car: door to door, stops when the children need them, and one place for your luggage. The Shimla toy train is the exception worth doing — as a ride, not as transport.
