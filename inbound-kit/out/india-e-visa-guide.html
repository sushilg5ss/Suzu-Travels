In short

How do I get an e-Tourist visa for India?

Apply online at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in at least 4 days before you arrive: fill in the form, upload a square face photo and your passport bio page, and pay the country-specific fee. The approval (ETA) arrives by email; print it, check it says GRANTED, and the visa is stamped at a designated airport. Look-alike sites charge extra — use only the official one.

How to apply for the e-Tourist visa — six steps

Everything happens on the official portal. There is no step that needs an agent, and the Government of India charges no "express" fee.

  1. Open the official siteType indianvisaonline.gov.in yourself (the e-Visa section is /evisa). Search ads often show look-alike sites first — they charge a service fee on top of the government fee.
  2. Pick e-Tourist and the length30-day, 1-year or 5-year (see the table below). Choose your arrival airport from the designated list.
  3. Fill in the form and uploadA square JPEG face photo and a PDF scan of your passport bio page, in the sizes the site asks for (see documents).
  4. Pay the feeBy card or the payment options shown, at least 4 days before travel. The fee depends on your nationality and is non-refundable, granted or not; the bank adds a small charge.
  5. Get your ETA by emailCheck the status on the same site with your application ID. If a photo or scan is unclear you will be asked to re-upload — answer quickly.
  6. Print it and flyCarry the ETA showing GRANTED. At immigration your fingerprints and photo are taken and the e-Visa is stamped. Fill in the e-Arrival card in the 72 hours before landing.

Passport, photo and documents

Passport

At least six months' validity when you apply, with two blank pages. Every traveller — children too — needs their own passport and their own application.

Photo

JPEG, 10 KB to 1 MB, square (height = width). Full face, eyes open, no glasses, plain white or light background, no shadows or border.

Passport scan

PDF of the bio (photo) page, 10 KB to 300 KB. Any other document must be in English.

Yellow fever

If you arrive from or through a yellow-fever country, carry your vaccination card or you may be quarantined. For vaccines and health advice, consult your doctor or an official travel-health site (CDC, NHS Fit for Travel).

Arriving in India on an e-Visa

Designated ports only. You must enter through one of the airports or seaports listed on the official site — Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kochi, Goa, Jaipur, Amritsar, Varanasi, Chandigarh, Bagdogra and dozens more. You can leave from any immigration check post. The official page's own lists and FAQ give different counts, so check that your arrival airport is on the current list before you book flights.

e-Arrival card. Since 1 October 2025, foreign visitors fill in an e-Arrival card within 72 hours before landing — on boi.gov.in, indianvisaonline.gov.in or the official "Indian Visa Su-Swagatam" app. It is arrival information, not a visa.

At the counter. Show your passport and printed ETA. Biometrics are taken on arrival. Nobody at the airport should ask you for extra money for the e-Visa.

What the e-Visa does not cover

An e-Visa cannot be extended or converted, and it is not valid for Protected, Restricted or Cantonment areas without separate permission. Several Himalayan and island routes include such areas — parts of Ladakh, the Kinnaur–Spiti road in Himachal, Sikkim and the Andaman Islands among them — so foreign visitors need an extra permit there. How to avoid look-alike visa sites

Check the address

The official e-Visa site is indianvisaonline.gov.in. Sites with "india-visa", "evisa-india" or similar names are private businesses, however official they look.

No express fee exists

The Government of India charges no emergency or express fee. Anyone selling a "fast-track" grant is charging you for nothing official.

Guard your data

Don't upload your passport to sites you reached through an ad or an email. Type the official address yourself.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

How early should I apply for an Indian e-Tourist visa?

At least 4 days before you arrive — the official FAQ allows applying up to 120 days ahead. Apply as soon as your dates are firm, so an unclear photo or scan can be fixed in time.

How long does the e-Visa take?

The official site does not promise a processing time. Allow at least 4 days, check your status on indianvisaonline.gov.in, and don't fly until your ETA shows GRANTED.

Which e-Tourist visa should I get?

For one trip of up to a month, the 30-day e-Tourist visa (valid from your first arrival). For more than one trip, or a stay longer than 30 days, the 1-year or 5-year visa — check the stay limits for your nationality.

How much does the India e-Visa cost?

It depends on your nationality, the visa length and, for the 30-day visa, the season (April–June is cheaper than July–March). The official fee table on indianvisaonline.gov.in lists it; a small bank charge is added and the fee is non-refundable.

Which website is the official one?

Only indianvisaonline.gov.in. Look-alike sites charge extra service fees; the Government of India charges no express fee and needs no agent.

Can I enter India by land on an e-Visa?

The e-Visa is for entry through designated airports and seaports; the official site also lists some land check posts, but its pages are not consistent. If you plan to arrive by land, confirm the current rule on the official site first.

Does the e-Visa cover Ladakh, Spiti, Sikkim or the Andamans?

The visa lets you into India, but some areas there are Protected or Restricted and need a separate permit for foreign visitors. We arrange those permits for the routes we run.

What is the India e-Arrival card?

An online arrival form for foreign visitors, in force since 1 October 2025, filled in within 72 hours before landing on boi.gov.in, indianvisaonline.gov.in or the Indian Visa Su-Swagatam app. It is not a visa.
