How to choose a yoga retreat in Rishikesh — five steps

Rishikesh has a great many ashrams, schools and retreat centres. We don't recommend or sell any of them — this is the checklist we would use ourselves.

  1. Decide what you want from itA gentle week of daily classes, a 200-hour teacher training, or a silent meditation course are very different experiences. Pick one before you compare places.
  2. Check registration yourselfTeacher trainings often say they are Yoga Alliance registered (RYS) — look the school up on yogaalliance.org. India's own Yoga Certification Board, under the Ministry of Ayush, certifies yoga professionals and accredits institutions. A listing is a starting point, not a guarantee.
  3. Read the daily schedule and house rulesWake-up time, hours of practice, silence, food, phones, what happens if you miss a session. Ashrams can be strict; that is part of the point.
  4. Ask what the fee coversRoom type, meals, course materials, certificate, airport transfer. Pay the retreat directly and keep its written confirmation.
  5. Plan the journey around itArrive a day early, leave a day spare at the end, and book the transfer from Delhi or Dehradun airport — that part we can do for you.

When to go to Rishikesh

February–March: festival week

Uttarakhand's Tourism Department and GMVN hold a week-long International Yoga Festival in Rishikesh; the 2026 edition closed on 22 March with more than 2,500 participants from India and abroad. Book rooms early for that week.

July–August: monsoon

Rafting on the Ganga is usually suspended for the monsoon, and in wet years it can stop earlier when the river rises. Ask your retreat how its programme runs in the monsoon.

The rest of the year

Rafting runs for about ten months a year outside the monsoon. Searches from abroad peak in October, January and February — book early for those months.

Check the forecast

For current weather and any river or road warnings, check India Meteorological Department (mausam.imd.gov.in) before you travel. We tell you about road conditions when we quote.

Visa for a yoga course in India

India's e-Tourist Visa lists attending a short-term yoga programme as an allowed purpose, as long as you have no other purpose or activity. Apply only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware look-alike sites that charge extra — at least 4 days before arrival. The e-Visa cannot be extended or converted. Rishikesh beyond the yoga mat

Triveni Ghat

The most popular bathing ghat in Rishikesh, at the traditional meeting point of the Ganga, Yamuna and Saraswati.

Ram Jhula and Lakshman Jhula

The two famous suspension-bridge landmarks across the Ganga; Ram Jhula was built in 1986.

The Beatles connection

The Beatles came to Maharishi Mahesh Yogi's ashram in Rishikesh in the 1960s — the reason many visitors first heard the town's name.

River and hills

White-water rafting, canoeing and bungee jumping; Neer Garh waterfall; the Neelkanth Mahadev temple; Rajaji National Park around 20 km (12 mi) away.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Do I need a special visa for a yoga retreat in India?

For a short-term yoga programme, the e-Tourist Visa is enough — the official list of purposes includes attending a short-term yoga programme. Apply only at indianvisaonline.gov.in, at least 4 days before you arrive. Longer or formal study may need a different visa; check the official site.

Why Rishikesh for yoga?

Incredible India, the Government of India's tourism site, describes Rishikesh as often called the yoga capital of the world. It sits on the Ganga, it is where the Beatles came to Maharishi Mahesh Yogi's ashram in the 1960s, and it hosts an International Yoga Festival every February–March.

How do I get from Delhi to Rishikesh?

By private car, door to door from your Delhi hotel or the airport. You can also fly from Delhi to Dehradun's Jolly Grant airport, about 20 km (12 miles) from Rishikesh, take a train to Rishikesh station, or a bus from Delhi.

How do I check that a yoga teacher training is genuine?

Look the school up yourself on yogaalliance.org if it claims Yoga Alliance registration, or ask whether it or its teachers hold Yoga Certification Board (Ministry of Ayush) accreditation. Read the syllabus, ask who the lead trainers are, and pay only against a written confirmation.

When is the best time for a retreat in Rishikesh?

Check your retreat's own dates first. February–March brings the International Yoga Festival; July–August is monsoon, when rafting on the Ganga is usually suspended. Interest from abroad peaks around October, January and February, so book early for those months.

Is a Vipassana course free?

Vipassana courses in the Goenka tradition, listed on dhamma.org, are run solely on a donation basis. You apply online and may wait up to two weeks for an acceptance email, so apply well before your flights.

Can I combine a retreat with the Taj Mahal or the Himalayas?

Yes. A common plan is Delhi and the Taj Mahal first, then Rishikesh, then the Himachal hills or the Char Dham temples. We arrange the car, driver and hotels; you book the retreat directly.
