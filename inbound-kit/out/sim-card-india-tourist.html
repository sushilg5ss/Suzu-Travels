In short

Can tourists buy a SIM card in India?

Yes. Foreign visitors can get an Indian prepaid SIM from an operator's store or counter with their passport, a valid Indian visa, a passport photo and a local address such as a hotel or tour operator. Activation can take from a few hours to a day or two, and a tourist connection runs for up to 3 months. For Kashmir and Ladakh, only postpaid connections from other states work.

Three ways to have a phone in India

Most visitors pick one of these. Which is best depends on how long you stay, where you go and whether you need an Indian number.

OptionHow it worksGood to know
Indian prepaid SIMBought in India from an operator's own store or counter, with in-person ID checksGives you an Indian number for calls, messages and one-time passwords; activation is not instant; up to 3 months per connection

An Indian eSIM from an Indian operator usually still needs the same in-person ID check, and online self-activation generally needs an Indian number to receive a one-time password — so first-time visitors usually can't set one up from home.

What the shop will ask for

These are the documents listed by COAI, the association of India's mobile operators, for international visitors.

  1. PassportThe original, plus a photocopy of the passport pages with your valid Indian visa.
  2. Indian visaYour visa or e-Visa. Apply yourself at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in; beware look-alike sites that charge extra. We are not a visa agency.
  3. A passport-size colour photoA printed photo, not a scan. Carry two in case.
  4. A local addressThe address of a local reference — it may be your tour operator — or, without one, where you're staying, such as your hotel. Keep your first hotel booking handy.
  5. The application formThe shop gives you a form to fill in and sign. Keep the receipt it hands back — you may need its details to activate the SIM.

Since a Department of Telecommunications order of 31 July 2024, a one-time password on a local Indian number is optional for foreign nationals; operators may send it to your email instead.

How long it takes, and how long it lasts

Not instant

COAI explains that the SIM is issued first but can't make or receive calls until the operator finishes verification — a wait that can run from a few hours to a day or two. Buy it on day one, not on the morning you need it.

Up to 3 months

For foreign tourists a connection must not run past your visa, and cannot be longer than 3 months at a time, even if your visa is longer.

Buy from the operator

Use an operator's own store or counter. Some international airports have operator counters in the arrivals area; otherwise ask your hotel for the nearest official store. Avoid street stalls, which may sell SIMs registered to someone else.

Keep your home SIM

Many phones take two SIMs, or one SIM plus an eSIM — keep your home number for bank messages and use the Indian one for data and local calls.

Kashmir and Ladakh: the prepaid rule

Prepaid SIMs from other parts of India don't work in Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh; postpaid connections do. Confirm with the operator before you go. This is a long-standing telecom licence condition for these border areas, so a prepaid SIM bought in Delhi will stop working when you arrive in Srinagar or Leh.

If your trip includes Kashmir or Ladakh, plan for it: a travel eSIM or roaming on your home SIM, or a connection issued inside the region. We tell you in your plan which days this affects. For Kashmir, also check current travel advice — the UK and US governments both advise against travel to most of Jammu & Kashmir; see our Kashmir seasons page for the dated details.

No signal in the high mountains — and no satellite phones

Himachal, Spiti, Ladakh and Uttarakhand are some of the best places in India — and some of the worst for phone signal.

Expect gaps

The UK Foreign Office: "You are extremely unlikely to have a phone signal in the mountains, so make sure your accommodation provider registers your whereabouts using the online C-form."

Satellite phones are illegal

"It is illegal to possess and operate satellite phones and GPS devices in India without a licence." "You can be fined or arrested for possession of these devices without permission" (UK Foreign Office, advice updated 7 Oct 2026). Leave them at home.

Weather and protests

The same advice notes that mobile networks and internet may be disrupted during demonstrations, and that monsoon storms can cut telephone lines, internet and power.

Download before you go

Save offline maps, your hotel list and booking details on your phone before mountain days, so you aren't relying on signal.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Can tourists buy a SIM card in India?

Yes. Foreign visitors can get an Indian connection from an operator's store or counter with a passport, valid Indian visa, a passport-size photo and a local address such as a hotel or tour operator (COAI).

What documents do I need for an Indian SIM card?

Your original passport and a photocopy with the visa page, a printed passport-size colour photo, a local address (hotel or tour operator) and the signed application form the shop gives you.

Can I get an Indian eSIM as a tourist?

Indian operators offer eSIMs, but they usually need the same in-person ID check, and online self-activation generally needs an Indian number. A travel eSIM bought before you fly is the easier eSIM option for most visitors.

How long does SIM activation take in India?

It is not instant. The SIM can't make or receive calls until the operator finishes verification, which can take from a few hours to a day or two. Buy it on your first day.

How long is a tourist SIM card valid in India?

For foreign tourists a connection must not run past the visa and cannot be longer than 3 months at a time, even with a longer visa (COAI).

Will my SIM work in Kashmir and Ladakh?

Prepaid SIMs from other parts of India don't work in Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh; postpaid connections do. Confirm with the operator before you go. Roaming on your home SIM or a travel eSIM are alternatives.

Is there phone signal in the Himalaya?

Often not. The UK Foreign Office says you are extremely unlikely to have a phone signal in the mountains. Download offline maps and share your plan before mountain days.

Can I bring a satellite phone to India?

No, not without a licence. The UK Foreign Office says it is illegal to possess and operate satellite phones and GPS devices in India without a licence, and you can be fined or arrested.
