Before you choose a month: what governments advise about Kashmir

The UK FCDO advises against all travel to the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir, and names Pahalgam, Gulmarg, Sonamarg, the city of Srinagar and the Jammu–Srinagar national highway; the only exceptions are flying to and from Jammu city and travel within Jammu city (updated 7 Oct 2026, checked 25 Oct 2026). The US State Department rates Jammu and Kashmir 'Level 4: Do not travel', except visits to the eastern Ladakh region and Leh (checked 25 Oct 2026). The FCDO also warns: 'Your travel insurance could be invalidated if you travel against advice from the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO).' Check your own government's latest advice and your insurer's terms before you book — if they rule Kashmir out, we will happily plan the same season in Himachal instead.

The four seasons, in short

Spring (March–April)

The Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden sits at the foot of the Zabarwan range above Dal Lake, with about 1.5–1.75 million bulbs of some 70–75 varieties; it opened for the 2026 season in March. March is also Srinagar's wettest month, so pack layers and a rain jacket.

Summer (May–August)

The warmest stretch: Srinagar averages 18–24 °C (64–76 °F). July and August overlap the Amarnath Yatra pilgrimage (2026: 3 July–28 August), when roads towards Sonamarg get busy — plan around it.

Autumn (September–November)

The driest months: October averages just 23 mm (0.9 in) of rain in Srinagar and 14.4 °C (57.9 °F). Nights turn cold by November.

Winter (December–February)

Chillai Kalan, the 40 hardest winter days, runs 21 December–31 January, followed by Chillai Khurd and Chillai Bachha to early March. Gulmarg's ski season usually runs mid-December to mid-April — see our Gulmarg skiing guide.

Which month suits which trip

Three common reasons people look at Kashmir, and the months that fit each.

  1. Flowers and gardensLate March to April for the tulip garden. Confirm the garden's opening dates for your year before you fix flights.
  2. Mild weather and lakesMay–June or September–October. September and October are drier; avoid July–August if you want quieter roads.
  3. SnowMid-December to February, with Chillai Kalan (21 Dec–31 Jan) the coldest weeks. Skiers look at Gulmarg from mid-December to mid-April; read our Kashmir in December notes too.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

What is the best month to visit Kashmir?

It depends on your goal: late March–April for the tulip garden, May–June or September–October for mild, drier days, and mid-December to February for snow. October is Srinagar's driest month (23 mm / 0.9 in) and July the warmest (24.2 °C / 75.6 °F).

When do the tulips bloom in Srinagar?

The Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden opens each spring; in 2026 it opened in March. The display lasts a few weeks into April, so check the garden's opening dates for your year before you book flights.

When does it snow in Kashmir?

Winter runs roughly December to February. Chillai Kalan, the 40 hardest winter days, is 21 December to 31 January. Gulmarg's ski season usually runs from mid-December to mid-April (JKTDC). Snow amounts vary every year.

What is Chillai Kalan?

The local name for the 40 coldest days of the Kashmir winter, 21 December to 31 January. It is followed by Chillai Khurd (20 days) and Chillai Bachha (10 days), ending in early March.

Is July or August a good time to visit Kashmir?

They are the warmest months (about 24 °C / 75 °F average in Srinagar), but they overlap the Amarnath Yatra pilgrimage (2026: 3 July–28 August), when roads and stays near Sonamarg and Pahalgam are busy.

Is it safe to visit Kashmir?

We don't make safety promises. The UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir, naming Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg (updated 7 Oct 2026), and the US State Department rates it Level 4: Do not travel (checked 25 Oct 2026). Read your own government's latest advice before deciding.

Will my travel insurance cover Kashmir?

Check with your insurer in writing. The UK FCDO says your travel insurance could be invalidated if you travel against its advice. We ask every guest to confirm cover before booking.
