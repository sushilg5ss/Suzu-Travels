Before anything else: what governments advise about Kashmir

The UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) advises against all travel to Jammu & Kashmir, including Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg (checked 13 Nov 2026). The US State Department rates Jammu & Kashmir Level 4: Do not travel. Read your own government's advice before you book. Travelling against your government's advice can invalidate your travel insurance. The FCDO's only exceptions are flying to and from Jammu city and travel within Jammu city, and it notes that there have been frequent terrorist incidents in Jammu and Kashmir.

We will tell you honestly if we don't recommend travelling on your dates. If your advice or your insurer rules Kashmir out, we plan mountains and snow in Himachal instead — see our Himachal 10-day itinerary and where to see snow in India.

Seven days in Kashmir, day by day

A sample we build from, once you have checked the advice above. The quote changes with the month and the hotels; the pace stays easy.

  1. DAY 1
    Fly into Srinagar · Dal Lake

    Your driver meets you at Srinagar airport — name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip. An afternoon shikara ride on Dal Lake and a night on a houseboat.

  2. DAY 2
    Srinagar: Mughal gardens and the old city

    The terraced Mughal gardens along the lake, then the old city at a walking pace. In spring the Tulip Garden in the Zabarwan foothills, overlooking Dal Lake, opens in March–April.

  3. DAY 3
    Day trip to Gulmarg

    About 52–56 km (32–35 mi) to the meadow at Gulmarg and the gondola: Phase 1 to Kongdoori at about 3,100 m (10,200 ft), Phase 2 to Apharwat at about 3,980 m (13,000 ft). Snow is likely from December to March. Back to Srinagar for the night.

  4. DAY 4
    Srinagar → Pahalgam

    About 95 km (59 mi), a road drive of about 2½ hours, to Pahalgam at 2,130 m (6,990 ft) in the Lidder valley. An easy afternoon by the river.

  5. DAY 5
    Pahalgam: Betaab Valley and Aru

    Betaab Valley, and the Aru valley 15 km (9 mi) from Pahalgam, by local transport. Pahalgam is also where the Amarnath Yatra starts — expect crowds and restrictions in July and August.

  6. DAY 6
    Pahalgam → Srinagar, or Sonamarg in summer

    Back to Srinagar. In summer, a day on the Srinagar–Leh road to Sonamarg (about 90–110 km, around 3 hours from Srinagar) and the Thajiwas glacier — private vehicles aren't allowed for sightseeing there, so you hire a local taxi. In winter we swap Sonamarg for a second Gulmarg day.

  7. DAY 7
    Fly out of Srinagar

    Your driver drops you at Srinagar airport with a margin before your flight — on to Delhi, home, or the rest of India.

Winter and summer: which days change

The shape of the week stays the same; a few days change with the season. Our month-by-month table is on the best time to visit Kashmir.

Spring (March–April)

The Tulip Garden opens. March is Srinagar's wettest month (104.6 mm / 4.1 in), so we keep Day 2 flexible.

Summer

July is the warmest month, with a mean of 24.2 °C (75.6 °F) in Srinagar. Sonamarg's hotels open only in the summer months. The Amarnath Yatra (3 Jul–28 Aug in 2026) uses Pahalgam and the Baltal side near Sonamarg — expect crowds and restrictions.

Autumn

October is the driest month in Srinagar (23.3 mm / 0.9 in) — clear days for the gardens and the drives.

Winter

Cold: January's mean is 2.5 °C (36.5 °F), and the harshest stretch is locally known as Chillai Kalan (21 Dec–31 Jan). Gulmarg's ski season usually runs mid-December to mid-April. No Sonamarg day — a second Gulmarg day instead. Skiing? See our Gulmarg ski holiday page.

Practical notes for Kashmir

Visa

Apply yourself for the India e-Visa at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware look-alike sites that charge extra. We are not a visa agency; our e-Visa guide walks you through it.

Insurance

Ask your insurer in writing whether you are covered in Jammu & Kashmir before you book — travelling against your government's advice can invalidate your travel insurance. Our India travel insurance guide explains what to ask.

Cold and altitude

Gulmarg's gondola tops out at about 3,980 m (13,000 ft) and winters are cold. If you have heart or breathing conditions, consult your doctor before you go and see an official travel-health site (CDC, NHS Fit for Travel).

Zoji La and Ladakh

Sonamarg is the last stop before the Zoji La pass (3,540 m / 11,600 ft), 20 km (12 mi) further east on the road to Ladakh. This itinerary turns back at Sonamarg.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Is it safe to visit Kashmir?

Read your own government's advice first. The UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu & Kashmir, including Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg (checked 13 Nov 2026), and the US State Department rates it Level 4: Do not travel. Travelling against your government's advice can invalidate your travel insurance. We will tell you honestly if we don't recommend travelling on your dates.

How many days do I need for Kashmir?

Five to seven days. Seven lets you base three nights in Srinagar with a Gulmarg day trip, spend two nights in Pahalgam, and add Sonamarg in summer without rushing.

In which order should I visit Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg?

Start in Srinagar, which is where you fly in. Gulmarg (about 52–56 km) works as a day trip from Srinagar; Pahalgam (about 95 km, about 2½ hours) is worth two nights; Sonamarg (about 90–110 km, around 3 hours) is a summer day trip on the way back.

What is the best month to visit Kashmir?

It depends on what you want: spring for the Tulip Garden (March–April), summer for Sonamarg, October for the driest weather, winter for snow at Gulmarg. Avoid July–August if you dislike crowds — that is the Amarnath Yatra season. Month by month on our best-time-to-visit-Kashmir page.

Can I see snow in Kashmir?

In winter, at Gulmarg: snow is likely from December to March, and the ski season usually runs mid-December to mid-April. Nothing is guaranteed. In winter we swap the Sonamarg day for a second Gulmarg day.

Can I drive myself in Kashmir?

We plan it with a private car and driver; your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip. In Sonamarg private vehicles aren't allowed for sightseeing — visitors hire a local taxi there.
