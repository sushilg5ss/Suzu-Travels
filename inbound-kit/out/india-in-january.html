Snow in January: the Himalayan hills

We are based in Himachal. January is the snowiest month in the hill towns — likely, but never certain on a given date. The India Meteorological Department's long-term figures for Shimla:

In Manali, Himachal Pradesh Tourism says temperatures in December–January can fall below −5 °C (23 °F); the slopes at Solang, 13 km away, become a ski area in winter. Rohtang Pass is closed from late November until about May, but the Atal Tunnel keeps the road to Lahaul open. In Uttarakhand, Auli's ski season runs from December, with snow until March. Sun in January: the south and the desert

If you are escaping winter at home, January is the heart of the best season for these:

Kerala

Kerala Tourism names November–February as peak season and October–February as the best time for houseboat cruises; December–February is mild. Up in Thekkady it can drop to 13 °C (55 °F), so pack a layer. See our houseboat guide.

Goa

Goa's foreign visitors come in winter, and Incredible India gives Palolem's best months as November to February.

Rajasthan

Clear, warm days and cold desert nights: Jaisalmer averages 4.6–29 °C (40–84 °F) in January, and Incredible India gives its best time as November to March. A desert camp night needs warm clothes.

The Golden Triangle

Delhi, Agra and Jaipur are dry and comfortable by day. Morning fog can delay early starts and sunrise at the Taj, so we keep spare time in the plan. The Taj Mahal opens 30 minutes before sunrise and is closed on Fridays.

What to plan around in January

Fog and night driving

The UK FCDO advises: 'Avoid travelling at night and in fog as accidents are common due to low visibility.' In January we plan daytime drives only and keep buffer time on long road days and early flights.

Cold mornings

The north is cold before sunrise and after dark, and the hills are colder still. Our India packing list covers layers for a winter trip.

Kashmir in deep winter

Srinagar averages about 2.5 °C (36.5 °F) in January, the heart of Chillai Kalan, the 40 harshest winter days (21 December–31 January), and Gulmarg's ski season runs mid-December to mid-April. The UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir except Jammu city, and the US State Department says 'Do not travel' to J&K except eastern Ladakh and Leh (advice checked 12 Nov 2026). Your travel insurance could be invalidated if you travel against FCDO advice. Check the latest before you book — see our Gulmarg guide.

Busy dates

The Kumbh bathing days in Haridwar, the literature festival in Jaipur and Republic Day in New Delhi are big occasions. If your dates touch them, book flights, hotels and your car early.

What about February?

February is just as good for most of India, and a little warmer. Shimla averages 3.3–11.5 °C (38–53 °F) with about 40 cm of snow, so the hills are still white; Kerala and Goa stay in season; Rajasthan's days warm up. In 2027 the Haridwar Kumbh continues through February and March. For a whole-year view, see our guide to the best time to visit India — and if you are coming earlier, India in December.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Is January a good time to visit India?

Yes, for most of the country. Kerala, Goa and Rajasthan are dry and sunny, the Golden Triangle is pleasant by day, and the Himachal hills get their heaviest snow. Plan for cold nights and morning fog in the north.

Where is it warm in India in January?

Kerala and Goa are warm and in peak season, and Rajasthan has warm, sunny days — Jodhpur averages up to 30 °C (86 °F) — with cold nights. Delhi, Agra and Jaipur are pleasant by day and cold early in the morning.

Does it snow in India in January?

Yes, in the Himalaya. The India Meteorological Department's average January snowfall in Shimla is about 40 cm (16 in), and Manali, Solang and Auli are snowy too. Snow is never guaranteed on a given date.

How cold is North India in January?

Shimla averages 2.5–10.3 °C (37–51 °F) in January, and Manali can fall below −5 °C (23 °F). On the plains days are mild but early mornings are cold and often foggy. Bring warm layers, a jacket and a hat.

What festivals are there in India in January 2027?

Makar Sankranti on Thursday 14 January (also the first bathing day of the Haridwar Ardh Kumbh), Pongal on Friday 15 January, the Jaipur Literature Festival on 14–18 January (as reported) and Republic Day on Tuesday 26 January.

Can tourists watch the Republic Day parade?

Yes, with a ticket. India's Ministry of Defence sells parade and Beating Retreat tickets online and at New Delhi counters in early January; carry your passport. The 2027 sale dates are not out yet — check rashtraparv.mod.gov.in.

Is Kerala or Goa better in January?

Both are in season. Kerala suits backwaters, tea hills and culture; Goa suits beaches and a slower pace. Either pairs well with the Golden Triangle — tell us what you enjoy and we suggest the better fit.
