What are the luxury trains in India, and how do you book them?

India's four best-known luxury trains are the Maharajas' Express (IRCTC), Palace on Wheels (Rajasthan), Golden Chariot (Karnataka, with IRCTC) and Deccan Odyssey (Maharashtra). Most journeys last about a week, with cabin, meals and sightseeing included, and run in the cooler months. Book only through the operator's official website, IRCTC offices or an authorised IRCTC agent.

Maharajas' Express: IRCTC's flagship train

The official site calls it "the flagship luxury train of IRCTC", and lists four journeys, all with departures from October to April:

Heritage of India · 6 nights

Mumbai – Udaipur – Jodhpur – Bikaner – Jaipur – Ranthambore and Fatehpur Sikri – Agra – Delhi.

The Indian Splendour · 6 nights

The reverse direction: Delhi – Agra – Ranthambore – Jaipur – Bikaner – Jodhpur – Udaipur – Mumbai.

Indian Panorama · 6 nights

Delhi – Jaipur – Ranthambore and Fatehpur Sikri – Agra – Orchha and Khajuraho – Varanasi – Delhi.

Treasures of India · 3 nights

Delhi – Agra – Ranthambore – Jaipur – Delhi. The short option if your trip is under two weeks.

What the official site lists as included

Meals, house wines, spirits and beers, sightseeing with entrance fees, a tour director, air-conditioned transfers and a paramedic on board.

How booking works

Through the official website, IRCTC offices or authorised IRCTC agents. The site asks for a 25% advance, with the balance 30 days before departure (60 days if you book through an agent).

Palace on Wheels: a week around Rajasthan

Rajasthan Tourism describes the Palace on Wheels leaving Safdarjung station in New Delhi every Wednesday night on a week-long run, and returning to Delhi after Jaipur, Sawai Madhopur (for Ranthambore National Park), Chittorgarh, Udaipur, Jaisalmer, Jodhpur, Bharatpur (for Keoladeo National Park) and Agra for the Taj Mahal.

RTDC's own itinerary page starts the day with a 4.00 pm welcome at Safdarjung and a 6.30 pm departure for Jaipur, dinner on board. The train has 14 saloons, each with four twin-bedded chambers with attached bathrooms, two restaurant cars and a spa. Ask RTDC, or an authorised agent, for this season's departure dates before you book flights.

Golden Chariot and Deccan Odyssey: the south and west

Two trains for travellers who have already seen the north, or want temples, palaces and coast instead of desert forts:

Golden Chariot — Karnataka and the south

Presented by Karnataka State Tourism and IRCTC. Pride of Karnataka runs Bengaluru – Bandipur – Mysore – Halebidu – Chikmagaluru – Hampi – Pattadakal and Aihole – Goa – Bengaluru; Jewels of South adds Mahabalipuram, Thanjavur and Chettinad, Kochi and Kumarakom; Glimpses of Karnataka is a 3-night loop.

Golden Chariot — cabins and booking

Karnataka Tourism lists 13 double cabins, 30 twin cabins and one cabin for guests with disabilities. Book on goldenchariot.org, at IRCTC offices or through an authorised IRCTC agent; the site asks for a 20% advance. The official pages differ on whether the long journeys are 6 or 7 nights — check the current itinerary.

Deccan Odyssey — Maharashtra

Run in association with Maharashtra Tourism (MTDC). The Indian Railways page describes a 7-night loop from Mumbai along the Konkan coast to Goa, then Kolhapur, Pune, Ellora, Ajanta and Nashik, running October to March. That page is old, and MTDC's current page lists no dates — ask MTDC what runs this season.

Book on the official site — and watch for look-alikes

Search for any of these trains and you will find many websites using the train's name. Some are authorised agents, some are not. Before you pay:

  1. Start from the operatorUse the booking links on IRCTC's own pages, Rajasthan Tourism / RTDC, Karnataka Tourism or MTDC — the official sites linked in our sources below.
  2. If you use an agent, ask if it is authorisedIRCTC lets you book through its offices and authorised IRCTC agents. Ask the agent to confirm it is authorised, and check before you pay.
  3. Read the advance and balance datesThe official sites ask for an advance (25% on the Maharajas' Express, 20% on the Golden Chariot) and the balance 30 days before departure, or 60 days through an agent.
  4. Get the e-ticket or travel voucherBoth IRCTC trains send a confirmation email and an e-ticket / travel voucher. Keep it with your passport.
  5. Book flights lastDeparture dates are fixed and some journeys run only a few times a season. Confirm the train first, then your flights.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Which is the most famous luxury train in India?

The Maharajas' Express (run by IRCTC) and the Palace on Wheels (Rajasthan Tourism) are the two names most visitors know. Both focus on Rajasthan and Agra; the Golden Chariot covers Karnataka and the south, and the Deccan Odyssey Maharashtra.

What is the difference between the Maharajas' Express and the Palace on Wheels?

The Maharajas' Express is IRCTC's train with three week-long journeys (via Mumbai, or Khajuraho and Varanasi) and a 3-night loop, departing October to April. The Palace on Wheels is Rajasthan Tourism's train: one week-long Rajasthan circuit from New Delhi, leaving on Wednesday nights.

How do I book a luxury train in India from abroad?

Book on the operator's official website, at IRCTC offices or through an authorised IRCTC agent. The official sites take an advance online and the balance 30 days before departure (60 days through an agent). Watch for look-alike websites that use the train's name.

When do India's luxury trains run?

In the cooler months. The Maharajas' Express lists departures from October to April; the Deccan Odyssey has historically run October to March. Departure dates are fixed and published by each operator — check them before booking flights.

How long is a luxury train journey in India?

Most journeys are about a week (6 or 7 nights). The Maharajas' Express also has a 3-night Treasures of India loop, and the Golden Chariot a 3-night Glimpses of Karnataka.

What is included on India's luxury trains?

Your cabin, meals and sightseeing on the route. The Maharajas' Express site also lists house drinks, entrance fees, a tour director, transfers and a paramedic on board. Check each operator's current list before you book.
