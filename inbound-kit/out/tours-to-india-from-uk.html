UK travel advice on Kashmir

The UK FCDO advises against all travel to the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir — including Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg — except air travel to and within Jammu city (FCDO advice updated 7 Oct 2026, checked 7 Nov 2026). Travel insurance may not cover you where the FCDO advises against travel.

Visa, passport and arrival card for British travellers

Entry rules as summarised by the UK FCDO (checked 9 Nov 2026); confirm before you travel.

  1. You need a visaThe FCDO says you must have a visa to visit India unless you are an Overseas Citizen of India (OCI). Most visitors use the e-Visa.
  2. Apply yourself, on the official siteWe are not a visa agency. Apply yourself, only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware look-alike sites that charge extra. Apply at least 4 days before you arrive; the fee depends on your nationality, so check the official fee table. Our e-Visa guide walks through the form.
  3. Check your passportIt must expire at least 6 months after the date you arrive and have at least 2 blank pages (FCDO).
  4. Fill in the e-Arrival cardForeign nationals must complete an e-arrival card online before reaching immigration; you can do it up to 72 hours before arrival. It is not a visa.
  5. HealthConsult your doctor; see TravelHealthPro or NHS Fit for Travel well before you go.

ATOL, in plain English

We arrange the India part of your trip (car and driver, stays, guides) and do not sell flights. ATOL protection (UK Civil Aviation Authority) applies to flight-inclusive package holidays sold in the UK, so it does not apply to our land arrangements. Your protection with us is the booking terms below — and we recommend travel insurance from the day you book.

The CAA describes ATOL as a scheme that protects travellers and their money if the travel operator they booked with ceases trading — and notes that ATOL does not cover situations such as cancelled flights. Our India travel insurance guide explains what to look for in a policy.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Do British citizens need a visa for India?

Yes, unless you are an Overseas Citizen of India (OCI), says the UK FCDO. Most visitors apply for the e-Visa themselves at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in, at least 4 days before arrival — beware look-alike sites that charge extra. We are not a visa agency.

What passport validity do I need for India?

The FCDO says your passport must expire at least 6 months after the date you arrive and have at least 2 blank pages. You also fill in an online e-arrival card within 72 hours before landing; it is not a visa.

Which UK airports have non-stop Air India flights to India?

London Heathrow, to Delhi. Other airlines also fly from the UK, so compare options. For winter 2026–27 Air India ended its Amritsar–Gatwick (25 Oct 2026) and Delhi–Birmingham (4 Nov 2026) flights, and Amritsar–Birmingham may be paused. Schedules change each season, so check the airline for this season's timetable.

Is my India tour ATOL protected?

Not with us. ATOL applies to flight-inclusive package holidays sold in the UK. We arrange only the India part (car and driver, stays, guides) and do not sell flights, so ATOL does not apply. Your protection is our booking terms — 20% deposit, full refund within 48 hours — and we recommend travel insurance from the day you book.

What is the time difference between the UK and India?

India Standard Time is UTC+5:30 with no daylight saving, so India is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of the UK in winter (GMT) and 4 hours 30 minutes ahead in summer (BST).

Can I pay from the UK?

Yes. Prices are quoted in US dollars and you pay by card through Razorpay (international cards accepted) or PayPal; your bank charges your UK card in pounds. A 20% deposit locks your dates and price, with a full refund if you cancel within 48 hours of paying.

Why book with a local company instead of a UK tour operator?

It is a different product: a private trip with your own car and driver, your own dates and pace, planned by the team that runs it. A UK escorted tour gives you a group, fixed dates and flights in one package. Our guide on how to choose a travel agency in India lists the checks to make with anyone.

When is the best time to visit India from the UK?

October to March suits Delhi, Agra and Rajasthan. The FCDO notes severe air pollution in North Indian cities, especially from October to February, so check air quality if you have breathing problems. Our best time to visit India guide goes month by month.
