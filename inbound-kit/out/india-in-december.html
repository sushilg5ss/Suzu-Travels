Is December a good time to visit India?

Yes. December is one of the best months to visit India: the Golden Triangle (Delhi, Agra, Jaipur) and Rajasthan are dry with sunny days, Kerala and Goa are in peak season, and the Himachal hill towns such as Shimla often get snow. Snow in December: the Himachal hills

We are based in Himachal, so this is the question we hear most. Snow in December is likely in the hills but never certain. The India Meteorological Department's long-term figures for Shimla:

Dec–JanShimla's snowy season (district administration)

Shimla's district administration says nights in December–January fall to about 0 °C (32 °F) and asks visitors to book hotels early for Christmas and New Year. Snowfall is also common in winter in Dharamshala and McLeod Ganj. Above Manali, Rohtang Pass closes from late November until about May, but the Atal Tunnel keeps the road to Lahaul open. Sun in December: the south and the desert

If you want warm days rather than snow, December is the start of the best stretch for these:

Kerala

Kerala Tourism names November–February as peak season and October–February as the best time for houseboat cruises; December–February is mild. Up in Thekkady it can drop to 13 °C (55 °F), so pack a layer. See our houseboat guide.

Goa

Goa's foreign visitors come in winter, and Incredible India gives Palolem's best months as November to February. Christmas and New Year are very busy — book early.

Rajasthan

Clear days and cold desert nights: Jodhpur averages 6–32 °C (43–89 °F) in December, and Incredible India gives Jaisalmer's best time as November to March. A night at a desert camp needs warm clothes.

The Golden Triangle

Delhi, Agra and Jaipur are dry and comfortable by day. Morning fog can delay early starts and sunrise at the Taj, so we keep spare time in the plan. The Taj Mahal opens 30 minutes before sunrise and is closed on Fridays.

What to plan around in December

Christmas–New Year crowds

The Christmas–New Year weeks are very busy in Goa, Kerala, Rajasthan and the hill towns. If those are your dates, book flights, hotels and your car early.

Fog and night driving

The UK FCDO advises: 'Avoid travelling at night and in fog as accidents are common due to low visibility.' In December we plan daytime drives only and keep buffer time on long road days.

Cold mornings

North India is cold before sunrise and after dark, and the hills are colder still. Our India packing list covers layers for a winter trip.

Skiing and Kashmir

Gulmarg's ski season usually starts around mid-December. The UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir except Jammu city, and the US State Department says 'Do not travel' to J&K except eastern Ladakh and Leh (advice checked 23 Oct 2026). FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Is December a good time to visit India?

Yes, for most of the country. The Golden Triangle, Rajasthan, Kerala and Goa are all in season, and the Himachal hills often get snow. Plan for cold nights and morning fog in the north, and book early if you travel over Christmas and New Year.

Where is it warm in India in December?

Kerala and Goa are warm and in peak season, and Rajasthan has warm, sunny days — Jodhpur averages up to 32 °C (89 °F) — with cold nights. Delhi, Agra and Jaipur are pleasant by day and cold in the early morning.

Does it snow in India in December?

Yes, in the Himalaya. The India Meteorological Department's average December snowfall in Shimla is about 7 cm, and snowfall is common in winter in Manali, Dharamshala and McLeod Ganj. Snow is never guaranteed on a given date.

How cold is North India in December?

Shimla averages 4.8–13.1 °C (41–56 °F) in December, with nights near freezing. On the plains days are mild but early mornings are cold and foggy. Bring warm layers, a jacket and a hat.

Is India crowded at Christmas and New Year?

Yes — the Christmas–New Year weeks are very busy in Goa, Kerala, Rajasthan and the hill towns. Book flights, hotels and your car early; Shimla's district administration gives the same advice.

Is Goa or Kerala better in December?

Both are in season. Kerala suits backwaters, tea hills and culture; Goa suits beaches and a slower pace. Either pairs well with the Golden Triangle — tell us what you enjoy and we suggest the better fit.

Can I see tigers in December?

Yes, the parks are open. Corbett's Dhikala zone opens from 15 November and central India's reserves are best from October to June. Sightings are never guaranteed, and safari permits are booked through the official portals.
