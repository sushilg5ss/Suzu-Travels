What to see in Varanasi

The places that make a first Varanasi trip, from the Incredible India (Ministry of Tourism) guide:

Dashashwamedh Ghat and the Ganga Aarti

The main ghat, open day and night. The evening Ganga Aarti here lasts forty-five minutes at dusk, around sunset — lamps, bells and chants from a row of priests. Arrive early for a place on the steps, or watch from a boat. Our Ganga Aarti guide has more.

A sunrise boat on the Ganga

The river at first light, with pilgrims bathing and praying on the ghats.

Kashi Vishwanath Temple

The great Shiva temple near Dashashwamedh Ghat. Strict rules on what you can carry — see below.

Assi Ghat

Another of the ghats listed by Incredible India — a good place to start a walk along the river.

Sarnath

Where the Buddha gave his first sermon, a short drive from the ghats. A UNESCO World Heritage Site since July 2026 — see below.

Ramnagar Fort and Bharat Kala Bhavan

Ramnagar Fort, the Bharat Kala Bhavan museum and the New Vishwanath Temple — for an afternoon away from the ghats.

Sarnath: India's newest UNESCO World Heritage Site

On the evening of 25 July 2026 the World Heritage Committee, meeting in Busan, inscribed the Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath on the World Heritage List — India's 45th World Heritage property (Press Information Bureau, 26 August 2026).

The first sermon

Sarnath is where the Buddha gave his first sermon after his enlightenment — the 'Turning of the Wheel of the Law'.

What is protected

The Chaukhandi Stupa and the archaeological remains of Sarnath: the Dhamek Stupa, the Dharmarajika Stupa, the Mulagandhakuti Vihara and the Ashokan Pillar — monuments from the 4th–3rd century BCE to the 12th century CE.

The Lion Capital

The Ashokan Lion Capital found at Sarnath became India's National Emblem. It is in the Sarnath Museum, founded in 1904 — India's earliest site museum.

How long

Half a day is enough for the stupa park and the museum. We plan it for the morning, before the afternoon heat.

Temple rules and cremation-ghat etiquette

Kashi Vishwanath Temple: "Any electronic or non-electronic gadgets, pen etc. is not allowed inside the temple premises" (Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple Trust FAQ, checked 28 Oct 2026). Free lockers are at Mandir Chauk, YSK-1/2/3 and the Helpdesk. There is no dress code, but the trust asks for decent clothes. Darshan runs 04:00–11:00, 12:20–19:00 and 20:30–21:00. Visitors of all faiths come; rules can change — ask at the helpdesk.

Cremation ghats: Manikarnika and Harishchandra are working cremation grounds. Watch quietly from a distance, don't photograph funerals, and decline anyone who 'guides' you in and then asks for money for wood.

The temple also holds ticketed aartis — the Mangla Aarti at 03:00–04:00 (report at 02:30 at Gate 1), the Sapta-Rishi Aarti at 19:00–20:15 and the Shringar Aarti at 21:00–22:15. Booked aarti tickets are non-refundable. A paid 'Sugam Darshan' with a temple Shastri is offered too.

Best time for a Varanasi trip — and when not to go

Before you go: visa, safety and health

You need an Indian visa before you fly. Apply only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware of look-alike sites that charge extra. We are not a visa agency. See our India e-Visa guide.

Touts: the UK Foreign Office advises "Be cautious in tourist areas, where scammers, pickpockets and ticket touts target foreign nationals" and to "avoid people at railway stations offering tickets and tours" (advice updated 7 October 2026). In Varanasi that means boat and 'guide' offers on the ghats — agree prices before you start, or let your arranged guide handle it.

Health: Winter air can be poor in North Indian cities — if you have asthma or a heart or lung condition, ask your doctor and see NHS Fit for Travel or the CDC. We don't recommend swimming in the Ganga; consult official health sites.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

How many days do you need in Varanasi?

Two full days cover a sunrise boat, the evening Ganga Aarti, Kashi Vishwanath Temple and Sarnath. Three days let you see it all at an easier pace, with a second aarti and time on the quieter ghats.

What time is the Ganga Aarti in Varanasi?

Every evening at dusk, around sunset, at Dashashwamedh Ghat; it lasts about forty-five minutes. The start time moves with the sunset through the year, so arrive early for a place on the steps or watch from a boat.

Can I take my phone into Kashi Vishwanath Temple?

No. The temple trust says no electronic or non-electronic gadgets — not even a pen — are allowed inside the premises. Free lockers are at Mandir Chauk, YSK-1/2/3 and the Helpdesk. Visitors of all faiths come; rules can change, so ask at the helpdesk.

Is it OK to watch cremations at Manikarnika Ghat?

Manikarnika and Harishchandra are working cremation grounds. Watch quietly from a distance, don't photograph funerals, and decline anyone who 'guides' you in and then asks for money for wood.

When is the best time to visit Varanasi?

October to March. Dev Deepawali, when the ghats are lit with thousands of lamps, is on 24 November 2026. Avoid July to September, when monsoon floods can cover the ghats and halt the boats; May can reach 45 °C (113 °F).

How do I get from Delhi to Varanasi?

Many visitors fly to Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport (VNS). There is also the Vande Bharat Express between Delhi and Varanasi — check current times and book on IRCTC. Varanasi airport is on the e-Visa entry list.

Is Sarnath worth visiting?

Yes. Sarnath is where the Buddha gave his first sermon, and it became a UNESCO World Heritage Site on 25 July 2026. The Dhamek Stupa, the Ashokan Pillar and the Sarnath Museum with the Lion Capital fit into half a day.
