When to go on a tiger safari

The short answer: October to June, when the core zones of most reserves are open.

October to February — the cool season

Parks reopen after the monsoon in October: Ranthambore on 1 October in recent seasons, Corbett's Bijrani on 15 October and the Central India core zones during October (dates are announced each year). Corbett's Dhikala zone opens on 15 November. This is also the best time for the rest of North India, so a safari fits easily into a wider trip.

March to June — the hot season

The parks stay open until about 30 June (Dhikala until 15 June). Days are hot, so drives are early in the morning and late in the afternoon. Ask us what to expect for your dates.

July to September — the monsoon

The core zones of Madhya Pradesh's tiger reserves close for the breeding season; buffer-zone safaris stay open. In Corbett only Jhirna and Dhela stay open through the year. We plan the Himalaya or other regions for these months instead.

How to book safari permits safely

Safari permits are sold only through the state forest departments' official portals; many look-alike 'safari booking' websites resell them at a mark-up. Here is how it works:

  1. Choose the park, zone and number of drivesEach reserve allows a limited number of vehicles per drive, one in the morning and one in the afternoon. Popular zones sell out early in winter, so decide your dates first.
  2. Book only on the official portalRanthambore: Rajasthan Forest Department (fmdss.forest.rajasthan.gov.in). Corbett: corbettonline.uk.gov.in. Kanha, Bandhavgarh and Pench: forest.mponline.gov.in. Foreign nationals pay a higher fee than Indian citizens and book with their passport details.
  3. Know the rules before you payIn Corbett, permits are issued for at most three days and are non-transferable and non-refundable once issued; private vehicles are not allowed — you ride in a registered safari vehicle with up to six guests.
  4. Or let us do itWe book your permits on the official portals as part of your trip, with the car, hotels and transfers, and send you the permit documents. Your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip.

Will I see a tiger? An honest answer

Tiger sightings are never guaranteed — the forest is wild, and anyone who promises a tiger is guessing.

Plan more than one drive

In our planning experience, two or three nights at one park (four to six drives) gives you a fair chance and time to enjoy the rest of the forest. One drive on a day trip is a gamble.

The forest is more than the tiger

Corbett has wild elephants and 580+ bird species; Ranthambore has sambar at its lakes and a UNESCO-listed fort in the middle of the forest; Kanha has the rare barasingha. A good drive without a tiger is still a good drive.

Mornings and evenings

Animals move most in the cooler hours, which is why both daily drives start early and end before sunset. No one may enter the reserves after sunset.

Rules inside the reserve

Stay in the vehicle, keep your voice low, never feed the animals and don't play music — the Corbett forest department lists getting out of vehicles, loud music and feeding wildlife as strictly prohibited, and the same rules apply in every reserve. Your naturalist and driver decide how close to go.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Which is the best national park to see tigers in India?

There is no single best park. Ranthambore is the easiest to add to a Golden Triangle trip, Corbett is closest to Delhi and the Himalaya, and Madhya Pradesh Tourism says Bandhavgarh offers one of the highest chances of spotting a tiger. Choose by your route and dates — we help you decide.

When is the best time for a tiger safari in India?

October to June, when the core zones are open. October to February is cooler and fits a wider North India trip; March to June is hot, with early-morning and late-afternoon drives. Most core zones close for the monsoon, July to September.

Is a tiger sighting guaranteed?

No. Tigers are wild animals and no one can promise a sighting. Two or three nights at one park, with four to six drives, gives you a fair chance — and the forests have elephants, deer, leopards and hundreds of bird species too.

How do I book a safari in Ranthambore or Corbett?

Only on the official portals: the Rajasthan Forest Department (fmdss.forest.rajasthan.gov.in) for Ranthambore and corbettonline.uk.gov.in for Corbett. Many look-alike websites resell permits at a mark-up. We can book them for you as part of your trip.

Can foreigners go on a tiger safari in India?

Yes. Foreign nationals book with their passport details and pay a higher fee than Indian citizens. Bring the same passport to the park gate.

Can I do Ranthambore as a day trip from Jaipur?

It is possible, but one drive is a gamble. We recommend at least two nights near the park, placed between Agra and Jaipur on a Golden Triangle route.

How far is Corbett from Delhi?

Ramnagar, the town at Corbett's main gates, is about 240 km (150 mi) from Delhi by road, according to the Nainital district administration. Ramnagar also has a railway station.
