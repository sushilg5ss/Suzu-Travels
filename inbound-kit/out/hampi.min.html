What Hampi is — and why it is worth the journey

The Group of Monuments at Hampi was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list in 1986. In the words of the Archaeological Survey of India, it holds the remnants of the capital city of the last great Hindu kingdom of Vijayanagara, from the 14th to the 16th century.

The site is huge: about 4,188 hectares (roughly 10,350 acres) in the basin of the Tungabhadra river, in what is now Vijayanagara district (formerly part of Ballari). Temples, halls, gateways and royal buildings sit among hills of piled granite boulders — a landscape that looks like nowhere else in India.

Hampi is far from India's big cities, and that is both the challenge and the reward. You need to plan the journey, but once you are there the pace is slow: early walks, temple courtyards, river views and sunsets over the boulders.

What to see in Hampi

Virupaksha Temple

Its entrance gateway rises about 50 m (160 ft) in nine tiers — the landmark you see from all around.

Vijaya Vittala Temple

Famous for the 56 musical pillars of its Ranga Mantapa and the Stone Chariot in the courtyard — the same chariot shown on India's 50-rupee note.

Lotus Mahal

An elegant pavilion with lotus-shaped arches, one of Hampi's best-known royal buildings.

Hemakuta and Matanga hills

Hemakuta Hill has groups of small temples above Virupaksha; Matanga Hill is the classic sunrise viewpoint — go with care, and no climbing in the dark.

The giant Ganeshas and Lakshmi Narasimha

Kadalekalu and Sasivekalu Ganesha, and the monolithic Lakshmi Narasimha, carved from single boulders.

Coracle rides

Karnataka Tourism lists round coracle boat rides on the river. Follow local guidance on river levels.

When to go: season and Hampi Utsav

October to February

The best months, as Karnataka Tourism advises. Hampi gets very hot outside October–February.

Hampi Utsav (Hampi festival)

In February 2026 the Government of Karnataka announced that the festival will move from February to 3, 4 and 5 November every year. Dates can change, so confirm the next edition before you plan around it.

Walk early and late

Start at sunrise, rest in the middle of the day, and go out again for the late-afternoon light. Carry water.

Health

Hampi gets very hot outside October–February; walk early and late, carry water, and consult your doctor or CDC / NHS Fit for Travel.

Tickets, timings and your visa

Monuments with a ticket charge foreign visitors a separate rate; buy at the counter or on the ASI site. Opening hours and fees change — confirm before travel (as of Nov 2026). For your visa: apply yourself only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware look-alike sites that charge extra. We are not a visa agency. In November 2026 the Government of India announced that the Tourist Visa is being renamed the Visitor Visa (PIB, 11 Nov 2026); until the official site changes, apply for the visa it shows there.
