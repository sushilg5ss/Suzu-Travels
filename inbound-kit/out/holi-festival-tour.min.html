When and where is the Holi festival in India in 2027?

Holi 2027 falls on Tuesday 23 March, with Holika Dahan bonfires on the evening of Monday 22 March (Government of India holiday list). The Braj region — Mathura, Vrindavan, Barsana and Nandgaon — celebrates longest: UP Tourism lists Rangotsav 2027 as 16–30 March. Where to see Holi: the Braj towns

Braj is the land of Krishna's childhood stories, and Holi here lasts far longer than one day. Each town has its own character:

Mathura

Believed to be Krishna's birthplace: the Shri Krishna Janmasthan complex, the Dwarkadhish temple (1814) and Vishram Ghat, the most important of its 25 ghats on the Yamuna. A practical base, with Mathura Junction railway station.

Vrindavan

A temple town a short drive from Mathura — Shri Banke Bihari, Radha Raman, ISKCON and Prem Mandir. UP Tourism lists Phoolon ki Holi (flower Holi), Widow Holi and Rangwali Holi in the streets of Braj.

Barsana and Nandgaon

Barsana, about 40 km (25 mi) from Mathura, is believed to be Radha's birthplace and has the Radha Rani temple. It is the home of Lathmar Holi; Incredible India calls Barsana's Lathmar Holi a huge attraction for visitors.

Gokul and Govardhan

Gokul (about 10 km / 6 mi from Mathura) and Govardhan (about 25 km / 16 mi) are part of the Rangotsav programme too — quieter places to add on a second Braj day.

Lathmar Holi, flower Holi and the other traditions

What you will see depends on the day and the town. These are the celebrations UP Tourism lists for Rangotsav:

Lathmar Holi

Held over two days in February or March. On day one the men of Nandgaon come to Barsana's Radha Rani temple, and the women of Barsana playfully strike them with bamboo sticks (lathis) while the men shield themselves; on day two Barsana's women go to Nandgaon. It is loud, crowded and joyful.

Phoolon ki Holi — flower Holi

In Vrindavan, Holi is also played with flower petals. UP Tourism lists it as part of Rangotsav.

Widow Holi

Celebrated in Vrindavan. Please watch with respect and do not photograph individuals without their consent.

Rangwali Holi

The day of coloured powder (gulal) and water in the streets of Braj — and on 23 March across India.

Is Holi safe for tourists? An honest answer

Holi is joyful, but the crowds are big and personal space disappears. The UK Foreign Office (FCDO, India travel advice updated 7 Oct 2026) says to take particular care during periods of national or religious significance, and notes that stampedes have occurred at some events with large crowds, such as religious gatherings.

Women travellers

The FCDO warns that female travellers often experience verbal and physical harassment by individuals or groups of men, and that there is a risk of sexual assault, including attacks on female foreign visitors in tourist areas. At Holi, go with a local guide, stay in a group, and consider watching Lathmar Holi from a rooftop or balcony — we arrange one where we can.

Food and drink

Drinks offered by strangers at Holi can be spiked or contain bhang (cannabis); the UK Foreign Office warns about spiked food and drink. Accept food and drink only from people you know.

Crowds

Lathmar Holi and the temple celebrations draw very large crowds. Keep your guide's number saved, agree a meeting point, and leave early if a crowd feels too tight.

Your phone and passport

Keep your passport in the hotel safe and your phone in a waterproof pouch. Colour and water get everywhere.

What to wear, what to bring

Holi colours stain. Pack for that:

Clothes

Old clothes and shoes you can throw away afterwards; long sleeves and trousers cover more skin. Many visitors wear white to show the colours — it will not stay white.

Eyes and skin

Colours can irritate skin and eyes — consult your doctor if you have allergies or sensitive skin; see CDC or NHS Fit for Travel. Sunglasses help. A common local tip is a layer of oil on hair and skin before you go out — a tip, not medical advice.

Weather

Braj is warm by March: Incredible India gives Barsana's February range as about 5–29 °C (41–85 °F), and days get warmer into March. Bring a hat and water.

Visa

Check whether your passport qualifies for India's e-Visa and apply yourself only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware look-alike sites that charge extra. We are not a visa agency; see our e-Visa guide.
