What “luxury” changes on a private India trip

Every Suzu trip is already private. An upgrade changes four things — you choose how far to take each one.

Hotels

Heritage hotels (old palaces, forts and havelis converted to hotels) or hotels in the 5-star deluxe category, as classified by India's Ministry of Tourism, where available on your route. You see the category for every night on the quote.

The car

An upgraded car class for the whole trip, with the same English-speaking driver from the airport to the airport.

Guiding

A private licensed guide at the big monuments — the Taj Mahal, Amber Fort, the old city of Delhi — booked for your timing, not a group's.

Pace

Fewer hours on the road each day, more nights in the places you like, early starts where they matter (the Taj opens 30 minutes before sunrise) and free afternoons.

What an upgrade can't change

Honest expectations make a better trip. Money buys comfort; it doesn't move the weather or the roads.

Roads and distances

Delhi to Agra is 204 km (127 miles) and Agra to Jaipur 232 km (144 miles). Hill roads in Himachal are slower still. We plan days around driving time, not the map.

Weather

Rajasthan reaches 43–46 °C (109–115 °F) in May. Delhi's monsoon normally arrives around 27 June and withdraws around 25 September.

Closures

The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays. Rohtang Pass above Manali is closed from late November to May; the Atal Tunnel (9.02 km / 5.6 miles) keeps the Lahaul side reachable.

Travel advice

Government advisories apply to every budget. Read your government's advice for India before you choose a route, and avoid driving at night or in fog.

What “heritage” and “5-star deluxe” mean in India

India's Ministry of Tourism classifies operational hotels from 1-Star to 5-Star and 5-Star Deluxe, and approves hotel projects in a separate Heritage category. So when a quote says “5-star deluxe” or “heritage”, those are official labels, not marketing words — ask any company which category each hotel holds.

In practice, a heritage hotel in Rajasthan is often an old palace, fort or haveli converted into a hotel. Rooms differ a lot inside one property, so we tell you the category and the room type for each night before you pay a deposit. We don't publish hotel names on this page; you get them on your quote.

Adding a luxury train

India's best-known luxury trains are IRCTC's Maharajas' Express (departures October to April) and Rajasthan's Palace on Wheels, which leaves Safdarjung station in New Delhi on Wednesdays for a week-long Rajasthan circuit. We don't sell luxury-train tickets; they are booked on the official sites or authorised IRCTC agents. We can plan your days before and after.

Questions travellers ask

What makes an India tour “luxury” with Suzu Travels?

Four things you choose: the hotel category (heritage or 5-star deluxe, where available), the car, a private licensed guide at the main monuments, and a slower pace with more nights. The trip is private either way — your own car, driver and plan.

How much does a luxury trip to India cost?

Our standard routes start from US$299 (Golden Triangle, 4 days) to US$1,099 (Golden Triangle + Himalayas, 12 days) per person. Upgraded hotels, car and guide are priced on request, because they depend on the category, season and nights. You get a US-dollar quote with a day-by-day plan.

Is it a private tour or a small group?

Private only. We don't run group departures: it is you, your own car and driver, and a plan made for your dates.

When is the best time for a luxury India trip?

October to March for Delhi, Agra and Rajasthan (November to March for Jodhpur, Udaipur and Jaisalmer). For Shimla and Manali, March to June and September to November.

Can I add a luxury train like the Maharajas' Express?

Yes, as part of your plan — but we don't sell luxury-train tickets. They are booked on the official sites or through authorised IRCTC agents. We plan your days before and after the train.

Can I add Kerala or South India?

Yes. We arrange Kerala and the south with trusted local partners and quote it together with the rest of your trip.

Is it safe, and do I need a visa?

Read your government's travel advice before you choose a route — the UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir (still current on 11 Nov 2026), and your travel insurance may not cover you against that advice. Apply for a visa only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware of look-alike sites. We are not a visa agency. For health, consult your doctor; see CDC or NHS Fit for Travel.

How do I pay from abroad?

A 20% deposit locks your dates and price, with a full refund if you cancel within 48 hours. Pay by card via Razorpay (international cards accepted) or PayPal; the balance is due before the trip.
