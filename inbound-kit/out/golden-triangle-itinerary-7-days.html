The route: Delhi, Agra, Fatehpur Sikri, Jaipur

The three cities sit in a triangle a few hours apart, so the trip is mostly sightseeing, not driving. Most people go anticlockwise — Delhi, Agra, Jaipur — but the order matters less than one rule: your Taj Mahal day must not be a Friday.

Delhi → Agra → Fatehpur Sikri (and the optional Abhaneri stepwell) → Jaipur → back to Delhi for your flight home. Distances are the official ones from the Taj Mahal site; drive times are our own experience on these roads.

The Golden Triangle in 7 days, day by day

Our sample for a comfortable first trip — two nights in each city, nothing rushed. It is where we start, not a fixed package: days move to fit your flights and the weekday rules below.

  1. DAY 1
    Arrive in Delhi

    Your driver meets you at the airport — name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip. Check in and rest; many international flights land late at night.

  2. DAY 2
    Delhi: old and new

    Old Delhi in the morning, New Delhi in the afternoon. If today is a Monday, the Red Fort, Akshardham and the Lotus Temple are closed — we swap in Humayun's Tomb and the Qutb Minar, which open every day. Our Delhi tour has the full list.

  3. DAY 3
    Delhi → Agra

    Drive to Agra on the expressway, about 3–4 hours. Agra Fort in the afternoon (open sunrise to sunset every day), then the Taj seen from across the river at Mehtab Bagh as the light goes.

  4. DAY 4
    Taj Mahal at sunrise → Fatehpur Sikri → Jaipur

    The Taj opens 30 minutes before sunrise — the calmest hour. After breakfast, Fatehpur Sikri, the red-sandstone city Akbar built in the second half of the 16th century and left after only about ten years (UNESCO, 1986). Arrive in Jaipur in the evening; the Abhaneri stepwell can be added on the way.

  5. DAY 5
    Jaipur: Amber Fort and the old city

    Amber Fort in the morning — commissioned by Maharaja Man Singh I in 1592, about 11 km (7 mi) from the centre, with the Sheesh Mahal, its palace of mirrors. Afternoon in the walled city founded in 1727: the City Palace and the Hawa Mahal.

  6. DAY 6
    Jaipur at your pace

    Bazaars, a slow lunch, or back to Amber for the evening light show (English at 19:30). Or ask us to add Ranthambore for a tiger safari instead.

  7. DAY 7
    Jaipur → Delhi, fly home

    Drive back to Delhi, about 5 hours, timed for an evening flight. Your driver takes you straight to the airport.

When to go — and what changes in the foggy months

October to March: the season

In our experience these are the best months for Delhi, Agra and Jaipur — the months most of our international guests choose.

December and January: fog

Thick morning fog is common on the North Indian plains. The UK Foreign Office (advice updated 7 October 2026) says: "Avoid travelling at night and in fog as accidents are common due to low visibility." We don't drive at night or in thick fog — in December and January we may start later and that is normal.

October to February: air quality

The UK Foreign Office notes that severe air pollution is a major health hazard, especially from October to February, with North Indian cities most affected. If you have asthma or a heart or lung condition, ask your doctor before a winter trip and see NHS Fit for Travel or the CDC.

Car or train?

A fast train runs from Delhi (Hazrat Nizamuddin) to Agra Cantt, 188 km (117 mi), and is a good choice for a day trip — check current timings on IRCTC. For the full triangle a private car is simpler: it waits at every stop, carries your luggage and takes in Fatehpur Sikri on the way.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

How many days do I need for the Golden Triangle?

Five days is the minimum that feels like a trip; seven is comfortable, with two nights in each city. Our shortest published tour is four days.

What order should I do the Golden Triangle in?

Either direction works. Most people go Delhi → Agra → Jaipur → Delhi; choose the direction that keeps your Agra day off a Friday.

Which day is the Taj Mahal closed?

Every Friday. On other days it opens 30 minutes before sunrise and closes 30 minutes before sunset. Foreign visitors buy a separate, higher ticket.

How long are the drives?

Delhi to Agra is 204 km (127 miles), about 3–4 hours on the expressway; Agra to Jaipur is 232 km (144 miles), about 4–5 hours with the stop at Fatehpur Sikri; Jaipur back to Delhi takes about 5 hours. The times are our own experience, not official figures.

Is it better to go by car or by train?

A fast train from Delhi to Agra is a good day-trip option — check current timings on IRCTC. For the whole triangle, a private car is easier: it waits at every stop and covers Fatehpur Sikri on the way. Your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip.

What is the best time for the Golden Triangle?

October to March, in our experience. In December and January expect morning fog — we don't drive at night or in thick fog, so some days start later — and the UK Foreign Office notes severe air pollution from October to February.

Can I add Rishikesh, Ranthambore or the Himalaya?

Yes. Our Golden Triangle with Rishikesh is 8 days from US$649 per person; Ranthambore fits after Jaipur; and Himachal Pradesh, where our office is, can follow for a two-week trip.
