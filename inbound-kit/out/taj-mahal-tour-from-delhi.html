How the same-day trip by car works

One car and one English-speaking driver for the day, from your Delhi hotel door and back.

Timing rules: Fridays, sunrise and winter fog

The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays. On other days it opens 30 minutes before sunrise and closes 30 minutes before sunset. A same-day sunrise visit means leaving Delhi in the dark — the UK Foreign Office advises visitors to avoid travelling at night and in fog, as accidents are common in low visibility. That is why, for the sunrise, we suggest one night in Agra instead of a 3 am departure, especially in the winter fog months.

Night viewing: the Taj opens on five nights a month — the full-moon night and two nights either side — from 8 pm to midnight, in batches of up to 50 people for 30 minutes. Tickets are sold online on the Archaeological Survey of India portal; you report at Shilpgram near the East Gate 30 minutes early. It is closed on Fridays and during Ramzan, and mobile phones are not allowed at night.

What you can — and can't — take into the Taj Mahal

Leave in the car

Food and snacks, tobacco, lighters, headphones, knives, mobile chargers and other electric goods (cameras are fine), tripods and drones. Your driver keeps the car near the parking, so nothing has to be thrown away.

Bring

Your passport or ID, your ticket, a phone on silent, a camera and water (allowed). Small bags only — large bags slow down the security check.

Inside

No photography inside the main mausoleum, no touching the marble, and quiet inside the tomb. The Taj Museum inside the complex is free, 10 am to 5 pm.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Can you see the Taj Mahal in a day trip from Delhi?

Yes. Agra is 204 km (127 mi) from Delhi by road, so a private-car day trip covers the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort and brings you back by night. It is a long day — about 408 km (254 mi) of road — so if you want the sunrise, stay one night in Agra.

Is it better to go from Delhi to Agra by car or by train?

The Gatimaan Express covers the 188 km (117 mi) from Hazrat Nizamuddin to Agra Cantonment in about 1 h 40 min, so the train saves road time; you still need a car in Agra. A private car is door to door and flexible. Check the train's current timings and run days on IRCTC.

Which day is the Taj Mahal closed?

Friday. The Taj Mahal is closed to general visitors every Friday; night viewing is closed on Fridays too. Agra Fort and Fatehpur Sikri stay open every day.

Is a sunrise visit worth it?

The gates open 30 minutes before sunrise, and many visitors love the early light and smaller crowds. From Delhi that means driving in the dark, which the UK Foreign Office advises against (night and fog). We suggest a night in Agra for the sunrise.

What can't I take into the Taj Mahal?

Food, tobacco, headphones, knives, mobile chargers and other electric goods except cameras, tripods and drones. Water bottles are allowed, and photography is not allowed inside the main mausoleum. Leave the rest in the car with your driver.

Can I see the Taj Mahal at night?

Yes, on five nights a month around the full moon, from 8 pm to midnight in small batches, with tickets sold online on the Archaeological Survey of India portal. It is closed on Fridays and during Ramzan, and phones are not allowed. We can add a night in Agra for it.

Do I need a guide at the Taj Mahal?

No, but a good one brings the history to life. Hire only approved guides who show an ID card — we can add a licensed guide to your quote so you don't have to choose one at the gate.
