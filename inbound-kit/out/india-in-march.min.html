The heat in March: go early, or go up

March can already bring heat waves in the plains (IMD warned of one on 8 March 2026). Drink water, plan sightseeing for mornings and evenings, and consult your doctor or the CDC / NHS Fit for Travel pages before you travel.

What happened in 2026

On 8 March 2026 the India Meteorological Department warned of severe heat-wave conditions over Himachal Pradesh and heat waves over West Rajasthan, Vidarbha, Saurashtra–Kutch, North Gujarat and Marathwada, with hot and humid weather on the Konkan and Gujarat coasts (as reported by All India Radio). Not every March is like that — but plan as if it could be.

Go early in the month

The first half of March is the more comfortable time for the Golden Triangle, Rajasthan and Khajuraho. If you can choose, put the plains at the start of your trip.

Or go up

The Himachal hills are mild in March and warmer in April. A common shape for a March trip: Delhi, Agra and Jaipur first, then the hills — see our two-week India itinerary.

How we plan the days

Early starts at the monuments, a long lunch break indoors, and the car does the moving in the afternoon. The Taj Mahal opens 30 minutes before sunrise.

Holi in March 2027

Holi 2027: Tuesday 23 March (Holika Dahan bonfires on the evening of Monday 22 March), per the Government of India's 2027 holiday list. Local celebrations in Braj start earlier — UP Tourism lists Rangotsav 2027 as 16–30 March. Our Holi festival guide has the full plan, and our Holi travel notes tell the story.

Where to see it

The Braj towns — Mathura, Vrindavan, Barsana and Nandgaon — are where Holi is celebrated longest; Barsana is about 40 km (25 miles) from Mathura. Hotels in Mathura and Vrindavan fill early, so book early.

Women travellers

The FCDO warns that female travellers often experience verbal and physical harassment by individuals or groups of men, and that there is a risk of sexual assault, including attacks on female foreign visitors in tourist areas. At Holi, go with a local guide, stay in a group, and consider watching Lathmar Holi from a rooftop or balcony — we arrange one where we can.

Food and drink

Drinks offered by strangers at Holi can be spiked or contain bhang (cannabis); the UK Foreign Office warns about spiked food and drink. Accept food and drink only from people you know.

Colours and your skin

Colours can irritate skin and eyes — consult your doctor if you have allergies or sensitive skin; see CDC or NHS Fit for Travel. Keep your passport in the hotel safe and your phone in a waterproof pouch.

Best places to visit in India in March

Our honest short list for a March trip, from the north we know best to the regions we arrange with trusted local teams:

The Golden Triangle + Holi

Delhi, Agra and Jaipur in the first part of the month, with Holi in Braj (Mathura and Vrindavan) added on the road between Delhi and Agra. Published prices below.

The Himachal hills

Spring in Shimla, Manali and Dharamshala: mild days, cold evenings, few crowds. This is our home — the Kalka–Shimla toy train is a good way up.

Haridwar and Rishikesh

The Ardh Kumbh runs in Haridwar from 14 January to 20 April 2027, with bathing days on 6 and 8 March. Rishikesh holds its International Yoga Festival each February–March; the 2027 dates are not announced yet.

Khajuraho and the tiger parks

Khajuraho's temples are at the end of their season; the English sound-and-light show runs from March. Central India's tiger reserves are open until June — see our tiger safari guide.

Kerala, for the wildlife

Hot and humid, but March–April gives better chances of seeing animals at the lake in Periyar. We arrange Kerala with local teams.

Snow, if you want it

Gulmarg's ski season usually runs mid-December to mid-April and Auli in Uttarakhand stays snowy till March. Snow is never guaranteed on a given date — see snow in India.

What to plan around in March

Kashmir in March

Srinagar is still cool and wet in March, and the Tulip Garden by Dal Lake usually opens during the month (it opened in March 2026; 2027 dates are not announced). The UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir except Jammu city, and the US State Department says 'Do not travel' to J&K except eastern Ladakh and Leh (advice checked 18 Nov 2026). Your travel insurance could be invalidated if you travel against FCDO advice. Check the latest before you book. See our Kashmir season guide.

Holiday weekends

Holi (23 March) and the Kumbh bathing days bring very large crowds and full trains. If your dates touch them, book flights, hotels and your car early.

Id-ul-Fitr and Easter

Id-ul-Fitr on Wednesday 10 March marks the end of Ramadan, Good Friday (26 March) is a national holiday and Easter Sunday is 28 March. If your dates touch these holidays, tell us and we plan around them.

What about April?

April is hot on the plains, so we plan April trips around the hills. Shimla averages 11.5–20.6 °C (53–69 °F) in April, Srinagar averages 14.3 °C (58 °F) and Gulmarg's ski season usually ends in mid-April. The calendar continues with Ugadi and Gudi Padava on Wednesday 7 April, Vaisakhi and the last Haridwar Amrit Snan on Wednesday 14 April and Ram Navami on Thursday 15 April 2027. FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Is March a good time to visit India?

Yes, especially early in the month. The Golden Triangle and Rajasthan are warm and bright, Holi falls on 23 March 2027 and the Himachal hills are mild. By late March the plains get hot, and the south is already hot and humid.

How hot is India in March?

It depends on the region. Shimla averages 6.9–15.7 °C (44–60 °F). The plains warm up through the month, and heat waves can come early: on 8 March 2026 the India Meteorological Department warned of heat waves in Rajasthan, Gujarat and even Himachal Pradesh.

When is Holi 2027 and where should I see it?

Holi is on Tuesday 23 March 2027, with the Holika Dahan bonfires on the evening of Monday 22 March. The Braj towns — Mathura, Vrindavan, Barsana, Nandgaon — celebrate longest; Uttar Pradesh Tourism lists Rangotsav 2027 as 16–30 March.

Is there still snow in India in March?

Sometimes. Shimla averages about 7 cm (3 in) of snow in March, Gulmarg's ski season usually runs to mid-April and Auli stays snowy till March. Snow is never guaranteed on a given date.

Is March good for Kerala and Goa?

Goa is at the end of its cooler season, so early March is better. Kerala Tourism says March–May is hot and humid, though March–April gives better chances of seeing animals at the lake in Periyar.

Is March good for a tiger safari?

Yes. Central India's tiger reserves are open from October to June, and March fits well with the Golden Triangle or Khajuraho. Book safari permits early.

What happens at Easter and Ramadan in March 2027?

Id-ul-Fitr, the end of Ramadan, is on Wednesday 10 March; Good Friday is 26 March and Easter Sunday is 28 March. Good Friday and Id-ul-Fitr are national holidays in India.

Is April still OK for India?

Yes, for the hills. Shimla averages 11.5–20.6 °C (53–69 °F) in April, while the plains get hot. We plan April trips with the Golden Triangle early and most of the time in the Himalaya.
