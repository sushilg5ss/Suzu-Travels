Two stops worth making between Agra and Jaipur

Fatehpur Sikri

Built by Emperor Akbar in the second half of the 16th century and the Mughal capital for only about ten years. A UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1986, with the great Jama Masjid inside — Agra's third UNESCO site after the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort.

Chand Baori, Abhaneri

A stepwell of 13 levels, about 20 m (66 ft) deep, in a village that dates from the 9th century. The geometric flights of steps — about 3,500 of them, per the Abhaneri Festival listing — make it one of the most striking stops of the day.

Harshat Mata temple

A 10th-century temple right beside the stepwell, so it adds only minutes to the stop. Dress modestly and take your shoes off where asked.

How foreigners book Indian trains from Agra to Jaipur

If the train suits you better, book it yourself online — it is straightforward once you know the rules.

  1. Use the Foreign Tourist QuotaIndian Railways keeps a Foreign Tourist Quota that international users can book online on IRCTC up to 365 days in advance. The general booking window was cut to 60 days from 1 November 2024; the 365-day limit for foreign tourists stayed.
  2. Have your passports readyYou need a valid passport number for each passenger.
  3. Pay by international cardThe quota accepts payment by international debit or credit card. Classes include EC, 1A, 2A, 3A, CC, SL and 2S, depending on the train.
  4. Buy only from official channelsBook only on the official IRCTC site or through your travel company — not with people at the station (UK FCDO warns about touts).
  5. Plan the station transfersAgra Cantt and Agra Fort stations are in the city, Jaipur Junction in Jaipur. We can meet you at either end with a car if you combine train and car.

Travelling safely between Agra and Jaipur

The UK Foreign Office advises: "Avoid travelling at night and in fog as accidents are common due to low visibility." It also warns: "Be cautious in tourist areas, where scammers, pickpockets and ticket touts target foreign nationals" and "avoid people at railway stations offering tickets and tours as you may be scammed." That is why we drive this road in daylight only, and why we suggest booking trains online rather than at the station.

App-based taxis like Uber and Ola are widely available, the FCDO notes, including in all airport terminal buildings — useful for short hops in Agra or Jaipur. For the long road between the two, a car booked through a registered company means you know in advance who is driving: your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip.

Jaipur to Agra: the same road in reverse

Going the other way works just as well. Leave Jaipur after breakfast, stop at Abhaneri first (it is near the Jaipur end), then Fatehpur Sikri, and reach Agra in the late afternoon. The same trains run in the other direction — check current timings on IRCTC.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

What's the best way to get from Agra to Jaipur?

By private car if you want to see Fatehpur Sikri and the Abhaneri stepwell on the way — it turns the transfer into a full sightseeing day. By train if you only want to arrive: several trains run daily from Agra Cantt and Agra Fort to Jaipur Junction. Check current trains and timings on IRCTC.

How far is Agra from Jaipur?

232 km (144 mi) by road, according to the official Taj Mahal website. With stops at Fatehpur Sikri and Abhaneri it is a full travel day; without stops, about half a day.

Can I see Fatehpur Sikri on the way to Jaipur?

Yes. Fatehpur Sikri is close to the Agra end of the road and open sunrise to sunset every day. Akbar's city has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1986; allow about two hours.

Is the Abhaneri stepwell worth the detour?

For most visitors, yes. The Chand Baori is a 13-level stepwell about 20 m (66 ft) deep, with the 10th-century Harshat Mata temple beside it. It is a short detour off the highway near the Jaipur end; allow about an hour.

How do foreigners book Indian train tickets?

Online on IRCTC under the Foreign Tourist Quota, up to 365 days in advance, with a valid passport number for each passenger and an international debit or credit card. Book only on the official site or through your travel company — not with people at the station.

Is it safe to take a taxi from Agra to Jaipur?

Travel in daylight: the UK Foreign Office advises avoiding night and fog driving, and warns about touts and scams in tourist areas. Book the car through a registered company. With us, your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip.

Can I go from Jaipur to Agra instead?

Yes — it is the same road in reverse. Stop at Abhaneri first, then Fatehpur Sikri, and reach Agra by late afternoon. Trains run in both directions; check timings on IRCTC.
