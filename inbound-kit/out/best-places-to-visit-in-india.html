What are the best places to visit in India?

For a first trip, the best places to visit in India are Delhi, Agra (Taj Mahal) and Jaipur, then one more region: the Himalayan hill towns of Shimla, Manali and Dharamshala, the Ganga at Rishikesh and Varanasi, Amritsar's Golden Temple, or Kerala and Goa in the south. October to March suits most of them; Ladakh is a summer trip. Allow 10–14 days for two regions.

India's main travel regions at a glance

India is a country of very different regions. The best trip is rarely the longest list — it is two regions that suit the month you travel, joined by a short flight or a good drive.

Twelve of the places in this guide, numbered on a simple map: the classic north (Delhi, Agra, Jaipur), Amritsar, the Himalaya (Dharamshala, Shimla, Manali, Leh), the Ganga (Rishikesh, Varanasi) and the coast (Goa, Kochi in Kerala). Distances inside India are large — plan one or two regions per trip, not all of them.

The classic north: Delhi, Agra, Jaipur, Varanasi and Amritsar

The places on almost every first-timer's list — and for good reason. Most are best from October to March.

Delhi

Your likely arrival city: Old Delhi's lanes, Mughal monuments and the start of the Golden Triangle. One or two days is enough for a first look.

Agra — the Taj Mahal

Agra has three UNESCO World Heritage sites: the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and Fatehpur Sikri. The Taj opens 30 minutes before sunrise, closes 30 minutes before sunset and is closed on Fridays. 204 km (127 mi) from Delhi — see our Taj Mahal tour from Delhi.

Jaipur — the Pink City

A UNESCO-listed walled city with forts, palaces and bazaars, 232 km (144 mi) from Agra. Delhi, Agra and Jaipur together make the Golden Triangle.

Varanasi

The evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat lasts about 45 minutes at dusk, with conch shells, bells and brass lamps. Dev Deepavali, 15 days after Diwali, fills the ghats with thousands of lamps. Fly in to Varanasi airport (VNS); allow two nights.

Amritsar — the Golden Temple

Sri Harmandir Sahib is the heart of Sikhism. Visitors can join the Langar, the free community meal, and see the Akal Takht inside the complex. One or two nights; it pairs well with Dharamshala.

The Himalaya and the Ganga: Rishikesh, Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala, Ladakh

This is our home region — Suzu Travels is based in Himachal Pradesh. Spring and autumn are the classic seasons; December to February brings snow to the hill towns.

Rishikesh

On the Ganga and often called the yoga capital of the world, with Haridwar 18 km (11 mi) away. An International Yoga Festival is held every February–March. See our Rishikesh yoga retreat guide.

Shimla

The old summer capital in the hills. The Kalka–Shimla railway is part of the UNESCO-listed Mountain Railways of India — a slow, scenic way up.

Manali

In the Kullu valley, 260 km (162 mi) from Shimla; Bhuntar airport is 50 km (31 mi) away. Solang valley is 13 km (8 mi) from town. Rohtang Pass (51 km / 32 mi) is closed by snow from late November until about May and needs a permit; the 9.02 km (5.6 mi) Atal Tunnel stays open all year to Lahaul.

Dharamshala and McLeod Ganj

McLeod Ganj (2,082 m / 6,831 ft) is the seat of the Tibetan Government in exile and the Dalai Lama's home since 1959 — Tsuglagkhang temple, Norbulingka and Kangra Fort are nearby. Kangra (Gaggal) airport is 14 km (9 mi) away; snow is common in winter.

Ladakh (Leh)

A high-altitude summer trip: Leh Palace, Shanti Stupa, Thiksey and Hemis monasteries, Pangong lake. The roads in from Manali and Srinagar are seasonal — we confirm them when we quote. The US State Department's 'Do not travel' advice for Jammu and Kashmir excludes eastern Ladakh and Leh.

The south and the coast: Kerala and Goa

A different India: backwaters, tea hills and beaches. Best from about November to February or March. Suzu arranges Kerala and Goa with local teams; our own office plans the rest of your trip.

Kerala backwaters

More than 900 km (560 mi) of waterways around Alappuzha (Alleppey), seen from a kettuvallam houseboat. Kerala Tourism names October–February as the best time for houseboats. See our 8-day Kerala tour.

Munnar and Fort Kochi

Munnar's tea hills sit at about 1,600 m (5,250 ft), planted since 1880. Fort Kochi's Chinese fishing nets have been on the waterfront for more than five centuries.

Goa

A coastline of about 125 km (78 mi) with at least 83 km (52 mi) of beaches, from Baga and Anjuna in the north to Palolem and Agonda in the south. The churches of Old Goa, including the Basilica of Bom Jesus and Se Cathedral, are a UNESCO World Heritage site. Foreign visitors mostly come in winter.

Hill railways: Darjeeling and Ooty

The Darjeeling Himalayan Railway (UNESCO, 1999) and the Nilgiri Mountain Railway to Ooty (added 2005) are the other two Mountain Railways of India — worth it if you are already in the east or the south.

How to combine places into one 10–14 day trip

Pick the month first, then the regions that are good in that month. Four combinations we plan often:

  1. Golden Triangle + Himalaya (Oct–Nov, Mar–Apr)Delhi, Agra and Jaipur, then Shimla, Manali and Dharamshala. Our day-by-day version is the India 2-week itinerary.
  2. Golden Triangle + the Ganga (Oct–Mar)Add Varanasi by air, or Rishikesh and Haridwar by road. Good for travellers who want India's spiritual side without long mountain drives.
  3. Golden Triangle + Kerala or Goa (Nov–Feb)The north's monuments, then a flight south for backwaters or beaches. North and south are very different trips — join them with a flight, not a drive.
  4. Himalaya + Amritsar (spring or autumn)Dharamshala and the Golden Temple sit close together; add Shimla and Manali for a hill-focused trip with fewer cities.

Practical basics before you choose

Visa

Check whether your passport qualifies for India's e-Visa and apply yourself only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware look-alike sites that charge extra. We are not a visa agency; see our e-Visa guide.

Getting around

Domestic flights for long hops (Delhi–Kerala, Delhi–Varanasi), trains on some routes, and a private car with an English-speaking driver for everything in between. Your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip.

Altitude and health

Leh, McLeod Ganj and Manali are at altitude — consult your doctor; see CDC or NHS Fit for Travel.

Monsoon and roads

The UK FCDO notes that the monsoon usually runs from June to October, with flash floods and landslides, and advises avoiding driving at night and in fog. We plan drives in daylight.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Where should I go in India for the first time?

Start with the Golden Triangle — Delhi, Agra for the Taj Mahal, and Jaipur — then add one region that suits your month: the Himalayan hill towns of Himachal, the Ganga at Rishikesh or Varanasi, Amritsar, or Kerala and Goa in the south.

What is the best month to visit India?

For the plains (Delhi, Agra, Jaipur, Varanasi) and for Kerala and Goa, October to March. The Himachal hills are best in spring and autumn, with snow from December to February. Ladakh is a summer trip. The monsoon usually runs from June to October.

How many days do I need in India?

Allow 10–14 days for two regions — for example the Golden Triangle plus the Himalaya, as in our India 2-week itinerary. A week covers the Golden Triangle with one extra stop; three weeks lets you add the south by air.

Is it better to visit North or South India?

They are different trips. The north has the Taj Mahal, Rajasthan's forts, the Ganga and the Himalaya; the south has Kerala's backwaters and tea hills and Goa's beaches. With two weeks, many travellers pick one; with three, combine them with a domestic flight.

Can I visit Kashmir?

Check your government's advice first. The UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir except Jammu city, and the US State Department says 'Do not travel' to J&K except eastern Ladakh and Leh (checked 19 Oct 2026). Ask us how the current advice affects your plans.

How do I get around India?

Domestic flights for long distances, trains on some routes, and a private car with a driver for road sections and day trips. Hill roads are slow, so each mountain drive usually takes a full day.

Do I need a visa for India?

Yes. Tourists from many countries, including the US, UK, Europe, Australia and Canada, can apply for an e-Visa online before travel — only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in. Beware look-alike sites that charge extra.
