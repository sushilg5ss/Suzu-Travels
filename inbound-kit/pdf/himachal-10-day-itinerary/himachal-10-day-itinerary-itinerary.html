Himachal Pradesh in 10 days: Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala, Dalhousie and Khajjiar

Toy train to Shimla, Manali, McLeod Ganj and Dalhousie — planned from our home state — a sample plan we build from; every night can move.

Day by day

DAY 1

Arrive in Delhi

Your driver meets you at Delhi airport — name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip. A quiet evening to get over the flight; the mountains start tomorrow.

DAY 2

Delhi → Kalka → toy train to Shimla

An early train or drive north to Kalka, then the narrow-gauge Kalka–Shimla railway: 96 km (60 mi) of curves, bridges and tunnels, part of UNESCO's "Mountain Railways of India" since 2008. Your car carries the luggage and meets you at Shimla. Prefer the road? Shimla is 370 km (230 mi) from Delhi — a full day. More in our toy train guide.

DAY 3

Shimla

The town on its ridge: the Mall and the Ridge on foot, Christ Church, and a walk up to the Jakhu temple if you have the legs for it. An easy day at altitude before the longer drives.

DAY 4

Shimla → Manali

260 km (162 mi) north through the Kullu valley — a full day with stops. We leave after breakfast and arrive before dark.

DAY 5

Manali: Solang and the Atal Tunnel

Solang valley is 13 km (8 mi) from Manali. When the road is open, the 9.02 km Atal Tunnel, at about 3,000 m (9,800 ft), takes you through to Sissu in Lahaul, which it keeps connected to Manali all year.

DAY 6

Manali: Old Manali, Hadimba, or Rohtang in season

A slower day: Old Manali, the Hadimba temple, cafés by the river. From May to November you can instead ride up to Rohtang Pass, 51 km (32 mi) away — it needs a vehicle permit and is closed by heavy snow from late November until May.

DAY 7

Manali → Dharamshala

A full-day drive west into the Kangra valley, into the foothills of the Dhauladhar range. Check in at McLeod Ganj, above Dharamshala.

DAY 8

McLeod Ganj

At 2,082 m (6,831 ft), McLeod Ganj is the seat of the Tibetan Government in exile and the Dalai Lama's home since 1959. The main temple, the monasteries, Bhagsu, and Tibetan food in the evening. Our McLeod Ganj guide has the details.

DAY 9

Dharamshala → Dalhousie and Khajjiar

A full-day drive to Dalhousie, the hill station founded by Lord Dalhousie in 1850 as a summer retreat, with its Mall Road and old churches. Then about 22–24 km to Khajjiar, a saucer-shaped meadow at about 1,980 m (6,500 ft) ringed by forest. If the drive runs long, we move Khajjiar to the morning of Day 10.

DAY 10

Down to the plains and home

To Pathankot, 87 km (54 mi), for the train to Delhi; or to Amritsar, 199 km (124 mi), to fly or to add the Golden Temple; or to Kangra (Gaggal) airport, 110 km (68 mi). We time the drive around your train or flight, with a margin.
