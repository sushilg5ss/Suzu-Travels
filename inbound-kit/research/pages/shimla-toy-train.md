- "864 bridges" / "103 tunnels" / "20 stations" (competitor/inconsistent figures) — use UNESCO: 102 tunnels, 988 bridges and viaducts, 917 curves, 96 km.
- "Built in the mid-19th century" (UNESCO news wording is wrong — line built 1899–1903; say 1903).
- That a steam train runs regularly; that Suzu can book FT quota; "Toy train included" without "(seasonal / where running)".

## FAQ candidates
1. Is the Shimla toy train worth it? (yes for a slow scenic day; tiring for some — car alternative 119 km from Chandigarh)
2. How long does it take? (about 4½–6 hours, 96 km)
3. How do I book from outside India? (IRCTC, FT quota if offered, international card, 365 days; else general quota 60 days)
4. Which train/coach should I choose? (Vistadome glass roof; Rail Motor heritage railcar; check current list)
5. Does it run in winter and snow? (yes as scheduled, but snow and weather can delay — Jan–Feb Shimla snow)
6. What about the monsoon? (suspensions possible, 2025 example)
7. How do I get to Kalka from Delhi? (train to Kalka/Chandigarh or private car; plan to arrive the night before for early trains)
8. Can I ride one way and drive the other? (yes — our car carries luggage and meets you at Shimla)
