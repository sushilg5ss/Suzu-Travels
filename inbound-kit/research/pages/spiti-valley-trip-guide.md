## Legal notes (verbatim)
- permits: "Foreign nationals need a Protected Area Permit for parts of the Kinnaur–Spiti route (for example Nako, Tabo and Dhankar). We arrange it with you through the official process; rules can change, so we confirm when we quote." No fee.
- health: "Spiti is high: much of the trip is above 3,000 m (about 10,000 ft) and the passes and lakes above 4,000 m (13,000 ft). Altitude affects people differently — consult your doctor before you book and see CDC or NHS Fit for Travel." No acclimatisation medical advice beyond "we plan the route to go up gradually".
- insurance: check your policy covers travel at this altitude (link india-travel-insurance).
- visa: "Apply yourself only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware look-alike sites that charge extra. We are not a visa agency."
- safety: FCDO night/fog + monsoon lines, dated; no "safe" promise.
