Basilica of Bom Jesus (1605, tomb of St Francis Xavier), Sé Cathedral (built 1562–1652 — begun 1562, finished 1619, altars 1652 per Incredible India se-cathedral page, checked 20 Oct 2026 by Reviewer), Church & Convent of St Francis of Assisi (1517), Church of St Augustine (1602, partly ruined), Chapel of St Catherine (1510), Our Lady of the Rosary (1549), St Cajetan (1661). | 4 | Palolem is in South Goa; airports Dabolim (GOI) and Manohar International (GOX); nearest station Madgaon Junction (MAO); sheltered bay, one of Goa's safest swimming spots; early-morning dolphin-watching trips; kayaking, paddleboarding; Cotigao Wildlife Sanctuary a short drive; best Nov–Feb | https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/goa/goa/palolem-beach | checked 20 Oct 2026 |
| 5 | Goa Tourism Department do's & don'ts: swim only where red-and-yellow flags show lifeguards on watch; do not enter water at red flags; water sports only between black-and-white chequered flags; no swimming after alcohol or after sunset; don't swim in unsafe areas or during the monsoon; don't jump/dive from rocks; keep away from turtle nesting areas; alcohol, drugs and smoking prohibited on beaches (fine/jail); nudity forbidden; use government-approved travel agents and guides (ask for ID); Dept of Tourism tel 0832-2494200 | https://goatourism.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/Tourism-Department-Updated-Do-and-Donts.pdf | document 2018, checked 20 Oct 2026 |
