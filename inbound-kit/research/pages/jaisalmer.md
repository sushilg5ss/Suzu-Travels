## Legal notes (verbatim lines the page must carry)
- Dunes rule (fact 21, dated): "Since October 2026 the forest department has not allowed visitors on the dunes inside Desert National Park after sunset. Many camps and sunset camel rides are outside the park and carry on as before — rules can change, so we confirm them for your dates." Never promise a night on the Sam dunes or stargazing "on the dunes".
- UNESCO: "Jaisalmer Fort is one of six forts inscribed together as the UNESCO World Heritage Site 'Hill Forts of Rajasthan' (2013)." Never "UNESCO World Heritage City".
- Border/safety: "Jaisalmer district borders Pakistan. The UK FCDO advises against all travel within 10 km of the India–Pakistan border, which is unmarked in places (advice as of Oct 2026 — check before you travel). Jaisalmer town and the Sam dunes are well away from that line, and our routes stay well away from it." (Builder: "well away" OK; no km figure for Sam→border — not sourced.)
- Desert camp/heat/health: "From April to June Jaisalmer can pass 45 °C (113 °F). Desert nights in December–January can be cold (about 5 °C / 41 °F). If you have a heart condition or are sensitive to heat, consult your doctor; see CDC or NHS Fit for Travel."
- Animal rides: "Camel rides are optional — if you'd rather not ride, a jeep or a walk on the dunes at sunset works too." (no welfare claims about operators)
- Temple etiquette: "The Jain temples are active places of worship: dress modestly, remove shoes, and follow the rules at the entrance (leather items may not be allowed — ask at the gate)."
