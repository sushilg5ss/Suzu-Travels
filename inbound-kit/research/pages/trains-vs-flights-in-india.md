## Legal notes
- No fares in ₹ or US$ for trains/flights ("check IRCTC / the airline"). Booking line fixed. Driver line: "your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip".
- Safety: quote FCDO with date, balanced ("generally safe").
- Visa: one line — e-Visa only at indianvisaonline.gov.in, beware look-alike sites (page links the e-Visa guide).

## Do not say
- Train names/timetables as current fact; airline names; flight durations; "fog season 15 Dec–15 Feb" (not sourced); that FT quota exists on every train with seats guaranteed; that agents (incl. Suzu) can book FT quota — FT says agents NOT allowed → Suzu helps you book yourself / meets you at the station; DigiYatra for foreigners (unverified).
