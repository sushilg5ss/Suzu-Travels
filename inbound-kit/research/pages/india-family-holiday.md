## Legal notes (verbatim lines the page must carry)
- visa: "Each child needs their own passport and their own visa. Apply only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — look-alike sites charge extra. We are not a visa agency."
- health: "We're a travel company, not doctors: see your GP or a travel clinic 6–8 weeks before you go, and read NHS Fit for Travel / TravelHealthPro or CDC for India." Quote FCDO air-pollution line with date; no medicine names or doses.
- safety: FCDO advice dated (15 Nov 2026 still current); balanced; no "100% safe for kids".
- consumer: from-prices per person, what they cover (hub wording); "child prices on request"; booking line fixed.
- car seats: "Tell us your children's ages — we'll confirm what child seats are available for your car" (no promise).
