## Do not say
- That UPI One World is available to every tourist at every airport — official text (Feb 2026) calls it a pilot at Delhi airport; say "offered at select points — check NPCI before you travel".
- 'CheqUPI' or any issuer/app name (NPCI names one; we don't endorse).
- Any ₹ figure (wallet caps, rupee import limit) — words only.
- Any wallet load limit, fee, exchange rate or ₹ amount until sourced (and never ₹ on the page — use "the RBI limit" wording or US$ only if the source gives it).
- Names of fintech apps/issuers as recommendations (product endorsement) — at most "issued by authorised banks/PPI issuers listed by NPCI".
- "UPI works everywhere" / "you don't need cash" — mountain areas and small vendors: keep "carry some cash".
- Anything about NRI international-number UPI as if tourists can use it.
