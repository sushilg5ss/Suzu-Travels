- permits: "Foreign nationals need a Protected Area Permit for parts of the Kinnaur–Spiti route (for example Nako, Tabo and Dhankar). We arrange it with you through the official process; rules can change, so we confirm when we quote." No fee in ₹.
- monsoon/safety: FCDO lines verbatim with date; no "safe" promises.
- health: altitude/cold → "consult your doctor; see CDC or NHS Fit for Travel".
- visa: official site indianvisaonline.gov.in + look-alike warning.

## Do not say
- exact 2027 opening dates for Rohtang, Kunzum, Atal Tunnel closures (temporary snow closures happen) — use "usually"
- guaranteed snow in Manali or Shimla; snowfall cm for Manali (no official source); Manali/Spiti temperatures (no official normals found)
- Kaza altitude; ILP fee; "peak Indian holiday season" (unsourced); hotel/camp names
- that the monsoon makes Himachal unsafe or safe
