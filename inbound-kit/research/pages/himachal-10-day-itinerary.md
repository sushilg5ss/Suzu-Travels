## Draft day plan (Suzu planning, km only where sourced)
D1 Delhi arrival · D2 Delhi → Kalka (train or car) → toy train to Shimla (96 km rail) or car (370 km from Delhi) · D3 Shimla (Mall, Jakhu, Christ Church — no new facts needed) · D4 Shimla → Manali (260 km, full day via Kullu valley) · D5 Manali (Old Manali, Hadimba, Solang 13 km; Atal Tunnel to Sissu when open) · D6 Manali (Rohtang only May–Nov, permit) or rest · D7 Manali → Dharamshala/McLeod Ganj (full-day drive) · D8 McLeod Ganj (Tsuglagkhang, Bhagsu, Norbulingka) · D9 → Dalhousie & Khajjiar (full-day drive) · D10 Dalhousie → Pathankot (87 km, train) or Amritsar (199 km) / fly from Kangra (110 km) or Amritsar → home or Golden Temple add-on (link amritsar-golden-temple-tour when live).
Winter version (Dec–Feb): same order, Rohtang replaced by Solang/Sissu, extra buffer day for snow; Dalhousie roads can close in heavy snow → "we re-route to Kangra valley".

## Legal notes
- Safety: "Mountain roads are narrow and weather changes fast; we don't drive at night in the hills" (Suzu practice, consistent with FCDO quote), monsoon line with FCDO date. No absolute safety promises.
- Health: altitude here is modest (≤ ~2,000 m for overnight stops; Rohtang ~3,980 m day visit only — **don't print Rohtang altitude unless sourced**) → "if you have heart or breathing conditions, consult your doctor; see CDC / NHS Fit for Travel".
