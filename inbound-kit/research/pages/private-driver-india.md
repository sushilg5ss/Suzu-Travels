## Legal notes (verbatim lines the page must carry)
- drivers: "Your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip." Never "verified / police-verified / background-checked".
- safety: "Several governments currently advise against travel to Jammu and Kashmir (UK FCDO, 7 Oct 2026; US State Department Level 4, except Ladakh). We plan Kashmir road trips only when your own government's advice and your travel insurance allow it — check both before you ask us." Road safety quoted from FCDO: avoid night driving and fog; livestock on roads.
- consumer: booking line fixed (gen_pages prints it); the inclusions card must say what the quote covers and excludes; no per-day US$ figure (not in prices.json) — "ask for a quote".
- health: none needed (no altitude medical claims; if Ladakh is mentioned, "ask your doctor about altitude").

## Do not say
- any per-day or per-km price in US$ or ₹; "cheapest"; "best price"
- "verified drivers", "police-verified", names of drivers, transporters or car owners
- "Rohtang open year-round" (it is the Atal Tunnel that is open year-round)
- "visa on arrival"; anything about visa fees (link the e-Visa page once it exists)
- that Kashmir is "safe" or "completely safe"; presenting a Kashmir route without the advisory line
- Taj Mahal ticket in rupees; "Taj is open every day" (closed Fridays)
- Delhi–Jaipur km (no official figure found this run — write "a half-day drive on the highway" or omit)

## FAQ candidates (6–8)
1. How much does a private driver in India cost? → depends on car, days, season; quote in US$; 20% deposit (no figure)
2. Is it safe to hire a car and driver in India? → FCDO points (well-maintained vehicle, no night driving) + how we work (same car/driver, details before the trip, office number)
3. Can I drive myself in India? → yes with an IDP + licence (FCDO), but most visitors choose a driver: left-hand traffic, livestock, night driving warnings
4. Is the driver a guide? → no; licensed monument guides added on request
5. Where does the driver stay overnight? → driver's own stay and meals are in the price; he stays near your hotel
6. Do I tip? → optional, at the end, if you were happy
7. Can we change the route on the way? → yes within the same region/days; extra nights or km quoted on the spot
8. Which day should we avoid for Agra? → Friday (Taj closed) — we plan the Agra day around it
9. Can we include Kashmir? → only when your government's advice and insurance allow; see advisory line
