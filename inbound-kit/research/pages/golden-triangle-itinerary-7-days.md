Suggested plans (Suzu's own wording, "in our experience"):
- **7 days:** D1 arrive Delhi (airport pickup, rest) · D2 Delhi (Old + New Delhi; if Monday, swap Red Fort for Humayun's Tomb/Qutb Minar) · D3 drive to Agra by expressway, Agra Fort, Taj view at sunset from across the river (Mehtab Bagh — name only, no hours) · D4 Taj Mahal at sunrise → Fatehpur Sikri → Jaipur (optional Abhaneri) · D5 Jaipur: Amber Fort morning, City Palace, Hawa Mahal · D6 Jaipur at your pace (bazaars, Amber light show) or Ranthambore add-on · D7 drive to Delhi, fly home (evening flight).
- **5 days:** D1 Delhi · D2 Delhi → Agra · D3 sunrise Taj → Fatehpur Sikri → Jaipur · D4 Jaipur · D5 Jaipur → Delhi airport.
- **Weekday rule:** the Taj day must not be a Friday — plan Agra on Sat–Thu; if you land on a Thursday, shift Delhi to the end.
