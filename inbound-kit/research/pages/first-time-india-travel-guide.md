## Legal notes (verbatim lines the page must carry)
- visa: "We are not a visa agency. Apply yourself, only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware look-alike sites that charge extra." + "Rules and fees change; confirm on the official site before you pay."
- safety: FCDO quotes (facts 4–8) with "UK FCDO, page updated 7 Oct 2026" and State Dept Level 2 line (fact 10) with "issued 16 Jun 2025, checked 23 Oct 2026 — check the current advice before you travel". Balanced: one "what this means in practice" line per quote, no scare-mongering, no "India is safe" promises.
- health: "Talk to your doctor or a travel clinic 6–8 weeks before you go; see CDC (US) or NHS Fit for Travel (UK)." — **"6–8 weeks" is not sourced → drop it unless the Builder sources it on CDC/NHS; otherwise just "well before you go".** No vaccine lists, no malaria advice, no water/food medical claims.
- drivers: "Your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip." Never "verified/background-checked".
- consumer: prices "on request" except Golden Triangle with 11412 wording; booking line fixed; registration line exact.
