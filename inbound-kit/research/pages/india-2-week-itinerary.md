Suggested day plan (Suzu's own, built from the facts above; Builder may adjust):
D1 arrive Delhi · D2 Delhi (Old + New Delhi) · D3 → Agra (204 km; Taj at sunrise D4 — never a Friday) · D4 Taj sunrise → Fatehpur Sikri → Jaipur (232 km) · D5 Jaipur · D6 Jaipur → Delhi (train or car) → evening Shatabdi/car to Chandigarh/Kalka (or overnight Delhi) · D7 Kalka–Shimla toy train or car to Shimla · D8 Shimla · D9 → Manali (260 km, full day) · D10 Manali (Solang; Atal Tunnel/Sissu if open) · D11 Manali (Old Manali, Naggar) · D12 → Dharamshala (full day) · D13 McLeod Ganj · D14 Kangra airport (14 km) flight or drive to Delhi; fly home.
- **10-day cut:** drop Dharamshala and one Manali day (D1–D10 then Manali→Chandigarh/Delhi).
- **3-week add:** Amritsar (Golden Temple) after Dharamshala, Rishikesh (link the yoga guide), or Ranthambore between Agra and Jaipur (only once the tiger-safari pack exists).
- **Season swap:** Dec–Feb snow can close roads above Manali and slow Shimla–Manali; Rohtang closed late Nov–May. In winter, keep Shimla + Dharamshala and present Kerala (14218) as the alternative second week.
- Rohtang is NOT in the core plan (permit + season); mention as "only May–Nov, permit needed".
