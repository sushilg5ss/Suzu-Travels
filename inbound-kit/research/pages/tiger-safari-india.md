## Legal notes (verbatim)
- visa: "Check whether your passport qualifies for India's e-Visa and apply yourself only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware look-alike sites that charge extra. We are not a visa agency."
- booking safety: "Safari permits are sold only through the state forest departments' official portals; many look-alike 'safari booking' websites resell them at a mark-up." Name the official portals only.
- wildlife: "Tiger sightings are never guaranteed — the forest is wild." Follow forest rules (stay in the vehicle, no loud noise, no feeding).
- health: "consult your doctor; see CDC or NHS Fit for Travel" (no advice).
- consumer: safaris "on request"; GT from US$299/449 with published coverage; booking line fixed; Central India parks "we arrange with local teams" — never name lodges, naturalists, operators.
