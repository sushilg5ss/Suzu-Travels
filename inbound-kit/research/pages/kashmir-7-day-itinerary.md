## Sample 7-day shape (built from facts; Builder may reword, not add facts)
D1 Fly into Srinagar, Dal Lake shikara, houseboat night · D2 Srinagar: Mughal gardens, old city · D3 Gulmarg day trip (Gondola; snow Dec–Mar) · D4 Srinagar → Pahalgam (about 95 km, ~2½ h) · D5 Pahalgam: Betaab Valley / Aru (local transport) · D6 Pahalgam → Srinagar; or Sonamarg day trip in summer (hotels summer-only; local taxi inside Sonamarg) · D7 Fly out. Winter version: swap Sonamarg for a second Gulmarg day.
