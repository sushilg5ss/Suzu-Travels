## Legal notes (verbatim)
- permits: "Foreign nationals need a Protected Area Permit for areas such as Nubra, Khardung La, Pangong and Tso Moriri. It is issued through the Leh district's official permit system; we arrange it with you. Rules can change, so we confirm when we quote." Citizens of the listed countries apply to MHA in New Delhi. No fee amounts.
- health: "Leh is at about 3,500 m (11,500 ft) and Khardung La at 5,602 m (18,379 ft). Altitude affects people differently — consult your doctor before you book and see CDC or NHS Fit for Travel." Quote the district's 48-hour rest rule as a local rule; NO medicine names or doses (the district/police pages mention Diamox — do not repeat).
- insurance: FCDO line on cover above 2,400 m + mountain rescue/helicopter.
- safety: "The UK FCDO advises against all travel to the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir, including Srinagar and Sonamarg; it does not name Ladakh. The US State Department rates Jammu and Kashmir 'Level 4: Do Not Travel' except visits to eastern Ladakh and Leh (both checked 15 Nov 2026 / 14 Nov 2026)." → we fly guests to Leh or drive the Manali road; the Srinagar–Kargil road passes through areas under the FCDO advice. "Check the latest advice before you book; your travel insurance could be invalidated if you travel against FCDO advice."
- visa: "Apply yourself only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware look-alike sites that charge extra. We are not a visa agency."
