The US State Department rates Jammu and Kashmir 'Level 4: Do Not Travel', except eastern Ladakh and Leh (checked 23 Oct 2026)." + insurance quote (fact 12). No "safe now", no "advisories are outdated".

- off-piste: we do not arrange off-piste/backcountry skiing.
