## Legal notes (verbatim lines the page must carry)
- health: "Ayurveda treatments are not a substitute for medical care from your own doctor. Consult your doctor before you book — especially if you are pregnant, take regular medication or have a health condition — and tell the centre's doctor about every medicine you take. See CDC or NHS Fit for Travel for India."
- take-home medicines: "Be careful with herbal medicines to take home: US and Australian health authorities have reported lead in some Ayurvedic products bought abroad (CDC 2012, NSW Health 2014). Show anything you plan to keep taking to your own doctor."
- visa: "Apply yourself at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware look-alike sites that charge extra. A short wellness stay alongside a holiday is usually planned on the e-Tourist visa; if treatment is the sole purpose of your trip, India has a separate e-Ayush visa for accredited centres. Check which applies to you on the official site. We are not a visa agency."
- safety: FCDO monsoon line dated (June–October, flash floods/landslides).
- consumer: treatment fees are set and paid to the centre; we take no commission on treatments; booking line fixed for Suzu's part (car, driver, hotels).
