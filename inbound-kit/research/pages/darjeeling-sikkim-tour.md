- Permit: "Foreign visitors need a Restricted Area Permit (RAP) to enter Sikkim, and a Protected Area Permit (PAP) for areas such as Tsomgo Lake, Nathu La and Yumthang. Since January 2026 these are issued online only through the Government of India's e-FRRO system — no paper permits at the border. Apply yourself on the official portal and check the current rules before you travel; we help you plan the dates but we do not issue permits."
