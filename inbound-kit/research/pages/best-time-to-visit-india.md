### Month-by-month (built from the facts above; Suzu planning where marked; Builder prints with "confirm before travel")
- Oct–Mar: plains (Delhi/Agra/Jaipur), Rajasthan (Jaisalmer Nov–Mar, Udaipur Sep–Mar), Varanasi, Kerala (Nov–Feb peak; houseboats Oct–Feb), Goa (Nov–Mar); Dec–Jan nights cold in the north (Jodhpur Jan min 5 °C, Barsana 4 °C); fog (FCDO night/fog driving line). Rishikesh Oct–Apr (our planning; yoga festival Feb–Mar).
- Dec–Feb: snow in Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala (Rohtang closed late Nov–May; Atal Tunnel open).
- Mar: Holi (23 Mar 2027). Apr–Jun: hot plains (Jodhpur May max 45 °C, Jaisalmer 46.2 °C) → Himachal hill towns (our planning: Mar–Jun, Sep–Nov) — and Ladakh from early summer (confirm).
- Jun–Sep: monsoon (Kerala from 1 Jun, Delhi from ~27 Jun; FCDO Jun–Oct); Kerala Aug Nehru Trophy; Ladakh summer (rain-shadow — NOT sourced, don't print); MP tiger cores closed Jul–Sep; Rishikesh rafting suspended Jul–Aug.
- Oct–Nov: monsoon withdraws (Delhi ~25 Sep, south ~15 Oct; Kerala NE monsoon rains Oct/Nov); Dev Deepavali; Corbett zones reopen 15 Oct / 15 Nov.
