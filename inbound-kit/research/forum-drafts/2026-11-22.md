## 1) Fodor's Travel Talk (Asia) — "Sikkim and Darjeeling reconsidered"
Question URL: https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/sikkim-and-darjeeling-reconsidered-826937/page2-printthread/
(Older thread — post only if replies are still accepted; otherwise reuse on a newer "Darjeeling/Sikkim permit" question on TripAdvisor India forum or r/IndiaTravel.)
Link to (optional, only if it helps): https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/darjeeling-sikkim-tour/

**Draft answer (≈175 words):**
Adding an update for anyone reading this now, because the Sikkim permit process changed in 2026. Since January 2026 the Sikkim government no longer issues paper permits to foreigners at the border — the Restricted Area Permit (to enter Sikkim) and the Protected Area Permit (for places like Tsomgo Lake, Nathu La and Yumthang) are handled online through the Government of India's e-FRRO system. News reports differ slightly on timing, so apply online once you are in India and before you reach Sikkim, and check the official portal (indianfrro.gov.in) for the current process rather than relying on older blog posts that still describe on-arrival permits.

On the route itself: most people fly into Bagdogra, spend two or three nights in Darjeeling (toy train joy ride, Tiger Hill at dawn, a tea garden), then continue to Gangtok. Six to seven days is comfortable. Avoid July–August if you can — landslides close mountain roads.

## 2) Rick Steves Travel Forum (Beyond Europe) — "India Holi 2026 tour recommendations"
Question URL: https://community.ricksteves.com/travel-forum/beyond-europe/india-holi-2026-tour-recommendations
(Thread was about Holi 2026; the answer is framed for readers planning Holi 2027. Post only if the thread is still open.)
Link to (optional): https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/holi-festival-tour/ (or /india-tours/india-in-march/ if the question is about weather)

**Draft answer (≈170 words):**
For anyone finding this thread while planning next year: Holi 2027 falls on Tuesday 23 March, with the Holika Dahan bonfires the evening before (Government of India holiday list). The Braj area around Mathura and Vrindavan celebrates for about two weeks before that, so if you want the famous Lathmar Holi in Barsana, check the local dates — they come earlier than the main day.

You don't need a group tour to enjoy it. Many travellers base themselves in Agra or Mathura, join the colour play on the morning of Holi, and keep the afternoon free (things go quiet). Wear old clothes, oil your hair and skin beforehand, keep your phone in a zip bag, and if you are travelling solo, a hotel-hosted celebration is a calmer option — read the UK FCDO advice before you go.

March is also the last comfortable month in the plains, so do Delhi/Agra/Jaipur early and go up to the hills later.
