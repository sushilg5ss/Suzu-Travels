## 1. TripAdvisor India forum — "North India 10-14 day itinerary advice"
Question URL: https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g293860-i511-k15007131-North_India_10_14_day_itinerary_advice-India.html
(Thread date not visible to the agent — if it is closed, reuse on any fresh "10–14 days in North India, first time" thread on TripAdvisor India or r/IndiaTravel.)
Our page (link only if it helps): https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/north-india-tour/

Draft answer (≈180 words):

> Disclosure: I work with a small travel company in Himachal, so take this as one local view.
>
> With 10–14 days I'd do the Golden Triangle (Delhi, Agra, Jaipur) and add ONE more region rather than three. Good add-ons:
> - **Rishikesh/Haridwar** — easiest, about a day's drive from Delhi; Ganga Aarti in the evening.
> - **Amritsar + Dharamshala** — Golden Temple, then the mountains; Amritsar is a short flight or a fast train from Delhi.
> - **Shimla/Manali** — best if you want Himalayan scenery; roads are slow, so give it 4–5 days.
>
> Practical bits: the Taj is closed on Fridays, so plan Agra around that. If you use a car and driver, ask for the driver's name, photo and car number before the trip, and pay only a deposit to a company with a real office address and a state registration you can check. Kashmir: read the UK FCDO and US State Department advice first — both currently warn against travel there.
>
> We wrote up the five combinations with day counts here if useful: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/north-india-tour/

## 2. snowHeads ski forum — "Gulmarg, Kashmir - Advice Needed"
Question URL: https://snowheads.com/ski-forum/viewtopic.php?t=127374
(Older thread — post only if replies are still open; otherwise reuse on a fresh snowHeads / TripAdvisor India thread asking "Is Gulmarg OK for skiing this winter?".)
Our page: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/gulmarg-ski-holiday/ (or no link — the FCDO link is the important one)

Draft answer (≈170 words):

> Disclosure: I work with a small travel company in Himachal (we're not in Kashmir), so no axe to grind on Gulmarg.
>
> The snow and the gondola are real — top station at roughly 3,980 m, season usually mid-December to mid-April. But before anything else, check the government advice, because it changes what you can insure:
> - UK FCDO (last updated 7 Oct 2026) advises against **all** travel to Jammu and Kashmir, naming Gulmarg, Srinagar and Pahalgam; only Jammu city by air is excepted.
> - The US State Department rates J&K "Do not travel".
>
> Many travel and ski policies won't cover a trip against FCDO advice — ask your insurer in writing before you book, and make sure off-piste/avalanche rescue is covered too. Gulmarg is serious avalanche terrain; go with a qualified local guide and proper kit.
>
> FCDO page: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/india — we keep a dated summary here: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/gulmarg-ski-holiday/
