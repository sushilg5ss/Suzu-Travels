## 1. Relaxed yoga retreat in Rishikesh for older / first-time visitors
**Question type (real example):** TripAdvisor Rishikesh Forum — "Rishikesh Yoga retreat and little sight seeing" (couple in their 70s, beginner/mid-level yoga, want relaxed + clean, ~2 yrs old, closed)
https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g580106-i9979-k14855233-Rishikesh_Yoga_retreat_and_little_sight_seeing-Rishikesh_Dehradun_District_Uttarakhand.html

**Draft answer (≈175 words):**
> Full disclosure: I work with a small travel company in Himachal, but we don't sell yoga retreats, so no stake in which school you pick.
>
> First decide what you actually want: a relaxed few days with a daily class, a structured ashram programme, or a 200-hour teacher training. They're very different. Ashram programmes often mean early wake-ups, set meals and house rules — check the daily schedule before paying. If comfort and hygiene matter most, many people stay in a good hotel near Ram Jhula / Laxman Jhula and join drop-in classes instead.
>
> If a school says it's "Yoga Alliance registered", look it up yourself on yogaalliance.org; India's Yoga Certification Board (Ministry of Ayush) is the Indian equivalent. Ask about cancellation terms in writing.
>
> Visa: a short yoga programme is an allowed purpose on the e-Tourist visa — apply only at indianvisaonline.gov.in, not look-alike sites.
>
> Getting there: Dehradun (Jolly Grant) airport is the closest; from Delhi it's a long road day.
>
> We wrote a longer, non-salesy checklist here if useful: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/rishikesh-yoga-retreat-guide/

## 2. Two weeks in India, first time, relaxed pace
**Question type (real example):** TripAdvisor India Forum — "Golden Triangle in a relaxed way" (couple in their 70s, first visit, October, ~2 weeks, ~2 yrs old)
https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g293860-i511-k14731471-Golden_Triangle_in_a_relaxed_way-India.html
(similar, also closed: "Advice please where next" — https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g293860-i511-k15294357-Advice_please_where_next-India.html — visitor tired of city crowds after Delhi/Agra/Jaipur)

**Draft answer (≈170 words):**
> I work with a small Himachal-based travel company, so take this with that in mind — but here's how I'd pace two weeks.
>
> The Golden Triangle (Delhi, Agra, Jaipur) fits comfortably in 5–6 days with a private car: Delhi to Agra is about 204 km, Agra to Jaipur about 232 km. Two practical traps: the Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays, and fog in winter makes early-morning and night driving slow — plan daylight drives.
>
> After a week of busy cities, many people want quiet. Two good directions: south-west into Udaipur and smaller Rajasthan towns, or north into the Himalayan foothills — Shimla, the Kullu valley at Manali, and McLeod Ganj above Dharamshala, where the pace is slower and the air is cleaner. Hill roads are slow, so don't cram: 2–3 nights per stop.
>
> October–November and March–April are the most comfortable months for this route.
>
> If it helps, here's a day-by-day version with real distances: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/india-2-week-itinerary/
