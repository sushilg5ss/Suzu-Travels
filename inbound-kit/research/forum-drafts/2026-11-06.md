## 1. TripAdvisor Shimla forum — "No train tickets from Kalka to Shimla"
Question URL: https://www.tripadvisor.in/ShowTopic-g304552-i10145-k15590171-No_train_tickets_from_Kalka_to_Shimla-Shimla_Shimla_District_Himachal_Pradesh.html
Our page (link only if it helps): https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/shimla-toy-train/

Draft answer (≈175 words):

> Disclosure: I work with a small travel company in Himachal, so we follow this line closely.
>
> You are not doing anything wrong — there are simply almost no Kalka departures right now. After monsoon damage near Bridge No. 57 (between Taksal and Gumma), the railway cancelled or cut short 10 toy-train services, and The Tribune (19 Oct 2026) reported the order extended to 31 December 2026. At the moment the Kalka end is shut and only a Dharampur–Shimla service runs, so you won't find Kalka–Shimla seats on IRCTC.
>
> What people are doing instead:
> - Take a taxi or bus from Kalka/Chandigarh up to Dharampur and catch the train from there, or
> - Skip the train this time and drive to Shimla, then ride a short stretch later in the trip if services return.
>
> Check NTES or call 139 a day or two before you travel — the dates have moved more than once this year. A longer write-up on booking the toy train (incl. the Foreign Tourist quota) is here if useful: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/shimla-toy-train/

## 2. FlyerTalk India forum — "Palace on Wheels"
Question URL: https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/india/942993-palace-wheels.html
(Older thread — post only if replies are still open; otherwise reuse on a fresh TripAdvisor India / r/IndiaTravel thread comparing the Palace on Wheels and the Maharajas' Express.)
Our page: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/luxury-trains-india/

Draft answer (≈165 words):

> Disclosure: I work with a small Himachal-based travel company (we don't sell these trains ourselves).
>
> The two names people compare are run by different bodies: the Maharajas' Express by IRCTC, and the Palace on Wheels with Rajasthan Tourism. Both run in the cooler months (roughly October to April) and focus on Rajasthan plus Agra; if you want the south, look at the Golden Chariot (Karnataka), and for Maharashtra the Deccan Odyssey.
>
> Two practical tips:
> - Book on the official sites (or an agent they list). Search any of these train names and you'll find lots of look-alike websites; some are real agents, some just resell at a mark-up.
> - Read the payment rules before you pay: the official sites take an advance online and the balance about a month before departure, and cancellation charges rise steeply close to the date.
>
> If you only want one night of the experience, check whether a shorter journey runs on your dates. We wrote a side-by-side comparison here, linked to the official pages: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/luxury-trains-india/
