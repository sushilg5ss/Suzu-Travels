## 1. Fodor's Travel Talk — "Is Khajuraho worth seeing if limited time?"
Question URL: https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/is-khajuraho-worth-seeing-if-limited-time-886485-print/
Our page (link only if it helps): https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/khajuraho-temples/

> Depends on what "limited" means. With under 10 days in the north, I'd usually skip it — Delhi, Agra, Jaipur and maybe Varanasi already fill that. With 12+ days, or if you're already doing Agra → Varanasi, Khajuraho fits naturally in between and breaks up a long journey.
>
> What you get: Hindu and Jain temples built by the Chandella dynasty around 950–1050 (UNESCO World Heritage since 1986), in western, eastern and southern groups — far more than the famous erotic panels. Most people focus on the Western Group; the evening sound-and-light show there is optional.
>
> Practical bits: Orchha makes a good overnight stop on the way from Agra, Panna Tiger Reserve is close if you want a safari (open roughly 15 Oct–15 Jun; tigers never guaranteed), and the Khajuraho Dance Festival runs 20–26 Feb 2027 if your dates line up. Check entry fees and show times on the ASI / MP Tourism sites — they change.
>
> Disclosure: I work with a small Himachal-based travel company; we wrote up a longer Khajuraho guide here if useful: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/khajuraho-temples/

## 2. Fodor's Travel Talk — "Khajuraho transportation options"
Question URL: https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/khajuraho-transportation-options-822173/
Our page: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/khajuraho-temples/ (getting-there table)

> The usual ways in are: (1) fly — the airport is about 2 km from town, but flights (e.g. from Delhi or Varanasi) run seasonally, so check current flights before building the trip around them; (2) train — Khajuraho station is about 5 km from town, or come via Jhansi and drive (check IRCTC for current trains); (3) a private car along the Agra → Orchha → Khajuraho → Varanasi line, which is what most people with 2+ weeks end up doing because Orchha is worth a night and the drive is otherwise awkward to piece together.
>
> If you go by road, split it: Agra–Orchha one day, Orchha–Khajuraho the next morning, so you have the afternoon at the Western Group. Onward to Varanasi is a long day by road, so check train or flight options from there too.
>
> Disclosure: I work with a small travel company in Himachal that arranges private cars on this route; our route table is here if it helps: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/khajuraho-temples/ — but the train + taxi option works fine if you're comfortable sorting it yourself.
