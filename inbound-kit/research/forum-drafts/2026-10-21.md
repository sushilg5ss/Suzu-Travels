## 1. "Ranthambore or Corbett for a tiger safari on our first India trip?"
- Where: next r/IndiaTravel / TripAdvisor Sawai Madhopur thread asking this (very common Oct–Mar).
- Link (only if it helps): https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/tiger-safari-india/

> If you're already doing Delhi–Agra–Jaipur, Ranthambore is the easy add: it's near Sawai Madhopur, a train or road trip from
> Jaipur, so it slots into a Golden Triangle with two nights. Corbett is the better pick if you're heading north anyway (Rishikesh,
> the hills) — it's roughly 240 km from Delhi by road.
>
> Two things people get wrong. First, seasons: at Corbett the zones open in stages — Bijrani, Sonanadi and Domunda from mid-October,
> Dhikala (the famous one) only from 15 November, Jhirna all year. Check the official calendar before you fix dates. Second, permits:
> buy them only on the official forest-department portals (Rajasthan Forest Department for Ranthambore, corbettonline.uk.gov.in for
> Corbett). There are lots of look-alike "safari booking" sites that resell at a mark-up. Permits are usually non-refundable.
>
> And nobody can promise a tiger — book 3–4 drives over two nights rather than one, and enjoy the rest (birds, deer, the fort at
> Ranthambore). I work with a small Himachal travel company, so happy to answer anything else.

## 2. "Is Jaisalmer worth the long drive on a 10–12 day Rajasthan trip?"
- Where: candidate https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/jaiselmer-577379/ (date unconfirmed) or the next r/IndiaTravel thread.
- Link (only if it helps): https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/rajasthan-tour/

> Short answer: yes if you have 10+ days and want the desert; skip it if you have a week. The drives are the catch — Jodhpur →
> Jaisalmer is a full day and Jaisalmer → Udaipur is the longest day on the road, so it only works with at least two nights in
> Jaisalmer itself. Rushing it in one night means you mostly see the car.
>
> What you get for it: a fort that people still live inside (one of Rajasthan's UNESCO Hill Forts), the carved havelis, and a night
> in the dunes with a properly dark sky. A rough shape that works: Jaipur 2 nights → Jodhpur 2 → Jaisalmer 2 → Udaipur 3, best
> November to March (it's very hot from April). If time is tight, do Jaipur–Jodhpur–Udaipur and keep the desert for another trip.
>
> One driver for the whole loop makes the long days much easier than buses. Disclosure: I work with a small Himachal-based travel
> company that plans these trips — happy to share more if useful.

(Facts: research/pages/rajasthan-tour.md — UNESCO Hill Forts 2013, Incredible India best months.)
