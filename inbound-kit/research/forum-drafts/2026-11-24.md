## 1. Fodor's Travel Talk (Asia) — "Help with Varanasi to Agra"
Thread: https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/help-with-varanasi-to-agra-666469/
Links to: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/orchha/

> I work with a small travel company in Himachal, so take this with that in mind. If you have two or three extra days, don't just take the overnight train between Agra and Varanasi: break the trip in Orchha and Khajuraho. Orchha is a quiet town on the Betwa river with Bundela palaces (Raja Mahal, Jahangir Mahal), the Ram Raja temple, and a row of royal cenotaphs (chhatris) on the riverbank. It's about 16–17 km from Jhansi station, so a train from Agra to Jhansi and then a short taxi ride works well. Khajuraho is about 172 km further by road (MP Tourism's figure), and Khajuraho has flights onward to Varanasi.
>
> One or two nights in Orchha is plenty. October to March is the comfortable season; April–June regularly passes 40 °C (104 °F). It's on UNESCO's Tentative List, not a World Heritage Site (yet), so expect fewer crowds than Agra.
>
> We wrote up the routing here if useful: [Orchha travel guide](https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/orchha/).

## 2. Fodor's Travel Talk (Asia) — "Delhi to Varanasi transport questions"
Thread: https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/delhi-to-varanasi-transport-questions-817583/
Links to: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/trains-vs-flights-in-india/

> Disclosure: I work with a small travel company in Himachal. A few things foreign visitors often don't know about Delhi–Varanasi by train:
>
> - Foreign passport holders can book the Foreign Tourist Quota on IRCTC up to 365 days ahead (the normal window is 60 days). The quota fare is about 1.5× the normal fare, and cancellation rules are stricter, so book once your dates are fixed.
> - Carry the original passport you booked with — it's your ID on board.
> - December–January fog in North India regularly delays overnight trains by hours, and it can affect flights too. In winter I'd fly this leg if your time is tight, and keep a buffer day before any international flight.
> - The overnight train in 2A/3A is still a great experience if you're flexible.
>
> More detail on classes, booking and the fog season: [Indian trains or flights?](https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/trains-vs-flights-in-india/) — and always check irctc.co.in for the current rules before you book.
