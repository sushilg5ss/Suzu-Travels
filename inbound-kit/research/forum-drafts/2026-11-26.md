## 1. TripAdvisor India forum (Leh) — "Leh basic queries"
Thread: https://www.tripadvisor.in/ShowTopic-g297625-i10473-k9561626-Leh_basic_queries-Leh_Leh_District_Ladakh.html
Links to: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/ladakh-trip-guide/

> Disclosure: I work with a small travel company in Himachal, on the Manali side of the Leh road. A few basics that trip up first-timers: if you fly into Leh (about 3,500 m / 11,500 ft), the district administration asks visitors to rest for 48 hours before going higher — Khardung La is 5,602 m (18,379 ft) — so keep the first two days for Leh town only. Foreign passport holders need a Protected Area Permit for Nubra, Pangong and Tso Moriri; it's arranged through the official Leh permit portal or a local agent. There has been talk since August 2026 of a single permit for most of Ladakh, but it isn't in force yet, so plan on the current rules. The roads in (Manali–Leh 473 km, Srinagar–Leh 434 km) are seasonal, roughly May/June to September/October for the Manali side. Altitude is personal — speak to your doctor before you go.
>
> We wrote it all up here if useful: [Ladakh trip guide for foreign travellers](https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/ladakh-trip-guide/).

## 2. Fodor's Travel Talk (Asia) — "Wagha Border Ceremony"
Thread: https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/wagha-border-ceremony-1110226/
Links to: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/amritsar-golden-temple-tour/

> I work with a small travel company in Himachal, so weigh this accordingly. The Beating Retreat at Attari–Wagah starts in the late afternoon — roughly 4:15 pm in winter and 5:15 pm in summer, moving with sunset — and the crowds are big, so go early and carry as little as possible. The border is about 32 km from Amritsar's bus stand, so allow for traffic on the way back into town.
>
> It pairs well with an early-morning visit to the Golden Temple, which is usually quieter then. Remove your shoes and cover your head before entering (scarves are available), and do eat at the free langar — it's one of the most memorable parts of the visit. Two days in Amritsar is comfortable for both plus Jallianwala Bagh.
>
> Our notes on doing it as a private trip (and continuing up to Dharamshala) are here: [Amritsar Golden Temple tour](https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/amritsar-golden-temple-tour/) — but it's easy to do on your own too.
