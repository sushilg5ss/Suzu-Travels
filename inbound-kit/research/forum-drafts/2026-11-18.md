## 1. Rick Steves Travel Forum — "Getting from Goa to Hampi"
URL: https://community.ricksteves.com/travel-forum/beyond-europe/getting-from-goa-to-hampi
Link to use (only if it helps): https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/hampi/

> Disclosure: I work with a small Himachal-based travel company, so take this as one opinion.
>
> Most people do Goa to Hampi either by overnight/early train to Hosapete Junction (the nearest station, about 13 km / 8 miles from the ruins, then an auto-rickshaw or taxi) or by road with a hired car and driver. Check current trains on IRCTC close to your date, as timetables change.
>
> Give it three days if you can — that's also what Karnataka Tourism suggests. Day one around the Virupaksha Temple and Hemakuta Hill, day two for the Vittala Temple (the Stone Chariot and the "musical" pillars) and the Royal Centre, day three for Matanga Hill at sunrise or a coracle ride, depending on river levels.
>
> October to February is the comfortable window; outside it, it gets very hot, so walk early and late and carry water. One more thing: Hampi Utsav is now held every year on 3–5 November, so book rooms early if you're there then.
>
> I put the sources together here if useful: [Hampi guide](https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/hampi/)

## 2. Tripoto Community — "How to reach Hampi and how many days are enough to explore Hampi"
URL: https://www.tripoto.com/community/how-to-reach-hampi-and-how-days-are-enough-to-explore-hampi
Link to use: none needed — answer stands alone (add the guide link only if the asker replies with follow-ups).

> Disclosure first: I work with a small travel company in Himachal.
>
> Hampi is about 340 km (211 miles) from Bengaluru — a full day by road, or the easier option for many people is a train to Hosapete Junction, about 13 km from Hampi, then a short auto or taxi ride. From Goa it's road or rail to Hosapete as well.
>
> How many days: two is rushed, three is right. The site is spread across boulder hills and the big temples are kilometres apart — Virupaksha (its gateway tower is about 50 m tall) near the bazaar, Vittala with the Stone Chariot further out along the river. A bicycle, scooter or hired car helps a lot.
>
> Best months are October to February. It's a UNESCO World Heritage Site (since 1986), and monuments that charge entry have a separate rate for foreign visitors — check at the counter or the ASI site. Start early to beat the heat, and don't climb the hills in the dark.
