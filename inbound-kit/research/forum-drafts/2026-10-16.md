## Draft 1 — TripAdvisor, New Delhi forum: "Looking for Recommended Private Driver"
- Thread: https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g304551-i3482-k15346276-Looking_for_Recommended_Private_Driver-New_Delhi_National_Capital_Territory_of_Delhi.html (open, ~51 replies; asker from the UK wants an English-speaking private driver for Delhi–Agra–Jaipur–Delhi, 4 nights, and explicitly *not* a tour company)
- Our page that answers it: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/ (hub; private-driver page not yet live — update the link to `/india-tours/private-driver-india/` once it is)
- **Link in the answer? No.** She asked for *no* companies; a link would read as a pitch. Disclose and give the checklist only.

> Disclosure first: I work with a small travel company in Himachal, so take my view with that in mind — I'm not going to pitch you anyone.
>
> A few practical things nobody tells you about hiring a driver for Delhi–Agra–Jaipur:
>
> 1. Ask for the car's **commercial (yellow) number plate** and the driver's name in writing before you pay anything. Private white-plate cars can't legally carry paying passengers between states and get stopped at the Rajasthan/UP borders.
> 2. Confirm in writing what the quote includes: fuel, tolls, parking, interstate taxes and the driver's own food and stay. Most arguments on the road are about one of these being "extra".
> 3. Pay a **small deposit only**, by card or PayPal, and the rest at the end — not cash up front. A driver or agency that insists on full payment before you arrive is a red flag.
> 4. The Taj Mahal is **closed on Fridays** — fix your Agra day around that, not around the driver's schedule.
> 5. Expect roughly 3–4 hours Delhi–Agra on the Yamuna Expressway and about 5 hours Agra–Jaipur with a stop at Fatehpur Sikri or the Abhaneri step-well. Drivers drive; they are not guides — hire a government-licensed guide at each monument if you want the history.
>
> Whoever you book, the test is simple: do they answer your questions in writing, and do they tell you who exactly is driving before the trip? If yes, you'll be fine.

## Draft 2 — Fodor's Asia forum: "Golden Triangle – To Hire a Car and Driver or Not???"
- Thread: https://www.fodors.com/community/11233156-post3/ (open; asker has ~10 days, asks whether to prepay one car + driver for the whole trip or hire city by city)
- Our page that answers it: https://suzutravels.com/golden-triangle-tour/ (private Golden Triangle, same car and driver throughout) — link only in the last line, clearly labelled.

> I work with a small Himachal travel company, so I see both setups from the inside. Honest comparison:
>
> **One car and driver for the whole loop** wins when you are 2–4 people, want to stop at Fatehpur Sikri, Abhaneri or Chand Baori on the way, or carry luggage you don't want to re-check every day. You also get the same person who knows your pace. Downside: the car sits idle on your sightseeing days in Jaipur and you still pay for it.
>
> **City by city** wins for solo travellers on a budget: Gatimaan/Shatabdi trains do Delhi–Agra in about 2 hours, far faster than the road, and a local taxi for the day in Jaipur is cheap. Downside: four separate bookings, and the Agra–Jaipur stretch has no great train — most people end up hiring a car for that leg anyway.
>
> Prepaying: pay a deposit, not the whole amount, and get the inclusions (fuel, tolls, parking, state taxes, driver's stay) in writing.
>
> Many first-timers do the hybrid: train Delhi→Agra, car Agra→Jaipur, fly or train back to Delhi. If you want to see how a fully private version is usually structured, our page spells one out (Suzu Travels, Golden Triangle tour) — not the only way, just one worked example.
