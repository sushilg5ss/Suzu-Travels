## 1. "First time in India, solo, as a woman — is it safe?"

- **Question found:** Fodor's Asia forum "First time solo trip" https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/first-time-solo-trip-1657603 and QVC Travel community "Solo female travel to India — anyone else been like this?" https://community.qvc.com/t5/Travel/Solo-female-travel-to-India-anyone-else-been-like-this/m-p/5664773 (dates not checked — WebSearch titles only). The same question is asked weekly on r/solotravel and r/IndiaTravel — post on the freshest thread.
- **Our page that answers it:** https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/is-india-safe-for-solo-female-travellers/

**Draft answer (≈180 words):**

> Honest answer: lots of women do it and have a great trip, but it's not a "just wing it" country. The UK, US and Australian governments all tell women to take extra care — the US page literally says it advises against women travelling alone in India, and the UK FCDO mentions harassment and assaults on foreign women in tourist areas. That's worth reading in full on the official pages before you book, not just on blogs.
>
> What lowers the risk in practice: arrive in daylight and pre-book your first night's transfer; never hail street taxis at night (use the pre-paid airport counter, Uber/Ola, or a car your hotel arranges); avoid road travel after dark and in winter fog; dress a bit more covered in small towns and temples; share your live location with someone at home. Save 112 (national emergency) and 1091 (women's helpline).
>
> A first trip on the well-worn route (Delhi–Agra–Jaipur, then Rishikesh or the Himachal hills) is easier than going remote.
>

## 2. "Agra to Jaipur — train, or a car so we can stop at Fatehpur Sikri?"

- **Question found:** Fodor's Asia forum "India Travel Help" https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/india-travel-help-659651 (looks old — dates not checked). Very frequent on r/IndiaTravel and the TripAdvisor Agra/Jaipur forums every winter — post on the next fresh thread.
- **Our page that answers it:** https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/agra-to-jaipur/

**Draft answer (≈150 words):**

> Depends on whether you want a travel day or a sightseeing day. Agra to Jaipur is about 232 km (144 mi) by road.
>
> If you just want to get there, take a train — book on the official IRCTC site, where foreigners can use the Foreign Tourist Quota up to 365 days ahead with a passport number and an international card. Check current timings there rather than on old blogs.
>
> If you go by car, two stops make it worth it: Fatehpur Sikri near the Agra end (UNESCO-listed since 1986, allow about 2 hours, open sunrise to sunset) and the Chand Baori stepwell at Abhaneri near the Jaipur end — 13 levels deep and genuinely striking. With both it's a full day, so leave after breakfast and arrive in Jaipur before dark; the UK Foreign Office advises against night and fog driving.
>
