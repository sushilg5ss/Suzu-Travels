## 1. Tripoto community — "How many days are enough for Varanasi?"
Question URL: https://www.tripoto.com/community/how-many-days-are-enough-for-varanasi
(Thread date not visible to the agent — if closed, reuse on any "Is 2 days enough for Varanasi?" thread on TripAdvisor India / r/IndiaTravel.)
Our page (link only if it helps): https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/varanasi-trip-guide/

Draft answer (≈170 words):

> Disclosure: I work with a small travel company in Himachal, so this is one local view.
>
> Two full days is enough for a first visit; three if you want it slow. What I'd fit in:
> - **Sunrise boat** along the ghats — the single best hour in the city.
> - **Evening Ganga Aarti** at Dashashwamedh Ghat — about 45 minutes, at dusk. Get there early for a spot (or watch from a boat).
> - **Kashi Vishwanath Temple** — security is strict and phones/bags are not taken in; there are lockers. Check the temple trust's current rules on the day.
> - **Sarnath**, a short drive out — added to the UNESCO World Heritage List in July 2026; half a day with the museum.
>
> Timing matters more than days: October to March is the comfortable season. In the monsoon (roughly July–September) the Ganga can flood the ghats and boat services get halted, so avoid that if the river is the reason you're going.
>
> We wrote a 2–3 day plan here if useful: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/varanasi-trip-guide/

## 2. Rick Steves Travel Forum — "Perfect itinerary for Golden Triangle — help"
Question URL: https://community.ricksteves.com/travel-forum/general-europe/perfect-itinerary-for-golden-triangle-help
(Thread date not visible to the agent — post only if replies are open; otherwise reuse on a fresh "Golden Triangle 7 days — which order?" thread.)
Our page: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/golden-triangle-itinerary-7-days/

Draft answer (≈175 words):

> Disclosure: I work with a small Himachal-based travel company, so weigh this accordingly.
>
> 5 days covers Delhi, Agra and Jaipur; 7 days lets you breathe. The order that works best for most people: **Delhi (1–2 days) → Agra → Fatehpur Sikri → Jaipur (2 days) → back to Delhi.**
>
> Three practical things people miss:
> 1. **The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays.** Count back from your arrival so your Agra morning isn't a Friday — sunrise is worth the early alarm.
> 2. **Drive times are longer than the map suggests.** Delhi–Agra is quick on the 165 km Yamuna Expressway, but Agra–Jaipur (about 232 km) is 4–5 hours once you stop at Fatehpur Sikri.
> 3. **December–January fog** can slow mornings on the plains; keep the schedule loose then. October to March is otherwise the best season.
>
> If you hire a car and driver, ask for the driver's name, photo and car number before you go, and only pay a deposit to a company with a real office and a registration you can check.
>
> Our day-by-day version: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/golden-triangle-itinerary-7-days/
