## 1. TripAdvisor India Forum — "Want snowfall in January but roads should not be blocked"
URL: https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g293860-i511-k14895197-Want_snowfall_in_January_but_roads_should_not_be_blocked-India.html
Our page that answers it: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/snow-in-india/

**Draft answer (≈170 words):**

January is a good month for this — it is one of the two snowiest months in the Himachal hills. Shimla averages roughly 40 cm of snow in each of January and February, but you get "snow days", not snow every day, so nothing is guaranteed on fixed dates.

The trade-off you're describing is real: the places that get the most snow are the ones whose roads close. A few practical tips:

- Base yourself in a town that stays reachable, like Shimla or Manali, and treat the higher spots (Kufri/Narkanda above Shimla, Solang above Manali) as day trips when the road is open.
- Rohtang Pass is shut from late November to about May, but the Atal Tunnel usually keeps the road from Manali to Sissu open.
- Build in one spare day, and drive the hills in daylight only — fog and black ice are the real risks, more than the snow itself.
- If you were thinking of Gulmarg, check your government's travel advice for Kashmir first (it can affect your insurance).

## 2. TripAdvisor Himachal Pradesh Forum — "10 days road trip from delhi"
URL: https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g297617-i9153-k14233974-10_days_road_trip_from_delhi-Himachal_Pradesh.html
Our page that answers it: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/himachal-10-day-itinerary/

**Draft answer (≈165 words):**

Ten days is a good length for a loop — it lets you do four hill areas without living in the car. The route most people enjoy:

1. Delhi → Kalka, then the Kalka–Shimla toy train up to Shimla (96 km of UNESCO-listed mountain railway; slow, but that is the point). 2 nights.
2. Shimla → Manali (about 260 km, a full day). 3 nights, so you get Solang valley and, depending on season, the Atal Tunnel to Sissu.
3. Manali → Dharamshala / McLeod Ganj. 2 nights.
4. Dharamshala → Dalhousie and Khajjiar. 2 nights.
5. Out via Pathankot, Amritsar or Kangra airport rather than driving all the way back to Delhi.

Best months are March–June and September–November. Avoid long drives in July–August (landslides), and plan every hill drive for daylight. If you're self-driving, the hill roads are narrow and slow — a local driver makes it far more relaxing.

I work with a small Himachal travel company, and we wrote the full day-by-day with distances here: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/himachal-10-day-itinerary/
