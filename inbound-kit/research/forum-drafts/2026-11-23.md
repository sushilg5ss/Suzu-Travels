## 1. Tripoto community — "guys which places do y'll think are best to go for christmas for snow"
Thread: https://www.tripoto.com/community/guys-which-places-do-y-ll-think-are-best-to-go-for-christmas-for-snow
Links to: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/christmas-and-new-year-in-india/

> Honest answer from someone who works with a small Himachal travel company: snow at Christmas is never guaranteed anywhere in Himachal. IMD's figures put Shimla's average December snowfall at only about 7 cm — January and February are much snowier. So plan for the cold and the views, and treat snow as a bonus.
>
> If snow really matters, go higher rather than to the Mall Road towns: Solang (above Manali) and Narkanda/Kufri (above Shimla) hold snow more often than the towns themselves. Keep one or two flexible days, because when it does snow, roads above Manali and Shimla can close for a day or two and traffic at Parwanoo/Manali entry gets very slow over the 24 Dec–1 Jan rush.
>
> Kashmir (Gulmarg) is the most reliable snow, but check your government's travel advice first — several countries advise against travel there, which can affect insurance.
>
> We put the dates and options for 2026–27 in one page if it helps: [Christmas and New Year in India](https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/christmas-and-new-year-in-india/).

## 2. Tripoto community — "where can i expect snowfall december 2nd week in himachal… McLeod Ganj, Palampur, Dalhousie 3 days each"
Thread: https://www.tripoto.com/community/where-can-i-expect-snowfall-december-2nd-week-in-himachal-my-original-plan-was-to-cover-mcleod-ganj-palampur-dalhousie-3-3-days-in-each-place-but-will-there-be-enough-snow-there-that-time-i-can-make-changes-if-it-isn-t-please-suggest-places
Links to: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/snow-in-india/

> I work with a small travel company in Himachal, so I get this question a lot. In the second week of December, McLeod Ganj, Palampur and Dalhousie usually do NOT have snow in town — they are lower than Manali or Shimla's upper areas. You will see the Dhauladhar peaks above McLeod Ganj and Palampur white, which is beautiful, but fresh snow on the ground that early is luck.
>
> Your plan is still a good, relaxed trip. If touching snow is a must, swap one stop for Manali and do a day up to Solang (or Kufri/Narkanda from Shimla); snow there is more likely, though still not certain in mid-December. Snow is far likelier from late December to February. Watch the IMD forecast a few days before and keep a spare day in case a road closes.
>
> Month-by-month details are here: [Snow in India](https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/snow-in-india/).
