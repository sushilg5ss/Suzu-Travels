## 1. TripAdvisor India forum (Rajasthan) — "Rajasthan Dec Jan"
Thread: https://www.tripadvisor.in/ShowTopic-g297665-i4566-k278177-Rajasthan_Dec_Jan-Rajasthan.html
Links to: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/jaisalmer/

> I work with a small travel company in Himachal, so weigh this accordingly. December–January is a good time for Jaisalmer: the comfortable season is November to March, but desert nights get cold, so pack a warm layer. Two nights is enough for most people — the fort (part of UNESCO's Hill Forts of Rajasthan, 2013, and still lived in), the Jain temples, the havelis and Gadisar Lake, then sunset at the Sam dunes, about 40 km (25 mi) out of town.
>
> One thing many older trip reports won't mention: since October 2026 the forest department hasn't allowed visitors on the dunes inside Desert National Park after sunset. Plenty of camps and sunset camel rides are outside the park and carry on as before, but ask your camp exactly where it is before you book a "night on the dunes". Camel rides are optional — a jeep or a walk works too.
>
> We put the details in one place here: [Jaisalmer travel guide](https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/jaisalmer/).

## 2. Fodor's Travel Talk (Asia) — "Seeking help on Rajasthan Itinerary"
Thread: https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/seeking-help-on-rajasthan-itinerary-991644/
Links to: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/rajasthan-tour/

> Disclosure: I work with a small travel company in Himachal. For a first Rajasthan loop, 10–12 days lets you travel without rushing: Jaipur (2 nights) → Jodhpur (2) → Jaisalmer (2) → Udaipur (3), and fly out of Udaipur so you don't have to drive back. Add Agra at the start if the Taj Mahal is on your list (it's closed on Fridays).
>
> The desert legs (Jodhpur–Jaisalmer and Jaisalmer–Udaipur) are full travel days, so a private car with one driver for the whole trip is the easiest way — you stop when you like and your luggage stays with you. Go November to March; from April the desert towns pass 40 °C (104 °F).
>
> If you'd rather not organise it, our sample [12-day Rajasthan itinerary](https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/rajasthan-tour/) lays out each day — but the route works just as well self-booked.
