## 1. "Two weeks in Rajasthan with a car and driver — how many nights where?"

- **Question found:** Fodor's Asia forum "Car and driver" https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/car-and-driver-442841 and "Rajasthan - 9 days" https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/rajasthan---9-days.cfm?8 (both look old — dates not checked). The same question comes up every winter on r/IndiaTravel and the TripAdvisor Rajasthan forum — post on the next fresh thread (Nov–Jan is peak planning time).
- **Our page that answers it:** https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/rajasthan-tour/

**Draft answer (≈170 words):**

> The classic loop is Jaipur (2 nights) → Jodhpur (2) → Jaisalmer (2) → Udaipur (3). That's 9 nights of Rajasthan; with arrival/departure days and maybe a night in Agra for the Taj (Agra to Jaipur is about 232 km / 144 mi, so do Delhi → Agra → Jaipur first) you land at 11–12 days. If you have two full weeks, add Pushkar or Ranthambore rather than more cities — the drives are already long (Jodhpur–Jaisalmer and Jaisalmer–Udaipur are full travel days), and two nights per stop is what keeps it enjoyable.
>
> Timing matters more than people think: November to March is the season. From April the desert towns pass 40 °C (104 °F) and in May Jaisalmer can reach about 46 °C (115 °F). The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays.
>
> Car + driver for the whole loop is the easy way; agree in writing what the daily rate covers (tolls, parking, driver's night allowance) before you go.
>
> Disclosure: I work with a small Himachal travel company; we wrote up the day-by-day with drive times here: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/rajasthan-tour/

## 2. "First trip to India in April/May — is it too hot, where should we go?"

- **Question found:** Fodor's "May trip to India" https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/may-trip-to-india-657296/ and "India visit in May" https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/india-visit-in-may-587323/ (old threads — dates not checked); VoyageForum "aller en Inde en avril-mai" (French). Post on the next fresh English thread on r/IndiaTravel / r/solotravel / TripAdvisor India (spring trips get planned Jan–Mar).
- **Our page that answers it:** https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/best-time-to-visit-india/

**Draft answer (≈165 words):**

> Honest answer: April–May is the hardest time for the classic Delhi–Agra–Jaipur–Rajasthan route. The plains regularly pass 40 °C (104 °F); a typical May high in Jodhpur is about 45 °C (113 °F). Kerala is hot and humid March–May, and the monsoon usually reaches Kerala around 1 June and Delhi around 27 June (IMD normals).
>
> What works instead is going up. April and May are good months for the Himachal hill towns — Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala — and Rishikesh is fine in April. Ladakh opens up as a summer trip from June. If the Taj Mahal is a must, do Agra at sunrise and keep the rest of the trip in the hills.
>
> If you can move your dates, October to March is the easy window for a first trip: dry, mild days in the north and peak season in Kerala and Goa.
>
> Disclosure: I work with a small Himachal travel company; our month-by-month table (with sources) is here if useful: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/best-time-to-visit-india/
