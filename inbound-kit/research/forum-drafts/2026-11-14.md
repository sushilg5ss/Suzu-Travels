## 1. Tripoto community — "Planning to visit Kashmir this May … best itinerary for 6N/7D … Srinagar, Pahalgam and Gulmarg"
URL: https://www.tripoto.com/community/hi-m-planning-to-visit-kashmir-this-may-please-suggest-me-best-itinerary-for-6n-n-7-d-also-suggest-me-best-hotels-also-not-very-costly-in-srinagar-pahalgam-n-gulmarg
Our page that answers it: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/kashmir-7-day-itinerary/

**Draft answer (≈170 words):**

Seven days fits Kashmir well if you keep Srinagar as the base and don't change hotels every night. A shape that works:

- **Days 1–3 Srinagar:** Dal Lake by shikara, the Mughal gardens, the old city, and one houseboat night if you want the experience.
- **Day 3 or 4 Gulmarg** (about 52–56 km): the gondola and the meadow; it can be a day trip or one night.
- **Days 4–5 Pahalgam** (about 95 km): two nights so you actually get time in the valleys instead of just the drive.
- **Day 6 Sonamarg** (roughly 90–110 km): a long day trip in summer; many hotels there only open in season.
- **Day 7** back to Srinagar airport.

Book the Srinagar–airport days with some slack — weather and security checks can slow the roads. If you are a foreign national, read your own government's advice first: the UK FCDO and the US State Department both currently advise against travel to J&K, and many travel insurers follow that.

I work with a small Himachal travel company; we put the day-by-day plan with distances here: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/kashmir-7-day-itinerary/

## 2. Tripoto community — "Visit Kashmir in January with my family — is it good, is it safe, will we see snowfall?"
URL: https://www.tripoto.com/community/i-was-planning-to-visit-kashmir-in-january-with-my-family-is-it-good-to-go-at-this-time-or-not-also-is-it-safe-or-not-plus-if-we-could-enjoy-the-snowfall-there-or-not
Our page that answers it: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/kashmir-7-day-itinerary/ (winter section); for a snow alternative: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/snow-in-india/

**Draft answer (≈160 words):**

January is the coldest stretch in Kashmir (the harshest stretch, "Chillai Kalan", runs 21 Dec–31 Jan), so expect sub-zero nights in Srinagar and real winter in Gulmarg. Snow is likely in Gulmarg in January and the ski season usually runs mid-December to mid-April — but nothing about snowfall on a given date is guaranteed.

What changes in winter: Sonamarg is usually skipped (its hotels are summer-only), so a winter week usually swaps that day for a second Gulmarg day. Roads can close after heavy snow, so keep a spare day before your flight home.

On safety: read your own government's current advice before booking and check your insurance covers the region — some foreign advisories (UK, US) currently advise against travel to J&K. Indian travellers mostly go as normal, but stay flexible.

I work with a small Himachal travel company; our winter notes are on this page: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/kashmir-7-day-itinerary/ — and if you mainly want snow, Himachal (Shimla/Manali) is another option.
