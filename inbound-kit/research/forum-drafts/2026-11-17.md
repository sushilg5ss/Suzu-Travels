## 1. immihelp — "Are babies eligible for e-Tourist Visa (eTV)?"
- Thread: https://immihelp.com/forum/india-passport-visa-oci/139758-are-babies-eligible-for-e-tourist-visa-etv (date not verifiable from here — team to check it is still open/recent)
- Our page that answers it: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/india-family-holiday/ (visa/health callout)

> Yes — babies and children can travel on the e-Visa, but each child needs their **own passport** and their **own application**. The official e-Visa page says it is "not available to individuals endorsed on Parent's/Spouse's Passport", so a child added to a parent's passport won't work. The passport should be valid for at least six months from arrival.
>
> Apply only on the government site, indianvisaonline.gov.in — several look-alike sites charge a big "service fee" for the same form. Upload a clear passport scan and a recent photo for the child just as for an adult.
>
> One thing to watch: India announced on 11 Nov 2026 (PIB press release) that the Tourist Visa is being renamed the Visitor Visa. Until the official site changes, apply for whatever it currently shows (e-Tourist Visa).
>
> For health, ask your GP or a travel clinic 6–8 weeks before — the UK's TravelHealthPro page for India has notes specific to infants.
>
> Disclosure: I work with a small Himachal travel company; we're not a visa agency. If useful, we wrote up what we tell families here: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/india-family-holiday/

## 2. FlyerTalk India — "Golden Triangle Driver/Tour operator"
- Thread: https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/india/1801823-golden-triangle-driver-tour-operator.html (date not verifiable — team to check; FlyerTalk bans touting → post **without** a link)
- Our page that answers it: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/private-driver-india/

> A few things that helped people I've planned with, whoever you book:
>
> - Ask for a car-and-driver price for the **whole route** (Delhi–Agra–Jaipur–Delhi), and ask what it includes: tolls, state permits, parking, the driver's food and night stay are the usual "extras".
> - Ask for the driver's name, photo and car number before you travel, and the company's registration (state tourism department registration is something you can look up).
> - Don't pay everything upfront. A deposit with a clear written refund window is normal; ask what happens if you cancel.
> - Agra: go early, Taj is closed on Fridays. Same-day Delhi–Agra works but is a long day; one night in Agra is easier.
> - Pay any visa only on indianvisaonline.gov.in.
>
> Disclosure: I work with a small Himachal-based travel company, so weigh my view accordingly — happy to answer questions here rather than by DM.
