## 1. TripAdvisor India Forum — "Where to go in January"
URL: https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g293860-i511-k15406507-Where_to_go_in_January-India.html
Our page that answers it: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/india-in-january/

**Draft answer (≈175 words):**

January is one of the easiest months to plan India, because you can pick your climate:

- **Warm and dry:** Kerala and Goa are in peak season, and Rajasthan is lovely by day — Jodhpur averages roughly 5–30 °C (41–86 °F), so pack a warm layer for desert nights.
- **Delhi, Agra, Jaipur:** sunny days, cold nights, and morning fog that can delay early trains and flights. Don't plan a tight connection before 10 am, and remember the Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays.
- **Snow:** the Himachal hills get their heaviest snow — Shimla averages about 2.5–10 °C (37–51 °F) — but snow on a given date is never guaranteed and higher roads can close.

Dates worth knowing for January 2027: Makar Sankranti on the 14th, the Jaipur Literature Festival 14–18 Jan (confirm on the festival's site), Pongal in Tamil Nadu on the 15th and Republic Day in Delhi on Tuesday the 26th — hotels in Jaipur and Delhi fill up around those dates.

I work with a small Himachal travel company; we wrote up the January details here: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/india-in-january/

## 2. TripAdvisor India Forum — "Driver only or hotels included?"
URL: https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g293860-i511-k14550116-Driver_only_or_hotels_included-India.html
Our page that answers it: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/private-driver-india/ (or, if the poster wants upgraded hotels, https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/india-luxury-tour/)

**Draft answer (≈160 words):**

Both work — it mostly depends on how picky you are about hotels.

**Driver only** makes sense if you enjoy choosing hotels yourself and want to book on points or with free cancellation. You pay the company for the car, driver, fuel, tolls and parking, and you keep full control of where you sleep. Just send them each hotel's address in advance so the driver's night stay is planned.

**Hotels included** saves time and is useful when you want a specific category — for example heritage palace hotels in Rajasthan or 5-star rooms — because the company can match the route and check-in times to the hotels.

Either way, before paying: ask for a written day-by-day plan, check what is excluded (entry tickets, guides, meals), ask whether the deposit is refundable, and check the company's state registration. You should get your driver's name, photo and car number before the trip.

I work with a small Himachal travel company; our notes on private drivers are here: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/private-driver-india/
