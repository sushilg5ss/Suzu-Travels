## 1. Goa: a week in North and a week in South — how to split it
**Question type (real example):** TripAdvisor Goa Forum — "Goa" (fryston, York UK, 22 Nov 2025: flights booked for Feb 2026, wants a week North + a week South, confused by hotel choice). Thread still open (last reply 13 Jun 2026) but the asker's trip is past → use this draft on the next open "North or South Goa?" / "first time Goa" thread.
https://www.tripadvisor.co.nz/ShowTopic-g297604-i6045-k15461004-Goa-Goa.html

**Draft answer (≈180 words):**
> Disclosure: I work with a small travel company in Himachal, so I'll keep this to how to split it rather than which hotel.
>
> A week each side works well because the two halves feel different. North Goa (Baga, Calangute, Anjuna) is lively — nightlife, water sports, busier beaches. South Goa (Palolem, Patnem, Agonda) is quieter and better for swimming and slow days; Palolem's bay is sheltered and is one of the calmer swimming spots. Old Goa's UNESCO churches sit in between, about 10 km from Panaji — half a day is enough.
>
> Two practical things people miss: Goa has two airports (Dabolim and Mopa) a long way apart, so check which one your flight uses before booking the first hotel. And on the beach, swim only between the red-and-yellow flags where lifeguards are on watch, never at a red flag, and not after drinks or sunset — the UK FCDO notes drownings happen every year. Drink only in licensed places and watch your glass.
>
> We put the North/South comparison and the official beach rules on one page if useful: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/goa-holiday/

## 2. First time in India, ~2 weeks, Oct–Dec — where to go without moving every two days
**Question type (real example, old):** Fodor's Asia — "First Time To India and Nepal - Recommendations?" (experienced SE-Asia/Morocco travellers, Oct/Nov/Dec, ~1 week India) and "Trip to India" (solo traveller, 2 weeks, wants ~3 bases, not a package). Both old → use on the next open "first trip to India, where should I go?" thread (r/IndiaTravel, r/solotravel, TripAdvisor India forum).
https://www.fodors.com/community/11135109-post7/ · https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/trip-to-india-491436/

**Draft answer (≈175 words):**
> I work with a small Himachal-based travel company, so factor that in — but the honest advice is: pick two regions, not five.
>
> For a first trip, most people start with the Golden Triangle — Delhi, Agra for the Taj Mahal (closed on Fridays) and Jaipur — about 5–6 days. Then add ONE more region that suits you: the Himalayan foothills (Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala) for cooler air and slower days, Varanasi for the Ganga ghats, or Kerala/Goa for backwaters and beaches. Three bases of 3–4 nights each is far more enjoyable than moving every second day.
>
> October to March is the comfortable window for the plains, Kerala and Goa; the monsoon runs roughly June to October. In winter, fog slows early-morning and night driving in the north, so plan daylight drives.
>
> Visa: apply yourself only on the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — there are look-alike sites that charge extra.
>
> If it helps, we made a month-by-month table of where's good when: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/best-places-to-visit-in-india/
