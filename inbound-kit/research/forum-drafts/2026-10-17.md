## Draft 1 — TripAdvisor, New Delhi forum: "Local private cab and guide suggestion" (last post 28 Sep 2026)
- Thread: https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g304551-i3482-k15586175-Local_private_cab_and_guide_suggestion-New_Delhi_National_Capital_Territory_of_Delhi.html

- **Link in the answer? One, labelled, at the end** — only because the "what's included / driver vs guide" part is exactly what the page explains. Drop it if the poster asked for "no companies".

> Full disclosure up front: I work with a small travel company based in Himachal, so read this knowing that — it's general advice, not a recommendation of anyone in particular.
>
> Two things that save most people trouble with a private cab in and around Delhi:
>
> **Cab and guide are two different hires.** A driver drives and sorts parking, tolls and the odd permit. He is not a guide, and in Delhi the monuments (Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb, Red Fort) have government-licensed guides at the gate or bookable through your hotel. Booking "a driver who also guides" usually gets you neither done well.
>
> **Get the inclusions in writing before you pay.** A fair quote states: fuel, tolls, parking, interstate tax (if you go to Agra or Jaipur), the driver's own food and overnight stay, and the hours/kilometres per day. Most roadside arguments are about one of those being "extra". Pay a small deposit by card or PayPal, the balance at the end — not cash up front — and ask for the car's commercial (yellow) plate number and the driver's name before the day.
>
> If you plan Agra, avoid Friday — the Taj Mahal is closed to visitors that day.
>
> If it helps, the company I work with has an honest write-up of what a multi-day car-and-driver quote should and shouldn't include (no prices on it, it's the checklist that matters): https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/private-driver-india/

## Draft 2 — TripAdvisor, New Delhi forum: "Reasonable rate for 8-hour taxi/driver including airport" (last post 3 Oct 2026)
- Thread: https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g304551-i3482-k15588563-Reasonable_rate_for_8_hour_taxi_driver_including_airport-New_Delhi_National_Capital_Territ.html
- Our page: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/private-driver-india/ (inclusions checklist)
- **Link in the answer? No.** The poster wants a number; we do not print rates for inbound guests (nothing in prices.json), so the honest contribution is *how to read a quote*, not a figure. Never add a ₹ or US$ rate to this draft.

> I'll declare an interest — I work with a small Himachal travel company — and I'll deliberately not quote you a number, because the "8-hour" rate in Delhi means different things from different operators and that is where people get caught.
>
> What to check before you compare two quotes:
>
> 1. **The package is usually "8 hours / 80 km"** (sometimes 100 km). Airport–hotel–monuments–hotel inside Delhi normally fits; add Gurgaon or Noida and you will run over the kilometres. Ask what the per-extra-hour and per-extra-km charges are — that is the real price.
> 2. **Ask whether tolls, parking and the airport pick-up charge are included.** Airport parking plus the toll on the way out are small but they are the classic "sir, extra".
> 3. **Night surcharge:** many operators add one for pick-ups before 6 am or after 10 pm — relevant if your flight lands at 2 am.
> 4. **Ask for the car's commercial (yellow) plate** and the driver's name and phone the day before. A white private-plate car is not permitted to carry paying passengers, and an insurer may take the same view if anything goes wrong.
> 5. **Pay a small deposit by card, the rest after the day.** Full cash in advance is a red flag.
>
> Get two or three written quotes with those five points answered the same way, and the cheapest of those is a fair rate. Hotel desks are convenient but usually add their margin; a sedan is fine for two people with carry-ons, ask for an SUV if you have big suitcases.
