> Sorry this happened — you are not alone. Air India dropped Amritsar–Gatwick from 25 October 2026 for the winter season, and Delhi–Birmingham went from 4 November, so many winter bookings on those routes were cancelled or moved.
>
> What usually helps:
> 1. Ask Air India in writing for the options they must offer — a refund, or re-routing (often via Delhi to Heathrow, which still has non-stop flights). Get the answer by email.

>

## 2. Fodor's Travel Talk (Asia) — "Diwali"
Thread: https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/diwali-1722480-printthread/
Our page: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/india-festivals-calendar/

> If you're planning around Diwali, 2027 is a good year to aim for: the Government of India's 2027 holiday list has Diwali on Friday 29 October, with Dussehra three weeks earlier on Saturday 9 October. The weather in Delhi, Agra and Rajasthan is pleasant by then.
>
> A few honest tips from seeing it many times:
> - The night itself is family time. Many shops close early and drivers and hotel staff want to be home, so plan a quiet evening at your hotel (rooftops are great for the fireworks) rather than a long drive that day.
> - Book trains and hotels early — the whole country travels in Diwali week, and prices rise.
> - Air quality in North Indian cities is often poor from late October; if you have breathing problems, check the AQI and consider ending in the hills or Rajasthan.
> - Jaipur and Udaipur decorate their old cities beautifully; Varanasi has Dev Deepawali about two weeks later.
>
> We keep a 2027 dates list with the source for each date here: https://suzutravels.com/india-tours/india-festivals-calendar/
>
> (Disclosure: I work with a small Himachal travel company.)
