## Full description
See the Himalaya from Manali with a private car and an English-speaking driver, planned by Suzu Travels, a team based in Himachal Pradesh.

Day 1 is a long but scenic drive from Chandigarh up into the hills and along the Beas river through the Kullu valley to Manali. We plan stops for tea and views and arrive before dark.

In Manali, visit the Hadimba temple in its cedar forest and walk the lanes of Old Manali. Take a day trip to Solang Valley, 13 km away, known for snow in winter and open meadows in summer. When the road is open, continue through the Atal Tunnel — 9.02 km long, at about 3,000 m — which keeps Manali connected to the Lahaul valley all year. Rohtang Pass (51 km from Manali) needs a permit and is closed by snow from late November until about May; we tell you before booking whether it is possible on your dates.

Mountain weather decides the day. In winter, snow can close or slow roads; in the monsoon (roughly June to September) landslides are possible. We change the order of the days when needed and follow local authority advice.

Your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip. Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022).

### Sample day plan
- Day 1: Chandigarh → Manali by car via the Kullu valley (long drive day; arrive before dark)
- Day 2: Manali — Hadimba temple, Old Manali, Vashisht area, evening on the Mall
- Day 3: Solang Valley and, road permitting, the Atal Tunnel (Rohtang only in season, with permit)
- Day 4: Kullu valley at leisure — riverside stops, local markets; or a slow day in Manali
- Day 5: Manali → Chandigarh (or drop at Kullu–Manali airport, Bhuntar, if flights suit)
