## Full description
Travel the Indian Himalaya with your own car and English-speaking driver, planned by a local team based in Himachal Pradesh.

Your trip starts at Chandigarh (or Kalka station). Take the narrow-gauge Kalka–Shimla railway, part of UNESCO's "Mountain Railways of India", or drive up to Shimla, the old summer capital. Walk the Ridge and Mall Road and see Christ Church.

Drive on through the Kullu valley to Manali, about 260 km (162 mi) from Shimla — a full day on mountain roads with stops along the Beas river. In Manali, visit the Hadimba temple and Old Manali, and take a day trip to Solang Valley, 13 km away. Higher passes such as Rohtang need a permit and open only in season (roughly May to November); we tell you before booking whether they are possible on your dates.

Finish in Dharamshala and McLeod Ganj (about 2,082 m / 6,831 ft), home of the Tibetan Government in exile since 1959. See the Tsuglagkhang complex, St John in the Wilderness church, Bhagsu waterfall and the Kangra tea gardens. Your tour ends at Kangra airport (Gaggal, 14 km from Dharamshala) or with a drop to Chandigarh.

Hill roads are slow. We plan short driving days and no night driving. In winter (December–February) snow can close or slow some roads; we adjust the order of stops when needed.

Your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip. Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022).

### Sample day plan
- Day 1: Chandigarh/Kalka → Shimla (toy train or car); evening on the Ridge
- Day 2: Shimla — Mall Road, Christ Church, Jakhu, optional Kufri
- Day 3: Shimla → Manali via the Kullu valley (full day)
- Day 4: Manali — Hadimba temple, Old Manali, Vashisht
- Day 5: Solang Valley day trip (Rohtang only in season, with permit)
- Day 6: Manali → Dharamshala (full day)
- Day 7: McLeod Ganj — Tsuglagkhang, Bhagsu, Norbulingka, tea gardens
- Day 8: Departure from Kangra airport or drive to Chandigarh
