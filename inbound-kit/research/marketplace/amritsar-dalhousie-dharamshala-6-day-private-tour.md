## Full description
Start in Amritsar, the Sikh holy city. Visit Sri Harmandir Sahib, the Golden Temple, early in the morning when it is quieter, and share a meal at the Guru Ka Langar, the free community kitchen that feeds around 20,000 people every day. Visitors remove their shoes and cover their heads before entering. Walk to Jallianwala Bagh, the memorial to the people shot by British troops on 13 April 1919. In the late afternoon, your driver takes you 32 km to the Attari–Wagah border for the Beating Retreat ceremony, where soldiers of both countries march in perfect drill (about 4:15 pm in winter, 5:15 pm in summer — times move with sunset, so arrive early).

Then head into the Himalaya. Dalhousie, about 199 km from Amritsar, was founded in 1850 as a summer retreat, with old churches and a quiet Mall Road. Khajjiar, about 22–24 km away, is a saucer-shaped meadow at around 1,980 m (6,500 ft) ringed by deodar forest.

Finish in Dharamshala and McLeod Ganj (2,082 m / 6,831 ft), seat of the Tibetan Government in exile and home of the Dalai Lama since 1959. Kangra (Gaggal) airport is just 14 km away for your onward flight.

Your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip. Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022), based in Himachal.

### Sample day plan
- Day 1: Arrive Amritsar (Sri Guru Ramdass International Airport is 13 km from the Golden Temple); evening at the Golden Temple
- Day 2: Golden Temple at dawn, langar, Jallianwala Bagh; afternoon Attari–Wagah Beating Retreat
- Day 3: Amritsar → Dalhousie (about 199 km, most of the day on the road)
- Day 4: Khajjiar meadow and Dalhousie's Mall Road and churches
- Day 5: Dalhousie → Dharamshala / McLeod Ganj (full-day mountain drive)
- Day 6: McLeod Ganj and the Tsuglagkhang temple complex; drop at Kangra (Gaggal) airport, 14 km
