## Full description
Climb into the Himalayan foothills the slow way. The Kalka–Shimla Railway opened in 1903 and was added to UNESCO's "Mountain Railways of India" in 2008. Its narrow-gauge track runs 96 km through 102 tunnels and over 900 bridges and viaducts, rising from Kalka at 656 m to Shimla at 2,075 m. The ride takes about 4½ to 6 hours depending on the train.

Day 1 starts at Kalka station (or Chandigarh, 119 km by road from Shimla). You take the toy train up while your driver takes the luggage by road and meets you in Shimla. In Shimla, walk the Ridge and Mall Road in the colonial old town.

Day 2 is a day out by car to the hills and orchards around Shimla, at your own pace.

Day 3: drive back down to Chandigarh or Kalka, or continue your Himachal trip to Manali (260 km) or Dharamshala.

Trains and weather decide the day. Train timings change, and in the monsoon (roughly July–September) landslides have suspended the line for several days (for example 31 Aug–6 Sep 2025). If the train is not running, we drive you up instead. In January and February Shimla often has snow — warm clothes are a must.

Your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip. Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022).
