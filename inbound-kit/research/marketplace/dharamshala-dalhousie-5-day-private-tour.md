## Full description
Spend five unhurried days in the Dhauladhar hills of western Himachal with a private car and an English-speaking driver, planned by Suzu Travels, a team based in Himachal Pradesh.

We meet you at Kangra (Gaggal) airport, only about 14 km from Dharamshala. Above the town sits McLeod Ganj, at 2,082 m, where the Dalai Lama settled in 1959 and the Tibetan Government in exile has its seat — monasteries, Tibetan cafés and mountain views at walking pace.

Then drive on through the hills to Dalhousie, founded by Lord Dalhousie in 1850 as a summer retreat, with its old churches and pine-lined walks. A half-day trip takes you to Khajjiar, about 22 km away, a wide meadow ringed by forest.

Mountain weather decides the day. Snow is common around McLeod Ganj in winter, and in the monsoon (roughly June to September) landslides are possible. We change the order of the days when needed and never drive at night or in fog.

Your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip. Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022).

### Sample day plan
- Day 1: Kangra airport → Dharamshala / McLeod Ganj (about 14 km); evening walk
- Day 2: McLeod Ganj — Tibetan temple and monasteries, Bhagsu area, cafés
- Day 3: McLeod Ganj → Dalhousie (full-day scenic drive with stops)
- Day 4: Dalhousie and Khajjiar (about 22 km)
- Day 5: Dalhousie → Kangra airport (about 110 km) or Pathankot railway station (about 87 km)
