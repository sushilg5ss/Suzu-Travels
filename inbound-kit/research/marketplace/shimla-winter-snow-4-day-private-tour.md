## Full description
See the Himalayan hill town of Shimla in winter, with a private car and an English-speaking driver, planned by Suzu Travels, a team based in Himachal Pradesh.

Day 1 is a long drive from Delhi (about 370 km / 230 miles) up into the hills; we plan stops and arrive before dark. Shimla's winter is cold — nights around 0 °C (32 °F), days around 10 °C (50 °F) in January and February, with snow on about four days a month on average (IMD normals).

In Shimla, walk the car-free Mall and the Ridge, visit Christ Church, and climb or drive up to Jakhu hill. If you like, we plan one leg on the UNESCO-listed Kalka–Shimla toy train (you book the ticket yourself on IRCTC; we meet you at the station).

Mountain weather decides the day. Snow and ice can close or slow roads; we change the plan when needed and follow local authority advice.

Your driver's name, photo and car number are shared with you before the trip. Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022).

### Sample day plan
- Day 1: Delhi → Shimla by car (long drive day; arrive before dark)
- Day 2: Shimla — the Mall, the Ridge, Christ Church, Jakhu hill; snow walks when there is snow
- Day 3: Shimla at leisure, or a slow scenic drive in the hills (road permitting)
- Day 4: Shimla → Delhi (or drop at Chandigarh / Kalka for a train)
