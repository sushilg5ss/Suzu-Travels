  • Delhi · Agra · Jaipur: Monsoon usually reaches Delhi around 27 June (IMD normal). The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays.
  • Varanasi: Dev Deepavali, 15 days after Diwali, lights the ghats with thousands of lamps.
  • Amritsar: Our planning experience — we found no official season for Amritsar.
  • Rishikesh · Haridwar: International Yoga Festival each February–March. Rafting usually pauses in July and August.
  • Shimla · Dharamshala: Winter snowfall is common in Dharamshala. Our home region — our own team plans it.
  • Manali: Rohtang Pass is closed by snow from late November and reopens by May (HP Tourism). Atal Tunnel to Lahaul is open year-round.
  • Ladakh (Leh): Summer trip. No official month source: roads from Manali and Srinagar are seasonal — confirm before you book.
  • Kerala: Kerala Tourism: November–February is peak season, October–February is best for houseboats, June–August is monsoon; March–May is hot and humid.
  • Goa: Foreign visitors come in winter (Govt of Goa). No sea swimming in the monsoon — follow the lifeguard flags.
  • Kashmir: The UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir except Jammu city, and the US State Department says ‘Do not travel’ to J&K except eastern Ladakh and Leh (advice checked 19 Oct 2026). Check the latest before you book.
