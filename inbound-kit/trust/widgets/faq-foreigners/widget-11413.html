How do I get an Indian e-Visa without being overcharged?

Apply yourself on indianvisaonline.gov.in. The Government of India says no agent is needed and it charges no express or emergency fee. Your passport needs at least six months’ validity. Apply at least 4 days before arrival, carry the printed ETA showing “GRANTED”, and enter through one of the designated airports or seaports on the official list.

We are not a visa agency. Apply yourself, only at the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in — beware look-alike sites that charge extra. Rules and fees change; confirm on the official site before you pay. Step by step: our India e-Visa guide (checked 18 Nov 2026).

Coming change: India is renaming the Tourist Visa the Visitor Visa (PIB, 11 Nov 2026); the official site still shows the e-Tourist Visa — until it changes, apply there only.

Holders of an OCI card need no visa. Travelling on an Indian passport? No visa is needed either.

Do I need to fill in an arrival card?

Yes. Since 1 October 2025, foreign nationals and OCI card holders submit the e-Arrival card within 72 hours before arrival — on boi.gov.in, indianvisaonline.gov.in or the “Indian Visa Su-Swagatam” app. It is arrival information, not a visa (Embassy of India notice; official site re-checked 18 Nov 2026).

What do the US and UK governments advise about travel in India?

The US State Department rates India Level 2 – Exercise increased caution “due to crime and terrorism. Some areas have increased risk.” It rates Jammu and Kashmir Level 4: Do not travel, except eastern Ladakh and Leh (advisory issued 16 Jun 2025; Kashmir line checked 14 Nov 2026).

The UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir except air travel to and from, and travel within, the city of Jammu; against all travel within 10 km (6 mi) of the India–Pakistan border except the Wagah–Attari crossing; and against all but essential travel to Manipur (page updated 7 Oct 2026, checked 18 Nov 2026).

What this means for you: read the current advice for your passport before you book, and ask your insurer whether you are covered in any area your government advises against. More: is India safe for solo female travellers?

How do I avoid scams and ticket touts?

The UK FCDO says: “Be cautious in tourist areas, where scammers, pickpockets and ticket touts target foreign nationals” and “avoid people at railway stations offering tickets and tours as you may be scammed.” It also notes that “app-based taxis like Uber and Ola are widely available, including in all airport terminal buildings” (updated 7 Oct 2026).

Do I need vaccinations for India?

The official e-Visa site says travellers arriving from or through yellow-fever countries need a yellow-fever vaccination card or may face quarantine. For everything else, talk to your doctor or a travel clinic well before you go, and see CDC (US) or NHS Fit for Travel (UK). We do not give medical advice. When does the monsoon arrive?

India Meteorological Department normals: the monsoon reaches Kerala around 1 June and New Delhi around 27 June, and withdraws from Delhi around 25 September. Weather varies year to year. Month by month, region by region: our best time to visit India guide.
