  • Best windows: May–June and September–October.
  • Avoid: July and August — monsoon landslides.
  • Opening: The temples usually open in late April or May. The temple committees announce the exact dates early each year, and we plan your trip around them.
  • Closing: The temples close in October or November for winter. Please confirm the closing dates before you book a late-season yatra.
  • Packing: nights are cold even in June — bring warm layers and a rain jacket.
  • Registration: every pilgrim registers on the official portal registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in — we complete it for our guests.
