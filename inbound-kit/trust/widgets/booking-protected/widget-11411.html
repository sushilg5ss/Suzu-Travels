My flight is delayed — what happens?

Send us your new arrival time on WhatsApp and we re-time your airport pick-up.

A road or monument is closed on the day.

We may re-order sightseeing days, for example when a monument or road is closed on a given day, and we talk the options through with you. As our terms say, we are not responsible for delays or changes caused by events beyond our control (bad weather, landslides, road closures, strikes, government orders), and extra costs from such events are paid by the guest. Travel insurance is worth considering.

What if I need to cancel?

Tell us in writing (email or WhatsApp). Full refund if you cancel within 48 hours of paying the deposit. After that, the cancellation terms written in your confirmation apply — read them before you pay.

What if you cancel my trip?

If we cancel your trip for a reason within our control, we refund everything you have paid, or offer you another date or trip if you prefer.

How do I know I am paying the real Suzu Travels?

Pay only on our Payment page or by a payment request we send you from info@suzutravels.com. We never ask you to pay into a personal account. Our registration certificate is on the certificates page.
