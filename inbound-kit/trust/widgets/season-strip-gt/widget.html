  • Delhi · Agra · Jaipur: Monsoon usually reaches Delhi around 27 June (IMD normal). The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays.
  • Haridwar · Rishikesh: International Yoga Festival each February–March. Rafting usually pauses in July and August.
  • Shimla · Manali: Rohtang Pass is closed by snow from late November and reopens by May (HP Tourism). Atal Tunnel to Lahaul is open year-round. Winter snowfall is common in the Himachal hills.
  • Kashmir: The UK FCDO advises against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir except Jammu city, and the US State Department says ‘Do not travel’ to J&K except eastern Ladakh and Leh (advice checked 19 Oct 2026). Check the latest before you book.
