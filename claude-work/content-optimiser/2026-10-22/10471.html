What does a budget Himachal package include, and what costs extra?

The rates above include a private AC car with driver for every transfer and sightseeing day, 3-star/Deluxe hotels, and daily breakfast and dinner. Each package page lists its exact inclusions. Before you pay, check these extras:

  • Lunch and entry tickets (monuments, cable cars, museums) are usually paid on the spot.
  • Rohtang Pass needs an online vehicle permit, and in Manali the trip to Solang or Rohtang is done in a locally registered union taxi, not your tour car.
  • Adventure activities such as paragliding, rafting and skiing are paid separately to licensed operators.
  • Peak dates: if any night falls between 20 December and 5 January, during October, or between 15 March and 30 June, the rate is 25% higher.

How can you cut the cost of a Himachal trip?

  1. Start in Chandigarh, not Delhi. On the 5-night Shimla–Manali trip, starting in Chandigarh saves about ₹2,000 per person when four share the car (₹12,499 vs ₹14,499).
  2. Pick off-season dates. 1 Nov–19 Dec is the next cheap window: clear skies, cold nights and a chance of early snow up high.
  3. Fill the car. The car is the biggest fixed cost. Four people in one sedan pay less per head than two.
  4. Stay on Deluxe hotels and upgrade only the honeymoon or birthday night.
  5. Do fewer places. Shimla + Manali in 5 nights costs less than squeezing in Dharamshala too, because a hill driving day still costs a full car day.

Which places do these packages cover?

Shimla

Most packages give Shimla 1–2 nights: the Ridge, Mall Road, Christ Church and Jakhu Temple, plus a Kufri day. The Kalka–Shimla toy train (a UNESCO World Heritage line) is not running from Kalka at the moment. After monsoon damage near Parwanoo, trains run only on the Dharampur–Shimla stretch, so check before you plan around it.

Manali and Kullu

Manali usually gets 2–3 nights: Hadimba Temple, Vashisht, Old Manali and Solang Valley. Kullu is normally a stop on the way, for shawl factories and the Beas river front. Rafting runs in season only.

Rohtang Pass and the Atal Tunnel

Rohtang Pass (3,978 m / 13,050 ft) needs an online permit, is closed to tourists on Tuesdays, and usually shuts from mid-to-late November until about May. In winter the alternative is Solang Valley, or the 9.02 km Atal Tunnel to Sissu when the road is open.

Dharamshala, McLeodganj and Dalhousie

The quieter circuit: the Dalai Lama temple complex in McLeodganj, tea gardens near Dharamshala, and Khajjiar meadow near Dalhousie. Drives are shorter, which suits grandparents and small children.

Kasol and Parvati Valley (add-on)

Kasol is a popular add-on for younger travellers. Kheerganga is now a day trek, because camping at the Kheerganga top has not been allowed since July 2024.

How do you tell a good cheap package from a bad one?

  • Private car or shared? A low price often means a shared vehicle or overnight bus legs. Ask which one.
  • Named hotel category. "Standard accommodation" with no category is a warning sign.
  • Meals written down. Breakfast only, or breakfast and dinner? Dinner every night adds up over a week.
  • A registered operator. Ask for the HP Tourism registration number and GSTIN. Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). You can see it on our certificates page.
  • Clear weather rules. What happens if Rohtang or a road shuts? A good operator names the alternative before you book.

A note for Spiti and Kinnaur

Indian citizens need no permit for Spiti or Kinnaur. Foreign nationals need an Inner Line Permit for the border stretch between Kinnaur and Spiti. Since 15 October 2026 the Manali side (Gramphu–Batal) is closed for the winter, so Spiti is reachable only via Shimla and Kinnaur until it reopens.
