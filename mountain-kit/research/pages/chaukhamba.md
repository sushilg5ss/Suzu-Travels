## Season, access status and permits
- Open as of Feb 2026 (Uttarakhand list, "Chaukhamba group") — no closure found (checked 2026-11-04).
- Indians: UKMPS, no state fees. Foreigners: IMF permission and fee band US$1,000 (>7,000 m).
- West approach is inside Gangotri National Park (Gaumukh) — page wording: "park entry is arranged with the expedition permit"; no fee figure.
- Best: May–June and September–October (dataset).

## History and notable ascents
1938, 1939 attempts → 13 Jun 1952 first ascent (George & Russenberger, NE face) → 1995 NIM west face + first ascent of II → 1996 Garhwal Rifles SW face; Korean II → 2001 avalanche, two deaths → 2004 NIM attempt on III/IV (6,300 m) → 2022 Textor solo → Oct 2024 IAF rescue on III. Sources: Wikipedia, HJ 61, AAJ 2023, Statesman/The Week.

## Questions people ask
1. What is the height of Chaukhamba? — 7,138 m (23,419 ft) for Chaukhamba I.
2. Why is it called Chaukhamba? — "Four pillars": four summits on one ridge (7,138 / 7,070 / 6,995 / 6,854 m).
3. Where is Chaukhamba? — Garhwal Himalaya, Uttarakhand, west of Badrinath, at the head of the Gangotri glacier.
4. Which glacier starts from Chaukhamba? — The Gangotri glacier (source of the Bhagirathi) rises on its north-western slopes.
5. Who first climbed Chaukhamba? — Lucien George and Victor Russenberger of a French expedition, 13 June 1952.
6. Is Chaukhamba open for climbing? — Yes, the Chaukhamba group is on Uttarakhand's Feb 2026 open list.
7. How difficult is Chaukhamba? — Technical 7,000 m expedition with avalanche and rockfall danger; the north flank is described as very dangerous.
8. What happened on Chaukhamba III in 2024? — Two foreign women climbers were stranded ~6,015 m for three days and rescued by IAF helicopters on 6 Oct 2024.
