## Season, access status and permits
- Open as of Feb 2026 (Uttarakhand UKMPS list) — no 2026 closure found (checked 2026-11-04).
- Indians: no state fees (2026 waiver). Foreigners: IMF permission + IMF fee band US$700 (6,501–7,000 m), per-member charges apply.
- Darma valley is a border valley; page wording: "border-area permits are arranged with the expedition — confirm with the Pithoragarh district administration". No source fetched for the exact ILP rule — do not state one.
- Best: May–June and September–October (dataset).

## History and notable ascents
1929 Ruttledge recce → 1950–51 attempts (Heinrich Harrer & Thomas via Uttari Balati 1951) → 1972 I (ITBP) → 26 May 1973 II (ITBP, Mahendra Singh) → 1991 2nd and 3rd ascents of II (Army) → 1992 Indo-British: V first ascent, II new west spur, Venables IAF rescue → 1995 IV (NZ) → III unclimbed (2025 attempt to ~5,900 m). Sources: explorersweb, Wikipedia, HJ 48, NOLS.

## Questions people ask
1. What is the height of Panchachuli? — Panchachuli II, the highest, is 6,904 m (22,651 ft); the five peaks range 6,312–6,904 m.
2. Where is Panchachuli? — Pithoragarh district, Kumaon, Uttarakhand, between the Gori (Munsiyari) and Darma valleys.
3. Why is it called Panchachuli? — "Five hearths": legend says the Pandavas cooked their last meal here.
4. Can you see Panchachuli from Munsiyari? — Yes, the group faces Munsiyari across the Gori valley (Wikipedia/H: "near Munsiyari"); keep it general.
5. Who first climbed Panchachuli? — ITBP team led by Mahendra Singh, 26 May 1973 (Panchachuli II).
6. Has Panchachuli III been climbed? — No verified ascent; a 2025 attempt turned back near 5,900 m.
7. Is Panchachuli open for climbing in 2026? — Yes, it is on Uttarakhand's Feb 2026 list of 83 open peaks.
8. What is the Panchachuli base camp trek? — A trek in the Darma valley from Dugtu to the east-side base camp (~3,650 m) below the peaks; ask Suzu about Himachal treks/climbs.
9. How hard is it to climb Panchachuli? — Technical: fixed-rope ridges, icefalls and avalanche-prone faces; an expedition for experienced climbers.
