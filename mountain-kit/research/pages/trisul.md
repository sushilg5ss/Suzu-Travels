## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Trishul peak?** Trisul I is 7,120 m (23,360 ft); Trisul II is about 6,690 m and Trisul III about 6,000 m.
2. **Where is Trishul Parvat?** In Chamoli district, Uttarakhand, on the south-west rim of the Nanda Devi Sanctuary, about 15 km from Nanda Devi.
3. **Why is it called Trishul?** Its three summits look like a trident — the *trishula* of Lord Shiva.
4. **Who first climbed Trishul?** Tom Longstaff with Alexis and Henri Brocherel and the Gurkha Karbir, on 12 June 1907.
5. **Which was the first 7,000 m peak ever climbed?** Trisul I, in 1907 — not Kamet (Kamet, in 1931, was the first above 25,000 ft).
6. **Can you climb Trishul in 2026?** Yes — the Trishul group is on Uttarakhand's February 2026 list of 83 opened peaks; Indians apply on the state's UKMPS portal and foreigners through the IMF.
7. **How difficult is Trisul?** A long snow-and-glacier climb to 7,120 m; the classic north side is technically easy for its height, but the west-ridge route has fixed-rope sections and the upper slopes carry avalanche risk.
8. **What happened on Trishul in 2021?** On 1 October 2021 an avalanche at about 6,700 m hit an Indian Navy summit team; four Navy climbers were confirmed dead and others went missing.
9. **What is the best season to climb Trisul?** May–June and September–October.
10. **Where can I see Trishul?** It dominates the view on the Roopkund trek route in Chamoli.
