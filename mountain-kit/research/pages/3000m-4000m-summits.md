## Questions people ask
1. What is the height of Chandrashila? → About 3,690 m (12,106 ft); some tourism sites say 4,000 m.
2. How high is Kedarkantha? → 3,810 m (12,500 ft); sources also give 3,800 m.
3. Sandakphu height? → 3,636 m (11,929 ft), the highest point in West Bengal.
4. Churdhar height? → 3,647 m (11,965 ft), Sirmaur, Himachal.
5. Which is the easiest Himalayan summit in India? → Road-or-short-walk summits like Hatu Peak and Shikari Devi; among real treks, Nag Tibba and Chandrashila are short.
6. Do I need a permit? → No IMF climbing permit; sanctuary/national-park entry and local rules apply.
7. Can beginners climb a 4,000 m summit? → Patalsu, Chanshal and Pangarchulla are trek summits; Tatakooti and Sunset Peak are graded "moderate" with glacier/ridge sections.
8. What's the next step after a summit trek? → A 5,000 m trekking peak such as Friendship Peak (5,289 m), with a registered Himachal outfitter.
