## Season, access status and permits (as of 23 Oct 2026)
- Indians: apply on UKMPS; no state fees in 2026; IMF rules for Indian teams (leader + AMC/BMC mix) still apply (rules-records §1.11).
- Foreigners: IMF permission ≥ 90 days ahead, LO, insurance, IMF fee band only; PAP/ILP in border valleys.
- Closed: Nanda Devi main peak. Restricted: Adi Kailash, Om Parvat. Gangotri NP 150/day cap on the Gaumukh–Tapovan approach.

## Questions people ask
1. **Which is the highest peak in Uttarakhand?** Nanda Devi, 7,816 m (some lists 7,817 m) — also the highest mountain wholly in India. It has been closed to climbers since 1983.
2. **Which is the second-highest peak in Uttarakhand?** Kamet, 7,756 m, in Chamoli district.
3. **How many peaks are there in Uttarakhand?** Wikipedia lists about 200 peaks above 6,000 m; our dataset tracks 60 notable peaks, 12 of them above 7,000 m.
4. **How many peaks did Uttarakhand open in 2026?** 83 peaks between 5,700 m and 7,756 m, announced on 3–4 February 2026.
5. **Do Indian climbers pay fees in Uttarakhand?** No state expedition fees in 2026 — the state now covers them. Foreign teams pay only IMF charges.
6. **Is Nanda Devi open for climbing?** No. Only Nanda Devi East (7,434 m) is on the 2026 open list.
7. **What is the best season for mountaineering in Uttarakhand?** May–June and September–October; avoid the July–August monsoon.
8. **Which was the first 7,000 m peak ever climbed?** Trisul I in Uttarakhand, in 1907.
9. **Where can beginners start in Uttarakhand?** Hiking summits like Kedarkantha (3,810 m) and Pangarchulla (4,575 m) are treks, not climbs; for a first guided 5,000–6,000 m summit, Himachal's Friendship Peak or Yunam are the usual next step.
