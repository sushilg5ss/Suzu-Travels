## Questions people ask
1. What are crampons? Metal frames with spikes that strap or clip onto boots so you can walk and climb on hard snow and ice.
2. How many points do crampons have? Usually 10–12 (some up to 14); the two front points let you climb steep slopes face-in.
3. Do crampons fit any boot? Strap crampons fit almost any boot; hybrid needs a heel welt; step-in needs welts front and back. Stiffness must match: crampons no stiffer than the boot.
4. What do B1, B2 and B3 mean? Boot stiffness: B1 flexible (trekking), B2 semi-rigid (most mountaineering), B3 fully rigid (technical ice).
5. Microspikes or crampons — which do I need? Microspikes for icy trails and packed snow; crampons for snow peaks, glaciers and steep slopes.
6. Do I need crampons for Friendship Peak? Yes — the top is steep snow; crampons, ice axe and a rope are used.
7. Can I rent crampons in Manali? Rental is common in Manali/Solang and outfitters often lend them; try them on your own boots first.
8. Can I use trekking shoes with crampons? Only flexible C1/strap crampons, and only on easy terrain; snow peaks need B2-type boots.
