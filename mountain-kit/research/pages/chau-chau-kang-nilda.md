## History and notable ascents
- 1939 J.O.M. Roberts (first; "first mountaineer to visit Spiti" per Wikipedia).
- 1955 Trevor Braham & Peter Holmes (second).
- 1966, 1981 Indian expeditions.
- AAJ 1989: Ordnance Factories Mountaineers, SW ridge, summit 5 Sep (team believed 3rd ascent — conflicts with Wikipedia's 1966/1981 list; do not number ascents beyond the second).
- AAJ 1991: Calcutta team, first ascent of the SE ridge, summit 15 Sep; summit party included Yana Banerjee-Bey and Kalpana Ghosh.

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Chau Chau Kang Nilda?** 6,303 m (20,679 ft).
2. **What does Chau Chau Kang Nilda mean?** It is known as "blue moon in the sky".
3. **Where is CCKN?** In Spiti, 13 km north-east of Kaza; Langza village sits at its base.
4. **Who first climbed Chau Chau Kang Nilda?** J.O.M. Roberts in 1939.
5. **How difficult is CCKN?** A moderate glaciated 6,000er: snow, scree and two high camps; crampon and rope skills needed, plus good acclimatisation.
6. **Best time to climb CCKN?** June to September.
7. **Can foreigners climb CCKN?** Yes, with IMF permission (US$500 base fee band for two, Oct 2026) and a liaison officer.
8. **How many days does the CCKN expedition take?** Base camp to summit took about six days on a documented ascent; allow about two weeks from Manali.
9. **Can you see CCKN from Langza?** Yes — Langza sits at its base.
