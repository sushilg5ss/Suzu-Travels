## Questions people ask
1. How high is Yunam Peak? 6,111 m (20,049 ft).
2. Where is Yunam Peak? In Lahaul, Himachal Pradesh, near Baralacha La on the Manali–Leh highway; the climb starts at the Bharatpur tent camp.
3. Is Yunam Peak a trek or an expedition? A non-technical trekking peak, but a real 6,000 m summit day: steep scree and snow, and very thin air.
4. Is Yunam the easiest 6,000 m peak in India? It is one of the most accessible because the road reaches about 4,700 m — which is also its main risk (fast altitude gain). Do not call it "the easiest".
5. How many days? About 8 days from Manali, with two acclimatisation days.
6. Best time? July–September (the season runs June–September).
7. Do I need a mountaineering course? Usually not; prior high-altitude trekking and good fitness are expected.
8. Do foreigners need a permit? Yes — IMF permission (apply ≥ 90 days ahead); base fee US$500 for two, plus per-member and LO charges (as of Oct 2026).
9. Who first climbed Yunam? No documented first ascent is known.

- A first-ascent year or party.
- The ETV Bharat "world's second-youngest" record claim (unverified per the article itself); do not name the child.
- The Tribune "20,300 feet" figure (contradicts the dataset height).
- That it is an IMF trekking peak, or that a protected-area permit is or is not needed (not verified for the Bharatpur route).

- Any price.
