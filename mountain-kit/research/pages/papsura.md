## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Papsura?** 6,451 m (21,165 ft); a government list gives 6,440 m.
2. **Why is Papsura called the Peak of Evil?** Local belief pairs it with Dharamsura, the "Peak of Good": whichever looks higher shows whether good or evil prevails.
3. **Is Papsura higher than Dharamsura?** Yes, by survey — 6,451 m vs 6,446 m, a few metres.
4. **Who first climbed Papsura?** Geoffrey Hill and Colin Pritchard of a British expedition, 3 June 1967.
5. **Where is Papsura?** At the head of the Tos glacier above Tosh in the Parvati valley, Kullu, on the Kullu–Lahaul divide.
6. **How do you reach Papsura base camp?** From Barshaini/Tosh up the Tos nala, about four days to the Kuta Thach base camp (~4,260 m).
7. **Can foreigners climb Papsura?** Yes — it is on the government list of peaks open to foreigners; IMF permission, a liaison officer and the US$500 base fee apply.
8. **How difficult is Papsura?** Technical: avalanche-prone couloirs, an icefall and steep rock — for experienced alpinists.
9. **Has anyone skied Papsura?** Yes — the first ski descent of the west couloir was in 2017.
10. **Best season?** Mid-May to June (most summits were in early June); September is possible.
