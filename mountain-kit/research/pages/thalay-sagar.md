## Season, access status and permits   (as of Nov 2026)
- **Season:** recorded summits fall in June (24 Jun 1979, 6 Jun 1992) and September (13 Sep 1984, Sept 1997, 9 Sep 2006, 17 Sep 2016, 23 Sep, Spanish AAJ 1991, 26 Sep, Siegrist HJ 61). Dataset "May–Jun & Sep–Oct" is consistent. The Kedartal trek's best season is May–Jun and Sep–Oct ([13]).
- **Gangotri National Park:** reopened 1 Apr 2026 and is open until 30 Nov. Permits come through the online Pathik portal or the Uttarkashi Forest Office. Kedartal is named among routes that were still partly snow-covered at reopening (Outlook Traveller, 4 Apr 2026 [3]). A Forest Department permit is mandatory, and a registered guide is required for Tapovan/Nelong. Winter closure runs Nov–Apr (Incredible India [17]). Fees for foreigners weren't found.
- **Uttarakhand 2026 rules:** on 4 Feb 2026 the state opened 83 peaks (5,700–7,756 m) via UKMPS. Indians pay no government expedition fees; foreigners pay IMF fees only (rules-records §1.5). **We could not confirm Thalay Sagar is on the 83-peak list**: none of the named-peak reports we read include it (OdishaBytes [20]; WanderOn summary), and the full list wasn't retrievable (thenewsmill/ETV Bharat fetch blocked today). Page wording: "Uttarakhand opened 83 peaks in Feb 2026. Check UKMPS/IMF that Thalay Sagar is on the current list."
- **Kedartal avalanche, Oct–Nov 2026 (dated, neutral):**
  - A group of 13 (7 trekkers, 6 support staff) left Gangotri on **27 Oct 2026** for a 3-day Kedartal trek, due back 29 Oct. Snowfall blocked the route.
  - Four support staff (3 porters, a cook) were rescued on 31 Oct. Avalanches were reported at about 10–12 points on an ~11–12 km stretch (Bhojkharak–Kedarkharak).
  - A 10-member SDRF + NIM team was airlifted in on 2 Nov. NDRF, the Forest Department and the district administration were also involved (Outlook Traveller, 3 Nov [21]).
  - By **6 Nov: seven dead (5 men, 2 women), one missing**. Bodies were flown out via the Matli helipad (ThePrint/PTI, 6 Nov [22]).
  - **No closure order for the Kedartal route was found** in these reports (search 13 Nov 2026). The national park closes for winter on 30 Nov anyway.
  - The ETV Bharat 3 Nov report named in the brief wasn't found by search; Outlook Traveller 3 Nov is used instead.
  - The kedar-dome pack's summary matches these facts.
  - Page: one short dated safety line, no names of victims, no blame.

## History and notable ascents
- 1979, 24 Jun: first ascent, Kligfield / Thackray / Thexton (Anglo-American), NW couloir and ridge [5] [7].
- 1983: **Polish–Norwegian** team (Andrzej Czok, Hans-Christian Doseth, Frode Guldal, Håvard Nesheim, Janusz Skorek) makes the first ascent of the **NE Pillar/Ridge** [8] [11]. Planetmountain spells the names "Andrez", "Doeseth" and "Guidal", so check the spelling before printing.
- 1984, 13 Sep: Michael Kennedy and Randy Trover, NE Pillar in alpine style, BC Kedar Tal 4,746 m [8].
- 1987: Spanish attempt on the NE line fails [4].
- AAJ 1991: Spanish (La Rioja) team. Corominas and Palacios summit on 23 Sep via the "American route on the west ridge", reported as the "sixth" ascent of the mountain [6].
- 1991 climbs: the Hungarians make the "first ascent of the north face" (Piolets d'Or archive, not nominated) [9].
- 1992, 6 Jun: Keith Milne and Tom Prentice, new line from the south joining the 1979 arête [12a].
- 1997, Sept: Lindblade and Whimp, **north face direct ("Australian route")** through the shale band. **Piolet d'Or 1999** [9].
- 1998, Sept: Korean north-face team. Choi Seung-chul, Kim Hyung-jin and Shin Sang-man died near the summit (AJ 1999: a cornice broke on 28 Sep). Their bodies were found roped together at the foot of the face [12b] [23].
- 2003: "One Way Ticket" (Benoist and Glairon-Rappaz). **Nominated** for the 2004 Piolets d'Or, not awarded [9] [11].
- 2004 (probable; the article doesn't give the year, HJ 61 = 2005 volume): Siegrist, Burdet, Senf and Weber, "Harvest Moon", NE face/pillar, summit 26 Sep [4].
- 2006, 9 Sep: Koreans Gu Eun-su and Yu Sang-beom, "Period For Friends" (central couloir, new finish), named for the 1998 dead [12b].
- 2016, 17 Sep: Russian "Moveable Feast", N buttress. **Honoured at the Piolets d'Or 2017** [10] [11].
- 2023: UK team (Ball, Pescod, Buckingham) tries the SE ridge; no summit [24].
- 2025–26 ascents: none found (search 13 Nov 2026).

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Thalay Sagar?** 6,904 m (22,651 ft).
2. **Where is Thalay Sagar?** In the Gangotri group of Garhwal, Uttarakhand, about 10 km SW of Gaumukh, above the lake Kedartal on the Uttarkashi–Tehri border.
3. **Who first climbed Thalay Sagar?** Roy Kligfield, John Thackray and Pete Thexton, on 24 June 1979, by the NW couloir and ridge.
4. **Why is Thalay Sagar so hard?** It is steep on all sides, and a band of black, crumbly shale guards the summit.
5. **Which Thalay Sagar climbs won the Piolet d'Or?** The 1997 Australian north face direct (Lindblade & Whimp), awarded in 1999. The 2016 Russian "Moveable Feast" was honoured in 2017.
6. **Where is Thalay Sagar base camp?** At Kedartal, about 4,750 m, three days' walk from Gangotri via Bhoj Kharak and Kedar Kharak. Advanced base is about 5,300 m.
7. **Is Kedartal the same as Thalay Sagar?** No. Kedartal is the glacial lake at its foot, and a popular trek in its own right (link to the Adventure page).
8. **Has anyone died on Thalay Sagar?** Yes. Three Korean climbers died near the summit in Sept 1998.
9. **What happened on the Kedartal trek in 2026?** Snow and avalanches trapped a 13-member trek group from 27 Oct. By 6 Nov, seven had died and one was missing (ThePrint/PTI).
10. **Can foreigners climb Thalay Sagar?** Only with IMF permission (6,501–7,000 m band: US$700 base plus US$500 LO equipment and per-member charges) and a Gangotri National Park permit. Confirm on UKMPS/IMF that the peak is on the 2026 open list.
