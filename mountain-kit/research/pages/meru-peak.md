## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Meru Peak?** Meru has three main summits: Meru South 6,660 m (the highest), Meru North 6,450 m and Meru Central 6,310 m, the Shark's Fin.
2. **Where is Meru Peak?** In the Gangotri group of Uttarakhand, between Shivling and Thalay Sagar, above Tapovan and the Gangotri Glacier.
3. **What is the Shark's Fin?** The north-east (east) pillar of Meru Central — a ~1,400 m granite prow, first climbed by Conrad Anker, Jimmy Chin and Renan Ozturk on 2 Oct 2011.
4. **Who first climbed Meru?** Japanese teams in 1980 (Meru North 4 Oct, Meru South 10 Oct); Meru Central was first climbed solo by Valery Babanov in 2001.
5. **Is the film Meru a true story?** Yes — the 2015 documentary follows the 2008 attempt and 2011 ascent, filmed by Chin and Ozturk.
6. **Has an Indian team climbed Meru?** Yes — Meru North in 1986 (Indo-Swedish team) and Meru South on 2 Sep 2023 by a new west-face route (team led by Umesh Zirpe).
7. **How hard is Meru?** All summits are expert-only technical climbs; the Shark's Fin is graded VII 5.10 A4 M6 WI5 and took over 20 attempts.
8. **Can I trek to Meru base camp?** The Gaumukh–Tapovan trail reaches the base (~4,300 m) with a Gangotri National Park permit (150 visitors a day; park open Apr–Nov).
9. **Is Meru Peak the same as Mount Meru in Tanzania?** No — Mount Meru (Tanzania) is a different mountain; India's Meru is in the Garhwal Himalaya.
