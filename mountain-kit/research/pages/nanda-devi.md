## Route and camps   (historic only — the peak is closed; **no profile video**, no route table with altitudes)
- 1936 route: through the Rishi Gorge into the sanctuary, then the **south ridge ("Coxcomb")**. 1976: north-west buttress. Intermediate camp altitudes are not reliably published — do not show any.
- Today a climber's legal option in the massif is **Nanda Devi East** from the Munsiyari / Milam side (IMF + UKMPS).

## Season, access status and permits
- **Closed since 1983 — checked 14 Oct 2026** (Wikipedia; dataset; no order in news). Reopening discussed July 2025, no decision found.

## Questions people ask
1. **What is the height of Nanda Devi?** 7,816 m (25,643 ft); some sources say 7,817 m.
2. **Is Nanda Devi the highest peak in India?** It is the highest wholly inside India and India's second-highest after Kangchenjunga (on the Sikkim–Nepal border).
3. **Can you climb Nanda Devi?** No. The main peak and inner sanctuary have been closed since 1983; a 2025 reopening proposal had no decision as of Oct 2026. Nanda Devi East (7,434 m) is open via IMF/UKMPS.
4. **Who first climbed Nanda Devi?** Bill Tilman and Noel Odell, 29 Aug 1936.
5. **Why is Nanda Devi closed?** Environmental damage in the sanctuary and its religious significance (1983 closure); it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
6. **What is the Nanda Devi plutonium story?** A 1965 CIA–Indian sensor powered by plutonium was lost near Camp IV and never recovered.
7. **What happened to Nanda Devi Unsoeld?** She died on the 1976 Indo-American expedition after illness at high altitude.
8. **Where is Nanda Devi?** Chamoli district, Uttarakhand, Garhwal Himalaya.
9. **What is the Nanda Devi Raj Jat?** A pilgrimage held about every 12 years; the 2026 yatra was postponed to 2027.
