## Questions people ask
1. **Who was the first Indian to climb Everest?** Avtar Singh Cheema and Nawang Gombu, on 20 May 1965 (1965 Indian expedition led by M.S. Kohli).
2. **Who was the first Indian woman on Everest?** Bachendri Pal, 23 May 1984.
3. **Which was the first 7,000 m peak ever climbed?** Trisul I (7,120 m), in 1907 — not Kamet (Kamet 1931 was the first above 25,000 ft).
4. **Who are the famous Indian mountaineers?** E.g. Nawang Gombu, Avtar Singh Cheema, Kohli, Gurdial Singh, Bachendri Pal, Santosh Yadav, Arunima Sinha, Malavath Purna, Anshu Jamsenpa, Baljeet Kaur, Arjun Vajpai (each with the verified record above).
5. **Who is the youngest Indian to climb Everest?** Only the approved wording: Malavath Purna is the youngest female summiter (13 y 11 m, 2014); "Arjun Vajpai was the youngest Indian at the time (2010)". Do not name an overall youngest male.
6. **Who first climbed Nanda Devi?** Tilman and Odell, 29 Aug 1936 (link nanda-devi).
7. **Has Kangchenjunga been climbed from India?** Its second ascent (1977, Indian Army) used the NE spur from Sikkim; climbing it from India is banned today (Sikkim 70/HOME/2001).
8. **What was the most recent first ascent of a major Indian peak?** Kangto (7,090 m), Arunachal's highest point, by the Indian Army in Nov–Dec 2025.
9. **Which Himachal climber holds records?** Baljeet Kaur of Solan — first Indian woman with seven 8,000 m peaks.
