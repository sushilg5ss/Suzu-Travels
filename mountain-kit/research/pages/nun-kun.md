## History and notable ascents
1906 Pinnacle Peak (Workman, claimed) → 1913 Kun (Piacenza) → 28 Aug 1953 Nun (Vittoz & Kogan, Bernard Pierre's team; women's altitude record) → 1976 NW face (Czech) → 1981 east ridge (British) → 2021 Army on Kun → 2022/2023 IAF rescues. Sources: Wikipedia (Nun Kun, Claude Kogan), AAJ 1954, Tribune.

## Questions people ask
1. What is the height of Nun Kun? — Nun 7,135 m and Kun 7,077 m, the two highest tops of the massif.
2. Where is Nun Kun? — On the J&K–Ladakh boundary above the Suru valley, Kargil district, ~250 km east of Srinagar.
3. Is Nun Kun in Ladakh or Kashmir? — It straddles the boundary; Nun is counted as J&K's highest point, Kun lies in Ladakh (dataset).
4. Who first climbed Nun? — Pierre Vittoz and Claude Kogan, 28 August 1953, by the west ridge.
5. Who first climbed Kun? — Italian Mario Piacenza, 1913.
6. How difficult is Mount Nun? — A technical 7,000 m snow-and-ice expedition with avalanche terrain; usually after prior 6,000 m+ experience.
7. Best time to climb Nun? — June to September.
8. How do you reach Nun Kun? — Kargil → Sankoo → Panikhar → Tangol road-head (~2 hours), then to base camp.
9. What permit is needed? — IMF permission and the >7,000 m fee band (US$1,000 base, as of Oct 2026).
