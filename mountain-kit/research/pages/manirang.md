## Season, access status and permits (as of 30 Oct 2026)
- Open; climbed by government teams in 2021 and 2024; no closure found (search 30 Oct 2026).
- Approach from Manali via Kunzum to Kaza and Mane needs no PAP; coming up the Kinnaur side (Pooh block) or exiting over the pass to Ropa does → foreigners need the PAP. Indians: no PAP.
- Foreigners also need IMF permission (≥ 90 days ahead), LO, insurance; HP rules: registered operator/guides, police registration.

## History and notable ascents
- 1952 Graaff party (first) → 1988 Indian Army Para Regt (2nd) → 10 Jul 1994 Nunn & Muni (3rd) → 15 Aug 2021 IAF all-women team → 16 Jun 2024 NDRF "Vijay".
- 14 Jun 1986: Kapadia/Contractor party climbed **Manirang South (5,888 m)**, not the main summit ~2 km away [5].

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Manirang?** 6,593 m (21,631 ft); some reports say 21,625 ft.
2. **Where is Manirang?** On the Spiti–Kinnaur border in Himachal Pradesh, above Mane village in Spiti.
3. **Who first climbed Manirang?** Dr J. de V. Graaff's party on 12 September 1952.
4. **What is the Manirang Pass?** A 5,550 m pass below the peak, once the trade route between Spiti and Kinnaur's Ropa valley; it is now a trekking crossing.
5. **Is Manirang difficult?** Mostly non-technical glacier and ridge climbing, but high and long, with brittle ice and loose rock near the top.
6. **Best time to climb Manirang?** June to September.
7. **Do foreigners need a permit?** IMF permission (US$700 base for two, 6,501–7,000 m band) plus a Protected Area Permit if the route enters Kinnaur's Pooh block (Ropa side).
8. **Which teams have climbed it recently?** An all-women IAF team in Aug 2021 and the NDRF "Vijay" team in Jun 2024.
