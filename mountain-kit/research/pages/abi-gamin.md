## Questions people ask
1. How high is Abi Gamin? — 7,355 m (24,131 ft).
2. Where is Abi Gamin? — In Chamoli, Uttarakhand, about 2 km north-east of Kamet on the Tibet border.
3. Who first climbed Abi Gamin? — Dittert, Tissières and Chevalley of an Anglo-Swiss team, 22 August 1950.
4. Is Abi Gamin part of Kamet? — It is a separate summit linked to Kamet by Meade's Col; its prominence is only 217 m.
5. How is Abi Gamin climbed? — The 1950 team used the NE ridge from Tibet; later Indian teams went via Meade's Col from the Kamet side.
6. Who was the first Indian on Abi Gamin? — N.D. Jayal's 1953 team (Jayal, Pemba, Pooran Singh) made the second ascent.
7. What is Meade's Col? — The ~7,100–7,200 m saddle between Kamet and Abi Gamin, named after C.F. Meade (reached 1913).
8. What does "Ibi Gamin" mean? — Only the alternative name is sourced; don't give a translation.
