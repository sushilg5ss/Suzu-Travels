## Season, access status and permits
- Course seasons follow the climbing seasons: Uttarakhand/Himachal batches pre-monsoon (Mar–Jun) and post-monsoon (Sep–Nov); J&K summer (May–Sep).
- No permit is needed by the trainee for the course itself; the institute runs the field phase.

## History and notable ascents
- ABVIMAS founded 1961 (WHMI), renamed 2008. NIMAS 2013. NIM: 2022 avalanche (29 dead) — state factually, once, in a safety context only if needed.

## Questions people ask
1. Which is the best mountaineering institute in India? There is no official ranking; the five government institutes (NIM, HMI, ABVIMAS, JIM&WS, NIMAS) run the recognised BMC/AMC syllabus. Choose by batch month, age limit and location.
2. How long is a basic mountaineering course? 24–28 days: ABVIMAS 26, NIM and HMI 28, JIM&WS 24.
3. What is the age limit for BMC? Roughly 16–45 depending on the institute and batch (NIM 16–40 with some 20–55 batches; ABVIMAS 16–45; HMI 17–45; JIM&WS 17–40).
4. Can foreigners do a BMC in India? Yes at ABVIMAS (Indians and foreign nationals) and NIM (foreign fee listed in US$); check each programme.
5. How do I get into AMC? You need an 'A' grade in the BMC.
6. Do I need a BMC to climb a peak? Not for most guided trekking-peak climbs; Indian teams applying for an IMF expedition need course-qualified members (50% AMC, rest BMC).
7. How fit do I need to be? NIM, for example, tests a 10 km walk with a 10 kg load.
8. Is there a mountaineering institute in Himachal? Yes — ABVIMAS in Manali, founded 1961.
