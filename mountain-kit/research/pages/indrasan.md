## Season, access status and permits (as of 19 Oct 2026)
- Open peak, no closure found (search 19 Oct 2026). Pre-monsoon (May–Jun) and post-monsoon (Sep–Oct).
- Foreigners: IMF ≥ 90 days ahead, LO, insurance. No PAP (Kullu, not border belt).
- The Kangra trek-registration / winter-ban orders do **not** cover this area (Kullu district) — don't mention them.

## History and notable ascents
- 1958 Pettigrew party: failed (storms).
- 1962 first ascent (13 Oct), Kyoto University: Tomita & Miyaki; Deo Tibba climbed by Omori & Tanaka the same day.
- 1971 West Ridge party (A.J. 1972).
- 1973 British Army Mountaineering Association, East Ridge; summit Owens, Anderson, Eskell.

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Indrasan?** 6,220 m in our dataset; most sources give 6,221 m.
2. **Where is Indrasan?** In the Pir Panjal above Manali, Kullu district, right next to Deo Tibba.
3. **Why is Indrasan so hard?** Icefalls, steep ice and rock bands on every side; it is called the hardest peak of the Pir Panjal.
4. **Who first climbed Indrasan?** Kojiro Tomita and Yasumasa Miyaki of the Kyoto University Alpine Club, 13 Oct 1962.
5. **What does Indrasan mean?** The seat (aasana) of Lord Indra.
6. **Indrasan vs Deo Tibba?** Same approach and col; Deo Tibba (6,001 m) is a snow dome, Indrasan a technical rock-and-ice summit.
7. **Best time to climb Indrasan?** May–June and September–October.
8. **Can beginners climb Indrasan?** No — it needs earlier technical climbs (e.g. Deo Tibba, Hanuman Tibba) and solid rope/ice skills.
9. **Can foreigners climb Indrasan?** Yes, with IMF permission (US$500 base band for two, plus LO hire, as of Oct 2026).
