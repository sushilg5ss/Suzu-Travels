## History and notable ascents
- 1974 (4 Jun) first ascent — Sandhu/Bonington, SE face–E ridge via Shipton's Col and the Kalanka col.
- 1976 **West Wall** — Peter Boardman & Joe Tasker, big-wall style, 25+ days (autumn); *The Shining Mountain*.
- 1976 **SW ridge** — Japanese Alpine Club team (Naoki Toda and others), 33 days.
- 1978 **direct South Face** — Wojciech Kurtyka, Krzysztof Żurek, Alex MacIntyre, John Porter; summit 27 Sep.
- 1981 **South Ridge** — Ugo Manera & Lino Castiglia.
- 1997 **North Face** first ascent — Andy Cave & Brendan Murphy summited 1 Jun (ED, ~1,300–1,600 m); Murphy killed by an avalanche on the descent 3 Jun; Mick Fowler & Steve Sustad reached the east ridge (MEF 97/14; Wikipedia).
- 1998 "Lightning Route", north face — Carlos Buhler with a Russian team (VII 5.9 A4 WI4, 1,580 m).
- 2006 two Mexican climbers (Andrés Delgado, Alfonso de la Parra) summited by a new route and vanished in a storm on the descent.
- 2018 (11–13 May) GMHM Chamonix (Léo Billon, Sébastien Moatti, Sébastien Ratel) — north face linking the 1996/97 lines to the north fore-summit, alpine style, 3 days (montagna.tv).
- 2022 (25 Apr–2 May) **first repeat of the West Wall** after 46 years — Matthew Scholes, Kim Ladiges (Australia), Daniel Joll (NZ), alpine style with portaledges, ~20 new pitches (ExplorersWeb; planetmountain).

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Changabang?** 6,864 m (22,520 ft).
2. **Where is Changabang?** In the Garhwal Himalaya, Chamoli district, Uttarakhand — on the north-east rim of the Nanda Devi Sanctuary, beside Kalanka.
3. **What does Changabang mean?** Roughly "shining mountain", for its pale granite.
4. **Who first climbed Changabang?** An Indo-British team led by Balwant Sandhu and Chris Bonington, on 4 June 1974.
5. **What is the Changabang West Wall?** A 1,600 m granite face first climbed by Peter Boardman and Joe Tasker in 1976 over 25+ days; it was not repeated until 2022.
6. **Why is Changabang so hard?** Every route is steep granite, ice and mixed ground at high altitude; there is no walk-up — even the 1974 line needed a night climb and a knife-edge ridge.
7. **Can you climb Changabang in 2026?** Yes, with permission: it is on Uttarakhand's February 2026 list of 83 open peaks (UKMPS for Indians, IMF for foreign teams). Only very experienced alpinists attempt it.
8. **How do climbers reach Changabang now?** From Joshimath to Jumma, then on foot via Dunagiri village to a base camp near 4,550 m on the Bagini glacier; the old Rishi Gorge route enters the Nanda Devi Sanctuary, closed since 1983.
9. **What is the IMF fee for Changabang?** 6,501–7,000 m band: US$700 for two + US$500 LO equipment hire + per-member charges (Oct 2026).
