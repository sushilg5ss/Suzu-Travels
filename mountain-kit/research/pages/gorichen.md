## Season, access status and permits
- Status (as of Nov 2026): climbed; restricted border area. Civilian teams need IMF permission plus special defence clearance (MoD / Military Operations) because the peak is near the LAC [3][5]. Not a peak Suzu sells. Foreigners: treat as not open ("restricted" in dataset).
- IMF fee band for reference only: 6,001–6,500 m → US$500 per team of two (foreigners; IMF page 1 Oct 2026) — but do not imply foreigners can get it.

## History and notable ascents
- 1913 Bailey–Morshead and 1939 Tilman passed nearby [1].
- 1980s: 19 Kumaon trains here before Siachen [1].
- HMI Darjeeling (AAJ 1995): first ascent of Gorichen East (N face, 28 Oct), new east-ridge route on Gorichen II (30 Oct), first ascent of P 6020 west of Kangto [2].
- 2016: Arunachal government team (Pemba Tshering) [3].
- 21 Oct 2024: MAK, first civilian team on Gorichen Main [1][3].
- 25 Sep 2025: NIMAS team on Gorichen East [4][5].

## Questions people ask
1. How high is Gorichen peak? — 6,488 m (Gorichen Main/II); some sources say 6,530 m and a 2024 GPS read 6,468 m.
2. Where is Gorichen peak? — Western Arunachal Pradesh, reached from Jang and Mago on the Tawang road; it is seen from the Tawang area.
3. Can I see Gorichen from Tawang? — Yes, it is a popular sightseeing view (holidify SERP) — say "visible from the Tawang region" only.
4. Has Gorichen been climbed? — Yes: army and HMI teams, an Arunachal government team in 2016, and the first civilian team on 21 Oct 2024.
5. Can civilians/foreigners climb Gorichen? — Indian civilian teams have, with IMF permission and special defence clearance; foreigners: restricted.
6. What does "Sa-Nga Phu" mean? — "Abode of the Guardian Deity"; the peak is held sacred locally.
7. How many days does a Gorichen climb take? — The 2024 team needed 10 days from Mago to the summit; the 2025 Gorichen East trip lasted 25 days.
8. What is the Gorichen trek? — Operator name for trekking towards the Gorichen base area (not a summit); out of our scope — one line, no link.
