## Season, access status and permits (as of 19 Oct 2026)
- Open peak, no closure found (search 19 Oct 2026). Lahaul rain shadow; climbing Jun–Sep/Oct.
- Foreigners: IMF permission ≥ 90 days ahead, LO, insurance (rules-records §1.2). No protected-area permit needed for Darcha/Milang (not in the Kinnaur/Spiti border belt) — do **not** claim a PAP.
- HP: registered operator and guides; police registration before the trek.

## History and notable ascents
- 1939 first ascent (7 Sep): Krenek, Johnson, Kolb — south ridge; Hilda Richmond killed by stonefall at base.
- 1977 Japanese ascent (10 Oct; leader Y. Watanabe).
- 1979 RAF–IAF expedition: summit 31 Aug by Ted Rogers; HJ says it was only the second known ascent at the time (conflicts with the 1977 HJ list → do not number ascents).
- 1979 Pune Mountaineers ladies' expedition (leader Usha Bhide) listed as climbed.
- 1989 first Indian ascent (22 Aug): Goutam Dutta, R.S. Negi (Wikipedia) — conflicts with the 1979 listings of Indian teams; write "Wikipedia records the first Indian ascent as 1989" or omit.

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Mulkila?** 6,517 m (21,381 ft); some sources give 6,507–6,518 m.
2. **Where is Mulkila?** In Lahaul, Himachal Pradesh, at the head of the Milang Glacier between the Chandra and Bhaga rivers; Darcha is the road-head.
3. **What is the highest peak in Lahaul?** Mulkila (M4), 6,517 m.
4. **Who first climbed Mulkila?** Ludwig Krenek, Robey Johnson and Fritz Kolb, 7 Sep 1939.
5. **How difficult is Mulkila?** Advanced: crevassed glacier, an icefall, steep ice and loose rock on the summit ridge. Not a first 6,000er.
6. **What does Mulkila mean?** It is translated as "Silver God".
7. **What is M4?** The survey label of Mulkila (Mulkila IV) in the group of "M" peaks above the Milang Glacier.
8. **Best time to climb Mulkila?** June to September (into early October); Lahaul is in the rain shadow.
9. **Can foreigners climb Mulkila?** Yes, with IMF permission (US$700 base fee band for two, plus LO hire, as of Oct 2026).
