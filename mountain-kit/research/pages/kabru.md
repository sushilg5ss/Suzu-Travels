## Questions people ask
1. How high is Kabru? — The main summit is 7,412 m; the north/central top 7,338 m, Kabru South 7,318 m.
2. What is Kabru North? — Most sources use it for the 7,338 m central top that C.R. Cooke climbed solo in 1935; some lists attach the 7,412 m main height to the name.
3. Who first climbed Kabru? — Cooke reached the north top on 18 Nov 1935; W.W. Graham's 1883 claim is disputed.
4. Is Kabru the southernmost 7,000 m peak? — Yes, per Wikipedia it is the southernmost 7,000 m peak in the world.
5. Where is Kabru? — On the Sikkim–Nepal border (Singalila ridge), south of Kangchenjunga; seen from Dzongri and Darjeeling.
6. Can you climb Kabru from Sikkim? — It is restricted and treated as sacred; the 2023 and 2025 ascents came from Nepal.
7. What is Kabru Dome? — A ~6,600 m top of the massif, declared holy by the Sikkim government (HJ 52).
8. Who climbed Kabru in 2025? — Nives Meroi, Romano Benet and Peter Hámor, alpine style from the Yalung glacier, 4 May 2025.
