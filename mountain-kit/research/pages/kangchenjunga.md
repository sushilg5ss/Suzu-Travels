## History and notable ascents
- 1852/1856: thought highest, then ranked 3rd.
- 1929, 1931: German attempts on the NE spur fail.
- **25 May 1955**: Brown & Band, stop short of the top (Chogyal promise).
- **31 May 1977**: Indian Army, NE spur from Sikkim, leader Col. N. Kumar; Prem Chand first Indian on top.
- 11 Jan 1986: first winter ascent (Kukuczka & Wielicki).
- 2000/2001: Sikkim ban. 2019: IMF refuses Sikkim sacred-peak permits.
- May 2025: Indian Army ascents from Nepal → Sikkim protest.

## Questions people ask
1. **What is the height of Kangchenjunga?** 8,586 m (28,169 ft).
2. **Is Kangchenjunga the highest peak in India?** It is the highest on ground India administers; India's map also shows K2 (8,611 m) in Ladakh, but K2 stands in Pakistan-administered Gilgit-Baltistan.
3. **Can you climb Kangchenjunga from India?** No — Sikkim has banned it as a sacred peak (since 2000/2001); expeditions go via Nepal.
4. **Who first climbed Kangchenjunga?** Joe Brown and George Band, 25 May 1955 — they stopped a few metres short of the top.
5. **Why do climbers stop below the summit?** A 1955 promise to the Chogyal of Sikkim to leave the top untouched; the custom continues.
6. **Which Indian first climbed Kangchenjunga?** Maj. Prem Chand, Indian Army expedition, 31 May 1977, from Sikkim.
7. **Where can I see Kangchenjunga in India?** Goecha La (4,940 m, Sikkim) and Sandakphu (3,636 m, West Bengal) are classic viewpoints.
8. **Is Kanchenjunga in Nepal or India?** On the border: Sikkim (Mangan district) and Nepal (Taplejung district).
9. **What does Kanchenjunga mean?** Usually "the five treasures of the high snow".
