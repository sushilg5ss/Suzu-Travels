## Season, access status and permits (as of 30 Oct 2026)
- Open peak; no closure or 2025–26 incident found (search 30 Oct 2026). Lahaul rain shadow: best Jun–Sep (May and Oct are cold, possible).
- Foreigners: IMF permission ≥ 90 days ahead, LO, insurance (rules-records §1.2). No PAP (not on HP protected-area list).
- HP: registered operator and guides; police registration before the climb.

## History and notable ascents
- 1970 first ascent — British services team (Bembrose; summit Rae & Cape), ENE ridge. A team member, Bob Steward, was hit by falling ice and evacuated by helicopter from base [3].
- 1973 — Army Mountaineering Association (UK): 18 summiteers from 3 Jun [4].
- 1983 — Dave Challis & Aloke Surin turned back ~6,250 m [1]; 1986 — Surin & Shaikh turned back [3]; 1994 — RAF team reached 6,257 m [5].
- 16 Oct 2018 — Indian Navy SNC team summited [7].
- 26 Aug 2019 — south ridge + traverse (Gray, Nanavatty) [2].

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Menthosa?** 6,443 m (about 21,140 ft); sources give 6,440–6,444 m.
2. **Where is Menthosa?** In Lahaul, Himachal Pradesh, above Urgos village and the Miyar valley; Udaipur is the old road-head.
3. **Who first climbed Menthosa?** A British services team led by Capt S. Bembrose in 1970; Lt S. Rae and Capt R. Cape reached the top.
4. **How difficult is Menthosa?** A glaciated climb graded about Alpine AD: long 40–50° snow and ice slopes on the east-northeast ridge, crevasses and cold; previous 6,000 m experience is wise.
5. **What is the best time to climb Menthosa?** June to September; Lahaul lies in the rain shadow.
6. **Do foreigners need a permit?** Yes — IMF permission and a liaison officer (US$500 base fee for two, up-to-6,500 m band); no protected-area permit for Lahaul.
7. **Is Menthosa higher than Mulkila?** No — Mulkila (6,517 m) is higher; Menthosa is Lahaul's second-highest.
8. **How many days does a Menthosa expedition take?** About 3 weeks from Manali is a common estimate (no official figure).
