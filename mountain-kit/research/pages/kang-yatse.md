## Season, access status and permits (as of 21 Oct 2026)
- Open and climbed in summer 2026; no closure order found. Unlike Stok Kangri (closed since 2020; Ladakh administration order 21 Dec 2023), Kang Yatse has no official restriction found.
- Best mid-June to September; June–July can bring snow at base camp and snowmelt river crossings.
- Permits: Hemis NP entry; Ladakh environment fee; foreigners confirm PAP and IMF needs (see table).

## History and notable ascents
- 1977: first ascent of Kang Yatse I (Fraser, Dravers, Ritchie, 23 Aug); they also climbed Kang Yatse III (6,310 m) on 5 Sep 1977 (AAJ 2023).
- 2018: HMI alpine-style ascents of I & II and first II → I traverse (17 Aug).
- Kang Yatse II has become Ladakh's most-climbed commercial 6,000er since Stok Kangri closed (keyword trend + 2026 summit-day report; phrase as "one of the most climbed").

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Kang Yatse 2?** 6,250 m (sources give 6,175–6,250 m); Kang Yatse I is 6,400 m.
2. **What is the difference between Kang Yatse 1 and 2?** II is the lower western summit, a non-technical PD snow climb; I is the true summit, reached by a knife-edge traverse or an AD+ face with fixed ropes.
3. **Is Kang Yatse 2 good for beginners?** It needs no technical climbing, but it is a 6,000 m glaciated peak: fitness, altitude experience and crampon/rope basics matter (link first-6000m-peak, trekking-peaks).
4. **Is Kang Yatse a good alternative to Stok Kangri?** Yes — it is the main Ladakh 6,000er still open; in Himachal, Yunam Peak (6,111 m) is the nearest equivalent.
5. **What is the best time to climb Kang Yatse 2?** Mid-June to September (rain-shadow Ladakh).
6. **How many days does Kang Yatse 2 take?** About 10–12 days from Leh including acclimatisation (estimate).
7. **Where is Kang Yatse?** At the head of the Markha Valley in Hemis National Park, Ladakh; base camp above Nimaling.
8. **Who first climbed Kang Yatse?** Kang Yatse I: probably a British party (Fraser, Dravers, Ritchie) on 23 Aug 1977; Kang Yatse II's first ascent is not documented.
9. **Do I need a permit?** Hemis National Park entry and the Ladakh environment fee; foreigners should confirm PAP and IMF requirements with the District Leh office / IMF.
