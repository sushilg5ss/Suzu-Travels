## Season, access status and permits   (as of Nov 2026)
- Season: dataset May–Jun & Sep–Oct. Dated ascents: 25 Oct 1974 (W summit), 3 May 1990, 3 Jun 1985 (II/III), 7 Jun 1993. Forest Dept: Apr–Oct, best post-monsoon. MEF 1992 May attempt was stopped by storms and heavy snow.
- Status: Forest Dept lists Swargarohini as an "**Open Peak**" ([7], checked 13 Nov 2026). It is reported among the 83 peaks opened on 3–4 Feb 2026 (OdishaBytes only). Page: "listed as an open peak by Uttarakhand's Forest Department and reported among the peaks opened in Feb 2026; confirm on UKMPS".
- Indians: apply on the state UKMPS portal. Government expedition fees are waived for Indian climbers in 2026 (rules-records §1.5).
- Foreigners: IMF permission (≥90 days ahead, LO, insurance). Up-to-6,500 m band: **US$500** base for two + **US$500** LO equipment hire, per-member charges apply (IMF page checked 1 Oct 2026). State/Forest fees abolished for foreigners in 2026.
- Govind NP/WLS entry for trekkers: no official 2025–26 fee page found. Page: "park entry permits apply; check with the Govind NP office (Sankri/Purola)". No amounts. The MEF 1992 team had a "minor dispute with National Park staff over entry fees" (history only).
- Accidents: no fatality or rescue on Swargarohini found in news 2015–2026 (search 13 Nov 2026).

## History and notable ascents
- 1950s: Doon School parties under J.T.M. Gibson attempted the group [2] ; [3].
- **25 Oct 1974**: Charles Clarke (UK), Dilsher Singh Virk, Peter Fuhrman, Bruce MacKinnon (Canada), Mohan Singh and Rattan Singh climbed the **western summit** "from the west", from Camp IV at 17,700 ft, and claimed "the higher peak" (AAJ 1976 [5]). HJ 47's table and HJ 42 treat this as **Swargarohini II** (6,247 m) [2] ; [4]. Wikipedia counts it as the first ascent of Swargarohini I (W summit) [1].
- 1977: Bengal team (Pradipto Chakravarty) made the first ascent of **IV** (5,966 m), "by mistake" for another summit [2] ; [4].
- 1984: Anil Kumar's team attempted **V**, mistaking it for II, and turned back about 200 ft short [4]. (HJ 47's table instead credits III's first ascent to 1984.)
- **3 Jun 1985**: Girivihar (Bombay), led by Anil Kumar, made the second ascent of **II** and the first of **III** (6,209 m); summiteers Sanjay Borole, Anil Bhatia, porters Himalay and Myla [4] (HJ 42, 1986). Year inferred from "eleven years after the 1974 Canadian climb".
- **3 May 1990**: Nehru Institute of Mountaineering instructors, leader Sqn Ldr A.K. Singh, climbed from Ruinsara via the E col and **claimed the first ascent of I** at 14:10 [2] ; [3]. The summiteer lists differ: 8 names (HJ 47), 7 (AAJ), 3 (Kapadia site [18]). **Disputed:** the 1992 MEF team says the NIM party reported "stopping slightly short of the summit due to dangerous cornices" [14]. The Swedes say an Indian team "claimed that they reached a point five metres lower than the summit" because of a cornice: "We found no such cornice on the summit" [15]. Kapadia's site: "within five metres of the summit after a cornice" [18].
- Jun 1990: IMF team (S.P. Chamoli) climbed II and III [2] ; [18].
- 1991: Arun Samant's Indian team on the S face reached 5,900 m and was turned back by an overhanging cornice [19].
- May 1992: Nick Banks and Chris Smith (UK, MEF) tried the N face from Har ki Dun; turned back by weather [14] ; [20].
- **7 Jun 1993**: Swedish team (leader Åke Nilsson; Birger Andrén and Ingela Nilsson summited) on a new route on the S face, BC Kiarkoti 4,100 m. AAJ: peak "attempted unsuccessfully by fifteen expeditions" before. Wikipedia: "first undisputed ascent" [15] ; [21] ; [1].

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Swargarohini?** Swargarohini I is 6,252 m (20,512 ft); II 6,247 m, III 6,209 m, IV 5,966 m.
2. **Where is Swargarohini peak?** In the Bandarpunch (Saraswati) range of Uttarkashi district, at the head of the Har ki Dun and Ruinsara valleys in Govind Pashu Vihar.
3. **Is the Swargarohini trek from Badrinath the same place?** No. The pilgrim "Swargarohini" beyond Satopanth Tal near Badrinath is a glacier/route tied to the Pandava legend. The peak is a different place, in Uttarkashi district far to the west (no distance stated).
4. **Why is it called the stairway to heaven?** Local legend says the Pandavas and their dog climbed it to reach heaven; other traditions place that ascent near Badrinath.
5. **Who first climbed Swargarohini?** It is disputed. A 1974 team reached the west summit, an NIM team claimed the main summit in May 1990 (others say they stopped a few metres short), and a Swedish team made the first undisputed ascent on 7 June 1993.
6. **Can you trek to Swargarohini base camp?** Yes, as a trek to Ruinsara Tal (~3,650 m) or Har ki Dun (~3,566 m) from Sankri/Taluka. The summit is a technical expedition.
7. **How difficult is it to climb?** Very: steep rock and ice on every side and about 2,000 m faces. Forest Dept calls it "High Risk Group".
8. **Is Swargarohini open for climbing in 2026?** The Forest Dept lists it as an "Open Peak", and one report names it among the 83 peaks opened in Feb 2026. Confirm on UKMPS.
9. **Best time?** May–June and Sep–Oct. The Forest Dept says post-monsoon is best.
10. **Can you see Swargarohini from Kedarkantha?** Operators say so. Not verified in a neutral source, so leave it out.
