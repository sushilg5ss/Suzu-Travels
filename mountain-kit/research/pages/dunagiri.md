## Season, access status and permits
- Season (dataset): **May–Jun & Sep–Oct**; 1939 summit July, 1975 October.
- 2026: **not named** in the Feb 2026 Uttarakhand "83 peaks" reports (ANI/Lokmat 4 Feb 2026; OdishaBytes 3 Feb 2026) — neither lists all 83 [9] [10]. Forest Dept peak page (forestonline.uk.gov.in/dunagiri) could not be fetched today. Dataset status_note "Opened to foreigners in the 2019 list" (UIAA/IMF 137 peaks). Page wording: "Dunagiri was among the peaks opened to foreign teams in 2019; check its current status on Uttarakhand's UKMPS portal" — do **not** say it is on the 2026 list.
- Permits: IMF band **above 7,000 m: US$1,000 base + US$500 LO**, per-member charges apply (rules-records §1.3, as of 1 Oct 2026). Indians: UKMPS, state fees waived in 2026 (§1.5).
- Access: the old Rishi-gorge route enters the Nanda Devi Sanctuary area (core closed since 1983 — see nanda-devi pack); the Niti-valley/Jumma side is in the border belt — page: "border-area permits are arranged with the expedition; confirm with the Chamoli district administration". No ILP rule stated.

## History and notable ascents
- **1883** W.W. Graham with Emil Boss attempted; Graham estimated ~22,700 ft reached [2].
- 1905–07 Longstaff's parties studied the approaches; **1933** Oliver & Campbell from the west; **1936** Shipton & Ang Tharkay turned back about 1,000 ft below the summit; **1937** Smythe & Oliver tried [2].
- **5 Jul 1939** first ascent, Roch/Steuri/Zogg (Swiss Garhwal expedition, Sherpas as carriers, sirdar Ang Dawa) [2] [1] [4].
- **8 Oct 1975** Joe Tasker & Dick Renshaw, two-man alpine-style, south rib/SE buttress (sources differ on the name — page: "a direct line on the south side"); Renshaw frostbitten [3] [1].
- **1978** first Australian Himalayan expedition (ANU Mountaineering Club), fourth ascent via SW ridge; Tim Macartney-Snape summited [1].
- 1987 Indian attempt on **Purbi Dunagiri** 6,489 m (not Dunagiri) [5].

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Dunagiri?** 7,066 m (23,182 ft); the 1939 report gave 23,184 ft.
2. **Where is Dunagiri peak?** Chamoli district, Garhwal, on the NW corner of the ring of peaks around Nanda Devi.
3. **Who first climbed Dunagiri?** André Roch, Fritz Steuri and David Zogg, 5 July 1939, by the SW ridge.
4. **Is Dunagiri the same as Dronagiri Parvat of the Ramayana?** Tradition at Dronagiri village (below the peak's north side) links the area with Hanuman and the Sanjeevani herb (GMVN); other traditions place the herb mountain elsewhere (Wikipedia notes the Valley of Flowers / Gandhamardan identifications). Say "tradition links", nothing stronger.
5. **Is Dunagiri peak the Dunagiri temple?** No — the Dunagiri Devi temple is a Kumaon hill shrine near Dwarahat (Almora), far from the 7,066 m peak in Chamoli.
6. **Can you trek to Dunagiri base?** The Dronagiri village – Bagini glacier trek (GMVN "mild trek", 8 days from Rishikesh) reaches the Bagini base-camp meadows; the summit is a technical expedition.
7. **How hard is Dunagiri?** Technical: rock, snow and ice on the SW ridge; few ascents.
8. **Can foreigners climb Dunagiri?** It was on the 2019 list of peaks opened to foreigners; foreign teams go through the IMF with a liaison officer.
