## Questions people ask
1. What is the highest peak in Himachal Pradesh? Reo Purgyil, 6,816 m, in Kinnaur on the Tibet border.
2. Which range is the highest in Himachal? The Zanskar range in Kinnaur/Spiti holds the highest peaks (Reo Purgyil, Leo Pargial, Gya).
3. How many peaks are there in Himachal? There is no single official count; our dataset lists 26 notable peaks, and the 2019 MHA list opened 47 HP peaks to foreigners.
4. What is the highest peak of the Dhauladhar? Hanuman Tibba (5,982 m in our dataset; heights quoted 5,860–5,982 m), where the Dhauladhar meets the Pir Panjal.
5. Which Himachal peak is best for beginners? Friendship Peak (5,289 m) above Manali; Yunam (6,111 m) for a first 6,000er.
6. Can foreigners climb in Himachal? Yes, with IMF permission (apply ≥ 90 days ahead) and, in the border belt, a Protected Area Permit.
7. When is the climbing season? May–June and September–October around Manali; June–September in Lahaul and Spiti.
8. Is there an easy summit near Shimla? Churdhar (3,647 m) and Hatu (3,400 m) are trekking summits in the dataset.
