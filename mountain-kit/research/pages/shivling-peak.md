## Season, access status and permits (as of 21 Oct 2026)
- Open (Uttarakhand Feb 2026 list). Indians: UKMPS portal, state fees waived. Foreigners: IMF permission (≥ 90 days ahead, LO, insurance) — US$700 band.
- Gangotri NP permit + 150/day cap on Gaumukh–Tapovan; park closes 30 Nov.
- Season May–Jun and Sep–Oct; the forest dept. calls post-monsoon the best time.

## History and notable ascents
- 1933 Pallis looked; 1938 Schwarzgruber found no route.
- **3 Jun 1974** first ascent, ITBP, west ridge (two earlier bids on 26 and 30 May failed — ExplorersWeb).
- 1980 Japanese route on the North Pillar (Fujita, Kubo, Yamamoto).
- 1981 **East Ridge** — Bettembourg, Child, Scott, White (AAJ "Shivling's East Ridge, Light is Right").
- 1983–84 Bonington & Fotheringham: first ascent of the West Summit by its SE ridge (HJ 40 — the article gives the West Summit as 6,038 m, an old figure; **use the dataset/Wikipedia 6,501 m** and don't quote 6,038).
- 1986 NE Face (Bernascone, Manoni, Rosso).
- 1993 **North Pillar** — Hans Kammerlander & Christoph Hainz.
- 2000 "Shiva's Line" — Thomas Huber & Iwan Wolf (direct North Pillar finish).
- 2005 North Face to NW Ridge — Kazuya Hiraide & Kei Taniguchi (AAJ 2006: C1 4,750 m, C2 5,650 m, C3 6,200 m; descended the normal route).
- 2005 first Indian civilian summiters per Wikipedia: Basanta Singha Roy & Debashis Biswas (Mountaineers' Association of Krishnanagar) — *see Do not say re "first Indian".*
- 25 May 2012 Valery Rozov — first BASE (wingsuit) jump from the summit.
- 2017 "Shiva's Ice" (Gietl & Messini).
- 2023 Astorga & Schmutz reached ~6,000 m on the east face; Sep 2024 Indian-led West Face attempt (Sunny Sharma) turned back from C2 5,800 m (icefall, avalanches).

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Shivling peak?** 6,543 m (21,467 ft); the SW summit is 6,501 m.
2. **Where is Shivling mountain?** In the Gangotri group, Uttarkashi, Uttarakhand — about 6 km south of Gaumukh, above the Tapovan meadow.
3. **Who first climbed Shivling?** An ITBP team led by Hukam Singh, on 3 June 1974, via the west ridge.
4. **Why is it called the Matterhorn of the Himalaya?** Its steep pyramid shape reminded early European visitors of the Alpine Matterhorn.
5. **How hard is Shivling to climb?** Very hard: every route is technical; even the west ridge has a serac barrier and steep ice — experienced alpinists only.
6. **Can you see Shivling from Gaumukh / Tapovan?** Yes — it is the most striking peak seen from Gaumukh, and Tapovan sits at its foot (Gangotri NP permit; 150 visitors a day).
7. **Is Shivling open for climbing in 2026?** Yes — it is on Uttarakhand's Feb 2026 list of 83 open peaks.
8. **What is the best time to climb Shivling?** May–June and September–October; the forest department says post-monsoon is best.
9. **What is the IMF fee for Shivling?** Foreign teams: the 6,501–7,000 m band, US$700 for two plus US$500 LO hire and per-member charges (Oct 2026); Indians pay no state fees in Uttarakhand in 2026.
