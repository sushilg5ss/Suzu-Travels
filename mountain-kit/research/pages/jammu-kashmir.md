## Questions people ask
1. **Which is the highest peak in Jammu and Kashmir?** Nun, 7,135 m, the higher summit of the Nun Kun massif on the J&K–Ladakh border; it is approached from Kargil's Suru valley.
2. **Which is the highest peak in the Kashmir Valley?** Kolahoi, 5,425 m, at the head of the Lidder valley above Pahalgam — often called the Matterhorn of Kashmir.
3. **Is Nun Kun in Jammu & Kashmir or Ladakh?** Both: Nun (7,135 m) is counted in J&K and Kun (7,077 m) in Ladakh; the massif is reached from the Ladakh (Kargil) side.
4. **Can you climb Kolahoi?** Yes — it was first climbed in 1912; the usual route is the south face from Aru near Pahalgam, a technical climb with rockfall risk (two climbers died in 2018).
5. **Which is the highest peak of the Pir Panjal in Kashmir?** Sunset Peak, 4,745 m, first climbed in 1901; it lies close to the Line of Control.
6. **Why is Harmukh famous?** It is the sacred "Kailash of Kashmir" above Gangabal lake; first climbed in 1899 by Ernest Neve and Geoffrey Millais.
7. **Which is the highest peak in the Kishtwar Himalaya?** Sickle Moon (Bharanzar), 6,574 m, first climbed in 1975 by an Indian Army team.
8. **When is the climbing season in Kashmir?** Mid-May to mid-October (J&K tourism); June to September is the usual window for peaks.
9. **Do foreigners need a permit to climb in Kashmir?** Yes, every peak expedition needs IMF permission; check current security advisories for areas near the Line of Control.
