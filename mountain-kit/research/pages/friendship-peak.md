## Questions people ask (with answers)
1. How high is Friendship Peak? 5,289 m (17,352 ft); Wikipedia lists 5,287 m.
2. How difficult is it? One of the easier Himalayan snow summits, but a real climb: steep snow near the top, crampons and ice axe, usually roped; a long summit day.
3. Can beginners climb it without a course? Fit trekkers who have been above about 4,000 m are usually accepted; the basics are taught at base camp. A Basic Mountaineering Course helps.
4. How many days? About 8 from Manali.
5. Best time? May–June and September–October.
6. Permits? The outfitter handles local formalities. Foreigners need IMF permission (apply ≥ 90 days ahead) and pay IMF fees.
7. Where does it start? The Dhundi road-head in the Solang valley, a short drive from Manali.
8. Has it been climbed in winter? Yes: an Indian Army team on 2 Jan 2015, reported as the first winter ascent.
