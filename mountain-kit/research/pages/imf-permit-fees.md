## Questions people ask
1. How much is the IMF peak fee for foreigners? Base fees for a team of two: US$500 (up to 6,500 m), US$700 (6,501–7,000 m), US$1,000 (above 7,000 m), US$200 for IMF-listed trekking peaks, plus per-member charges and US$500 LO equipment hire (as of Oct 2026).
2. How early must I apply? At least 90 days ahead; bookings open up to three years ahead.
3. Is a liaison officer compulsory? Yes, for every foreign expedition.
4. Do Indian climbers pay IMF fees? Indian teams apply through the IMF with course-qualification rules; in Uttarakhand, Indian climbers' government fees are waived since Feb 2026.
5. Do I need a mountaineering visa? A tourist visa covers open-area peaks under the 2018 guidelines; restricted areas need an X visa — confirm with the Indian mission.
6. Is there a garbage deposit? No national deposit; waste must be carried down with photo proof.
7. Does Himachal charge its own peak fee? No separate HP peak fee was found; outfitters and guides must be HP Tourism-registered and insured.
8. How long is a permit valid? 30–45 days base camp to base camp.
9. Is there a new IMF portal in 2026? No — the IMF portal dates from 2017; the 2026 change is Uttarakhand's UKMPS portal.
