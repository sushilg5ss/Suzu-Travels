## Season, access status and permits (as of 9 Nov 2026)
- Open (Feb 2026 list; Forest Dept "Open Peak"). No closure, accident or ascent news 2025–26 found (search 9 Nov 2026).
- Indians: UKMPS application; no state/IMF expedition fee since Feb 2026; IMF rules for Indian teams (AMC/BMC) still apply (rules-records §1.11).

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Neelkanth peak?** 6,596 m (21,640 ft).
2. **Where is Neelkanth peak?** About 10 km west of Badrinath temple in Chamoli, Uttarakhand — the snow peak you see behind the temple.
3. **Why is it called the Queen of Garhwal?** Frank Smythe called it that in 1931 for its perfect pyramid shape.
4. **What is the legend of Neelkanth?** It is named after Shiva ("blue-throated"); a legend says Shiva raised it to block a priest's route between Kedarnath and Badrinath.
5. **Who first climbed Neelkanth?** An ITBP team led by S.P. Chamoli on 3 June 1974; a 1961 claim is disputed.
6. **Is Neelkanth open for climbing?** Yes — named among the 83 peaks Uttarakhand opened in Feb 2026.
7. **Can tourists trek to Neelkanth?** Not to the summit — it is a technical climb; tourists see it from Badrinath (the base camps are expedition approaches).
8. **Best time to see / climb Neelkanth?** Clear views in the Badrinath season (May–Jun, Sep–Oct); climbs May–June and post-monsoon.
9. **How hard is Neelkanth?** Technical: knife-edge ridges, cornices and serac fall; many attempts have failed.
