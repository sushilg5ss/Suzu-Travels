## Checklist structure for the page (brand-free; Page Builder writes the copy)
1. **Usually provided by the outfitter** (confirm in writing): tents (base and high camp), kitchen/mess tent and meals, fixed and climbing ropes, snow anchors/pickets, group first-aid and oxygen/communication on bigger trips; often ice axe, crampons, helmet, harness on loan.
2. **You bring (personal):** layers (below), boots, sleeping bag rated for high camp, 50–60 L pack + small summit pack, headlamp + spare batteries, glacier sunglasses (category 3–4) and ski goggles, sunscreen/lip balm, 2 water bottles (one insulated), personal first aid and medicines, toiletries, trekking poles, gaiters, documents (ID/passport, permits, insurance).
3. **Can be rented in Manali/Solang** (generic, no shop names): sleeping bags, down jackets, snow boots/gaiters, poles; technical items should be checked for fit and condition (UIAA/CE marking).
- Layers table: base (wicking, no cotton) → mid (fleece) → insulation (down/synthetic) → shell (waterproof jacket + trousers); hands: liner + insulated gloves + mitts; head: sun hat, warm hat, buff/balaclava; feet: liner + warm socks.
- Summit-day pack box: shell + down jacket, spare gloves/mitts, goggles, headlamp, 1–1.5 L warm water, snacks, sunscreen, personal meds, small first-aid kit.

## Questions people ask
1. What gear do I need for mountaineering in India? Boots that take crampons, crampons, ice axe, harness, helmet, a 3-layer clothing system, a high-camp sleeping bag, pack, headlamp and glacier glasses; group gear (ropes, tents) normally comes from the outfitter.
2. What does the outfitter provide on a guided climb? Usually tents, meals, ropes and group technical gear, often ice axe, crampons and helmet on loan — always confirm the list in writing.
3. Can I rent mountaineering gear in Manali? Yes, rental of jackets, sleeping bags and snow boots is common in Manali and Solang; check technical items for fit and safety marking.
4. Do I need special boots? For snow peaks, boots that take crampons; ordinary trekking shoes are not enough above the snow line.
5. How warm should my sleeping bag be? Rated for well below freezing at high camp (the outfitter will tell you the camp altitude; 5,000 m-plus camps are cold even in September).
6. Do I need my own ice axe and crampons? Not on most guided trekking-peak climbs, where they can be provided on loan; bring your own if you already own fitted gear.
7. What should I wear on summit day? All your layers: base, fleece, down jacket and shell, with mitts, goggles and a warm hat.
8. Is there a rule about gear in the mountains? The IMF requires expeditions to retrieve all ropes and technical gear and carry non-biodegradable waste down.

## Do not say
- Any brand or shop name, product model, or price (kit rule).
- That gear is "provided by Suzu" or "our gear" — say "your outfitter provides… (confirm in writing)".
- Exact sleeping-bag temperatures or jacket fill numbers as rules (they vary by peak/month; keep as guidance).
- Medicine names as advice (only "as advised by your doctor").
- That rental gear is safe/certified as a blanket statement.
- Operator inclusion lists as official.
