## Questions people ask
1. **What is the toughest mountain to climb in India?** No official ranking; by climbers' records the Shark's Fin on Meru Central and Thalay Sagar's shale band are among the hardest; Changabang's granite walls too.
2. **Has the Shark's Fin been climbed?** Yes — Oct 2011, Anker, Chin and Ozturk, after decades of failed attempts.
3. **Why is Thalay Sagar so hard?** Steep on all sides and capped by a band of rotten shale.
4. **Which is the hardest peak in Himachal?** Indrasan is called the hardest of the Pir Panjal; Mulkila is the most serious high peak of Lahaul.
5. **Is India's highest peak the hardest?** No — Kangchenjunga can't be climbed from India (Sikkim ban); difficulty depends on route, not height.
6. **How do India's hardest peaks compare with K2?** They are lower (6,300–7,500 m) but many are steeper and more technical; don't rank them against K2 numerically.
7. **Can beginners climb any of these?** No — start with a trekking peak (Friendship, Yunam) and a basic course.
8. **What is the Piolet d'Or?** An annual mountaineering award; Indian winners include Thalay Sagar (1999) and Saser Kangri II East (2012).
