## History and notable ascents
- 1860 — survey khalasi "ascent" (reported 1910; doubted) [2] ; [3].
- 1874 — survey sheet 64 SW: "Parang La No. 2 S", 23,064 ft [2].
- 1939 — J.O.M. Roberts saw it from Chau Chau Kang Nilda and "felt its height to be suspect" [4].
- 1952 — Graaff & Snelson (South African) camped at Langza to identify it; "There was no peak of 23,000 ft" [3].
- 1966 — first documented ascent, Indian expedition; no survey pole found [2].
- 1983 — Kapadia's Lingti expedition crossed Shilla jot and described the Lingti-side approach [2].

## Questions people ask
1. **What is the height of Shilla peak?** 6,132 m (about 20,118 ft).
2. **Was Shilla once thought to be over 7,000 m?** Yes — old survey sheets showed it at 23,064 ft (about 7,030 m); modern surveys fixed it at 6,132 m.
3. **Who first climbed Shilla?** An 1860 climb by a Survey of India khalasi was reported in 1910 but is doubted; the first documented ascent was by an Indian expedition in 1966.
4. **Is Shilla the highest peak in Himachal Pradesh?** No — Reo Purgyil (6,816 m) is; in Spiti, Gya (6,794 m) is higher too.
5. **Where is Shilla peak?** In Spiti, Himachal Pradesh, between the Lingti valley and the Shilla nala above Langza, inside the Kibber Wildlife Sanctuary.
6. **How do you approach Shilla?** From Langza over Shilla jot (5,670 m) into the Lingti side, then up to the base; the direct western side from the Shilla nala is not easy.
7. **When can you climb Shilla?** June to September.
8. **Do foreigners need a permit for Shilla?** IMF permission and a liaison officer (US$500 base fee for two); no Protected Area Permit for the Kaza–Langza side.
