## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Hathi Parvat?** 6,727 m (22,070 ft).
2. **Why is it called Hathi Parvat?** "Hathi" means elephant — its outline is likened to an elephant; it is also called Elephant Peak.
3. **Where can I see Hathi Parvat?** Best from Joshimath; it rises in Chamoli district near the Bhyundar valley.
4. **What is the legend of Hathi Parvat?** Two rocks on its spur are said to be the crow Kakbhushundi and the eagle Garuda, talking about the universe (Ramayana tradition).
5. **Who first climbed Hathi Parvat?** An eight-member Indian team including Sonam Gyatso and Sonam Wangyal, on 6–7 June 1963.
6. **Is Hathi Parvat near the Valley of Flowers?** Yes, one approach starts from Ghangaria, the base for the Valley of Flowers.
7. **Can I climb Hathi Parvat?** It is a technical glaciated peak; expeditions go through Uttarakhand's UKMPS portal (Indians) or the IMF (foreigners).
8. **Is Kakbhushundi Tal below Hathi Parvat?** Yes, the lake lies in its vicinity.
