## Questions people ask
1. **Is Kanamo Peak open in 2026?** Unclear: a 2019 village restriction was never matched by a published reopening order; climbers did summit in 2026. Check locally first.
2. **How high is Kanamo Peak?** 5,964 m in our dataset; sources quote 5,970–5,974 m. It is just under 6,000 m.
3. **Is Kanamo a 6,000 m peak?** No — it is a high 5,000er (5,964 m). For a true 6,000er with clear access in Himachal, see Yunam (6,111 m).
4. **How many days does the Kanamo trek take?** About 7–9 days from Manali and back; summit day is a long push from a base camp near 4,800 m.
5. **Where does the Kanamo trek start?** Kibber village (about 4,270 m), above Kaza in Spiti.
6. **Is Kanamo difficult?** Non-technical (no ropes in normal conditions) but very high; acclimatisation is the main challenge — link `altitude-sickness`.
7. **Best time for Kanamo?** June–September (Spiti is in the rain shadow, so the monsoon matters less).
8. **Why was Kanamo banned?** In July 2019 village leaders above Kibber stopped trekking groups after a water source was contaminated.
9. **Do I need a permit for Kanamo?** Himachal rules require registered operators/guides and police registration; local permissions may apply — confirm with Kaza authorities.
