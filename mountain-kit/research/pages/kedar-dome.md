## Season, access status and permits (as of 9 Nov 2026)
- Open; no closure order. No Kedar Dome accident or ascent news 2025–26 found (search 9 Nov 2026).
- **Kedartal avalanche (Oct–Nov 2026) is NOT on Kedar Dome**: a 13-member trek group left Gangotri 27 Oct for Kedartal (the lake at the foot of Thalay Sagar, Kedar Ganga valley) — snowfall/avalanches at 10–12 points; by 6 Nov seven bodies recovered, one missing (ThePrint 6 Nov; ETV Bharat 3 Nov). Different valley from the Gaumukh–Tapovan approach. Page may mention it only in a safety line, dated.
- Foreigners: IMF permission ≥ 90 days ahead, LO, insurance; Gangotri is not an Inner Line area for this approach (Nelong valley is) — say "no Inner Line Permit on the Gangotri–Tapovan approach" only if the Page Builder needs it; source uttarakhand pack (ILP = Nelong, Mana, Niti).
- Indians: UKMPS application; no government expedition fee since Feb 2026.

## History and notable ascents
- 1947 — Swiss (Roch) cross the dome, 25 Jun turn-back and 11 Jul en route to Kedarnath's first ascent [3].
- 1967–76 — Indian attempts, partly successful [2].
- 7 May 1978 — IAF team summits and skis from the summit to ABC [2].
- 25 Sep 1987 — Polish team (Grochowski) summits by the normal route; east-face attempt fails [6].
- Apr–May 1994 — British ski expedition (John Cleare and others) stopped at ~6,000 m by storms/avalanche risk [5].
- 1–9 Oct 2006 — Parnell & Emmett, SE Pillar, ED+ alpine style [7] ; [8].

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Kedar Dome?** 6,831 m (22,411 ft).
2. **Is Kedar Dome the same as Kedarnath peak?** No — it is the NW subpeak of the Kedarnath massif; Kedarnath main is 6,940 m, about 2 km away.
3. **Who first climbed Kedar Dome?** André Roch's Swiss team in 1947, on their way to Kedarnath's first ascent.
4. **Is Kedar Dome a trekking peak / easy?** No. Its NW flank is the easiest big snow route in the Gangotri group, but it is a glaciated 6,800 m expedition with crevasses, avalanche slopes and a steep top section.
5. **Where is Kedar Dome base camp?** Tapovan (~4,400 m) above Gaumukh, at the foot of Shivling, reached from Gangotri in about three days.
6. **Has anyone skied Kedar Dome?** Yes — an Indian Air Force team skied from the summit to advanced base on 7 May 1978.
7. **Best time to climb Kedar Dome?** May–June and September–October.
8. **Is Kedar Dome open in 2026 / fees?** Reported among the 83 peaks Uttarakhand opened in Feb 2026 (UKMPS); Indians pay no government expedition fee; foreigners pay the IMF fee (US$700 base for two + US$500 LO).
9. **Can you see Kedar Dome from Kedarnath temple?** Not claimed — the dome sits on the Gangotri side of the massif (no source confirms the temple view) → avoid.
