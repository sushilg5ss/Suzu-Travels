## Season, access status and permits (as of 23 Oct 2026)
- Open (Uttarakhand Feb 2026 list). Indians: apply on UKMPS, state fees waived (IMF rules for Indian teams still apply: leader/AMC requirements — rules-records §1.11). Foreigners: IMF permission ≥ 90 days ahead, LO, insurance; **US$1,000 band** + US$500 LO hire + per-member charges.
- Inner Line: Niti valley — ILP/PAP via Chamoli district (SDM Joshimath); confirm before travel.
- Season May–Jun (most summits mid–late June) and Sep–Oct; state page says post-monsoon is best.

## History and notable ascents
- 1855 Schlagintweits on Abi Gamin (thought it was Kamet); 1907 Longstaff/Bruce/Mumm ~6,100 m; 1911–13 Meade & Slingsby — 1913 Meade and Pierre Blanc reach Meade's Col; 1920 Kellas & Morshead above the col (Wikipedia).
- **21 Jun 1931** first ascent, Smythe's team — first summit above 25,000 ft.
- **6 Jul 1955** second ascent, first Indian — HMI, Nandu Jayal.
- 2003 Indian Navy (Cdr Satyabrata Dam) — Kamet 12 Jun (NW-ridge variation from the col) + Abi Gamin 11 Jun (HJ 60).
- 24 Jun 2006 Himalayan Club Kolkata Section 75th-anniversary ascent, ten summiters (Wikipedia; HJ 63).
- 2008 **SE face** first ascent — Kazuya Hiraide & Kei Taniguchi, alpine style ("Samurai Direct", AAJ 2009; Piolet d'Or 2009 — not verified this run, see Do not say).
- 2010 avalanche during an Indian Army expedition killed two officers (Wikipedia).
- 2012 SW face "Spicy Game" — French team, Piolet d'Or 2013 (Wikipedia).
- 2024 ITBP summits of Kamet + Abi Gamin; Aug 2026 ITBP expedition flagged off (Pioneer).

## Questions people ask
1. **What is the height of Kamet?** 7,756 m (25,446 ft).
2. **Where is Mount Kamet?** In the Zanskar range of the Garhwal Himalaya, Chamoli district, Uttarakhand, near the Tibet border; road-head Ghamsali in the Niti valley via Joshimath and Malari.
3. **Is Kamet the highest peak in Uttarakhand?** No — it is the second-highest after Nanda Devi (7,816 m), and the 3rd-highest in India in our dataset.
4. **Who first climbed Kamet?** Frank Smythe's British team on 21 June 1931 (Smythe, Shipton, Holdsworth, Lewa Sherpa first; Greene and Birnie two days later).
5. **Was Kamet the first 7,000 m peak climbed?** No — Trisul (1907) was. Kamet was the first above 25,000 ft (7,620 m).
6. **Who was the first Indian to climb Kamet?** An HMI team led by Nandu Jayal made the second ascent on 6 July 1955.
7. **Is Kamet open for climbing in 2026?** Yes — it is on Uttarakhand's February 2026 list of 83 open peaks; apply on the UKMPS portal (Indians) or through the IMF (foreigners).
8. **How hard is Kamet?** Not a steep technical face on the normal route, but a full 7,700 m expedition: five camps, crevassed glacier, a rockfall gully and steep icy snow above Meade's Col.
9. **What is the IMF fee for Kamet?** Above-7,000 m band: US$1,000 for two, plus US$500 LO equipment hire and per-member charges (Oct 2026). Indians pay no state fees in Uttarakhand in 2026.
10. **Which river rises from Kamet's glaciers?** The East Kamet glacier feeds the Dhauliganga, the West Kamet glacier the Saraswati — both Alaknanda tributaries.
