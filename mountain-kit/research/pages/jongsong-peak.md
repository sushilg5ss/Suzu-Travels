## History and notable ascents
1909 British attempt (no summit, HDB) → 1930 Dyhrenfurth expedition (failed on Kangchenjunga first, then Jongsong: 3 Jun + 8 Jun) → record until Kamet 21 Jun 1931 → 1983 ×2 → 1998 ×2 → 2000 → 2012 Domo.

## Questions people ask
1. How high is Jongsong Peak? — 7,462 m (24,482 ft); older surveys give 7,459 m.
2. Where is Jongsong Peak? — On the India (Sikkim)–Nepal–Tibet tripoint, about 20 km north of Kangchenjunga.
3. Who first climbed Jongsong Peak? — Hermann Hoerlin and Erwin Schneider, early June 1930, on Dyhrenfurth's international expedition.
4. Why is Jongsong famous? — For a year it was the highest summit ever climbed, until Kamet in June 1931.
5. Can you climb Jongsong from India today? — The Sikkim side is a restricted border area; post-1983 ascents were mostly Nepal-based teams.
6. How many times has it been climbed? — The Himalayan Database lists ascents in 1930 (two parties), 1983 (one disputed), 1998 (two) and 2000.
7. What is the Jongsong La? — The ~6,120 m pass the 1930 team crossed between Nepal and Sikkim.
8. Is Jongsong higher than Pauhunri? — Yes, 7,462 m vs 7,128 m; it took over the height record in 1930.
