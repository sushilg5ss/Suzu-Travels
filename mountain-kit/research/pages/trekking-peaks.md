## Season, access status and permits
- Kullu peaks (Friendship, Shitidhar, Ladakhi): May–June and September–October; monsoon July–August brings rain and poor visibility on the south side (rules-records §3).
- Lahaul, Spiti and Ladakh (Yunam, Kanamo, Mentok, Dzo Jongo): June–September, rain-shadow (dataset seasons).
- **Kanamo — still unverified (5 Oct 2026):** Indiahikes says "restricted until further notice"; Bikat still says "banned"; an operator guide updated 11 Sep 2026 describes a 2026 itinerary without any closure note. No official (DC Lahaul-Spiti / Kaza) source found. Keep `hold`: page says "access status unclear — ask us before planning".
- **Stok Kangri:** closed (see stok-kangri.md).
- Foreign teams: IMF permit through the IMF (fee bands above); Ladakh peaks also need a PAP for Tso Moriri.
- Kangra registration (8 Jul–15 Oct 2026) does **not** apply to these peaks (it covers ten Dhauladhar routes only).

## Questions people ask
1. What is a trekking peak? A loose label for a summit that needs limited technical climbing but still crampons, ice axe and rope skills; it is not one official list in India.
2. Which is the easiest peak to climb in India? Among Himachal summits, Friendship Peak (5,289 m) and Shitidhar (5,294 m) above Solang are the usual first snow climbs; "easy" still means altitude, snow slopes and a pre-dawn start.
3. What is the highest trekking peak in India? There is no official ranking. Popular non-technical 6,000-plus summits include Mentok Kangri (6,250 m), Dzo Jongo (6,280 m) and Lungser Kangri (6,666 m) in Ladakh, and Yunam (6,111 m) in Himachal.
4. Do I need a mountaineering course to climb a trekking peak? Most guided trekking-peak climbs do not ask for a BMC; outfitters ask about high-altitude trekking experience. Indian teams applying to the IMF on their own must meet the IMF's course rules.
5. Can foreigners climb trekking peaks in India? Yes, with an IMF permit (trekking-peak base fee US$200 for two where the peak is IMF-listed, otherwise US$500), an IMF liaison officer, and a PAP in border areas.
6. Is Stok Kangri open? No — closed since 2020; Ladakh's administration closed all expeditions on 21 Dec 2023.
7. Is Kanamo open in 2026? Sources conflict and no official order was found; ask before planning.
8. When is the best time? May–June and September–October for Kullu peaks; June–September for Lahaul, Spiti and Ladakh.
9. How many days does a trekking peak take? About a week from Manali for Friendship or Yunam (see their fact packs); Ladakh peaks need extra acclimatisation days in Leh.
