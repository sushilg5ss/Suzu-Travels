## History and notable ascents
- 1933: Marco Pallis's party — first documented ascent of **Leo Pargial**; Pallis named the higher summit the "South Peak" (Wikipedia; HJ 48).
- 1967: Indian party (Maj. Inder Valia) approached Leo Pargial via Lamkhaga Pass; snow stopped them (HJ 28).
- 1971: ITBP — first ascent (records: "Leo Pargial", 20/24 Sep 1971; cairn on Reo Purgyil) (HJ 48).
- 27 Jun 1974: IMA team on Leo Pargial (HJ 34).
- 26 Jul 1991: Indian team via the west ridge (HJ 48).
- 2020: ITBP, 12 members (Wikipedia).
- 16 Sep 2026: ITBP "Parvat Sankalp-2026" (IANS/ITBP).

## Questions people ask
1. **Which is the highest peak in Himachal Pradesh?** Reo Purgyil, 6,816 m, in Kinnaur on the Tibet border.
2. **How high is Reo Purgyil?** 6,816 m (22,362 ft); some 2026 reports give 6,759 m.
3. **Where is Reo Purgyil?** Pooh tehsil, Kinnaur, above Nako village, southern end of the Zanskar range.
4. **Is Reo Purgyil the same as Leo Pargial?** Twin summits of one massif: Reo Purgyil 6,816 m (south), Leo Pargial 6,791 m (~2 km north); names are often mixed.
5. **Who first climbed Reo Purgyil?** An ITBP team in 1971.
6. **Can you climb Reo Purgyil?** Yes, as a full expedition for experienced climbers, with IMF permission (and a PAP for foreigners); about three weeks from Nako, Jun–Sep.
7. **Do I need a permit for Nako / Kinnaur?** Indians no; foreigners need a Protected Area Permit beyond Jangi.
8. **Is there a 7,000 m peak in Himachal?** No — Reo Purgyil is the highest.
9. **How hard is Reo Purgyil?** Technical: glacier plus a long loose ridge with short rock pitches, strong winds — not a trekking peak.
