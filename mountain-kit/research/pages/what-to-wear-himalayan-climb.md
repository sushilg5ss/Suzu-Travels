## Questions people ask
1. What is the layering system? Base layer to manage sweat, mid-layer to hold warmth, shell to block wind and rain — you add or remove layers as you heat up or cool down.
2. Why no cotton? It holds moisture and makes you cold once you stop.
3. Down or synthetic jacket? Down is warmest for its weight but useless when wet; synthetic keeps warm when damp.
4. What should I wear on summit day? Everything: base, fleece, down/synthetic jacket, shell, warm hat, mitts, goggles.
5. Gloves or mittens? Carry both; mitts are warmer and help prevent frostbite at high altitude.
6. What do I wear for a snow trek in winter Manali? The same idea on a smaller scale: thermal base, fleece, warm jacket, waterproof outer, warm socks and waterproof shoes.
7. Can I rent winter clothing in Manali? Down jackets and other snow wear are commonly rented in Manali/Solang; check fit and condition.
8. How cold is it at high camp? Well below freezing at night on 5,000–6,000 m camps, even in September — no exact numbers.
