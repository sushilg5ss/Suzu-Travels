## Questions people ask
1. **Which is the highest peak in South India?** Anamudi, 2,695 m, in Kerala's Eravikulam National Park — the highest point in India outside the Himalayas.
2. **Which is the highest peak in Karnataka?** Mullayanagiri, about 1,925 m, in Chikkamagaluru district.
3. **Which is the highest peak in Maharashtra?** Kalsubai, 1,646 m, on the Ahmednagar–Nashik border in the Sahyadri.
4. **Which is the highest peak in Kerala?** Anamudi (2,695 m).
5. **Which is the highest peak in Tamil Nadu?** Doddabetta, 2,637 m, in the Nilgiris near Ooty.
6. **Which is the highest peak in Rajasthan?** Guru Shikhar, 1,722 m, at Mount Abu in the Aravallis.
7. **Which state has the highest peak in India?** Sikkim — Kangchenjunga, 8,586 m (India's map also shows K2 in Ladakh, but K2 is in Pakistan-administered Gilgit-Baltistan).
8. **Which is the highest peak in the North-East outside Sikkim and Arunachal?** Mount Saramati, 3,826 m, in Nagaland on the Myanmar border.
9. **Which is the highest point in Delhi?** Deheri, 315 m, on the Aravalli ridge.
10. **Which state or UT has the lowest highest point?** Lakshadweep — an unnamed point of about 15 m on Agatti Island.
