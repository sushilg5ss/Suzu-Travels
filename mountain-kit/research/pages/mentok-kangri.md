## Season, access status and permits   (as of Nov 2026)
- Season: dataset Jun–Sep. Operators give mid-June to late September (early Oct in good years). Dated ascents run from 10 Jun (Navy 2018) and July (Giripremi 2026) to 3 Aug (JG 2015), 24 Aug (Army 2026) and 3 Oct (SummitPost 2016). Early season means more snow (crampons, possible gully or avalanche risk). By late season it is dry, loose rock with scarce water at base camp (JG 2015 vs SP 2016).
- Status: open and climbed in 2026 (Giripremi Mentok II, Jul 2026; Indian Army Corps of Signals, 24 Aug 2026). No closure order found. Stok Kangri remains closed (since 2020), so never present it as an option.
- Foreigners: a Protected Area Permit is required for Tso Moriri. Minimum 2 foreigners, valid up to 15 days, issued via DC Leh / lahdclehpermit.in (rules-records §1.7). Validity was raised from 7 to 15 days in Aug 2021 (https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india/indian-citizens-no-longer-need-inner-line-permit-to-visit-protected-areas-in-ladakh-1017167.html). lahdclehpermit.in could not be fetched today.
- Indians: no Inner Line Permit for Tso Moriri since the 6 Aug 2021 order (Deccan Herald, 7 Aug 2021). Ladakh environment/wildlife fees may still apply; no official fee page could be verified today, so don't give amounts.
- IMF (foreigners): peaks ≤6,500 m are US$500 for a team of two plus US$500 LO equipment hire, "per-member charges apply" (IMF peak-fee page as of 1 Oct 2026, see imf-permit-fees.md). indmount.org could not be fetched today, so we have **not** verified whether Mentok is on IMF's open-peak or trekking-peak list. Page: "check the IMF peak list; foreign teams apply via IMF".
- Indian teams: Indian Army, Navy and club teams climb it (2018, 2026). IMF clearance rules for Indian teams are covered in imf-permit-fees.md.

## History and notable ascents
- Ramesh Bhattacharjee explored the Rupshu peaks and wrote about them in 1984 and 1993 (Himalayan Journal vols 41 and 50). Harish Kapadia credits him with the first ascent of Mentok II, year not given (HJ 52, 1996). https://www.himalayanclub.org/hj/52/12/nangpas-are-flying-changpas-are-smiling/
- 1994: Tso Moriri area opened to tourists. 1995: Kapadia's Indian team made the first ascents of Lungser Kangri and Chamser Kangri (same HJ article; https://www.harishkapadia.com/?p=8051).
- First ascent of Mentok I (the highest summit): not found. Say "not recorded in the sources we checked".
- 3 Aug 2015: Jagged Globe team summits "Mentok I" via a snow gully (https://www.jagged-globe.co.uk/news/item.php?id=2811).
- 3 Oct 2016: SummitPost report, GPS 6,151 m on a lower summit.
- 10 Jun 2018: Indian Navy (Eastern Naval Command) team of 11, including two women officers, summits Mt Mentok Kangri, 6,250 m (https://indiannavy.nic.in/node/19170).
- Jul 2026: Giripremi all-women team (leader Sonia Bhavsar) climbs Mentok Kangri II, 6,210 m (punekarnews, 25 Jul 2026).
- 24 Aug 2026: Indian Army Corps of Signals team of 20 climbs Mt Mentok Kangri, 6,250 m (ssbcrack, 26 Aug 2026).

## Questions people ask
1. **How high is Mentok Kangri?** The highest summit (Mentok I) is about 6,250 m. Sources give 6,250–6,270 m, and Mentok II is about 6,210 m.
2. **Which Mentok is the highest?** Mentok I, the southernmost of the group per SummitPost. Names and heights differ between maps and operators.
3. **Is Mentok Kangri a good alternative to Stok Kangri?** Stok Kangri has been closed since 2020. Mentok is one of the Rupshu peaks people climb instead, and it was climbed in 2026.
4. **How difficult is it?** Usually non-technical: moraine and loose rock when dry. In snowy conditions parties have needed crampons and a rope, and some reports describe a steep ice or rock section.
5. **Where does the climb start?** Korzok village (about 4,527 m) on Tso Moriri's west shore. Base camp is about 5,300–5,400 m.
6. **How many days?** Plans usually run 8–12 days from Leh including acclimatisation (operator itineraries).
7. **Best time?** June to September. Early season is snowier, and late season is dry, with scarce water at base camp.
8. **Do foreigners need a permit?** Yes. A Protected Area Permit for Tso Moriri (min 2 people, up to 15 days) plus IMF permission for the peak.
9. **Do Indians need an Inner Line Permit?** No, not since Aug 2021, though Ladakh fees may apply.
10. **Who first climbed it?** Ramesh Bhattacharjee is credited with the first ascent of Mentok II. The first ascent of Mentok I isn't recorded in the sources we checked.
