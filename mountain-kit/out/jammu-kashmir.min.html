Which is the highest peak in Jammu and Kashmir?

Nun (7,135 m / 23,409 ft), the higher summit of the Nun Kun massif on the Jammu & Kashmir–Ladakh border, is the highest peak in Jammu and Kashmir; climbers reach it from Kargil's Suru valley. Inside the Kashmir Valley the highest is Kolahoi (5,425 m) above Pahalgam, and in the Kishtwar Himalaya it is Sickle Moon (6,574 m). All 10 are in our list of India's highest peaks.

Jammu & Kashmir is a small union territory with big mountains on three sides. The Great Himalaya runs along its east, from the Nun Kun massif through Kishtwar to the peaks above Pahalgam and Sonamarg. The Pir Panjal closes the Kashmir Valley to the south-west, and the low Zabarwan range rises straight behind Srinagar's Dal Lake.

The question "which is the highest peak in Jammu and Kashmir?" has two common answers, and both are right in their own way. For the whole union territory it is Nun (7,135 m), the higher twin of Nun Kun. Nun is counted in Jammu & Kashmir and its neighbour Kun (7,077 m) in Ladakh, but the massif is approached from the Ladakh side, through Kargil and the Suru valley. When people ask for the highest peak in Kashmir, they usually mean the Kashmir Valley, and there the answer is Kolahoi (5,425 m), the "Matterhorn of Kashmir" above Pahalgam.

Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh became separate union territories on 31 October 2019, so older lists that mix them up are out of date; our Ladakh peaks guide covers the other side of the line. Our table holds the 10 notable J&K peaks in the dataset behind our Indian Himalaya guide: one above 7,000 m, two in the 6,000s, three in the 5,000s, three in the 4,000s and one in the 3,000s. A few smaller summits on Wikipedia, such as Kazinag and Sirbal, are not in our dataset.

Permits

Permits for climbing in Jammu & Kashmir

Every peak expedition by foreigners needs permission from the Government of India through the Indian Mountaineering Foundation (IMF), as J&K's tourism corporation states. The IMF base fee for a foreign team of two is US$500 for peaks up to 6,500 m, US$700 for 6,501–7,000 m and US$1,000 above 7,000 m, plus US$500 for liaison-officer equipment; per-member charges apply (IMF, checked 1 Oct 2026).

In August 2019 the Home Ministry's list of 137 peaks opened to foreigners counted 15 in what was then Jammu & Kashmir, before Ladakh became a separate union territory; that figure covers both of today's territories. A tourist visa covers open areas; confirm visa rules with the Indian mission.

Nun is reached through Kargil, which is in Ladakh, so plan that approach with Ladakh's rules too. See our IMF permit and fees guide and how to climb in India.

Where they are

Jammu & Kashmir's peaks, area by area

The ten peaks fall into six areas, from the remote Nun side in the east to the Srinagar skyline.

Nun (from Kargil)

  • Nun (7,135 m / 23,409 ft): J&K's highest, on the Ladakh border
  • Usual base camp about 4,600 m in the upper Sentik valley; the normal route is the west ridge

The road-head is Tangol in the Suru valley, reached from Kargil in Ladakh.

Kishtwar Himalaya

  • Sickle Moon (Bharanzar, 6,574 m): the range's highest, first climbed in 1975
  • Brammah I (6,416 m): Bonington and Estcourt, 1973

Remote and technical: about 145 km of road from Srinagar to Kishtwar, then about 50 km of track to the Sickle Moon base.

Lidder valley (Pahalgam)

  • Kolahoi (5,425 m / 17,799 ft): the Kashmir Valley's highest, the "Matterhorn of Kashmir"

The usual route is the south face, from Aru near Pahalgam: about 21 km of alpine trekking to the glacier.

Sind valley and Gangabal

  • Harmukh (5,142–5,300 m by source): the sacred "Kailash of Kashmir" above Gangabal lake
  • Amarnath Peak (5,186 m): above the Amarnath cave, not climbed

Harmukh's easiest route starts at Arin (Bandipora); pilgrims to Gangabal start at Naranag.

Pir Panjal

  • Sunset Peak (4,745 m): the range's highest on the Kashmir side, first climbed in 1901
  • Tatakooti (4,725 m, also given as 4,760 m), also 1901
  • Apharwat (4,390 m): above Gulmarg, served by one of the world's highest ski gondolas

These peaks lie close to the Line of Control: check the local security advisory first.

Srinagar

  • Mahadev (3,966 m / 13,012 ft): the highest of the Zabarwan range and a historic Hindu pilgrimage

A day or overnight hike from the edge of the city.

FAQ

Jammu & Kashmir's peaks: your questions

Which is the highest peak in Jammu and Kashmir?

Nun, 7,135 m (23,409 ft), the higher summit of the Nun Kun massif on the Jammu & Kashmir–Ladakh border. It is approached from Kargil through the Suru valley.

Which is the highest peak in the Kashmir Valley?

Kolahoi, 5,425 m (17,799 ft), at the head of the Lidder valley above Pahalgam. It is often called the Matterhorn of Kashmir.

Is Nun Kun in Jammu & Kashmir or Ladakh?

Both. Nun (7,135 m) is counted in Jammu & Kashmir and Kun (7,077 m) in Ladakh; the massif is reached from the Ladakh side, through Kargil.

Can you climb Kolahoi?

Yes. It was first climbed in 1912, and the usual route is the south face from Aru near Pahalgam. It is a technical climb with rockfall risk: two climbers died on the descent in 2018.

Which is the highest peak of the Pir Panjal in Kashmir?

Sunset Peak, 4,745 m (15,568 ft), first climbed in 1901. It lies close to the Line of Control, so check local security advisories.

Why is Harmukh famous?

It is the sacred "Kailash of Kashmir" above Gangabal lake, 5,142–5,300 m depending on the source. Ernest Neve and Geoffrey Millais first climbed it in 1899.

Which is the highest peak in the Kishtwar Himalaya?

Sickle Moon (Bharanzar), 6,574 m (21,568 ft), first climbed in 1975 by an Indian Army team led by Lt Col D.N. Tankha.

When is the climbing season in Kashmir?

Mid-May to mid-October, according to J&K's tourism corporation; June to September is the usual window for the peaks. Foreign teams also need IMF permission for every peak.
