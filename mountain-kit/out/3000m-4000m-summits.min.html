Which are the easy Himalayan summits in India?

Our dataset lists 15 summits between 3,000 and 4,999 m, from Nag Tibba (3,022 m / 9,915 ft) to Sunset Peak (4,745 m / 15,568 ft). The best known are Chandrashila (3,690 m), Kedarkantha (3,810 m), Sandakphu (3,636 m) and Churdhar (3,647 m). Thirteen are graded trekking summits; Tatakooti and Sunset Peak need glacier skills. All 15 are in our list of India's highest peaks.

Not every Himalayan summit needs a high camp, a rope and an IMF permit. Below 5,000 m the Indian Himalaya is full of real tops that a fit walker can stand on: a temple summit above Narkanda, a snow dome above Sankri, a ridge in Darjeeling with Everest and Kangchenjunga on the horizon.

We keep only notable summits in our dataset, so this page lists 15 rather than claim a full count. Every height, season and road-head below comes from our dataset, with metres and feet side by side, and where sources give a different height we say so once. Treks and itineraries are not covered here; this page is about the summits themselves.

Permits

Permits and access

No Indian Mountaineering Foundation (IMF) climbing permit is needed for these trek summits; IMF permission is for peak expeditions. Protected-area entry fees and local rules still apply, for example in Govind Wildlife Sanctuary (Kedarkantha), on the Singalila Ridge (Sandakphu and Phalut) and in the Churdhar Sanctuary: check with the forest department before you go.

In Kashmir, Apharwat and Sunset Peak are close to the Line of Control, so check local security advisories. For guided treks to these summits, see our adventure section.

From easy to harder

Which summit trek is the easiest?

Grades below come from our dataset and the routes as described by our sources. None of these needs technical climbing, except where noted.

  • Road or a short walkHatu Peak above Narkanda, Shikari Devi from Janjehli and the Chanshal pass area above Rohru. Good for families; the altitude is still real.
  • Short summit treksNag Tibba from Pantwari, and Chandrashila: about 3.5 km from Chopta to Tungnath, then about 1.5 km more to the summit.
  • Longer treks, one or more nights outKedarkantha from Sankri, Sandakphu and Phalut on the Singalila Ridge, Churdhar, Patalsu above Solang, Mahadev above Srinagar and Apharwat above the Gulmarg gondola.
  • Snow summit with an exposed finishPangarchulla (4,575 m): a spring snow trek with a boulder ridge near the top.
  • Not a hikeTatakooti (4,725 m) and Sunset Peak (4,745 m) are graded moderate: their routes cross glacier, snow and rock ridges.

Altitude still counts. Most of these tops are above 3,500 m, where headaches and breathlessness are common. Our altitude sickness guide explains the warning signs.

FAQ

Summit treks: your questions

What is the height of Chandrashila?

About 3,690 m (12,106 ft), above Tungnath in Rudraprayag district, Uttarakhand. Some tourism sites say 4,000 m, which is too high.

How high is Kedarkantha?

3,810 m (12,500 ft) in our dataset; some sources give 3,800 m. It is climbed from Sankri in Uttarkashi district, mostly in winter (December to April).

What is the height of Sandakphu?

3,636 m (11,929 ft). It is the highest point of West Bengal, on the Singalila Ridge, with views of Everest, Kangchenjunga, Lhotse and Makalu.

How high is Churdhar?

3,647 m (11,965 ft), in Sirmaur district of Himachal Pradesh. It is the highest peak of the outer Himalaya and sacred to Shirgul Maharaj.

Which is the easiest Himalayan summit in India?

Hatu Peak above Narkanda and Shikari Devi in Mandi can be reached by road or a short walk. Among real treks, Nag Tibba and Chandrashila are short.

Do I need a permit for a summit trek?

Not an IMF climbing permit. Wildlife sanctuary or national park entry and local rules apply, so check with the forest department.

Can beginners climb a 4,000 m summit?

Patalsu, Chanshal and Pangarchulla are trek summits, though Pangarchulla has an exposed boulder ridge. Tatakooti and Sunset Peak are graded moderate, with glacier and ridge sections.

What should I climb after a summit trek?

A 5,000 m trekking peak with a guide, such as Friendship Peak (5,289 m) above Manali.
