How high is Friendship Peak, and how hard is it to climb?

Friendship Peak (5,289 m / 17,352 ft) rises above the Solang valley near Manali, in the Pir Panjal range of Himachal Pradesh. It is one of the best-known 5,000 m peaks in India for a first Himalayan summit: about 8 days from Manali via Bakarthach and Lady Leg base camp, ending with a long summit day on steep snow with crampons, ice axe and rope. Climb it in May–June or September–October.

Route and camps

Route, camps and altitudes

The climb starts in Manali. A short drive up the Solang valley brings you to the Dhundi road-head, where the trail climbs through meadows to Bakarthach and then to Lady Leg, the base camp below the peak.

After an acclimatisation day with snow-craft practice at base camp, teams move up to a summit camp at about 4,200 m. Summit day starts in the dark: a long climb over moraine and snowfields to the steep snow slopes below the top, where you move roped to your guide with crampons and an ice axe. Most teams are back in camp by the afternoon, before the snow softens.

Typical itinerary

  4. Day 4 · AcclimatisationRest, snow-craft practice (crampons, ice axe, rope) and a load ferry towards summit camp.

What it takes

How hard is Friendship Peak?

By Himalayan standards Friendship Peak is a trekking peak: there are no vertical rock or ice walls. It is still a real climb. The upper slopes are steep snow, so you wear crampons, carry an ice axe and are usually roped to your guide, and summit day is long — 8 to 10 hours from summit camp and back, starting in the dark.

Most outfitters take fit trekkers without a formal mountaineering course and teach the basics at base camp. You should already have trekked above about 4,000 m. If you want technical peaks later, such as Deo Tibba or Hanuman Tibba, a Basic Mountaineering Course is the natural next step.

  • FitnessWalking 5–7 hours a day with a daypack, then one 8–10 hour summit day.
  • ExperienceAt least one high-altitude trek (about 4,000 m or more) before this climb.
  • Skills taught on the climbCrampon technique, ice-axe self-arrest, moving on a rope and descending steep snow.
  • AcclimatisationSleeping altitude rises in steps (3,300 m, 3,850 m, 4,200 m) with a rest day at base camp.
When to go

Best time to climb Friendship Peak

May and June bring full snow cover: the classic snow climb, with long days before the monsoon.

September and October are drier and clearer after the monsoon. The lower route has more rock and scree, and the nights are colder.

July and August are monsoon months on the Kullu side, with rain and poor visibility. In winter the route lies under deep snow; an Indian Army team climbed it in January 2015, but winter is for experienced teams only.

Permits

Permits for Friendship Peak

Himachal Pradesh requires trekking and mountaineering outfitters and their guides to be registered with HP Tourism, and every participant to be insured. Your outfitter arranges the local permissions for the climb and for camping on the route.

Foreign nationals also need permission from the Indian Mountaineering Foundation (IMF): apply at least 90 days ahead. The IMF fee for a team of two is US$200 on IMF-listed trekking peaks or US$500 on other peaks up to 6,500 m, plus liaison-officer charges; your outfitter confirms which applies. Full details are in our guide to climbing permits in India.

History

History

The date and party of the first ascent are not reliably recorded, and we could not verify where the peak's name comes from.

On 2 January 2015 an Indian Army team led by Major R.S. Jamwal reached the summit in deep winter, reported at the time as the first winter ascent, while training for the Army's 2015 Everest expedition. Today Friendship Peak is a popular first summit for climbers from India and abroad.

FAQ

Friendship Peak: questions climbers ask

How high is Friendship Peak?

5,289 m (17,352 ft), the most-cited figure. Wikipedia lists 5,287 m (17,346 ft).

How difficult is Friendship Peak?

It is one of the easier Himalayan snow summits, but a real climb: steep snow near the top, crampons and an ice axe, usually roped to a guide, and a summit day of 8–10 hours.

Can a beginner climb Friendship Peak without a mountaineering course?

Usually yes, if you are fit and have trekked above about 4,000 m. Guides teach crampon, ice-axe and rope basics at base camp. A Basic Mountaineering Course helps and is the next step for technical peaks.

How many days does the Friendship Peak climb take?

About 8 days from Manali, including an acclimatisation day at base camp and a reserve day for weather.

What is the best time to climb Friendship Peak?

May–June for a full snow climb, and September–October for drier, clearer weather. Avoid the July–August monsoon.

Do I need a permit to climb Friendship Peak?

Your registered outfitter handles the local permissions. Foreign nationals also need Indian Mountaineering Foundation permission, applied for at least 90 days ahead, and pay IMF fees.

Where does the Friendship Peak climb start?

At the Dhundi road-head in the Solang valley, a short drive from Manali. The route goes via Bakarthach and Lady Leg base camp.
