What is the highest peak in each Indian state?

Sikkim has India's highest state summit, Kangchenjunga (8,586 m / 28,169 ft), followed by Uttarakhand's Nanda Devi and Ladakh's Saltoro Kangri. Outside the Himalayas, the highest is Kerala's Anamudi (2,695 m). Karnataka's top is Mullayanagiri, Maharashtra's is Kalsubai. All 36 states and UTs are in the table below; the big ones are in our list of the highest peaks in India.

Every Indian state and union territory has a highest point, but they could hardly be more different. Six of them are serious Himalayan summits above 6,800 m; the rest range from Nagaland's Mount Saramati on the Myanmar border to Delhi's Deheri on the Aravalli ridge and a 15 m point on a Lakshadweep island.

Outside the Himalayas

Highest peaks in South India, Maharashtra and the plains states

Most searches for a state's highest peak come from outside the Himalaya. These are the answers people look for most, from the Western Ghats to the Aravallis.

Highest peak in South India: Anamudi

Anamudi, 2,695 m / 8,842 ft, in Kerala's Eravikulam National Park (Idukki district), is the highest peak in the Western Ghats and the highest point in India outside the Himalayas. It is also the answer for Kerala. The summit lies inside a protected national park, so check the park's current rules before planning any visit.

Highest peak in Karnataka: Mullayanagiri

Mullayanagiri, about 1,925 m / 6,316 ft (some sources give 1,930 m), in Chikkamagaluru district. It is the highest point of the Western Ghats north of the Nilgiris. A road runs almost to the top, with a short climb at the end.

Highest peak in Maharashtra: Kalsubai

Kalsubai, 1,646 m / 5,400 ft, in the Sahyadri on the Ahmednagar–Nashik border. The district administration calls it "the Everest of Maharashtra", and it is one of the state's most popular day treks.

Highest peak in Tamil Nadu: Doddabetta

Doddabetta, 2,637 m / 8,652 ft, the highest point of the Nilgiris, near Ooty. Unlike Anamudi, you can drive up to its viewpoint.

Rajasthan, Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh

Guru Shikhar (1,722 m / 5,650 ft) at Mount Abu tops the Aravalli Range and Rajasthan. Girnar (1,145 m / 3,757 ft) near Junagadh is Gujarat's highest, climbed on temple steps. Dhupgarh (1,352 m / 4,436 ft) at Pachmarhi is the top of the Satpuras and of Madhya Pradesh.

The Eastern Ghats and the east

Arma Konda (1,680 m / 5,512 ft) in Andhra Pradesh is the highest peak of the Eastern Ghats; Deomali (1,672 m / 5,486 ft) is Odisha's highest. In Jharkhand, Parasnath (1,365 m / 4,478 ft) is a Jain pilgrimage hill, also called Shikharji.

Read the question twice

Four answers that depend on how you ask

"India's highest peak is K2"

India's official map shows K2 (8,611 m / 28,251 ft) inside Ladakh, but K2 lies in Pakistan-administered Gilgit-Baltistan. On ground India administers, the highest peak is Kangchenjunga (8,586 m) in Sikkim.

"Ladakh's highest peak is K2"

For the same reason, lists differ. The highest peak Ladakh actually administers is Saltoro Kangri (7,742 m / 25,400 ft), on the Siachen AGPL. More on our Ladakh peaks page.

"Nun is in Ladakh"

The Nun Kun massif straddles the boundary and is approached from Kargil, but Nun (7,135 m / 23,409 ft) is listed as the highest point of Jammu & Kashmir.

"Nanda Devi is India's highest"

Only if you mean wholly within India. Nanda Devi (7,816 m / 25,643 ft) is the highest peak entirely inside the country; Kangchenjunga, on the Nepal border, is higher.

Unnamed high points. Six states and UTs (Assam, Punjab, Chandigarh, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu, Puducherry and Lakshadweep) have no named summit: their highest point is a survey point, so we do not give it a name.

From summits to viewpoints

Which state high points can you actually climb?

Only a handful of these 36 high points are real mountaineering objectives, and fewer still are open to climbers.

Closed or restricted

Kangchenjunga (sacred, banned since 2001), Nanda Devi (closed since 1983), Saltoro Kangri (military zone) and Kangto (remote border area, very few attempts).

Expedition peaks

Nun (J&K) is climbed by expeditions from the Suru valley. Reo Purgyil, the highest peak in Himachal Pradesh, is an expedition peak above Nako; foreigners need a Protected Area Permit.

Treks and day hikes

Sandakphu, Saramati, Kalsubai, Mullayanagiri, Arma Konda, Deomali and Saddle Peak are reached on foot. Doddabetta, Guru Shikhar, Shillong Peak and Dhupgarh are drive-up viewpoints.

Want to stand on a real Himalayan summit? Start in Himachal. Friendship Peak (5,289 m) and Yunam Peak (6,111 m) are classic first summits; Reo Purgyil is the state's big one. We pair you with a registered Himachal outfitter and certified guides.

At true height

Seven Himalayan state high points, drawn to scale

Seven states and UTs reach into the Himalaya: Sandakphu (3,636 m, West Bengal), Reo Purgyil (6,816 m, Himachal), Kangto (7,090 m, Arunachal), Nun (7,135 m, Jammu & Kashmir), Saltoro Kangri (7,742 m, Ladakh), Nanda Devi (7,816 m, Uttarakhand) and Kangchenjunga (8,586 m, Sikkim).

The gap after them is huge: the next-highest state summit, Mount Saramati in Nagaland, is 3,826 m, and Kerala's Anamudi, the highest point of the south, is under 2,700 m.

FAQ

Questions people ask about the highest peak in each state

Which is the highest peak in South India?

Anamudi, 2,695 m (8,842 ft), in Kerala's Eravikulam National Park. It is the highest peak in the Western Ghats and the highest point in India outside the Himalayas.

Which is the highest peak in Karnataka?

Mullayanagiri, about 1,925 m (some sources give 1,930 m), in Chikkamagaluru district.

Which is the highest peak in Maharashtra?

Kalsubai, 1,646 m (5,400 ft), in the Sahyadri on the Ahmednagar–Nashik border. It is often called the Everest of Maharashtra.

Which is the highest peak in Kerala?

Anamudi (2,695 m), inside Eravikulam National Park in Idukki district.

Which is the highest peak in Tamil Nadu?

Doddabetta, 2,637 m, the highest point of the Nilgiris, near Ooty.

Which is the highest peak in Rajasthan?

Guru Shikhar, 1,722 m, at Mount Abu, the highest point of the Aravalli Range.

Which state has the highest peak in India?

Sikkim, with Kangchenjunga (8,586 m). India's official map also shows K2 (8,611 m) in Ladakh, but K2 lies in Pakistan-administered Gilgit-Baltistan.

Which state or UT has the lowest highest point?

Lakshadweep: its highest point is an unnamed spot of about 15 m on Agatti Island. Delhi's highest point, Deheri on the Aravalli ridge, is 315 m.
