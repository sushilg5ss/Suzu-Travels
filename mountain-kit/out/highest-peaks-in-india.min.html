Quick answer

The 10 highest mountains in India

  1. Kangchenjunga — 8,586 m (28,169 ft), Sikkim
  2. Nanda Devi — 7,816 m (25,643 ft), Uttarakhand
  3. Kamet — 7,756 m (25,446 ft), Uttarakhand
  4. Saltoro Kangri — 7,742 m (25,400 ft), Ladakh
  5. Saser Kangri I — 7,672 m (25,171 ft), Ladakh
  6. Mamostong Kangri — 7,516 m (24,659 ft), Ladakh
  7. Saser Kangri II East — 7,513 m (24,649 ft), Ladakh
  8. Saser Kangri III — 7,495 m (24,590 ft), Ladakh
  9. Teram Kangri I — 7,462 m (24,482 ft), Ladakh
  10. Jongsong Peak — 7,462 m (24,482 ft), Sikkim

What about K2? India's official map shows K2 (8,611 m) inside Ladakh, but it stands in Pakistan-administered Gilgit-Baltistan and is climbed from Pakistan. This list covers peaks on ground India administers, so Kangchenjunga (8,586 m) comes first. The highest peak wholly inside India is Nanda Devi (7,816 m).

Method

How we built this list

We started from the Wikipedia lists of Indian peaks by state, then checked first ascents against the Himalayan Journal, the American Alpine Journal, Explorersweb and news reports for 2019–2026 events. Climbing status reflects IMF, state government and news sources as of 1 Oct 2026. Seasons, bases and grades are general guidance, not guarantees. First ascents are left blank where no reliable record was found; we never guess.

About this list

What are the 10 highest mountains in India?

Kangchenjunga (8,586 m), Nanda Devi (7,816 m), Kamet (7,756 m), Saltoro Kangri (7,742 m), Saser Kangri I (7,672 m), Mamostong Kangri (7,516 m), Saser Kangri II East (7,513 m), Saser Kangri III (7,495 m), Teram Kangri I (7,462 m) and Jongsong Peak (7,462 m) — ranked among major summits with at least 500 m of prominence.

Why isn't K2 listed as India's highest peak here?

K2 (8,611 m) is shown inside Ladakh on India's official map, but it stands in Gilgit-Baltistan, which Pakistan administers, and is climbed only from Pakistan. This list covers peaks on ground India administers, so Kangchenjunga (8,586 m) comes first.

How many peaks above 6,000 m are there in India?

No official total exists; estimates run to several hundred. Wikipedia's Uttarakhand list alone names 188 peaks between 6,000 and 6,999 m. Our list covers 123 notable summits above 6,000 m.

Which Indian peaks have never been climbed?

Peaks with no confirmed ascent include Apsarasas Kangri II and III in the Siachen, Shudu Tsenpa and Chombu in Sikkim, Nyegyi Kansang and Chiumo in Arunachal Pradesh, and the sacred Manimahesh Kailash in Himachal. Filter the table by 'Unclimbed' to see them all.
