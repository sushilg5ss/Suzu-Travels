Who are India's most famous mountaineers, and which climbs made history?

The best-known Indian mountaineers include Avtar Singh Cheema and Nawang Gombu, the first Indians on Everest (1965), Bachendri Pal, the first Indian woman (1984), and Gurdial Singh, who led independent India's first expedition. The Indian Himalaya's landmark first ascent is Trisul I (1907), the first 7,000 m summit ever climbed. Explore every peak in our full list.

Records in the Indian Himalaya fall into two kinds. The first is the mountains themselves: who first stood on each summit, starting with Trisul in 1907. The second is the people: the Indian climbers who opened Everest to India in 1965 and the women and young climbers who set new marks after them.

Both stories are full of half-remembered "facts". Quiz books often give the first 7,000 m summit as Kamet (it was Trisul), and Kangchenjunga is sometimes said to be climbable from India (it is not, today). Every date on this page comes from a named source listed at the bottom, and peak heights and years come from our dataset of 157 notable Indian peaks.

India on Everest

India on Everest: the milestones

Everest sits on the Nepal–China border, not in India, but India's Everest story is the backbone of its mountaineering records.

  1. 1965 · First Indians on topAvtar Singh Cheema and Nawang Gombu summit on 20 May. Nine Indians reach the top on that expedition.
  2. 1984 · First Indian womanBachendri Pal summits on 23 May.
  3. 1992–93 · Twice on EverestSantosh Yadav becomes the first woman in the world to climb it twice.
  4. 2010 · A teenage recordArjun Vajpai, then the youngest Indian on Everest.
  5. 2013 · Beyond limitsArunima Sinha, the first female amputee on the summit.
  6. 2014 · Youngest womanMalavath Purna, aged 13 years 11 months.
  7. 2017 · Twice in one seasonAnshu Jamsenpa summits twice within five days.
  8. 2026 · A crowded dayOn 21 May 2026, 274 climbers summited from Nepal in one day, a Nepal-side record; three Indians were among them.
Set the record straight

Three records people get wrong

"The first 7,000 m summit was Kamet"

Wrong. Trisul I was, in 1907. Kamet (1931) was the first summit above 25,000 ft, which is a different record.

"Kangchenjunga can be climbed from India"

Not today. Its 1977 second ascent came up from Sikkim, but Sikkim treats the mountain as sacred and banned climbing it in 2001. The 1955 first ascent was made from Nepal.

"The youngest Indian on Everest is…"

Sources disagree for men, so we name no overall record holder. Arjun Vajpai was the youngest Indian at the time (2010); Malavath Purna is the youngest female summiter.

Still waiting for a result. The NDRF "Shikhar" expedition to Jongsong Peak (7,462 m) ran from 4 September to 11 October 2026. No summit result had been published when we checked on 31 October 2026, so we do not list one.

At true height

Landmark first ascents, drawn to scale

Five summits that each set a milestone, drawn at their true heights: Trisul I (7,120 m, 1907), Kangto (7,090 m, 2025), Kamet (7,756 m, 1931), Nanda Devi (7,816 m, 1936) and Kangchenjunga (8,586 m, 1955, from Nepal).

Height was never the whole story. Trisul is lower than Kamet but set the older record, and Kangto waited until 2025 because it sits in a remote border state.

FAQ

Questions people ask about Indian mountaineering records

Who was the first Indian to climb Mount Everest?

Avtar Singh Cheema and Nawang Gombu, on 20 May 1965, on the Indian Everest Expedition led by M.S. Kohli. Nine members of that team reached the summit.

Who was the first Indian woman to climb Everest?

Bachendri Pal, on 23 May 1984.

Which was the first 7,000 m peak ever climbed?

Trisul I (7,120 m / 23,360 ft) in Uttarakhand, on 12 June 1907, by Tom Longstaff, Alexis and Henri Brocherel and Karbir. It is often wrongly given as Kamet, which in 1931 became the first summit above 25,000 ft.

Who are the famous Indian mountaineers?

Among the best known: Nawang Gombu, Avtar Singh Cheema, M.S. Kohli, Gurdial Singh, Bachendri Pal, Santosh Yadav, Arunima Sinha, Malavath Purna, Anshu Jamsenpa, Baljeet Kaur, Priyanka Mohite and Arjun Vajpai.

Who is the youngest Indian to climb Everest?

Sources disagree for men, so we name no overall record holder. Malavath Purna is the youngest female summiter (13 years 11 months, 2014), and Arjun Vajpai was the youngest Indian at the time of his 2010 climb.

Who first climbed Nanda Devi?

Bill Tilman and Noel Odell, on 29 August 1936. It was the highest summit climbed until 1950. Nanda Devi has been closed to climbers since 1983.

Has Kangchenjunga been climbed from India?

Yes, once: its 1977 second ascent, by an Indian Army team, used the north-east spur from Sikkim. Today Sikkim treats the mountain as sacred and has banned climbing it since 2001.

What is the most recent first ascent of a major Indian peak?

Kangto (7,090 m / 23,261 ft), the highest peak of Arunachal Pradesh, first climbed by an Indian Army Eastern Command team on 26 November 2025.

Which mountaineer from Himachal Pradesh holds records?

Baljeet Kaur of Solan: the first Indian woman on Manaslu without supplemental oxygen (2022), and now described as the first Indian woman with seven 8,000 m peaks.
