Quick answer

Paragliding in Bir Billing at a glance

Tandem flights take off from Billing (about 2,400 m) and land at Bir, in Kangra district. Flights run from mid-September to June and stop for the monsoon from 15 July to 15 September. A joyride lasts about 15–30 minutes. Children under 12 or under 30 kg cannot fly. Only HP-registered pilots may fly paying passengers.

What happens

Your flight, step by step

  1. Drive up to Billing

    A 14 km mountain road from Bir to the take-off ridge.

  2. Briefing & gear

    Harness, helmet and a short run-and-lift briefing from your pilot.

  3. Take-off

    A few running steps and you are airborne over the valley.

  4. Land in Bir

    Touch down at the landing field, then tea in the Tibetan colony.

Billing → Bir, the flight path

Take-off at around 2,400 m, landing near 1,500 m. Pilots stop flying an hour before sunset.

FAQ

Paragliding in Bir Billing — questions

Is paragliding in Bir Billing safe for first-timers?

Yes, tandem flights are made for beginners — you fly with a registered pilot who controls the glider. Choose registered operators only; we check this for you.

When is paragliding closed in Bir Billing?

Every year from 15 July to 15 September for the monsoon, by government order. Flights also stop on windy or rainy days.

What is the minimum age and weight?

HP rules do not allow passengers under 12 years or under 30 kg. Heavier passengers should mention their weight when asking for a quote.
