Summer Hill half-day

Potter Hill loop + Chadwick Falls in one picture

The 4.7 km Potter Hill loop (rated Easy on the HP trekking portal) starts at the Summer Hill police post. Chadwick Falls lies below it: a short forest walk from the road-head, at about 1,586 m, with most water from July to October.

Go in daylight, stay off wet rocks by the pool and be out before dusk. We plan the cab from your hotel and the timing.
