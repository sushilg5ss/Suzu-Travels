## Answer-first paragraph (≈65 words)
Kuppar Bugyal is a high meadow at about 3,200 m above Kharapathar, roughly 75 km from Shimla on the Shimla–Rohru road. From Kharapathar a 6–7 km track climbs to the Giri Ganga temple (source of the Giri river), then an unmarked 3–5 km forest trail reaches the meadow. It is easy to moderate but needs a local guide. Best: October and March–June; snow-bound December–March.

## Quick facts
1. Start: Kharapathar (~2,700 m), Shimla district, on the Shimla–Theog–Kotkhai–Rohru road.
2. Route: Kharapathar → Giri Ganga temple (6–7 km track; 4x4 or walk) → Kuppar Bugyal (3–5 km forest climb).
3. Top: meadow ~3,200 m (Indiahikes altimeter reading; travelcoffee same).
4. Time: day hike possible (~10–12 km one way from Kharapathar if you walk the track); easier as an overnight at Kharapathar.
5. Best time: October; March–June (flowers Apr–Jun). Snow Dec–Mar (walk-only track). Avoid monsoon.

## Steps
1. Share dates + group on WhatsApp / quote form.
2. Day 1: Shimla → Kharapathar by cab (~75 km; HPTDC says ~2 h, allow 3 h+ on narrow stretches / in snow); stay at Kharapathar.

5. Descend to Giri Ganga → Kharapathar; drive on to Hatkoti/Rohru or back to Shimla next morning.

## Safety checks (specific)
- **Unmarked trail:** no signs or markers; trail forks — hire a local guide (both Indiahikes and travelcoffee stress this).
- **Bears:** Himalayan black/brown bear sightings reported; Indiahikes reports camping was temporarily disallowed by a Forest Officer for this reason. Walk in a group, daylight only, no food waste.
- **Camping:** only with HP Forest Department permission (enquire at Kharapathar forest office) — do not promise camping on the page.
- **No signal / no ATM / no fuel** after/at Kharapathar (travelcoffee) — plan cash and fuel at Theog/Kotkhai.
- **Water:** none on the climb; carry 2 L.
- **Winter driving:** Shimla–Rohru road crosses the Kharapathar ridge — snow can block it after heavy snowfall (Tribune winter road-closure reports for Shimla district); 4x4/chains as advised on the day.
- **HP trekking rules:** no polythene; don't disturb wildlife; register details with the guide/host; nearest hospital Rohru (~30 km).

## FAQ
1. **Where does the Kuppar Bugyal trek start?** At Kharapathar on the Shimla–Rohru road; a 6–7 km track leads to Giri Ganga temple, where the forest climb to the meadow begins.
2. **How high is Kuppar Bugyal?** About 3,200 m (altimeter reading reported by Indiahikes). Kharapathar is about 2,700 m.
3. **How far is Kharapathar from Shimla?** About 75 km (HPTDC); HPTDC says about two hours, but allow three hours or more on narrow sections and in winter.
4. **Is Kuppar Bugyal trek difficult?** Easy to moderate in fitness terms, but the trail is unmarked and forks, so go with a local guide.
5. **Can I do it in winter?** December–March the meadow and track are under snow and the approach is walk-only. Go only with an experienced local guide; most visitors enjoy a snow day at Kharapathar instead.
6. **Can I camp at Kuppar Bugyal?** Only with HP Forest Department permission; camping has been stopped at times because of bear sightings. Ask us and we check before you go.
7. **What is Giri Ganga?** A sacred spot and the source of the Giri river about 7 km above Kharapathar, with old Durga, Shiva and Kali shrines around a holy kund.
8. **Where to stay?** Kharapathar has HPTDC's Giriganga Resort, a forest rest house and small guesthouses; we book what is available for your dates.
