## Answer-first paragraph (≈60 words)
Chadwick Falls is a forest waterfall below Summer Hill, about 5–7 km from central Shimla, at roughly 1,586 m. Vehicles get close to the entry point and a short walk (about 10 minutes, per trawell) leads through thick forest to the falls. It is an easy, listed HP trekking-portal route. Go July–October for real water, and walk out well before sunset.

## Quick facts (5–6)
1. Where: below Summer Hill, Shimla (HP University side); HP trekking-portal route "Chadwick fall" = Shimla → Summer Hill → Chadwick fall and back.
2. Altitude: ~1,586 m (HP Tourism Shimla district site + trekking portal). Fall height is quoted as 86 m (trawell, Tripoto) — say "often quoted as about 86 m".
3. Difficulty: Easy (HP trekking portal); 1 day / half-day.
4. Walk: short — roughly 10 minutes from the road-head entry point (trawell); longer if you walk from Summer Hill village (trail via Hyun/Huen village side).
5. Best time: July–October (post-monsoon water); little water in dry summer and in winter.
6. Price: Get Quote

## Safety checks (specific)
- **Daylight:** walk out well before sunset — Holidify reports leopards seen in the area after dark. No night visits.
- **Wet rocks:** HP trekking portal rule — no photos/selfies on river banks and other dangerous places; stay off slippery rocks at the pool.
- **Wildlife & waste:** don't feed or approach animals; no food left out; polythene is banned in HP (portal DO/DON'T list).
- **Monsoon:** check road/landslide status on the day; skip during heavy rain alerts.
- **Help:** nearest police Boileauganj; CHC Boileauganj and PHC Summer Hill; IGMC Shimla for anything serious (HP trekking portal 151/152).
- **Kids/elders:** short walk is fine with steady footing; carry water (no eateries near the falls per Holidify).

## FAQ (verified answers)
1. **How far is Chadwick Falls from Shimla?** Roughly 5–7 km from central Shimla, below Summer Hill; trawell gives 5 km from Shimla railway station and HP Tourism's Shimla district page gives about 7 km from the city centre.
2. **How long is the walk to Chadwick Falls?** Vehicles can get close to the entry point and the walk from there is short — about 10 minutes through thick forest (trawell). Walking all the way from Summer Hill takes longer.
3. **When does Chadwick Falls have water?** Best after the monsoon, July–October. It carries little water in the dry summer and in winter.
4. **How high is Chadwick Falls?** It is often quoted as about 86 m high. The 1,586 m figure many sites give is the altitude above sea level, not the fall height.
5. **Is Chadwick Falls safe?** It is an easy route (HP trekking portal), but rocks near the water are slippery and leopards are reported in the area after dark — go in daylight, ideally in a group, and stay off wet rocks.
6. **Is there an entry fee or fixed timing?** We found no official fee or timing notice; go in daylight and check locally on the day. (Do not state "free" or "open 24 hours" as fact.)
7. **Can I combine it with Potter Hill?** Yes — the official 4.7 km Potter Hill loop starts at the Summer Hill police post and passes above Chadwick Falls.
8. **Is it worth visiting in December?** Only as a forest walk — the falls run thin in winter. In December most visitors pair Shimla with Kufri or Narkanda snow and the Shimla ice rink.
