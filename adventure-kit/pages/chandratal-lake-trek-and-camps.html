Quick answer

Chandratal Lake, at a glance

Chandratal is a crescent-shaped glacial lake at 4,337 m in Lahaul, listed as a Ramsar wetland in 2005. From Manali you cross the Atal Tunnel, follow the Chandra river to Batal, then take a 14 km rough track to the parking and walk 1.5–2 km to the shore. It opens only mid-June to early October; tents stay well back from the lake.

How it works

How a Chandratal trip works, step by step

  1. Acclimatise first

    Spend 1–2 nights in Manali (or Kaza if you come from Spiti) before going up to 4,300 m+.

  2. Early start

    Cross the Atal Tunnel and drive the Chandra valley via Gramphu and Chhatru to Batal — a long day's drive.

  3. 4x4 track

    A 4x4 or high-clearance car takes the 14 km rough track from Batal to the parking — or you trek it.

  4. Walk to the shore

    1.5–2 km to the lake; the walk around it takes about 1.5 hours.

  5. Night or return

    Night at the camp area well back from the lake, or carry on to Batal or the Kaza side.

  6. Onward

    Over Kunzum La to Kaza for the Spiti circuit, or back through the Atal Tunnel to Manali.

Manali to Chandratal

  • By road → Manali → Atal Tunnel → Gramphu → Chhatru → Batal.
  • By 4x4 → 14 km rough track from Batal to the parking.
  • On foot → 1.5–2 km to the shore at 4,337 m.
  • Or trek in from Batal (~14 km) or along the Kunzum La ridge.

Safety & rules

What we check before we book your Chandratal trip

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange the 4x4, camp and trek with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • AltitudeAt 4,300 m+, first-timers should not do Manali → lake → Manali in one day. Climb high, sleep low, and go down at any sign of altitude sickness.
  • RoadThe Batal track needs a 4x4 or high-clearance vehicle; glacial streams are crossed in the morning and we drive in daylight only.
  • Lake rulesNo camping or fires on the shore, no swimming, carry all waste back. Chandratal is a Ramsar wetland; trekking pressure and litter are its listed threats.
  • Forest entry feeThe HP Forest Department charges an entry and activity fee at Chandratal (since June 2025). We include it in your quote.
  • Winter banLahaul-Spiti district orders ban trekking and mountaineering in winter — we only run the trip in the open season.
  • No networkMobile network is mostly absent between Gramphu and the lake: tell family your plan and carry cash.

FAQ

Chandratal Lake — questions

How high is Chandratal Lake?

About 4,337 m (14,230 ft), per its Ramsar wetland listing.

When is Chandratal open?

Roughly mid-June to early October. Snow closes Kunzum and the Batal side from late October to May, so the next season opens in summer 2027.

Can you camp at Chandratal Lake?

Not on the shore. Tents are at the camp area a few kilometres before the lake, and you walk in to see it.

How far is the walk from the parking?

1.5–2 km one way — about 30–45 minutes on easy ground, but slower at this altitude.

How do you reach Chandratal from Manali?

Through the Atal Tunnel to Gramphu, along the Chandra valley via Chhatru to Batal, then a 14 km rough track to the parking.

Is Chandratal a trek?

It can be: Batal to Chandratal is about 14 km on foot, and a ridge trail comes down from Kunzum La. Most visitors drive and walk the last 1.5–2 km.

Is there an entry fee?

Yes, the HP Forest Department charges an entry fee at Chandratal (since June 2025). We include it in your quote.

Is there mobile network at Chandratal?

Mostly none between Gramphu and the lake. Plan offline and keep someone informed.
