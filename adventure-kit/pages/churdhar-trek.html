Quick answer

Churdhar trek, at a glance

Churdhar (3,647 m) is the highest peak of the outer Himalaya and the home of Shirgul Maharaj, in Sirmaur's Churdhar Sanctuary. You trek from Nohradhar (about 14–18 km one way, 2 days) or the shorter Sarain–Chaupal side (about 7–10 km). It is open roughly from Baisakhi in April to late November; the trek is usually banned from about 1 December.

Height
3,647 m (11,965 ft) — the highest point in Sirmaur and in the outer Himalaya
Routes
Nohradhar (Sirmaur, via Solan and Rajgarh) · Sarain (Chaupal, via Theog) · Pulbahal (third entry point)
Days & distance
Nohradhar about 14–18 km one way, usually 2 days; Sarain about 7–10 km, a long day for fit walkers
Difficulty
Easy–moderate: a long, steady climb of over 1,500 m; the last stretch is rocky

Two ways up to 3,647 m

  • Long route → Chandigarh → Solan → Rajgarh → Nohradhar, then about 14–18 km on foot.
  • Short route → Shimla → Theog → Chaupal → Sarain, then about 7–10 km on foot.
  • Both meet near the Shirgul Maharaj temple, just below the top.
  • Closed from about 1 December until Baisakhi in April.

Safety

What we check before we book your trek

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange the guide with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • Winter banThe Sirmaur administration usually closes the trek from about 1 December until Baisakhi, and walking it then can bring legal action. We confirm the order for your dates.
  • Sanctuary feesTrek, temple and camping fees are collected at Nohradhar, Sarain and Pulbahal. We include them in your quote.
  • Fog in the forestPeople do get lost in fog. Stay on the main trail, go with a guide, and start down by early afternoon.
  • Weather near the topIt turns fast even in May — carry a warm layer, a rain shell and a torch.
  • Patchy signalPhone signal is weak in the forest; do not rely on GPS alone.
  • Temple customsNo alcohol on the trek, and follow the footwear rules at the shrine.

FAQ

Churdhar trek — questions

How high is Churdhar?

Churdhar peak is 3,647 m (11,965 ft), the highest point in Sirmaur district and the highest peak of the outer Himalaya.

How long is the Churdhar trek?

From Nohradhar it is about 14–18 km one way and usually takes 2 days. From Sarain on the Chaupal side it is about 7–10 km one way.

Which route is best, Nohradhar or Sarain?

Nohradhar is the classic forest route if you come from Chandigarh, Solan or Rajgarh. Sarain is shorter and better if you come from Shimla.

Is Churdhar open in December?

No. The district administration usually bans the trek from about 1 December until the temple reopens on Baisakhi in April. We confirm the order in force for your dates.

What is the best time for the Churdhar trek?

May–June and September–November. Avoid the July–August monsoon, when the trail has fog, mud, leeches and slides.

Is there an entry fee for Churdhar?

Yes. The sanctuary collects trek, temple and camping fees at the Nohradhar, Sarain and Pulbahal entry points. We include them in your quote.

Can beginners do the Churdhar trek?

Yes, if you are fit. It is a long, steady climb of over 1,500 m, not a technical one, with a rocky last stretch. Take 3 days if you are new to the hills.
