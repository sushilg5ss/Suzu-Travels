First jump? That's normal

Most jumpers are first-timers. The jump master fits your gear, explains the count and the position, and the crew lowers you down after the rebound.

Wear comfortable clothes and closed shoes, empty your pockets and tie long hair back. Tell us the ages and weights in your group when you book.

Safety first

What we check before we book your jump

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange bungee only with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • Established site onlyJump only with a registered operator at an established site; we never book roadside or 'new' jumps.
  • Weigh-in on the dayThe operator weighs every jumper; you must meet the age and weight limits on the day.
  • Health firstDo not jump with heart conditions, high blood pressure, epilepsy, recent surgery, a back or neck injury, or during pregnancy (standard bungee exclusions — the operator has the final word).
  • Clear headNo alcohol before the jump; follow every instruction from the jump master.
  • Weather bufferWeather or wind can pause jumps; we keep a buffer in your plan.

Is bungee jumping available in Manali?

We have not found a verified, operating bungee site in Manali. A 182 m site near Marhi was announced in 2019 but never opened. Himachal's verified jump is at Bir.

Where is bungee jumping in Himachal?

Near Bir in Kangra district, in the Slabak / Gunaid area — a site set up in 2018.

How high is the Bir bungee jump?

Operator listings give about 50 m. We confirm the exact height and setup on booking.

What are the age and weight limits?

The operator sets them; published figures vary (about 12/13–55 years, 40–100/110 kg). We confirm for your date before you travel.

Can I do bungee and paragliding on the same day?

Yes, both are at Bir; plan the jump and the tandem flight as one Bir day.
