Quick answer

Chandrakhani Pass trek, at a glance

Chandrakhani Pass (3,660 m) is a ridge-top pass in Kullu district linking Rumsu, above Naggar, with Malana on the Parvati side. Most groups walk 4–5 days and 20–26 km from Rumsu through deodar forest to meadow camps like Naya Tapru and Chaklani, reach the pass and return; HP Tourism's route continues down to Malana and Jari. It is graded easy–moderate and runs May to October.

Day by day

How the Chandrakhani Pass trek goes

  1. Day 1 — to Rumsu

    Drive from Manali or Naggar to Rumsu and walk a short first stretch to camp.

  2. Day 2 — into the meadows

    Climb through deodar forest to the Naya Tapru / Ghan Kuradi meadows.

  3. Day 3 — high camp

    On to Chaklani meadow below the ridge; an easy day to settle in.

  4. Day 4 — the pass

    Summit Chandrakhani Pass, then back to the meadows — or down towards Nagruni Thach on the Malana route.

  5. Day 5 — down

    Back to Rumsu and the drive to Manali or Kullu; or Malana → Jari → Kasol / Manikaran.

  6. Before and after

    We add hotel nights in Naggar, Manali or Kasol and the cab at both ends.

Safety & rules

What we check before we book your trek

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange the trek with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • TMS registrationKullu district requires treks to be registered on the HP Trekking Management System (trekking.hp.gov.in), run by registered operators. We arrange it with a registered local operator.
  • ID and fitnessCarry photo ID (forest check posts ask for it) and a medical certificate.
  • Pass restrictionsDistrict orders can still restrict treks after accidents or weather alerts — we check the latest status before your date.
  • Trail rulesNo polythene, no open defecation, no selfies on river banks (HP Tourism TMS rules).
  • Malana villageMalana follows its own deity's rules: do not touch people, temples, walls or belongings, follow the notice boards, ask before photos, no liquor. Overnight stays may not be allowed inside the village.
  • Weather turn-backNo pass attempt in fresh snow or thunder — the guide decides on the day.

FAQ

Chandrakhani Pass trek — questions

What is the height of Chandrakhani Pass?

HP Tourism's trekking portal lists 3,660 m; trek operators quote about 12,100–12,185 ft.

How many days does the Chandrakhani Pass trek take?

HP Tourism lists 4 days minimum; most groups take 5 days, about 20–26 km in total.

Where does the trek start?

At Rumsu village above Naggar, about an hour's drive from Manali.

Is the Chandrakhani Pass trek easy?

It is graded easy (HP Tourism) to easy–moderate (operators) — a good first Himalayan pass for fit beginners.

What is the best time for Chandrakhani Pass?

May to October. May–early June has snow near the top, September–October the clearest views. It is snowbound in winter, so book now for the 2027 season.

Can I go to Malana via Chandrakhani Pass?

Yes, HP Tourism's itinerary descends to Malana and Jari. Malana has strict village rules — no touching people or property, follow the posted notices.

Do I need a permit?

Register the trek on HP's Trekking Management System and carry ID; we arrange it with a registered local operator.
