Quick answer

Chanshal Pass, at a glance

Chanshal Pass (about 3,750 m) is the high road from Rohru to the Dodra Kwar valley in Shimla district, about 160 km from Shimla via Hatkoti and Rohru. It is open to traffic roughly May to November and snowbound the rest of the year. The last stretch above Larot is rough, so a high-clearance SUV and an early start are best.

FAQ

Chanshal Pass — questions

How high is Chanshal Pass?

About 3,750 m; the Shimla district site gives 3,755 m. Chanshal Peak above it (about 4,520 m) is the highest peak of Shimla district.

How far is Chanshal Pass from Shimla?

About 160 km via Kharapathar and Hatkoti, or about 175 km via Narkanda — both through Rohru. Plan 7–9 hours.

When is Chanshal Pass open?

Roughly May to November. Snow closes the top for the rest of the year; the Rohru and Larot side stays reachable.

Can a normal car go to Chanshal Pass?

Up to Rohru and Chirgaon, yes. The stretch above Larot is rough and steep, so a high-clearance SUV is the safer choice.

Where do I stay for Chanshal Pass?

Rohru has the most hotels and is the usual base. Larot has basic homestays. There is no stay at the top.

Can I go to Dodra Kwar by road?

Do not plan on it. The road beyond the pass is seasonal and often not motorable; we check the status before you go.

Is there a Chanshal trek?

Yes — a multi-day trek from Larot over the pass to Kwar, best in May–June and mid-September–October. We arrange it with registered local operators on request.
