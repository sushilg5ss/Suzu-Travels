Quick answer

Pin Bhaba Pass trek, at a glance

The Pin Bhaba Pass trek crosses the main Himalayan ridge from Kafnu in Kinnaur's Bhaba Valley to Mudh in Spiti's Pin Valley. It covers about 50 km in five to six trekking days, with the pass at about 4,900 m. It opens only from late June to mid-September and suits fit trekkers with some high-altitude experience.

Start
Kafnu, Bhaba Valley, Kinnaur (about 2,400 m) — about 200 km from Shimla, 8–10 hours by road
End
Mudh, Pin Valley, Spiti (about 3,800 m); then a drive of about 50 km to Kaza
Camps

Safety

What we check before we book your trek

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange the trek with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • AltitudeA crossing at about 4,900 m: the plan builds in acclimatisation, and anyone with signs of altitude sickness goes down.
  • Point of no returnThere is no easy bail-out once you leave Phutsirang, so the go/no-go call is made there with the guide.
  • Stream crossingsSeveral crossings run fast and wide in the afternoon and after rain — the crew crosses early in the day.
  • RockfallIn August 2019 a landslide above Phutsirang blocked the pass for about two weeks and groups were re-routed. We check the trail status before every departure.
  • Road bufferThe Shimla–Kafnu road along the Sutlej sees landslide closures in July–August; keep a spare day.
  • Permits and IDRegistration and permits for your group are arranged through the operator — carry government ID. Foreign nationals: send your passport country first and we check the rules before quoting.

FAQ

Pin Bhaba Pass trek — questions

How high is the Pin Bhaba Pass?

About 4,890–4,910 m (around 16,000–16,100 ft); sources differ slightly.

Where does the Pin Bhaba trek start and end?

It starts at Kafnu in Kinnaur's Bhaba Valley (about 2,400 m) and ends at Mudh in Spiti's Pin Valley (about 3,800 m).

How many days does it take?

About five to six days on the trail and roughly eight days from Shimla to Kaza, covering about 50 km on foot.

What is the best time for the Pin Bhaba Pass trek?

Late June to mid-September. July and August are the main months; from October to May the pass is under snow.

Is the Pin Bhaba Pass trek difficult?

It is moderate to difficult: long days, stream crossings and a high pass. Some high-altitude trekking experience helps.

Is there a road from Kafnu to Mudh?

Not yet — the trek is the only direct link. From Mudh you drive about 50 km to Kaza.

How do I reach Kafnu?

By road from Shimla along the Sutlej via Rampur and Wangtu, about 200 km and 8–10 hours. We add the cab to your quote.
