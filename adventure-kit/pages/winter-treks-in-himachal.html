Quick answer

Winter treks in Himachal at a glance

In winter, Himachal's safe treks are the low and mid-height ones: Prashar Lake, Jogini Waterfall, Dainkund, Kalatop, Bijli Mahadev, Sethan and Shangarh. Snow usually lies December to February. High passes are shut, Lahaul-Spiti has barred trekking since a November 2024 order, Churdhar closes from about 1 December, and Kangra bars trekking above 3,000 m.

How high, and is it open?

  • Below ~2,800 m → the open snow walks: Jogini, Bijli Mahadev, Kalatop, Prashar, Dainkund, Sethan.
  • Around 3,000 m → Triund, Kareri, Serolsar: ask first, permission or road.
  • Above 3,000 m → Indrahar, Churdhar, Shikari Devi, Kamrunag and the passes: closed till spring.

Safety & area rules

Rules we check before every winter trek

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). Before your trek we check:

  • Kangra winter orderRecent Kangra winter orders bar trekking above 3,000 m and need prior SP Kangra permission for Kareri, Triund and Adi Himani Chamunda; any IMD warning cancels permits. We check the order in force for your dates.
  • Kangra check postsTrekkers register at Kangra's check posts at 10 trek entry points where required (November 2025 order).
  • Lahaul-SpitiA November 2024 district order prohibits all trekking and mountaineering until further orders — no winter treks there.
  • Churdhar (Sirmaur)Closed from about 1 December to Baisakhi; walking it in the closure can bring legal action.
  • Snow kitBoots or gaiters, microspikes on iced trails, warm layers; a guide is a must on Serolsar and any snow trail with drop-offs.
  • DaylightStart by 8–9 am and be down two hours before dark.

FAQ

Winter treks in Himachal — questions

Which treks in Himachal are open in winter?

The low and mid-height ones: Prashar Lake, Jogini Waterfall, Dainkund, Kalatop, Bijli Mahadev, Sethan and Shangarh, plus the lower Kasol trails. Snow usually lies from December to February.

Is Triund open in winter?

Only with prior permission from the SP Kangra under the district winter order. Trekking above 3,000 m (the snowline beyond Triund) is barred, and any weather warning cancels the permission. See the Triund trek guide.

Can I trek in Lahaul-Spiti in winter?

No. A district order of 27 November 2024 prohibits all trekking and mountaineering in Lahaul-Spiti until further orders. We check each season whether it still applies.

Is Churdhar open in December?

No. The Sirmaur administration closes Churdhar from about 1 December until Baisakhi in April. We can book it for spring — see the Churdhar trek guide.

Which winter trek is best for beginners?

Prashar Lake — about 7.5–8 km from Baggi to 2,730 m — for a day or an overnight, or short walks like Jogini Waterfall and Dainkund for half a day.

When is the snow most reliable?

January and February. December snow is likely on the 2,400–2,800 m walks from mid-month, but it varies from year to year.

Is Kareri Lake frozen in winter?

Yes, usually from early December to March, but a winter visit depends on that year's Kangra order and prior permission.

What gear do I need?

Snow boots or gaiters, microspikes for icy stretches, warm layers and gloves. Boots and spikes can be arranged on request — see snow suit and boots on rent.
