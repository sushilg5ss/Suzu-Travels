Quick answer

River rafting in Shimla at a glance

River rafting near Shimla happens at Tattapani, on the Satluj about 55 km from Shimla, in Mandi district's Karsog area. Rafting started here in 2002 and about 20 rafts now run. Since the Kol Dam reservoir formed in 2015, Tattapani also has a lake with jet skis and speed boats, and the old sulphur hot springs. It all fits in one day from Shimla.

No experience needed

Trips are guided for first-timers. The guide steers and calls the strokes; you paddle and hold on through the rapids.

Wear quick-dry clothes and shoes that strap on, and carry a full change of clothes and a towel — you will get wet. Tell us about any health issue and the ages in your group when you book.

Safety first

HP rules that apply at Tattapani

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange rafting and lake rides with registered local operators and plan around these rules:

  • Registered operators onlyRafting is run by operators registered with HP Tourism under the HP Tourism Development & Registration Act 2002 and the HP River Rafting Rules 2005.
  • Life jacket + helmet, rescue backupBoth are compulsory, and every rafting trip needs rescue cover — two rescue rafts, or a raft plus a kayak (Rule 11(b), HP River Rafting Rules 2005).
  • Certified boat driversLake rides fall under the HP Water Sports and Allied Activities Rules 2021: motor-boat and jet-ski drivers need a power-boat handling certificate from the National Institute of Water Sports.
  • Certified crews, every timeThe HP High Court (Sep 2024) stopped one Kol Dam reservoir operator for an uncertified boat driver — another reason we book only registered operators with certified crews.
  • Rain days & high waterNo rafting or lake rides on rain-day suspensions or high water.
  • Kids & seniorsTell us the ages in your group; the guide decides on the day.

FAQ

Rafting at Tattapani near Shimla — questions

Where is river rafting in Shimla?

At Tattapani on the Satluj, about 55 km from Shimla, in the Karsog area of Mandi district. Shimla town itself has no river rafting.

Is rafting still on at Tattapani after Kol Dam?

Yes. The Kol Dam reservoir (2015) turned part of the river into a lake, but rafting that started in 2002 still runs, with about 20 rafts (Dec 2024).

What water sports are there at Tattapani?

On the Kol Dam lake: jet ski, speed boat, bumper boat, banana ride, e-foil and water ski, plus ATV rides on the shore.

How far is Tattapani from Shimla?

About 55 km, so rafting, a lake ride and the hot springs fit into one day.

Are the hot springs still there?

Yes, Tattapani is known for its sulphur-rich hot springs; the original spring was inaugurated by President Rajendra Prasad in 1952.

Is it safe for beginners?

Trips are guided; life jacket and helmet are compulsory and every rafting trip needs a rescue raft or kayak under HP rules.

When is the best time?

October to June; monsoon months often see rain-day stops. We confirm the river is running before you go.
