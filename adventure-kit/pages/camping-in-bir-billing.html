Quick answer

Camping in Bir Billing, at a glance

Camping in Bir Billing means one of three zones: farm-field camps around Bir village (about 1,450–1,500 m, open most of the year), ridge camps near the Billing take-off (about 2,400 m, 14 km up the road) and Rajgundha valley (about 2,700 m, a 12–14 km walk). October–November and March–June are best; in December–February stay low at Bir — Billing gets snow and sub-zero nights.

Which camp for your month?

  • Oct–Nov → every zone is good; the clearest skies and flying days.
  • Dec–Feb → stay at a Bir village camp; Billing gets snow, Rajgundha is snowed in.
  • Mar–Jun → all zones; Rajgundha opens from March–April, May–Jun is busiest.
  • Jul–mid-Sep → wet; no flying, and skip the ridge and Rajgundha.

Rules & safety

What we check before we book your camp

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange camps with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • Registered camps onlyWe book registered or designated camps — no wild camping on forest land without permission, and no open fires outside the camp's fire pit.
  • Rajgundha check postRajgundha is one of Kangra's 10 trek check posts (set up in November 2025): register before you walk, and go with a local guide.
  • Winter roadNo sedan up the Billing road on ice — 4x4 or walk, and only when the road is open. Sleeping bags rated below zero.
  • Paragliding add-onRegistered pilots at the notified Bir Billing site only; no passengers under 12 years or 30 kg (HP Aero Sports Rules 2022, amended 2025).
  • Monsoon closuresNo flying from 15 July to 15 September; skip ridge and Rajgundha nights in the monsoon (landslides, flash streams).
  • Bonfires & litterBonfire only where the camp is allowed to light one; carry out all your waste.

FAQ

Camping in Bir Billing — questions

Where can you camp in Bir Billing?

In three zones: the fields around Bir village (Bir, Gharnala and Chowgan side), near the Billing take-off ridge, and in Rajgundha valley behind Billing.

Can you camp at Billing in December?

Billing gets snow and sub-zero nights from December to February, and the road can ice over. We suggest a Bir village camp and a day trip up to Billing when the road is open.

How far is Billing from Bir?

14 km by road, or a steep footpath of about 7 km.

Is paragliding included in camping packages?

Not always — the tandem flight is usually a separate add-on. We quote the camp and the paragliding flight together so you know exactly what is included.

When is paragliding closed in Bir Billing?

Every year from 15 July to 15 September (the monsoon ban), and on bad-weather days.

How cold is Bir camping in winter?

Days are mild, but nights drop to around freezing at Bir and below zero at Billing. Carry thermals and a warm sleeping bag.

Can we trek and camp at Rajgundha?

Yes, in March–June and September–November. It is a 12–14 km walk from Billing, and you register at the Rajgundha check post before you go.
