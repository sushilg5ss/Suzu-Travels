Quick answer

Gilbert Trail, Kasauli, at a glance

The Gilbert Trail is an easy, roughly 1.5 km nature walk on the wooded cliffside of Kasauli in Solan district. Most guides start it near the Kasauli Club and take it towards the Air Force Station area and Sunset Point. Allow one to three hours with stops. Go early for birds and quiet, and avoid wet or foggy days.

What to carry

  • Shoes with good grip
  • Water and a light snack (carry no loose food: monkeys are common)
  • Binoculars for birds
  • A warm layer; in winter fog, a torch
  • ID if you plan to visit Monkey Point

Polythene is banned in Himachal: take all your waste back with you.

Winter in Kasauli

Dec–Feb mornings are cold and often foggy, and Kasauli sometimes gets light snow. Walk late morning or midday, carry a jacket and a torch, and skip the cliff edge in thick fog. See Kasauli snowfall and our winter treks guide.

Safety

What we check before your Gilbert Trail walk

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We plan your Kasauli day around these checks:

  • Cliff edgeThe path runs along a cliffside. Stay on the trail, keep children close and take no selfies at the edge.
  • Rain and fogWet days and winter fog make it slippery. Wear proper shoes, carry a torch in fog and skip it in rain.
  • Fire seasonA forest fire burned the Gilbert Trail slopes on 26–27 May 2026. In Apr–Jun never smoke or light a fire, and turn back if you see smoke.
  • MonkeysMonkeys are common in Kasauli. Carry no loose food and keep bags closed.
  • Air Force areaFollow the signboards. At Monkey Point, ID is checked and phones, cameras and bags are not allowed.
  • Leave no tracePolythene is banned; don't litter, and don't feed wild animals.

FAQ

Gilbert Trail, Kasauli — questions

How long is the Gilbert Trail?

About 1.5 km. Most people take one to three hours with stops for views and birds.

Where does the Gilbert Trail start?

Most guides start it near the Kasauli Club, about 2 km from the bus stand, and walk it towards the Air Force Station area and Sunset Point. Sources differ slightly on the exact start and end.

Is there an entry fee for the Gilbert Trail?

No entry fee is reported for the trail.

Is the Gilbert Trail difficult?

No, it is an easy walk. Parts run along a cliff edge and get slippery in rain or fog, so wear shoes with grip and keep children close.

When is the best time to walk it?

Early morning in Mar–Jun or Sep–Nov. Avoid monsoon rain and foggy winter mornings. More on winter in our Kasauli snowfall guide.

Can I visit Monkey Point the same day?

Yes. It is inside the Air Force Station and open roughly 9 am to 4–5 pm. Carry ID and leave phones, cameras and bags behind.

How far is Kasauli from Chandigarh?

About 60 km by road via Parwanoo and Dharampur. See our Chandigarh to Kasauli taxi page.
