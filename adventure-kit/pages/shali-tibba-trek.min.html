Quick answer

Shali Tibba trek, at a glance

Shali Tibba is a ~2,870 m peak beyond Mashobra, the highest viewpoint near Shimla. The road ends at Khatnol village, roughly 35–48 km from Shimla via Dhalli, Mashobra and Baldeyan. From there a 7 km forest trail, built in 1936, zigzags up to the Bhima Kali (Shali Mata) temple. Allow 3–5 hours up. Carry all your water, and expect snow in winter.

Is Shali Tibba for you?

  • Want the best full-day trek near Shimla → this is the highest viewpoint around, with a real summit.

  2. Drive to Khatnol

  4. Bhima Kali temple

  5. Walk down

    About 2 hours back to Khatnol, well before dusk.

  6. Onward

    The cab drops you in Shimla or Mashobra, or goes on towards Kufri, Narkanda or Chandigarh.

What to carry

  • 2–3 litres of water per person: no water on the trail or at the top
  • A packed lunch and snacks
  • Shoes with good grip and a warm layer
  • In winter: microspikes, gloves and a proper jacket
  • A rain shell in the monsoon

Polythene is banned in Himachal: take all your waste back down.

Winter on Shali Tibba

Dec–Feb brings real snow to the trail and the top, often deep after a storm, so the path is hard to follow. Go only with a local guide and microspikes, skip it during and right after a storm, and check the road beyond Mashobra after snowfall.

Safety

What we check before your Shali Tibba trek

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange local guides with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • Carry your waterThere is no water on the trail or at the top. Carry 2–3 litres per person and a packed lunch.
  • Never alone, down before duskDense forest with no houses, and wildlife lives here. Trek in a group and be back at Khatnol before dark.
  • Snow needs a guideIn winter the trail is snow-covered and hard to follow. Local guide and microspikes only; skip it in or right after a storm.
  • LightningThe open hilltop attracts lightning. Come down if clouds build up in the afternoon.
  • Monsoon and fire seasonJul–Sep the path is slippery; Apr–Jun is fire season. Check local news and never light a fire.
  • Leave no tracePolythene is banned; don't litter, and don't feed or approach wild animals.

FAQ

Shali Tibba trek — questions

Where is Shali Tibba?

It is a peak beyond Mashobra in Shimla district. The road ends at Khatnol village, and the trek starts there.

How far is Shali Tibba from Shimla?

Khatnol is roughly 35–48 km from Shimla by road via Dhalli, Mashobra and Baldeyan, about 2–2.5 hours. Sources differ on the exact distance.

How long is the Shali Tibba trek?

About 7 km one way from Khatnol. Most hikers take 3–5 hours up and about 2 hours down; that is a planning figure, not a promise.

How high is Shali Tibba?

About 2,870 m. The HP trekking portal lists 2,865 m.

Which temple is at the top?

The Bhima Kali (Shali Mata) temple. Devotees visit especially during Navratri.

Is there water or food on the way?

No. Carry 2–3 litres of water per person and a packed lunch.

Does it snow at Shali Tibba?

Yes, the peak gets heavy snow in winter. Trek then only with a local guide and traction, and check the road after snowfall. More snow ideas in our winter treks guide.

Can I stay overnight at the top?

There is no hotel. A basic pilgrim serai near the temple is for emergencies, by permission of the temple caretaker. A same-day trek is the usual plan.
