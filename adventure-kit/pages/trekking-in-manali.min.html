Quick answer

Trekking in Manali at a glance

Manali is a base for eight good treks. Jogini Waterfall from Vashisht is a half-day walk; Beas Kund, Bhrigu Lake and Patalsu take 2–4 days; Chandrakhani Pass and Hampta Pass (about 4,270 m, 4–5 days) are the classic crossings. Summer treks run roughly May to October. In winter, Jogini and the Sethan snow walks stay open.

Safety & area rules

Rules we follow on every Manali trek

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). Before your trek we check:

  • Kullu trek registrationKullu district registers treks on the HP Trekking Management System (trekking.hp.gov.in); we register your group
  • Hampta check postThe forest and tourism check post at Jobdi Nullah records trekkers so there is a record in an emergency
  • Lahaul-Spiti sideHampta comes down into Lahaul-Spiti: prior registration with the DDMA-authorised officer and check-out on return; winter trekking is banned there
  • Altitude limitThe state advisory on tracks above 15,000 ft does not cover these eight treks; Friendship Peak is higher — see guided climbs
  • Sethan roadVehicles need the online Hamta road permit; after fresh snow only 4x4s with chains get through
  • Team and weatherRegistered local trek team, no solo pass crossings, weather checked at Solang or Gulaba before going up

FAQ

Trekking in Manali — questions

Which is the best one-day trek in Manali?

Jogini Waterfall: a 3–4 km walk from Vashisht to a fall of about 150 ft, easy and open most of the year. See the Jogini Waterfall trek guide.

Which Manali treks are open in winter?

Jogini Waterfall and the Sethan snow walks on the Hamta road. Beas Kund, Bhrigu Lake, Patalsu, Chandrakhani and Hampta are summer treks.

Which Manali trek is best for beginners?

Beas Kund: about 15–16 km over 2–3 days from Dhundi near Solang, May to October. Patalsu Peak is a beginner-friendly summit too.

Do I need a permit for Hampta Pass?

Trekkers register at the Jobdi Nullah check post, and the Lahaul-Spiti side needs prior registration with the DDMA-authorised officer and check-out on return. We do this for you.

Do I need to register to trek in Kullu?

Kullu district runs trek registration on the HP Trekking Management System portal (trekking.hp.gov.in). We register your group with the trek.

Bhrigu Lake or Beas Kund?

Beas Kund is shorter (2–3 days to about 3,890 m); Bhrigu Lake is higher (about 4,235 m) and takes 3–4 days from Gulaba.

How much does a Manali trek cost?

It depends on the trek, days, group, month and add-ons. Send your dates on WhatsApp and get today's rate within minutes.

Can you add hotel and cab?
