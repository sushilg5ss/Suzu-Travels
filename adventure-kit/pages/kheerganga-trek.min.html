Quick answer

Kheerganga trek, at a glance

Kheerganga is a 12–13 km trek from Barshaini, the road-end above Kasol, to a meadow and hot spring at about 2,960 m. Since July 2024 the Forest Department allows no camping at the top: walkers must reach by 10 am and leave by 2 pm. Most people take 4–6 hours up and 3–4 down, and sleep in Kasol, Tosh, Kalga or Nakthan.

Your day

How your Kheerganga day works

  1. Night before

    Stay in Kasol, Tosh, Kalga or Barshaini — we book it together with your cab.

  2. Early start, 5–6 am

    Start from the Barshaini road-head and register at the forest checkpoint with a photo ID.

  3. Climb 4–6 hours

    Up through Nakthan and Rudranag (or via Kalga), through forest and past waterfalls.

  4. At the top by 10 am

    The hot spring, the Shiva shrine and the meadow views at about 2,960 m.

  5. Down well before 2 pm

    About 3–4 hours back to Barshaini — the 2 pm turn-around is the rule.

  6. Onward

    Cab back to Kasol or Manali, or on to the next stop of your package.

Where you sleep

No one may stay at the top any more, so the night before and after is in the valley: riverside camps in Kasol, or guesthouses in Tosh, Kalga, Barshaini or Nakthan, closer to the trail.

Carry water, snacks, a warm layer, a rain shell and shoes with grip. Pack your litter back down.

FAQ

Kheerganga trek — questions

Is Kheerganga open in 2026?

Yes, as a day trek. Since July 2024 the Forest Department has banned camping and night stays at the top; you must reach by 10 am and leave by 2 pm.

Can I camp at Kheerganga?

No. Overnight stays are allowed lower down in Nakthan, Barshaini, Kalga or Tosh. Kasol has riverside camps.

How long is the Kheerganga trek?

About 12–13 km one way from Barshaini, roughly 24–26 km return; most walkers take 4–6 hours up and 3–4 hours down.

How do I reach the start?

Drive or take a bus to Kasol (about 30 km from Bhuntar airport), then about 15–20 km more to the Barshaini road-head.

What is the best time?

April to June and mid-September to October. Trekking is permitted from April to October; skip the monsoon months.

Is there a hot spring?

Yes, the Parvati Kund hot spring at the top (about 2,960 m), next to a Shiva shrine. It is a sacred site — follow the posted rules.

Is it good for beginners?

It is long but not technical. Start early, carry water and rain gear, and keep the 2 pm turn-around.
