Quick answer

Camping in Himachal, at a glance

Camping in Himachal means a night in a fixed tent camp near a river, forest or lake, or a tent on a trek. Kasol, Jibhi–Tirthan, Bir, Manali–Solang and the hills near Shimla and Kasauli run camps most of the year; Chandratal only from June to early October. Book only registered camps, and avoid riverbanks in monsoon, when Kullu has banned riverside camping before.

What a camp night looks like

Dome or Swiss tents with bedding, dinner and breakfast, and a bonfire where the camp allows it. Riverside camps are in Kasol; forest camps in Jibhi–Tirthan — see our Jibhi and Tirthan itinerary.

Carry a warm layer even in May, a torch and shoes with grip. Take all your waste back with you.

Rules & safety

What we check before we book your camp

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange camps with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • Registered camps onlyA November 2024 joint inspection shut 45 tourism units running without proper papers in Kasol, camps included. We only book registered camps.
  • Monsoon riverside ruleOn 6 July 2022 Kullu banned camps near rivers under the Disaster Management Act. Never camp on a riverbed or island — hydro projects release water without warning.
  • Forest rulesKullu's 2022 forest order bans fixed camps on routes like Hampta and wants tents at least 25 m from rivers and water bodies — we use operator camps, not wild pitches.
  • ChandratalNo camping at the lake shore — it is a Ramsar wetland, and the lake is frozen in winter. We check the current permit for your date.
  • Kangra treks in winterA November 2024 order needed SP Kangra permission for Triund and Kareri and stopped trekking above 3,000 m — expect a fresh order each winter.
  • Kheerganga & bonfiresNo camping at Kheerganga top — do it as a day trek. Bonfire only where the camp allows it; carry out all waste.

FAQ

Camping in Himachal — questions

Where can I go camping in Himachal?

The main camp areas are Kasol in the Parvati valley, Jibhi–Tirthan, Bir, Manali–Solang, the hills near Shimla and Kasauli, and Chandratal in Lahaul in summer.

Is camping legal in Himachal Pradesh?

Yes, at registered camps. Kullu officials shut 45 tourism units running without proper papers in Kasol in November 2024, so check the camp is registered — we book only those.

Can I camp by the river in monsoon?

Avoid it. In July 2022 Kullu banned camps near rivers under the Disaster Management Act, and water sports stop 15 Jul–15 Sep every year. We check the current orders for your dates.

Can I camp at Chandratal?

Only from late May or June to early October, when the road is open, and only at camps away from the lake — camping at the lake shore is not allowed. We check the current permit for your date.

Can I camp at Kheerganga?

No — camping at the top is banned, so Kheerganga is a day trek; stay in Kasol.

Which Himachal camp is best in winter?

Lower camps such as Kasol, Bir and the Shimla side stay open with cold nights; for snow, add a day at Solang and sleep in a heated hotel or an igloo stay.

Which camp is closest to Delhi and Chandigarh?

The forest and hilltop camps around Shimla and Kasauli — an easy weekend by road.
