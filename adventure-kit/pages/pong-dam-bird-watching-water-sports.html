Quick answer

Pong Dam Lake, at a glance

Pong Dam Lake (Maharana Pratap Sagar) is the large Beas reservoir in Kangra district, protected as a wildlife sanctuary and Himachal's first Ramsar wetland. Between November and February tens of thousands of migratory birds, led by bar-headed geese, winter here; the count by December 2024 alone passed 92,000. A state water-sports centre runs boating and paddle sports, and the Bathu ki Ladi temples rise from the water in spring and early summer.

Rules & safety

What we check before we book your lake day

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange water activities with registered local operators and check these first:

  • Registered operators, life jackets onWater activities only at the state centre or with registered operators, life jackets on at all times (HP water-sports rules).
  • Monsoon banKangra stops water sports from 15 Jul to 15 Sep — nothing on the water in that window.
  • It is a sanctuaryKeep your distance from the flocks: no chasing birds by boat, no feeding, no loud music. Follow the forest staff.
  • Winter fogDec–Jan mornings can be foggy: slower drives, and boating may start late.
  • Bathu ki Ladi groundThe approach can be muddy and slippery, and the water returns with the monsoon — do not walk out on wet ground.
  • Sun and waterShade is limited on the shore — carry sun protection and drinking water.

FAQ

Pong Dam Lake — questions

When is the best time to see birds at Pong Dam?

November to February, with the peak in December and January. Most migratory birds leave by March.

How many birds come to Pong Dam?

The forest department counted 92,885 migratory birds by December 2024, and earlier seasons have topped one lakh. More than 420 bird species have been recorded at the lake.

Is boating allowed at Pong Dam?

Yes. The state water-sports centre at Khatiyar offers boating, kayaking, canoeing, sailing and rowing. We confirm what is running on your date before you go.

When can you see the Bathu ki Ladi temples?

When the lake level drops, usually from about March to June. For the rest of the year the temples are under water.

Is Pong Dam a Ramsar site?

Yes. Pong Dam Lake was declared a Ramsar wetland in 2002, the first in Himachal Pradesh, and it is also a wildlife sanctuary.

How far is Pong Dam from Dharamshala?

About 55–65 km by road, roughly 1.5–2 hours. Kangra (Gaggal) airport is about 76 km away.

Are water sports open in the monsoon?

No. Water sports in Kangra stop for the monsoon from 15 July to 15 September.

Is Pong Dam the same as Maharana Pratap Sagar?

Yes. Maharana Pratap Sagar is the official name of the reservoir behind the Pong dam on the Beas, built in 1975.
