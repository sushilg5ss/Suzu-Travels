Quick answer

Zipline in Manali, at a glance

You can ride a zipline in Manali at Solang Valley (about 13 km from town) and at a few Beas-valley adventure spots. In Himachal every zipline site must be approved and inspected each year by a state Technical Committee, and the operator must be registered with the Tourism Department. Ask for that registration before you ride — we book only registered local operators.

Rules & safety

What we check before we book your zipline

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange ziplines with registered local operators and check these first:

  • Registered operatorThe operator must be registered with the HP Tourism Department under the Miscellaneous Adventure Activities Rules 2017/2021 — ask to see the certificate.
  • Site inspected every yearZipline and flying-fox sites need approval and a yearly inspection by the state Technical Committee, chaired by the Director of Tourism.
  • Safety gear and rescueHelmet and harness to the specified standard (a life jacket on river crossings), and a rescue possible within 30 minutes.
  • InsuranceThe rules require the operator to insure participants.
  • Why it mattersIn June 2025 a 12-year-old girl fell about 30 ft from a zipline near Nehru Kund; the minister said that operator had neither Tourism registration nor technical approval (The Tribune).
  • Kids, weight, healthLimits are set by the operator on the spot — tell us ages and any health issue when you ask, and we confirm before you go.

FAQ

Zipline in Manali — questions

Where can I do a zipline in Manali?

At Solang Valley and a few adventure spots along the Beas near Manali. We book only sites run by operators registered with HP Tourism.

Is ziplining in Manali safe?

It is when the site is approved and the operator is registered: HP rules require yearly site inspection by a Technical Committee, proper safety gear and rescue within 30 minutes. A 2025 accident happened at a line with no approvals — so ask first.

How do I know a zipline is registered?

Ask the operator for the HP Tourism Department registration certificate. When you book through us, we check it before your date.

What is the price of a zipline in Manali?

It depends on the site, number of rides, group and date. Send your dates on WhatsApp and we share today's rate within minutes — Get Quote.

Can kids do the zipline?

Many sites take children, but age and weight limits are set by the operator on the spot. Tell us the ages when you ask for a quote.

Does the zipline run in winter or rain?

Lines stop in rain, storm or strong wind. In winter they run on calm, dry days, so keep the zipline flexible in your plan.

Is there a zipline in Dharamshala or Shimla?

Kufri near Shimla has one in its adventure park. A long zipline over Naddi in Dharamshala is planned by the state but not open yet.

Can I combine it with paragliding?

Yes — Solang mornings often fit a zipline, a tandem flight and the ropeway. We plan it with your hotel and cab.
