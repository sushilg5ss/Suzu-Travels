Quick answer

Bahadurpur Fort, at a glance

Bahadurpur Fort sits on Bahadurpur hill near Tepra village, at about 1,980 m the highest point in Bilaspur district. Raja Keshab Sen built it in 1620; today it is a ruin in the district's only reserve forest of deodar, ban oak and chil. A metalled road runs 11 km up from Namhol to the Forest Rest House. The hill gets light snow in winter.

  4. Forest walk to the top

  5. Take in the views

What to carry

  • Water and food: no facilities are reported at the top beyond the Forest Rest House
  • Shoes with good grip for the forest path
  • A warm layer; more in winter
  • A rain shell in the monsoon, and leech socks on wet days

Polythene is banned in Himachal: take all your waste back down.

Hoping for snow?

Bahadurpur gets light snow on some winter days, the closest snow to Bilaspur town. It is never certain, and the hill road can be icy just after a snowfall, so we check before we send the cab.

Safety

What we check before your Bahadurpur trip

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange guides with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • Stay on the pathIt is a forest, not a park: no trail map is published. Go with the driver or a local guide, and don't leave the path in fog.
  • Road after snowThe hill road can be icy after a snowfall. We send a hill-trained driver and advise against self-drive on snow.
  • Reserve forest rulesNo fires and no litter. Camping only with Forest Department permission; we don't arrange camping here.
  • Carry your ownWater and food: no facilities are reported at the top beyond the Forest Rest House.
  • Loose stone wallsThe ruins are unguarded. Keep children off the walls.
  • MonsoonJul–Sep brings mist and leeches. We go on clear days and check the hill road for slips.

FAQ

Bahadurpur Fort — questions

Where is Bahadurpur Fort?

On Bahadurpur hill near Tepra village in Bilaspur district, about 40 km from Bilaspur town. A metalled road climbs 11 km from Namhol to the Forest Rest House below the top.

How high is Bahadurpur Fort?

About 1,980 m, the highest point in Bilaspur district. The Forest Rest House just below is listed at 1,872 m.

Who built Bahadurpur Fort?

Raja Keshab Sen of Bilaspur (Kahlur) built it in 1620 AD. It is in ruins today.

Does it snow at Bahadurpur?

Yes, the hill gets light snow some winter days, which is rare this close to Bilaspur town. We check the road after a snowfall before we send the cab. For sure snow, see our winter treks guide.

Is there a place to stay at Bahadurpur?

The HP Forest Department's Bahadurpur Forest Rest House (built 2007-08) can be booked through HP Eco-tourism. We can also plan a hotel in Bilaspur or Ghumarwin.

How long is the walk to the fort?

It is a forest walk from the road to the hilltop; your driver or a local guide shows the path. We don't publish a distance or time because no reliable source gives one.

Is there an entry fee?

No entry fee is reported for the fort.

What can I combine it with?

Bandla paragliding, Gobind Sagar water sports, Kol Dam and the Naina Devi ropeway, all in Bilaspur district.
