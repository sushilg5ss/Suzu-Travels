Quick answer

Dharamshala Skyway, at a glance

The Dharamshala Skyway is a 1.8 km gondola from Kotwali Bazaar in lower Dharamshala to Temple Road in McLeodganj, near the Dalai Lama Temple. The ride takes about five minutes and replaces a 9 km hill drive. Opened in January 2022, it is Himachal's longest ropeway. It was suspended after a landslide in August 2025 and has run again since, but it stops in bad weather.

From Kotwali Bazaar to McLeodganj

The lower station sits near Kotwali Bazaar on the bypass side of lower Dharamshala. The gondola climbs over the pine slopes with the Dhauladhar ahead, and the upper station opens onto Temple Road, McLeodganj. Going in winter?

December–February brings snow to the Dhauladhar and, some years, to McLeodganj itself. Plan the ride for a clear morning — storms can halt it — and keep the road as backup. Pair it with a winter Triund trek or a trip on to Bir Billing.

Safety

What we check before your ride

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We plan the Skyway into your Dharamshala day around these checks:

  • District ordersThe Kangra district disaster management authority can suspend the ropeway at once — as on 21 August 2025, when a landslide came within about 10 feet of pillar 6. We re-check status on the morning of your ride.
  • Weather stopsRides stop in storms, high wind or heavy rain — we keep the road drive as the backup for the same slot.
  • Capacity & kidsFollow cabin capacity and staff instructions; children ride with an adult.
  • MonsoonFrom mid-July to September expect pauses; we plan McLeodganj by road on those days.
  • AccessA lift serves the lower station — tell us about wheelchair users or elders and we confirm access at the top before planning.

FAQ

Dharamshala Skyway — questions

How long is the Dharamshala Skyway?

1.8 km, about five minutes, from Kotwali Bazaar in lower Dharamshala to Temple Road in McLeodganj, near the Dalai Lama Temple.

Is the Dharamshala ropeway open now?

It was suspended after a landslide in August 2025 and riders report trips through 2026, but it pauses for weather and maintenance. We confirm the status for your date before you travel.

What are the Dharamshala ropeway timings?

Daytime, roughly 9:30–10 am to 6–7 pm. Hours change with season and weather, so we check on the day.

Where is the lower station?

Near Kotwali Bazaar on the bypass side of lower Dharamshala; a lift serves it. The upper station is on Temple Road, McLeodganj.

Can I buy a return ticket?

Yes — one-way and return tickets are sold at the counter.

Is it the longest ropeway in Himachal?

Yes. The Kangra district website lists it as the longest ropeway in Himachal Pradesh, with 18 gondolas on 10 towers.

When did the Dharamshala Skyway open?

It was inaugurated on 19 January 2022.
