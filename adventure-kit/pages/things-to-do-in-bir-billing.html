Quick answer

Things to do in Bir Billing, at a glance

Bir Billing in Kangra is best known for tandem paragliding from Billing (about 2,400 m) down to Bir (about 1,400 m), but one trip can also hold Himachal's only verified bungee jump, the Tibetan colony monasteries, waterfall walks, mountain biking and the Billing–Rajgundha trek with a camp night. Fly mid-September to November or March to June; flying stops 15 July–15 September.

Where
Bir, Baijnath tehsil, Kangra — about 35 km from Palampur, about 70 km from Dharamshala; Billing is 14 km above Bir by a narrow hill road
Main draw
Tandem paragliding Billing → Bir with registered pilots; 12 years+ and 30 kg+ (HP Aero Sports Rules)
Also here
Bungee near Slabak, monasteries (Chokling, Sherab Ling), Gunehar waterfall walk, cycling, Billing–Rajgundha trek
Best months
Mid-Sep–Nov and Mar–Jun; Dec–Feb open but cold, Billing can get snow
Closed
Paragliding 15 Jul–15 Sep every monsoon (district order)

Safety

The rules we check before we book

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange each activity with registered local operators and check these first:

  • Registered pilots onlyPilots registered with HP Tourism at the notified Billing site, insured; no passengers under 12 years or 30 kg, no stunts with passengers (HP Aero Sports Rules 2022, amended 2025).
  • District committee rulesKangra's Aero Sports Regulatory Committee sets timings, a daily flight limit per pilot and no flying in bad weather; long cross-country flights only in groups with GPS trackers.
  • Weather call is finalBad weather can ground flights even in peak weeks (it did on 21 Oct 2026) — always plan a buffer morning.
  • MonsoonNo flying 15 Jul–15 Sep; avoid the Billing road and treks in heavy rain.
  • SnowThe Billing road shuts to vehicles after snowfall — in winter we confirm the road on the morning.
  • Bungee and treksWe confirm bungee age and weight limits for your date — tell us about back, heart or pregnancy issues. Rajgundha walks go with a local guide; carry warm layers, Billing is about 1,000 m higher than Bir.

FAQ

Bir Billing — questions

What can you do in Bir Billing besides paragliding?

Bungee near Slabak, the Tibetan colony monasteries (Chokling, and Sherab Ling at Bhattu), Gunehar waterfall and village walks, cycling through tea gardens and the Billing–Rajgundha trek.

How many days are enough for Bir Billing?

Two days covers a flight, the monasteries and one more activity. Add a third for the Rajgundha trek, or as a buffer if weather grounds the flight.

What is the best time to visit Bir Billing?

Mid-September to November and March to June. Paragliding is closed every year from 15 July to 15 September.

Is Bir Billing worth visiting in winter?

Yes for monasteries, walks and flights on clear days, but mornings are cold and snow can close the Billing road — keep a spare day.

Can we do bungee and paragliding on the same day?

Yes, both are at Bir. Fly in the morning and jump later, subject to weather and slots.

What is the minimum age for paragliding at Bir?

HP rules allow no passengers under 12 years or under 30 kg. See paragliding in Bir Billing for the full rules.

How far is Bir from Dharamshala and Palampur?

About 70 km from Dharamshala and about 35 km from Palampur. The nearest airport is Kangra (Gaggal), and the toy-train halt at Ahju is about 3 km away.
