Quick answer

Paragliding near Dalhousie, at a glance

Yes — you can paraglide near Dalhousie, but the flying happens at Khajjiar, a meadow at about 1,920 m roughly 24 km away. Licensed tandem pilots fly between marked launch sites at Dadota and Lahara. Flights stop every monsoon (in 2026 until 15 September) and restarted on 16 September 2026. Under Himachal's aero sports rules, passengers must be at least 12 years old and 30 kg.

First flight? That's normal

No licence or experience is needed for a tandem flight. The pilot clips you in, explains the run and does all the flying; you just run a few steps and sit back.

Khajjiar is cool even in summer: wear closed shoes and a warm layer, tie long hair back and keep phones in a zipped pocket. Tell us the ages and weights in your group when you book.

Safety first

What we check before we book your flight

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange flights only with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • Licensed pilot onlyRegistered operators and licensed pilots only, from notified sites (HP Aero Sports Rules 2022, amended 2025).
  • Age and weightPassengers under 12 years or under 30 kg are not allowed.
  • Licence and gearABVIMAS, Manali renews tandem pilot licences after inspecting equipment at Khajjiar — ask your pilot to show the licence.
  • Monsoon banThe district stops flying every rainy season; anyone flying during the ban faces action.
  • Safety standardsOn the 2026 restart the Chamba ADM asked pilots and tourists to follow the prescribed safety standards.
  • Pilot's weather callWind or rain means the pilot can cancel or shorten a flight on the spot; we keep a buffer in your plan.

FAQ

Paragliding in Khajjiar and Dalhousie — questions

Is there paragliding in Dalhousie?

The flying near Dalhousie happens at Khajjiar, about 24 km away. We did not find a separate notified site in Dalhousie town (checked Oct 2026).

Where do you take off in Khajjiar?

The district has marked launch sites for flights from Dadota to Lahara and from Lahara to Dadota; your pilot picks one by the wind.

Is Khajjiar paragliding open now?

Yes. It restarted on 16 September 2026 after the monsoon ban; we re-check the week of your trip.

When is paragliding closed in Khajjiar?

Every monsoon. In 2026 the Chamba tourism office stopped flying until 15 September.

Who can fly?

Under Himachal's aero sports rules passengers must be at least 12 years old and 30 kg, and fly tandem with a licensed pilot.

How high is Khajjiar?

The meadow sits at about 1,920 m (6,300 ft).

Are the pilots licensed?

Tandem pilots at Khajjiar hold licences that ABVIMAS, Manali renews after inspecting their equipment. Ask your pilot to show the licence.
