Quick answer

Buran Ghati trek, at a glance

Buran Ghati is a 7–8 day crossover trek from Janglik village above Rohru (Shimla district) over a pass of about 4,575 m to Barua in Kinnaur's Sangla valley. You camp at Dayara meadow, Litham and Dhunda, visit Chandranahan lake, then descend a steep snow slope on a fixed rope. It runs May–June and September–October.

Start
Janglik (about 2,800 m), about 150–160 km from Shimla via Rohru and Chirgaon — a full day's drive, rough after Chirgaon
Highest point
Buran Ghati, about 4,575 m (15,000 ft)
Distance & days
About 37–44 km over 5–6 walking days; 7–8 days from Shimla
Camps
Dayara (~3,400 m) · Litham (~3,550 m) · Dhunda (~4,000 m) · river camp at Munirang (~3,350 m) · end at Barua (~2,400 m)
Season
May–June (snow wall) and September–October; not in the monsoon or winter

Day by day

Your Buran Ghati trek, step by step

  1. Send your dates

    WhatsApp your dates, group size and past treks — we check the snow and the forest-permit paperwork.

  2. Day 1: drive to Janglik

    Cab Shimla → Rohru → Chirgaon → Janglik, about 150–160 km and a full day; the last stretch is rough and slow.

  3. Days 2–3: Dayara and Litham

    Up through forest to the wide Dayara meadow, then on to the Litham camp.

  4. Day 4: Chandranahan lake

    Acclimatisation walk to the glacial lake that feeds the Pabbar river, and back to camp.

  5. Days 5–6: Dhunda and the pass

    Climb to Dhunda, then the long pass day (9–10 hours) over about 4,575 m and down the roped snow slope to the river camp.

  6. Day 7: Barua and the cab

    Down to Barua in the Sangla valley; our cab takes you to Sangla or back to Shimla via Karcham and Rampur.

Shimla to Kinnaur, on foot

  • By cab → Shimla → Rohru → Chirgaon → Janglik.
  • On foot → Dayara → Litham (+ Chandranahan lake) → Dhunda → Buran Ghati → Munirang → Barua.
  • By cab → Barua → Sangla, or back to Shimla via Karcham and Rampur.
  • Next season May–June 2027.

Safety

What we check before we book your trek

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange the trek with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • ExperienceNot a first trek. We ask about your past multi-day treks and fitness — operators usually ask for about a 5 km run in 25–30 minutes.
  • Forest permitArranged through the operator with each trekker's government ID and a medical certificate.
  • Guide, rope and harnessA trained guide fixes the rope for the snow descent; the team turns back if the snow is unsafe.
  • AltitudeThe acclimatisation day at Litham and Chandranahan lake is never skipped.
  • Network and helpMobile signal only up to around Janglik and Dayara; the nearest hospital and ATM are at Rohru.
  • WeatherNo crossing in fresh snow or rain — which is why we suggest a buffer day.

FAQ

Buran Ghati trek — questions

How high is Buran Ghati?

About 4,575 m (15,000 ft). Sources give 4,550–4,590 m for the pass.

How long is the Buran Ghati trek?

About 37–44 km over 5–6 walking days, or 7–8 days in all including the drives at both ends.

Where does the trek start and end?

It starts at Janglik village above Rohru, about 150–160 km from Shimla, and ends at Barua village in the Sangla valley, Kinnaur.

What is the best time for Buran Ghati?

May–June, when the snow wall forms below the pass, and September–October after the monsoon. It is not run in the monsoon or in winter.

Is the Buran Ghati trek difficult?

Moderate to difficult. The pass day is 9–10 hours with a steep roped snow descent, so it is not a first trek — prior multi-day trekking helps.

What is the snow wall?

A steep snow slope just below the pass on the Kinnaur side. Guides fix a rope and you slide or rappel down it in a harness.

What is Chandranahan lake?

A high glacial lake at about 4,000 m above Litham, the source of the Pabbar river, visited on the acclimatisation day.

Do I need a permit, and what does it cost?

Yes, a forest permit, arranged through the operator with each trekker's ID and a medical certificate. The total depends on days, group, season and stays — send your dates and we share today's rate in minutes. Get Quote.
