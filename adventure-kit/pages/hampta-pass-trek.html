Quick answer

Hampta Pass trek, at a glance

Hampta Pass (about 4,270 m) is a 4–5 day trek of roughly 26 km that crosses the Pir Panjal from the green Kullu side near Manali to the dry Lahaul side at Chhatru. It is open only from mid-June to September; snow closes it from October to May. Treks start at Jobra, about an hour's drive from Manali, and many groups add a Chandratal day.

Day by day

How your Hampta trek works

  1. Arrive in Manali a day early

    Sleep one night at about 2,000 m to acclimatise. Kit check and briefing with the trek team; carry ID copies and any medical form the operator asks for.

  2. Day 1 — Jobra to Chika

    About an hour's drive to Jobra on the Prini–Sethan road, then a short walk to the riverside camp at Chika (about 3,050 m).

  3. Day 2 — Chika to Balu ka Ghera

    Walk up the valley to Balu ka Ghera (about 3,600 m), the last camp below the pass.

  4. Day 3 — the pass day

    Early start, cross Hampta Pass (about 4,270 m), then a steep descent to Shea Goru (about 3,900 m). Plan 7–9 hours on your feet.

  5. Day 4 — down to Chhatru

    Descend to Chhatru (about 3,350 m) on the Gramphu–Kaza road; your cab drives you back to Manali through the Atal Tunnel.

  6. Day 5 — Chandratal (optional)

    If the road is open, drive on to Chandratal lake before heading back. Never guaranteed — it depends on the road and the weather.

Life at camp

Nights are spent in tents at meadow camps beside the stream. The trek team sets up camp and cooks; you carry a day pack, or add a mule/porter for your backpack.

Pack a warm layer and rain shell even in summer, good trekking shoes with grip, and spare socks for the river crossings. There is no mobile signal after Sethan.

FAQ

Hampta Pass trek — questions

How high is Hampta Pass?

About 4,270 m (around 14,000 ft). Published figures range from 4,200 to 4,300 m.

How long is the Hampta Pass trek?

Roughly 26 km on foot from Jobra to Chhatru over 4 trekking days, or 5 days with the Chandratal drive.

What is the best time for the Hampta Pass trek?

Mid-June to September. June still has snow on the pass, July–August is the monsoon with high streams, and September has the clearest views.

Can I do Hampta Pass in October or winter?

No. Snow closes the pass from about October to May. In winter we plan Sethan snow and igloo nights in the same valley instead.

Is Hampta Pass difficult for beginners?

It is rated moderate. Fit beginners manage it with a guide, but the pass day is long (7–9 hours) with a steep descent and cold river crossings.

Do I need a permit for Hampta Pass?

Trekkers register at the forest/tourism check post on the Jobra side, and your operator arranges the forest permits. No Rohtang permit is needed to return through the Atal Tunnel — see Rohtang permit vs Atal Tunnel.

Is there mobile network on the trek?

No. Signal fades after Sethan and the camps have none until you are back on the road at Chhatru.

Can children do the Hampta trek?

There is no government age rule; operators usually take fit children of about 9–10 years and up with a parent. Tell us the ages and we suggest the 4-day plan or the Jobra–Chika walk.
