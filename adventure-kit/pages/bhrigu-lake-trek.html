Quick answer

Bhrigu Lake trek, at a glance

The Bhrigu Lake trek is a 3–4 day walk from Gulaba, about 22–26 km above Manali on the Rohtang road, to a small glacial lake at about 4,235–4,300 m. You camp at Rola Kholi, climb to the lake on day two and return to Gulaba or down to Vashisht. The season runs June to mid-October; in winter the trail is under deep snow.

Day by day

How your Bhrigu Lake trek works

  1. Day 0 — arrive in Manali

    Rest at your hotel in Manali (about 2,050 m), meet the trek team for a kit check and briefing.

  2. Day 1 — Gulaba to Rola Kholi

    Cab up the Rohtang road to Gulaba (about 3,000 m), then about 7–8 km (4–5 hours) through the Jonker Thach meadows to the Rola Kholi camp (about 3,820 m).

  3. Day 2 — up to the lake

    A steep climb of about 2 hours to the first ridge, then on to Bhrigu Lake (about 4,235–4,300 m) and back to camp, or across to Pandu Ropa — about 12–14 km, 7–8 hours.

  4. Day 3 — back to Manali

    Walk down to Gulaba, or about 8 km down to Vashisht on the loop; your cab drives you to your hotel.

  5. Start early, finish before dark

    Leave camp early and be back before dark. Never walk down alone or after sunset on this route.

  6. Hotel and cab included

    We add your Manali hotel nights and the cab to Gulaba and back in the same quote.

Nights at the meadow camp

You sleep in tents at Rola Kholi, a high meadow at about 3,820 m. The trek team sets up camp and cooks; you carry a day pack, or add a porter or mule for your backpack.

There is no mobile network beyond Gulaba. Carry 2 litres of water and refill at the streams before Rola Kholi, and pack a warm layer and a rain shell — nights are cold even in summer.

FAQ

Bhrigu Lake trek — questions

How long is the Bhrigu Lake trek?

About 24 km out and back from Gulaba over 3–4 days. The loop down to Vashisht is about 27–30 km.

What is the height of Bhrigu Lake?

About 4,235–4,300 m, roughly 14,000 ft. Wikipedia gives 4,235 m; most trek guides say about 4,300 m.

Where does the Bhrigu Lake trek start?

At Gulaba, about 22–26 km from Manali on the Rohtang road. Some treks end at Vashisht, near Manali.

What is the best time for the Bhrigu Lake trek?

September to mid-October for clear skies; June for snow. From November the trail is under snow and regular treks stop.

Do I need a Rohtang permit for the trek?

Not for the trek itself — the Rohtang permit is for vehicles going beyond the Gulaba barrier. A cab drop at Gulaba is enough.

Is the Bhrigu Lake trek good for beginners?

Yes, for fit beginners. It is non-technical but climbs about 1,300 m in two days, so rest a day in Manali first.

Does Bhrigu Lake freeze?

It can stay partly frozen into June, and nights at the lake drop below freezing from late September.

Can I camp at Bhrigu Lake?

Treks camp lower, at Rola Kholi or Pandu Ropa. Camping at the lake itself is not advised.
