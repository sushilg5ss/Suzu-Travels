Quick answer

Thatharana trek, at a glance

Thatharana (Thatharna) Top is a meadow at about 2,500–2,750 m on the Dhauladhar slope above Dharamshala. The trail starts at Khadota (Kharota) village, about 2 km above Khaniyara, and climbs 5–7 km through forest in 3–5 hours. Most people camp one night and come down the next morning. Register at the Khadota check post; the best months are March–June and September–November.

The route, step by step

Going in winter?

Snow can lie on the upper trail from December to February. Thatharana Top is below the 3,000 m line used in past Kangra winter orders, but Kundli Pass beyond it is not, and any IMD alert can stop treks. We check the order in force for your date.

Rules & safety

What we check before we book your trek

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange the trek with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • Registration at KhadotaKhadota village is one of the 10 trek entry check posts notified by Kangra district in November 2025. Trekking these routes without registering is barred under the Disaster Management Act.
  • The 3,000 m winter ruleKangra orders in November 2024 and January 2026 banned trekking above 3,000 m in winter. Thatharana Top is below that line, Kundli Pass is not — we check the order in force for your date.
  • Groups and guidesKangra has asked that all trekkers register and that guides take no more than 10 trekkers per group.
  • TrailParts of the forest trail are unmarked — go with a local lead, start before about 11 am and carry a headlamp.
  • Water and snowWater sources on the way are few: carry 2 litres each. In Dec–Feb use microspikes or turn back if the top section is icy.
  • NetworkMobile signal is patchy above Khadota; share your plan with your hotel.

FAQ

Thatharana trek — questions

Where does the Thatharana trek start?

At Khadota (also spelt Kharota) village, about 2 km above Khaniyara, which is about 8 km from Dharamshala. Some operators count from Khaniyara market, which adds 1–2 hours of walking.

How long is the Thatharana trek?

About 5–7 km one way from Khadota: 3–5 hours up and 2–3 hours down. Operators who start at Khaniyara quote 12–18 km for the round trip.

What is the height of Thatharana Top?

Roughly 2,500–2,750 m (about 8,200–9,000 ft); sources differ and operators usually say about 8,500 ft. It is lower than the Triund ridge but feels steeper.

Is Thatharana harder than Triund?

Slightly, yes — shorter, but steeper all the way and less clearly marked. Fit beginners manage it with a local trek lead.

Do I need a permit for Thatharana?

There is no separate permit, but you must register with original ID at the Khadota check post, one of Kangra's notified trek entry points. Fees, if any, are paid locally.

Can I camp at Thatharana Top?

Yes. One-night camps on the meadow, with tents, dinner and breakfast, are arranged through a registered local operator — not wild camping. See also camping in Dharamshala.

Is the Thatharana trek open in winter?

The Top is below Kangra's 3,000 m winter limit, so it can open on clear days, but snow on the upper trail or an IMD alert can stop it. We confirm the order in force for your date before we quote — see our winter treks guide.

Thatharana or Triund — which should I do?

Triund is easier, busier and has capped tents; Thatharana is quieter, with wide Kangra-valley views. Many guests do both on one Dharamshala trip — see the Triund trek guide.
