Quick answer

Paragliding in Bilaspur at a glance

Paragliding in Bilaspur happens at Bandla Dhar, the ridge right above Bilaspur town. You fly tandem with a registered pilot and land on the Luhnu ground beside Gobind Sagar lake. The site hosted an international championship in March 2025 and flying restarted with the district on 28–29 Sep 2025. Flights stop in the monsoon; passengers must be 12+ and 30 kg+.

First flight? That's normal

No licence or experience is needed for a tandem flight. The pilot clips you in, explains the run and does all the flying; you just run a few steps and sit back.

Wear closed shoes and a light jacket, tie long hair back and keep phones in a zipped pocket. Tell us the ages and weights in your group when you book.

Safety first

What we check before we book your flight

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange flights only with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • Registered pilot onlyPilot and operator registered with HP Tourism; tandem pilots hold a valid licence and logbook (HP Aero Sports Rules 2022, amended 2025).
  • Age and weightPassengers under 12 years or under 30 kg are not allowed.
  • Notified siteTake off only from the notified Bandla site, never from a random ridge.
  • Gear onHelmet, harness and reserve parachute are fitted before take-off — ask the pilot to show them.
  • Pilot's weather callStrong wind or cloud means no flight that hour; we keep a buffer in your plan.
  • Monsoon closureNo flying during the monsoon suspension; the district reopens the season by order.

FAQ

Paragliding in Bilaspur — questions

Where is paragliding in Bilaspur?

At Bandla Dhar, the hill right above Bilaspur town. You land on the Luhnu ground beside Gobind Sagar lake.

Is Bandla a real paragliding site?

Yes. The Bilaspur district administration names Bandla top as the take-off with Luhnu as the landing, and the site hosted an international paragliding championship with 70+ pilots in March 2025.

When can I fly at Bandla?

Outside the monsoon. The district flagged off the 2025-26 season on 28–29 Sep 2025; we confirm the day's flying before you travel.

Who can fly tandem?

Anyone 12 years or older and at least 30 kg, under the HP Aero Sports Rules. No licence is needed for a tandem flight.

What does paragliding in Bilaspur cost?

The rate depends on flight type, video and your date. Send your plan on WhatsApp and we share today's rate within minutes — Price: Get Quote.

Can I combine it with Naina Devi?

Yes. Naina Devi is in the same district; many guests do darshan in the morning and fly in the afternoon.

What else is there at Gobind Sagar?

The district runs speed boat, water scooter, banana and cruise rides on the lake in season.

Bandla or Bir Billing?

Bir is the big, high Kangra site; Bandla is closer to Chandigarh and the Manali highway and lands by a lake. See our paragliding in Himachal comparison.
