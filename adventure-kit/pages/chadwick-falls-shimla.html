Quick answer

Chadwick Falls, Shimla, at a glance

Chadwick Falls is a forest waterfall below Summer Hill, about 5–7 km from central Shimla, at roughly 1,586 m. Vehicles get close to the entry point, and a short walk of about 10 minutes leads through thick forest to the falls. It is an easy route listed on the HP trekking portal. Go July to October for real water, and walk out well before sunset.

When to go

Chadwick Falls by month

MonthsWaterTrail and verdict
Jul – Sep (monsoon)Full flowWet, slippery path, leeches possible, landslip delays on roads. Go in the morning with good shoes; skip it in heavy rain.
Oct – NovStill flowing, clear skiesDry path and clear views. The best balance overall.
Dec – FebThin, low waterCold and frosty; snow after a Shimla snowfall. Go only for the forest walk.
Mar – JunLow before the rainsDry and warm, busy Shimla summer. A pleasant forest walk; pair it with Potter Hill.

Why July to October?

The falls are fed by the rains. After the monsoon the flow is at its best, and by October the path dries out and the skies clear. In winter, pair Shimla with Kufri, Narkanda snow or the Shimla ice rink; see also New Year in Shimla.

What we check before your Chadwick Falls walk

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We plan your Shimla day around these checks:

  • Daylight onlyWalk out well before sunset. Leopards are reported in the area after dark; no night visits.
  • Wet rocksStay off slippery rocks at the pool and take no selfies at the water's edge.

FAQ

Chadwick Falls, Shimla — questions

How far is Chadwick Falls from Shimla?

Roughly 5–7 km from central Shimla, below Summer Hill. Sources give about 5 km from Shimla railway station and about 7 km from the city centre.

How long is the walk to Chadwick Falls?

Vehicles get close to the entry point, and the walk from there is short: about 10 minutes through thick forest. Walking all the way from Summer Hill takes longer.

When does Chadwick Falls have water?

Best after the monsoon, July to October. It carries little water in the dry summer and in winter.

How high is Chadwick Falls?

It is often quoted as about 86 m high. The 1,586 m figure many sites give is the altitude above sea level, not the height of the fall.

Is Chadwick Falls safe?

It is an easy route on the HP trekking portal, but rocks near the water are slippery and leopards are reported in the area after dark. Go in daylight, ideally in a group, and stay off wet rocks.

Is there an entry fee or fixed timing?

We found no official fee or timing notice. Go in daylight and check locally on the day.

Can I combine it with Potter Hill?

Yes. The official 4.7 km Potter Hill loop starts at the Summer Hill police post and passes above Chadwick Falls. See treks near Shimla.

Is it worth visiting in December?

Only as a forest walk, because the falls run thin in winter. In December most visitors pair Shimla with Kufri or Narkanda snow and the Shimla ice rink.
