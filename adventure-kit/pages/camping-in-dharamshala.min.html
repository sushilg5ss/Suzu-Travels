Quick answer

Camping in Dharamshala, at a glance

Camping around Dharamshala happens in three kinds of places. Drive-in forest and hillside camps sit at Dharamkot, Naddi and the Bhagsu side, a few kilometres above McLeodganj. Trek camps sit on the Triund ridge (about 2,850 m, regulated tents only) and halfway up the Kareri Lake trail. Best months are March–June and September–November; in winter the high camps depend on Kangra district orders.

Which camp suits you?

  • No trekking, families, a first camp → Naddi or Dharamkot drive-in camps.
  • One big night out → the Triund ridge, after a 4–5 hour walk.
  • Quieter, lake views → the Kareri Lake trail with a river camp.
  • Fit and experienced → Indrahar alpine camps, guided, summer only.

Getting there: Delhi to Dharamshala taxi, Chandigarh to Dharamshala taxi or Pathankot to Dharamshala taxi. Check the Dharamshala weather before you pack.

Rules & safety

What we check before we book your camp

Suzu Travels is an HP Tourism registered travel agent (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). We arrange camps with registered local operators and plan around these checks:

  • RegistrationKangra district orders under the Disaster Management Act made registration compulsory at 10 trek check posts, including Gallu temple. Trekking without it can invite legal action.
  • Winter ordersPast winter orders (November 2024 and 2025) needed permission for Triund and Kareri and stopped trekking above 3,000 m. Expect a fresh order each winter — we check it before quoting.
  • Triund is regulatedLimited trekkers per day and a capped number of tents, camping only in the marked area, through a registered local operator. No self-pitching.
  • FireA forest fire on the Triund route in January 2026 put the area on alert. No fires or smoking on trails; bonfires only where the camp allows them.
  • Turn backCarry a headlamp, rain shell and warm layers; turn back if cloud builds or the last steep bends are icy.
  • Leave no traceNo alcohol on trails; carry back all waste — plastic may be checked at the post.

FAQ

Camping in Dharamshala — questions

Can you camp at Triund?

Yes, but only in the regulated tent area on the ridge, within a daily cap, after registering at the Gallu check post. We arrange it with a registered local operator — see our Triund trek guide.

Is camping allowed around Dharamshala in winter?

Lower camps at Dharamkot and Naddi can run in winter. Triund and Kareri get snow, and Kangra issues winter orders that have restricted trekking above 3,000 m, so we check the order for your dates before we quote.

Where can we camp without trekking?

At drive-in forest and hillside camps at Dharamkot, Gallu Devi and Naddi, about 2–4 km above McLeodganj.

What is included in a Dharamshala camp?

Usually a tent with bedding or a sleeping bag, dinner and breakfast; trek camps add a guide. Bonfires only where the camp allows them.

Do we need to register for Triund or Kareri?

Yes. Kangra made registration compulsory at 10 trek check posts, including Gallu temple, and you need original ID.

When is the best time to camp in Dharamshala?

March to June and September to November. July–August brings heavy rain and leeches; December–February brings snow on the high camps.

Is it safe for families?

Drive-in camps at Naddi or Dharamkot suit families. The Triund night needs a 4–5 hour uphill walk and suits fit teens and adults.
