## Answer-first paragraph (≈60 words)
Thatharana (Thatharna) Top is a meadow at about 2,500–2,750 m on the Dhauladhar slope above Dharamshala. The trail starts at Khadota (Kharota) village, about 2 km above Khaniyara, and climbs 5–7 km through forest in 3–5 hours. Most people camp one night and come down the next morning. Register at the Khadota check post; best months are March–June and September–November.

## Safety checks (specific)
- **Kangra registration:** Khadota village is one of the 10 trek entry check posts notified by DM Kangra (Tribune, 15 Nov 2025); trekking these routes without registration is barred under the Disaster Management Act.
- **3,000 m winter rule:** Kangra DDMA orders (Nov 2024, Jan 2026) banned trekking above 3,000 m and need SP Kangra permission for Kareri, Triund and Adi Himani Chamunda; permissions lapse on an IMD warning. Thatharana Top is below 3,000 m but Kundli Pass beyond it is not — we check the order in force for your date.
- **Group & tracking:** DM Kangra (Jun 2025) asked for registration of all trekkers, guides with max 10 trekkers per group, and GPS tracking on expeditions.
- **Trail:** parts of the forest trail are unmarked — go with a local lead; don't start after ~11 am; carry headlamp.
- **Water:** only one pipe source reported on the way; carry 2 L per person.
- **Snow:** Dec–Feb microspikes / turn back if the top section is icy.
- **Network:** patchy above Khadota; share your plan with the hotel.

## FAQs (verified, 1–3 sentences)
1. **Where does the Thatharana trek start?** At Khadota (also spelt Kharota/Khadota) village, about 2 km above Khaniyara, which is ~8 km from Dharamshala. Some operators count from Khaniyara market, which adds 1–2 hours.
2. **How long is the Thatharana trek?** About 5–7 km one way from Khadota, 3–5 hours up and 2–3 down. Operators who start at Khaniyara quote 12–18 km round trip.
3. **What is the height of Thatharana Top?** Roughly 2,500–2,750 m (8,200–9,000 ft); operators usually say 8,530 ft. It is lower than Triund's ridge but feels steeper.
4. **Is Thatharana harder than Triund?** Yes, slightly — shorter, but steeper throughout and less clearly marked. Fit beginners manage it with a local lead.
5. **Do I need a permit?** No separate permit, but you must register with ID at the Khadota check post, one of Kangra's 10 notified trek entry points. Forest fees, if any, are paid locally.
6. **Can I camp at Thatharana Top?** Yes — operators run 1-night camps on the meadow with tents, dinner and breakfast. Camping is arranged through a registered local operator, not wild-camping.
7. **Is the Thatharana trek open in winter?** The Top is below Kangra's 3,000 m winter limit, so it can open on clear days, but snow on the upper trail and any IMD alert can stop it. We confirm the order in force for your date.
8. **Thatharana or Triund — which should I do?** Triund is easier, busier and has a capped number of tents; Thatharana is quieter with wider Kangra-valley views. Many guests do both on one Dharamshala trip.
