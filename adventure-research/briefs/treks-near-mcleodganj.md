## Answer-first paragraph (≈65 words)
The best treks near McLeodganj are Triund (~9 km, ridge at ~2,850 m), Thatharana (quieter meadow camp from Khadota), Guna Devi temple (easy half-day from Naddi), Kareri Lake (2 days, 2,934 m) and Indrahar Pass (3–4 days, ~4,342 m). Easy walks are Bhagsu waterfall and Gallu Devi. Register with ID at the Kangra check post; above 3,000 m is shut in winter.

## Safety checks (specific)
- **Kangra check posts:** DM Kangra order (Tribune, 15 Nov 2025): registration mandatory at 10 trek entry points incl. Gallu Temple and Khadota village; trekking without registration attracts action under the DM Act.
- **Winter 3,000 m ban:** DDMA Kangra orders (28 Nov 2024; restated 6 Jan 2026): complete ban above 3,000 m; SP Kangra permission for Triund, Kareri, Adi Himani Chamunda; permissions cancelled on any IMD Shimla warning.
- **Guides & groups:** DM directions (Jun 2025): registered guides, max 10 trekkers per guide, GPS tracking on expeditions.
- **Triund camping:** only regulated tents; daily caps change — book ahead.
- **Wildlife & weather:** leopard and bear country on forest trails — start early, no solo night walks; turn back in fog/snow.
- No phone network beyond Triund; nearest hospital Dharamshala.

## FAQs (verified)
1. **Which is the easiest trek near McLeodganj?** Bhagsu waterfall (~3 km) and the Gallu Devi walk from Dharamkot (~2 km) — both fine for families. Guna Devi from Naddi is the easiest full-day hike.
2. **Which trek near McLeodganj is best for beginners with one night?** Triund (~9 km, ridge ~2,850 m) or the quieter Thatharana meadow camp from Khadota. Both are best Mar–Jun and Sep–Nov.
3. **Do I need a permit for treks near McLeodganj?** No separate permit for Indians on day treks, but Kangra requires registration with ID at its trek check posts (e.g., Gallu, Khadota) and, under winter orders, SP permission for Triund and Kareri.
4. **Can I trek near McLeodganj in December or January?** Yes on lower trails — Bhagsu, Gallu, Naddi, Guna Devi, and Triund/Thatharana on permitted clear days. Anything above 3,000 m (Snowline, Indrahar, Kareri beyond) is closed by district order.
5. **How many days do I need?** One day for Triund or Guna Devi, 2 days for Thatharana camp or Kareri Lake, 3–4 days for Indrahar Pass.
6. **Where is Guna Devi temple?** On a ridge above Naddi; the trail goes Naddi → Baal village → temple, about 7–8 km one way from McLeodganj. It's an easy, quiet alternative to Triund.
7. **Which trek is not crowded?** Thatharana and Guna Devi see far fewer people than Triund.
