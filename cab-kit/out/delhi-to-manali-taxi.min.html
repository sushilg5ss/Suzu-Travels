Know before you go

Road tips for Delhi to Manali

No night driving in the hills

For safety our drivers do not run hill sections late at night. Leave early (5–6 am from Delhi) and plan to reach before dark; the quote tells you the best start time.

Himachal entry fee

Cars registered outside Himachal pay an entry fee at the state barrier (₹100 a day for passenger vehicles up to 12 seats in 2026–27; commercial and larger vehicles pay more). It is added to your quote at actuals. Details: Himachal entry tax & permits 2026.

Local sightseeing in Manali

Solang, Atal Tunnel–Sissu and Rohtang trips are done in Manali-registered local taxis (union practice). We book those for you at union rates — see Manali taxi service.

Break the journey?

Families with small children often stop a night in Mandi or Kullu, or go Delhi → Shimla → Manali. Ask for a split itinerary.

Snow months need a plan

From mid-December to February roads above Shimla and Manali can close after fresh snow. Drivers carry chains; sometimes the last stretch is done in a local 4×4. We confirm the day before.

Monsoon caution

Mid-July to August brings landslides on hill highways. We watch road advisories and move your start time or route when needed — no extra charge for a route change we suggest.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

What is the Delhi to Manali taxi fare?

One-way starts around ₹7,599 for a sedan, ₹9,199 for an Ertiga, ₹10,299 for a Kia Carens and ₹12,999 for an Innova Crysta. A 12-seater Tempo Traveller starts around ₹17,299. Tolls, parking and the Himachal entry fee are extra at actuals.

How many hours from Delhi to Manali by taxi?

11–13 hours for about 540 km with breakfast and lunch stops. The Kiratpur–Nerchowk four-lane has cut 2–3 hours off the old road through Bilaspur.

Is it better to leave Delhi at night for Manali?

We don't recommend it. The last 180 km is mountain road and night driving there is riskier. A 4:30–5 am start gets you to Manali by early evening.

Can the same taxi take us to Solang and Rohtang?

Local sightseeing around Manali is done in Manali-registered taxis as per local union practice. We book that local taxi for you at union rates, so you don't have to bargain.

Do you offer Delhi to Manali round trips?

Yes. A round trip or tour is billed per kilometre (250 km daily minimum) plus driver allowance. A 6-day Delhi–Manali–Delhi trip with a Shimla stop is our most booked cab tour.

Which car is best for Delhi to Manali?

A sedan for 2–3 people with light bags; an Ertiga or Innova Crysta for families and heavy luggage; a Tempo Traveller for 8 or more people.
