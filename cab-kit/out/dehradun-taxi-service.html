Know before you go

Good to know about taxis in Dehradun

Airport pickups at Jolly Grant

Share your flight number when you book. The driver tracks the flight and waits at arrivals; Jolly Grant sits between Dehradun and Rishikesh, so a direct airport-to-Rishikesh or airport-to-Mussoorie drop saves a trip into town.

Mussoorie at weekends

Saturday, Sunday and holiday traffic on the Mussoorie road and Mall Road can stand still for hours. Leave Dehradun before 9 am, or do Mussoorie on a weekday and keep the weekend for Rishikesh.

Uttarakhand green cess

Cars registered outside Uttarakhand pay a green cess, read by camera and taken from FASTag at entry points such as Asharodi and Kulhal (Uttarakhand-registered, CNG and electric vehicles are exempt). If it applies to your car, it is added at actuals.

Char Dham season

Dehradun is a common start for the Char Dham yatra. Biometric registration is compulsory for every pilgrim, and the 2026 temples close for winter around 10–13 November, so book late-season trips early. See our Char Dham yatra.

Daylight on hill roads

For safety our drivers avoid late-night hill driving. Mussoorie and Dhanaulti return runs finish before dark in winter; for Shimla or Chakrata start by 7 am.

Monsoon caution

Mid-July to August brings landslides on hill highways. We watch road advisories and move your start time or route when needed — no extra charge for a route change we suggest.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

How much does a taxi in Dehradun cost?

A full-day hire of up to 250 km starts at ₹3,000 in a sedan, ₹3,500 in an Ertiga and ₹4,750 in an Innova Crysta. One-way drops start around ₹2,299 to Mussoorie and ₹2,899 to Delhi in a sedan. Short city and airport runs are quoted on WhatsApp.

What is the Dehradun to Mussoorie taxi fare?

A one-way drop to Mussoorie (about 35 km, 1–1.5 hours) starts around ₹2,299 in a sedan, ₹2,999 in an Ertiga and ₹3,999 in an Innova Crysta. For a same-day return with Kempty Falls or Dhanaulti, the full-day rate of ₹3,000 is better value.

Do you pick up from Dehradun airport (Jolly Grant)?

Yes. Jolly Grant is about 25 km from Dehradun city and closer to Rishikesh. The driver tracks your flight and can take you straight to Dehradun, Mussoorie, Rishikesh or Haridwar.

What is the taxi fare from Dehradun to Delhi?

One-way starts around ₹2,899 in a sedan, ₹3,499 in an Ertiga and ₹4,899 in an Innova Crysta. The drive is about 4–5 hours on the Delhi–Dehradun Expressway; expressway tolls are extra.

How much is a taxi from Dehradun to Rishikesh or Haridwar?

A one-way drop to Rishikesh (about 45 km) or Haridwar (about 55 km) starts around ₹2,299 in a sedan. To see both and come back the same day, take the full-day rate of ₹3,000 (up to 250 km).

Can I book a taxi from Dehradun to Chandigarh or Shimla?

Yes. Dehradun to Chandigarh starts around ₹2,399 in a sedan (about 165 km, 4–4.5 hours via Paonta Sahib and Nahan) and Dehradun to Shimla around ₹3,599. Both cross into Himachal, so the Himachal entry fee is added at actuals.

Do you provide taxis for Char Dham from Dehradun?

Yes. We plan Char Dham and Do Dham trips from Dehradun in a sedan, Innova Crysta or Tempo Traveller, priced per kilometre with a 250 km daily minimum and driver allowance. Pilgrim registration is compulsory and the season runs until the temples close in November.

Is self-drive car rental available in Dehradun?

No — every Suzu car comes with a driver who knows Mussoorie and the Garhwal roads. You get a hatchback, sedan, Ertiga, Kia Carens, Innova Crysta, Tempo Traveller or Force Urbania with driver.
