Delhi to Dehradun Taxi: distance, time and fare

A Delhi to Dehradun taxi now takes about 4–5 hours door to door (roughly 240 km from central Delhi) on the Delhi–Dehradun Expressway via Baghpat, Shamli and Saharanpur. One-way fares start around ₹2,899 for a sedan, ₹3,499 for an Ertiga and ₹4,899 for an Innova Crysta; tolls are extra. Mussoorie is another 35 km, about 1.5 hours.

Know before you go

Road tips for Delhi to Dehradun

Expressway tolls

The expressway is tolled; tolls are paid at actuals and shown in your quote.

Uttarakhand permit

Cars registered outside Uttarakhand pay a state permit/entry fee where applicable — added at actuals.

Mussoorie drops

Weekend traffic on the Mussoorie road is heavy; leave Dehradun before 10 am.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

What is the taxi fare from Delhi to Dehradun?

One-way starts around ₹2,899 for a sedan, ₹3,499 for an Ertiga, ₹3,899 for a Kia Carens and ₹4,899 for an Innova Crysta. Tolls and any state permit are extra at actuals.

How long is Delhi to Dehradun by the new expressway?

About 4–5 hours door to door from central Delhi, depending on city traffic at both ends.

Do you do Dehradun to Delhi taxis?

Yes, the same one-way fares apply from Dehradun, Mussoorie or Jolly Grant airport to Delhi NCR and IGI Airport.

Can we continue to Mussoorie?

Yes — add Mussoorie (35 km, about 1.5 hours) to the same booking.

Do you cover Rishikesh and Haridwar?

Yes, Delhi to Haridwar and Delhi to Rishikesh taxis are available at similar per-km fares.

Is a Tempo Traveller available for Dehradun?

Yes, 12–14 seat Tempo Travellers and Force Urbania vans for groups.
