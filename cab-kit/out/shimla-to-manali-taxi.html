Route plan

Shimla to Manali by road, stop by stop

The route our drivers take, with the stops worth making.

  1. ShimlaPickup from your hotel or the nearest road point (Mall Road is car-free), Shimla railway station, Kufri, Mashobra or Chail.
  2. Ghanahatti · ShalaghatThe road drops west off the Shimla ridge through pine forest; the steepest bends of the day are in the first hour.
  3. DarlaghatCement-plant town on the descent towards the Sutlej; a good tea stop once the hairpins are behind you.
  4. BilaspurRoughly a third of the way, beside the Gobind Sagar lake. The usual breakfast halt.
  5. SundernagarFlat valley town with a lake by the main road; the slowest traffic of the day is often between Bilaspur and here.
  6. MandiThe four-lane bypass skirts the old temple town; lunch here or in Ner Chowk.
  7. Pandoh · Aut tunnelsPast the Pandoh dam the four-lane runs through a string of tunnels along the Beas gorge, then the Aut tunnel opens into the Kullu valley.
  8. Bhuntar · KulluKullu airport sits at Bhuntar; Kasol is a 30 km side trip from here. Shawl and apple stalls line the road through Kullu.
  9. ManaliDrop at your hotel in Manali, Old Manali, Vashisht, Aleo or on the Solang road.

Know before you go

Road tips for Shimla to Manali

Start early, arrive in daylight

Seven to eight hours of hill and valley road is a full day. A 6–7 am start from Shimla gets you to Manali by mid-afternoon with breakfast at Bilaspur and lunch near Mandi; our drivers avoid the hill stretches late at night.

Kullu–Manali stretch

The highway along the Beas between Kullu and Manali was badly hit by the August 2025 floods. As of April 2026 NHAI repairs were still under way and some sections ran single-lane, so the last 40 km can be slow. The driver checks the day's traffic plan before you leave and uses the left-bank road via Naggar if the main road is held up.

No border, no entry fee

Shimla and Manali are both in Himachal, so a Shimla-based car crosses no state barrier on this route. Tolls and parking on the way are added at actuals and shown in your quote.

Jalori Pass instead?

The quieter route via Narkanda, Jalori Pass and Banjar is beautiful but narrow, steep and slower, and Jalori closes after snow. Ask for it only in good weather between May and November — we quote it separately.

Manali sightseeing is a different car

Solang, Atal Tunnel–Sissu and Rohtang trips run in Manali-registered union taxis, and Rohtang needs an online permit and is closed on Tuesdays. We book those at union rates — see Manali taxi service.

Snow months need a plan

From mid-December to February roads above Shimla and Manali can close after fresh snow. Drivers carry chains; sometimes the last stretch is done in a local 4×4. We confirm the day before.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

What is the taxi fare from Shimla to Manali?

One-way fares start around ₹3,399 for a hatchback, ₹3,899 for a sedan, ₹4,699 for an Ertiga, ₹5,299 for a Kia Carens and ₹6,699 for an Innova Crysta. A 12-seater Tempo Traveller starts around ₹8,799. Tolls and parking are extra at actuals; peak dates (May–June, Christmas–New Year) cost more.

How long does Shimla to Manali take by taxi?

About 7–8 hours for roughly 250 km, including breakfast and lunch stops. Weekend traffic near Kullu, or one-way sections on the Kullu–Manali road, can add an hour or more.

Which route does the taxi take from Shimla to Manali?

The usual route goes west from Shimla via Ghanahatti and Darlaghat to Bilaspur, then up the Kiratpur–Manali highway through Sundernagar, the Mandi bypass, the Pandoh–Aut tunnels, Bhuntar and Kullu. The Narkanda–Jalori Pass–Banjar road is a scenic option in good weather only.

Is the Manali to Shimla fare the same?

Yes. The same one-way fares apply from any Manali hotel to anywhere in Shimla, Kufri or Mashobra, and we also run Manali to Shimla drops that continue to Chandigarh or Delhi.

Can we stop at Kullu or Kasol on the way?

Kullu is on the route, so a stop for shawl shopping or river rafting costs only time. Kasol and Manikaran are a 30 km detour from Bhuntar — add it when you book and we quote one fare.

Can I book a Shimla–Manali tour in one car?

Yes. Multi-day tours are billed per kilometre driven (a sedan is ₹12/km, 250 km daily minimum) plus ₹400 a night driver allowance. Delhi or Chandigarh → Shimla → Manali → back in the same car is usually cheaper than separate one-way drops.

Is Shimla to Manali by road open in winter?

Usually, yes — the valley road via Mandi and Kullu sits lower than Shimla and Manali, and fresh snow mostly hits Shimla's upper roads and the area above Manali. From mid-December to February we confirm road status the day before and may move your start time.

Is the Shimla to Manali road safe in the monsoon?

Mid-July to August brings landslides on hill highways. We watch road advisories, avoid late starts, and change the start time or route when needed at no extra charge for a route change we suggest.
