Know before you go

Good to know before you book

Crysta vs Ertiga

For 5+ adults with luggage on hill roads the Crysta's diesel torque, rear AC and ride comfort make a real difference; for 4 people with light bags an Ertiga saves money.

Himachal entry fee

Cars registered outside Himachal pay an entry fee at the state barrier (₹100 a day for passenger vehicles up to 12 seats in 2026–27; commercial and larger vehicles pay more). It is added to your quote at actuals. Details: Himachal entry tax & permits 2026.

No night driving in the hills

For safety our drivers do not run hill sections late at night. Leave early (5–6 am from Delhi) and plan to reach before dark; the quote tells you the best start time.

Captain seats

Want the 6+1 captain-seat version for elders? Ask when you book.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

What is the Innova Crysta rent per km?

About ₹19 per km for round trips and multi-day tours, with a 250 km daily minimum and ₹400 a night driver allowance. Tolls, parking and state entry fees are extra.

What is the Innova Crysta rent per day?

It works out to about ₹4,750 a day (250 km × ₹19) plus driver allowance on a multi-day tour. Local city use is quoted separately.

How much is an Innova Crysta one-way from Delhi to Manali?

From about ₹12,999; Delhi to Shimla from about ₹8,299 and Chandigarh to Manali from about ₹7,699.

How many people fit in an Innova Crysta?

Six passengers comfortably (6+1 captain seats) or seven in the bench version, with about five medium bags.

Do you give Innova Crysta on self-drive?

No, all our cars come with a driver.

Is Innova Hycross or Fortuner available?

On request for some dates; ask on WhatsApp.
