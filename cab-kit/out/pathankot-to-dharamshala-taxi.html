Know before you go

Road tips for Pathankot to Dharamshala

Train timing

Overnight trains reach Pathankot early morning; the driver waits outside with your name. Share the train number.

Himachal entry fee

Cars registered outside Himachal pay an entry fee at the state barrier (₹100 a day for passenger vehicles up to 12 seats in 2026–27; commercial and larger vehicles pay more). It is added to your quote at actuals. Details: Himachal entry tax & permits 2026.

McLeodganj lanes

Some McLeodganj lanes are one-way or narrow; we drop at the closest point to your hotel.

Add Dalhousie

Pathankot → Dalhousie → Khajjiar → Dharamshala is a popular 3–4 day circuit in one cab.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

How much is a taxi from Pathankot to Dharamshala?

One-way starts around ₹2,299 for a sedan, ₹2,999 for an Ertiga and ₹3,999 for an Innova Crysta. Tolls, parking and the Himachal entry fee are extra at actuals.

How far is Dharamshala from Pathankot?

About 90 km — 2.5 to 3 hours by car. McLeodganj is 10 km above Dharamshala.

Can you pick us up at Pathankot or Chakki Bank station?

Yes, from both stations and Pathankot airport; the driver tracks your train.

What is the Pathankot to Dalhousie taxi fare?

Around the same as Dharamshala for a similar distance: sedans start around ₹2,299 one-way.

Do you drop at Kangra (Gaggal) airport?

Yes, Dharamshala to Gaggal airport and Pathankot to Gaggal drops are available.

Can I book a Dharamshala–Dalhousie tour in the same cab?

Yes, multi-day tours are billed per kilometre with a 250 km daily minimum plus driver allowance.
