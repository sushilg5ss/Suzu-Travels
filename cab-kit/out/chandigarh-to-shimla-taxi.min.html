Chandigarh to Shimla Taxi: distance, time and fare

A Chandigarh to Shimla taxi covers about 115 km in 3–3.5 hours via Zirakpur, Pinjore, Kalka–Parwanoo and the four-lane to Solan. One-way fares start around ₹2,499 for a sedan, ₹3,299 for an Ertiga and ₹4,499 for an Innova Crysta, plus tolls and the Himachal entry fee at actuals. Same-day return trips with Kufri are popular too.

Know before you go

Road tips for Chandigarh to Shimla

Himachal entry fee

Cars registered outside Himachal pay an entry fee at the state barrier (₹100 a day for passenger vehicles up to 12 seats in 2026–27; commercial and larger vehicles pay more). It is added to your quote at actuals. Details: Himachal entry tax & permits 2026.

Kasauli, Chail, Kufri

Each adds 1–3 hours. Tell us when you book and we fold it into one fare.

Shimla's sealed roads

The Mall and Ridge are car-free. We drop at the nearest allowed point and help with porters if your hotel is uphill.

Weekend traffic

Friday evening and Sunday afternoon queues at Parwanoo can add 1–2 hours; an early start avoids them.

Snow months need a plan

From mid-December to February roads above Shimla and Manali can close after fresh snow. Drivers carry chains; sometimes the last stretch is done in a local 4×4. We confirm the day before.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

How much is a taxi from Chandigarh to Shimla?

One-way starts around ₹2,499 for a sedan, ₹3,299 for an Ertiga, ₹3,799 for a Kia Carens and ₹4,499 for an Innova Crysta. Tolls, parking and the Himachal entry fee are extra at actuals.

How long does Chandigarh to Shimla take?

About 3–3.5 hours for 115 km. Add 1–2 hours on busy weekends.

Can I do Chandigarh to Shimla and back in one day?

Yes. A same-day return with Kufri or the Mall is common; it's billed as a local round trip. Ask for the day-trip fare.

Do you pick up from Chandigarh airport and railway station?

Yes, from IXC arrivals, Chandigarh railway station, Kalka station, ISBT-43 and any address in Mohali, Panchkula or Zirakpur.

Is Shimla to Chandigarh the same price?

Yes, the same fares apply in reverse, including drops at Chandigarh airport.

Can we add Kasauli on the way?

Yes, Kasauli is a short detour from Dharampur. Most people add 2–3 hours for Christ Church and Sunset Point.
