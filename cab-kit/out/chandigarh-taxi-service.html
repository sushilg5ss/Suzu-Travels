Know before you go

Good to know about taxis in Chandigarh

Airport pickups at IXC

Share your flight number when you book. The driver tracks the flight and meets you at arrivals with a name card; airport parking is added at actuals.

Daylight on hill roads

For safety our drivers do not run hill sections late at night. For Manali, Kasol or Dharamshala leave Chandigarh by 6–7 am; Shimla and Kasauli are short enough for a relaxed morning start.

Himachal entry fee

Cars registered outside Himachal pay an entry fee at the state barrier (₹100 a day for passenger vehicles up to 12 seats in 2026–27; commercial and larger vehicles pay more). It is added to your quote at actuals. Details: Himachal entry tax & permits 2026.

Winter fog on the plains

In December and January dense morning fog can slow the NH44 towards Delhi and Amritsar. We may suggest a slightly later start so you drive in better visibility.

One car for a multi-stop trip

Chandigarh → Kasauli → Shimla → Manali → back is cheaper as one per-km tour than as separate one-way drops. Ask for a split itinerary and we'll price both.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

How much does a taxi in Chandigarh cost?

A full-day hire of up to 250 km starts at ₹3,000 in a sedan, ₹3,500 in an Ertiga and ₹4,750 in an Innova Crysta. One-way drops start around ₹2,299 to Shimla or Kasauli and ₹2,999 to Delhi in a sedan. Short airport and city runs are quoted on WhatsApp.

What is the Chandigarh to Shimla taxi fare?

One-way starts around ₹2,299 in a sedan, ₹2,999 in an Ertiga and ₹3,999 in an Innova Crysta for about 115 km (3–3.5 hours). The Himachal entry fee and tolls are extra at actuals.

How much is a taxi from Chandigarh to Kasauli?

A one-way drop to Kasauli (about 60 km, around 2 hours via Parwanoo) starts at ₹2,299 in a sedan. For a same-day return, the full-day rate of ₹3,000 (up to 250 km) is better value and lets you add Pinjore or Morni on the way.

Do you pick up from Chandigarh airport (IXC)?

Yes. IXC is about 12 km from the city centre. The driver tracks your flight and meets you at arrivals, for a Tricity drop or straight to Shimla, Kasauli, Manali or Dharamshala.

What is the Chandigarh to Amritsar taxi fare?

One-way starts around ₹2,799 in a sedan and ₹4,699 in an Innova Crysta. The drive is about 230 km, 4–5 hours on the NH44 via Ludhiana and Jalandhar.

Can I book a one-way taxi from Chandigarh to Delhi?

Yes. Chandigarh to Delhi one-way starts around ₹2,999 in a sedan and ₹5,099 in an Innova Crysta, about 4–5 hours. We drop at IGI airport, any station or your address.

How much is a Chandigarh to Manali or Kasol taxi?

Chandigarh to Manali starts around ₹4,499 and Chandigarh to Kasol around ₹4,399 in a sedan, one-way, both about 7–8 hours via the Kiratpur–Nerchowk four-lane and Mandi.

Do you have Innova Crysta and Tempo Traveller in Chandigarh?

Yes — hatchback, sedan, Ertiga, Kia Carens, Innova Crysta, 12–14 seat Tempo Traveller and Force Urbania, all with a driver.
