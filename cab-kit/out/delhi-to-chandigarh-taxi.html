Delhi to Chandigarh Taxi: distance, time and fare

A Delhi to Chandigarh taxi covers about 250 km in 4–5 hours on the NH44 via Murthal, Panipat, Karnal and Ambala. One-way fares start around ₹2,999 for a sedan, ₹3,699 for an Ertiga and ₹5,099 for an Innova Crysta; tolls are extra at actuals. The same fares apply for Chandigarh to Delhi and Chandigarh to IGI Airport drops.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

What is the one-way taxi fare from Delhi to Chandigarh?

Sedans start around ₹2,999, hatchbacks around ₹2,599, Ertiga around ₹3,699 and Innova Crysta around ₹5,099. Tolls are extra at actuals.

Is the Chandigarh to Delhi fare the same?

Yes — the same one-way fares apply from Chandigarh, Mohali or Panchkula to anywhere in Delhi NCR, including IGI Airport.

How long does Delhi to Chandigarh take by taxi?

4–5 hours for about 250 km on NH44, depending on traffic when leaving Delhi.

Do you do Chandigarh to Delhi airport drops?

Yes, to IGI T1, T2 and T3. We add a buffer for check-in and track your flight time.

Can I book a hatchback for Delhi to Chandigarh?

Yes, a hatchback is the cheapest option for 1–3 people with light luggage.

Can the taxi continue to Shimla or Manali?

Yes. Book Delhi → Shimla or Delhi → Manali directly and save on the second leg.
