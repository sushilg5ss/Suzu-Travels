Route plan

Popular taxi trips from Haridwar

Where our Haridwar cabs go most, and what each trip is known for.

  1. HaridwarPickup at Haridwar Junction railway station, the Rodibelwala bus stand, Har Ki Pauri parking, Bhupatwala and Shantikunj ashrams, or your hotel. Cars cannot drive to the ghat itself; the driver uses the nearest parking.
  2. Mansa Devi · Chandi DeviThe two hilltop temples are reached by ropeway — Mansa Devi above the town, Chandi Devi across the Ganga at Neel Dhara. A half-day local run covers both plus Daksh Mahadev at Kankhal.
  3. RishikeshAbout 25 km, 45 minutes via Raiwala — Tapovan, Laxman Jhula side, Ram Jhula parking, AIIMS or the Yog Nagari station. Shivpuri rafting camps sit about 16 km further up the Badrinath road.
  4. Neelkanth MahadevThe Shiva temple in the forest beyond Rishikesh is a common half-day add-on; the hill road is narrow and crowded in Sawan, so go early.
  5. Jolly Grant · DehradunDehradun airport is about 38 km (around an hour) and Dehradun city about 55 km through the Rajaji forest belt and Doiwala.
  6. MussoorieAbout 90 km via Dehradun, 3 hours or so with the climb — Mall Road, Kempty Falls and Landour make a long full day or an easy overnight.
  7. Sonprayag · BadrinathChar Dham drops along the Ganga: Sonprayag (the last stop for cars on the Kedarnath route) is about 235 km via Devprayag and Rudraprayag; Badrinath is about 320 km, a 10-hour mountain day.
  8. Delhi · ChandigarhDelhi about 220 km, 4.5–5.5 hours via Roorkee, Muzaffarnagar and the Delhi–Meerut Expressway; Chandigarh about 210 km via Roorkee, Saharanpur and Yamunanagar.

Know before you go

Good to know about taxis in Haridwar

Har Ki Pauri is on foot

The lanes around Har Ki Pauri are closed to cars. The driver drops you at the nearest parking and waits; from there it is a short walk or an e-rickshaw. For the evening Ganga aarti (around sunset) be at the ghat an hour early.

Uttarakhand green cess

Cars registered outside Uttarakhand pay a small green cess (₹80 for light vehicles since 15 February 2026), read by cameras at the border; CNG and electric cars are exempt. It is added to your quote at actuals. Our Haridwar pickups are local trips, so it only applies on the way in from Delhi.

Kanwar Yatra and festival days

During the Sawan Kanwar Yatra (late July to early August) the Delhi–Haridwar highway closes to cars for several days and the town fills with kanwariyas. Ganga Dussehra, Somvati Amavasya and other bathing days bring police diversions too — we plan pickup points around them.

Ardh Kumbh 2027

Haridwar's Ardh Kumbh runs through March–April 2027, with main bathing days on 6 and 8 March and 14 April. Book cars early and expect drop points well away from the ghats.

Char Dham from Haridwar

Biometric yatra registration is compulsory for every pilgrim, and in 2026 Gangotri, Yamunotri and Kedarnath are expected to close around 10–11 November, Badrinath later in November. Leave Haridwar by 5–6 am for Sonprayag or Badrinath; our drivers do not run mountain roads after dark. See our Char Dham yatra.

Monsoon caution

Mid-July to August brings landslides on hill highways. We watch road advisories and move your start time or route when needed — no extra charge for a route change we suggest.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

How much does a taxi in Haridwar cost?

A full-day hire of up to 250 km starts at ₹3,000 in a sedan, ₹3,500 in an Ertiga and ₹4,750 in an Innova Crysta. One-way drops start around ₹2,699 to Delhi and ₹3,699 to Sonprayag in a sedan. Short station, ghat and Rishikesh runs are quoted on WhatsApp.

What is the Haridwar to Rishikesh taxi fare?

Rishikesh is about 25 km (45 minutes), so a simple drop is quoted on WhatsApp for your pickup point and car. To see Rishikesh, Laxman Jhula side and Neelkanth Mahadev and come back the same day, a full day of up to 250 km starts at ₹3,000 in a sedan.

What is the Haridwar to Kedarnath taxi fare?

Cars go as far as Sonprayag, about 235 km from Haridwar. A one-way drop starts around ₹3,699 in a sedan, ₹4,399 in an Ertiga and ₹6,299 in an Innova Crysta. From Sonprayag a shuttle runs to Gaurikund, where the trek to Kedarnath begins. Most families book the whole trip as a per-km round tour instead.

What is the Haridwar to Badrinath taxi fare?

A one-way drop to Badrinath (about 320 km, around 10 hours via Devprayag, Rudraprayag and Joshimath) starts around ₹4,999 in a sedan and ₹8,499 in an Innova Crysta. A Badrinath round trip is billed per kilometre with a 250 km daily minimum plus ₹400 a night driver allowance.

What is the taxi fare from Haridwar to Delhi?

One-way starts around ₹2,699 in a sedan, ₹3,199 in an Ertiga and ₹4,499 in an Innova Crysta for about 220 km (4.5–5.5 hours). Drops anywhere in Delhi NCR including IGI Airport; tolls are extra at actuals.

How much is a taxi from Haridwar to Mussoorie or Dehradun?

Mussoorie is about 90 km via Dehradun; a one-way drop starts around ₹2,299 in a sedan, and a same-day return with Kempty Falls fits in the full-day rate of ₹3,000. Dehradun city (about 55 km) also starts around ₹2,299.

Do you pick up from Haridwar railway station and Jolly Grant airport?

Yes. The driver meets you at Haridwar Junction or tracks your flight into Jolly Grant (Dehradun) airport, about 38 km away, and takes you to your Haridwar ashram, Rishikesh, or straight onward.

Can the same taxi take us on the Char Dham or Do Dham yatra?

Yes. We plan Char Dham and Do Dham (Kedarnath + Badrinath) trips from Haridwar in a sedan, Innova Crysta or Tempo Traveller, priced per kilometre with a 250 km daily minimum and driver allowance. Pilgrim registration is compulsory.
