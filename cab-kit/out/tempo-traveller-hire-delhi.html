Know before you go

Good to know before you book

State permits

Tempo Travellers pay a state permit/tax when they enter Himachal, Uttarakhand, Punjab or J&K. It's charged at actuals and shown in your quote.

Luggage

12 people need a roof carrier and a tarpaulin in rain; both are included.

No night driving in the hills

For safety our drivers do not run hill sections late at night. Leave early (5–6 am from Delhi) and plan to reach before dark; the quote tells you the best start time.

Luxury options

Maharaja-seat Tempo Travellers and 20–26 seaters can be arranged on request.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

What is the Tempo Traveller rent per km in Delhi?

From about ₹26 per km for a 12–14 seat Force Tempo Traveller and about ₹36 per km for a Force Urbania, with a 250 km daily minimum and ₹500 a night driver allowance.

How much is a Tempo Traveller from Delhi to Shimla or Manali?

One-way Delhi to Shimla starts around ₹11,099 and Delhi to Manali around ₹17,299 for a 12-seater. A 6-day Shimla–Manali round trip is billed per km.

What's included in the Tempo Traveller fare?

Vehicle, fuel and driver. Tolls, parking, state permits and the driver's night allowance are added as per your quote.

Is a 12 seater enough for 12 adults with luggage?

It's comfortable for 10–11 adults with bags; for 12 adults with heavy luggage choose the Urbania or two vehicles.

Do you have 16 or 20 seater Tempo Travellers?

Yes, on request; availability depends on the date.

Can we book a Tempo Traveller for Char Dham from Delhi?

Yes, with a driver experienced on the Char Dham routes. Book early for May–June.
