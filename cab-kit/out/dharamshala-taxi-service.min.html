Know before you go

Good to know about taxis in Dharamshala

Airport pickups at Gaggal

Share your flight number when you book. The driver tracks the flight and waits outside arrivals at Kangra airport; if your hotel is in McLeodganj or Dharamkot, tell us — the last stretch is steep and narrow, and we plan the drop point in advance.

McLeodganj lanes and parking

Upper Dharamshala runs on a one-way traffic loop and parking near the main square fills fast at weekends. We drop at the closest allowed point to your hotel. The Dharamshala Skyway ropeway between lower Dharamshala and McLeodganj is a handy hop for a day in town.

Himachal entry fee

Cars registered outside Himachal pay an entry fee at the state barrier (₹100 a day for passenger vehicles up to 12 seats in 2026–27; commercial and larger vehicles pay more). It is added to your quote at actuals. Details: Himachal entry tax & permits 2026.

Bir paragliding season

Bir–Billing flying is best from March to June and in October–November, when skies are clear. Combine Bir with Palampur and Baijnath in one full-day hire instead of two separate drops.

Daylight on hill roads

For safety our drivers avoid late-night hill driving. For Manali or Shimla leave Dharamshala by 7 am; Delhi is a full day, so a 5–6 am start is best.

One car for the Kangra circuit

Dharamshala → Dalhousie → Khajjiar → Pathankot or Amritsar is cheaper as one per-km tour than as separate drops. Ask for both prices and pick the better one.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

How much does a taxi in Dharamshala cost?

A full-day hire of up to 250 km starts at ₹3,000 in a sedan, ₹3,500 in an Ertiga and ₹4,750 in an Innova Crysta. One-way drops start around ₹2,299 to Pathankot and ₹2,799 to Amritsar in a sedan. Short airport and McLeodganj runs are quoted on WhatsApp.

Do you pick up from Dharamshala (Gaggal) airport?

Yes. Kangra airport at Gaggal is about 13 km from Dharamshala, 20–30 minutes by car. The driver tracks your flight and can take you to Dharamshala, McLeodganj, Palampur, Bir or Dalhousie.

What is the taxi fare from Dharamshala to Pathankot?

One-way starts around ₹2,299 in a sedan, ₹2,999 in an Ertiga and ₹3,999 in an Innova Crysta for about 90 km (2.5–3 hours). We drop at Pathankot Junction, Chakki Bank or Pathankot airport.

How much is a taxi from Dharamshala to Dalhousie?

A one-way drop to Dalhousie (about 118 km) starts around ₹2,299 in a sedan. To include Khajjiar and come back, a 2-day hire of up to 500 km starts at ₹6,400 in a sedan.

How do I get from Dharamshala to Bir Billing by taxi?

Bir is about 70 km from Dharamshala, 2–2.5 hours via Palampur and Baijnath. A full day of up to 250 km starts at ₹3,000 in a sedan and covers Bir, Palampur's tea gardens and Baijnath temple in one go.

What is the Dharamshala to Amritsar taxi fare?

One-way starts around ₹2,799 in a sedan and ₹4,799 in an Innova Crysta. The drive is about 200 km, 4–5 hours via Pathankot.

Can I book a taxi from Dharamshala to Manali, Chandigarh or Delhi?

Yes. Manali starts around ₹3,699 and Chandigarh around ₹3,699 in a sedan, one-way; Dharamshala to Delhi starts around ₹6,699 for about 475 km. Tolls are extra at actuals.

How do I get from Dharamshala to McLeodganj?

McLeodganj is about 10 km uphill from lower Dharamshala. A taxi takes around 20–30 minutes depending on traffic; the Skyway ropeway is the other option. We include McLeodganj drops in airport and outstation bookings.
