India's most famous route, explained in 40 seconds 🇮🇳
Delhi → Agra → Jaipur: three cities, one loop of about 720 km by road, with each leg around 4–6 hours.
Humayun's Tomb and Qutub Minar in Delhi, the Taj Mahal at sunrise in Agra, then Amber Fort and Hawa Mahal in Jaipur. There are UNESCO World Heritage Sites in every city.
Our advice: 5–6 days, a private driver and an early start at the Taj.
