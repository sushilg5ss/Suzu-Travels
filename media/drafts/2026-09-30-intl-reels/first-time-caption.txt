First time in India? Here are 5 things to know before you book ✈️
1. Your visa is online. The India e-Visa comes as 30 days, 1 year or 5 years. Apply at least 4 days before you fly.
2. Come between October and March, when most of India is cool and dry. For the Himalayas, go April to June.
3. India is big. Fly between regions and drive within them.
4. Get a local ground team: one WhatsApp number for hotels, driver, guides and 24/7 help.
5. Give it time. Plan 5–7 days for one region, or 12–14 days for two.
