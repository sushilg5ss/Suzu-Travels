October is here — India's best travel season just opened. 🇮🇳

October to March is when India shines: Rajasthan's forts and the Golden Triangle in golden light, Kerala green and calm after the monsoon, Goa's beaches, and snow in Manali & Solang Valley from December to February.
Our tip: December–January is the busiest time of year (over 1 million international visitors in Dec 2025, Ministry of Tourism) — so plan and book early. Save Ladakh & Spiti for June–September.
