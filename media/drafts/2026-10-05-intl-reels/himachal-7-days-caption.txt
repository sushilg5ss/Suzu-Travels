Himachal in 7 days — from Delhi to the Himalayan snowline. 🏔️

Day 1–2 Shimla: ride the UNESCO-listed Kalka–Shimla toy train (102 tunnels, running since 1903).
Day 3–4 Manali: cedar forests, cafés and rivers — our tip: stay in Old Manali for slower, quieter days and better views.
Day 5–6: drive through the 9.02 km Atal Tunnel at ~3,000 m, then play in the snow at Solang Valley.
Our tip: Mar–Jun & Sep–Nov for green valleys, Dec–Feb for snow.
