Kerala in 7 days: coast, tea hills and backwaters 🌴

Day 1 – Fort Kochi and its Chinese fishing nets at sunset
Day 2–3 – Munnar's rolling tea hills (go early, before the mist lifts)
Day 4 – Thekkady, in the UNESCO-listed Western Ghats
Day 5–7 – A night on an Alleppey houseboat. Our tip: Sep–Mar, after the monsoon.
