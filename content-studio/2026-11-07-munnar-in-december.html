What is the weather like in Munnar in December?

Munnar sits at about 1,500–1,600 m in the Western Ghats of Idukki district, so it runs about ten degrees cooler than Kochi on the coast. The northeast monsoon is fading by December, which is why the month is both greener and drier than November. Long-term averages compiled by weather2travel give Munnar a December high of about 22°C, a low of about 13°C, around 96 mm of rain on about five days and roughly seven hours of sunshine a day.

Two things matter more than the averages. First, the town and the estates behave differently: Munnar town sits in a valley, while estates such as Chenduvara, Sevenmallay, Lakshmi and Kundala are higher or more exposed and lose heat fast on clear nights. Second, the cold arrives in the second half of the month. Early December feels like a pleasant hill-station autumn; the last ten days are when locals start talking about frost.

Does Munnar get frost or snow in December?

Munnar does not get snow. It does get ground frost, and that is what social media calls "snowfall in Munnar". On a clear, windless night the grass in the high estates freezes, and for an hour or so after sunrise the slopes look white. Recent records show how it plays out:

  • December 2025: Kerala Kaumudi reported the season's first sub-zero reading at the Sevenmallay section of the KDH plantation on 20 December 2025; Chenduvara had touched 0°C on the 15th and 2°C on the 19th.
  • December 2024: Onmanorama reported about 0°C at Chenduvarai, Lakshmi Estate, Chittuvara and nearby estates on the morning of 25 December 2024, with Munnar town, Mattupetty and Kundala at about 2°C, while afternoons were still around 22°C.
  • January 2019: The News Minute reported −4°C at Chenduvara and −2°C at Sevenmallay and Silent Valley on 7 January 2019, one of the coldest mornings in recent memory.

So if frost is the goal, aim for the last ten days of December or the first three weeks of January, stay a night in or near the estates rather than in town, and be out by 6 am. It is never guaranteed: a cloudy night keeps the ground warm and there is no frost at all.

Which places in Munnar are open in December?

Everything on the usual Munnar list is open in December, and the dry weather makes this one of the better months to see it. The main stops, with the details that change your day:

  • Eravikulam National Park (Rajamala): home of the Nilgiri tahr and the Anamudi slopes. Open daily 8 am to 4 pm per the March 2026 reopening notice, which listed ₹125 for Indian adults, ₹95 for children and ₹420 for foreign nationals. The park closes for about two months, usually February and March, for tahr calving, so December is safe. Book online or reach the gate at opening in Christmas week.
  • Tea Museum (Nallathanni, KDHP): close to town; closed on Mondays. Good for an hour on the first afternoon.
  • Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point and Kundala Lake: on one road east of town towards Top Station, easy to cover in a half day with boating on clear afternoons.
  • Top Station: about an hour beyond Kundala on the Tamil Nadu border, with the best valley views on clear December mornings before cloud rises.
  • Kolukkumalai sunrise: a 4 am jeep from Suryanelli, about 22 km from Munnar, up to one of the highest tea estates in the area. Worth it on a clear night; pointless in cloud, so decide the evening before.
  • Attukal and Lakkam waterfalls: still flowing after the monsoon, though with less force than in October.

Is December too crowded in Munnar?

The first half of December is quiet and good value. From about 20 December to 2 January Munnar fills with Christmas and New Year visitors from Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and further north; resort rates rise to their highest of the year, many properties set two-night minimums and the Mattupetty road and the Eravikulam entry queue slow down by mid-morning. Weekends in the second half of December are busy too.

Three practical rules from planning these trips: book the Munnar hotel before anything else, ideally by mid-November for Christmas week; do Eravikulam at 8 am, not at 11 am; and keep the Top Station drive for a weekday morning. If your dates are flexible, 1–18 December gives you the same weather with half the traffic.

How do you reach Munnar in December?

The usual entry is Kochi. Cochin International Airport and Ernakulam railway station are both about 3.5–4 hours from Munnar by road via Kothamangalam and Adimali, with the Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls on the way. There is no railway or airport in Munnar itself. The ghat road is fine in December, but after dark it is narrow and misty in places, so plan to arrive in daylight. What should you pack for Munnar in December?

  • T-shirts and a light full-sleeve shirt for the 20–22°C afternoons
  • A fleece or light jacket for every evening
  • A warmer jacket, cap and socks if you are doing a dawn jeep trip or staying in the high estates
  • Walking shoes with grip; estate paths stay damp under the tea bushes
  • A compact umbrella or rain shell for the odd afternoon shower
  • Sunscreen: the high-altitude sun is strong on clear days even when the air is cool

December is one of the two best months for Munnar (January is the other). Frequently asked questions

Is Munnar cold in December?

Cool rather than cold by north Indian standards. Long-term averages put Munnar at about 22°C by day and 13°C at night in December. Early mornings in the higher tea estates can fall to 0–2°C in the second half of the month, and frost has formed on the grass on some nights, so pack a fleece and one warm layer.

Does it snow in Munnar in December?

No. What people call snow in Munnar is ground frost: on clear, still nights late in December the grass in estates such as Chenduvara and Sevenmallay freezes white for an hour or two after dawn. Munnar town usually stays a few degrees above zero. Real snowfall needs the Himalaya.

Is Eravikulam National Park open in December?

Yes. Eravikulam closes for about two months each year, usually February and March, while the Nilgiri tahr have their young. In December it is open daily, 8 am to 4 pm according to the March 2026 reopening notice. Go at opening time on Christmas week, because ticket queues build fast.

Does it rain in Munnar in December?

A little. December averages about 96 mm of rain over roughly five days, far less than November (about 176 mm) and the monsoon months. Showers tend to be short and in the afternoon. By January rainfall drops to about 22 mm, so late December is usually the clearer half of the month.

Is December a good time for a Munnar honeymoon?

Yes, it is one of the best months: green tea slopes, cool nights for a fire or a hot drink, and clear sunrise views. The catch is price and crowds between about 20 December and 2 January, so book your resort early or come in the first two weeks of December.

How many days do you need in Munnar in December?

Two nights is right for most travellers: one full day for Eravikulam, the Tea Museum, Mattupetty and Echo Point, and one for Top Station or a Kolukkumalai sunrise. Add a third night if you want an estate stay or a slower pace. Most people combine Munnar with Thekkady and an Alleppey houseboat.

What should I wear in Munnar in December?

Layers. T-shirts and a light shirt for the 20–22°C afternoons, a fleece or light jacket for evenings, and a warmer jacket, cap and socks if you are doing a dawn jeep trip or staying high up in the estates. Add walking shoes with grip and a small umbrella for the odd shower.
