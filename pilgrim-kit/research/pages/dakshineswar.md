## 2. Facts
- Names: **Dakshineswar Kali Temple**, deity **Bhavatarini** (a form of Kali, "she who carries devotees across the ocean of existence"); Devanagari **दक्षिणेश्वर काली मंदिर** (no Bengali script — fonts) — yatradham; kamarpukur (as_of 2026-11-06).
- Location: **east bank of the Hooghly**, Dakshineswar, Kamarhati, **North 24 Parganas, West Bengal**, at the eastern end of Vivekananda Setu — OSM/Nominatim (as_of 2026-11-06).
- Founder & history (dated): built by **Rani Rashmoni** of Janbazar; construction began **1847**, temple consecrated **31 May 1855** (Snana Yatra day) — yatradham, kamarpukur, The Telegraph all agree (as_of 2026-11-06). Orthodox priests at first hesitated to serve a temple founded by a woman of a non-Brahmin caste — **do not print (caste content)**. First priest **Ramkumar Chattopadhyay**; his younger brother **Gadadhar — later Sri Ramakrishna** — soon became the priest (The Telegraph; kamarpukur says Gadadhar directly → use "Ramkumar, then his brother Gadadhar").
- Legend (own words, as tradition, ≈110 words): Tradition says Rani Rashmoni was preparing a long river pilgrimage to Kashi when, the night before departure, she had a dream in which the Divine Mother told her there was no need to travel — she should build a temple on the bank of the Ganga and worship her there. (Sources differ on whether Kali or Shiva appeared → say "the Divine Mother", per the more common telling, or "a dream".) The Rani bought land on the river and built the temple, and the goddess was installed as **Bhavatarini**. Here a young priest, Gadadhar, worshipped Kali with such intensity that he became known as **Sri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa**; his wife **Sarada Devi** lived in the Nahabat, and a young **Narendranath — later Swami Vivekananda** — first came to meet him here. — yatradham; kamarpukur; Belur Math media (as_of 2026-11-06). Biography, not miracle claims.
- Architecture: **navaratna** (nine-spired) Bengal-style main temple, **over 100 ft**, on a raised platform; **12 Shiva temples** in **aat-chala** style along the river front; **Radha-Kanta (Radha-Krishna) temple**; **natmandir** — kamarpukur; yatradham (as_of 2026-11-06).
- Idol: black-stone **Bhavatarini** standing on **Shiva** lying on his back (white marble) — kamarpukur (as_of 2026-11-06). "Thousand-petalled silver lotus" — Wikipedia only → UNVERIFIED, omit. Avoid "self-manifested" (kamarpukur says it, unverified).
- Campus: **Sri Ramakrishna's room** (north-west corner), **Panchavati** (grove where he meditated), **Nahabat** (Sarada Devi's quarters), bathing ghat — kamarpukur; Belur Math (as_of 2026-11-06). Security note: Panchavati/ghat gate may be closed and the room seen only from outside (The Telegraph) → "parts of the campus may be cordoned off — follow staff instructions".
- New (2026): a **new temple of Sri Ramakrishna was consecrated at Ramakrishna Math, Dakshineswar on 16 Jul 2026** (Rathayatra) — separate from the Kali temple — Belur Math media https://media.belurmath.org/consecration-of-sri-ramakrishna-temple-dakshineshwar-july-2026-24528/ (as_of 2026-11-06). ## 4. Questions people ask
1. **What are Dakshineswar temple timings?** About 6 am–12:30 pm and 3:30–8:30 pm daily (till about 9 pm in summer); confirm locally.
2. **Is there an entry ticket?** No — entry is free.
3. **Can I carry my phone inside?** No. Phones, cameras, leather items and big bags go into the free lockers near the skywalk entrance.
4. **Who built Dakshineswar Kali Temple?** Rani Rashmoni; it was consecrated on 31 May 1855.
5. **Is Dakshineswar a Shakti Peeth?** No. It is a 19th-century temple; Kolkata's Shakti Peeth is Kalighat.
6. **How do I go from Dakshineswar to Belur Math?** By ferry across the Hooghly (about 20 minutes, timings vary with the tide) or about 5 km by road over the bridge.
7. **What is the nearest metro station?** Dakshineswar Metro (Blue Line), linked to the temple by the Rani Rashmoni skywalk.
8. **What is the connection with Sri Ramakrishna?** He served as the temple's priest; his room, the Panchavati and Sarada Devi's Nahabat are on the campus.
