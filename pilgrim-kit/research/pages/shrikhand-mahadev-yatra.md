## 2. Facts
- Names: Shrikhand Mahadev / **श्रीखंड महादेव**; also **Shrikhand Kailash**, Shikhar Kailash — data; Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrikhand_Mahadev (as_of 2026-10-28).
- Location: peak above the **Nirmand sub-division, Kullu district, Himachal Pradesh**; road head **Jaon village**, Nirmand — official portal https://shrikhandyatra.hp.gov.in/ ; Tribune 18 Jun 2026 (2026-10-28). Coordinates of the lingam **UNVERIFIED** (Wikipedia gives none) → no `place` lat/lon; use Nirmand 31.4250, 77.5742 only for the route map start (Nominatim, 2026-10-28).
- What is worshipped: a natural **rock pinnacle revered as a Shiva lingam**, **72 ft** (official portal; Tribune 2024 + 2026) — Wikipedia says 75 ft → print "about 72 ft (22 m)". Seen as an abode of Shiva and Parvati — portal; Wikipedia (2026-10-28).
- Altitude: official portal "**approx. 17,000 ft**"; Tribune "18,570 ft" (2024) / "18,750 ft" (2026); FPJ 5,227 m; Wikipedia 5,155 m (and 18,570 ft — internally inconsistent) → print "**above 5,100 m (about 17,000–18,500 ft; sources differ)**". Mountains page 15844 prints 5,227 m — leave it (theirs).
- Panch Kailash: counted as one of the **Panch Kailash** (Kailash, Adi Kailash, Shrikhand, Kinnaur Kailash, Manimahesh) — Wikipedia (order of "importance" given there is not authoritative → don't rank).
- Legend (own words, as tradition — ≈120 words): Local tradition tells that the demon **Bhasmasura** won from Shiva the power to turn to ash anyone whose head he touched, and then tried it on Shiva himself. Shiva withdrew into these mountains, and **Vishnu, taking the form of the enchantress Mohini**, led the demon into a dance in which he touched his own head and was destroyed. Pilgrims point to the reddish rock and water along the route as reminders of the story. The **Pandavas** are believed to have passed this way in exile — the camps **Bheem Dwar** and **Bheem Talai** carry Bhima's name. **Parvati** is believed to have planted the meadow of **Parvati Bagh**, where Brahma Kamal flowers grow, a flower linked in tradition with Shiva restoring Ganesha. — data/yatras.json; portal "more info"; Wikipedia (paraphrased). **Not scripture-sourced** → write "local tradition".
- Organisation: yatra **organised by the District Administration Kullu and managed by the Shrikhand Mahadev Yatra Trust, Nirmand**, in **July for 10–12 days**; DC Kullu = trust chairman — official portal; Tribune 18/26 Jun 2026 (2026-10-28).
- **2026 status: SUSPENDED.** Planned **10–23 Jul 2026** (Trust; Tribune 18 Jun, FPJ 18 Jun). Joint team (Revenue, Forest, ABVIMAS Manali) inspected **8 and 18 Jun 2026**; experts called the **Bheem Dwar–Parvati Bagh** stretch extremely hazardous (loose soil, stream crossings, flash floods; temporary bridges not feasible) and recommended a GSI geological survey — Tribune 26 Jun 2026 https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/himachal/uncertainty-surrounds-shrikhand-mahadev-yatra-as-experts-flag-high-risk-route/ . DC Kullu (Anurag Chandra Sharma) **suspended the yatra "until further notice" on 29 Jun 2026** under BNSS s.163 and ordered camps removed within 3 days — FPJ 29 Jun 2026 https://www.freepressjournal.in/lifestyle/shrikhand-mahadev-yatra-2026-pilgrimage-suspended-till-further-notice-over-route-safety-concerns-in-himachal-pradesh-when-will-it-resume . Searched today: **no resumption in 2026, no 2027 dates** (as_of 2026-10-28). The portal itself still says "dates will be updated soon" (2026-10-28).
- Past seasons (for "usual pattern"): 2024 flagged off **14 Jul** from Singhgad, planned to 27 Jul; >4,000 registered online; **4 deaths that year so far, about 35 in 13 years**; 2023 largely suspended by weather — Tribune 15 Jul 2024 https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/himachal/shrikhand-mahadev-yatra-begins-pilgrims-told-to-exercise-caution-639865 . → "usually mid-July, about two weeks, only when the DC notifies it".
- Registration (official, 2026 portal notice): **mandatory** — pilgrims without registration can be sent back from any base camp; **medical check-up at base camp Singhgad**; fee **non-refundable** if you don't go or are found unfit; persons **under 18 and over 60 are advised not to go**; registration fee per pilgrim is printed on the portal (**do not print the amount** — build.py blocks ₹; say "a small registration fee, paid on the official portal"); offline registration at Singhgad in past seasons (Tribune 2024) — https://shrikhandyatra.hp.gov.in/ (2026-10-28). **Only official site: shrikhandyatra.hp.gov.in** (+ DC Kullu hpkullu.nic.in) → fake-site warning.
- Helplines (official portal): SDM Nirmand **01904-299139**; Police Station Nirmand **01904-255126** (emergency) — print them as official numbers, not Suzu's (2026-10-28).
- Route (official portal "more info", 2026-10-28) — total **about 35 km one way** (portal; Tribune 2026 "35 km"; Tribune 2024 / Wikipedia "32 km"), **4–5 days** return:
  - Jaon (road head) → **Singhgad** ~3 km (1st base camp; homestays, tents, langar; guest houses in Nirmand & Jaon)
  - Singhgad → **Thachru** ~12 km, ~11,000 ft (steep "Dandi Dhar"; recommended night halt)
  - Thachru → (Kali Ghati) → **Kunsha** ~7 km, ~12,500 ft
  - Kunsha → **Bheem Dwar (Bhimdwari)** ~3.5 km
  - Bheem Dwar → **Parvati Bagh** ~3 km (last camp)
  - Parvati Bagh → **Nain Sarovar** (lake, ~14,500 ft, ~1.5 h) → **Shrikhand lingam**: 6–7 km, 4+ h — "the most difficult and challenging stretch"
  - **Five base camps**: Singhgad, Thachru, Kunsha, Bheem Dwar, Parvati Bagh — portal; Tribune 2024 + 2026. Stage km differ between sources (FPJ/Wikipedia split Kali Ghati, Bheem Talai) → print the portal's figures as "about".
- On-site options: **trek only** — no ropeway, no helicopter; ponies not used on the upper route (data; UNVERIFIED for lower stretch → don't promise any). Glacier/boulder sections, no night trekking (Tribune 2024).
- Safety set-up (2026 plan, never executed): six sectors with magistrates, 18 SDRF + 24 ABVIMAS rescuers, medical teams, water at camps — Tribune 18 Jun 2026.
- What to carry (portal): water bottles, glucose, warm clothing, rainwear, torch, dry fruit, own sleeping bag, light luggage; expect rain and even snow in July; "medically unfit are not advised".
- How to reach (portal): leave the Shimla–Rampur highway at **Duttnagar or Bajirbavli**, cross the **Satluj** to **Nirmand**, drive on to **Jaon**. OSRM from Nirmand (2026-10-28): **Rampur 18 km**, **Shimla Ridge 127 km** (~3 h), **Shimla airport (Jubbarhatti) 145 km**, **Bhuntar (Kullu) airport 150 km**, **Kalka railway 208 km**, Chandigarh 235 km, Delhi 505 km, **Ghumarwin (Suzu office) 220 km**. Shimla = narrow-gauge rail from Kalka (no rail beyond).
- Nearby (portal): **Parshuram temple, Nirmand**, **Ambika Mata temple**, **Dev Dhank** cave; Dattatreya temple at Dutt Nagar is a government-trust temple (lac.hp.gov.in); **Bhimakali, Sarahan** is on the same Rampur side (≈ Rampur 42 km, per bhimakali pack).
- Accessibility: **not for elders** — official advice under 18 / over 60 should not go; medically screened; summit day 6–7 km of boulder/glacier at >4,400 m. Elders → suggest Bhimakali (Sarahan) or Naina Devi instead.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **Is Shrikhand Mahadev Yatra open in 2026?** No. It was planned for 10–23 July 2026 but the Kullu administration suspended it on 29 June 2026 on safety grounds; it did not resume.
2. **When is the Shrikhand Yatra 2027?** Not announced yet. It is usually notified by DC Kullu for about two weeks in mid-July; watch shrikhandyatra.hp.gov.in.
3. **How high is Shrikhand Mahadev?** Above 5,100 m — sources give 17,000 to about 18,500 ft.
4. **How long is the trek?** About 32–35 km one way from Jaon village; 4–5 days return.
5. **Is registration compulsory?** Yes — online on the trust portal (offline at Singhgad in past years), with a medical check at Singhgad base camp; unregistered pilgrims can be sent back.
6. **Is there an age limit?** The trust advises people under 18 and over 60 not to attempt it.
7. **Which are the base camps?** Singhgad, Thachru, Kunsha, Bheem Dwar and Parvati Bagh.
8. **How do I reach Jaon?** Shimla–Rampur highway → Duttnagar/Bajirbavli → cross the Satluj to Nirmand → Jaon; Nirmand is ~127 km from Shimla, 18 km from Rampur.
(Plus: "Is it the toughest yatra?" → widely considered one of India's hardest, glacier and boulder stretches; "Ponies/helicopter?" → no, trek only.)
