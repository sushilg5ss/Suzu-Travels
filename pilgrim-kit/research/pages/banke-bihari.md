## 2. Facts
- Names: **Shri Banke Bihari Ji Temple** — **श्री बांके बिहारी मंदिर, वृंदावन**; deity first worshipped as **Kunj Bihari**; "Banke" = bent in three places (tribhanga), "Bihari" = one who enjoys — bihariji.org MandirHistory (official) + Wikipedia (2026-10-31).
- Location: **Vrindavan, Mathura district, Uttar Pradesh**, in the old town lanes near Nidhivan — coords ~27.5800 N, 77.6905 E (approx.).
- Deity: Krishna as Banke Bihari — tradition holds the single black image is **Radha and Krishna united** (bihariji.org, official).
- Legend / history (own words, as tradition, ≈120 words): The temple's tradition says the saint-musician **Swami Haridas** (born on Radha Ashtami, **1478 CE / Vikram Samvat 1535** per the temple) sang to Radha and Krishna in the groves of **Nidhivan**; at his request the divine couple appeared and merged into one dark image, which he worshipped as Kunj Bihari. His disciple **Goswami Jagannath** was given the seva, and his descendants — the **Goswami families** — still serve the deity. The image was moved from Nidhivan to the present temple, built by the Goswamis around **1862** (temple site; Wikipedia says c. 1864 → print "in the 1860s" or "1862, per the temple"). Haridas is remembered as the teacher of **Tansen**; a well-loved story tells of Akbar visiting in disguise to hear him (tradition). The appearance day is celebrated as **Bihar Panchami** — https://bihariji.org/public/MandirHistory.aspx (official, 2026-10-31); https://bihariji.org/akbarvisit.php.
- **Customs** (Wikipedia + temple site; tradition): a **curtain (parda) is drawn every few minutes** — tradition says the deity's gaze is too captivating to be watched unbroken; **no bells** are rung because the deity is cared for as a child; **Mangla aarti only once a year (Janmashtami)**; **flute (bansi) only on Sharad Purnima**; **charan (feet) darshan only on Akshaya Tritiya**; the **jhulan (swing) in Shravan** (Hariyali Teej). ## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Banke Bihari temple timings?** Per the temple website (summer, till 10 Nov 2026): 7:45 am–12 noon and 5:30–9:30 pm; winter timings start on Bhai Dooj (11 Nov 2026). Hours have been extended by the court-appointed committee since 2025 — confirm locally.
2. **Why is the curtain drawn again and again?** Tradition says Banke Bihari's gaze is so captivating that devotees could lose themselves, so the parda is drawn every few minutes.
3. **Why are there no bells in the temple?** The deity is cared for as a child, and bells are not rung so as not to disturb him.
4. **When is Mangla aarti at Banke Bihari?** Only once a year, in the early hours after Janmashtami, with limited entry passes.
5. **Are mobile phones allowed?** The temple's own guidelines ask devotees not to bring phones or electronic gadgets; keep them in your vehicle or hotel.
6. **Is there VIP darshan?** No — VIP slips were stopped in 2025 and everyone queues together; there is no online booking.
7. **How far is Banke Bihari from Mathura Junction?** About 13 km — 20 minutes by road, then a short walk or e-rickshaw through the lanes. From Delhi airport it is about 150 km.
8. **When is the best time to visit?** Weekday mornings, October to March; avoid weekends, Ekadashi, Purnima and festival days with elders or small children.
