## 2. Facts
- Names: Shri Mata Vaishno Devi / **श्री माता वैष्णो देवी**; the yatra **माता वैष्णो देवी यात्रा** — data.
- Location: **Bhawan** (holy cave) on the three-peaked **Trikuta** hill, **Reasi district, Jammu & Kashmir (UT)**; base town **Katra**; Bhawan ~1,585 m (data, UNVERIFIED exact); 33.0299, 74.9482 — data / Wikipedia.
- Deity: no idol — **three natural rock pindis** of **Mahakali, Mahalakshmi and Mahasaraswati** in the cave — data.
- Shakti Peeth status: **not in the classical 51 / Ashtadasha-18 lists**; counted in broader 64/108 traditions; skull vs right-arm tradition contested (trap 1) — data.
- Legend (own words, as tradition — popular Vaishno Devi tradition, Shrine Board retelling): The Goddess took birth as Vaishnavi, uniting the powers of Kali, Lakshmi and Saraswati, and was asked by Rama to wait in the Trikuta hills. The cave was revealed to **Pandit Shridhar** of Hansali village after a divine girl attended his feast. When the tantric **Bhairon Nath** pursued her up the mountain, she struck the ground with an arrow to create the **Banganga** stream, rested at **Charan Paduka**, and stayed nine months in the narrow **Ardhkuwari (Garbh Joon)** cave. At the holy cave she took the form of Mahakali and ended his chase; his head fell where the **Bhairon temple** now stands. She forgave him and declared that the yatra is complete only after visiting his shrine. (~130 words) — data.
- Aarti & darshan (**Shrine Board, maavaishnodevi.org/blog/aarti, 2026-10-15**): aarti **twice daily — just before sunrise and just after sunset**, takes **nearly two hours**, darshan **suspended** during it; otherwise darshan round the clock as a continuously moving queue (no group darshan) — no clock times published → "confirm with the Shrine Board helpline **1800-180-7212** / 9906019494". **Atka Aarti** seats bookable online (data).
- **Registration (RFID)** — Shrine Board page (2026-10-15): separate **RFID Yatra Access Card** for every pilgrim, free; issued at the **Yatri Registration Counter (YRC) near Katra bus stand**, **YRC-II** near the second bus stand on the Udhampur road (~1 km), or **online**; checked at **Banganga** check post (~1.5 km from YRC); without it you are turned back. Time limit: page says **cross Banganga within 6 h** of issue; Dec 2025 reports say **start within 10 h** (Dainik Jagran) / **12 h** (SPO India) and **return to Katra within 24 h** — conflicting → print "within the time limit printed on your card; return within 24 hours per Dec 2025 rules — check maavaishnodevi.org" (trap 17). Daily cap ~30,000–35,000, registration pauses in rush (ETV Bharat 23 Dec 2025, data).
- Route (data, km "about"): Katra → Banganga ~1.5 km → Charan Paduka → **Ardhkuwari** (~6 km) → old track via Sanjichhat **or new track via Himkoti** → **Bhawan** (total **~12–13 km**, 5–8 h up for most walkers — UNVERIFIED hours) → **Bhairon temple** ~1.5 km above Bhawan (ropeway or ~45–60 min walk) → descent via Sanjichhat to Katra. Paved, lit, shelters, langar.
- On-track options (book only on **online.maavaishnodevi.org** or Shrine Board counters):
  - **Helicopter Katra (Serli helipad) → Sanjichhat**; walk ~2.5 km Sanjichhat → Bhawan (data; distance UNVERIFIED) — not to the Bhawan (trap 13). Fares not stored (official notice controls).
  - **Battery cars** Ardhkuwari ↔ Bhawan, priority for seniors and disabled (data).
  - **Bhawan–Bhairon passenger ropeway**, ~9 am–5 pm (data).
  - Ponies, palkis, pithoos (registered, rates fixed by authorities — UNVERIFIED current rates; not printed).

- Festivals & crowds: open **all year**; peaks at **Navratri** (Sharad 2026: 11–19 Oct; 47,346 pilgrims on day 1, >95,000 in two days — Kashmir Observer 12 Oct 2026; Shat Chandi Maha Yagya at the Yagyashala, Purnahuti on Mahanavami) — **Chaitra Navratri 2027 from 7 Apr 2027** (data); New Year week and summer holidays (May–Jun) very crowded.

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Not one of the classical 51 Shakti Peeths** (README §2); skull vs right arm contested.
- **Helicopter lands at Sanjichhat, not Bhawan**; book only on online.maavaishnodevi.org — fake helicopter-booking sites/WhatsApp sellers are common (33,100 searches/month = big scam target).
- RFID time limit 6 h / 10 h / 12 h conflict (trap 17) — don't state one number as settled.
- Trek distance: "12 km", "13 km", "14 km" all appear — print "about 12–13 km one way (plus ~1.5 km to Bhairon)".
- Aarti clock times on blogs are invented — Shrine Board only says "before sunrise / after sunset".
- Fake "official" sites: maavaishnodevi.live, vaishnodevidevasthan.org.in etc. appeared in today's search — never cite them.
- Butter? No — don't mix Kangra traditions here. Kashmir combo keyword belongs to 9367.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **How long is the Vaishno Devi trek from Katra?** About 12–13 km one way to the Bhawan, plus about 1.5 km to Bhairon temple.
2. **Is RFID card mandatory?** Yes — free, one per pilgrim, from Katra counters or online; start within the time limit on the card.
3. **Where do I book the Vaishno Devi helicopter?** Only on online.maavaishnodevi.org; it flies Katra to Sanjichhat, then you walk to the Bhawan.
4. **What are the aarti timings?** Twice a day, just before sunrise and just after sunset; darshan pauses for about two hours.
5. **Is the yatra complete without Bhairon temple?** By tradition, no — visiting Bhairon Nath completes it.
6. **Nearest railway station?** Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra (SVDK); Jammu airport is about 50 km away.
7. **Is Vaishno Devi open in winter?** Yes, all year; Navratri, New Year and summer holidays are the busiest.
8. **Is Vaishno Devi a Shakti Peeth?** It is not in the classical 51 lists but is revered as a great Devi shrine and the "elder sister" of the Saat Behna.
