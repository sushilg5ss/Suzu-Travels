## 2. Facts
- Names: Shree Somnath Temple (Somnath Mahadev Mandir) / सोमनाथ ज्योतिर्लिंग (श्री सोमनाथ मंदिर); first of the 12 in the Shiva Purana list and in the popular stotra ("सौराष्ट्रे सोमनाथं च") — data (2026-10-08); notes FACT TRAP #3
- Location: Prabhas Patan, near Veraval, Gir Somnath district, Gujarat; Arabian Sea shore; Triveni Sangam (Hiran, Kapila, Saraswati) nearby; 20.888, 70.4014 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somnath_Temple (as_of 2026-10-08)
- Managing body: Shree Somnath Trust — official site https://somnath.org (as_of 2026-10-08)
- Deity: Shiva as Somnath, "Lord of Soma (the Moon)"; Jyotirlinga — data
- Legend (own words, as tradition — Shiva Purana, Koti Rudra Samhita; Skanda Purana, Prabhas Khanda): The Moon, Chandra, was married to Daksha's twenty-seven daughters but gave his heart to Rohini alone. When the other sisters were neglected, Daksha warned him, and when the warning was ignored he cursed the Moon to wither away. As moonlight faded, plants and people suffered. Advised by Brahma, Chandra came to Prabhas on the Saurashtra coast and worshipped Shiva for a long time. Shiva could not undo the curse completely, so he softened it: the Moon would shrink through one fortnight and grow again through the next — which is how the waxing and waning began. Chandra, also called Soma, asked Shiva to stay at Prabhas for ever, and the shrine became Somnath, the Lord of Soma. (≈125 words) — data legend_summary (2026-10-08)
- History (dated):
  - **January 1026** — Mahmud of Ghazni raided the temple (two Persian sources say 1027) — Wikipedia (2026-10-08); FACT TRAP #5 (not 1025)
  - **10–11 Jan 2026** — "Somnath Swabhiman Parv" marked 1,000 years since the raid; PM Modi attended — https://www.theweek.in/news/india/2026/01/10/what-is-the-somnath-swabhiman-parv-inside-the-grand-event-attended-by-pm-modi.amp.html (2026-10-08)
  - **c. 1782–83** — Ahilyabai Holkar built a separate smaller Somnath temple nearby so worship could continue (Trust: 1782; Wikipedia: c. 1783 or 1785/86) — https://somnath.org/somnath-darshan/ (2026-10-08)
  - **12/13 Nov 1947** — Sardar Patel resolved to rebuild (Trust 13 Nov visit; Wikipedia 12 Nov order) — somnath.org/somnath-darshan (2026-10-08)
  - **11 May 1951** — Pran-Pratishtha by President Dr Rajendra Prasad at K. M. Munshi's invitation — somnath.org/somnath-darshan (2026-10-08); FACT TRAP #6 (not Nehru, not 1950)
  - **11 May 2026** — "Somnath Amrut Parv" / Amrut Mahotsav for 75 years of the Pran-Pratishtha; PM Modi attended — https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/pm-visits-somnath-temple/ (headline-level, as_of 2026-10-11; added to data today)
  - Present temple style: Chalukya/Solanki-style (Maru-Gurjara) — Wikipedia — UNVERIFIED today; architect Prabhashankar Sompura — UNVERIFIED today (only use with "according to…").
  - Bāṇa Stambha (arrow pillar) inscription saying no land lies between this shore and the South Pole — popular, UNVERIFIED today → if used, "an inscription on the Baan Stambh is said to…".

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- Raid year 1026, not 1025 (#5). Rebuild: Rajendra Prasad 11 May 1951 (#6); Patel date 12 vs 13 Nov 1947 — give "November 1947".
- Light-show time differs between two Trust pages (#20).
- Booking frauds: only somnath.org (#15).
- History of repeated destruction and rebuilding: state dates plainly, no communal framing; avoid "destroyed 17 times" (popular number, UNVERIFIED).
- "First Jyotirlinga" is true in both the Shiva Purana and smaranam lists — safe.
- Ahilyabai's temple (1782) is a separate, older-style shrine nearby, not the main 1951 temple.
- Don't call the Baan Stambh claim "scientific fact".

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Somnath temple darshan timings?** 6 am – 10 pm daily; aartis at 7 am, 12 noon and 7 pm (Trust).
2. **Can I take my phone inside Somnath temple?** No — phones, cameras and smartwatches stay in the free cloakroom.
3. **How do I book a room at Somnath Trust guest houses?** Only on somnath.org; the Trust warns against WhatsApp/phone "agents".
4. **What time is the Somnath light and sound show?** Around 8 pm nightly except in monsoon (Trust pages say 8–9 pm and 7:45–8:45 pm) — confirm on arrival.
5. **Why is it called Somnath?** Soma (the Moon) worshipped Shiva here to be freed from Daksha's curse; Shiva stayed as the Lord of Soma.
6. **Who rebuilt the present Somnath temple?** After Sardar Patel's resolve in Nov 1947, the jyotirlinga was consecrated by President Rajendra Prasad on 11 May 1951.
7. **How to reach Somnath?** Somnath station is 0.5 km away and Veraval Jn 7 km; nearest airport Diu (~85 km), then Rajkot (~200 km).
8. **Is there a dress code at Somnath?** Modest clothing; mini skirts and disrespectful dress are not allowed.
