## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- "Nau Devi = Navadurga" — wrong: Nau Devi Yatra is a circuit of nine shrines; Navadurga are nine forms worshipped on the nine nights (and the "Nau Devi" cave near Katra enshrines Navadurga pindis).
- Which nine? Traditional (Kalka + Shakumbhari) vs package variant (Baglamukhi + Sheetla Devi). Show both; never call one "the official list".
- Not all nine are classical Shakti Peeths: only Naina Devi, Jwala Ji, Brajeshwari are in compiled 51-tables (Chintpurni disputed). Vaishno Devi, Mansa Devi, Chamunda, Kalka, Shakumbhari are not.
- Mansa Devi here = **Panchkula**, not Haridwar.
- Naina Devi = **Bilaspur (HP)**, not Nainital.
- Vaishno Devi RFID time limit: 6 h vs 10 h (Dec 2025 reports) — don't state one number as settled.
- Vaishno Devi helicopter lands at Sanjichhat, not Bhawan.
- Sharad Navratri 2026 = 11 Oct (not 22 Sep).
- Competitor pages quote prices ("from ₹18,000") — we never show prices.

## 4. Questions people ask (with short answers)
1. **Which are the 9 Devis of the Nau Devi Yatra?** Traditionally Vaishno Devi, Mansa Devi (Panchkula), Kali Mata (Kalka), Shakumbhari Devi, Naina Devi, Chintpurni, Jwala Ji, Kangra Devi (Brajeshwari) and Chamunda. Many packages swap Kalka & Shakumbhari for Baglamukhi and Sheetla Devi.
2. **How many days does the Nau Devi Yatra take?** About 9 days from Delhi for all nine; 7 days from Chandigarh for the common package route.
3. **What is the best route order?** Shakumbhari → Mansa Devi → Kalka → Naina Devi → Chintpurni → Jwala Ji → Kangra → Chamunda → Vaishno Devi (or the reverse, starting at Jammu/Katra).
4. **Is the Nau Devi Yatra the same as Navadurga?** No — Navadurga are nine forms of Durga; Nau Devi is a trip to nine temples.
5. **Are all nine Shakti Peeths?** No. Naina Devi, Jwala Ji and Kangra Devi are in the classical lists; Chintpurni's place is disputed; the others are revered local-tradition shrines.
6. **Do I need registration?** Only for Vaishno Devi — the free RFID card (online.maavaishnodevi.org or Katra counters); start the climb within the time limit on your card.
7. **Best time to do the Nau Devi Yatra?** Mar–Jun and Sep–Nov. Navratri is the most auspicious but also the most crowded; avoid peak monsoon.
8. **Can elderly parents do it?** Yes, with planning: cable car at Naina Devi; helicopter or battery car options at Vaishno Devi; travel outside Navratri/Shravan and keep 9 days rather than 7.
