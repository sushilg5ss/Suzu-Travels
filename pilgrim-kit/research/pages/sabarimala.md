## 2. Facts
- Names: **Sabarimala Sree Dharma Sastha Temple** (Ayyappa / Ayyappan, Hariharaputra, Manikandan, Dharma Sastha) — **सबरीमाला श्री धर्म शास्ता मंदिर** / **सबरीमला अय्यप्पा मंदिर** — Kerala Tourism https://www.keralatourism.org/destination/sabarimala-temple/47 (2026-10-31); Devanagari = standard transliteration.
- Location: **Sabarimala, Ranni taluk (Perunad), Pathanamthitta district, Kerala**, on a hill inside the **Periyar Tiger Reserve**, Western Ghats; ~72 km from Pathanamthitta town — Kerala Tourism (2026-10-31); Wikipedia. Coords **9.4356 N, 77.0806 E** (Nominatim, Sannidhanam, 2026-10-31). **Altitude: sources conflict (≈ 470–480 m commonly quoted for the shrine; Wikipedia 1,260 m for the hill) → do not print a figure.**

- Deity: **Ayyappa (Dharma Sastha)**, in tradition the son of **Shiva and Mohini** (Vishnu's female form) — Wikipedia Ayyappan (2026-10-31).
- Legend (own words, as tradition, ≈130 words): Tradition — found in Kerala folklore and later Puranic-style retellings — says Ayyappa was born of **Shiva and Mohini** to defeat the demoness **Mahishi**. The childless **king of Pandalam** found a baby boy beside the **Pamba river**, a bell around his neck, and raised him as **Manikandan**. When the jealous queen feigned illness and asked for **tigress's milk**, the boy went into the forest, slew Mahishi and returned riding a tiger, revealing his divinity. He is believed to have shot an arrow to mark where his shrine should stand and to have taken his seat at Sabarimala as a celibate yogi. His companion **Vavar** is honoured too — variants make him a warrior, a reformed robber or a saint. — Wikipedia Ayyappan / Sabarimala (paraphrased, 2026-10-31). King's name (Rajasekhara) appears only in some variants → omit or "the Pandalam king". Scholars see the cult as layered (local forest deity + Puranic story) → one neutral line max.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **When does Sabarimala open for Mandala 2026?** The nada is expected to open on the evening of 16 November 2026, with the Mandala season from 17 November; confirm on the TDB site.
2. **How do I book the Sabarimala virtual queue?** Free, only on sabarimalaonline.org — booking for 2026-27 opened on 29 October 2026; carry the printout and your ID.
3. **How many pilgrims are allowed per day?** Up to 80,000 a day in 2026-27, including limited spot booking at base centres.
4. **When is Makaravilakku 2027?** Expected on 14 January 2027 (Makara Sankranti); the Mandala Pooja is expected on 27 December 2026.
5. **How far is the temple from Pamba?** About 5 km uphill on foot via Neelimala and Appachimedu.
6. **Which is the nearest railway station?** Chengannur, about 85 km from Pamba; Kottayam is about 95 km. Nearest airports: Kochi (~155 km) and Thiruvananthapuram (~170 km).
7. **What is the 41-day vratham and irumudi?** A 41-day period of simple, disciplined living before the pilgrimage; the irumudi is the two-part offering bundle carried on the head — only pilgrims with it climb the 18 holy steps.
8. **Can women visit Sabarimala?** Girls under 10 and women over 50 make the pilgrimage; entry for ages 10–50 is before a nine-judge Supreme Court bench, which reserved its verdict in May 2026.
