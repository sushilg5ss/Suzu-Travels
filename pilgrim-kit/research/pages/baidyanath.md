## 2. Facts
- Names: Baba Baidyanath Dham / वैद्यनाथ ज्योतिर्लिंग (बाबा बैद्यनाथ धाम); also Baba Dham, Vaidyanath; Deoghar = "abode of the gods" — data + https://deoghar.nic.in/about-district/ (2026-10-18).
- Place in lists: Shiva Purana list "वैद्यनाथं चिताभूमौ" (in the cremation-ground land) → tradition links to Deoghar; the popular smaranam says "परल्यां वैद्यनाथं" → Parli (Maharashtra). FACT TRAPS #3, #4.
- Location: Deoghar town, Deoghar district, **Jharkhand** (Santhal Pargana); 24.4925, 86.70 (Wikipedia, low precision — **verify on map before pinning**) — data.
- **Jyotirlinga + Shakti Peeth:** the district administration counts it as one of the 12 Jyotirlingas **and** one of the 51 Shakti Peeths, the only place with both together — deoghar.nic.in (2026-10-18). Tradition: Sati's heart fell here (Hriday Peeth; Devi as Jaya Durga) — tradition, lists differ (state as "according to tradition").
- Deity: Shiva as Vaidyanath, "Lord of Physicians".
- Legend (own words, as tradition — Shiva Purana's Vaidyanath account): Ravana did fierce penance for Shiva, offering his heads one by one. Shiva healed him — hence Vaidyanath, the Lord of Physicians — and gave him a linga to take to Lanka, on one condition: wherever it first touched the ground, it would stay. Worried about Ravana's growing power, the gods made sure he had to stop on the way. A cowherd — said to be Vishnu in disguise, remembered locally as Baiju — took the linga from him and set it down. Ravana could not lift it again; in anger he pressed it with his thumb, and the linga has stayed at Deoghar ever since. (≈115 words) — data legend_summary
- History (state plainly):
  - Origin "lost in antiquity"; Sanskrit texts call the area Haritakivan / Ketakivan; parts of the front portion said to have been built by **Puran Mal**, ancestor of the Maharaja of Gidhaur, in **1596** — deoghar.nic.in (2026-10-18).
  - Gidhaur rulers (principality founded **1266**) closely attached to the temple — Wikipedia (data).
  - **1788** — East India Company official supervised pilgrim dues; control later returned to the high priest — Wikipedia (data).
  - **12 Jul 2022** — Deoghar Airport inaugurated, ~9 km from the temple — narendramodi.in (data; cloud fetch of .in sites often fails, headline-level).

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Which Vaidyanath?** (#4) Three shrines claim it: **Deoghar** (most widely accepted; Shiva Purana "chitabhumi"), **Parli Vaijnath**, Beed, Maharashtra (smaranam "Paralyam"; 18.8428, 76.5354), and **Baijnath, Kangra, HP** (Ravana legend, temple of 1204 CE; less commonly counted). State all three neutrally; Baijnath HP gets its own page (row 30).
- **Baidyanath ≠ Baijnath** — spelling confusion all over the web; disambiguate in the first screen.
- **Nearest station:** many sites say only "Jasidih" — Baidyanath Dham station is in town; Jasidih is the main-line junction ~7 km away. (FIX NEEDED raised for the 12-JL page today.)
- **Kanwar distance:** 105 km (data/PTI) vs 108 km (district) → "about 105–108 km".
- **Shravan start:** Deoghar follows Purnimanta Shravan (2026: 30 Jul), not the Amanta date (13 Aug) used in Maharashtra (#16).
- **Timings:** header 4 am–9 pm vs schedule ending 8 pm (#20).
- **Shakti Peeth body part / Devi name** varies between lists — "according to tradition".
- Trikut ropeway: don't present as operating.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Baba Baidyanath Dham darshan timings?** The temple's website lists about 4 am – 4 pm and 6 – 8 pm, with longer hours in Shravan and on Mahashivratri; confirm locally.
2. **Where is the real Vaidyanath Jyotirlinga?** Deoghar (Jharkhand) is the most widely accepted; Maharashtra tradition honours Parli Vaijnath, and Baijnath in Himachal has its own Ravana legend.
3. **What is the Shravani Mela?** A month-long Shravan fair when kanwariyas walk about 105–108 km from Sultanganj in Bihar with Ganga water for Baba Baidyanath.
4. **When is Shravani Mela 2027?** Not announced yet; it follows the North-Indian (Purnimanta) Shravan month, usually July–August (2026 began 30 July).
5. **Is Baidyanath also a Shakti Peeth?** Yes, by tradition — the district administration describes it as both a Jyotirlinga and one of the 51 Shakti Peeths.
6. **Which is the nearest railway station and airport?** Baidyanath Dham station in Deoghar town, Jasidih Junction (main line) about 7 km away, and Deoghar Airport about 9 km away.
7. **Why is it called Vaidyanath?** Vaidya means physician; tradition says Shiva healed Ravana here.
8. **What else can I visit nearby?** Basukinath (about 43 km), Naulakha Mandir, Tapovan and Trikut Parvat — check the Trikut ropeway's status locally.
