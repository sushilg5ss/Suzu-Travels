## 2. Facts
- Names: **Sri Harmandir Sahib** ("abode of God"), also **Darbar Sahib**, popularly the **Golden Temple** — Gurmukhi **ਸ੍ਰੀ ਹਰਿਮੰਦਰ ਸਾਹਿਬ**, Hindi **श्री हरमंदिर साहिब (स्वर्ण मंदिर)** (data) — Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Temple ; SGPC https://sgpc.net/sri-harmandir-sahib-2/ (2026-10-27).
- Location: **Amritsar, Punjab**, in the walled old city; coords **31.6200, 74.8764** (Wikipedia).
- Management: **SGPC**, Amritsar, under the **Sikh Gurdwara Act 1925** (SGPC history page, 2026-10-27).
- Faith context: the holiest gurdwara of Sikhism; the **Guru Granth Sahib** is enshrined in the sanctum; open to people of every faith, caste and background — SGPC (four doors).
- History (own words; dated; SGPC first):
  - **1570–1577** — the sacred pool (Amrit Sarovar) dug under **Guru Ram Das** (conceived by Guru Amar Das), with **Baba Budha ji** supervising; the town (Ramdaspur, later Amritsar) grew around it — SGPC; Wikipedia gives 1577 for the pool.
  - Foundation: **SGPC: foundation stone laid in December 1588 (1 Magh 1645 Bikrami) by Hazrat Mian Mir of Lahore, at Guru Arjan's request**; Wikipedia: construction began **1581**, brick temple done **1589**, and calls the Mian Mir account a later tradition → print "Guru Arjan Dev ji built the Harmandir; Sikh tradition (and the SGPC) records that the Sufi saint Mian Mir laid its foundation stone in 1588".
  - **1604** — Guru Arjan installed the **Adi Granth** (first edition of the Guru Granth Sahib) and appointed Baba Budha ji the first granthi — SGPC (Bhadon Sudi 1, 1661 Bikrami = Aug/Sep 1604); Wikipedia **16 Aug 1604**. SGPC page also says "1601" for completion in one line → print "completed and the Adi Granth installed in 1604".
  - **1757, 1762 (10 Apr), Dec 1764** — destroyed/desecrated by Ahmad Shah Abdali's forces; rebuilt by the Sikhs each time (SGPC; Wikipedia).
  - **1809** marble and copper rebuild, **1830** gold leaf over the upper storeys under **Maharaja Ranjit Singh** — giving the name "Golden Temple" (Wikipedia; SGPC confirms his patronage, no year).
  - 1984: **do not narrate** (no political/communal content). If the Page Builder needs a line: none — skip.
- Architecture: the sanctum stands in the middle of the ~150 m square **Amrit Sarovar**; reached by a causeway through the **Darshani Deorhi** gateway; marble **parikrama** walkway around the pool, walked clockwise; **four entrances** (symbol of openness to all); the sanctum is built **lower** than the surrounding ground, so visitors step down (humility) — Wikipedia; SGPC (four doors). **Akal Takht** faces the causeway (seat of Sikh temporal authority; the Palki Sahib procession moves between it and the sanctum — SGPC routine).
- Daily routine (**official SGPC, winter 18 Dec – 17 Feb**, old.sgpc.net, read 2026-10-27): doors open and kirtan begins **3:00 am** · Asa di Var kirtan 4:00 am · **Palki Sahib** leaves Akal Takht **5:00 am** (Prakash — the Guru Granth Sahib is brought to the sanctum) · first Hukamnama 5:30 am · Ardas 6:00 am · Rehras after sunset · last Hukamnama **9:30 pm** · Palki Sahib leaves the sanctum 9:45 pm and reaches Akal Takht **10:00 pm** (Sukhasan) · then the sanctum floor is washed (SGPC: with diluted milk) — kirtan is continuous. **Summer timings: not on the official page** → "earlier in summer — confirm locally" (sites say ~2 am open — UNVERIFIED). The complex and parikrama are open round the clock (data/yatras.json; widely reported).
- Entry: **free**, no ticket or registration (data).
- Rules (SGPC, as reported by The Tribune 26 Jun 2024 / 9 Aug 2024): **cover your head** (scarves available), modest clothing; **shoes and socks** left at the free shoe deposit, wash hands and feet at the entrance; **tobacco, cigarettes, alcohol and any intoxicants are banned** (even in bags); phones on silent; **videos prohibited** on the premises; photos on phones allowed only as a keepsake (SGPC secretary, Aug 2024); sit with legs folded on the parikrama; don't put anything in the sarovar — https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/amritsar/devotees-can-click-photos-for-memory-sake-cant-record-videos-in-golden-temple-complex-sgpc ; https://tribuneindia.com/news/influencers-photo-incident-puts-spotlight-back-on-sgpc-rule-book-633832 (read 2026-10-27).
- **Langar** (Guru ka Langar): free vegetarian meals for all, **over 100,000 a day** (Wikipedia; data) — volunteers welcome to help (sewa); sit in pangat (rows on the floor).
- Stay: SGPC sarais near the complex; **book only on www.sgpcsarai.com** — SGPC warned (Jun & Sep 2024) about fake sites/WhatsApp numbers asking for advance via QR codes; SGPC never asks for QR/wallet payment — The Tribune https://tribuneindia.com/news/sgpc-gets-fake-web-portal-used-to-book-accommodation-blocked-632793 ; https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/amritsar/sgpc-warns-devotees-against-booking-city-based-sarais-through-fake-websites/amp (read 2026-10-27). **No prices on our page.**

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Mian Mir foundation stone**: SGPC affirms (Dec 1588); Wikipedia calls it unsubstantiated/later tradition → present as Sikh tradition recorded by the SGPC, neutral.
- **Construction dates**: 1581 start / 1588 foundation / 1589 completion (Wikipedia) / 1601 or 1604 (SGPC) → use "built under Guru Arjan Dev ji; Adi Granth installed 1604".
- **"Golden" since**: gold leaf 1830 under Ranjit Singh (Wikipedia); some sites say 1802/1809 → print "in the early 19th century under Maharaja Ranjit Singh" or 1830.
- Say **gurdwara**, not "Hindu temple"; don't use HinduTemple schema; don't call Guru Granth Sahib an "idol"; don't print Gurbani text (sacred scripture; we quote nothing).
- **Fake sarai booking sites** — only sgpcsarai.com.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Golden Temple timings?** The complex is open day and night. In winter (18 Dec–17 Feb) the SGPC schedule opens the doors at 3 am and ends with Sukhasan around 10 pm; summer times are earlier — confirm locally.
2. **Is there an entry fee at the Golden Temple?** No. Entry and langar are free for everyone.
3. **What is the dress code?** Cover your head (scarves are available), dress modestly, remove shoes and socks and wash your feet; tobacco and alcohol are not allowed.
4. **Can I take photos inside the Golden Temple?** Videos are prohibited; the SGPC allows phone photos only as a keepsake — never inside the sanctum or of the Guru Granth Sahib; follow the boards.
5. **Who built the Golden Temple?** Guru Arjan Dev ji, the fifth Sikh Guru, around the pool dug under Guru Ram Das ji; the Adi Granth was installed in 1604. Maharaja Ranjit Singh added the gold in the 19th century.
6. **How many people eat at the langar?** Over one lakh free meals are served every day, cooked and served by volunteers.
7. **How do I book a room at the Golden Temple?** Only through the SGPC's official website, sgpcsarai.com; the SGPC warns about fake sites asking for QR-code payments.
8. **How far is Wagah border from the Golden Temple?** About 30 km (45 minutes); the retreat ceremony is in the late afternoon.
