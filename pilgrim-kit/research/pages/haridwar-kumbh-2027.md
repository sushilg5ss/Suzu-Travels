## 2. Facts
- Names: **Haridwar Kumbh 2027** / Ardh Kumbh · हरिद्वार कुंभ 2027 (अर्धकुंभ). The Uttarakhand government brands it **"Kumbh Mela-2027"** (logo/website/anthem, FPJ 12 Oct 2026); the 6-year mela is traditionally the Ardh Kumbh (PTI 14 Apr 2026 calls it "Ardh Kumbh Mela") — say both, neutrally — https://www.freepressjournal.in/uttar-pradesh/haridwar-kumbh-2027-cm-pushkar-singh-dhami-unveils-official-logo-website-and-anthem (as_of 2026-11-07) ; https://theprint.in/india/dates-announced-for-bathing-festivals-during-ardh-kumbh-mela-in-haridwar/2905092/ (as_of 2026-11-07)
- Period: four-month mela, **14 Jan – 20 Apr 2027** — announced by the Haridwar mela administration (Mela Officer Sonika Singh), 14 Apr 2026 — ThePrint/PTI (as_of 2026-11-07)
- **10 bathing days (snan)** — ThePrint/PTI + Deccan Herald https://www.deccanherald.com/india/uttarakhand/ardh-kumbh-mela-know-dates-venue-and-other-significant-details-3968042 (as_of 2026-11-07):

- Legend (our words, as tradition): According to the Puranas, when devas and asuras churned the ocean for amrit, drops of the nectar fell at four places — Haridwar, Prayagraj, Ujjain and Nashik — and the Kumbh rotates between them by the positions of Jupiter and the Sun. At Haridwar the Ganga leaves the hills for the plains, and Har Ki Pauri ("steps of Hari") is believed to hold the footprint of Vishnu; bathing there on the snan days is held to wash away sins. Sadhus of the akharas lead the Amrit Snans before householders bathe. (Retell; no "grants moksha" claims — "it is believed".)
- History: Haridwar hosts a Purna Kumbh every 12 years (last 2021, shortened by Covid — **not verified today, don't print the year without a source**) and an Ardh Kumbh in between. UNESCO inscribed "Kumbh Mela" on its Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2017 (well known; Page Builder may cite unesco.org if reachable).
- Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri: daily at sunrise and sunset — time shifts with the season → "around sunset, confirm locally".
- How to reach (approx., **not verified today** — Page Builder re-check): Haridwar Junction railway station (HW) ~3 km from Har Ki Pauri; Dehradun Jolly Grant airport ~35–40 km; Delhi ~220 km by road (NH-334/Meerut expressway). Expect road diversions and vehicle bans in the mela zone on snan days — "follow the mela police plan published before each snan".
- Nearby: Mansa Devi & Chandi Devi (ropeways) in Haridwar; Rishikesh ~25 km; Kankhal (Daksh Prajapati temple, Sati legend). Char Dham gates are closed in winter — Kumbh does not combine with Char Dham before late Apr 2027 (2027 openings not announced yet).
- Elders/safety: very large crowds on Amrit Snan days; advise elders to bathe on the non-Amrit snan days or weekdays, keep a family meeting point and ID card, use the official lost-and-found (khoya-paya) centres. No fear-mongering.

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- "Kumbh" vs "Ardh Kumbh": state branding = Kumbh 2027; traditional cycle = Ardh Kumbh. Say both neutrally.
- Don't confuse with **Nashik–Trimbakeshwar Simhastha Kumbh** (flag hoisted 31 Oct 2026; Amrit Snans Aug–Sep 2027) or Prayagraj Maha Kumbh 2025.
- Many travel sites list "Shahi Snan" dates from the Sep 2025 Akhara Parishad announcement — use the 14 Apr 2026 mela-administration list above (10 snans, 3 Amrit).
- 15 Apr 2027 has no festival name in sources — don't invent one.
- Fake "Kumbh tent booking" / "VIP snan pass" sites appear for every Kumbh → warn: book only via the official website / Uttarakhand govt (GMVN) links listed there.
- Don't state crowd figures, budget, or mela area (none verified today).

## 4. Questions people ask
1. When is the Haridwar Kumbh 2027? — 14 Jan to 20 Apr 2027 (mela administration, Apr 2026).
2. What are the Amrit Snan dates? — 6 Mar (Mahashivratri), 8 Mar (Phalguna Amavasya), 14 Apr 2027 (Mesh Sankranti).
3. How many bathing days are there? — Ten, from Makar Sankranti (14 Jan) to Chaitra Purnima (20 Apr 2027).
4. Is it Kumbh or Ardh Kumbh? — Traditionally the 6-yearly Ardh Kumbh; the Uttarakhand government calls it Kumbh Mela-2027.
5. Is registration needed? — None announced so far; check the official website before travelling.
6. Where is the main bathing ghat? — Har Ki Pauri (Brahma Kund), plus many ghats along the Ganga in Haridwar.
7. What is the official website? — haridwarkumbh.uk.gov.in (launched 12 Oct 2026; Page Builder to confirm it loads).
8. Best day for elders/families? — A non-Amrit snan day or a weekday in Feb–early Apr; Amrit Snan days are the most crowded.
