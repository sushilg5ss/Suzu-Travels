## 2. Facts
- Names: **Panch Kedar — पंच केदार**: Kedarnath (केदारनाथ), Tungnath (तुंगनाथ), Rudranath (रुद्रनाथ), Madhyamaheshwar / Madmaheshwar (मध्यमहेश्वर / मदमहेश्वर), Kalpeshwar (कल्पेश्वर) — data; Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panch_Kedar (2026-10-24).
- Location: Garhwal Himalaya, **Rudraprayag** (Kedarnath, Tungnath, Madhyamaheshwar) and **Chamoli** (Rudranath, Kalpeshwar) districts, Uttarakhand — data.
- The five (Wikipedia coords/altitudes, 2026-10-24; altitudes vary by source → "about"):
  | # | Temple | Part (tradition) | Coords | Altitude | Start → distance on foot | Winter seat |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | Kedarnath | hump | 30.7352, 79.0669 | ~3,583 m | Gaurikund → ~16 km (IRCTC; see kedarnath.md) | Omkareshwar temple, Ukhimath |
  | 2 | Tungnath | arms | 30.4894, 79.2153 | ~3,680 m | Chopta → ~3.5 km (Chandrashila summit ~1.5 km more) | Makkumath (Markateshwar) |
  | 3 | Rudranath | face | 30.5194, 79.3183 | ~3,559 m | Sagar (near Gopeshwar) → ~18–20 km | Gopinath temple, Gopeshwar |
  | 4 | Madhyamaheshwar | navel/middle | 30.6369, 79.2161 | ~3,490 m | Ransi → ~16 km via Gaundar (data/Indiahikes; Wikipedia says 8–10 km — **disputed** → "about 16 km"; doli halts Gaundar→Ransi confirm the route) | Omkareshwar temple, Ukhimath |
  | 5 | Kalpeshwar | jata (hair) | 30.5770, 79.4229 | ~2,200 m | short walk from road head in **Urgam valley** (Devgram) — sources say 0.3–3 km → "a short walk" | open all year |
- Legend (own words, as tradition — Mahabharata cycle / Kedar Khand of the Skanda Purana; ≈130 words): After the Kurukshetra war, the Pandavas set out to seek Shiva's pardon for killing their own kin. Shiva did not wish to meet them so easily; he left Kashi and hid in the Garhwal hills as a bull. Near **Guptkashi** ("hidden Kashi") the brothers recognised him — Bhima stood astride two hillsides so the herd had to pass below — and as the bull plunged into the earth, Bhima caught its hind part. The hump stayed at Kedarnath, and tradition says the other parts appeared at four more places: the arms at Tungnath, the face at Rudranath, the navel at Madhyamaheshwar and the matted hair at Kalpeshwar. The Pandavas are believed to have built a shrine at each. One tradition adds that the head appeared at **Pashupatinath** in Nepal. — Wikipedia; data legend_summary. Paraphrased.
- Priests (Wikipedia): Kedarnath & Madhyamaheshwar — Jangam (Veerashaiva/Lingayat) priests from Karnataka (Kedarnath Rawal); Tungnath — local Brahmins of Makkumath; Rudranath & Kalpeshwar — Dasnami Gosains. Neutral, optional detail.

- How to reach (OSRM 2026-10-24):

Ukhimath → **Chopta ~26 km** · Ukhimath → **Sonprayag ~42 km** (Kedarnath shuttle to Gaurikund ~5 km) · Chopta → **Gopeshwar ~44 km** (Rudranath via Sagar, ~5 km beyond, UNVERIFIED) · Gopeshwar → **Helang ~55 km** (turn for Urgam/Kalpeshwar) · Ukhimath → Ransi (Madhyamaheshwar) km UNVERIFIED (~20 km commonly quoted).
  - Air: **Jolly Grant, Dehradun**; rail: **Rishikesh** / Haridwar (kedarnath.md).
- On-site options: helicopter **only for Kedarnath** (IRCTC heliyatra.irctc.co.in only); ponies/porters on most treks (rates official at counters; UNVERIFIED for non-Kedarnath) — data.
- Days: all five **~12–16 days** from Rishikesh (data); Wikipedia ~10–12 days, ~95–100 km on foot. Traditional order: Kedarnath → Tungnath → Rudranath → Madhyamaheshwar → Kalpeshwar (Wikipedia); practical road order often Kedarnath → Madhyamaheshwar → Tungnath → Rudranath → Kalpeshwar (editorial, say "many pilgrims").
- Difficulty: Tungnath + Kalpeshwar easy-moderate; Kedarnath moderate-hard (pony/heli); Madhyamaheshwar & Rudranath hard multi-day treks, Rudranath the toughest — data.
- Best time: May–June and Sep–Oct; monsoon (Jul–Aug) landslide risk — data. Winter: worship at Ukhimath, Makkumath, Gopeshwar (a winter "Panch Kedar by road" is possible: Ukhimath, Makkumath, Gopeshwar, Kalpeshwar — editorial idea, label as such).
- Rules: Kedarnath — registration, phone/photo restrictions inside (see kedarnath.md). Others: none official found.
- Accessibility for elders: realistic options are Kedarnath (heli/pony), Tungnath (short but steep 3.5 km; ponies common), Kalpeshwar (short walk) and the winter seats; Rudranath/Madhyamaheshwar only for fit trekkers.

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Highest Shiva temple**: Tungnath (~3,680 m) is widely called the world's highest Shiva temple — Wikipedia's Panch Kedar page doesn't state the superlative; our kedarnath page already says it (yatras-notes trap #12) → keep "widely regarded as the highest Shiva temple in the world".
- **Body parts**: some lists differ (e.g. Madhyamaheshwar navel vs "middle/belly"; head at Pashupatinath per one tradition) → "according to tradition".
- **Distances**: Madhyamaheshwar 8–10 vs 16 km; Rudranath 18–24 km by route; Kalpeshwar 0.3–3 km → "about", name the start point.
- **Spelling**: Madhyamaheshwar = Madmaheshwar = Madhmaheshwar.
- **Rudranath 2026 closing** not verified from a primary report — don't print a date as fact.
- **Kalpeshwar** sometimes replaced by **Vriddha/Kalpnath** naming in blogs — use Kalpeshwar (Urgam).
- Don't confuse Ukhimath's **Omkareshwar** temple with the Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga (MP) — link our omkareshwar page only with that clarification.
- Not the same as **Panch Badri** or **Panch Prayag** (sister circuits).

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are the Panch Kedar?** Five Shiva temples in Garhwal — Kedarnath, Tungnath, Rudranath, Madhyamaheshwar and Kalpeshwar — where, by tradition, parts of Shiva's bull form appeared.
2. **What is the Panch Kedar story?** The Pandavas sought Shiva's pardon after the Kurukshetra war; he hid as a bull, sank into the earth, and his body appeared at five places.
3. **Which Panch Kedar is open all year?** Kalpeshwar, in the Urgam valley.
4. **Which is the highest?** Tungnath, about 3,680 m — widely called the highest Shiva temple in the world.
5. **Which Panch Kedar trek is hardest?** Rudranath (about 18–20 km from Sagar), then Madhyamaheshwar (about 16 km from Ransi).
6. **When do the Panch Kedar temples close in 2026?** Rudranath closed in mid-October, Tungnath 6 Nov, Kedarnath 11 Nov and Madhyamaheshwar 16 Nov 2026; Kalpeshwar stays open.
7. **How many days does Panch Kedar take?** About 12–16 days from Rishikesh for all five.
8. **Where are they worshipped in winter?** Kedarnath and Madhyamaheshwar at Omkareshwar temple, Ukhimath; Tungnath at Makkumath; Rudranath at Gopinath temple, Gopeshwar.
