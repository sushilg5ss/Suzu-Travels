## 2. Facts
- Names: Shri Bagalamukhi Mata Temple, Bankhandi — श्री बगलामुखी माता मंदिर, बनखंडी — data; district https://hpkangra.nic.in/tourist-place/shri-bagalamukhi-mata-temple/ (2026-10-22). Also **Pitambara Maa / Pitambara Devi** (Kangra Temples; Tribune 2026).
- Location: **Bankhandi, near Dehra, Kangra district, Himachal Pradesh**, on the Chandigarh–Dharamshala highway **NH503** — district. Coords **31.968, 76.207** — Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagalamukhi_Temple,_Bankhandi,_HP (2026-10-22; was null in data → added).
- Deity: **Bagalamukhi**, the **eighth of the ten Mahavidyas** (Tantric Shaktism) — district; Tribune 24 Apr 2026. Associated with **stambhana** — the power to halt and silence: enemies, false speech, obstacles; shown holding a club (cudgel) — Kangra Temples https://kangratemples.hp.gov.in/temple_management/bagalamukhi-temple/ (2026-10-22). Sanctum with the goddess, sometimes shown with Lakshmi and Saraswati; shrines of Shiva, Bhairava, Hanuman nearby (district).
- Legend (own words, as tradition — district page; ≈120 words): Long ago a great storm is said to have threatened to destroy the world. At **Vishnu's** prayer the goddess rose from a **lake of turmeric**, dressed in yellow, and stilled it — which is why everything here is yellow. Local tradition says the **Pandavas** raised this temple in a single night during their exile, and that **Rama**, guided by **Hanuman**, worshipped Bagalamukhi here before the war with Ravana and received the **Brahmastra**; a havan kund at the temple is pointed out as the place of Rama's havan (Wikipedia). Devotees come for her protection in disputes and for night havans.
- History (dated):
  - Tradition: Pandavas (Dwapar Yuga) — legend only.
  - **Early 19th century** — the present shrine is linked to the **former rulers of Guler**, devotees of the goddess, who are believed to have built it at Bankhandi after losing **Kotla fort** to **Maharaja Ranjit Singh** — The Tribune 24 Apr 2026 https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/himachal/tide-of-faith-sweeps-baglamukhi-shrine-on-jayanti-as-devotees-throng-temple/ → print "believed to have been built in the early 1800s by the rulers of Guler".
  - Called a **Siddha Peeth**; one of **three major Bagalamukhi shrines** in India with **Datia** and **Nalkheda** (both Madhya Pradesh) — district; Wikipedia.
  - Management: Kangra Temples portal lists it (kangratemples.hp.gov.in) — trust/administrator details not on the page → UNVERIFIED; don't name an authority. Wikipedia lists maabaglamukhiofficial.org — not verified as an official trust site → **don't link**.

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Three Baglamukhis** — Bankhandi (Kangra, HP) ≠ **Pitambara Peeth, Datia** ≠ **Nalkheda, Agar Malwa (MP)**. Add a short "Not the Datia or Nalkheda temple" note. There is also a smaller Baglamukhi temple near **Nari/Amb, Una** (keyword "baglamukhi mandir nari una amb himachal pradesh" 50) → don't confuse.
- **Kotla** — Kangra Temples page says the temple is "at the entrance of Kotla Fort"; the Tribune says Guler rulers built Bankhandi *after losing* Kotla fort; the district page places it at Bankhandi on NH503 → print Bankhandi only; mention Kotla only in the history line.
- **Shakti Peeth?** — Kangra Temples calls it "a Sakti Peetha" loosely, but it is **not** in the classical 51/52 lists (data: classical_shakti_peeth false) → "a Mahavidya Siddha Peeth, not one of the classical 51 Shakti Peeths".
- **Nau Devi** — not one of the traditional nine; many commercial 9-Devi packages add it (shaktipeeth-notes FACT TRAP / FAQ 5).
- **"Built by the Pandavas"** — legend; history = early 1800s Guler rulers (belief per Tribune).
- **Distances** — district's 40 km (airport, station) vs OSRM 23–34 km → always "about".
- **Tantric/enemy wording** — keep respectful: "devotees seek protection and success in disputes"; no "guaranteed court victory", no black-magic wording, no havan prices, no named pandits.
- Spelling: Baglamukhi / Bagalamukhi / Bagla Mukhi — all one goddess; use "Baglamukhi" (higher volume) + "Bagalamukhi" once.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **Where is Baglamukhi temple in Himachal?** At Bankhandi near Dehra in Kangra district, on the Chandigarh–Dharamshala highway (NH503).
2. **What are Baglamukhi temple Kangra timings?** Generally about 5 am to 9:30 pm per the Kangra district site; aarti times aren't published — confirm locally.
3. **Why is everything yellow at Baglamukhi temple?** Yellow (pitambara) is the goddess's colour — devotees wear yellow and offer yellow flowers, turmeric and besan laddoos.
4. **Why do people do havan at Baglamukhi?** Devotees perform havans, often at night, seeking protection and success in disputes and court cases — a matter of faith.
5. **Is Baglamukhi Kangra a Shakti Peeth?** It is revered as a Siddha Peeth of one of the ten Mahavidyas, but it is not in the classical 51 Shakti Peeth lists.
6. **How far is Baglamukhi from Jwala Ji?** About 24 km by road; Kangra town and Brajeshwari Devi are about 23 km, Chintpurni about 36 km.
7. **Which are the three main Baglamukhi temples?** Bankhandi (Kangra, HP), Pitambara Peeth (Datia, MP) and Nalkheda (MP).
8. **When is Baglamukhi Jayanti?** On Vaishakh Shukla Ashtami (April–May); in 2026 it fell in late April. Check the 2027 date with a panchang.
