## 2. Facts
- Names: **Belur Math** · बेलूड़ मठ · headquarters of the **Ramakrishna Math and Ramakrishna Mission** — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belur_Math (as_of 2026-11-07)
- Location: west bank of the Hooghly (Ganga), Belur, Howrah district, West Bengal, PIN 711202; ~16 ha (40 acres) campus — official https://belurmath.org/our-location/ + Wikipedia (as_of 2026-11-07)
- Founder & dates: founded by **Swami Vivekananda**; land bought **4 Mar 1897** (Wikipedia; infobox "Established January 1897"); Sri Ramakrishna's relics installed **9 Dec 1898** — Wikipedia (as_of 2026-11-07)
- **Sri Ramakrishna Temple**: foundation stone 13 Mar 1929 (Swami Shivananda); consecrated **14 Jan 1938** (Makar Sankranti); designed by **Swami Vijnanananda** (a direct disciple, former civil engineer) on Vivekananda's ideas; marble statue of Sri Ramakrishna by Gopeswar Pal; ~34 m tall, mainly Chunar stone — Wikipedia (as_of 2026-11-07)
- Architecture: blends Hindu, Buddhist, Christian (and, as often described, Islamic) motifs — seen from different sides it suggests a temple, a mosque and a church; cross-shaped ground plan; gateway recalls Sanchi/Ajanta; domes recall Bengal thatched huts. Phrase as "is said to resemble", not as fact of intent — Wikipedia (as_of 2026-11-07)
- Other shrines: **Holy Mother (Sri Sarada Devi) temple** consecrated 21 Jul 1920; **Swami Vivekananda temple** consecrated 28 Jan 1924 at the spot of his cremation (1902), alabaster OM upstairs; **Swami Brahmananda temple** dedicated 7 Feb 1924 — Wikipedia (as_of 2026-11-07)
- **Timings (official)** — https://belurmath.org/our-location/ (as_of 2026-11-07): Morning **6:30–11:30 am**; Evening **Apr–Sep 4:00–9:00 pm**, **Oct–Mar 3:30–8:30 pm**. Temples close at 11:30 am. After the evening arati bell, devotees may sit only in the Sri Ramakrishna temple. (A May 2026 blog says 6:00 am in summer — official wins.)
- **Evening arati (official)** — https://belurmath.org/faq/sandhya-arati-timings/ (as_of 2026-11-07): time moves with sunset through the year — about **5:20–5:40 pm in Nov–Jan** and about **6:50 pm in late June**; the official page has a date-by-date table (first bell ~10 min before). Print "around sunset — see the official arati table".
- **Museum (Ramakrishna Sangraha Mandira)** — official: 8:30–11:30 am; evening Apr–Sep 4:00–6:00 pm, Oct–Mar 3:30–5:30 pm; **closed Mondays** and holidays (as_of 2026-11-07). Small ticket per a blog — **no amounts on our page**.
- Entry to the campus/temples: free (kamarpukurjayrambati.com guide, May 2026) — official page silent → "entry is free (confirm locally)".
- Rules (secondary — kamarpukurjayrambati.com, 20 May 2026; official silent): no photography/video inside the temples; phones silent/off (blog FAQ says no phone use in temples & museum); silence in the main hall; modest dress advised; large bags/cameras not allowed in the museum (cloakroom). - Festivals: Durga Puja with **Kumari Puja** (tradition begun by Vivekananda, 1901) is the biggest draw; birth anniversaries (tithi) of Sri Ramakrishna, Sri Sarada Devi and Swami Vivekananda; also Kali Puja, Shivaratri, Janmashtami and Christmas Eve are observed — Wikipedia (as_of 2026-11-07). ## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Belur Math (West Bengal) ≠ Belur (Karnataka)** Chennakesava temple — our post 9780 is the Karnataka one. One disambiguation line.
- Belur Math is a monastery/temple complex of the Ramakrishna Order, **not a Shakti Peeth or Jyotirlinga**; open to people of all faiths (Wikipedia) — faith-neutral tone, no miracle claims, no portrait-likeness art.
- Timings: many sites say 6:00 am or a fixed "7 pm arati" — use official (6:30 am; arati with sunset).
- "Islamic motifs" — Wikipedia's lead lists Hindu/Buddhist/Christian; the mosque resemblance is widely cited. Phrase softly.
- Entry/bhog/guest-house fees appear on blogs (some old) — **no ₹ on our page**.
- The new Sri Ramakrishna temple consecrated 16 Jul 2026 noted in the Dakshineswar pack is at a Ramakrishna Math branch (per Belur Math media) — **not** a change at Belur Math itself; don't print without re-checking.

## 4. Questions people ask
1. What are Belur Math timings? — 6:30–11:30 am and 3:30–8:30 pm (Oct–Mar) / 4–9 pm (Apr–Sep) (official).
2. What time is the evening arati? — Around sunset; about 5:20–5:40 pm in winter, near 6:50 pm in June (official table).
3. Is there an entry fee? — Campus and temples are free; the museum has a small ticket (confirm locally).
4. Is photography allowed? — Not inside the temples; keep phones silent.
5. Which district is Belur Math in? — Howrah, West Bengal, on the west bank of the Hooghly.
6. How to go from Dakshineswar to Belur Math? — Ferry across the river (~20 min) or by road over Bally/Vivekananda Setu.
7. Who designed the main temple? — Swami Vijnanananda, following Swami Vivekananda's ideas; consecrated 14 Jan 1938.
8. Is the museum open on Monday? — No, closed Mondays.
