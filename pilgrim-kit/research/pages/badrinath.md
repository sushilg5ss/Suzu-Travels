## 2. Facts
- Names: Shri Badrinath Dham / श्री बद्रीनाथ धाम; Badri Vishal / बद्री विशाल; Badrinarayan / बद्रीनारायण — data + Wikipedia (2026-10-14).
- Status: one of the four **Char Dham** of Uttarakhand (the last, eastmost dham of the circuit) and also one of the four all-India **Char Dham** (with Dwarka, Puri, Rameswaram) — data; one of the **108 Divya Desams** (Naalayira Divya Prabandham) — https://incredibleindia.gov.in/en/uttarakhand/badrinath/badrinath-temple + https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badrinath_Temple (2026-10-14). First of the **Panch Badri** (Vishal Badri).
- Location: Badrinath town, **Chamoli district**, Uttarakhand, on the **Alaknanda** river between the Nar and Narayan ranges; 30.7447, 79.4911 (30°44′41″N 79°29′28″E) — Wikipedia (2026-10-14).
- Altitude: **3,133 m** (Incredible India and Wikipedia body text) / 3,100 m (Wikipedia infobox) → print "about 3,100 m (3,133 m per Incredible India)" — https://incredibleindia.gov.in/en/uttarakhand/badrinath/badrinath-temple (2026-10-14).
- Managing body: **Shri Badarinath-Kedarnath Temple Committee (BKTC)** — https://badrinath-kedarnath.gov.in/ (data 2026-10-08).
- Deity: Vishnu as Badrinarayan, in seated meditation; the image is a **black Shaligram stone, about 1 ft tall** (Wikipedia; an 1828 account said ~3 ft — don't print size, say "small black stone image"). Sanctum also holds Kuber, Narada, Uddhava, Nar–Narayan (Incredible India, 2026-10-14). Lakshmi has her own shrine in the complex (Wikipedia infobox "Badridevi").
- Priest: the **Rawal** is by custom a **Nambudiri Brahmin from Kerala** — said to date from 1776, when the Garhwal king invited Nambudiris (Wikipedia, 2026-10-14). Nice "north–south unity" angle; keep factual.
- Legend (own words, as tradition — Vishnu Purana / Bhagavata / Skanda Purana are named as references by Wikipedia; data legend_summary): Vishnu once sat in deep meditation in the bitter Himalayan cold. To shield him from snow and sun, Lakshmi stood over him in the form of a **badri (jujube) tree**; moved by her devotion, Vishnu named the place Badrikashram and himself Badri-Nath, "Lord of the Badri". Another popular telling says this valley was once Shiva and Parvati's home, until Vishnu came as a crying infant, was sheltered by Parvati, and then claimed the spot for his meditation, so Shiva moved on to Kedarnath. Tradition also places the penance of the sages **Nar and Narayan** here, which is why the two ranges carry their names. (≈125 words) — data (2026-10-08) + Incredible India (2026-10-14).
- History (dated):
  - **Tradition / 8th–9th century CE** — Adi Shankaracharya is credited with re-establishing Badrinath (Wikipedia says 9th century, with Parmar ruler Kanak Pal; popular tradition says 8th) → print "Adi Shankaracharya is credited with reviving it", no century, or "around the 8th–9th century (traditions differ)".
  - **16th century** — a Garhwal king moved the image to the present temple; **17th century** — Garhwal kings expanded it — Wikipedia (2026-10-14).
  - **1776** — Nambudiri Rawal tradition begins (Wikipedia).
  - **1803** — Garhwal earthquake badly damaged the temple; rebuilt largely with help from the King of Jaipur — Wikipedia (2026-10-14).
  - **23 Jan 2026** (Basant Panchami) — 2026 opening announced at Narendranagar royal court — https://thedailyguardian.com/news/badrinath-dham-portals-to-reopen-on-april-23-691250/ (data).
  - **23 Apr 2026, 6:15 am** — doors opened (NOT 24 Apr — trap #2); first puja in the PM's name, CM Dhami present — data + https://www.patrika.com/dehradun-2/badrinath-kedarnat-special-puja-ban-20523061 (2026-10-14). (Aaj Tak article mentions both 22 and 23 Apr — ignore its "22".)
  - **Apr 2026** — BKTC chairman Hemant Dwivedi **suspended special (VIP) pujas** for the rush so everyone gets general darshan (Patrika, 23 Apr 2026). Whether it was later lifted — UNVERIFIED → don't print as current.
  - **2026 season** — Badrinath **18.10 lakh+** pilgrims by 29 Sep, a season record — https://ddindia.co.in/2026/09/char-dham-yatra-draws-record-pilgrims-badrinath-and-gangotri-set-new-highs/ (data).
- Darshan & aarti: Incredible India (Govt of India) gives **4:30 am – 1:00 pm and 4:30 pm – 9:00 pm** (2026-10-14) — print with source and "may change in peak season / on festival days — confirm locally". BKTC does not publish a daily schedule we could read. Morning **Mahabhishek** and evening **Geet Govind / Shayan aarti** are the well-known rituals; clock times UNVERIFIED → "confirm locally". Pujas booked **only on badrinath-kedarnath.gov.in** (data). General darshan free; no fees printed.
- Dates:
  - Opened 2026: **23 Apr, 6:15 am**.
  - Closing 2026: **not announced yet** (14 Oct search check). Fixed on **Vijayadashami, 20 Oct 2026** at Badrinath; Badrinath usually closes 2–3 weeks after Bhai Dooj (2025: **25 Nov**; many blogs recycle it — trap #1). Link post 12021.
  - **2027 opening: not announced yet** — fixed on **Basant Panchami (11 Feb 2027)** at Narendranagar (yatras-notes calendar); usually late Apr / early May.
  - Winter: idols of **Uddhav and Kuber** go to **Yogdhyan Badri, Pandukeshwar**; Adi Shankaracharya's gaddi to **Narsingh temple, Jyotirmath (Joshimath)** — worship continues there (data winter-seats record, 2026-10-08; Wikipedia says the image is "notionally" moved to Narasimha temple).
  - Festivals: **Mata Murti ka Mela** (Mana, around Vamana Dwadashi in Bhadrapad — 2027 date not checked) · **Badri-Kedar festival** (June, 8 days — Wikipedia; 2027 not announced) · Kapat opening/closing.
- Registration: Char Dham registration is **mandatory and free** — registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in, Tourist Care Uttarakhand app, helpline **1364** (data; trap #11).
- How to reach:
  - **Road (drive-up, no trek):** Rishikesh → Devprayag → Rudraprayag → Karnaprayag → Nandaprayag → Jyotirmath → Badrinath, **about 298 km** from Rishikesh — Wikipedia (2026-10-14). Jyotirmath → Badrinath ~45 km (UNVERIFIED, editorial — say "about 45 km").
  - **Air:** Jolly Grant, Dehradun — Incredible India (2026-10-14); road distance ~315 km (approx., UNVERIFIED → "about").
  - **Rail:** Rishikesh (Yog Nagari Rishikesh) — Incredible India (2026-10-14).
  - Helicopter: **no IRCTC HeliYatra route for Badrinath** (HeliYatra covers Kedarnath/Hemkund — trap #4); private charters from Dehradun exist — operators/prices UNVERIFIED → don't name, warn about fakes.
  - Road risk: Badrinath NH (NH-7) is landslide-prone in monsoon (Jyotirmath/Animath slides, e.g. Amar Ujala 8 Oct 2025 video) → "keep a buffer day; check road status".
- On-site: **Tapt Kund** hot spring below the temple, ~55 °C, traditional bath before darshan — Incredible India + Wikipedia (2026-10-14). Brahma Kapal (pind-daan ghat), Narad Kund — editorial.
- Rules / health: health advisory for over-50s / heart, lung, BP conditions (data health_rules); altitude ~3,100 m — cold even in June; phones/photography inside the sanctum UNVERIFIED → "follow temple staff instructions". Dress: modest, warm.
- Best time: **May–June and Sep–Oct**; Jul–Aug landslide risk (temple stays open).
- Nearby (Incredible India 2026-10-14 unless noted): **Mana village** (~3 km, UNVERIFIED distance; "first Indian village" signboard — avoid "last village" vs "first village" debate, say "the border village of Mana") · **Vyas Gufa, Ganesh Gufa, Bheem Pul** (at Mana) · **Mata Murti temple** · **Brahma Kapal** · **Charan Paduka** · **Vasudhara falls** (trek from Mana, approx 5–6 km UNVERIFIED) · **Valley of Flowers / Hemkund** (via Govindghat) · **Auli** · **Panch Badri**: Yogdhyan Badri (Pandukeshwar), Bhavishya Badri (Subain, 17 km from Jyotirmath), Vridh Badri (Animath, 7 km from Jyotirmath), Adi Badri (17 km from Karnaprayag) — Wikipedia (2026-10-14).
- Accessibility for elders: **the easiest of the four dhams** — motorable to the town; short walk across the Alaknanda bridge to the temple; queues can be long in May–June. Altitude care still needed.

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Opening 23 Apr 2026, not 24 Apr** (trap #2); Aaj Tak's "22 Apr" line is a typo.
- **Closing date**: nothing official until **20 Oct 2026**; "25 Nov" is 2025's date (trap #1). Badrinath closes later than Kedarnath — don't say "all four close on Bhai Dooj".
- Century of Adi Shankara's revival differs (8th vs 9th) — say "credited with", no century.
- Altitude: 3,100 vs 3,133 m (also 3,300 m on some blogs) — give "about 3,100 m".
- "Badrinath is a Jyotirlinga" — **no**, it is a Vishnu (Divya Desam) shrine; Kedarnath is the Jyotirlinga.
- Panch Badri lists differ (some add/replace Ardh Badri / Dhyan Badri) — "one common list".
- Winter seat: Pandukeshwar (Yogdhyan Badri) for Uddhav/Kuber and Jyotirmath for Shankaracharya's gaddi — many blogs say only "Joshimath".
- "Mana = last village of India" — the state now signs it as the **first** village; say "border village".
- No IRCTC helicopter for Badrinath; any "Badrinath heli seat" on WhatsApp is suspect.
- Shiva–Parvati / infant Vishnu story is a popular tale, not in every Purana — "a popular telling".

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **When will Badrinath close in 2026?** Not announced yet; the date is fixed on Vijayadashami, 20 Oct 2026 — usually in the second half of November. See our closing-dates post.
2. **When does Badrinath open in 2027?** Not announced yet; fixed on Basant Panchami (11 Feb 2027) at Narendranagar; usually late April or early May.
3. **What is the altitude of Badrinath?** About 3,100 m (3,133 m per Incredible India).
4. **What are Badrinath darshan timings?** Per Incredible India, about 4:30 am–1 pm and 4:30–9 pm; may change on festival days — confirm locally.
5. **Is there a trek to Badrinath?** No — the road goes to the town; it's a short walk to the temple.
6. **How to reach Badrinath by road?** From Rishikesh via Devprayag, Rudraprayag, Karnaprayag and Jyotirmath, about 298 km; nearest airport Dehradun, railhead Rishikesh.
7. **Is registration needed?** Yes, Char Dham registration is compulsory and free (official portal, app, helpline 1364).
8. **Where is Badrinath worshipped in winter?** At Yogdhyan Badri, Pandukeshwar, and Narsingh temple, Jyotirmath.
