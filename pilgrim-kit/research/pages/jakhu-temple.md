## 2. Facts
- Names: Jakhu (Jakhoo) Temple, Shri Hanuman Mandir Jakhoo — जाखू मंदिर (common Hindi spelling; official form UNVERIFIED) — Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakhu_Temple (2026-10-23).
- Location: top of **Jakhu Hill, the highest peak of Shimla**, Shimla district, Himachal Pradesh; ~**2.5 km east of the Ridge**; **31.1012, 77.1818**; altitude **~2,455 m** (Wikipedia text; infobox says 2,467 m → print "about 2,450 m") — Wikipedia (2026-10-23).
- Deity: **Lord Hanuman**. Temple in Himachali **kath-kuni** style (Wikipedia); founder in tradition: **Yaksha / Yakku Rishi** (Wikipedia infobox; legend below).
- Legend (own words, as tradition — Ramayana-linked local telling; ≈110 words): When Lakshmana lay wounded in the battle of Lanka, Hanuman flew north to fetch the Sanjeevani herb. Local tradition says he paused on this peak on the way, where the sage **Yakku (Yaksha) Rishi** was meditating, to ask the way or rest — and the hilltop is said to have flattened under his weight. Hanuman promised to return but, pressed for time, did not; the sage is believed to have raised the shrine where he had stood, and a set of **footprints** at the temple is shown as Hanuman's. "Jakhu" is said to come from the sage's name. Tellings differ (outward vs return flight) — present as "local tradition". — Wikipedia (name/founder); yatradham blog https://blog.yatradham.org/jakhu-temple-in-shimla/ (paraphrased; legend detail is popular tradition, not scripture — say so).

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **Where is Jakhu temple?** On top of Jakhu Hill, Shimla's highest point (about 2,450 m), roughly 2.5 km uphill from the Ridge.
2. **How tall is the Hanuman statue at Jakhu?** 108 feet; it was unveiled in November 2010 and is visible from much of Shimla.
3. **What are Jakhu temple timings?** Open every day; official hours and aarti times aren't published — confirm locally, and go in daylight.
4. **How do I go up to Jakhu temple?** Walk about 2.5 km uphill from the Ridge, take a taxi to the top area, or use the ropeway when it is running.
5. **Are the monkeys at Jakhu dangerous?** They are bold and grab glasses, phones and food — keep them out of sight and don't feed them.
6. **What is the story of Jakhu temple?** Local tradition says Hanuman paused here, where Yakku Rishi was meditating, while fetching the Sanjeevani herb; footprints at the temple are shown as his.
7. **When is Jakhu temple most crowded?** On Dussehra, Hanuman Jayanti, Tuesdays and Saturdays, and in the May–June holiday season.
8. **How far is Jakhu temple from Shimla railway station?** A short taxi ride across town; Kalka (broad gauge) is about 93 km by road and Chandigarh airport about 127 km.
