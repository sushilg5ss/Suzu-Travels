- Legend (own words, as tradition — Devi Bhagavata / Shakta tradition, as told by the trust): Sati, Daksha's daughter, went uninvited to her father's yagna, heard Shiva insulted and gave up her body in the sacred fire. Shiva wandered the worlds carrying her in grief; to calm his dance Vishnu's Sudarshan Chakra divided the body, and tradition says her eyes fell on this Shivalik hilltop — hence "Naina" (eyes). Ages later the spot was revealed again: a cowherd named Naina saw one of his cows pour its milk over a stone each day; in a dream the Goddess told the ruler the stone was her pindi, and he raised a temple and named it after the herdsman. A second strand says Durga killed Mahishasura here after the gods gave her their weapons — so the seat is also called Mahish Peeth. (≈125 words) — srinainadevi.com/mythology-legends; data/shaktipeeth.json (2026-10-09)

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Naina Devi (Bilaspur, HP) ≠ Naina Devi (Nainital, Uttarakhand).** Both claim the eyes. The trust even claims Nainital is named after this temple — present only as the trust's view.
- Timings: many sites copy 05:00–21:00 or "4 am – 10 am" (utsav.gov.in typo). Official: 04:00–22:00, Navratri 02:00–24:00.
- Founder story differs (Raja Bir Chand vs Prince Virchand of Chanderi, 8th c.; Gujjar boy vs Ahir cowherd) → tell it without caste labels, as tradition.
- "One of 51" vs "52" — counts differ by text (shaktipeeth-notes §2).
- Anandpur Sahib–Naina Devi ropeway is NOT running (proposal since ~2012).
- Altitude quoted as 1,177 m and 1,219 m on the same official site.
- nainadevi.com is not the official trust site (official: srinainadevi.com).
- Sharad Navratri 2026 is 11 Oct, not 22 Sep.
- 2008 Shravan stampede: don't dramatise; at most a neutral crowd-safety tip.

## 4. Questions people ask (with short answers)
1. **What are Naina Devi temple timings?** 4 am – 10 pm on normal days; 2 am – midnight in Navratri (temple trust). Aarti times: confirm locally.
2. **Where is Naina Devi temple?** On a Shivalik hilltop in Bilaspur district, Himachal, near the Punjab border, above Gobind Sagar — about 20 km from Anandpur Sahib and ~108 km from Chandigarh.
3. **Which body part of Sati fell at Naina Devi?** Her eyes, by tradition (Wikipedia: right eye) — hence the name.
4. **Is there a ropeway / cable car to Naina Devi?** Yes, a cable car runs from the base to the hilltop; the long Anandpur Sahib ropeway is still only a proposal.
5. **What is the nearest railway station?** Anandpur Sahib (~20 km); Nangal and Kiratpur Sahib are other options. Nearest airport: Chandigarh (~108 km).
6. **Where to stay at Naina Devi? (naina devi dharamshala)** Trust rooms (paid and free) bookable at online.srinainadevi.com, an HP Govt rest house near the bus stand, and simple private guest houses; no starred hotels.
7. **When is Naina Devi most crowded / Navratri 2026 dates?** Chaitra & Sharad Navratri and the Shravan Ashtami mela. Sharad Navratri 2026: 11–19 Oct (Dussehra 20 Oct).
8. **Is Naina Devi Bilaspur the same as Naina Devi Nainital?** No — two different temples in two states.
(Also: Naina Devi to Chintpurni ~112 km; Naina Devi helpline 1800 180 80 69.)
