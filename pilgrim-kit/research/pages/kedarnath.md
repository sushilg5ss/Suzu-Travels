## 2. Facts
- Names: Shri Kedarnath Dham / श्री केदारनाथ धाम; Kedarnath Jyotirlinga / केदारनाथ ज्योतिर्लिंग — data (2026-10-08)
- Place in lists: 5th in the Shiva Purana (Koti Rudra Samhita) list; 11th in the popular smaranam stotra ("हिमालये तु केदारं") — say which list (jyotirlinga-notes trap #3).
- Also one of the **Panch Kedar** (the hump) and one of the Uttarakhand **Char Dham**.
- Location: Kedarnath town, **Rudraprayag district**, Uttarakhand, at the head of the Mandakini valley; 30.7352, 79.0669; altitude **~3,583 m** (BKTC says 3,584 m) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kedarnath_Temple (2026-10-08)
- Managing body: **Shri Badarinath-Kedarnath Temple Committee (BKTC)** — https://www.badrinath-kedarnath.gov.in (2026-10-08)
- Deity: Shiva as Kedarnath; the linga is a natural triangular/humped rock (describe as "a rock form, not a carved idol" — tradition; shape wording UNVERIFIED on BKTC today → "believed to be").
- Legend (own words, as tradition — Mahabharata cycle & Shiva Purana): After the Kurukshetra war the Pandavas went looking for Shiva to be freed from the sin of killing their own kin. Shiva did not want to be found so easily and hid in the Garhwal hills as a bull among a herd. Bhima planted his feet on two hillsides so the cattle had to pass beneath him; one bull would not, and the brothers knew it was the Lord. As it sank into the ground, Bhima caught its hump, which remained at Kedarnath; tradition places the other parts at the other four Panch Kedar shrines. The Shiva Purana adds that Shiva chose to dwell here as a Jyotirlinga at the prayer of the sages Nara and Narayana. (≈120 words) — data legend_summary (2026-10-08)
- History (dated):
  - **8th century CE (tradition)** — Adi Shankaracharya is believed to have revived the temple; BKTC credits the present structure to him, beside an earlier Pandava-era shrine — https://www.badrinath-kedarnath.gov.in/AboutUs/shri-kedarnath.aspx (2026-10-08)
  - **16–17 Jun 2013** — floods devastated Kedarnath; the temple survived as a large boulder behind it diverted debris — now worshipped as **Bhim Shila** — Wikipedia (2026-10-08)
  - **5 Nov 2021** — 12-ft statue of Adi Shankaracharya unveiled at his rebuilt samadhi behind the temple — https://www.thenewsminute.com/news/pm-modi-unveils-adi-shankaracharya-statue-rebuilt-samadhi-kedarnath-157271
  - **15 Feb 2026** (Mahashivratri) — opening date announced at Omkareshwar Temple, Ukhimath — https://www.etvbharat.com/en/state/mahashivratri-2026-kedarnath-temple-opening-announced-enn26021501713

  - **2026 season** — Kedarnath 15.57 lakh pilgrims by late Sep (DD India, 29 Sep 2026) https://ddindia.co.in/2026/09/char-dham-yatra-draws-record-pilgrims-badrinath-and-gangotri-set-new-highs/ · Char Dham total "56 lakh+, passing 2023" = blog headline (hindutone) — UNVERIFIED, don't print.

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- "Kedarnath opens on Akshaya Tritiya" — no: Gangotri/Yamunotri do; Kedarnath's date is fixed on Mahashivratri (22 Apr 2026).
- "Kedarnath 2026 closing date confirmed" — not until BKTC's Vijayadashami (20 Oct) announcement; 2025's 23 Oct is recycled on many blogs.
- "5th" vs "11th" Jyotirlinga — depends on the list (Shiva Purana vs smaranam stotra).
- "Highest Shiva temple" — that is Tungnath; Kedarnath is the highest Jyotirlinga.
- "Trek is 14 km" — old route; now ~16 km.
- "Temple closed in monsoon" — no; only temporary yatra halts.
- Helicopter: only IRCTC HeliYatra; no agent/WhatsApp "confirmed seats"; fares change each season — official notice only.
- Panch Kedar body-part traditions vary by source (e.g. Rudranath face, Tungnath arms, Madhyamaheshwar navel, Kalpeshwar hair) — present as tradition, "according to tradition"; not re-verified today.
- Don't confuse Kedarnath's winter seat **Omkareshwar (Ukhimath)** with Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga (MP).
- Bhim Shila: say "is believed to have shielded" — the physical fact (boulder diverted debris) is reported; the divine reading is faith.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **When will Kedarnath close in 2026?** Not announced yet; by custom on Bhai Dooj (around 10–11 Nov), with the muhurat announced on Vijayadashami, 20 Oct 2026 — see our closing-dates post.
2. **When will Kedarnath open in 2027?** Not announced yet; the date is fixed on Mahashivratri (6 Mar 2027) at Ukhimath; usually late April or early May.
3. **How do I book a Kedarnath helicopter?** Register for the Char Dham yatra first, then book only on heliyatra.irctc.co.in (Guptkashi, Phata, Sersi). Ignore WhatsApp/agent "confirmed tickets".
4. **How long is the Kedarnath trek?** About 16 km one way from Gaurikund, 6–8 hours up; pony, palki and pithoo are available.
5. **Is registration compulsory?** Yes, free, on registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in / Tourist Care Uttarakhand app / helpline 1364.
6. **How to reach Kedarnath from Haridwar/Delhi?** Road to Sonprayag via Rishikesh–Rudraprayag–Guptkashi, shuttle to Gaurikund, then trek or fly from Guptkashi/Phata/Sersi. Nearest airport Dehradun, railhead Rishikesh.
7. **What are the darshan timings?** BKTC does not publish daily hours — doors open early morning and close at night; confirm locally. Pujas are booked on BKTC's site.
8. **Where is Kedarnath worshipped in winter?** At Omkareshwar Temple, Ukhimath, from the closing until the spring reopening.
