## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What is the dress code at Padmanabhaswamy temple?** Men wear a mundu/dhoti without a shirt; women wear a saree or a set-mundu/long skirt with blouse. Mundus can be rented outside.
2. **Can non-Hindus enter?** Entry inside is restricted to Hindus; check the temple's rules at spst.in.
3. **What are the darshan timings?** Darshan is in short slots from about 3:30 am to about noon and again in the evening till about 7:20 pm; the temple is closed in the afternoon. Confirm the day's slots.
4. **Why is the idol seen through three doors?** The reclining Vishnu is about 18 feet long, so the face, the navel with Brahma's lotus and the feet are seen through three separate doors.
5. **Is it a Divya Desam?** Yes — one of the 108 Divya Desams of Vishnu.
6. **What is Lakshadeepam?** One lakh lamps lit once every six years at the end of the 56-day Murajapam; the last was on 14 Jan 2026.
7. **Can I take a phone or camera?** Photography is not allowed inside; leave phones and cameras at the cloakroom outside.
8. **How far is it from the railway station and airport?** About 2 km from Thiruvananthapuram Central and about 5–6 km from Trivandrum airport.
