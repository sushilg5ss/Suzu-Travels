## 2. Facts
- Names: **Shri Saibaba Samadhi Mandir** (also "Shree Buti Wada" in Wikipedia's infobox) — Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shri_Sai_Baba_Temple,_Shirdi (as_of 2026-11-05). Devanagari: **श्री साईबाबा समाधि मंदिर, शिर्डी**; data name_hi **शिर्डी साईं बाबा** (data/yatras.json). Not found verbatim on a fetched page — use the data name for `deva`.
- Location: **Shirdi, Rahata taluka, Ahilyanagar district (formerly Ahmednagar), Maharashtra**; Shirdi also called Sainagar — Wikipedia Shirdi https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirdi (district shown as Ahmednagar there; temple article says Ahilyanagar) (as_of 2026-11-05). On page: "Ahilyanagar (Ahmednagar) district".
- Trust: **Shri Saibaba Sansthan Trust (SSST), Shirdi**, public charitable trust under the Bombay Public Trusts Act 1950 — Wikipedia temple article (as_of 2026-11-05). Do not name the CEO.
- Who: **Sai Baba of Shirdi**, saint revered by Hindus and Muslims; taught **shraddha** (faith) and **saburi** (patience); kept the **dhuni** (sacred fire) whose ash **udi** devotees receive — data/yatras.json (as_of 2026-10-08) + Wikipedia Sai Baba of Shirdi.
- Life (own words, ≈120 words, as tradition/biography): Sai Baba's birth and origin are unknown (Wikipedia: c. 1838 — print "his origins are unknown"). Tradition says he first appeared in Shirdi as a young man of about sixteen, sitting in meditation under a neem tree (now **Gurusthan**), left for a while and returned to stay around **1858**. He lived in an old mosque he named **Dwarkamai**, kept its dhuni burning and gave its ash as udi; on alternate nights he slept at the **Chavadi**. He cared for the garden **Lendi Baug**. He took **Mahasamadhi on 15 October 1918, Vijayadashami**, and was laid to rest in **Buti Wada**, a stone building that Nagpur devotee **Gopalrao Buti** had been building — today's Samadhi Mandir — Wikipedia Sai Baba of Shirdi + temple article; yatradham (built 1917–18) (as_of 2026-11-05). No miracle claims.
- History: Samadhi Mandir established **1918** (Wikipedia); marble statue of Baba by sculptor **Balaji Vasant Talim** (Wikipedia; yatradham says 1954 — print year only as "1954, by temple accounts" or omit); gold-covered interior and spire (Wikipedia). Shirdi airport opened **1 Oct 2017**; Sainagar Shirdi station opened **2009** (Wikipedia Shirdi).
- Campus: **Samadhi Mandir · Dwarkamai** (dhuni still burns) **· Chavadi · Gurusthan** (neem tree) **· Lendi Baug** (Nanda Deep lamp) · Khandoba temple · Sai museum — Wikipedia temple article; yatradham (as_of 2026-11-05).

## 4. Questions people ask
1. **What are Shirdi Sai Baba darshan timings?** The temple opens about 4:45 am and closes about 11 pm; general darshan starts after the morning rituals, around 6:25 am.
2. **What are the Shirdi aarti timings?** Four aartis: Kakad at dawn (~5:15 am), Madhyan at noon, Dhoop at sunset and Shej at night (~10 pm). Confirm on the Sansthan's site.
3. **How do I book Shirdi darshan online?** General darshan is free. Paid darshan and aarti passes are sold only on online.sai.org.in — avoid other "VIP" sites.
4. **How far is Shani Shingnapur from Shirdi?** About 75–80 km by road, roughly 1.5–2 hours.
5. **Which is the nearest airport and railway station?** Shirdi airport at Kakadi, about 14 km; Sainagar Shirdi station is in the town.
6. **When is the Shirdi palkhi?** Every Thursday evening Baba's palanquin goes from the Samadhi Mandir to Dwarkamai and the Chavadi and back.
7. **Can I take my phone inside?** Phones are not allowed to be used inside; leave it at your hotel or locker.
8. **When did Sai Baba take Samadhi?** On 15 October 1918, Vijayadashami; his samadhi is in Buti Wada, today's Samadhi Mandir.
