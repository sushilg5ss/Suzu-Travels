## 2. Facts
- Names: Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir / Ram Mandir, Ayodhya — **श्री राम जन्मभूमि मंदिर, अयोध्या** (trust name: श्री राम जन्मभूमि तीर्थ क्षेत्र) — srjbtkshetra.org; Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ram_Mandir (2026-10-25).
- Location: **Ayodhya (Ayodhya Dham), Ayodhya district, Uttar Pradesh**, near the Saryu; **26.7956, 82.1943** (Wikipedia).
- Deity: **Ram Lalla** (Lord Rama as a child) on the ground floor; **Ram Darbar** (Rama with Sita, Lakshmana, Hanuman…) on the **first floor** — Telangana Today 5 Aug 2026; Wikipedia. Ram Lalla idol by sculptor **Arun Yogiraj** (widely reported; not on today's read pages → "by Mysuru sculptor Arun Yogiraj" is UNVERIFIED today — Page Builder may omit the name).
- Legend / significance (own words, as tradition — ≈120 words): In the **Valmiki Ramayana**, Ayodhya on the Saryu is the capital of the Ikshvaku kings of Kosala, where King Dasharatha's eldest son **Rama** is born — an event celebrated every year on **Ram Navami**, the ninth day of Chaitra. Devotees revere this spot as **Ram Janmabhoomi**, Rama's birthplace. Tradition lists Ayodhya first among the **seven sacred cities (Saptapuri)** that grant moksha. After fourteen years of exile and the war in Lanka, Rama's return to Ayodhya is remembered at **Diwali**, when the city is lit with lamps (today the state's Deepotsav on the Saryu ghats). Hanuman is believed to guard the city from **Hanumangarhi**, which is why many pilgrims bow there first. — paraphrased; scripture named; present as tradition.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Ram Mandir darshan timings?** Per the trust (from 18 Apr 2026), free darshan runs 7 am–9 pm with a 12–1 pm break; Mangala aarti 4 am, Shringar 6 am, Shayan 10 pm.
2. **Is a pass needed for Ram Mandir darshan?** No — general darshan is open; free passes for slots and aartis can be reserved on the trust's site. The trust charges no fee.
3. **Do I need a separate pass for Ram Darbar?** Not since 5 Aug 2026 — after Ram Lalla's darshan you go straight to the first-floor Ram Darbar.
4. **Are mobile phones allowed in Ram Mandir?** No; phones and electronic devices are not allowed — use the lockers before entry.
5. **When was the Ram Mandir inaugurated?** Ram Lalla's pran pratishtha was on 22 Jan 2024; the Ram Darbar was consecrated on 5 Jun 2025 and the temple flag hoisted on 25 Nov 2025.
6. **How far is Ram Mandir from Ayodhya railway station and airport?** About 1.6 km from Ayodhya Dham Junction and about 8 km from Ayodhya airport; Lucknow is about 150 km (2 h).
7. **What else to see in Ayodhya?** Hanumangarhi (under 1 km, many pilgrims go here first), the Saryu ghats and Ram ki Paidi, Kanak Bhawan and Dashrath Mahal in the old town.
8. **When is Ram Navami 2027?** Thursday 15 April 2027 — expect the biggest crowds of the year; check the trust's site for festival timings.
