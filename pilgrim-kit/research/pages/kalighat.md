## 2. Facts

- Deity: **Kali** — data: **Dakshina Kali** (Wikipedia, as_of 2026-10-08). - Shakti Peeth: one of the **51 Shakti Peeths** (Incredible India; Outlook); **toes of Sati's right foot** (data; yatradham); Bhairav **Nakuleshwar** (data; yatradham "Nakulesh"); counted among the **four Adi peethas** in several lists (research/shaktipeeth-notes.md — Kamakhya, Vimala-Puri, Kalighat, Tara Tarini).
- Legend (own words, as tradition, ≈110 words): When Sati gave up her life at Daksha's yajna and grieving Shiva carried her body across the worlds, Vishnu's Sudarshana chakra divided it so that Shiva's sorrow could end; wherever a part fell, a Shakti Peeth arose. Tradition holds that the **toes of her right foot** fell at Kalighat, on the banks of the old Ganga. A later local story says a devotee named **Atma Ram**, guided by a beam of light, found a toe-shaped stone in the river and, nearby, a self-manifested lingam of **Nakuleshwar Bhairav**, Shiva who guards the Devi — yatradham (2023); Sati story per data/shaktipeeth-notes. As tradition, not history.
- History (dated, as sources say): a Kali shrine at Kalighat is referred to in **15th–16th-century Bengali texts** (Incredible India summary — "Manasar Bhasan", "Kavi Kankan Chandi"); **present temple completed 1809** under the **Sabarna Roy Choudhury family** of Barisha — Incredible India (summary) + yatradham + data; **Natmandir c. 1835** (zamindar Kashinath Roy) — Incredible India, yatradham. ## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Kalighat temple timings?** Roughly 5 am to 10:30 pm, closed for bhog about 2–5 pm; later closing on Tue/Sat/Sun — confirm locally.
2. **Is Kalighat a Shakti Peeth?** Yes — tradition says the toes of Sati's right foot fell here; Nakuleshwar is its Bhairav, and several lists count it among the four Adi peethas.
3. **Do I need to pay for darshan?** No, general darshan is free; a paid faster queue exists. Agree any puja offering before you start.
4. **What is the dress code?** Modest, covered clothing; shorts, sleeveless tops and mini-skirts are not allowed.
5. **Nearest metro station?** Kalighat Metro, about 600 m away; the skywalk (opened April 2025) leads towards the temple.
6. **How far is Kalighat from Howrah station and the airport?** About 9 km from Howrah and about 25 km from Kolkata airport by road.
7. **Is Kalighat the same as Dakshineswar?** No — Dakshineswar Kali temple is about 19 km north, on the Hooghly; both are often visited the same day.
8. **When is Kali Puja?** On the Kartika Amavasya (Diwali) night — 8 Nov 2026; 2027 date follows Diwali (Oct–Nov).
