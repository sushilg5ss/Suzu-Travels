## 2. Facts
- Names: Char Dham Yatra (Uttarakhand) / चार धाम यात्रा (उत्तराखंड); also called Chhota Char Dham — not the all-India Char Dham of Badrinath–Dwarka–Puri–Rameswaram (say this once).
- Region: Garhwal Himalaya, Uttarakhand — four shrines in **Uttarkashi** (Yamunotri, Gangotri), **Rudraprayag** (Kedarnath) and **Chamoli** (Badrinath) districts.
- The four (coords for the route map; Kedarnath = data, others Wikipedia town/temple level, UNVERIFIED to the decimal; altitudes approx., vary by source — trap #14):
  - **Yamunotri** / यमुनोत्री — goddess Yamuna; ~3,290 m; 31.0140, 78.4600; trek ~6 km from Janki Chatti.
  - **Gangotri** / गंगोत्री — goddess Ganga; ~3,100 m; 30.9947, 78.9398; drive-up.
  - **Kedarnath** / केदारनाथ — Shiva (Jyotirlinga); ~3,583 m; 30.7352, 79.0669; trek ~16 km from Gaurikund or helicopter.
  - **Badrinath** / बद्रीनाथ — Vishnu as Badri Narayan; ~3,100–3,300 m; 30.7433, 79.4938; drive-up.
- Order: traditionally **west to east — Yamunotri → Gangotri → Kedarnath → Badrinath** — data faqs.
- Legend (own words, as tradition): Adi Shankaracharya is credited, in tradition, with reviving these four Himalayan shrines as one circuit in the 8th century. At Yamunotri pilgrims honour Yamuna, the sister of Yama, and a dip is believed to free one from the fear of untimely death. Gangotri remembers King Bhagiratha, whose penance is said to have brought the Ganga down from heaven. At Kedarnath, Shiva is believed to have hidden from the Pandavas as a bull, leaving his hump in the rock. At Badrinath, Vishnu is said to have meditated in the bitter cold while Lakshmi sheltered him as a badri (jujube) tree. (≈110 words) — data legend_summary (2026-10-08)
- Managing bodies: **BKTC** (Badrinath, Kedarnath) https://www.badrinath-kedarnath.gov.in · Gangotri and Yamunotri temple committees (no verified official URLs → don't link) · State: Uttarakhand Tourism https://uttarakhandtourism.gov.in/

Pattern: Gangotri & Yamunotri on **Akshaya Tritiya 2027** (date to be confirmed from panchang — not checked today); Kedarnath date fixed on **Mahashivratri 6 Mar 2027** (Ukhimath); Badrinath date fixed on **Basant Panchami 11 Feb 2027** (Narendranagar). - Registration (**mandatory, free**) — data registration: register every pilgrim (and vehicle) on **registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in**, the **Tourist Care Uttarakhand** app, WhatsApp, or offline counters (e.g. Haridwar, Rishikesh); create a tour → dates per dham → add pilgrims → download the QR **Yatra Registration Letter**, checked at each dham. Helpline **1364** / 0135-2559898. Aadhaar-based e-KYC since 2025 — https://www.newsonair.gov.in/aadhaar-authenticated-registration-for-char-dham-yatra-begins. The registration number is also needed for Kedarnath helicopter tickets. Any "registration fee" site is fake (trap #11).
- Route (data route, approximate km/time):
  1. Haridwar/Rishikesh → **Barkot** ~210 km, 7–8 h
  2. Barkot → **Janki Chatti** 45 km road + **~6 km trek** to Yamunotri (1 day return)
  3. Barkot → Uttarkashi/Harsil → **Gangotri** (~100 + 100 km, motorable to temple; 1–2 days)
  4. Uttarkashi → **Guptkashi/Sitapur** via Tehri & Rudraprayag ~220 km, 8–9 h
  5. Sonprayag → shuttle **~5 km** to Gaurikund → **~16 km trek** to Kedarnath (6–8 h up; pony, palki, pithoo or helicopter)
  6. Guptkashi → **Badrinath** via Ukhimath, Chopta/Chamoli, Joshimath ~200 km, 7–8 h
  7. Badrinath → Rishikesh/Haridwar ~300 km, 9–10 h
- Duration: **10–12 days** ex Haridwar/Delhi for all four; **Do Dham** (Kedarnath + Badrinath) 5–6 days — data.
- Getting there: Jolly Grant airport (Dehradun); railheads Haridwar, Rishikesh, Dehradun; cabs/tempo travellers/buses — data.
- Helicopter: Kedarnath shuttles from **Guptkashi / Phata / Sersi** — **only heliyatra.irctc.co.in**, after registration (2 tickets per user ID, 6 pilgrims per ticket). "Char Dham by helicopter" packages from Dehradun are private charters — present as info only; no fares, no operator names (human-only: partner names). DGCA guidelines for Char Dham heli ops (ETV Bharat 2 Apr 2026, headline). Fake-site warning (Tribune).
- Difficulty: **moderate** — two treks (Yamunotri ~6 km, Kedarnath ~16 km to ~3,580 m), long mountain drives, landslide-prone monsoon roads; Gangotri and Badrinath drive-up — data.
- Health: Uttarakhand advisory — over-50s and those with heart/lung/BP conditions get a check-up; acclimatise; carry prescriptions; screening kiosks on Kedarnath & Yamunotri routes; descend if unwell — data. Age limits: none officially reported for Char Dham (unlike Amarnath) → don't invent one.
- Best time: **May–June and mid-Sep to Oct**; avoid Jul–Aug peak monsoon — data. Weather halts happen even in October (Kedarnath 3-h halt 10 Oct 2026, Amar Ujala headline).
- What to carry: QR registration letter + photo ID, warm layers, rain jacket, trekking shoes, medicines/ORS, torch, power bank, cash (patchy ATMs/network) — data.
- Winter Char Dham (Nov–Apr, by road only): **Kharsali** (Yamuna), **Mukhba** near Harsil (Ganga), **Omkareshwar, Ukhimath** (Kedarnath), **Yogdhyan Badri, Pandukeshwar** + Narsingh temple, **Jyotirmath** (Badrinath) — data winter-char-dham-yatra (2026-10-08). 2026-27 winter season dates not announced. ## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- Recycled 2025 closing dates (Kedarnath/Yamunotri 23 Oct, Gangotri 22 Oct, Badrinath 25 Nov 2025) shown as 2026 — wrong (trap #1).
- Badrinath 2026 opened **23 Apr**, not 24 (trap #2). Kedarnath **22 Apr**, not Akshaya Tritiya.
- Panchang drift on Govardhan Puja / Bhai Dooj (9/10/11 Nov) — use committee announcements (trap #3).
- "Char Dham" = Uttarakhand Chhota Char Dham here; the all-India Char Dham (Badrinath, Dwarka, Puri, Rameswaram) is different.
- Registration is free; heli only on IRCTC; WhatsApp/agent "confirmed heli" = scam risk (traps #4, #11).
- Tungnath, not Kedarnath, is the highest Shiva temple (trap #12). Altitudes vary by source (trap #14) — print "about".
- Order west→east is tradition/practical, not a rule; Do Dham is a common shorter variant.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **When does Char Dham Yatra 2027 start?** Not announced yet. Gangotri and Yamunotri open on Akshaya Tritiya; Kedarnath's date is fixed on Mahashivratri (6 Mar 2027) and Badrinath's on Basant Panchami (11 Feb 2027) — usually late April/early May.
2. **How do I register for Char Dham Yatra?** Free, on registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in or the Tourist Care Uttarakhand app (or WhatsApp/offline counters); download the QR letter; helpline 1364.
3. **Is registration fee charged?** No — official registration is free; sites charging a fee are fake.
4. **What is the correct order?** Yamunotri → Gangotri → Kedarnath → Badrinath (west to east).
5. **How many days are needed?** About 10–12 days from Haridwar/Delhi by road; Do Dham (Kedarnath + Badrinath) 5–6 days.
6. **Can senior citizens do Char Dham?** Yes, with a medical check-up and planning — Gangotri and Badrinath are drive-up; for Yamunotri and Kedarnath use pony, palki or (Kedarnath) helicopter.
7. **When do the dhams close in 2026?** Dates are announced around Vijayadashami (20 Oct 2026); by custom Gangotri ~Annakut, Yamunotri and Kedarnath ~Bhai Dooj, Badrinath later in November — see our closing-dates post.
8. **Can I do Char Dham in winter?** Not at the main shrines; the deities are worshipped at winter seats — Kharsali, Mukhba, Ukhimath and Pandukeshwar/Jyotirmath — reachable by road.
