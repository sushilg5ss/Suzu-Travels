## 2. Facts
- Names: **Arulmigu Meenakshi Sundareswarar Temple / Meenakshi Amman Temple** — **मीनाक्षी अम्मन मंदिर, मदुरै** — data/shaktipeeth.json; managed by Tamil Nadu **HR&CE** department (official site, data as_of 2026-10-08).
- Location: centre of **Madurai, Madurai district, Tamil Nadu**, on the south bank of the Vaigai (UNVERIFIED today — phrase "in the heart of old Madurai"); **9.9195 N, 78.1188 E** (Nominatim 2026-11-01).
- Deities: **Meenakshi** (a form of Parvati, "the fish-eyed one") and **Sundareswarar** (Shiva) — data/shaktipeeth.json.
- Legend (own words, as tradition, ≈130 words): The **Thiruvilaiyadal Puranam** tells that the childless Pandya king **Malayadhwaja** and queen **Kanchanamalai** performed a fire sacrifice for a son; instead a three-year-old girl rose from the flames — with three breasts — and a divine voice said the third would vanish when she met her destined husband. Raised as a warrior heir, **Meenakshi** ruled Madurai and set out to conquer the worlds, until on **Mount Kailash** she faced **Shiva**; the sign vanished, and he came to Madurai as **Sundareswarar** to marry her. Tradition says **Vishnu**, as her brother (Kallazhagar in local belief), gave the bride away. Shiva's playful miracles in Madurai, the 64 Thiruvilaiyadals, are painted in the temple's corridors. — Paraphrased from data/shaktipeeth.json; TN Tourism (murals of Thiruvilaiyadal Puranam; Kallazhagar as brother) 2026-11-01.
- **Not a Shakti Peeth** — not in the classical 51 or 18 lists; present it as one of India's greatest Devi temples — data/shaktipeeth.json; shaktipeeth-notes trap #14.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Meenakshi temple timings?** 6 am–12:30 pm and 4–9 pm, per Tamil Nadu Tourism; first and last poojas fall slightly outside — confirm locally.
2. **Is there a dress code at Meenakshi temple?** Modest traditional dress is expected — dhoti or trousers with a shirt for men, saree, salwar or churidar for women; avoid shorts.
3. **Are mobile phones allowed?** No — phones and cameras have been banned inside since March 2018; use the deposit counters.
4. **When is Chithirai festival 2027?** Not announced yet; it falls in the Tamil month of Chithirai (April–May), with Meenakshi Thirukalyanam as the high point.
5. **How many gopurams does Meenakshi temple have?** Fourteen, roughly 45–50 metres tall.
6. **Is Meenakshi temple a Shakti Peeth?** No — it is not in the classical 51 or 18 lists, though it is one of India's greatest Devi temples.
7. **How far is it from Madurai railway station and airport?** About 1.5 km from Madurai Junction and 11 km from Madurai Airport.
8. **Can I combine Meenakshi temple with Rameswaram?** Yes — Rameswaram's Ramanathaswamy Jyotirlinga is about 175 km (3 hours) by road.
