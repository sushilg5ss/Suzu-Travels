- Deity: **Ganesha as Siddhivinayak** — a single **black stone idol about 2 ft 6 in tall and 2 ft wide**, **trunk turned to the right**; four hands hold a **lotus** (upper right), **axe** (upper left), **rosary** (lower right) and a **bowl of modaks** (lower left); a serpent winds from the left shoulder across the belly; **Riddhi and Siddhi** stand on either side — Incredible India (2026-11-02). Right-trunk ("siddhi") Ganesha is traditionally held to be quick to grant and strict in worship — phrase as belief only.
- Legend / meaning (own words, as tradition, ≈120 words): Ganesha is the son of **Shiva and Parvati**, worshipped first in every Hindu rite as **Vighnaharta**, the remover of obstacles. According to the **Puranas**, Parvati shaped him to guard her door; when Shiva, not knowing the boy, struck off his head, he restored him with the head of an elephant and made him lord of the ganas — leader of Shiva's hosts — to be honoured before all other gods. "Siddhivinayak" means the Vinayaka who grants **siddhi** — success and fulfilment; his consorts in tradition are **Riddhi** (prosperity) and **Siddhi** (accomplishment). Devotees in Mumbai begin new ventures, exams, journeys and marriages with his darshan, and Tuesday — especially an **Angaraki Sankashti Chaturthi** that falls on a Tuesday — draws the longest queues. (Ganesha Purana / Mudgala Purana may be named; keep tradition framing.)

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Mumbai Siddhivinayak ≠ Siddhatek Siddhivinayak** (Ahmednagar district, one of the **Ashtavinayak** eight) — the Prabhadevi temple is NOT an Ashtavinayak temple. Say so clearly.
- Station names: nearest mainline station is **Dadar**, not "Prabhadevi" (Prabhadevi station = Western line, also close — name only "Dadar"); Incredible India lists Mumbai Central — fine as the long-distance train terminal.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Siddhivinayak temple timings?** Usually from about 5:30 am to about 10 pm, with short pauses for aartis and the noon naivedya; Tuesdays start pre-dawn. Confirm the day's schedule on the trust's website.
2. **Why is Tuesday special at Siddhivinayak?** Tuesday is Ganesha's day for many Mumbai devotees, and an Angaraki Sankashti (a Sankashti Chaturthi on Tuesday) draws the biggest queues of the year.
3. **Is there an entry fee or online booking?** General darshan is free; there is no official online darshan-slot booking. Watch live darshan on the trust's site and app.
4. **Can I take my phone inside?** No — phones and electronic items must be deposited at the counters outside.
5. **Is there a dress code?** Yes, since early 2025: modest clothing that covers the body; revealing clothes are not allowed.
6. **Which is the nearest station?** Dadar (about 2 km) and the Siddhivinayak Temple metro station right next to it.
7. **Is Siddhivinayak one of the Ashtavinayak temples?** No. The Ashtavinayak Siddhivinayak is at Siddhatek in Ahmednagar district; the Mumbai temple is separate.
8. **Who built Siddhivinayak temple?** It was built by Laxman Vithu Patil and his wife Deubai Patil (Incredible India); the popular story about their reasons is UNVERIFIED — print only the names.
