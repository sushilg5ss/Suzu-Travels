## 2. Facts
- Names: Shri Omkareshwar Mandir / ओंकारेश्वर ज्योतिर्लिंग; with **Mamleshwar (Amaleshwar) / ममलेश्वर (अमलेश्वर)** on the south bank — data (2026-10-08)

- Location: Mandhata (Shivapuri) island in the Narmada, Omkareshwar town, **Khandwa district**, Madhya Pradesh; island near the Narmada–Kaveri confluence; 22.2456, 76.1511 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omkareshwar_Temple (2026-10-08)
- Managing body: Shri Omkareshwar Mandir Trust — https://www.shriomkareshwar.org (2026-10-08)
- Deity: Shiva as Omkareshwar, Lord of the sacred syllable Om; tradition treats Omkareshwar and Amaleshwar/Mamleshwar as **one Jyotirlinga in two aspects** (FACT TRAP #18).
- Legend (own words, as tradition — Shiva Purana, Koti Rudra Samhita): Once the sage Narada praised Mount Meru in front of the Vindhya mountain, and Vindhya's pride was hurt. Wanting greatness of his own, the mountain went to the banks of the Narmada, shaped a linga of earth and worshipped Shiva with long, single-minded penance. Shiva appeared, granted his wish, and — at the request of gods and sages — agreed to remain here in two forms, Omkareshwar and Amaleshwar. Another tradition remembers Mandhata, a king of the Ikshvaku line, whose devotion gave the island its name. Because the island, circled by the Narmada, is believed to look like the syllable Om, Shiva here is worshipped as the Lord of Omkara. (≈120 words) — data legend_summary (2026-10-08)

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Which is the Jyotirlinga — Omkareshwar or Mamleshwar?** Tradition and the stotra treat them as one Jyotirlinga in two aspects; Shiva Purana says "Omkare Parameshwaram", the smaranam "Omkaram Amaleshwaram". Advise visiting both (#18).
- Don't confuse with **Omkareshwar Temple, Ukhimath** (Kedarnath's winter seat, Uttarakhand) nor with the Omkareshwar shrine on Mahakal temple's middle level in Ujjain.
- Holkar rebuild: "begun by Gautama Bai, completed by Ahilyabai" — not "Ahilyabai alone".
- "Island shaped like Om" — say "is believed to resemble".
- Shighra Darshan fee / Narmada Aarti fee — never print amounts; official site only; warn about fake "VIP darshan" sellers.
- MP Tourism "Temple View" hotel is a commercial stay, not a temple facility.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Omkareshwar darshan timings?** 5 am – 10:30 pm with breaks; Mangal Aarti 4:30–5 am, Shayan Aarti 9:30–10 pm (Trust time table).
2. **Is Omkareshwar or Mamleshwar the real Jyotirlinga?** Tradition treats them as one Jyotirlinga in two forms — visit both.
3. **How do I book Shighra (VIP) darshan?** Same day, only on shriomkareshwar.org, 2-hour slot; normal darshan is free.
4. **How to reach Omkareshwar from Indore?** About 77 km by taxi or bus; nearest railway station Sanawad (12 km), bigger junction Khandwa (72 km).
5. **How far is Omkareshwar from Mahakaleshwar (Ujjain)?** About 140 km by road (Trust FAQ).
6. **Is there a dress code?** No official code; modest clothing is recommended.
7. **Why is it called Omkareshwar?** The Narmada wraps Mandhata island in a shape believed to resemble Om; Shiva here is Lord of Omkara.
8. **What is the Statue of Oneness?** A 108-ft statue of Adi Shankaracharya on Mandhata island, unveiled on 21 Sep 2023.
