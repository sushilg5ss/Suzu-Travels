## 2. Facts
- Names: Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple (Vishweshwar) / श्री काशी विश्वनाथ ज्योतिर्लिंग; Kashi = Avimukta, Varanasi, Banaras — data (2026-10-08)
- Location: Vishwanath Gali, old city of Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, on the western bank of the Ganga; 25.3108, 83.0106 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashi_Vishwanath_Temple (2026-10-08)
- Managing body: Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple Trust (UP govt) — official https://shrikashivishwanath.org + official app (2026-10-08). A new CEO was reported in Oct 2026 (hindutone headline, name UNVERIFIED — don't print).
- Deity: Shiva as Vishwanath / Vishweshwar, "Lord of the Universe"; Jyotirlinga — data
- Legend (own words, as tradition — Shiva Purana, Koti Rudra Samhita; Kashi Khanda of the Skanda Purana): Tradition calls Kashi "Avimukta", the city Shiva never leaves. It is believed that Shiva and Shakti made this city their own home at the dawn of creation, and that it rests on the point of Shiva's trident, so it survives even when the universe dissolves. Vishweshwara is also linked with the endless pillar of light that appeared when Brahma and Vishnu argued over who was greater — neither could reach its top or bottom. Devotees hold that anyone who dies in Kashi hears the liberating Taraka mantra from Shiva himself, which is why Kashi is seen as a doorway to moksha and why Manikarnika ghat is so sacred. (≈120 words) — data legend_summary
- History (dated, plainly stated):
  - **1194** — forces of Muhammad of Ghor destroyed the original temple — Wikipedia (2026-10-08)
  - **1669** — Aurangzeb ordered the temple demolished; the Gyanvapi mosque was built on the site — Wikipedia (2026-10-08) (see §3: state neutrally, no comment on the court case)
  - **1780** — Maharani Ahilyabai Holkar built the present temple — Wikipedia (2026-10-08)
  - **1835** — Maharaja Ranjit Singh donated about one tonne of gold to plate the dome and spire — Wikipedia (2026-10-08)
  - **13 Dec 2021** — PM Modi inaugurated the Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham corridor linking the temple to the Ganga ghats — https://narendramodi.in/prime-minister-narendra-modi-to-visit-varanasi-and-inaugurate-shri-kashi-vishwanath-dham-on-13-december (FACT TRAP #11 — not 2022)

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Kashi Vishwanath darshan timings?** Temple 3 am – 11 pm; general darshan roughly 4–11 am, 12:20–7 pm and 8:30–9 pm, pausing for aartis (Trust FAQ).
2. **What time is the Mangala Aarti and how do I book it?** 3–4 am; report by 2:30 am at Gate 1; book only on shrikashivishwanath.org or the official app (non-refundable).
3. **What is Sugam Darshan?** A paid, escorted, queue-free darshan with a priest, locker and prasad — fee on the official site.
4. **Are phones allowed inside Kashi Vishwanath?** No — no gadgets or even pens; free lockers are provided.
5. **Is there a dress code?** No formal code; the Trust asks for decent clothing.
6. **When was the Kashi Vishwanath corridor opened?** 13 December 2021.
7. **How far is Kashi Vishwanath from Varanasi Junction / the airport?** About 5 km from Varanasi Jn and about 25 km from Babatpur airport.
8. **When is Dev Deepawali 2026?** On Kartik Purnima, 24 Nov 2026 (per panchang) — the most crowded night of the year at the ghats.
