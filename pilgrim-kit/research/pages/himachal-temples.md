## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Mansa Devi (Panchkula) is in Haryana**, not Himachal — keep it OFF this page (or one line "nearby in Haryana" with link when live). Kali Mata Kalka also Haryana.
- **Vaishno Devi is J&K**; don't list it as a Himachal temple (link from the Devi section only as "beyond Himachal").
- **Baijnath (Kangra) ≠ Baidyanath Dham (Deoghar, Jharkhand)** — not a Jyotirlinga claim; don't target "baijnath dham".
- **Chamunda and Vaishno Devi are not classical Shakti Peeths; Chintpurni's body part is disputed**; Kangra vs Jalandhar (left breast) — keep the neutral wording of the live pages.
- **Jwala Ji flames** = natural gas (ONGC wells from 1957) side by side with tradition.
- Wikipedia coords traps: **Baijnath** (use town 32.052/76.648) and **Bhimakali** (Wikipedia temple coords = Rampur → use Sarahan 31.52/77.80).
- **Shrikhand 2026 suspended**; Kinner Kailash 2026 reopened 30 Jul–10 Aug; Manimahesh 2026 25 Aug–19 Sep with e-pass — don't swap them.
- No "most powerful temple", "wish-fulfilling", or ranking language; no prices; tour packages only via product 10861 / Get Quote.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **Which are the famous temples in Himachal Pradesh?** Naina Devi, Chintpurni, Jwala Ji, Kangra (Brajeshwari) Devi and Chamunda for the Devi; Baba Balak Nath, Baijnath, Jakhu, Hidimba Devi and Bhimakali — plus the high yatras of Manimahesh and Shrikhand.
2. **Which Shakti Peeths are in Himachal?** By tradition Naina Devi, Jwala Ji, Kangra Devi and Chintpurni; Chamunda is visited on the same circuit though it is not in the classical lists → link `shakti-peeth-himachal`.
3. **How many days for a Himachal Devi darshan?** The five-Devi circuit is usually done in about 5 days (product 10861 "5 days 4 nights") — Get Quote.
4. **Which temple is near Shimla?** Jakhu (Hanuman) on Jakhu hill, ~2.5 km from the Ridge; Bhimakali at Sarahan is ~5 h further east.
5. **Which temple is in Manali?** Hidimba Devi, in the Dhungri deodar forest ~2 km from central Manali.
6. **Are Himachal temples open in winter?** The lower-hill temples are open year-round; high yatras (Manimahesh, Shrikhand, Kinner Kailash) run only in Jul–Sep when the district notifies them.
7. **Who manages the big temples?** Most are run by government temple trusts under the HP Hindu Public Religious Institutions and Charitable Endowments Act, 1984.
8. **Is Baijnath in Himachal a Jyotirlinga?** It honours Shiva as Vaidyanath, but the Jyotirlinga is usually placed at Deoghar (Jharkhand); some traditions differ → link `12-jyotirlinga`.
