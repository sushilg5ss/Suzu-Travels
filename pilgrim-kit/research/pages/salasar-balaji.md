## 2. Facts
- Names: **Shri Salasar Balaji** — **श्री सालासर बालाजी** / सालासर बालाजी मंदिर (bhaktibharat); also "Siddhpeeth Salasar Balaji" (सिद्धपीठ — Wikipedia citations), "Salasar Dham" — https://www.bhaktibharat.com/amp/en/mandir/salasar-balaji-mandir (as_of 2026-11-04).
- Location: **Salasar town, Sujangarh tehsil, Churu district, Rajasthan**, on the Jaipur–Bikaner route — Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salasar_Balaji_Temple + Nominatim (as_of 2026-11-04); 27.7258 N, 74.7243 E.
- Deity: **Hanuman (Balaji)** — the image is known for a **round face with beard and moustache**, described as unique among Hanuman temples — temple.yatradham.org https://temple.yatradham.org/temple/shri-salasar-balaji-dham-mandir , bhaktibharat (as_of 2026-11-04). On page: "devotees know it for its bearded, moustached Balaji" (belief wording; avoid "only temple in India" superlative).
- Legend (own words, as tradition, ≈120 words): Local tradition says that in **1754 CE (Vikram Samvat 1811), on Shravan Shukla Navami**, a farmer ploughing his field at **Asota village** (Nagaur district) struck a stone image of Balaji; his wife offered it the bajra churma she had brought for lunch. That night Balaji is said to have appeared in a dream to **Mohandas**, a devotee living at Salasar, asking to be brought to Salasar, while the Thakur of Asota dreamed the same. The image travelled to Salasar by bullock cart and was installed where the cart stopped — the story as the temple tradition tells it. **Mohandas Maharaj's akhand dhuna** (ever-burning sacred fire) is still tended at the temple. — Wikipedia; Zee News Hindi (Sthapana Diwas, Shravan Shukla Navami 1754 / VS 1811; dhuna "271 years"); blog.yatradham.org (as_of 2026-11-04). Weekday of the find **differs (Tuesday — Wikipedia; Saturday — yatradham) → omit**. "Cart stopped" detail: Wikipedia summary via agent — treat as "it is said".
- History: shrine founded **1754 CE** by **Mohandas** (rajasthantourplanner: built with mud-stone, craftsmen **Noora and Dau**; later enlarged with help of **Rao Devi Singh** of Sikar and Mohandas's successors Kaniram & Ishwardas) — https://www.rajasthantourplanner.com/Religious-Places-in-Rajasthan/salasar-balaji-temple.html (as_of 2026-11-04; secondary — print as "according to temple tradition").
- Management: **Shri Hanuman Seva Samiti** (bhaktibharat; rajasthantourplanner) — as_of 2026-11-04. Foundation day observed on **Shravan Shukla Navami** each year (Zee News Hindi).
- **Darshan:** about **6 am – 9 pm** (temple.yatradham, blog.yatradham, bhaktibharat; as_of 2026-11-04). Mangla aarti before opening; temple open through the day on Hanuman Jayanti (blog.yatradham).

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Salasar Balaji darshan timings?** About 6 am to 9 pm, with Mangla aarti before dawn and Shayan aarti at night; times shift between summer and winter.
2. **How far is Salasar Balaji from Khatu Shyam?** About 100 km by road, roughly 1.5–2 hours via Sikar.
3. **Which is the nearest railway station?** Sujangarh, about 26 km; Salasar itself has no station.
4. **How far is Salasar from Jaipur?** About 170 km from Jaipur Junction and 185 km from Jaipur airport, around 2.5–3 hours.
5. **Can I take my phone inside?** No — since July 2026 an SDM order bans mobile phones and smartwatches inside the temple.
6. **Why does Salasar Balaji have a beard and moustache?** That is how the image found in 1754 is worshipped; devotees know Salasar for its round-faced, bearded Balaji.
7. **When is the Salasar mela?** Big fairs on Chaitra Purnima (Hanuman Jayanti — 20 Apr 2027) and Ashwin (Sharad) Purnima.
8. **Why do people tie coconuts at Salasar?** It is a custom: devotees tie a coconut with a sacred thread while making a vow, and return when it is fulfilled.
