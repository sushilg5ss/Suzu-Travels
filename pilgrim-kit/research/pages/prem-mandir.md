## 2. Facts
- Names: **Prem Mandir** ("Temple of Divine Love") — **प्रेम मन्दिर** — premmandir.org.in (2026-11-02).
- Location: **Vrindavan, Mathura district, Uttar Pradesh** — official contact "Prem Mandir, Vrindavan, Mathura"; OSM places it on **Bhaktivedanta Swami Marg, Chhatikara (Vrindavan's southern entrance)**, **27.5721 N, 77.6720 E** (2026-11-02).
- Founder: **Jagadguru Shri Kripalu Ji Maharaj**; built and run by **Jagadguru Kripalu Parishat** (formerly Sadhana Bhawan Trust, est. 1972) — premmandir.org.in /the-history/, /the-architecture/ (2026-11-02).
- Dates: **foundation stone 14 Jan 2001** (before about a thousand devotees); about **12 years** of work by about **1,000 artisans**; **inauguration & pran pratishtha 15–17 Feb 2012** — premmandir.org.in /the-history/.
- Deities: **Shri Radha Krishna** (ground floor) and **Shri Sita Ram** (upper floor) — premmandir.org.in home. (Floor placement from common reporting → UNVERIFIED; print only the two deity pairs.)
- Architecture: **white Italian Carrara marble**, on a **20-ft-deep granite foundation**; **125 ft high (with flag), 190 ft long, 128 ft wide**; outer walls carry **84 carved panels of Radha Krishna leelas**; inside, images of the four earlier Jagadgurus, Rasik saints and the Ashta-sakhis; gardens with fountains and life-size tableaux of **Jhulan, Govardhan, Raas and Kaliya-daman leelas** — premmandir.org.in /the-architecture/ and home. Premises **55 acres** (official home) — Incredible India says 54 → print "about 55 acres" or omit. Construction cost → never print.
- Legend / meaning (own words, as tradition, ≈120 words): Vrindavan is held in the **Bhagavata Purana** to be the forest where **Krishna** spent his childhood among the cowherds, and where his **Raas** with **Radha** and the gopis became the image of selfless divine love. Prem Mandir tells these stories in stone: Krishna lifting **Govardhan hill** to shelter the people of Braj from Indra's storm, subduing the serpent **Kaliya** in the Yamuna, swinging with Radha in the monsoon **Jhulan**, and the circle of the Raas. Its founder, Kripalu Ji Maharaj, taught **bhakti** — loving devotion — as the simplest path to God, and named the temple for that love. Sita Ram on the upper level honour the same devotion in the Ramayana. (Modern temple — say "the temple's founder taught…", never "history".)

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Prem Mandir is modern (2001–2012)** — not an ancient temple; no "5,000-year-old" claims.
- Marble: official says **Italian Carrara** — many sites say Makrana; don't say Makrana.
- Height: **125 ft including the flag** (official) — other sites give 125/150 ft.
- Area 55 (official) vs 54 acres (Incredible India).
- "Light show" timings online vary wildly — use only the official **fountain** times (season-based).
- Early-morning 5 am aarti listed only on /timings/ → don't make it a "darshan" time.
- Kripalu Ji Maharaj — no controversy narrative; neutral, respectful.
- Not ISKCON; don't confuse with ISKCON Vrindavan or Banke Bihari.
- Cost of construction — never print.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Prem Mandir timings?** 8:30 am to 12 noon and 4:30 pm to 8:30 pm, every day (official site).
2. **What time is the Prem Mandir light/fountain show?** The musical fountain runs 7–7:30 pm from October to March and 7:30–8 pm from April to September; the temple is lit in changing colours after sunset.
3. **Who built Prem Mandir?** Jagadguru Shri Kripalu Ji Maharaj; Jagadguru Kripalu Parishat built it between 2001 and 2012.
4. **When was Prem Mandir inaugurated?** On 15–17 February 2012.
5. **Which deities are in Prem Mandir?** Shri Radha Krishna and Shri Sita Ram.
6. **What is Prem Mandir made of?** White Italian marble, rising about 125 ft with the flag.
7. **How far is Prem Mandir from Mathura station and Banke Bihari?** About 12 km from Mathura Junction and about 3 km from Banke Bihari temple.
8. **What is the best time to visit Prem Mandir?** The evening — arrive before sunset to see the lights come on and stay for the fountain show; October to March is the most pleasant season.
