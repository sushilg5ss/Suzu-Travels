## 2. Facts
- The five usually grouped: Naina Devi (Bilaspur), Chintpurni (Una), Jwala Ji (Kangra), Brajeshwari / Kangra Devi (Kangra), Chamunda (Kangra) — https://hpkangra.nic.in/kangra-pilgrimage/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chintpurni (as_of 2026-10-08)
- Classical status: Jwala Ji (tongue), Brajeshwari (left breast), Naina Devi (eyes) are in compiled 51-peetha tables; Chintpurni's body part is disputed (foot / forehead / "consciousness"; the temple tells the Chhinnamasta story); Chamunda is NOT a classical peetha — local grouping — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakta_pithas ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shri_Chamunda_Devi_Mandir (2026-10-08)
- Legend (own words, notes §1): Sati, daughter of Daksha, married Shiva against her father's wish; Daksha's yagna excluded Shiva; Sati went, heard Shiva insulted, gave up her body (fire or yogic power, texts differ); Virabhadra destroyed the yagna; Shiva carried Sati's body in grief; Vishnu's Sudarshan Chakra divided it; where parts fell became peethas, each with a Devi and a Bhairava — Wikipedia Shakta pithas, Daksha yajna; hpkangra.nic.in (2026-10-08)
- Counts differ: 4 / 18 / 51 / 52 / 64 / 108 depending on the text — Wikipedia Shakta pithas (2026-10-08)
- Naina Devi: Shivalik hilltop above Gobind Sagar, Bilaspur; 31.306, 76.5364; hours ~05:00–21:00 (Incredible India), aarti not published; cable car from base; Anandpur Sahib–Naina Devi ropeway not built (Tribune Aug 2024); Chandigarh ~100–106 km; rail Anandpur Sahib ~20–30 km, Kiratpur Sahib ~30 km — incredibleindia.gov.in; hpbilaspur.nic.in (2026-10-08)
- Chintpurni: Chhinnamastika; Chhaproh village, Una; 31.80862, 76.10287; darshan hours and aarti clock times not published (three aartis listed); paid Sugam Darshan bookable online; darshan parchi from the guard required; legend of Pandit Mai Das; four Shiva temples guard the dham (Kaleshwar, Narahna, Muchhkund, Shiv Bari); melas: Sawan Ashtami (Jul–Aug), Asuj Navratra (Oct), New Year 28 Dec–2 Jan — https://matashrichintpurni.com (2026-10-08)
- Jwala Ji: flames worshipped, no idol; tongue; 31.8752, 76.3243; district page 05:00–22:00 approx. with Mangal, Bhog, evening and Shaiyan aartis; HP temples portal summer 06:00–11:30 & 12:30–22:00 (indicative); flames = natural gas seepage, ONGC wells from 1957; Akbar's chhatra = legend — hpkangra.nic.in; kangratemples.hp.gov.in; Wikipedia (2026-10-08)
- Brajeshwari (Kangra Devi): Nagarkot/Kangra; 32.10183, 76.26987; 06:00–12:00, 12:30–20:00 (Incredible India); left breast; Pandavas tradition; plundered by Mahmud of Ghazni; 1905 earthquake; Makar Sankranti ghrit (butter) festival ~7 days mid-Jan; Gaggal airport ~8 km; Kangra NG station ~11 km — incredibleindia.gov.in; hpkangra.nic.in (2026-10-08)
- Chamunda Nandikeshwar Dham: Padar near Dharamshala, Baner river; 32.1483, 76.4192; generally 06:00–19:00/20:00; Devi Mahatmya Chanda–Munda legend; priest's dream, idol moved from Aadi Himani Chamunda; Dharamshala 15 km — hpkangra.nic.in (2026-10-08)
- Road km (OSRM, 2026-10-08): Ghumarwin→Naina Devi 64 · Naina Devi→Chintpurni 112 · Chintpurni→Jwala Ji 31 · Jwala Ji→Brajeshwari 36 · Brajeshwari→Chamunda 22 · Chamunda→Ghumarwin 113 (loop ~378 km). Delhi→Naina Devi ~350–368.
- Airports: Chandigarh (for Naina Devi), Gaggal/Kangra (for the Kangra temples). Broad-gauge railhead Pathankot; Kangra Valley narrow-gauge railway (Jawalamukhi Road/Ranital, Kangra).
- Dates: Sharad Navratri 11–19 Oct 2026, Vijayadashami 20 Oct (one source also quotes 12–21 Oct — confirm local panchang); Chaitra Navratri 2027 from 7 Apr 2027; Shravan Ashtami melas Jul–Aug (2027 dates not announced) — dekhopanchang; navbharatlive (2026-10-08)
- Best season: Mar–Jun and Sep–Nov; avoid peak monsoon; Navratri and Shravan = heaviest crowds — notes §3c.
