## 4. Questions people ask
1. **What are Kolhapur Mahalaxmi temple timings?** About 4:30 am (Kakad aarti) to about 10:30 pm (after Shej aarti), with a short noon break for naivedya; confirm locally.
2. **What is Kirnotsav?** For a few evenings around 9–13 November and 30 January–3 February the setting sun's rays reach the idol — the feet first, then higher each day — if the sky is clear.
3. **Is Kolhapur Mahalaxmi a Shakti Peeth?** Yes — it is in the 18 Maha Shakti Peeth hymn attributed to Adi Shankara and one of Maharashtra's three-and-a-half peethas; lists differ on the body part.
4. **Why is she called Ambabai?** Ambabai ("Mother") is Kolhapur's local name for Mahalakshmi, its protecting goddess.
5. **Is there a dress code?** Modest traditional clothes; shorts, skirts and revealing clothes are not allowed. No photos in the sanctum.
6. **How far is Jyotiba temple from Kolhapur Mahalaxmi?** About 16 km by hill road, roughly 40 minutes.
7. **Which is the nearest railway station and airport?** Kolhapur (Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Terminus) about 2 km; Kolhapur airport about 13 km.
8. **Why does the idol face west?** It is a distinctive feature of Ambabai's image; tradition gives it special meaning — and it is why the evening sun can reach her during Kirnotsav.
