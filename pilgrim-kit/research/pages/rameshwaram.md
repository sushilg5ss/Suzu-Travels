## 2. Facts
- Names: Arulmigu Ramanathaswamy Temple, Rameswaram / रामेश्वरम् (रामनाथस्वामी) ज्योतिर्लिंग; Tamil "Rāmanātasvāmi Kōyil" — data (2026-10-08), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanathaswamy_Temple (as_of 2026-10-17)
- Place in lists: 11th in the Shiva Purana (Koti Rudra Samhita) order; 7th in the popular smaranam stotra ("सेतुबन्धे तु रामेशं") — FACT TRAP #3; say which list. The **southernmost** of the 12 — Wikipedia (2026-10-17)
- Location: Rameswaram, Pamban island, Ramanathapuram district, Tamil Nadu; on the sea-front of the Gulf of Mannar / Palk Strait (Agni Theertham); 9.2881, 79.3173 — Wikipedia (2026-10-17)
- Deity: Shiva as Ramanathaswamy (lingam), consort **Parvathavardhini**; also Vishalakshi, Vishnu, Ganesha shrines. Two lingams in the sanctum: **Ramalingam** (sand, made by Sita/Rama per tradition) and **Vishwalingam** (brought by Hanuman from Kailash) — Wikipedia + Incredible India (2026-10-17)
- Also: one of the **four Char Dham** of Adi Shankara's tradition (Badrinath N, Puri E, Dwarka W, Rameswaram S) — Wikipedia (2026-10-17); and one of the **276 Paadal Petra Sthalams** sung by the Nayanars (Appar, Sundarar, Sambandar) — Wikipedia. Revered by Shaivites, Vaishnavites and Smartas.
- Legend (own words, as tradition — Shiva Purana + temple tradition / Ramayana retellings): Before crossing to Lanka, Rama camped on this southern shore and prayed to Shiva for victory; according to the Shiva Purana, Shiva appeared, blessed him and agreed to remain here as Rameshwara, "the Lord whom Rama worships". A much-told temple tradition adds a second episode after the war: Rama wished to atone for slaying Ravana, a learned Brahmin, and sent Hanuman to Kailash for a linga. Hanuman was delayed, so at the auspicious hour Sita shaped a linga from sand and Rama consecrated it. When Hanuman arrived, Rama asked that his Vishwalingam be worshipped first, out of respect for his devotion — a custom the temple still keeps. It is also believed Rama's bridge to Lanka began near here. (≈130 words) — data legend_summary; Incredible India (2026-10-17)

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- "World's longest corridor" → Asia's longest (Incredible India) / longest among India's Hindu temples (Wikipedia) (#19).
- Spelling: Rameshwaram (Hindi/North) vs Rameswaram (official/TN) — same place; use both.
- Stotra order vs Shiva Purana order (#3): 7th vs 11th.
- Char Dham ≠ Uttarakhand Chhota Char Dham — disambiguate in one line, link `char-dham-yatra` (row 13) when live.
- Ram Setu / Adam's Bridge: tradition says Rama's bridge; geologists describe a chain of shoals — give both neutrally if mentioned; no political angle.
- Which linga first: Vishwalingam (Hanuman's) is worshipped first — not the Ramalingam.
- "Rameshwaram temple online booking" / "VIP darshan" sites are agencies; no official online darshan booking verified.
- Ticket fees and Spatika Linga time come only from travel sites → never print.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are the Rameswaram temple timings?** 5 am – 1 pm and 3 pm – 9 pm (Incredible India). Spatika Linga darshan is early morning — confirm locally.
2. **What are the 22 theerthams?** Twenty-two sacred wells inside the temple; pilgrims bathe in them in order after a sea dip at Agni Theertham, then go for darshan.
3. **Is the Agni Theertham dip compulsory?** It is the traditional first step, not a rule; many elders do darshan without the baths.
4. **Which linga is worshipped first at Rameswaram?** The Vishwalingam brought by Hanuman from Kailash, by Rama's wish, before the sand Ramalingam.
5. **Can I take my phone inside?** No — phones are restricted in Tamil Nadu temples; use the deposit counter.
6. **How do I reach Rameswaram?** Fly to Madurai (about 175 km) and drive, or take a train to Rameswaram station over the new Pamban bridge.
7. **Is Rameswaram one of the Char Dham?** Yes — the southern dham of the four (Badrinath, Puri, Dwarka, Rameswaram).
8. **How far is Dhanushkodi from the temple?** About 20–25 km by road to Arichal Munai, the island's tip.
