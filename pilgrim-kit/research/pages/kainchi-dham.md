## 2. Facts
- Names: Kainchi Dham — कैंची धाम; Shri Kainchi Hanuman Mandir and Ashram ("Neem Karoli Baba Ashram"); administered by **Shri Kainchi Hanuman Mandir and Asram Trust**, P.O. Kainchi Dham, Nainital 263132 — trust site title/description (2026-10-20). "Kainchi" = scissors, after the two hairpin bends of the road here (popular explanation — UNVERIFIED, say "is said to").
- Location: Kainchi village, **Nainital district**, Kumaon, **Uttarakhand**; on the **Nainital–Almora road**, beside the small **Shipra** stream — **29.4225, 79.5125** — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kainchi_Dham (2026-10-20). Altitude **UNVERIFIED** (~1,400 m often quoted — don't print).
- Deities (Wikipedia): **Hanuman** (main), Lakshmi-Narayan, Parvati, Ganesha, Kartikeya, Vindhyavasini Devi and a Shiva linga — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kainchi_Dham (2026-10-20).
- The saint: **Neem Karoli Baba** ("Maharaj-ji"), a Hanuman devotee in the bhakti tradition; born **Lakshman Narayan Sharma, c. 1900**, in Uttar Pradesh (Wikipedia gives both Akbarpur, Firozabad and Neem Karoli village, Farrukhabad — say "Uttar Pradesh"); named after Neem Karoli village (Farrukhabad district) where he spent his early years; **died 11 Sep 1973 in Vrindavan**, where his **samadhi** is — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neem_Karoli_Baba (2026-10-20). (The Taos ashram page says 10 Sep → print "September 1973".)
- Story (own words, as tradition — no scripture; ≈110 words): In 1962 Neem Karoli Baba stopped at this bend of the Shipra, where sadhus were said to have performed yagyas long before, and asked for a platform to be built. Two years later, in June 1964, a Hanuman temple was consecrated here, and the ashram grew around it. Devotees remember his teaching in a few simple words — love everyone, serve everyone, remember God, and feed people. He spent much of his last decade at Kainchi. Western seekers such as Ram Dass, who met him in 1967, carried his name abroad; decades later visitors from around the world still come. Every **15 June** the foundation day draws a huge fair. — data legend_summary + Wikipedia + Taos ashram page.
- History (dated):

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Samadhi** — Incredible India says his samadhi is in the main building at Kainchi. Wrong: the samadhi is in **Vrindavan**; Kainchi keeps an urn of his ashes.
- **Founding** — 15 Jun 1964 consecration (Wikipedia, local tradition) vs "murti installed 15 Jun 1976" (Taos page) → say "June 1964; 15 June is foundation day".
- **Death date** — 11 Sep (Wikipedia) vs 10 Sep 1973 (Taos) → "September 1973".
- **Birth name/place** — Lakshman (also seen "Lakshmi") Narayan Sharma; Akbarpur (Firozabad) vs Neem Karoli (Farrukhabad) → "born in Uttar Pradesh around 1900".
- **Distance from Nainital** — 17 km (Wikipedia/data) vs 38 km (Taos page).
- **"Registration is now compulsory / daily cap fixed"** — viral headlines (and the title of our own post 12444) — as of 20 Oct 2026 it is only a proposal awaiting state approval.
- **"Book a room at the ashram / online darshan booking"** — no such public booking; ashram not hosting visitors overnight; beware sites selling "Kainchi Dham darshan passes".
- Don't present miracles as fact; "devotees tell…" only. No health/wish-granting claims.
- Don't confuse the **Shipra** stream here with the Shipra (Kshipra) river at Ujjain.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Kainchi Dham timings?** About 6 am to 7 pm (Incredible India); confirm locally, especially on weekends and festival days.
2. **Is registration required for Kainchi Dham?** Not as of 20 Oct 2026. Online registration with a daily limit of about 20,000–25,000 is proposed and awaits state approval.
3. **How to reach Kainchi Dham?** By road on the Nainital–Almora highway: 9 km from Bhowali, about 17 km from Nainital. Nearest railway: Kathgodam (~38 km); nearest airport: Pantnagar.
4. **When is Kainchi Dham foundation day?** 15 June every year — the Hanuman temple was consecrated in June 1964. Expect shuttles and no private vehicles near the temple around the fair.
5. **Who was Neem Karoli Baba?** A Hanuman devotee and saint (c. 1900–1973) who founded the Kainchi ashram; he taught love, service and remembering God.
6. **Can we stay at Kainchi Dham ashram?** The ashram does not offer overnight stays to visitors; most stay in Bhowali, Bhimtal or Nainital.
7. **Is there an entry fee?** No.
8. **What is the best time to visit Kainchi Dham?** March–June and September–November, on a weekday morning; avoid long weekends and 13–16 June unless you're there for the fair.
