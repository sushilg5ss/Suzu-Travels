## 2. Facts
- Names: **Shri Amarnathji Yatra / श्री अमरनाथ यात्रा**; the cave **Amarnath Gufa / अमरनाथ गुफा**; deity **Baba Barfani (बाबा बर्फ़ानी)** — data.
- Location: holy cave in the Kashmir Himalaya, Jammu & Kashmir (UT), on the Anantnag/Ganderbal side; **~3,880–3,888 m** (trap 14 — print "about 3,880 m"). Coordinates UNVERIFIED (see §1).
- Deity form: a **naturally forming ice lingam** of Shiva inside the cave, with smaller ice forms that devotees identify as Parvati and Ganesha (tradition; data). Size varies every year — never promise its height.
- Legend (own words, as tradition — popular retelling linked to the Amar Katha; Bhringish Samhita/Amarnath Mahatmya are cited by tradition, UNVERIFIED which text): Parvati asked Shiva why he alone was beyond death. He agreed to reveal the **Amar Katha**, the secret of immortality, but only where no living creature could hear. On the way he left his bull **Nandi at Pahalgam**, the moon at **Chandanwari**, his serpents at **Sheshnag**, Ganesha at **Mahagunas** and the five elements at **Panchtarni**. Inside the cave a pair of **pigeons**, hidden from sight, overheard the story and became immortal — pilgrims still look for them. Folklore credits a Muslim shepherd, **Buta Malik**, with rediscovering the cave when a sadhu's gift of coal turned to gold; his family's link to the shrine is part of local lore. (~120 words; data legend_summary.)
- Some Shakta lists also place a Shakti Peeth (Mahamaya, **throat**) at Amarnath — Wikipedia Shakta pithas table, 16 Oct 2026 — tradition, not in every list.
- **Season & dates**
  - 2026: **3 Jul – 28 Aug 2026 (57 days)**, pratham puja 29 Jun (Jyeshtha Purnima); advance registration from **15 Apr 2026** — data / News On AIR 15 Apr 2026.
  - 2026 disruptions: weather suspension from **19 Jul 2026** (IANS headline, ianslive.in 18 Jul — not opened); **public yatra suspended indefinitely from 10 Aug 2026** — Baltal and Pahalgam tracks damaged, heavy-rain advisory, registration closed, pilgrims at base camps and Bhagwati Nagar asked to return — Telangana Today/IANS 10 Aug 2026; **formal conclusion 28 Aug 2026** with Chhari Mubarak (Mahant Deependra Giri) at the cave; **4.8 lakh+** pilgrims had darshan — News On AIR 28 Aug 2026.
  - **2027: not announced yet** (searched 16 Oct 2026; no SASB/LG announcement). Pattern: SASB announces in spring, registration ~mid-April, yatra starts late Jun/early Jul and ends on **Raksha Bandhan (Shravan Purnima) = 17 Aug 2027 per panchang** (calendardate.com — pattern, not an announcement) → print "expected to end on Raksha Bandhan, 17 Aug 2027 — dates not announced yet".
- **Registration (official, every pilgrim)** — News On AIR 15 Apr 2026 + data:
  - Advance registration online through the Shri Amarnathji Shrine Board (SASB) or at **554 designated bank branches** of J&K Bank, PNB, SBI and Yes Bank (2026), first-come-first-served **daily quota per route**.
  - **Compulsory Health Certificate (CHC)** from an SASB-authorised doctor/institution, issued in the season window (2026: on/after 8 Apr 2026); SASB lists authorised doctors in 37 states/UTs.
  - **Age 13–70**; women **more than 6 weeks pregnant** not allowed.
  - **RFID card** for every pilgrim, collected **in person** at J&K centres (e.g. Jammu railway station, Bhagwati Nagar, Udhampur, Banihal, Lakhanpur — data, UNVERIFIED for 2027) and worn throughout; limited on-the-spot registration in Jammu.
  - 2026: group accident insurance for registered pilgrims (sum assured stated in rupees by AIR — **do not print amounts**; say "group accident insurance for registered yatris").
  - Fee: not stored (each season's notice) → "see the SASB notice".

- **Routes** (data; trap 7):
  - **Pahalgam route** (traditional, gentler, longer): Pahalgam → Chandanwari 16 km by road → Pissu Top → **Sheshnag** (12 km) → **Mahagunas Top (~4,270 m)** → **Panchtarni** (10.6 km) → Sangam → Holy Cave (6 km). Trek ≈ **32 km** from Chandanwari (≈ 48 km from Pahalgam), **3–4 days** up.
  - **Baltal route** (short, steep): Baltal → Domail → Barari → Sangam → Holy Cave ≈ **14 km**; up and back in 1–2 days.
- Road distances (OSRM, 16 Oct 2026, "about"): Jammu → Pahalgam ~223 km · Srinagar → Pahalgam ~98 km · Srinagar → Baltal ~92 km (Baltal point UNVERIFIED) · Jammu → Baltal ~326 km. Travel from Jammu is in **SASB/security convoys** from the Bhagwati Nagar base camp (data).
- How to reach: **Air** — Srinagar airport (nearest for both base camps); Jammu airport for the Jammu start. **Rail** — Jammu Tawi (convoy start; RFID counter at the station per data); Kashmir rail link details UNVERIFIED for yatra use. **Road** — convoys to Nunwan/Pahalgam and Baltal base camps.
- **On-route options:** ponies, palkis, pithoos (registered service providers, data). **Helicopter: none in 2026** — Baltal and Pahalgam routes declared **no-fly zones**, helicopter operations suspended (Zee News 3 Jun 2026; IANS 10 Aug 2026 "area beyond base camps is a no-fly zone"), as in 2025 (trap 5). 2027: not announced.
- Rules & safety: carry permit + RFID + CHC + photo ID; acclimatise, no alcohol/smoking; dress for sub-zero nights and sudden rain/snow; weather causes frequent suspensions — keep 1–2 buffer days (data + 2026 events). Strict security protocols on the route (2026 QR-coded IDs reported by Outlook headline — UNVERIFIED, not opened).
- Difficulty: **hard** — cave ~3,880 m, pass above 4,200 m, steep exposed tracks (data). Elders: within the 70 upper age limit, CHC required; ponies/palkis help; Baltal is short but steep, Pahalgam longer but gentler.
- Best time: early in the window (July) when the ice lingam is usually fullest; weather volatile throughout (data).
- What to carry (data): permit, RFID, CHC, ID; waterproof shell, down jacket, gloves, cap; grippy trekking shoes, stick; medicines, dry fruit, water bottle.
- Nearby: Pahalgam (Betaab/Aru valleys), Sonamarg near Baltal, Srinagar; Vaishno Devi (Katra) on the way from Jammu → `vaishno-devi-yatra`; Kashmir add-ons via the PKG card. Distances to these not stored (UNVERIFIED).

## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **No helicopter in 2026** (no-fly zone). Blogs and WhatsApp sellers still advertise Baltal/Pahalgam–Panjtarni helicopter "packages" (amar nath helicopter booking 4,400/mo, Apr–Jun 12k–15k → scam target). Never present helicopters as available for 2027 until SASB announces.
- **2026 did not run the full 57 days for pilgrims** — public movement stopped 10 Aug (track damage); 28 Aug was the formal Chhari Mubarak close. Many sites just say "3 Jul–28 Aug".
- **Distances:** "48 km" includes the 16 km road to Chandanwari; trek ≈ 32 km; Baltal ≈ 14 km (trap 7).
- **Altitude:** 3,880 vs 3,888 m (trap 14).
- **Not a Jyotirlinga.** Often wrongly bundled in "12 Jyotirlinga" lists.
- 2027 "dates" on blogs (e.g. hindutone-type pages) are guesses — print "not announced yet".
- jksasb.nic.in stale banners (trap 6); look-alike "registration" sites/agents charging fees — only SASB online and designated bank branches.
- Ice lingam size/"melted early" stories change yearly — don't promise.
- Buta Malik story: folklore; keep respectful, no communal framing.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **When will Amarnath Yatra 2027 start?** Not announced yet. The Shrine Board usually announces in spring; the yatra normally begins late June/early July and ends on Raksha Bandhan (17 Aug 2027 by panchang).
2. **When does Amarnath registration open?** Usually mid-April (15 Apr in 2026), online with SASB and at designated bank branches.
3. **What is the age limit?** 13 to 70 years; women more than six weeks pregnant cannot go.
4. **Is a medical certificate needed?** Yes — a Compulsory Health Certificate from an SASB-authorised doctor, issued in that season's window.
5. **Baltal or Pahalgam — which is easier?** Baltal is short (~14 km) but steep; Pahalgam is longer (~32 km trek from Chandanwari, 3–4 days) but more gradual.
6. **Is there a helicopter to Amarnath?** Not in 2026 — the routes were a no-fly zone. Check the SASB notice for 2027 and never pay unofficial sellers.
7. **How high is the Amarnath cave?** About 3,880 m above sea level.
8. **Is Amarnath a Jyotirlinga?** No. It is a cave shrine with a natural ice lingam; some Shakta lists count it as a Shakti Peeth.
