## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Three claimants** (state neutrally, data `disputes`): Nageshwar near Dwarka (most widely accepted; Shiva Purana's Darukavana "by the western sea"); **Aundha Nagnath**, Hingoli, Maharashtra (Maharashtra tradition, reading Shankaracharya's stotra line on "Sadanga" as Aundha); **Jageshwar**, Almora, Uttarakhand (Daruka-vana read as deodar forest). Never call the others "fake".
- **Supriya is a man** (a vaishya merchant) in the Shiva Purana; Gujarat Tourism's page writes "her chanting" — don't copy.
- "First Jyotirlinga" (Gujarat Tourism) — Somnath is first in both standard lists; Nageshwar is 10th (Shiva Purana) — FACT TRAP #3.
- Statue: seated, ~25 m — not "reclining", not "world's tallest".
- Timings: no official schedule; three different private versions online.
- Nageshwar (Dwarka) ≠ Nageshwar Nath, Ayodhya; ≠ Nagnath temples elsewhere.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **Where is Nageshwar Jyotirlinga?** On the Dwarka–Okha road in Devbhumi Dwarka district, Gujarat, about 16–18 km from Dwarka town.
2. **Which is the real Nageshwar Jyotirlinga?** Dwarka's is the most widely accepted; Maharashtra honours Aundha Nagnath and some honour Jageshwar in Uttarakhand — traditions differ.
3. **What are Nageshwar temple timings?** There's no official published schedule; it opens early morning and closes at night with an afternoon break — confirm locally.
4. **How do I go from Dwarka to Nageshwar?** By taxi, auto or local bus on the Okha road, about 25–35 minutes.
5. **How tall is the Shiva statue at Nageshwar?** About 25 m (roughly 80 ft), a seated Shiva in the temple garden.
6. **What is the story of Nageshwar?** The Shiva Purana tells how Shiva appeared as light to free his devotee Supriya from the demon Daruka and stayed as Lord of the Serpents.
7. **Can I do Dwarkadhish, Nageshwar and Bet Dwarka in one day?** Yes, they're on one road; Sudarshan Setu (Feb 2024) links Okha to Bet Dwarka.
8. **Which airport is nearest?** Porbandar (about 107 km from Dwarka) or Jamnagar (about 126 km); Dwarka railway station is 16–18 km away.
