## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **How do I book Tirupati darshan?** Only on TTD's official portal ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in when monthly quotas open; free Sarva darshan needs no booking but can take many hours.
2. **How long is the free darshan wait?** In October 2026 TTD reported roughly 6 to 18 hours for Sarva darshan without a time-slot token, longest at weekends.
3. **Is there a dress code?** Yes — traditional Indian dress is expected for darshan; check TTD's latest list.
4. **Can I walk up to Tirumala?** Yes — Alipiri (about 9 km, open round the clock) or the shorter Srivari Mettu path (daytime); confirm current gate timings with TTD.
5. **How far is Tirumala from Tirupati station/airport?** About 23 km from Tirupati station and 40 km from Renigunta airport by the ghat road.
6. **Why do pilgrims offer hair?** Tradition links offerings to Lord Venkateswara's wedding loan from Kubera; tonsure is a vow of humility — tens of thousands are offered daily.
7. **When is Vaikunta Dwara Darshan 2026?** Around Vaikuntha Ekadashi in late December; TTD has not announced the 2026–27 schedule yet.
8. **Where can I get Tirupati laddu?** At TTD's laddu counters on Tirumala after darshan (and TTD outlets) — buy only from TTD.
