- Legend (own words, as tradition, ≈130 words): When **Sati** gave up her life at her father **Daksha's** sacrifice, a grief-struck **Shiva** wandered the worlds carrying her body, and **Vishnu's Sudarshana chakra** divided it so the dance of grief would end. Tradition holds that her **yoni** fell on **Nilachal hill**, making Kamakhya the seat of Devi's creative power. A second story kept by the temple says **Kamadeva**, burnt to ashes by Shiva's third eye, regained his form here and built the first shrine with **Vishwakarma** — hence the name Kamakhya and the region **Kamarupa**. The hill also carries the memory of **King Naraka**, remembered as the earliest ruler of the Brahmaputra valley. — Paraphrased from maakamakhya.org/history and data/shaktipeeth.json (2026-11-01). ## 3. Disputes & fact traps
- **Kamakhya does NOT close during Navratri** — only for Ambubachi (June). (shaktipeeth-notes trap #15.)
- 2026 closure was **23–25 June** (not 22–25); Prabritti began 22 June 9:26 pm.
- Ambubachi 2027 dates — not announced; never copy "2027" dates from agency sites.
- Official notice typo "25th June 2022" — don't propagate.
- "No idol" is correct; don't call the stone a statue; don't describe it graphically.
- Naranarayan 1565 / Kalapahar destruction — popular but not on the official page; keep generic.
- VIP/coupon portals: only the three links on maakamakhya.org; the "Special Coupon Booking portal" is in testing — warn against unofficial sellers.
- Kamakhya ≠ Kamakshi (Kanchipuram) — different Devi, different state.
- No Tantric sensationalism (black magic, "vashikaran") — none on page.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Kamakhya temple timings?** Doors open 8 am–1 pm and 2:30–5:15 pm on the regular schedule; aarti at 7:30 pm. On festival days it opens at 9 am and closes at sunset (official site).
2. **When is Kamakhya temple closed?** For three days of Ambubachi in June — in 2026 the doors stayed shut 23–25 June and reopened on the morning of 26 June.
3. **When is Ambubachi Mela 2027?** Not announced yet; it usually begins around 22 June and the temple reopens about four days later.
4. **Is there an idol in Kamakhya temple?** No. A natural, spring-fed stone in the shape of a yoni is worshipped in the sanctum.
5. **How do I book special or VIP darshan?** Only online through the links on maakamakhya.org (counter sales stopped in June 2026); the general queue is free and can also be booked there.
6. **Why is Kamakhya a Shakti Peeth?** Tradition holds that Sati's yoni fell here; it is one of the four Adi Peethas and is in the 18 and 51 lists.
7. **How far is Kamakhya from Guwahati station and airport?** About 8 km from Guwahati station, 7 km from Kamakhya Junction and 24 km from the airport.
8. **Which temple should I visit with Kamakhya?** Umananda, the Bhairava shrine on an island in the Brahmaputra, and Bhuvaneshwari temple on top of Nilachal hill.
