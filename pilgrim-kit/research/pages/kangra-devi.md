## 2. Facts
- Names: Shri Bajreshwari / Brajeshwari / Vajreshwari Devi Temple; locally **Kangra Devi / Kangrewali Mata**; Devanagari **श्री बज्रेश्वरी देवी मंदिर, कांगड़ा** (also ब्रजेश्वरी) — data + https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bajreshwari_Mata_Temple,_Kangra (2026-10-15). Name from *vajra* (thunderbolt) + *ishwari* (goddess) — Wikipedia.
- Location: **Nagarkot** area of **Kangra town**, Kangra district, Himachal Pradesh, near Kangra Fort; **32.10183, 76.26987** (32°06′07″N 76°16′12″E); elevation ~738 m — Wikipedia (2026-10-15). District page: overlooks the Banganga and Manjhi rivers — https://hpkangra.nic.in/?p=33871 (2026-10-15).
- Deity: Devi as **Vajreshwari**, the Durga who wields the thunderbolt, worshipped as a **pindi**; a small **Bhairav** temple in the complex; an image of **Dhyanu Bhagat** (devotee who, by tradition, offered his head to the Devi in Akbar's time) faces the main shrine — trust page https://kangratemples.hp.gov.in/about-bajreshwariji/ (2026-10-15).
- Shakti Peeth: **classical 51-peeth table includes it — Sati's left breast** is believed to have fallen here (trust, district, Incredible India, Wikipedia agree); Bhairav listed as Abhiru in a compiled table (data). One of Himachal's five major Devi shrines and of the Saat Behna / Nau Devi.
- Legend (own words, as tradition — Devi Bhagavata / Shakta tradition): After Sati gave up her life at Daksha's yajna, Shiva wandered with her body; Vishnu's Sudarshan chakra divided it, and wherever a part fell a Shakti Peeth arose. At Nagarkot, the old name of Kangra, her left breast is said to have fallen. Local tradition adds that the Devi appeared in a dream to the Pandavas and asked them to build her a shrine at Nagarkot, and that she slew a demon (Kalikala, per Wikipedia) with a thunderbolt — hence Vajreshwari. Each Makar Sankranti the priests cover the pindi in butter, recalling how the Devi's battle wounds were soothed with ghee after she defeated Mahishasura. (~120 words) — trust + Wikipedia + Tribune.
- History (state plainly, no communal framing): once famed for its wealth; **Mahmud of Ghazni plundered it, from 1009 CE** (trust: "at least five times") · district page: Firoz Shah Tughlaq destroyed it in **1360**, **Akbar** restored it · **4 April 1905 Kangra earthquake** razed it (date of quake = general knowledge, UNVERIFIED today — print "the 1905 Kangra earthquake") · rebuilt — trust says "within a year", Incredible India says **1920**, district says "1920s, with donations incl. from Amritsar devotees" → print "rebuilt in the years after, completed around 1920". Main gate has a **Nagarkhana** (drum house); stone wall around the complex like a fort — trust (2026-10-15).
- Darshan & aarti — **official trust page https://kangratemples.hp.gov.in/shri-bajreshwari-mata-temple/ (2026-10-15)**, use this table and add "confirm locally" (trust's own 'About' page gives slightly different times, §3):
  - **Winter: 5:30 am – 12:00 noon, bhog break 12:00–12:30, 12:30 – 8:00 pm.**
  - **Summer: 5:00 am – 12:00 noon, bhog break 12:00–12:30, 12:30 – 9:00 pm.**
  - Aarti winter: Mangal 5:45–6:00 am; morning 6–7 am; evening (with shayan) 6:30–7:30 pm. Summer: Mangal 5:00–5:15 am; morning ~5:00–6:15 am; evening 7–8 pm.
  - Changeover date summer/winter not stated → "confirm locally".
  - **Langar** free, 12:30–2:30 pm and 7:30–9 pm (Navratri: afternoon to night); ~700–1,200 served daily (trust).
  - Online: darshan parchi (sign-in), havan booking via temple office; e-pooja "coming soon" — **don't print prasad/photo prices**; link only kangratemples.hp.gov.in.
- Festivals:
  - **Makar Sankranti — Ghrit Mandal Parv** (butter festival): pindi covered with butter (reported 21 quintals in 2025; 2,500 kg in 2019), kept about **one week**, then distributed as prasad (believed to help skin/joint ailments — say "believed"); jagran, Shobha Yatra through Kangra bazaar — The Tribune 15 Jan 2025 + 15 Jan 2019. **Makar Sankranti 2027 = Fri 15 Jan 2027** (timeanddate); 2027 fair schedule **not announced yet**.
  - **Navratri**: Sharad 2026 runs **11–19 Oct** (Dussehra 20 Oct); **Chaitra 2027 begins 7 Apr 2027** — data.
- Planned works: HP govt preparing a **master plan** for Brajeshwari (with specialised agencies); online langar/darshan in phases; aarti at **Van Ganga** ordered — The Tribune 16 Nov 2025. Say "upgrades are planned"; no timelines.

## 4. Questions people ask (short answers)
1. **What are Kangra Devi temple timings?** Per the temple trust: summer 5 am–9 pm, winter 5:30 am–8 pm, closed 12–12:30 pm for bhog; confirm locally.
2. **Is Kangra Devi a Shakti Peeth?** Yes — tradition holds that Sati's left breast fell here; it is in the classical 51-peeth lists.
3. **Is Brajeshwari the same as Kangra Devi?** Yes — Bajreshwari, Brajeshwari, Vajreshwari and Kangrewali Mata are one temple in Kangra town.
4. **What is the butter festival at Kangra Devi?** Ghrit Mandal Parv: at Makar Sankranti (15 Jan 2027) the pindi is covered in butter for about a week, then shared as prasad.
5. **How far is Kangra Devi from Jwala Ji and Chamunda?** About 35 km to Jwala Ji and about 22 km to Chamunda.
6. **Nearest airport and railway?** Gaggal (Kangra) airport ~8 km; Kangra on the narrow-gauge line; Pathankot (~90 km) for broad gauge.
7. **Is there free food?** Yes, a free langar 12:30–2:30 pm and 7:30–9 pm.
8. **Who built the temple?** By tradition the Pandavas; the present structure was rebuilt after the 1905 Kangra earthquake, around 1920.
