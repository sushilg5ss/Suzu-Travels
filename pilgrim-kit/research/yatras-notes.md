### Expected Char Dham 2026 closings (traditional festivals only, NOT official)

The temple committees set and announce the actual dates and times.

- **Vijayadashami 2026 is Tue 20 Oct.** Badrinath's closing date is fixed that day at Badrinath. Kedarnath's and Yamunotri's dates are usually confirmed then too.
- **Gangotri** closes on Annakut/Govardhan Puja. That falls on **10 Nov 2026** per Delhi panchang (haindavam.org).
- **Yamunotri and Kedarnath** close on Bhai Dooj. That falls on **11 Nov 2026** per Delhi panchang (dekhopanchang.com).
- **Conflict:** some calendars (goodreturns.in) give Govardhan Puja as 9 Nov and Bhai Dooj as 10 Nov. Drik Panchang's default page showed a US location. Do not publish either set as "official".
- **Badrinath** usually closes about 2 to 3 weeks after Bhai Dooj (2025: 25 Nov). The 2026 date is unknown until 20 Oct.

### 2027 openings: none announced

- Kedarnath: date is fixed on Mahashivratri 2027.

## Fact traps (widely copied errors)

1. **Recycled 2025 closing dates.** In 2025 Kedarnath and Yamunotri closed on **23 Oct**, Gangotri on **22 Oct** and Badrinath on **25 Nov 2025**. Many blogs still show these, or show 2026 "closing dates" as if they were final. As of 8 Oct 2026, **none of the 2026 Char Dham closing dates is officially announced.**
2. **Badrinath 2026 opening: 23 Apr, not 24 Apr.** Some aggregators (e.g., triptotemples) list 24 April. The announcement made at Narendranagar on Basant Panchami said **23 Apr, 6:15 AM**, and news reports confirm the temple opened on 23 Apr.
3. **Festival-date drift.** Panchang sites disagree by a day on Govardhan Puja and Bhai Dooj 2026, and also on Makar Sankranti 2027 (14 or 15 Jan). Rath Yatra 2026 was also shown as both 15 and 16 Jul. Always use the temple or mela authority announcement.
4. **Fake helicopter booking.** For Kedarnath and Hemkund Sahib, **heliyatra.irctc.co.in is the only official booking site.** It needs your Char Dham registration number. Each user ID can book 2 tickets of up to 6 passengers. Police, I4C and the Uttarakhand STF have repeatedly warned about cloned sites, "discount" packages and WhatsApp/Facebook sellers (Tribune 2026-05-09). Vaishno Devi helicopters are booked only on online.maavaishnodevi.org.
5. **Amarnath helicopters in 2026.** Many blogs still advertise Baltal/Pahalgam to Panjtarni helicopter packages. In **2026 the routes were a no-fly zone and helicopter services were suspended** (Zee News 2026-06-03), as in 2025.
6. **Amarnath age limits:** **13 to 70 years.** Women more than **6 weeks** pregnant are barred. The Compulsory Health Certificate must be issued within the season's window (2026: after 8 Apr 2026) by an SASB-authorised doctor. The RFID card is collected in person. jksasb.nic.in's home page still showed 2024 content when checked, so do not quote dates from its stale banners.
7. **Amarnath distances.** "Pahalgam route 48 km" includes the 16 km motorable Pahalgam to Chandanwari stretch. The trek itself is about 32 km. Baltal is about 14 km.
8. **Kailash Mansarovar.** The MEA yatra is by computerised draw only, through kmy.gov.in. It is open to Indian citizens aged 18 to 70 with a BMI of 25 or less; OCI holders and foreigners are not eligible. It takes place in Tibet (China). **Suzu cannot sell it**, so present it as information only. Private Nepal-route tours are a different product.
9. **Adi Kailash age range and permits.** Age 10 to 70 is reported. In-person verification at SDM Dharchula is compulsory. Fake permit and CSC scams have been flagged. Monsoon suspension began in early July 2026.
10. **Himachal yatras can be cancelled at short notice.** Shrikhand was **suspended in 2026**. Kinner Kailash was suspended, then rescheduled to 30 Jul to 10 Aug. Manimahesh's Kugti route was closed in 2026 and the yatra now needs an e-pass. Never sell fixed departures before the DC's notification.
11. **Char Dham registration is free.** Any site charging a "registration fee" is not official. The only official channels are registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in, the Tourist Care Uttarakhand app, WhatsApp, and helpline 1364.
12. **Tungnath** is the world's highest Shiva temple, at about 3,680 m. **Kedarnath** is not.
13. **Kumbh confusion.** The next Kumbh-type event is **Haridwar Ardh Kumbh 2027** (14 Jan to 20 Apr 2027). Prayagraj's next is **Ardh Kumbh 2031**; dates are not announced. Prayagraj's annual **Magh Mela 2027** is not a Kumbh.
14. **Altitudes vary by source.** Amarnath cave is 3,880 to 3,888 m. Kedarnath is about 3,583 m, Hemkund about 4,632 m, and Shrikhand about 5,227 m (~18,570 ft per FPJ; some sources say 5,155 m).

## Gaps and items to re-verify

- **Char Dham closing dates and times:** re-check after **20 Oct 2026** (BKTC, Gangotri and Yamunotri temple committees; Amar Ujala, Hindustan, TOI Dehradun).
- **Panch Kedar 2026:** opening and closing dates for Tungnath, Madhyamaheshwar and Rudranath were not verified.
- **Adi Kailash:** whether permits resumed after the 2026 monsoon is not verified.
- **Kinner Kailash:** the registration portal (logdest.com/kk/register) is only reported by Travel Trade Journal and is not a .gov.in domain. Verify with DC Kinnaur.
- **Magh Mela 2027:** dates are panchang-derived (HappyFares blog). They are cross-checked against the officially announced Haridwar Ardh Kumbh 2027 bathing festivals, which share the same festival days. The official UP Mela schedule is still awaited.
- **Puri Rath Yatra 2027:** 5 Jul 2027 is panchang-derived; the temple administration has not announced it yet.
- **Fares:** helicopter fares (Kedarnath, Vaishno Devi) and the Amarnath registration fee are not stored, because each season's official fare notice controls them.
