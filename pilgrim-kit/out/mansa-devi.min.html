Where is Mansa Devi temple in Panchkula and what are its timings?

Mata Mansa Devi Temple stands in the Shivalik foothills at Bilaspur village, Panchkula district, Haryana — about 10 km from the Chandigarh bus terminus. Built in 1811–1815 by Raja Gopal Singh of Mani Majra, it is run by a Shrine Board. Darshan is 4 am–10 pm in summer and 5 am–9 pm in winter. It is not the hill temple in Haridwar.

Shri Mata Mansa Devi Mandir — श्री माता मनसा देवी मंदिर — is the best-loved Devi shrine of the Chandigarh tricity. Mansa Devi is worshipped as a form of Shakti, the goddess who knows the wishes (mansa) of the mind. In the sanctum she is worshipped not as a large idol but as three pindis — natural stones.

Not the Haridwar temple. There are two famous Mansa Devi temples. This one is in Panchkula, Haryana, on the Shivalik foothills, and vehicles reach the complex. The other is on Bilwa Parvat above Haridwar, Uttarakhand, reached by a ropeway. The traditional Nau Devi Yatra visits the Panchkula temple.

The legend

The story of Mansa Devi

A cow, three stones and a devoted raja — the hill's story as the Shrine Board and local tradition tell it.

  1. Milk on three stonesLong ago, it is said, a cow climbed this Shivalik hill every day and let its milk flow over three stones lying side by side.
  2. Sati's foreheadVillagers began to worship the stones. In local tradition, kept by the Shrine Board, the three pindis are linked to the forehead of Sati.
  3. A raja's tunnelRaja Gopal Singh of Mani Majra was so devoted that, legend says, he came to the goddess every day with his queen through a tunnel about 3 km long from his fort.
  4. The temple risesHe built the present temple between 1811 and 1815. In 1840 Maharaja Karam Singh of Patiala added a Shiva temple close by, the Patiala Shivalaya.

After the princely states merged, royal patronage ended and the shrine was neglected for years. In 1991 Haryana passed the Shri Mata Mansa Devi Shrine Act, and a Shrine Board has run the complex since — about 100 acres of temples, courtyards and pilgrim facilities. Look for the fine 19th-century wall paintings in the temple complex.

How to reach

How to reach Mansa Devi temple, Panchkula

By road

About 4 km from Panchkula bus stand and about 10 km from the Chandigarh bus terminus. Vehicles reach the temple complex; Delhi is about 272 km away.

By rail

Chandigarh railway station is about 13 km away; Kalka about 25 km.

By air

Chandigarh airport is about 19 km by road.

Nearby Devis

Chandi Mandir is about 10 km away and Kali Mata Mandir, Kalka about 25 km; Naina Devi in Bilaspur is about 110 km.

Before you go

Before you go

  1. Easy for eldersOne of the easiest Devi shrines to visit: close to the city, with vehicles reaching the complex. Avoid the Ashtami and Navami crowds of Navratri with elders.
  2. Go early in NavratriQueues build from the morning aarti. Weekday mornings outside the melas are the calmest.
  3. Respect the shrineDress modestly, remove footwear and follow the Shrine Board's instructions on photography.
  4. Use only the official siteFor notices, donations or prasad, use the Shrine Board's own website, mansadevi.org.in — beware of look-alike sites.

FAQ

Questions pilgrims ask

What are Mansa Devi temple Panchkula timings?

In summer 4 am to 10 pm, in winter 5 am to 9 pm, as published by the Shrine Board. Bhog is offered from 11 to 11:15 am. The changeover dates are not published, so confirm locally.

What is the Mansa Devi aarti time?

Summer: 4:30–5 am and 7–7:30 pm. Winter: 5:30–6 am and 6–6:30 pm (Shrine Board).

Is Mansa Devi Panchkula a Shakti Peeth?

Local tradition and the Shrine Board link the three pindis to Sati's forehead, but the temple is not in the classical lists of 51 Shakti Peeths. It is revered as one of the Nau Devi goddesses.

Who built Mansa Devi temple?

Raja Gopal Singh of Mani Majra built the present temple between 1811 and 1815. The nearby Patiala Shivalaya was added in 1840 by Maharaja Karam Singh of Patiala.

How far is Mansa Devi from Chandigarh?

About 10 km from the Chandigarh bus terminus, about 13 km from Chandigarh railway station and about 19 km from the airport.

Is Mansa Devi Panchkula the same as Mansa Devi Haridwar?

No. The Panchkula temple is in Haryana's Shivalik foothills and vehicles reach it. The Haridwar temple is on Bilwa Parvat in Uttarakhand and is reached by a ropeway.

When is the Navratri mela at Mansa Devi?

Twice a year, for the nine days of Chaitra and Ashvin Navratri. The next mela begins with Chaitra Navratri on 7 April 2027.

Which Mansa Devi is in the Nau Devi Yatra?

The Panchkula temple. It is one of the nine Devis of the traditional yatra, usually visited early in the route.
