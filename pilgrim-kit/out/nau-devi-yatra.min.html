What is the Nau Devi Yatra?

The Nau Devi Yatra (9 Devi Yatra) is a road pilgrimage to nine Devi temples of the North Indian hills: Shakumbhari, Mansa Devi (Panchkula), Kali Mata of Kalka, Naina Devi, Chintpurni, Jwala Ji, Brajeshwari (Kangra), Chamunda and Vaishno Devi. All nine take about 9 days from Delhi; the shorter package route of seven Devis takes about 7 days from Chandigarh.

Hindi pamphlets and family stories call the hill goddesses Saat Behna, the seven sisters, with Mata Vaishno Devi as the eldest. Over time the seven grew to nine — Kali Mata of Kalka and Shakumbhari Devi were added — so that a family could take one darshan for each night of Navratri. That is the yatra this guide follows, with the common package variant explained below.

Five of the nine are in Himachal and also make up our 5 Shakti Peeths of Himachal circuit. For the wider sacred map, see the pilgrimage hub.

The legend

The nine Devis and their stories

No single legend links all nine shrines. Each temple keeps its own story — here they are in our own words, as tradition tells them.

  1. The fall of SatiWhen Shiva carried Sati's body across the worlds, Vishnu's chakra divided it. Tradition places her eyes at Naina Devi, her tongue at Jwala Ji and her left breast at Brajeshwari in Kangra.
  2. The seven sistersIn the hills the Devis are spoken of as seven sisters — Saat Behna — with Mata Vaishno Devi, in her cave on Trikuta hill, as the eldest.
  3. The Mother of herbsThe Devi Bhagavata tells of Shakumbhari, the hundred-eyed Mother, whose tears ended a hundred-year famine and who fed the world with vegetables and herbs.
  4. Kali of Kalka, Chamunda and ChintpurniKali of Kalka is believed to have destroyed the demons who drove the gods into the hills; Chamunda slew Chanda and Munda; and at Chintpurni the Devi is said to have freed her devotee Mai Das of all worry.

Only Naina Devi, Jwala Ji and Brajeshwari appear in the compiled lists of the 51 Shakti Peeths, and Chintpurni's place is disputed. Vaishno Devi, Mansa Devi, Chamunda, Kalka and Shakumbhari are deeply revered shrines by their own traditions — we say so plainly rather than call all nine 'peethas'.

Navratri is the most auspicious time for the nine Devis — and the most crowded. Start each day early, book stays ahead, and keep a spare day. The Vaishno Devi Shrine Board asks pilgrims not to trek during weather alerts.

Route

Nau Devi Yatra route, with road distances

The classic order runs from the Shivalik foothills of UP and Haryana up into Himachal's Kangra valley, and ends at Trikuta hill in Jammu. Distances are by road.

Nine Devis across four states and union territories. The order below saves backtracking; the reverse works too — start at Vaishno Devi from Jammu (Katra is about 50 km away) and end at Shakumbhari.

  1. ~210 km
    Delhi → Shakumbhari Deviशाकुम्भरी देवी

    North to Behat in Saharanpur district; darshan at Bhura Dev first.

  2. ~156 km
    Shakumbhari → Mansa Devi, Panchkulaमनसा देवी

    Across the Yamuna to Panchkula, next to Chandigarh.

  3. ~25 km
    Mansa Devi → Kali Mata, Kalkaकाली माता

    A short drive — both are easy on the same day.

  4. ~110 km
    Panchkula → Naina Deviनैना देवी

    Into Himachal via Anandpur Sahib; cable car to the hilltop.

  5. ~112 km
    Naina Devi → Chintpurniचिंतपूर्णी

    Down to Una and up to Chhaproh.

  6. ~31 km
    Chintpurni → Jwala Jiज्वाला जी

    The shortest hill leg.

  7. ~36 km
    Jwala Ji → Brajeshwari, Kangraब्रजेश्वरी

    Baglamukhi at Bankhandi is on the way if you want a tenth darshan.

  8. ~22 km
    Kangra → Chamundaचामुंडा

    Below the Dhauladhar, near Dharamshala.

  9. ~241 km
    Chamunda → Katra (Vaishno Devi)वैष्णो देवी

    Via Pathankot to Katra; the trek is about 12 km each way. Katra back to Delhi is about 649 km.

Road distances from OpenStreetMap routing, 8 Oct 2026. Real hill driving takes 30–60% longer than map estimates — allow extra time.

How to reach

How to start and end the Nau Devi Yatra

From Delhi

Drive north to Shakumbhari (about 210 km) and follow the route above; return from Katra by road, train or air from Jammu (a Delhi to Jammu taxi covers the same NH44 drive in one day).

From Chandigarh

Mansa Devi and Kalka are on the city's doorstep, and Naina Devi is about 100 km away — the usual start for the 7-day package route.

By air

Chandigarh airport for the start, Gaggal (Kangra) airport near Brajeshwari and Chamunda, and Jammu airport for Vaishno Devi.

By rail

Kalka, Anandpur Sahib (for Naina Devi), Pathankot for Kangra, and Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra station at the end.

From Bilaspur

Suzu Travels is based in Bilaspur district, about 64 km from Naina Devi — we plan the Himachal leg of this yatra every Navratri.

Before you go

Before you go

  1. Vaishno Devi registrationThe free RFID Yatra Access Card is compulsory — one per pilgrim, from the Katra counters or the only official site, online.maavaishnodevi.org. Start the climb within the time limit printed on your card (recent reports differ, so check the Shrine Board). Beware of fake booking sites.
  2. Vaishno Devi helicopterThe helicopter flies Katra–Sanjichhat, not to the Bhawan; book only through the Shrine Board portal. Battery cars and the Bhawan–Bhairon ropeway also run.
  3. Chintpurni parchiTake the darshan slip from the temple guard and use only the official queue — no back-door darshan. Please don't feed the monkeys.
  4. EldersChoose 9 days over 7, travel outside Navratri and Shravan, use the Naina Devi cable car and the Vaishno Devi helicopter or battery-car quotas. Vaishno Devi helpline: 1800-180-7212 (toll-free).
  5. What to carryPhoto ID (needed for the RFID card), a light bag, grippy shoes, a rain poncho, a warm layer, cash/UPI, and printed hotel bookings in Navratri.
  6. Respect the shrinesModest clothes, shoes off at the gate. At Vaishno Devi, shoes and belts are not allowed in the holy cave — use the free cloak room at the Bhawan.
The nine Devis

Which are the 9 Devis of the Nau Devi Yatra?

1 · Shakumbhari Devi
शाकुम्भरी देवी

Behat, Saharanpur (UP), in the Shivalik foothills. Pilgrims first visit Bhura Dev, the guardian Bhairav, about 1 km before the main shrine.

2 · Mata Mansa Devi
मनसा देवी

Panchkula, Haryana, run by the Mata Mansa Devi Shrine Board. This is the Panchkula temple — not Mansa Devi of Haridwar.

3 · Kali Mata, Kalka
काली माता, कालका

In Kalka town at the foot of the Shimla hills, about 25 km from Mansa Devi and managed by the same Shrine Board.

4 · Naina Devi
नैना देवी

A Shivalik hilltop in Bilaspur, Himachal — Suzu's home district. A cable car climbs from the base. Not the Naina Devi of Nainital. Full Naina Devi guide.

5 · Chintpurni
चिंतपूर्णी

Chhaproh in Una district, seat of Chhinnamastika. Join the queue with a darshan slip (parchi) from the guard.

6 · Jwala Ji
ज्वाला जी

Jawalamukhi, Kangra: no idol, only flames from the rock. Devotees see the Devi in them; geologists describe natural gas seeping from the ground.

7 · Brajeshwari (Kangra Devi)
ब्रजेश्वरी

Kangra town, old Nagarkot. Rebuilt after the 1905 earthquake; the pindi is covered in butter every Makar Sankranti.

8 · Chamunda Nandikeshwar Dham
चामुंडा

On the Baner river near Dharamshala, below the Dhauladhar range.

9 · Mata Vaishno Devi
वैष्णो देवी

The cave shrine on Trikuta hill above Katra, Jammu & Kashmir — the eldest of the sisters and the last, steepest stop of the yatra.

The package variant. Many tour operators drop Kalka and Shakumbhari and add Baglamukhi (Bankhandi, between Jwala Ji and Kangra) and a Sheetla Devi temple. Neither list is “official” — choose by your route and time. Nau Devi is not Navadurga: Navadurga are the nine forms of Durga worshipped on the nine nights; the Nau Devi Yatra is a journey to nine temples.

FAQ

Nau Devi Yatra: questions pilgrims ask

Which are the 9 Devis of the Nau Devi Yatra?

Traditionally Vaishno Devi, Mansa Devi (Panchkula), Kali Mata of Kalka, Shakumbhari Devi, Naina Devi, Chintpurni, Jwala Ji, Kangra Devi (Brajeshwari) and Chamunda. Many packages swap Kalka and Shakumbhari for Baglamukhi and a Sheetla Devi temple.

How many days does the Nau Devi Yatra take?

About 9 days from Delhi for all nine temples. The common package route of seven Devis, starting from Chandigarh, takes about 7 days.

What is the best route order for the 9 Devi Yatra?

Shakumbhari → Mansa Devi → Kalka → Naina Devi → Chintpurni → Jwala Ji → Kangra → Chamunda → Vaishno Devi. The reverse also works, starting at Vaishno Devi from Jammu.

Is the Nau Devi Yatra the same as Navadurga?

No. Navadurga are the nine forms of Durga worshipped on the nine nights of Navratri. The Nau Devi Yatra is a journey to nine different Devi temples.

Are all nine temples Shakti Peeths?

No. Naina Devi, Jwala Ji and Kangra Devi appear in the classical lists, and Chintpurni's place is disputed. Vaishno Devi, Mansa Devi, Chamunda, Kalka and Shakumbhari are revered by their own traditions.

Do I need registration for the Nau Devi Yatra?

Only for Vaishno Devi: the free RFID Yatra Access Card, from the Katra counters or online.maavaishnodevi.org. Start the climb within the time limit printed on your card.

What is the best time for the Nau Devi Yatra?

March–June and September–November. Navratri (the next is Chaitra Navratri, from 7 April 2027) is the most auspicious time but also the most crowded; avoid peak monsoon.

Can elderly parents do the Nau Devi Yatra?

Yes, with planning: take 9 days rather than 7, use the Naina Devi cable car and the Vaishno Devi helicopter or battery-car options, and travel outside Navratri and Shravan.
