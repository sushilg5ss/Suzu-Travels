What are the Ayodhya Ram Mandir darshan timings, and do you need a pass?

From 1 Nov 2026 the temple follows a winter schedule: free darshan about 7 to 11:30 am and 1:35 to about 9:15 pm, with short bhog breaks. Aartis: Mangala 4:30 am, Shringar 6:30 am, Raj Bhog 12 noon, Sandhya 7 pm, Shayan 9:30 pm. Ram Mandir stands at the spot devotees revere as Ram Janmabhoomi, the birthplace of Lord Rama, in the old town of Ayodhya on the river Saryu in Uttar Pradesh. On the ground floor is Ram Lalla, Rama as a child; on the first floor is the Ram Darbar, Rama with Sita, Lakshmana and Hanuman. The temple is built in the Nagara style to designs by the Sompura family of temple architects — three storeys on a raised plinth, carried on 366 columns and rising about 49 m (161 ft).

The temple was built by the Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra trust after the 2019 Supreme Court judgment. The bhoomi pujan was on 5 Aug 2020, Ram Lalla's pran pratishtha on 22 Jan 2024 and the Ram Darbar's on 5 Jun 2025. In October 2025 the trust said all construction of the main temple and its six surrounding shrines — to Shiva, Ganesha, Hanuman, Surya, Bhagwati and Annapurna — was complete, and the flag was raised on the shikhara on 25 Nov 2025. Many pilgrims combine Ayodhya with Kashi Vishwanath in Varanasi; see all our Kashi–Ayodhya circuit ideas.

The legend

The story of Ayodhya, city of Rama

Retold in our own words from the Valmiki Ramayana and living tradition — as faith, not as a claim of history.

  1. A prince is bornIn the Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya on the Saryu is the capital of the Ikshvaku kings of Kosala. Here King Dasharatha's eldest son, Rama, is born — remembered every year on Ram Navami, the ninth day of Chaitra.
  2. Fourteen years awayBound by his father's promise, Rama leaves for the forest with Sita and Lakshmana. Fourteen years of exile follow, and the war at Lanka.
  3. The city of lampsWhen Rama comes home, tradition says Ayodhya lit rows of lamps to welcome him. Diwali keeps that memory alive, and today the Saryu ghats glow with the Deepotsav.
  4. The guardianHanuman is believed to guard the city from Hanumangarhi, on a mound in the old town — which is why many pilgrims bow there before going on to Ram Lalla.

Tradition names Ayodhya first among the Saptapuri, the seven sacred cities of India. For devotees, darshan of Ram Lalla is darshan of Rama in the home where he was born, and the temple's mandapas and shrines remember the sages and companions of his story — Valmiki, Vashishtha, Vishwamitra, Agastya, Nishadraj, Shabari and Ahilya, with a temple to Tulsidas and statues of Jatayu and the little squirrel who helped build the bridge to Lanka.

How to reach

How to reach Ayodhya Ram Mandir

By air

Maharishi Valmiki International Airport, Ayodhya Dham, is about 8 km from the temple. Lucknow airport is about 150 km (around 2 hours by road); Gorakhpur is about 134 km and Varanasi about 209 km.

By train

Ayodhya Dham Junction is about 1.6 km from the temple, and Ayodhya Cantt (Faizabad) about 7 km. Vande Bharat and regular trains connect Ayodhya with Lucknow, Delhi and Varanasi.

By road

Lucknow is about 2 hours away. From Delhi it is roughly 627 km by the expressways; Prayagraj is about 168 km and Varanasi about 209 km — easy to link into one circuit.

In the old town

Hanumangarhi is under 1 km from the temple and the Saryu ghats and Ram ki Paidi about 1.5 km; Kanak Bhawan and Dashrath Mahal are within walking distance in the old town.

Sample itinerary

A simple Ayodhya darshan plan (1–2 days)

  1. Day 1
    Hanumangarhi and Ram Lalla

    Morning at Hanumangarhi, then Ram Mandir in an early slot — Ram Lalla on the ground floor, Ram Darbar above. Evening on the Saryu at Ram ki Paidi and the ghats for the aarti and lamps. Night in Ayodhya.

  2. Day 2
    Old town and onward

    Kanak Bhawan and Dashrath Mahal in the old town, then drive on to Lucknow, or continue to Prayagraj and Varanasi for Kashi Vishwanath.

Before you go

Before you go

  1. Leave your phone outsideMobile phones and electronic devices — even electronic car keys — are not allowed inside the temple premises. Use the lockers before entry and confirm the rule at the gate.
  2. Never pay for darshanThe trust charges no fee for darshan or aarti passes. Websites or agents selling 'VIP darshan' are not the trust — book only on srjbtkshetra.org.
  3. Carry ID, dress simplyKeep a photo ID with you. Modest clothing is a good idea; no official dress code is published.
  4. Elders and heatFestival queues mean long walks. Ask at the entry about wheelchair help (confirm locally), and avoid midday in May–June heat. October to March is the most comfortable season.

FAQ

Questions pilgrims ask

What are Ayodhya Ram Mandir darshan timings?

Winter timings from 1 Nov 2026: free darshan about 7 to 11:30 am and 1:35 to about 9:15 pm, with short bhog breaks. Mangala aarti is at 4:30 am, Shringar 6:30 am, Raj Bhog 12 noon, Sandhya 7 pm and Shayan 9:30 pm. Confirm on the trust site, srjbtkshetra.org.

Is a pass needed for Ram Mandir darshan?

No. General darshan is open to all. Free passes for darshan slots and aartis can be reserved on the trust's own site, srjbtkshetra.org — the trust charges no fee.

Do I need a separate pass for the Ram Darbar?

Not since 5 Aug 2026. After Ram Lalla's darshan on the ground floor you can go straight up to the Ram Darbar on the first floor.

Are mobile phones allowed in Ram Mandir?

No. Phones and electronic devices, including electronic car keys, are not allowed inside. Lockers are available before entry — confirm at the gate.

When was the Ayodhya Ram Mandir inaugurated?

Ram Lalla's pran pratishtha was on 22 Jan 2024. The Ram Darbar was consecrated on 5 Jun 2025 and the flag raised on the shikhara on 25 Nov 2025.

How far is Ram Mandir from Ayodhya station and airport?

About 1.6 km from Ayodhya Dham Junction and about 8 km from Ayodhya's airport. Lucknow is about 150 km, around 2 hours by road.

What else should I see in Ayodhya?

Hanumangarhi, under 1 km away, where many pilgrims go first; the Saryu ghats and Ram ki Paidi; Kanak Bhawan and Dashrath Mahal in the old town.

When is Ram Navami 2027?

Thursday 15 April 2027. It is the busiest day of the year at Ram Mandir — check the trust site for festival-day timings.
