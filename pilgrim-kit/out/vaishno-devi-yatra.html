What is the Vaishno Devi Yatra and how does it work?

The Vaishno Devi Yatra is a walk of about 12–13 km one way from Katra to the holy cave (Bhawan) on the Trikuta hill in Jammu & Kashmir, plus about 1.5 km more to Bhairon temple. Every pilgrim needs a free RFID Yatra card, checked at Banganga. The shrine is open all year; helicopter, battery car and ropeway tickets are sold only by the Shrine Board.

Shri Mata Vaishno Devi is worshipped in a cave on the three-peaked Trikuta hill in the Reasi district of Jammu & Kashmir. There is no idol: inside the cave are three natural rock pindis revered as Mahakali, Mahalakshmi and Mahasaraswati. The base town is Katra, and the shrine is run by the Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine Board.

Vaishno Devi is not part of the classical lists of 51 Shakti Peeths, but it is one of the most visited Devi shrines in India and is remembered as the elder sister in the Himachal Nau Devi yatra. Many families combine it with the Shakti Peeths of Himachal — Chamunda Devi is about 241 km from Katra by road via Pathankot.

The legend

The story of Mata Vaishno Devi

This is the popular tradition of the shrine, retold in our own words as devotees tell it — a story of faith, not history.

  1. Vaishnavi waits on TrikutaIt is believed the Goddess was born as Vaishnavi, uniting the powers of Kali, Lakshmi and Saraswati, and that Rama asked her to wait for him in the Trikuta hills.
  2. Pandit Shridhar's feastA divine girl is said to have helped the devotee Pandit Shridhar of Hansali village at his feast. Searching for her afterwards, he was led to the holy cave.
  3. Banganga and ArdhkuwariWhen the tantric Bhairon Nath chased her up the hill, she struck the ground with an arrow to bring out the Banganga stream, rested at Charan Paduka and stayed nine months in the narrow Ardhkuwari (Garbh Joon) cave.
  4. Forgiveness at BhaironAt the holy cave she took the form of Mahakali and ended the chase. His head is said to have fallen where the Bhairon temple stands; she forgave him and declared the yatra complete only after visiting his shrine.

That is why pilgrims walk the same path the story follows — Banganga, Charan Paduka, Ardhkuwari, the Bhawan — and then climb on to Bhairon Nath before coming down.

Route

The Vaishno Devi trek route from Katra

Distances are approximate. The path is paved and lit, with shelters, water and langar along the way. Most walkers take several hours to climb — go at your own pace.

  1. 0 km
    Katraकटरा

    Base town with the registration counters (YRC), the railway station and the helipad.

  2. ~1.5 km
    Bangangaबाणगंगा

    RFID check post. By tradition, the stream the Goddess brought out with her arrow.

  3. on the way
    Charan Padukaचरण पादुका

    Where she is believed to have rested; footprints are worshipped on a rock.

  4. ~6 km
    Ardhkuwariअर्धकुंवारी

    The narrow Garbh Joon cave, roughly halfway. Battery cars start here for the Bhawan.

  5. ~12–13 km
    Bhawan (holy cave)भवन

    Via the old track through Sanjichhat or the new track through Himkoti. Cloak rooms for bags and phones.

  6. ~1.5 km more
    Bhairon templeभैरों मंदिर

    Ropeway or a 45–60 minute walk uphill. Then down via Sanjichhat to Katra.

After rains the new track or battery cars may be closed while the old track stays open, as in July 2026 — check the current status before you go.

How to reach

How to reach Katra for Vaishno Devi

By train

Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra (SVDK) station is on the Jammu–Udhampur–Katra line, with through trains from Delhi.

By air

Jammu airport is the nearest, about 50 km (around 2 hours) from Katra by bus or taxi.

By road

Katra is about 649 km from Delhi and about 241 km from Chamunda Devi in Himachal via Pathankot.

Where to stay

Katra has many stays; Shrine Board bhawans and rooms on the track are bookable on the official portal.

Sample itinerary

A simple 2-day Vaishno Devi Yatra from Katra

  1. Day 1
    Arrive at Katra

    Reach Katra by train or from Jammu airport, collect your RFID cards at the YRC, rest and start the climb in the evening or early next morning.

  2. Day 2
    Bhawan, Bhairon and back

    Walk via Banganga and Ardhkuwari to the Bhawan for darshan, take the ropeway or path to Bhairon temple, and come down via Sanjichhat to Katra.

  3. Day +1
    Optional

    Add Shiv Khori cave near Katra, or continue to Kashmir or to the Devi temples of Himachal.

Before you go

Before you go

  1. CarryYour RFID card and photo ID, comfortable walking shoes, light woollens (nights are cool), a water bottle and a rain jacket in the monsoon.
  2. Leave behindLeather items and large bags are restricted at the Bhawan; mobiles and cameras are deposited in the cloak rooms before darshan.
  3. Elders and heart patientsNo medical certificate is needed, but the climb is long. Use the helicopter, a battery car, a pony or a palki, and the seniors' help desks.
  4. Weather and safetyHeavy rain has stopped the yatra before — a landslide near Ardhkuwari in August 2025 and a six-day halt in July 2026. Follow Shrine Board advisories and keep a spare day in the monsoon.
  5. Beware of fake sitesThe only official sites are maavaishnodevi.org and online.maavaishnodevi.org. Pages with names like ".live" or "devasthan" are not the Shrine Board.
  6. Complete the yatraBy tradition the yatra is complete only after darshan at Bhairon Nath, about 1.5 km above the Bhawan.
Free and compulsory

Vaishno Devi registration: the RFID Yatra card

Every pilgrim needs their own RFID Yatra Access Card. It is free. Get it at the Yatri Registration Counter (YRC) near the Katra bus stand, at YRC-II near the second bus stand on the Udhampur road (about 1 km away), or online on online.maavaishnodevi.org. The card is checked at the Banganga check post, about 1.5 km from the counter — without it you will be turned back.

  1. Register on the official portal or at a Katra counter with a photo ID.
  2. Collect or activate your RFID card — one per person, children included.
  3. Start the climb within the time limit printed on your card.
  4. Under the December 2025 rules, plan to be back in Katra within 24 hours — check maavaishnodevi.org for the current rule.

Sources give different start limits (6, 10 or 12 hours), so go by what is printed on your card. In heavy rush the Shrine Board can pause registration for the day; daily numbers are capped at roughly 30,000–35,000.

Book only with the Shrine Board

Vaishno Devi helicopter, battery car and ropeway

The helicopter flies from the Katra (Serli) helipad to Sanjichhat — not to the Bhawan. From Sanjichhat you walk about 2.5 km to the holy cave. Battery cars run between Ardhkuwari and the Bhawan, with priority for seniors and the disabled, and a passenger ropeway links the Bhawan with Bhairon temple, roughly 9 am to 5 pm. Ponies, palkis and pithoos are available on the track at rates fixed by the authorities.

Book helicopter, battery car and ropeway tickets only on online.maavaishnodevi.org or at Shrine Board counters. Look-alike websites and WhatsApp sellers offering "confirmed helicopter tickets" are not the Shrine Board — never pay them. Helpline 1800-180-7212.

FAQ

Questions pilgrims ask

How long is the Vaishno Devi trek from Katra?

About 12–13 km one way from Katra to the Bhawan, plus about 1.5 km more to Bhairon temple. The path is paved and lit, with shelters and langar.

Is the RFID card mandatory for Vaishno Devi?

Yes. Every pilgrim needs a free RFID Yatra card from the Katra counters or online.maavaishnodevi.org. It is checked at Banganga; start within the time limit printed on your card.

Where can I book the Vaishno Devi helicopter?

Only on online.maavaishnodevi.org or at Shrine Board counters. It flies from Katra to Sanjichhat, and you walk about 2.5 km to the Bhawan. Ignore other websites and WhatsApp sellers.

What are the Vaishno Devi aarti timings?

The aarti is held twice a day, just before sunrise and just after sunset, and takes nearly two hours; darshan pauses meanwhile. Exact clock times are not published — confirm locally.

Is the Vaishno Devi yatra complete without Bhairon temple?

By tradition, no. The Goddess is believed to have declared that the yatra is complete only after darshan at Bhairon Nath.

Which is the nearest railway station and airport?

Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra (SVDK) station is in Katra itself. Jammu airport is about 50 km away.

Is Vaishno Devi open in winter?

Yes, the shrine is open all year. Winter is cold and quieter; Navratri, New Year week and summer holidays are the busiest times.

Is Vaishno Devi a Shakti Peeth?

It is not in the classical lists of 51 Shakti Peeths, though some broader traditions include it. It is revered as one of India's great Devi shrines.
