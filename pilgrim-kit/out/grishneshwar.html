Where is Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga and is it open now?

Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga is in Verul village near Ellora, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar district (formerly Aurangabad), Maharashtra — about 30 km from the city and 1.5 km from the Ellora Caves. It is traditionally the twelfth of the 12 Jyotirlingas. Development works began around the temple in September 2026; the district administration has said it will not close for six months, but check local news before you travel.

Grishneshwar — घृष्णेश्वर, also written Ghrishneshwar, Ghushmeshwar or, locally, Ghushneshwar — is the Shiva temple of Verul, the village that gives the Ellora Caves their name. The twelve-name stotra ends with "घुश्मेशं च शिवालये", Ghushmesha at Shivalaya, which is why the temple is traditionally counted as the twelfth and last Jyotirlinga. The Shivalaya Teerth tank lies beside it.

The temple of red basalt, carved with deities and stories, has been destroyed and rebuilt several times. It was restored in the 16th century by Maloji Bhosale, grandfather of Chhatrapati Shivaji, and the present structure was rebuilt by the Holkars in the 18th century. With Trimbakeshwar and Bhimashankar, it is one of Maharashtra's three Jyotirlingas in the Sahyadri country.

The legend

The legend of Grishneshwar

The Shiva Purana (Koti Rudra Samhita) tells the story of Ghushma, whose faith did not break. This is how it is told.

  1. Two sistersThe Brahmin Sudharma and his wife Sudeha had no children, so Sudeha asked him to marry her younger sister, Ghushma, a devoted worshipper of Shiva.
  2. 101 clay lingasEvery day Ghushma shaped 101 small lingas of clay, worshipped them and let them dissolve in a nearby pond. In time she had a son, and Sudeha's jealousy grew.
  3. Faith unbrokenSudeha took the young man's life and left him in the pond. Ghushma did not stop her worship — and by Shiva's grace her son came back to her alive.
  4. ForgivenessShiva wished to punish Sudeha, but Ghushma asked him to forgive her sister and to stay there for everyone's good. He remained as Ghushmeshwar.

This is a story of faith, told as tradition rather than history. For many devotees, Grishneshwar is the temple of compassion: the last Jyotirlinga of the list, remembered for a mother who chose forgiveness over revenge.

How to reach

How to reach Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga

By air

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar airport is about 30–37 km from the temple.

By rail

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar railway station is about 30 km from the temple by road.

By road

About 30 km north-west of the city via Daulatabad and Khuldabad. MSRTC buses and taxis run to Ellora and Verul; check timings locally.

At the temple

Level village approach — but during the 2026–27 works vehicles may be stopped 1–2 km short, and part of the queue is in the open.

Before you go

Before you go

  1. Check the worksLook up the latest local news on closures and traffic before you leave — the works around the temple run into 2027.
  2. Dress for the sanctumMen should wear something easy to take off above the waist; everyone should dress modestly.
  3. EldersThe ground is flat, but walking distances have grown during the works. Avoid Mondays in Shravan and Mahashivratri, and carry water.
  4. Pick your seasonJuly to March is the most comfortable — green after the monsoon, pleasant in winter. April to June is hot.
Verul village

Grishneshwar and the Ellora Caves

The temple is about 1.5 km from the Ellora Caves, the UNESCO World Heritage rock-cut caves — so most visitors see both on the same day. Grishneshwar is not inside the caves: the great Kailasa temple of Cave 16 is a different monument.

Go for darshan early, then spend the rest of the day at Ellora (check the caves' weekly closing day before you go). Nearby are the Bhadra Maruti temple at Khuldabad (about 4 km) and Daulatabad (Devagiri) Fort (about 15 km); Ajanta is about 100 km away.

FAQ

Questions pilgrims ask

What are Grishneshwar temple timings?

About 5:30 am to 9:30 pm according to Incredible India. Aarti times vary between sources, so confirm them locally.

Is Grishneshwar temple closed in November 2026?

A six-month closure was reported, but the district collector has said the temple will not close for six months. Development works are on, so check local news before you go.

Why do men remove their shirts at Grishneshwar?

By temple custom men enter the sanctum bare-chested. Devotees are also allowed to touch the linga.

Which is the 12th Jyotirlinga?

Grishneshwar near Ellora is traditionally listed as the twelfth and last Jyotirlinga.

How far is Grishneshwar from the Ellora Caves?

About 1.5 km — most visitors see the temple and the caves on the same day. The temple is not inside the caves.

How far is Grishneshwar from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad)?

About 30 km by road via Daulatabad and Khuldabad. The airport is roughly 30–37 km away.

What is the story of Grishneshwar?

The Shiva Purana tells of Ghushma, whose faith brought her son back to life and who asked Shiva to forgive her jealous sister. Shiva stayed there as Ghushmeshwar.

Can I carry a phone inside Grishneshwar temple?

Phones and cameras were banned in the premises on Mahashivratri 2026, and rules on other days are not published. Be ready to leave them outside.
