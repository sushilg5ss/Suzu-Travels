Who is Khatu Shyam Ji and where is the temple?

Khatu Shyam Ji is Barbarika, a grandson of Bhima, who according to tradition gave his own head to Krishna before the Mahabharata war and was blessed to be worshipped as Shyam in the Kali Yuga. His temple, where the sheesh (head) is enshrined, stands in Khatu village, Sikar district, Rajasthan — about 17 km from Ringas and 78 km from Jaipur.

Devotees call him Baba Shyam and Sheesh ke Daani — the giver of the head. The temple at Khatu (Khatoo), a small town in the Shekhawati country of Sikar district, is a centuries-old shrine that has been rebuilt over time, and today it is run by the Shri Shyam Mandir Committee. Every month on Shukla Ekadashi the lanes fill with pilgrims, and in the month of Phalgun the town hosts the huge Lakkhi Mela.

Please don't confuse it with the many newer Khatu Shyam temples in Delhi, Agra and other cities — the original temple is at Khatu, Sikar. Families from Delhi and Jaipur often combine it with Salasar Balaji, about 102 km away by road. The legend

About Khatu Shyam Ji: the story of Barbarika

Retold in our own words from Mahabharata-related lore (tellings in the Skanda Purana are often cited) — told as tradition, not history. Regional tellings differ in the details.

  1. A vow to the weaker sideBarbarika, a grandson of Bhima, was a matchless archer. Before the great war he vowed that he would always fight for whichever side was losing.
  2. Krishna asks a giftKrishna saw that such a vow would keep switching sides until no one was left standing. He came to Barbarika in disguise and asked him for a gift.
  3. Sheesh ka daanBarbarika offered the greatest gift he had — his own head. Tradition says he asked only to watch the war, and his head saw it all from a hilltop.
  4. Worshipped as ShyamMoved by the sacrifice, Krishna blessed him: in the Kali Yuga he would be worshipped by Krishna's own name, Shyam. It is believed the head was later found at Khatu.

Local tradition holds that the sheesh was found at Shyam Kund, a sacred tank about 1 km from the temple, and enshrined here. Pilgrims bathe or sprinkle its water before darshan. Devotees also call Baba Shyam the support of those who feel defeated — a belief rooted in his vow to stand with the losing side.

Most tellings name Barbarika as the son of Ghatotkacha; some regional versions differ, so we simply call him Bhima's grandson. In every version he gives his head before the war — he never fought at Kurukshetra.

How to reach

How to reach Khatu Shyam Ji

By train

The nearest railhead is Ringas (Reengus) Junction, about 17 km away — shared jeeps, autos and taxis run to Khatu. Jaipur Junction is about 78 km by road.

By air

Jaipur International Airport is about 93 km by road — roughly 2 hours by taxi.

By road

Good roads link Khatu with Jaipur and Sikar. On mela days the road to the temple becomes a no-vehicle zone, so be ready to walk the last stretch.

With Salasar Balaji

Salasar Balaji in Churu district is about 102 km by road — many families visit both in one trip from Jaipur or Delhi.

Sample itinerary

A simple Khatu Shyam darshan plan

  1. Day 1
    Jaipur to Khatu

    Drive or take the train to Ringas and on to Khatu (about 2 hours from Jaipur). Visit Shyam Kund, then evening darshan and Sandhya aarti. Night in Khatu.

  2. Day 2
    Mangla aarti and Salasar

    Early Mangla aarti and morning darshan, then drive about 102 km to Salasar Balaji for darshan. Return towards Jaipur or Delhi.

Before you go

Before you go

  1. Pick a quiet dayGo on a weekday morning that is not an Ekadashi if crowds trouble you. On Ekadashi and mela days the queues can take hours.
  2. Elders and childrenQueues are long and crowded on peak days, and no special elder queue is published. Keep water, keep children close and agree a meeting point.
  3. Dress and conductModest traditional dress, footwear left outside, no alcohol or tobacco. Phones may be restricted on peak days.
  4. OfferingsDevotees offer roses, itra, coconut, kheer and churma. Buy only from the stalls; ignore anyone selling 'special' darshan.
  5. Season and stayOctober to March is pleasant; April to June is very hot. Rooms near the mandir fill up fast on Ekadashi and mela days — book early.

FAQ

Questions pilgrims ask

What are Khatu Shyam Ji darshan timings?

Roughly 4:30 am to noon, 1:30 to 4:30 pm and 5:30 to 9 pm, with five aartis from Mangla (about 5:30 am) to Shayan (about 10 pm). Times shift between summer and winter, so confirm locally.

Who is Khatu Shyam Ji?

According to tradition, Barbarika, a grandson of Bhima, who gave his head to Krishna before the Mahabharata war and was blessed to be worshipped as Shyam in the Kali Yuga.

When is the Khatu Shyam Phalgun Mela 2027?

Not announced yet. The Lakkhi Mela falls in Phalgun Shukla (late February to March) and peaks on Ekadashi, a few days before Holi. In 2026 it ran 21–28 February.

Do I need online booking for Khatu Shyam darshan?

Not for general darshan, which is free. Follow only the Shri Shyam Mandir Committee's notices and never pay touts for VIP darshan.

What is the Nishan yatra?

Devotees walk about 17 km from Ringas to Khatu carrying saffron flags (nishan) and coconuts to offer to Baba Shyam, especially during the Phalgun Mela.

Which is the nearest railway station to Khatu Shyam?

Ringas (Reengus) Junction, about 17 km away. Jaipur Junction is about 78 km by road.

How far is Khatu Shyam from Jaipur airport?

About 93 km by road, roughly 2 hours by taxi.

What is Shyam Kund?

A sacred tank about 1 km from the temple where, it is believed, Barbarika's head was found before it was enshrined at Khatu.
