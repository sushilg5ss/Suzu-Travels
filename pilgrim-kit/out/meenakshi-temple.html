The Arulmigu Meenakshi Sundareswarar Temple — better known as the Meenakshi Amman temple or simply the Madurai temple — stands in the heart of old Madurai, Tamil Nadu, on the south bank of the Vaigai river. It is managed by the Tamil Nadu Hindu Religious and Charitable Endowments (HR&CE) department.

Two shrines sit at its centre: Meenakshi, "the fish-eyed one", a form of Parvati, and Sundareswarar, "the beautiful lord", Shiva as her groom. Note that Meenakshi is one of India's great Devi temples but not a Shakti Peeth — it is not in the classical lists explained on our 51 Shakti Peeth guide.

Most families pair Madurai with Rameswaram, about 175 km away, as part of a wider South India temple tour.

The legend

Meenakshi temple history and legend

Told in our own words, as the Thiruvilaiyadal Puranam and Madurai's tradition remember it — faith, not a history lesson.

  1. A child from the fireThe childless Pandya king Malayadhwaja and queen Kanchanamalai performed a fire sacrifice for a son. Instead, it is said, a three-year-old girl rose from the flames, with a sign that would vanish only when she met her destined husband.
  2. The warrior queenRaised as the heir, Meenakshi ruled Madurai and rode out to conquer the worlds — until her campaign reached Mount Kailash.
  3. Meeting ShivaOn Kailash she faced Shiva. The sign vanished at once, and she knew him as her lord. He promised to come to Madurai to marry her.
  4. The wedding in MaduraiShiva came as Sundareswarar and married Meenakshi in Madurai. In local tradition Vishnu, as her brother Kallazhagar, gave the bride away — the story Madurai still re-enacts every Chithirai.

The temple's corridors are painted with the Thiruvilaiyadal, the 64 playful miracles that tradition says Shiva performed in Madurai.

History, as far as it is recorded: the temple is mentioned in old Tamil texts. It was plundered in the early 14th century, during Malik Kafur's raids after 1310, and restored afterwards. Most of the present masonry dates from the 14th century onward, and the Madurai Nayak rulers expanded it greatly in the 16th and 17th centuries; the Muthupillai Mandapam was built in 1613. Its most recent Kumbhabhishekam (consecration) was held on 17 September 2026.

How to reach

How to reach Meenakshi temple

By train

Madurai Junction is about 1.5 km from the temple, on lines from Chennai, Bengaluru and across India.

By air

Madurai Airport is about 11 km away, around 20 minutes by car. From Himachal, fly from Chandigarh or Delhi to Madurai with one stop.

By road

The Periyar bus stand is about 1 km away. Rameswaram is about 175 km (around 3 hours) by road.

Near the temple

Thirumalai Nayakkar Mahal, the Gandhi Memorial Museum, Alagar Kovil (Kallazhagar) and the Murugan temple at Thiruparankundram are all near Madurai.

Before you go

Before you go: dress code, phones and crowds

  1. Dress codeModest traditional dress is expected: dhoti, or trousers with a shirt, for men; saree, salwar or churidar for women. Shorts are not appropriate. This is temple guidance, not a court-enforced rule.
  2. Leave the phonePhones and cameras are not allowed inside. Carry little, and use the deposit counters at the gates.
  3. Go earlyThe early morning is cooler and calmer. Weekends, the December–January holidays and the Chithirai festival bring long queues.
  4. With eldersThe complex is flat but very large, and you walk barefoot on stone that gets hot by midday. October to March is the most comfortable season.
Towers and halls

The 14 gopurams and the hall of a thousand pillars

The temple is ringed by 14 gopurams — tall, tapering gateway towers covered in painted figures of gods, demons and animals. Tamil Nadu Tourism puts them at roughly 45–50 metres; the southern tower, built in the late 16th century, is usually given as the tallest at about 52 m.

Inside you'll find the hall of a thousand pillars, with carved musical pillars, the Potramarai Kulam or Golden Lotus tank, and long corridors painted with the Thiruvilaiyadal stories. One mandapam is used as a temple museum.

FAQ

Questions pilgrims ask

What are Meenakshi temple timings?

Per Tamil Nadu Tourism, 6 am to 12:30 pm and 4 pm to 9 pm. The first and last poojas fall slightly outside these hours, and festival days differ, so confirm locally.

Is there a dress code at Meenakshi temple?

Modest traditional dress is expected — a dhoti or trousers with a shirt for men, a saree, salwar or churidar for women. Avoid shorts.

Are mobile phones allowed inside Meenakshi temple?

No. Phones and cameras have not been allowed inside since March 2018; there are deposit counters at the gates.

When is the Chithirai festival 2027?

Not announced yet. It falls in the Tamil month of Chithirai, usually mid to late April, with Meenakshi Thirukalyanam — the divine wedding — as its high point.

How many gopurams does Meenakshi temple have?

Fourteen gateway towers, roughly 45–50 metres tall; the southern tower is usually given as the tallest, at about 52 m.

Is Meenakshi temple a Shakti Peeth?

No — it is not in the classical 51 or 18 Shakti Peeth lists, though it is one of India's greatest Devi temples.

How far is Meenakshi temple from Madurai railway station and airport?

About 1.5 km from Madurai Junction and about 11 km (20 minutes) from Madurai Airport.

Can I combine Meenakshi temple with Rameswaram?

Yes. Rameswaram's Ramanathaswamy Jyotirlinga is about 175 km, around 3 hours by road — the classic two- or three-day pairing.
