Where is Badrinath temple and why is it sacred?

Badrinath temple stands at about 3,100 m on the Alaknanda river in Chamoli district, Uttarakhand, between the Nar and Narayan ranges. It is the shrine of Vishnu as Badrinarayan — one of the four dhams of India, one of the 108 Divya Desams and the last stop of the Char Dham Yatra. A road reaches the town, so there is no trek.

For many families Badrinath is the gentlest of the Himalayan dhams: the road climbs all the way to the town, and the temple is a short walk across the Alaknanda bridge. Its bright painted façade faces the river, with the Neelkanth peak behind and the Nar and Narayan ranges on either side. The Badrinath Mandir is managed by the Shri Badarinath-Kedarnath Temple Committee (BKTC).

The deity is a small black Shaligram stone image of Vishnu seated in meditation, flanked in the sanctum by Kuber, Narada, Uddhava and Nar–Narayan; Lakshmi has her own shrine in the complex. By a long custom the chief priest, the Rawal, is a Nambudiri Brahmin from Kerala — a living link between the Himalaya and the far south. Badrinath is a Vishnu shrine, not a Jyotirlinga; its Shiva neighbour on the yatra is Kedarnath.

The legend

The legend of Badrinath

The Puranas and local tradition tell why Vishnu chose this cold valley. This is how it is told.

  1. Vishnu in meditationIt is believed that Vishnu once sat in deep meditation here, in the bitter Himalayan cold, unmoved by snow and sun.
  2. Lakshmi becomes a treeTo shield him, Lakshmi stood over him in the form of a badri (jujube) tree, through every storm.
  3. Lord of the BadriMoved by her devotion, Vishnu named the place Badrikashram and took the name Badri-Nath, the Lord of the Badri.
  4. Nar and NarayanTradition also places the long penance of the sages Nar and Narayan here — which is why the two ranges carry their names.

A popular telling adds that this valley was once the home of Shiva and Parvati, until Vishnu came as a crying infant, was sheltered by Parvati, and then claimed the spot for his meditation — so Shiva moved on to Kedarnath. Not every Purana has this tale; it is told as tradition, not history.

Route

The road from Rishikesh to Badrinath

Badrinath is a drive-up dham: the highway follows the Ganga and then the Alaknanda past the five prayags. About 298 km in all; distances are approximate.

  1. Start
    Rishikeshऋषिकेश

    Railhead, registration counters and the start of the mountain road.

  2. Road
    Devprayagदेवप्रयाग

    Where the Alaknanda and Bhagirathi meet to become the Ganga.

  3. Road
    Rudraprayagरुद्रप्रयाग

    The Kedarnath road turns off up the Mandakini; Badrinath traffic stays on the Alaknanda.

  4. Road
    Karnaprayag & Nandaprayagकर्णप्रयाग / नंदप्रयाग

    Two more confluences; Adi Badri lies about 17 km from Karnaprayag.

  5. Road
    Jyotirmath (Joshimath)ज्योतिर्मठ

    Narsingh temple, Badrinath's winter seat; Auli is close by.

  6. ~45 km
    Badrinathबद्रीनाथ

    Past Pandukeshwar to the town; a short walk across the bridge to the temple.

The highway is landslide-prone in the monsoon, especially around Jyotirmath — keep a buffer day and check road status before you set off.

How to reach

How to reach Badrinath

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun, is the nearest — about 315 km by road.

By rail

Yog Nagari Rishikesh station is the nearest railhead; Haridwar has more trains.

By road

Rishikesh → Devprayag → Rudraprayag → Karnaprayag → Jyotirmath → Badrinath, about 298 km. Buses, shared taxis and cars run in season.

By helicopter

There is no IRCTC HeliYatra service for Badrinath. Private charters from Dehradun exist — verify the operator yourself and never pay "seat sellers" on WhatsApp.

Sample itinerary

A sample three-day Badrinath yatra from Rishikesh

  1. Day 1
    Rishikesh to Jyotirmath

    Drive up past Devprayag, Rudraprayag and Karnaprayag along the Alaknanda. Evening at Narsingh temple and rest early.

  2. Day 2
    Badrinath darshan and Mana

    Drive on to Badrinath, bathe at Tapt Kund and take darshan. Afternoon at Mana — Vyas Gufa, Ganesh Gufa and Bheem Pul — and the evening aarti.

  3. Day 3
    Early darshan and descent

    Early-morning darshan, visit Brahma Kapal, then drive down via Pandukeshwar towards Rudraprayag or Rishikesh.

Before you go

Before you go

  1. Register first — it is freeChar Dham registration is compulsory and free on registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in, the Tourist Care Uttarakhand app or helpline 1364. Any site charging a "registration fee" is fake.
  2. Easiest dham for eldersThe road reaches the town and the temple is a short walk away. The altitude still matters: Uttarakhand advises a check-up for anyone over 50 or with heart, lung or blood-pressure conditions.
  3. Best monthsMay–June and September–October. July–August brings landslides on the highway, though the temple stays open.
  4. Pack warmAt about 3,100 m it is cold even in June, and nights are freezing in October. Dress modestly and in layers.
  5. Visit ManaThe border village of Mana is close by, with Vyas Gufa, Ganesh Gufa and Bheem Pul; the trek to Vasudhara falls starts here.
  6. Panch BadriOne common list adds Yogdhyan Badri (Pandukeshwar), Bhavishya Badri, Vridh Badri and Adi Badri to Badri Vishal — lists differ by tradition.
Shankaracharya to the Rawal

A short history of Badrinath temple

Adi Shankaracharya is credited with reviving Badrinath as a centre of worship (traditions differ on the century). Garhwal kings moved the image to the present temple in the 16th century and expanded it in the 17th. The custom of a Nambudiri Rawal from Kerala is said to date from 1776.

The 1803 Garhwal earthquake badly damaged the temple; it was rebuilt largely with help from the King of Jaipur. In 2026 the temple drew a record 18.10 lakh+ pilgrims by 29 September (DD India).

FAQ

Questions pilgrims ask

When will Badrinath close in 2026?

Not announced yet. The date is fixed on Vijayadashami, 20 October 2026, at Badrinath; the temple usually closes a few weeks after Bhai Dooj, later than Kedarnath. See our Char Dham closing dates 2026 post.

When does Badrinath open in 2027?

Not announced yet. The date is fixed on Basant Panchami, 11 February 2027, at Narendranagar — usually in late April or early May.

What is the altitude of Badrinath?

About 3,100 m (Incredible India gives 3,133 m). It is cold even in June, so carry warm layers.

What are Badrinath darshan timings?

Per Incredible India, about 4:30 am to 1 pm and 4:30 pm to 9 pm. Hours can change in peak season and on festival days — confirm locally.

Is there a trek to Badrinath?

No. The road goes all the way to Badrinath town, and the temple is a short walk across the Alaknanda bridge.

How do I reach Badrinath by road?

From Rishikesh via Devprayag, Rudraprayag, Karnaprayag and Jyotirmath — about 298 km. The nearest airport is Dehradun and the railhead is Rishikesh.

Is registration needed for Badrinath?

Yes. Char Dham registration is compulsory and free on registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in, the Tourist Care Uttarakhand app or helpline 1364.

Where is Badrinath worshipped in winter?

At Yogdhyan Badri in Pandukeshwar (Uddhav and Kuber) and at Narsingh temple in Jyotirmath (Adi Shankaracharya's gaddi).
