What are the Shirdi Sai Baba temple timings, and how do you get darshan?

According to the Sansthan's official daily programme, the Samadhi Mandir in Shirdi opens at 4:45 am, the Kakad aarti is at 5:15 am and darshan begins at 6:25 am. The Madhyan aarti is at 12 noon, Dhoop at sunset and Shej at 10 pm. General darshan is free; paid darshan and aarti passes are sold only on the trust's portal, online.sai.org.in.

Shirdi (also called Sainagar) is in Rahata taluka of Ahilyanagar (formerly Ahmednagar) district, Maharashtra. At its heart is Shri Saibaba Samadhi Mandir, the stone building — once known as Buti Wada — where Sai Baba of Shirdi was laid to rest in 1918. The temple and the shrines around it are run by the Shri Saibaba Sansthan Trust, Shirdi, a public charitable trust.

Sai Baba is revered by people of every faith. He taught two simple words — shraddha (faith) and saburi (patience) — and the trust carries his message "Sabka Malik Ek". Most pilgrims spend a day here and pair the Shirdi temple with Shani Shingnapur, about 80 km away, or with the Jyotirlingas of Trimbakeshwar near Nashik and Grishneshwar near Ellora. The legend

The story of Sai Baba of Shirdi

Told in our own words, as devotees remember it — his birth and origins are not known, and we make no claims beyond the tradition.

  1. A young fakir under the neemTradition says Sai Baba first appeared in Shirdi as a young man of about sixteen, sitting in meditation under a neem tree. That spot is honoured today as Gurusthan.
  2. Dwarkamai and the dhuniHe returned around 1858 and stayed for the rest of his life in an old mosque he named Dwarkamai, where he kept a sacred fire, the dhuni, burning. Its ash, udi, is still given to devotees.
  3. Shraddha and saburiHe welcomed everyone, slept at the Chavadi on alternate nights and tended a garden, Lendi Baug. His teaching was faith and patience — shraddha and saburi.
  4. Samadhi at Buti WadaSai Baba took Mahasamadhi on 15 October 1918, Vijayadashami. He was laid to rest in Buti Wada, the stone house a Nagpur devotee, Gopalrao Buti, had been building — today's Samadhi Mandir.

Walking through Shirdi today, you follow the same small circuit: the Samadhi Mandir with Baba's marble statue, Dwarkamai where the dhuni still burns, the Chavadi, Gurusthan under the neem tree, and Lendi Baug with its Nanda Deep lamp. The Khandoba temple on the edge of town and the Sai museum complete the visit.

How to reach

How to reach Shirdi

By air

Shirdi airport at Kakadi, opened in 2017, is about 14 km from the temple (20–30 minutes). Aurangabad airport is about 115 km away.

By train

Sainagar Shirdi station, opened in 2009, is about 2 km from the temple. Kopargaon (about 15 km) and Manmad Junction (about 57–60 km) have more trains.

By road

About 240 km from Mumbai (5–6 hours), about 200 km from Pune (4–5 hours) and about 90 km from Nashik.

Shirdi to Shani Shingnapur

About 75–80 km by road, roughly 1.5–2 hours — most pilgrims visit both in a day or two. Trimbakeshwar is about 122 km away (2.5–3 hours).

Sample itinerary

A simple Shirdi and Shani Shingnapur plan (1–2 days)

  1. Day 1
    Shirdi darshan

    Early darshan in the Samadhi Mandir from 6:25 am, then Dwarkamai, the Chavadi, Gurusthan and Lendi Baug. Lunch at the Sai Prasadalaya; Dhoop aarti at sunset (or watch the Thursday palkhi).

  2. Day 2
    Shani Shingnapur and onward

    Drive about 80 km to Shani Shingnapur for the morning, then return towards Shirdi airport — or continue to Nashik and Trimbakeshwar.

Before you go

Before you go

  1. Avoid Thursdays if you canThursdays, weekends and festivals bring the longest queues. A weekday morning is the calmest time for darshan.
  2. Phones stay outsidePhones are not to be used inside the temple. Leave yours at the hotel or in the lockers, and ask at the gate.
  3. Dress and respectModest clothes, no shorts; shoes off at the counters. Don't eat inside the main mandir.
  4. Free meals and eldersThe trust's Sai Prasadalaya serves meals to pilgrims. The campus is flat; for elders or wheelchair help, ask at the Sansthan's enquiry counter.
Darshan and aarti passes

Shirdi Sai Baba darshan booking: free and paid passes

General darshan in the Samadhi Mandir is free — you simply join the queue. Paid darshan passes, aarti passes, rooms in the trust's accommodation and donations are available only on the Sansthan's portal, online.sai.org.in, and at the trust's own counters in Shirdi. The trust asks devotees to book passes online, because offline passes are limited and given first come, first served. VIP darshan is limited to fixed slots in the day.

Beware of fake sites. The trust says it does not authorise any agents for online darshan, aarti or accommodation bookings, and it has warned about fake websites, mobile numbers and duplicate Google Maps listings for its rooms. Never pay for a puja, donation or "VIP pass" over WhatsApp or UPI — book on online.sai.org.in or at the counters, and give donations in the hundi or through the official portal.

FAQ

Questions pilgrims ask

What are the Shirdi Sai Baba darshan timings?

The temple opens at 4:45 am and darshan begins at 6:25 am, according to the Sansthan's official daily programme. It continues through the day and the temple closes after the 10 pm Shej aarti, around 11 pm.

What are the Shirdi aarti timings?

There are four aartis a day: Kakad at 5:15 am, Madhyan at 12 noon, Dhoop at sunset and Shej at 10 pm. Times can change on festival days, so check sai.org.in before you go.

How do I book Shirdi Sai Baba darshan online?

General darshan is free. Paid darshan and aarti passes and the trust's rooms are booked only on online.sai.org.in. The trust does not authorise agents, so avoid other "VIP" sites and WhatsApp offers.

How far is Shani Shingnapur from Shirdi?

About 75–80 km by road, roughly 1.5–2 hours. Many pilgrims visit Shani Shingnapur on the morning after their Shirdi darshan.

Which is the nearest airport and railway station to Shirdi?

Shirdi airport at Kakadi is about 14 km from the temple. Sainagar Shirdi railway station is about 2 km away; Kopargaon and Manmad Junction have more trains.

When is the palkhi in Shirdi?

Every Thursday evening Baba's palanquin is carried from the Samadhi Mandir to Dwarkamai and the Chavadi and back. Thursdays are the busiest day, so confirm the time locally.

Can I take my phone inside the Shirdi temple?

Phones are not to be used inside the temple, and security may turn you back. Leave your phone at your hotel or in the lockers.

When did Sai Baba take Samadhi?

On 15 October 1918, Vijayadashami. He was laid to rest in Buti Wada, the stone building that is now the Samadhi Mandir.
