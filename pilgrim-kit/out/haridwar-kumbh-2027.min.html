When is the Haridwar Kumbh Mela 2027?

The Haridwar Kumbh Mela 2027 runs from 14 January to 20 April 2027, with ten bathing (snan) days announced by the Haridwar mela administration. Three are Amrit Snans, led by the akharas: 6 March (Mahashivratri), 8 March (Phalguna Amavasya) and 14 April 2027 (Mesh Sankranti). The main ghat is Har Ki Pauri. No pilgrim registration has been announced yet.

Every few years Haridwar becomes the largest gathering on the Ganga. The 2027 mela is, by the traditional cycle, the Ardh Kumbh (अर्धकुंभ) — the half-way Kumbh held about six years after a full Kumbh. The Uttarakhand government has branded it Kumbh Mela-2027: on 12 October 2026 Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami unveiled its official logo, anthem and website, haridwarkumbh.uk.gov.in. You will see both names; they mean the same mela.

Pilgrims bathe at Har Ki Pauri (Brahma Kund) and the many ghats along the river. Haridwar's own hill shrines of Mansa Devi and Chandi Devi watch over the town — not to be confused with Mata Mansa Devi in Panchkula. The Kumbh is not the Simhastha Kumbh of Nashik and Trimbakeshwar, which falls in 2027 too but in a different season. See all our guides on the pilgrimage tours page.

The legend

The story of the Kumbh

Retold in our own words from the Puranic tradition — told as belief, not history.

  1. The churning of the oceanLong ago, it is said, the devas and asuras churned the ocean of milk together, and from its depths rose a kumbh — a pot — filled with amrit, the nectar of immortality.
  2. Drops on four tirthasAs the pot was carried away, drops of amrit are believed to have fallen at four places: Haridwar, Prayagraj, Ujjain and Nashik. The Kumbh moves between them, timed by the positions of Jupiter and the Sun.
  3. Where the Ganga meets the plainsAt Haridwar the Ganga leaves the Himalaya for the plains. Har Ki Pauri, the 'steps of Hari', is believed to hold the footprint of Vishnu.
  4. The snanOn the snan days pilgrims bathe in the Ganga, believing it washes away sins. On the Amrit Snan days the sadhus of the akharas go to the river first, in procession, before householders bathe.

The Amrit Snan was earlier called the Shahi Snan. In 2017 UNESCO inscribed the Kumbh Mela on its list of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

How to reach

How to reach Haridwar for the Kumbh

By rail

Haridwar Junction (HW) is about 3 km from Har Ki Pauri and is linked to Delhi and much of north India. Book early — trains fill up around the snan days.

By air

The nearest airport is Dehradun's Jolly Grant, roughly 35–40 km from Haridwar by road.

By road

Delhi is about 220 km away. Expect diversions, parking zones and vehicle bans inside the mela area on snan days — follow the mela police plan published before each snan.

Nearby

Rishikesh is about 25 km upstream; Kankhal, with the Daksh Prajapati temple of the Sati legend, is on Haridwar's southern edge.

Sample itinerary

A sample 3-day Haridwar Kumbh plan from Delhi

  1. Day 1
    Delhi → Haridwar

    Drive or take the train (about 220 km). Check in, walk to Har Ki Pauri and watch the evening Ganga Aarti.

  2. Day 2
    Snan day

    Bathe early at your chosen ghat, then visit the Mansa Devi and Chandi Devi hill shrines (ropeways) and Kankhal.

  3. Day 3
    Rishikesh → home

    A morning in Rishikesh, about 25 km upstream, then return to Delhi.

Before you go

Before you go

  1. Pick your dayThe three Amrit Snans are the most crowded days. Elders and families with children usually find the other snan days, or a weekday between February and early April, calmer.
  2. Keep the family togetherFix a meeting point before you enter the ghats, carry ID for everyone, and note the official lost-and-found (khoya-paya) centres.
  3. Beware of fake sitesEvery Kumbh brings fake 'tent booking' and 'VIP snan pass' sellers. Book only through the official website or the Uttarakhand government links listed on it.
  4. Ganga AartiThe aarti at Har Ki Pauri is held at sunrise and around sunset; the time shifts with the season, so confirm locally.
  5. Char Dham afterwardsThe Char Dham temples are closed in winter and their 2027 opening dates are not announced yet, so a Kumbh trip cannot include them before late April.
  6. Respect the riverBathe only at marked ghats, hold the chains in the current, and follow the instructions of the mela police and volunteers.

FAQ

Questions pilgrims ask

When is the Haridwar Kumbh 2027?

From 14 January to 20 April 2027, as announced by the Haridwar mela administration in April 2026.

What are the Amrit Snan dates of Haridwar Kumbh 2027?

6 March (Mahashivratri), 8 March (Phalguna Amavasya) and 14 April 2027 (Mesh Sankranti). On these days the akharas bathe first, in procession.

How many snan days are there in the Haridwar Kumbh 2027?

Ten: 14 Jan, 6 Feb, 11 Feb, 20 Feb, 6 Mar, 8 Mar, 7 Apr, 14 Apr, 15 Apr and 20 Apr 2027. Three of them are Amrit Snans.

Is the 2027 mela a Kumbh or an Ardh Kumbh?

By the traditional cycle it is the Ardh Kumbh, held about six years after a full Kumbh. The Uttarakhand government calls it Kumbh Mela-2027. Both names refer to the same mela.

Do I need to register for the Haridwar Kumbh?

No pilgrim registration has been announced so far. Check the official website, haridwarkumbh.uk.gov.in, before you travel, and ignore sites selling 'VIP snan passes'.

Where is the main bathing ghat in Haridwar?

Har Ki Pauri (Brahma Kund) is the main ghat; there are many more ghats along the Ganga in Haridwar.

Which day is best for elders and families?

A non-Amrit snan day, or a weekday between February and early April. The three Amrit Snan days are the most crowded.

Can I combine the Kumbh with the Char Dham Yatra?

Not during the mela's busy months: the Char Dham temples are closed in winter and their 2027 opening dates are not announced yet. Rishikesh and Haridwar's own shrines combine easily.
