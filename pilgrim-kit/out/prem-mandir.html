What are Prem Mandir Vrindavan timings, and when is the fountain show?

Prem Mandir is open every day, 8:30 am to 12 noon and 4:30 pm to 8:30 pm (official temple site). The musical fountain runs 7–7:30 pm from 1 Oct to 31 Mar and 7:30–8 pm from 1 Apr to 30 Sep. Arrive a little before sunset to see the marble lit in changing colours, then stay for the fountain and the 8:10 pm shayan aarti.

Prem Mandir stands on Bhaktivedanta Swami Marg at Chhatikara, the southern entrance to Vrindavan in Mathura district, Uttar Pradesh. It was founded by Jagadguru Shri Kripalu Ji Maharaj and is run by Jagadguru Kripalu Parishat. The foundation stone was laid on 14 Jan 2001, and after about twelve years of carving the temple was inaugurated with pran pratishtha on 15–17 Feb 2012. It is a modern temple — not an ancient one — and much of its beauty is in that fresh white stone.

Inside are Shri Radha Krishna and Shri Sita Ram. The temple is built of white Italian Carrara marble on a granite foundation about 20 ft deep, and measures about 125 ft high with the flag, 190 ft long and 128 ft wide. Its outer walls carry 84 carved panels of Radha Krishna leelas, and the gardens around it — about 55 acres in all — hold fountains and life-size tableaux of Krishna's leelas. Many pilgrims pair it with the old temples of Vrindavan and Mathura, or with Ram Mandir in Ayodhya; see all our pilgrimage guides.

The legend

Prem Mandir history and the leela of Vrindavan

Retold in our own words from the Bhagavata Purana and Braj tradition — as faith, not as a claim of history.

  1. Krishna's forestThe Bhagavata Purana remembers Vrindavan as the forest where Krishna spent his childhood among the cowherds of Braj, grazing cows on the banks of the Yamuna.
  2. Govardhan liftedWhen Indra sent a storm to punish Braj, it is believed Krishna raised Govardhan hill on one finger and sheltered everyone beneath it for seven days.
  3. Kaliya and the RaasHe subdued the serpent Kaliya in the Yamuna, and under the autumn full moon danced the Raas with Radha and the gopis — the image of selfless divine love.
  4. A temple of lovePrem Mandir tells these leelas in marble and in its gardens. Its founder taught bhakti, loving devotion, as the simplest path to God, and named the temple for that love.

The garden tableaux show the Jhulan, when Radha and Krishna swing together in the monsoon, Govardhan lifted against the storm, the Raas and the taming of Kaliya. Inside, images of the four earlier Jagadgurus, the Rasik saints and the Ashta-sakhis surround the shrine. On the upper level, Sita Ram honour the same devotion as it is told in the Ramayana.

How to reach

How to reach Prem Mandir, Vrindavan

By train

Mathura Junction, the nearest major station, is about 12 km by road. Autos, e-rickshaws and taxis run from the station to Vrindavan.

By air

Agra airport is about 70 km away. Delhi's IGI airport is about 147 km, mostly by the Yamuna Expressway.

By road from Delhi

Prem Mandir is on the main road into Vrindavan from the Chhatikara (NH-19) side, so it is often the first temple you reach. See our Delhi to Vrindavan taxi.

In Vrindavan

Banke Bihari temple is about 3 km away; ISKCON's Krishna-Balaram temple is a short drive on the same road. E-rickshaws link the temples.

Sample itinerary

A simple Vrindavan plan (1–2 days)

  1. Day 1
    Vrindavan: old temples and Prem Mandir

    Morning darshan at Prem Mandir from 8:30 am and a walk through the leela gardens. While it is closed (12–4:30 pm), see the old temples and lanes of Vrindavan and the Yamuna ghats — Banke Bihari is about 3 km away. Back by sunset for the lights, the fountain show and the 8:10 pm shayan aarti.

  2. Day 2
    Mathura and onward

    Mathura's temples in the morning, then drive on to Agra or Delhi — or stay another evening in Vrindavan.

Before you go

Before you go

  1. Come for the eveningArrive around 6:30 pm in winter to see the lights come on, watch the fountain and stay for the 8:10 pm shayan aarti.
  2. Mind the midday breakThe temple is closed from 12 noon to 4:30 pm — plan the old temples or lunch for those hours. Summer afternoons are very hot.
  3. Weekends are crowdedEvenings on weekends and holidays get very busy. Keep children and elders close, and fix a meeting point with your group.
  4. EldersThe lawns are flat and easy to walk; ask staff for the easiest way into the shrine. Phones, cameras and bags — ask at the gate.

FAQ

Questions pilgrims ask

What are Prem Mandir timings?

Every day from 8:30 am to 12 noon and from 4:30 pm to 8:30 pm, according to the official temple site. The shayan aarti is at 12 noon and 8:10 pm.

What time is the Prem Mandir fountain show?

The musical fountain runs 7 to 7:30 pm from 1 October to 31 March, and 7:30 to 8 pm from 1 April to 30 September. The temple is lit in changing colours after sunset.

Who built Prem Mandir?

It was founded by Jagadguru Shri Kripalu Ji Maharaj and built by Jagadguru Kripalu Parishat between 2001 and 2012, with about 1,000 artisans.

When was Prem Mandir inaugurated?

The inauguration and pran pratishtha were held on 15–17 February 2012. The foundation stone had been laid on 14 January 2001.

Which deities are in Prem Mandir?

Shri Radha Krishna and Shri Sita Ram.

What is Prem Mandir made of?

White Italian Carrara marble on a deep granite foundation. It rises about 125 ft including the flag, and its outer walls carry 84 carved panels of Radha Krishna leelas.

How far is Prem Mandir from Mathura station and Banke Bihari?

About 12 km by road from Mathura Junction and about 3 km from Banke Bihari temple.

What is the best time to visit Prem Mandir?

The evening — arrive before sunset to see the lights come on and stay for the fountain show. October to March is the most pleasant season.
