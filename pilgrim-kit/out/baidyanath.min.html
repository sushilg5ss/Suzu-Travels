Where is Baidyanath temple and what are its timings?

Baba Baidyanath Dham is in Deoghar town, Jharkhand, and is the most widely accepted of the 12 Jyotirlingas' Vaidyanath. The temple's website lists darshan from 4 am to 4 pm and 6 pm to 8 pm, with longer hours in Shravan and on Mahashivratri. Deoghar Airport is about 9 km away; Jasidih Junction about 7 km.

Deoghar means "abode of the gods", and for millions of Shiva devotees in eastern India it simply means Baba Dham. Here Shiva is worshipped as Vaidyanath — vaidya means physician — the Lord who heals. The Deoghar district administration describes the shrine as both one of the 12 Jyotirlingas and one of the 51 Shakti Peeths, a rare pairing in one place.

The Shiva Purana's list places Vaidyanath "चिताभूमौ" — in the land of the cremation ground — and tradition reads that as Deoghar. Every Shravan the town fills with saffron-clad kanwariyas who have walked from the Ganga at Sultanganj in Bihar to pour its water on the linga.

The legend

The legend of Baba Baidyanath

The Shiva Purana tells how Ravana's devotion brought a Jyotirlinga to Deoghar. This is how the story is told.

  1. Ravana's penanceRavana, king of Lanka, did fierce penance for Shiva, offering his heads one by one into the sacred fire.
  2. The Lord of PhysiciansPleased, Shiva restored him — and so is worshipped here as Vaidyanath, the healer. He gave Ravana a linga for Lanka, on one condition: wherever it first touched the ground, it would stay.
  3. A cowherd on the roadThe gods, worried about Ravana's power, made sure he had to stop on the way. A cowherd — said to be Vishnu in disguise, remembered locally as Baiju — held the linga and set it down.
  4. It stayed at DeogharRavana could not lift it again; it is said he pressed it with his thumb in anger. The linga has stayed at Deoghar ever since.

These are stories of faith, told as tradition rather than history. Tradition also holds that Sati's heart fell here, which is why the shrine is honoured as a Shakti Peeth (Hriday Peeth) — Shakti Peeth lists differ on the details.

Deoghar follows the Purnimanta (North-Indian) Shravan month, which begins about two weeks earlier than the Amanta Shravan used in Maharashtra. The district administration estimates 7–8 million devotees during the mela.

How to reach

How to reach Baidyanath Dham

By air

Deoghar Airport, opened in July 2022, is about 9 km from the temple. Patna and Kolkata airports have far more flights.

By rail

Baidyanath Dham station is in town on a branch line. Jasidih Junction, on the Howrah–Delhi main line, is about 7 km away.

By road

Patna is about 229 km, Kolkata about 315 km and Ranchi about 322 km by road (district administration).

At the temple

The temple is at town level — no climb. The challenge is the queue, not the terrain.

Before you go

Before you go

  1. Pick your seasonOctober to March is the most comfortable. Shravan (Jul–Aug) is the most sacred and by far the most crowded.
  2. Go early on a weekdayReach for the 4 am opening or the 6 pm reopening, and avoid Mondays if you want a calm darshan.
  3. Elders and queuesNo steps, but standing queues can last hours in peak season. Carry water and plan rest.
  4. Trikut ropewayThe Trikut Parvat ropeway was shut after the April 2022 accident; its current status is unverified — confirm locally.
Sultanganj to Deoghar

The kanwar yatra to Baba Dham

In Shravan, kanwariyas collect Ganga water at Sultanganj (Ajgaibinath) in Bihar and carry it on their shoulders in a kanwar, walking about 105–108 km to Deoghar to offer it on the Jyotirlinga. PTI counted about 53 lakh kanwariyas in 2025.

By custom, many pilgrims then go on to Basukinath in Dumka district, about 43 km away. If you are planning the walk with elders, plan rest stops and a vehicle to follow — and expect very dense crowds on Shravan Mondays.

Deoghar, Parli and Baijnath

Which is the real Vaidyanath Jyotirlinga?

Three temples are linked with the name. Deoghar (Jharkhand) is the most widely accepted, following the Shiva Purana's "चिताभूमौ". The popular Jyotirlinga smaranam says "परल्यां वैद्यनाथं", so Maharashtra's tradition honours Parli Vaijnath in Beed district. Baijnath in Kangra, Himachal — a stone temple of 1204 CE — has its own Ravana legend but is less often counted. We respect all three traditions.

Baidyanath is not Baijnath. The spellings are mixed up all over the web: this page is about Deoghar in Jharkhand.

FAQ

Questions pilgrims ask

What are Baba Baidyanath Dham darshan timings?

The temple's website lists doors open 4 am to 4 pm and 6 pm to 8 pm, with Shringar Puja at 6:10 pm. Hours are longer in Shravan and on Mahashivratri; confirm locally.

Where is the real Vaidyanath Jyotirlinga?

Deoghar in Jharkhand is the most widely accepted. Maharashtra's tradition honours Parli Vaijnath, and Baijnath in Himachal has its own Ravana legend.

What is the Shravani Mela?

A month-long fair in Shravan when kanwariyas walk about 105–108 km from Sultanganj in Bihar to offer Ganga water to Baba Baidyanath.

When is Shravani Mela 2027?

Not announced yet. It follows the North-Indian (Purnimanta) Shravan month, usually July–August; in 2026 it began on 30 July.

Is Baidyanath also a Shakti Peeth?

Yes, by tradition. The Deoghar district administration describes it as both a Jyotirlinga and one of the 51 Shakti Peeths, where Sati's heart is believed to have fallen.

Which is the nearest railway station and airport?

Baidyanath Dham station is in Deoghar town, Jasidih Junction on the main line is about 7 km away, and Deoghar Airport is about 9 km away.

Why is it called Vaidyanath?

Vaidya means physician. Tradition says Shiva healed Ravana here, so he is worshipped as Vaidyanath, the Lord of Physicians.

What else can I visit near Deoghar?

Basukinath (about 43 km), Naulakha Mandir, Tapovan and Trikut Parvat — check the Trikut ropeway's status locally.
