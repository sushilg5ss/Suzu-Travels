Where is Jakhu temple and why do pilgrims go there?

Jakhu temple stands on top of Jakhu Hill, the highest point of Shimla (about 2,450 m), roughly 2.5 km uphill east of the Ridge. It is dedicated to Lord Hanuman: local tradition says he paused here while carrying the Sanjeevani herb. A 108-ft Hanuman statue, unveiled in 2010, rises beside the old shrine. It is open every day of the year.

Jakhu is where Shimla goes to meet Hanuman. The small shrine sits in a ring of tall deodars on the crown of the hill, built in the Himachali kath-kuni manner of timber and stone, and a set of footprints kept at the temple is shown to pilgrims as Hanuman's. Beside it stands the bright orange 108-ft Hanuman statue, unveiled on 4 November 2010, which can be seen from much of the town below.

The name is believed to come from Yakku (Yaksha) Rishi, the sage of the local legend. You will see it spelt Jakhu, Jakhoo or Jakhu Mandir — it is one place. Tuesdays and Saturdays, Hanuman's days, bring the biggest everyday crowds. If you are travelling on through Himachal, pair it with Hidimba Devi in Manali or the Shakti Peeths of Himachal.

The legend

The story of Jakhu temple

Retold in our own words from the local tradition of Shimla — told as belief, not history. The Valmiki Ramayana does not name Jakhu, and tellings differ.

  1. Lakshmana fallsIn the battle of Lanka, Lakshmana lay gravely wounded. Only the Sanjeevani herb from the Himalaya could save him, and Hanuman flew north to fetch it.
  2. A sage on the peakOn the way, local tradition says, Hanuman came down on this peak, where the sage Yakku Rishi sat in meditation — to rest, or to ask the way to the herb.
  3. The hill bowsIt is said the hilltop flattened under Hanuman's weight. He promised the sage he would return, then hurried on — Lakshmana's life could not wait.
  4. The footprintsHanuman never came back. The sage, it is believed, raised a shrine where he had stood, and the footprints shown at the temple are honoured as his.

Some tellings place the halt on the outward flight, others on the way back to Lanka; all agree on the sage and the footprints. Jakhu is old — a postcard from 1912 already shows the temple and its monkeys — but no founding date is recorded, so we don't print one.

The hill is also the centre of Shimla's Dussehra, which has been held at Jakhu since it moved from Annadale in 1972. In 2010 the 108-ft Hanuman statue was unveiled on the hilltop, and it has become the landmark people look for on the Shimla skyline.

How to reach

How to reach Jakhu temple

On foot from the Ridge

About 2.5 km, steep uphill through the deodars — around half an hour to an hour depending on your pace. It is hard work at this altitude, so go slowly.

By taxi

Local taxis drive up to near the top of the hill; from there it is a short walk to the shrine. Escalators have been installed for the final steep climb (check that they are running).

By ropeway

A ropeway (opened 2017) runs up Jakhu Hill from near central Shimla when it is operating — check on the day. It is not the new Shimla city ropeway, which is still being built.

Reaching Shimla

By the Kalka–Shimla toy train (UNESCO World Heritage) from Kalka, about 93 km by road below; by air to Shimla (Jubbarhatti), about 27 km, or Chandigarh, about 127 km. From our office at Ghumarwin it is about 107 km — roughly 2 h 10 min by car.

Before you go

Before you go

  1. Mind the monkeysJakhu's rhesus monkeys are bold: they snatch spectacles, phones, food and bags. Keep glasses and phones put away, carry no food in your hands, and please don't feed them.
  2. Elders and childrenThe walk is steep at about 2,450 m. Take a taxi to the top area and use the escalators if they are running.
  3. Best seasonOpen all year. Snow and ice are possible on the paths from December to February; May–June is the busiest tourist season.
  4. Combine itThe Ridge and Mall Road, Christ Church below the hill, and Kufri further up the Hindustan–Tibet road.

FAQ

Questions pilgrims ask

Where is Jakhu temple?

On top of Jakhu Hill, the highest point of Shimla at about 2,450 m, roughly 2.5 km uphill east of the Ridge.

How tall is the Hanuman statue at Jakhu?

108 feet. It was unveiled on 4 November 2010 and can be seen from much of Shimla.

What are Jakhu temple timings?

The temple is open every day. Official darshan hours and aarti times are not published, so please confirm locally and plan to go in daylight.

How do I go up to Jakhu temple?

Walk about 2.5 km uphill from the Ridge, take a taxi to near the top, or use the Jakhu ropeway when it is running. Escalators help with the final steps.

Are the monkeys at Jakhu dangerous?

They are bold and grab glasses, phones and food. Keep these out of sight, don't carry food in your hands and don't feed them.

What is the story of Jakhu temple?

Local tradition says Hanuman paused on this peak, where Yakku Rishi was meditating, while flying to fetch the Sanjeevani herb. Footprints at the temple are honoured as his.

When is Jakhu temple most crowded?

On Tuesdays and Saturdays, Hanuman Jayanti, Dussehra and in the May–June holiday season.

How far is Jakhu temple from Shimla railway station?

A short taxi ride across town. Kalka, the broad-gauge railhead, is about 93 km by road, and Chandigarh airport about 127 km.
