3. Packing list for elders

  • Photo ID for every traveller (hotel check-in)
  • Regular medicines for the whole trip + prescriptions
  • BP / sugar monitor if used at home
  • Comfortable non-slip shoes that slip off easily (shoes come off at every temple)
  • Socks for cold temple floors
  • Warm layer or shawl — Kangra evenings are cool
  • Umbrella or rain jacket in July–August
  • Cap, sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Water bottle and light dry snacks for queues
  • Small notes and coins for prasad and offerings
  • Walking stick, knee cap or support belt if used
  • Power bank and a list of emergency contacts
  • Modest clothes; a scarf or cap to cover the head where asked
  • Small cushion or folding seat for long queues
At the temples: go early, especially in Navratri · visit the Bhairava shrine too — tradition says he guards every peetha · no photos inside the sanctum · follow each shrine's notices · tell your driver about mobility needs so he can drop you closest to the gate.
