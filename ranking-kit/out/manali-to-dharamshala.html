Manali to Dharamshala is about 235 km by road and takes 6–7 hours by car — down the Kullu valley and through the Aut tunnel to Mandi on NH-3, then NH-154 via Jogindernagar, Baijnath and Palampur to Dharamshala. Buses, including HRTC services, take about 7–9 hours. The road is open all year; the slowest part is the narrow, winding stretch between Mandi and Baijnath, and in December–February drivers may need snow chains after a fresh fall higher up. Good stops on the way: Bir (a short detour near Baijnath) and the tea gardens of Palampur.

  • DistanceAbout 235 km: Manali → Kullu → Mandi → Jogindernagar → Baijnath → Palampur → Dharamshala.

Winter

Manali to Dharamshala in winter

The highway stays open in winter. The cold, shaded bends near Manali and Kullu can be icy in the early morning after snow, and drivers sometimes carry chains from December to February — start after sunrise. Dharamshala town rarely gets heavy snow, but McLeodganj and the Dhauladhar above often do: see Dharamshala snowfall and Dharamshala in December.

Plan

Making it a two-day trip

If you have time, break the drive at Bir or Palampur: Manali → Bir on day one (about 5 hours), with an evening at the landing site; then Bir → Palampur → Dharamshala on day two, with a stop at the Baijnath temple. Coming from Delhi instead? See Delhi to Dharamshala.

FAQ

Manali to Dharamshala: common questions

What is the distance from Manali to Dharamshala?

About 235 km by road via Kullu, Mandi, Jogindernagar, Baijnath and Palampur.

How long does Manali to Dharamshala take?

6–7 hours by car and about 7–9 hours by bus.

Is there a bus from Manali to Dharamshala?

Yes, HRTC and private buses run on this route, including overnight services; book ahead in season.

What are the best stops between Manali and Dharamshala?

Mandi for lunch, Bir for the paragliding landing site, Baijnath's old temple and Palampur's tea gardens.

Is the Manali–Dharamshala road open in winter?

Yes; start after sunrise, as shaded bends near Manali can be icy after snow.

How far is McLeodganj from Dharamshala?

About 10 km uphill from the main town.
