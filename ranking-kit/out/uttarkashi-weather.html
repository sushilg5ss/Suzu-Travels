Uttarkashi is the district town of the upper Bhagirathi valley, at about 1,160 m and about 145 km from Rishikesh — the last big town on the way to Gangotri, and the base for treks such as Dodital and Dayara Bugyal. Winters are cool rather than snowy in town: January days are around 16 °C and nights a few degrees above freezing, while Harsil and Gangotri higher up are under snow. Summer afternoons are warm, around 30 °C in May–June. July and August are the wettest months, with cloudbursts and landslides on the highway. March–June and September–November are the most comfortable months.

The Bhagirathi valley

Uttarkashi as a base: Gangotri, Harsil and the treks

Uttarkashi sits on the Bhagirathi with the Kashi Vishwanath temple in town and the Nehru Institute of Mountaineering just above it. On the Char Dham route it is the usual night stop between Yamunotri and Gangotri. Harsil is about 75 km up the valley and Gangotri about 100 km; both are much colder and get snow in winter.

It is also the starting point for treks such as Dodital and Dayara Bugyal, which are popular in spring, autumn and, for snow, in winter. In the monsoon, landslides and cloudbursts regularly close the road towards Gangotri, sometimes for days — keep spare time in July–August.

Before you go

Uttarkashi trip: what the weather means for you

Clothing: a warm jacket for winter evenings and for any trip up the valley; light layers in summer; rain gear and good shoes in the monsoon.

Getting there: Uttarkashi is about 145 km from Rishikesh and about 170 km from Dehradun's Jolly Grant airport, via Chamba or via Mussoorie and Suwakholi. Cab: Char Dham yatra taxi.

What is the weather in Uttarkashi today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Uttarkashi, Harsil and Gangotri (MET Norway data).

Does it snow in Uttarkashi?

Rarely in town; Harsil, Dayara Bugyal and Gangotri higher up get regular winter snow.

How cold is Uttarkashi in winter?

January days are about 16 °C and nights about 3 °C (an estimate), with frosty mornings.

Which are the wettest months in Uttarkashi?

July and August; landslides often close the highway towards Gangotri.

When is the best time to visit Uttarkashi?

March–June and September–November.

How far is Uttarkashi from Gangotri?

About 100 km by road, via Harsil.
