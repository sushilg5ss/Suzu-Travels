When it snows

When does it snow in Auli?

Auli is a set of wide, open meadows on the slopes above Joshimath in Chamoli district, facing Nanda Devi, Dronagiri and the peaks of the Garhwal Himalaya. Its height and its long, open slopes are why it became India's best-known ski area outside Kashmir.

The first snow usually reaches the higher slopes in November or December, with a proper snow cover building by late December in a normal year. January and February are the most reliable months, and March often still has good snow up high.

Last winter, a strong western disturbance brought heavy snowfall to Auli on 23 January 2026, together with Badrinath, Kedarnath, Mussoorie and Munsiyari. The Joshimath–Auli road, which leads to the National Skiing Centre, was closed for a time because of the accumulated snow — a good reminder to keep a spare day in your plan.

Plan safely

Reaching Auli in snow season

Road: most travellers drive from Delhi, Dehradun, Haridwar or Rishikesh to Joshimath along the Alaknanda valley — a long mountain day — then up the 16 km road to Auli. After heavy snow, the Joshimath–Auli road can close until it is cleared.

Cable car: the Joshimath–Auli cable car has been closed since January 2023 and remained closed in 2026, so plan on the road. The smaller chairlift on the slopes is separate.

Skiing: lessons and equipment hire are available at Auli in season; book ahead for January–February weekends and the holiday weeks.

Clothes: proper winter gear — thermals, a down jacket, waterproof gloves, a cap and snow boots. Nights at Auli fall well below freezing in January.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Which month is best for snowfall in Auli?

January and February are the most reliable months for snow, and the best for skiing. Late December often has snow too, and March can still be good up high.

When did Auli last get heavy snow?

Auli had heavy snowfall on 23 January 2026, which closed the Joshimath–Auli road for a time because of the accumulated snow.

Is the Auli ropeway open?

The Joshimath–Auli cable car has been closed since January 2023 and remained closed in 2026, so you drive the 16 km road from Joshimath. The 800 m chairlift on the slopes is separate and usually reopens before Christmas.

When is the skiing season in Auli?

Usually January to March, once the snow base is deep enough. Lessons and equipment hire are available at Auli in season.

How do I reach Auli in winter?

Drive to Joshimath from Delhi, Dehradun, Haridwar or Rishikesh, then take the 16 km road up to Auli. Dehradun's Jolly Grant airport is the nearest airport.
