Kullu (about 1,220 m) sits low in the Beas valley, so it is much warmer than Manali. India Meteorological Department averages for Bhuntar, the Kullu–Manali airport (1991–2020), put January as the coldest month (about 16 °C by day and 1.6 °C at night) and June as the warmest (about 33 °C by day, 17 °C at night). July–August are the wettest months, followed by March. Snow rarely settles in Kullu town — for reliable snow go up to Manali–Solang (40–55 km), Bijli Mahadev or Jalori Pass. October and November are the clearest, driest months.

Snow

Does it snow in Kullu? Where to find it

Because Kullu lies on the valley floor, snow falls in town only in some years and rarely settles — even January nights average above freezing. The peaks around the valley stay white from December to February.

Manali and Solang (40–55 km): the most reliable snow in the valley — Snowfall in Manali.

Bijli Mahadev (~2,460 m): directly above Kullu; snow makes the winter trek hard — Bijli Mahadev trek.

Jalori Pass (~3,120 m): towards Banjar and Jibhi; often closed by heavy snow in winter. Nearby: Jibhi.

Atal Tunnel–Sissu: the surest snow, but the road depends on the weather. Latest conditions: snow tracker.

Monsoon

Kullu in the monsoon: watch the roads

In July and August the Beas runs high and landslides between Mandi and Kullu sometimes close the Kiratpur–Manali highway for hours. Rafting is usually suspended, and a spare day in the plan is wise.

Bhuntar takes small aircraft and weather cancellations are common — see Kullu–Manali airport.

FAQ

Kullu weather: questions

What is the weather in Kullu today?

The live forecast at the top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days for Kullu, Kasol and Manali, using MET Norway data.

Does it snow in Kullu?

Only in some years and lightly in Kullu town. For reliable snow go to Manali–Solang (40–55 km), Bijli Mahadev or Jalori Pass, from late December to February.

How cold is Kullu in December?

IMD averages for Bhuntar are about 18 °C by day and 1.5 °C at night, with frost common. The record low is −5.2 °C.

When does it rain in Kullu?

Most in July–August (~130 mm a month) and then March (~117 mm). October and November are the driest months.

What is the best time to visit Kullu?

March–June and September–November. Kullu Dussehra is in October; for snow, go from late December to February and continue to Manali–Solang.

How different is the weather in Kullu and Manali?

Manali is about 800 m higher and usually 4–6 °C colder; it gets snow in winter, while Kullu mostly does not.
