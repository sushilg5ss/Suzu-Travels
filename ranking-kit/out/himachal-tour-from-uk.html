From the UK, fly nonstop to Delhi (about 9 hours from London) and continue by road, or by the historic Kalka–Shimla toy train, into Himachal. Most British visitors start in Shimla, the old summer capital of British India, with Christ Church, the Mall and the Viceregal Lodge, then go on to Dharamshala and McLeod Ganj for Tibetan culture and the Dhauladhar, or north to Manali and, in summer, Spiti. The best UK holiday windows are October half-term (clear skies), Christmas (cold, with snow likely from late December) and Easter (spring); the long summer break falls in the Indian monsoon, so choose Spiti or Ladakh then. UK passport holders can apply for an Indian e-Visa online.

Heritage

Shimla and the hill stations of the Raj

Shimla became the summer capital of British India in 1864. Walk the Mall and the Ridge to Christ Church, see the Viceregal Lodge on Observatory Hill (now the Indian Institute of Advanced Study), and ride the narrow-gauge Kalka–Shimla railway, opened in 1903 and now a UNESCO World Heritage site, through 100-plus tunnels and over arched bridges. Smaller hill stations — Barog and Dagshai, Kasauli, Naldehra and Mashobra and Dalhousie — keep the old cottages, churches and pine walks.

Further north, McLeod Ganj above Dharamshala is home to the Tibetan government-in-exile and the Tsuglagkhang temple — see Dharamshala for international travellers.

Getting there

Flights, trains and visas

Nonstop flights run from London to Delhi daily (about 9 hours); from other UK airports you usually connect. From Delhi, drive to Shimla (about 345 km) or take a train to Kalka and the toy train up — see Delhi to Shimla for foreigners and our toy train status. For Dharamshala, fly to Gaggal (Kangra) or drive from Delhi or Amritsar — see Amritsar to Dharamshala.

UK passport holders can apply for an Indian e-Visa online before travel. Foreign nationals need an Inner Line Permit for the Kinnaur–Spiti border stretch; we arrange it with a Spiti trip.

FAQ

Himachal from the UK: frequently asked questions

How long is the flight from London to Delhi?

About 9 hours nonstop.

Do UK citizens need a visa for India?

Yes. UK passport holders can apply for an Indian e-Visa online; check the official site before booking.

When is the best time to visit Himachal from the UK?

October half-term for clear skies, Christmas for snow, Easter for spring; choose Spiti or Ladakh in July–August.

Can we ride the Kalka–Shimla toy train?

Yes, when services are running; book ahead, and check the current status before travel.

Is there snow in Shimla at Christmas?

Sometimes, usually from late December; Kufri and Narkanda nearby are more reliable.

How many days do we need?

8–10 days for Shimla with Dharamshala or Manali; 12–14 days to add Spiti.
