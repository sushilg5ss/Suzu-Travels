Solan is the headquarters of Solan district, at about 1,500 m on the Kalka–Shimla highway and toy-train line, roughly 45 km before Shimla. In and near town, visit the Shoolini Mata temple (Solan takes its name from the goddess), the Jatoli Shiva temple (about 8 km on the Rajgarh road), the hill and cave of Karol Tibba, and the Menri Bon monastery at Dolanji. Within an hour are Barog and Dagshai, Kasauli, Chail (about 38 km) and Arki Fort (about 50 km). Solan rarely gets snow; October–March brings the clearest days.

Getting there

How to reach Solan

By road: Solan is on the Chandigarh–Shimla highway — about 65–70 km from Chandigarh (around 2 hours) and about 45 km from Shimla. Buses on this route stop in Solan. By toy train: the Kalka–Shimla railway stops at Barog and Solan; check the current status before you plan — see our Shimla toy train guide. By air: Chandigarh airport is the practical gateway.

Plan

A 1–2 day Solan plan

Day 1: morning at the Shoolini temple and the bazaar, Jatoli temple before lunch, Menri monastery at Dolanji in the afternoon. Day 2: choose one direction — Barog and Kasauli towards Chandigarh, Chail on the way to Shimla, or Arki Fort to the west — and continue your trip from there.

FAQ

Solan: common questions

What is Solan famous for?

Its mushrooms (it is called India's mushroom city), tomatoes, the Mohan Meakins brewery of 1855 and the Shoolini Mata temple after which it is named.

How far is Solan from Shimla?

About 45 km by road, roughly 1.5 hours.

How far is Solan from Chandigarh?

About 65–70 km by road, around 2 hours.

Does it snow in Solan?

Rarely in the town itself; Chail, Kufri and Shimla have better snow odds.

What is the Menri monastery?

The main monastery of the Bon religious tradition outside Tibet, at Dolanji near Solan.

Which is better for a stop, Solan or Kasauli?

Kasauli is quieter and more scenic for an overnight stay; Solan is handy for its temples and as a base for Chail and Arki.
