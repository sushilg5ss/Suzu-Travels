The main Kufri tourist places are Mahasu Peak (by pony or yak), the Himalayan Nature Park, Kufri Fun World, the Green Valley viewpoint, Chini Bungalow, the Snow Kingdom indoor snow park and, a few kilometres on, the Fagu ridge. Kufri is 16–20 km from Shimla on NH-5 via Dhalli, about 30–45 minutes, at roughly 2,500–2,700 m — high enough to turn white more often than Shimla. Snow usually comes between mid-December and February, with January the most reliable month, but no date is certain. A half-day (3–5 hours) covers the highlights; in snow season leave early, because police stop traffic beyond Dhalli after heavy snowfall.

Snow

When does it snow in Kufri?

Kufri sits roughly 300 m above Shimla, so a western disturbance that brings sleet to the town often leaves Kufri white. Most winters see a handful of snow spells between the second half of December and late February; fresh snow can stay on the slopes for a week or more if nights stay cold. Early spells happen — Kufri got snow on 16 November 2020 — but no date is promised, and in 2025–26 the first snow came only on 23 January 2026.

Getting there

How to reach Kufri

Kufri is on NH-5 (the old Hindustan–Tibet road), 16–20 km from Shimla via Dhalli. From Chandigarh it is about 135 km and from Delhi about 365 km — most people stay in Shimla and visit Kufri for half a day. In snow season the Dhalli–Kufri stretch is the first to close; police stop ordinary cars beyond Dhalli after heavy snow until the road is cleared, so keep the Kufri day flexible. FAQ

Kufri: common questions

What are the best tourist places in Kufri?

Mahasu Peak, the Himalayan Nature Park, Kufri Fun World, Snow Kingdom, the Green Valley viewpoint, Chini Bungalow and the Fagu ridge.

How far is Kufri from Shimla?

About 16–20 km on NH-5 via Dhalli, 30–45 minutes on a clear road.

When does it snow in Kufri?

Usually between mid-December and February, with January the most reliable month; no date is certain.

Is half a day enough for Kufri?

Yes, 3–5 hours covers Mahasu Peak, the nature park and a ride or two. Add Fagu, Chini Bungalow or Chail for a full day.

Can I see snow in Kufri when it is not snowing?

Yes, the Snow Kingdom indoor park keeps real snow all year.

Is the Kufri road open after snowfall?

After heavy snow the police stop ordinary cars beyond Dhalli until the road is cleared, usually within hours to a day.
