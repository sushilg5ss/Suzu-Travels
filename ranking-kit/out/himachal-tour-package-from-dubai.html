For a snow holiday from the UAE, fly to Delhi or Chandigarh and go to Shimla–Kufri–Narkanda and Manali–Solang between late December and February, when snow is most likely. Flights from Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah to Delhi take about 3½ hours. A comfortable family trip is 6–7 days: two nights near Shimla for Kufri and Narkanda, three nights in Manali for Solang and the Atal Tunnel, and a buffer day for weather. Indian passport holders and OCI cardholders need no visa; other UAE residents usually apply for an Indian e-Visa online, and UAE nationals can also get a visa on arrival at major Indian airports including Delhi. The UAE winter school break (usually mid-December to early January) is the busiest time — book hotels and cars early.

When to go

When to visit Himachal from the UAE

Gulf families usually travel in three windows. The winter school break (mid-December to early January) has cold, clear days and the first real snow, usually from late December. January and February bring the most reliable snow at Kufri, Narkanda and Solang. Spring break and the Eid holidays fall in spring in 2027 (Eid al-Fitr is expected around 9–10 March and Eid al-Adha around 16–17 May, depending on the moon) — Manali still has snow at Solang and Sissu in March, while May is pleasant and green with snow only at the high passes.

The UAE summer holidays (July–August) fall in the Indian monsoon, when the lower hills are wet. If you travel then, choose the rain-shadow high valleys — Spiti or Ladakh — or come in June before the rains.

Getting there

Flying in from Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah

Most travellers fly to Delhi, which has many daily flights from all three UAE airports. From Delhi it is about 345 km to Shimla and 540 km to Manali by road, or you can take a short domestic flight to Shimla or Kullu (Bhuntar) when weather allows. Some airlines also fly from the UAE to Chandigarh and Amritsar, which are closer to the hills — check current schedules.

We meet you at the airport with a private car and a driver used to mountain and snow roads. See our Delhi to Shimla and Delhi to Manali guides, and the Himachal distance chart for drive times.

Visas

Do I need a visa?

Indian passport holders and OCI cardholders living in the UAE need no visa. Other UAE residents (for example, many expatriate nationalities) usually apply for an Indian e-Visa online before travel. UAE nationals can apply for an e-Visa or use India's visa-on-arrival facility, which is available at several major airports including Delhi. Rules change, so always check the official Indian visa website before you book.

Sample plan

A 7-day Himachal snow holiday from the UAE

Day 1: fly into Delhi or Chandigarh, drive to Shimla (or overnight in Chandigarh if you land late). Day 2: Kufri and Mashobra snow day. Day 3: Narkanda, or Shimla's Mall Road and Ridge. Day 4: drive to Manali. Day 5: Solang valley — snow play and ropeway. Day 6: Atal Tunnel and Sissu, Hadimba temple and Old Manali. Day 7: drive or fly back for your flight home.

Packing: a padded waterproof jacket, thermal layers, gloves, woollen caps and waterproof boots with grip for every family member; snow suits and boots can also be hired at Solang and Kufri. Food: vegetarian and North Indian food is everywhere; tell us if you need halal meals and we will suggest hotels and restaurants. Money: cards work in hotels and larger shops; carry Indian rupees for small purchases.

FAQ

Himachal from Dubai and the UAE: frequently asked questions

When is the best time to see snow in Himachal from the UAE?

Late December to February; January is the most reliable month at Kufri, Narkanda and Solang.

How long is the flight from Dubai to Delhi?

About 3½ hours. Abu Dhabi and Sharjah flights take about the same.

Do UAE residents need a visa for India?

Indian passport holders and OCI cardholders do not. Most other residents apply for an Indian e-Visa; UAE nationals can also use visa on arrival at major airports. Check the official Indian visa website before booking.

How many days do we need for Shimla and Manali?

6–7 days is comfortable for a family, including a buffer day for weather.

Is Himachal safe for families in winter?

Yes, with a good hotel, warm clothes and a driver experienced on snow roads. We avoid night driving and keep spare time for roads that close after heavy snow.

Can we get halal food in Shimla and Manali?

Ask us when you book; we can suggest hotels and restaurants that serve halal meals. Vegetarian food is widely available.
