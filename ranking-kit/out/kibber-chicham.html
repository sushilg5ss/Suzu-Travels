Kibber is a village at about 4,270 m, about 18 km above Kaza by way of Key Monastery, and the base for the Kibber Wildlife Sanctuary. A few kilometres on, the Chicham bridge — a suspension bridge opened in 2017 — spans the deep Samba Lamba gorge between Kibber and Chicham village, at about 4,100 m; it is often described as one of the highest bridges in Asia. In summer (May–October) you can drive to both villages, walk the fields and look for blue sheep on the slopes. In winter (January–March), Kibber is the main base for snow leopard trips with local trackers. Allow half a day from Kaza, and at least two nights in Spiti before you go this high.

Kibber

Kibber village and the wildlife sanctuary

Kibber's whitewashed stone houses sit on a broad shelf of land under the Kanamo peak, surrounded by barley and pea fields in summer. It is one of the highest permanently inhabited villages in the region and was long described as one of the highest villages in the world with a motor road.

The Kibber Wildlife Sanctuary, set up in 1992, covers the high slopes around the village. It protects blue sheep (bharal), ibex, red fox and the snow leopard. In winter, when snow drives the blue sheep lower, local trackers lead small groups from Kibber to watch for snow leopards with spotting scopes — see snow leopard safari in Spiti and snow leopard conservation in Spiti.

Chicham

Chicham bridge and village

Before the bridge, villagers crossed the Samba Lamba gorge in a box on a cable. The Chicham bridge, opened in 2017, now carries a road across the gorge, which drops hundreds of metres below. Stop on the far side to look back, and walk only on the bridge's footway — do not climb the rails.

Chicham village beyond is small and quiet, with fields and a view back across the gorge to Kibber. A road continues from Chicham towards Kee and Kaza, making a short loop possible when it is open.

Getting there

How to reach Kibber and Chicham

From Kaza, the road climbs past Key Monastery to Kibber (about 18 km, 45 minutes) and on to the Chicham bridge. In winter the road is cleared after snowfall but can stay icy. Reach Spiti via Shimla and Kinnaur all year, or via Manali in summer — see Spiti via Shimla or Manali.

Kibber is high: spend at least two nights at Kaza's height first, drink plenty of water and avoid exertion on the first day.

FAQ

Kibber and Chicham: frequently asked questions

How far is Kibber from Kaza?

About 18 km via Key Monastery, roughly 45 minutes by car.

How high is Kibber?

About 4,270 m above sea level.

When did the Chicham bridge open?

In 2017. It spans the Samba Lamba gorge between Kibber and Chicham.

Is Chicham bridge the highest in Asia?

It is often described as one of the highest bridges in Asia, at about 4,100 m.

When is the best time to see snow leopards near Kibber?

January to March, with local trackers; sightings are never certain.

Can I stay in Kibber?

Yes, there are homestays; Kaza has more choice if you prefer a hotel.
