Kishtwar (about 1,634 m) is the main town of the Chenab valley in eastern Jammu, about 212 km from Jammu and 210 km from Srinagar. Its climate is close to neighbouring Bhaderwah's: by IMD averages for Bhaderwah (1991–2020), January is about 11.6 °C by day and −0.4 °C at night and July about 30 °C, with the most rain in February–March and July–August and snow in winter. Sinthan Top, the high pass on the Kishtwar–Anantnag road, is far colder and snowbound for much of the year; the road is usually open only from about late spring to autumn. April–June and September–October are the most pleasant months in Kishtwar.

High roads

Sinthan Top, Paddar and Machail: when the roads are open

Sinthan Top (about 3,700 m) on the Kishtwar–Daksum–Kokernag road links the Chenab valley with south Kashmir. It is snowbound in winter and the road is usually open only from about late spring to autumn — check the J&K Traffic Police advisory before planning a crossing.

Paddar valley, with villages such as Gulabgarh and Machail, lies further up the Chenab towards the Himachal border. The Machail Mata yatra is held in summer; in winter the high villages are snowbound.

Monsoon rain (July–August) can bring landslides on the Chenab valley roads. Nearby weather: Anantnag weather, Bhaderwah weather and Patnitop weather.

What is the weather in Kishtwar today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Kishtwar, Sinthan Top and Bhaderwah (MET Norway data).

Does it snow in Kishtwar?

Yes, on some winter days in town, and heavily on the heights such as Sinthan Top and Paddar.

Is Sinthan Top open in winter?

No — it is snowbound in winter; the road is usually open only from about late spring to autumn.

What is the best time to visit Kishtwar?

April–June and September–October.

How far is Kishtwar from Jammu?

About 212 km; Srinagar is about 210 km away.
