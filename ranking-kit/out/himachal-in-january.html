January is Himachal's coldest month and the most reliable month for snow. Shimla averages about 9 °C by day and 2 °C at night, Manali about 10.5 °C and −1 °C with five to six snow or rain days, and lower Dharamshala about 16 °C and 6 °C. High places are far colder: Kaza in Spiti averages about −13 °C at night. Snow is likeliest at Kufri and Narkanda near Shimla, at Solang and Sissu near Manali, at Dalhousie's Dainkund and on the Dhauladhar above McLeod Ganj. Roads usually reopen within hours to a day or two after heavy snow. New Year week is crowded and costly; the rest of January is quieter.

  • ColdestSpiti and Lahaul (−13 °C to −25 °C nights); Manali and Shimla below freezing on many nights.
  • Snow oddsHighest at Kufri, Narkanda, Solang, Sissu, Dainkund; lower in Shimla and Manali towns.
  • RoadsShimla and Manali stay reachable; Solang–Atal Tunnel–Sissu and Kufri–Narkanda close after heavy snow.
  • Crowds25 Dec – 2 Jan is peak; mid and late January are quiet.
  • PackThermals, a down or padded jacket, gloves, cap and shoes with grip.

Roads

Which roads close in January?

Shimla and Manali themselves stay reachable through January; the main highways are cleared quickly, though a heavy snowfall can stop traffic for a few hours to a day.

Manali → Solang → Atal Tunnel → Sissu: after snow, the police often stop private cars at Solang or Palchan until the road is cleared; 4x4s and chains may be required. Shimla → Kufri → Narkanda: traffic is held at Dhalli or Kufri after heavy snow. Spiti is reachable only through Shimla and Kinnaur; the Manali–Kunzum road is shut all winter. Jalori Pass and the Hatu road are often closed.

Travel in daylight with a driver who knows snow roads, keep a buffer day, and check the forecast the night before.

FAQ

Himachal in January: common questions

Is January a good time to visit Himachal?

Yes, if you want snow and can handle the cold. It is the coldest month and the best for snow near Shimla and Manali; pack warm and keep a buffer day for weather.

Will I see snow in Shimla in January?

Possibly, but far from certain in town. Kufri and Narkanda, higher up, have much better odds.

Is the Atal Tunnel open in January?

The tunnel itself stays open, but after snowfall the approach from Solang is often closed to private cars until the road is cleared.

How cold is Manali in January?

About 10.5 °C by day and −1 °C at night on average, colder after a snowfall.

Can I visit Spiti in January?

Yes, only via Shimla and Kinnaur, with a winter-experienced driver; nights fall to −15 °C to −25 °C.

What should I pack for Himachal in January?

Thermal layers, a down or padded jacket, gloves, a woollen cap, warm socks and shoes with good grip.
