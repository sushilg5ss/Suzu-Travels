January is Rishikesh's coolest month, but it is mild by day. Afternoons average about 20 °C and nights about 7 °C (Dehradun IMD normals, used as a reference), with cold, often foggy mornings by the river. It never snows here, which makes Rishikesh a comfortable winter choice for yoga, the evening Ganga aarti, walks along the river and day trips. In 2027 there is one big difference: the Ardh Kumbh in Haridwar, about 25 km away, begins on 14 January 2027 (Makar Sankranti), so expect heavy traffic and crowds around the bathing dates.

January weather

What is the weather like in Rishikesh in January?

Rishikesh sits where the Ganga leaves the hills, at about 340 m. January days are usually sunny and pleasant, around 20 °C in the afternoon, while nights fall to about 7 °C and mornings by the river are cold and often misty. A western disturbance brings a few grey or rainy days in most Januaries.

Rishikesh has no recent published IMD normals, so the figures below are Dehradun's (IMD 1991–2020), about 40 km away and a little higher — Rishikesh is usually similar or slightly warmer by day.

Rishikesh in January: Kumbh dates, travel and packing

Ardh Kumbh 2027: the Kumbh in Haridwar runs from 14 January to 20 April 2027. The January–February bathing dates are 14 January (Makar Sankranti), 6 February (Mauni Amavasya), 11 February (Basant Panchami) and 20 February (Magh Purnima) — around these days, roads between Rishikesh and Haridwar are very busy, so plan your arrival and departure on other days.

Getting there: Dehradun's Jolly Grant airport is about 20 km away; Haridwar and Rishikesh have railway stations. Winter fog can delay morning trains and flights. Cab: Dehradun taxi service.

Packing: a warm jacket and layers for mornings and evenings, light clothes for the afternoon sun, and comfortable shoes for the ghats.

FAQ

Rishikesh in January: frequently asked questions

Is January a good time to visit Rishikesh?

Yes — days are mild and sunny, ideal for yoga, the Ganga aarti and walks. Mornings are cold and foggy, and the Kumbh in Haridwar brings crowds around the bathing dates.

How cold is Rishikesh in January?

About 20 °C by day and 7 °C at night on average (Dehradun IMD reference).

Does it snow in Rishikesh?

No. The nearest places with a chance of snow in January are Mussoorie, Dhanaulti and Surkanda Devi.

When does the Ardh Kumbh 2027 start?

On 14 January 2027 (Makar Sankranti) in Haridwar; it runs until 20 April 2027.

How far is Rishikesh from Haridwar?

About 25 km by road.

How far is Rishikesh from Dehradun airport?

About 20 km from Jolly Grant airport.
