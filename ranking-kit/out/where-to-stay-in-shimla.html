For a first visit, stay near Mall Road and the Ridge — you can walk to Scandal Point, Christ Church and Lakkar Bazaar — but remember the Mall is car-free, so luggage goes up by lift, porter or the hotel's own vehicle permit. If you are driving or travelling with elders, a hotel on Cart Road or Circular Road with parking, or in quieter Chhota Shimla, is easier. Shoghi on the highway suits a late arrival from Chandigarh, Kufri puts you close to the snow, and Mashobra or Naldehra are for forest resorts. Typical off-season rates are about ₹800–1,800 for a budget room and ₹2,000–4,500 for a 3-star room a night; 25 December–1 January and May–June cost far more.

Winter

Choosing a Shimla hotel in winter

Snow access: after snowfall, roads above the Mall and towards Kufri can stay icy for a day or two; a Cart Road hotel is easiest to leave. Heating and water: confirm room heaters and hot-water hours — nights in January are close to freezing. Carnival weeks: rooms near the Ridge sell out for the Winter Carnival (24 December–1 January); book early or stay at Chhota Shimla or Shoghi. Snow day: plan it from the forecast — see Kufri, Narkanda and Shimla snowfall.

FAQ

Where to stay in Shimla: common questions

Which is the best area to stay in Shimla?

For a first visit without a car, near Mall Road and the Ridge; with a car or elders, Cart Road, Circular Road or Chhota Shimla.

Can cars reach hotels on Mall Road Shimla?

Generally no — the Mall is car-free; luggage goes up by the public lift, a porter or the hotel's own vehicle permit.

Is it better to stay in Shimla or Kufri for snow?

Kufri gets snow more often and is closer to the slopes, but the road beyond Dhalli can close after heavy snow; many people stay in Shimla and visit Kufri for a day.

How much does a hotel in Shimla cost?

Off-season, about ₹800–1,800 for a budget room and ₹2,000–4,500 for a 3-star room a night; peak dates cost far more.

Where should I stay in Shimla with a car?

Cart Road, Circular Road, Chhota Shimla, or Shoghi on the highway — check that the hotel has parking.

Is Mashobra a good place to stay?

Yes for a quiet forest stay or honeymoon; it is a drive from the Mall, so you need a car.
