Pithoragarh is the largest town of eastern Kumaon, spread across the Soar valley at about 1,600 m near the Nepal border — the gateway to Munsiyari, Dharchula and the high valleys. Its weather is mild for a hill town: winter days are around 15–17 °C with cold, sometimes frosty nights, and snow in town is rare, though the ridges and Munsiyari higher up get it regularly. Summer days reach the high 20s. July and August bring heavy rain and frequent landslides on the hill roads. October to February has the clearest skies; March–June and September–November are the most comfortable months to travel.

  • ColdestJanuary — days about 16 °C, nights about 5 °C (estimate).

Gateway to the high valleys

Pithoragarh as a base: Munsiyari, Chaukori and the border valleys

From Pithoragarh, roads run north to Munsiyari, facing the Panchachuli peaks, and north-east along the Kali river to Dharchula and the high valleys towards Adi Kailash and Om Parvat, which need permits. Chaukori and Patal Bhuvaneshwar lie to the west. All of these are long mountain drives, and in the monsoon landslides often close them for hours or days.

The best time for clear views of the snow peaks is October to February; spring (March–May) is pleasant with rhododendrons in bloom. In winter, snow regularly closes the higher roads near Munsiyari for a day or two after heavy falls.

Before you go

Pithoragarh trip: what the weather means for you

Clothing: warm layers from November to February; a light jacket in summer; rain gear and good shoes in the monsoon. Pack much warmer for Munsiyari.

Getting there: Pithoragarh is a long day's drive from Kathgodam via Almora, or from Tanakpur via Champawat. Keep spare days in the monsoon, when landslides are common. Cab: Nainital taxi service.

Pithoragarh weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather in Pithoragarh today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Pithoragarh, Munsiyari and Almora (MET Norway data).

Does it snow in Pithoragarh?

Rarely in town. Munsiyari and the higher ridges of the district get regular winter snow.

How cold is Pithoragarh in winter?

January days are about 16 °C and nights about 5 °C (an estimate), with frosty mornings.

Which are the wettest months in Pithoragarh?

July and August, when landslides often close the hill roads.

When is the best time to visit Pithoragarh?

March–June and September–November; October–February for the clearest skies.

How far is Munsiyari from Pithoragarh?

Several hours' drive to the north; check the road before you set out in the monsoon or after snow.
