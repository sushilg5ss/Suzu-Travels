Katra, the base town for Vaishno Devi, sits at about 875 m and the Bhawan (the shrine) about 700 m higher, at roughly 1,585 m — so the Bhawan is usually 5–7 °C colder than Katra. India Meteorological Department averages for Katra (1991–2020) put January at 16.2 °C by day and 6.8 °C at night and June at 35.1 °C by day. The monsoon is heavy: July and August bring 500–670 mm of rain each. In cold spells of January–February, snow can fall around Bhairon Ghati above the Bhawan. The most comfortable months for the climb are October–November and February–April.

  • ColdestDecember–January — Katra nights ~7–8 °C, the Bhawan near freezing.
  • HottestMay–June — Katra 34–35 °C by day; climb early or at night.
  • WettestJuly–August, ~514 and ~666 mm at Katra.
  • DriestOctober–November, ~35 mm a month.
  • SnowSome winters around Bhairon Ghati; rarely at the Bhawan itself.

Snow and cold

Does it snow at Vaishno Devi?

Katra itself does not get snow. Higher up, light snow falls in some winters around Bhairon Ghati (roughly 2,000 m) and on the Trikuta peaks, usually during cold spells in January and February. The Bhawan gets snow only occasionally. When it does, the stretch to Bhairon becomes slippery and the ropeway or the climb may be paused for a while.

In December–January, temperatures at the Bhawan can drop close to freezing at night. Carry a warm jacket, cap and gloves even if Katra feels mild in the afternoon. More on winter darshan: Vaishno Devi in December.

Monsoon

Monsoon at Vaishno Devi: what changes

July and August are the wettest months, with over 500 mm of rain each at Katra. The track stays open on most days, but heavy rain can slow it down. Shelters along the route help, and the helicopter service between Katra and Sanjichhat is often suspended in low cloud.

If you travel in the monsoon, keep a spare day, carry a light raincoat (umbrellas are awkward on the steps) and shoes with good grip.

Vaishno Devi weather tips and getting there

What to wear: layers. From November to February, thermals, a warm jacket, cap and gloves for the Bhawan; from April to September, light cotton clothes and a rain jacket. Good walking shoes in every season.

Getting to Katra: Katra is about 48 km from Jammu (1–1.5 hours by road). See Jammu to Katra taxi, and our full Vaishno Devi yatra guide for the route, RFID cards and the ropeway.

FAQ

Vaishno Devi weather: questions

What is the weather at Vaishno Devi today?

The live forecast at the top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days for Katra, the Bhawan and Jammu, using MET Norway data.

How cold is Vaishno Devi in December and January?

IMD averages for Katra are about 16–18 °C by day and 7–8 °C at night. The Bhawan, about 700 m higher, is usually 5–7 °C colder and can drop close to freezing at night.

Does it snow at Vaishno Devi?

Katra does not get snow. Light snow falls in some winters around Bhairon Ghati and on the Trikuta peaks, mostly in January–February; the Bhawan itself sees snow only occasionally.

Which is the best month for the Vaishno Devi yatra?

October and November are the most comfortable — dry, clear and cool. February to April is also good. Avoid July–August if you can, when Katra gets over 500 mm of rain a month.

Is it safe to go to Vaishno Devi in the monsoon?

The yatra continues in the monsoon, but the track is slippery, the helicopter is often grounded and heavy rain can cause delays. Keep a spare day and follow the shrine board's advisories.

How hot is Katra in summer?

IMD averages for June are about 35 °C by day and 23.5 °C at night. Start the climb early in the morning or in the evening.
