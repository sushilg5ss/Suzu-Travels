Sissu (about 3,120 m) is the first village in Lahaul after the Atal Tunnel, about 40 km from Manali; Keylong (about 3,080 m), the district town, is 71 km from Manali through the tunnel. Both sit north of the Pir Panjal, so they are far colder than Manali and get heavy snow — the fields at Sissu are often white from October to March, even when Solang has none. Keylong's IMD records run from about −30 °C (January) to 31.5 °C (August). May to September is the comfortable season; December to February brings the deepest snow, and after fresh snow police allow only 4×4s with chains beyond Solang Nala, or close the road until it is cleared.

Getting there

The Atal Tunnel road in winter: what to expect

The 9.02 km Atal Tunnel (opened 3 October 2020) runs under the Rohtang Pass from Dhundi, beyond Solang, to the Lahaul side near Sissu. On a clear day Manali to Sissu is about 40 km, roughly 1.5 hours; Manali to Keylong is 71 km (it was 116 km over Rohtang). Stopping or taking photos inside the tunnel is not allowed.

In winter the road has three states: open to all, 4×4 with chains only beyond Solang Nala after fresh snow, or closed until cleared after heavy snow; on snow days police may also fix timings. Start early — water on the road freezes again in the afternoon and the return queue builds up.

No permit is needed for the tunnel or Sissu; the online permit is only for the Rohtang Pass road. More on the trip: Atal Tunnel and Sissu snow trip and Rohtang permit and road status.

FAQ

Sissu and Keylong weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather in Sissu today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Sissu, Keylong and Solang (MET Norway data).

When does it snow in Sissu?

From October to March; December to February bring the deepest snow. Sissu often has snow when Solang has none.

Is the Atal Tunnel open in winter?

Most days, yes. After fresh snow police allow only 4×4s with chains beyond Solang Nala, or close the road until it is cleared.

How cold does Keylong get?

Keylong's recorded lows reach about −30 °C in January and −28 °C in February; typical winter nights are well below freezing.

How far is Sissu from Manali?

About 40 km through the Atal Tunnel — roughly 1.5 hours on a clear day. Keylong is 71 km from Manali.

Do I need a permit for Sissu?

No. The online permit is only for the Rohtang Pass road, not for the tunnel or Sissu.
