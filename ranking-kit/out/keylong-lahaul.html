Keylong: Lahaul's mountain town, between Manali and Ladakh

Keylong is the main town of Lahaul, at about 3,080 m on the Bhaga river, roughly 70 km (2.5–3 hours) from Manali through the Atal Tunnel. Around it are some of Lahaul's oldest monasteries: Kardang, across the river, and Shashur, on the slope above the town. Keylong is the usual overnight stop on the Manali–Leh road, with Jispa (about 22 km on) a quieter riverside alternative. In winter Keylong is snowbound and very cold, but since the Atal Tunnel opened it can be reached on most days; the road onward to Leh and over Kunzum to Spiti is open only from about June to October.

What to see

Things to do in and around Keylong

Kardang monastery, on the far side of the Bhaga river, is the largest in Lahaul and belongs to the Drukpa Kagyu school; it has a large library and old murals. Shashur monastery, a short drive or steep walk above Keylong, is known for its tall thangka and its view down the valley. Both are reached from town in under an hour.

Beyond Keylong, Jispa sits on a broad, flat bend of the Bhaga with camps and guesthouses — a calm place to sleep on the way to Leh. In the other direction, the Chandra–Bhaga confluence at Tandi and the old temples of Trilokinath and Udaipur in the Pattan valley make a good day out.

Getting there

How to reach Keylong

From Manali, drive through the Atal Tunnel and along the Chandra valley via Sissu and Tandi — about 70 km, 2.5–3 hours. The old route over Rohtang (about 115 km) is open only in summer. Government buses also run from Manali when the road is open. From Delhi, add the 540 km to Manali — see our Himachal distance chart.

Keylong is at about 3,080 m: if you plan to continue to Leh, an extra night here or at Jispa helps you acclimatise.

FAQ

Keylong: frequently asked questions

How far is Keylong from Manali?

About 70 km through the Atal Tunnel, 2.5–3 hours; about 115 km over Rohtang in summer.

Is Keylong open in winter?

Yes, it can usually be reached through the Atal Tunnel, but heavy snow can close the road for a day or more.

How high is Keylong?

About 3,080 m above sea level.

Keylong or Jispa — where should I stay on the way to Leh?

Keylong has more facilities; Jispa is quieter, by the river and about 22 km closer to Leh.

Which monasteries can I visit from Keylong?

Kardang across the river, Shashur above the town, and Tayul nearby.

When does the road from Keylong to Leh open?

Usually in June, closing again in October or November with the first heavy snow.
