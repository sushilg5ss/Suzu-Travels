January is Dharamshala's coldest month. Lower Dharamshala averages about 16 °C by day and 6 °C at night (IMD 1991–2020), and McLeod Ganj, about 600 m higher, roughly 12 °C and 2 °C. The Dhauladhar peaks behind the town are white all winter. McLeod Ganj, Naddi and Dharamkot can get snow in January and February, though not every year, while Triund (about 2,850 m) is often under snow — last winter it got fresh snow on 23 January 2026. Expect sunny, crisp days, a few wet or snowy spells, and quiet streets outside the New Year week.

January weather

What is the weather like in Dharamshala in January?

Dharamshala spreads over a steep slope, from about 1,450 m in the lower town to about 2,080 m in McLeod Ganj, so temperatures change a lot within a few kilometres. In January lower Dharamshala averages about 16 °C by day and 5.9 °C at night; McLeod Ganj is roughly 4 °C colder. Most days are sunny, with a few wet or snowy spells brought by western disturbances.

Snow is rare in the lower town. McLeod Ganj, Naddi and Dharamkot can whiten in a cold spell, mostly in January–February, and the Triund ridge is often snowy through mid-winter. The Dhauladhar peaks behind the town are white all season, so you see snow from almost everywhere.

Dharamshala in January: getting there and packing

Getting there: Kangra (Gaggal) airport is about 15 km from lower Dharamshala, and Pathankot, the nearest main railway station, about 90 km. Winter fog and weather can delay flights; from Delhi it is an overnight drive or bus.

Roads: the roads to McLeod Ganj, Naddi and Dharamkot are steep and can be icy after snow — travel in daylight with a driver used to hill roads.

Packing: warm layers, a padded jacket, gloves, a woollen cap and shoes with grip; full winter gear if you go up to Triund.

FAQ

Dharamshala in January: frequently asked questions

Does it snow in Dharamshala in January?

Rarely in the lower town. McLeod Ganj, Naddi and Dharamkot get snow in some Januaries, and the Triund ridge is often snowy; the Dhauladhar peaks are white all winter.

How cold is Dharamshala in January?

Lower Dharamshala averages about 16 °C by day and 5.9 °C at night (IMD 1991–2020); McLeod Ganj is about 4 °C colder.

Can I trek to Triund in January?

Only with a local guide and in settled weather — the trail is often snowy and icy, and conditions change quickly.

Is January a good time to visit Dharamshala?

Yes, for sunny, crisp days, snowy peaks and quiet streets outside the New Year week.

When did it last snow at Triund?

Last winter Triund got fresh snow on 23 January 2026.

How far is Dharamshala from Pathankot?

About 85 km by road.
