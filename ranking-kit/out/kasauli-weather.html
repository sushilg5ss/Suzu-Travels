Kasauli is a small cantonment town at about 1,800 m, 58 km from Chandigarh and 25 km from Solan. It is cooler than the plains all year: summers (April–June) are mild and pleasant, the monsoon (July–August) brings heavy rain and thick fog, and winters are cold with frosty nights. Snow falls only in some winters, most often in January, and rarely stays long; for sure snow, Shimla–Kufri and Narkanda are 2–3 hours further. October and November have the clearest skies and the longest views.

  • Mild summerApril – June: the nearest cool escape from Chandigarh and Delhi.
  • MonsoonJuly – August: heavy rain and fog; slippery hill roads.
  • ClearestOctober – November: dry, crisp, long views.
  • ColdestDecember – January: frosty nights; sunny days.
  • SnowOnly some winters, mostly January — see Kasauli snowfall.

Snow

Does it snow in Kasauli?

Sometimes. Snow falls in Kasauli only in some winters — most often in early January — and usually melts within a day or two. It is never something to plan a trip on.

For snow you can count on, go higher: Chail (~2,250 m) in Solan district gets it more often, and Shimla, Kufri and Narkanda are the surest bets within a few hours. Full history and odds: Kasauli snowfall, Shimla snowfall and our live snow tracker.

Monsoon

Kasauli in the monsoon: rain and fog

July and August are the wettest months, and cloud often sits on the ridge, cutting visibility to a few metres. The Parwanoo–Dharampur–Kasauli road is narrow and slippery in the rain; drive slowly and in daylight.

Landslides on the Chandigarh–Shimla highway near Parwanoo can slow traffic in heavy rain, so keep some slack in weekend plans.

FAQ

Kasauli weather: questions

What is the weather in Kasauli today?

The live forecast at the top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days for Kasauli, Solan and Chail, using MET Norway data.

Does it snow in Kasauli?

Only in some winters, most often in early January, and it rarely lasts. For reliable snow go to Chail, Shimla–Kufri or Narkanda.

How cold is Kasauli in December and January?

Cold, with frosty nights. Solan's IMD averages (25 km away and lower) are about 18–21 °C by day and 2–3 °C at night; Kasauli is a little colder.

Is Kasauli good in summer?

Yes — April to June is mild and pleasant, and Kasauli is one of the closest hill escapes from Chandigarh and Delhi. Weekends are busy.

Is it safe to visit Kasauli in the monsoon?

You can go, but expect heavy rain, thick fog and slippery roads in July–August. Drive in daylight and keep plans flexible.

What is the best time to visit Kasauli?

October–November for clear views, March–June for pleasant weather, and December–January if you want cold and a small chance of snow.
