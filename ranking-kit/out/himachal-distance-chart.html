Delhi to Shimla is about 345 km (7–8 hours), Delhi to Manali about 540 km (11–13 hours), Delhi to Dharamshala about 475 km (9–10 hours) and Shimla to Manali about 250 km (7–8 hours). From Chandigarh, Shimla is about 115 km (3–3.5 hours) and Manali about 290 km (7–8 hours). On Himachal's hill roads, plan on an average of 25–35 km an hour once you leave the plains, and add time for snow, landslides and weekend traffic. In winter, Rohtang Pass, Kunzum Pass and Jalori Pass close, so Kaza and Spiti are reached only via Shimla and Kinnaur, while the Atal Tunnel keeps Lahaul (Sissu, Keylong) open on most days.

Winter roads

Passes and roads that close in winter

Opening and closing dates change every year with the snow; check before you travel.

Rohtang Pass

~3,980 m · Manali

Usually closed from about November to May/June; Solang and the Atal Tunnel stay open on most days.

Kunzum Pass

~4,590 m · Spiti

Usually closed from about November to late May or June; in winter reach Kaza via Shimla and Kinnaur.

Jalori Pass

~3,120 m · Banjar

Often closed after snowfall from about late December to March. Jalori Pass →

Atal Tunnel

Manali – Lahaul

Open most days of the year; closed for a few hours or days after heavy snow.

Narkanda / Kufri

Shimla – Rampur road

Can close for a day or two after heavy snowfall; usually cleared quickly.

Sach Pass

~4,420 m · Chamba – Pangi

Open only from about June to October.

Planning

How many days do you need?

Do not try to see Shimla and Manali in a weekend from Delhi — the driving alone is about 20 hours. As a guide: Shimla–Kufri 3–4 days, Manali–Solang 4–5 days, Shimla–Manali 6–7 days, Dharamshala–Dalhousie 5–6 days, and Spiti 8–10 days by the Kinnaur route. Night travel on hill roads is best avoided.

FAQ

Himachal distances: frequently asked questions

What is the distance from Delhi to Manali by road?

About 540 km, 11–13 hours by car via Chandigarh, the Kiratpur–Mandi four-lane and Kullu.

What is the distance from Shimla to Manali?

About 250 km, 7–8 hours by road via Bilaspur, Mandi and Kullu.

How far is Shimla from Delhi?

About 345 km, 7–8 hours by car via Chandigarh and Solan.

How far is Dharamshala from Delhi?

About 475 km, 9–10 hours via Una and Kangra.

Can I reach Spiti in winter?

Yes, only via Shimla and Kinnaur (about 420 km from Shimla to Kaza, two days); the Manali–Kunzum road is closed in winter.

Is Rohtang Pass open in winter?

No. Rohtang is usually closed from about November to May/June; the Atal Tunnel and Solang usually stay open.
