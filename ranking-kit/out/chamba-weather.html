Chamba (about 1,000 m) is an old town on the Ravi, well below Dalhousie and Khajjiar and much warmer than both. India Meteorological Department averages make January the coldest month (about 17.9 °C by day, 4.2 °C at night) and May the warmest (about 33.5 °C by day). Rain peaks twice — March (~160 mm) and July–August (~170 mm). Snow rarely settles in Chamba town; for snow go to Khajjiar, Dalhousie or Bharmour.

  • ColdestDecember–January — nights 4–6 °C; snow on the hills above.
  • WarmestMay–July — 32–33 °C by day.

Snow

Where to find snow near Chamba

Chamba sits low in the Ravi valley, so snow rarely settles in town. Higher up:

Khajjiar (~1,920 m) and Dalhousie (~1,970 m): late December to February, mostly January; the Khajjiar road is the first to close after snow — Dalhousie weather by month.

Bharmour (~2,195 m): about 65 km from Chamba; heavy winter snow and frequent road closures. In summer, the base for the Manimahesh yatra.

Sach Pass: the high pass to Pangi is snowbound in winter and usually opens only in summer.

Fairs and seasons

The Minjar fair and Chamba's monsoon

Chamba's famous Minjar fair is held on the Chaugan in Shravan (July–August) — in the middle of the monsoon. Carry an umbrella and shoes with grip, and watch for landslides on the Pathankot–Chamba road.

October–November has the clearest weather — the best time for the Lakshmi Narayan temples, the Chaugan and the Bhuri Singh Museum.

FAQ

Chamba weather: questions

What is the weather in Chamba today?

The live forecast at the top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days for Chamba, Khajjiar and Dalhousie, using MET Norway data.

Does it snow in Chamba?

Snow rarely settles in Chamba town. For snow go to Khajjiar or Dalhousie (late December–February) or Bharmour.

Which is the hottest month in Chamba?

May — IMD averages are about 33.5 °C by day; June and July are also warm.

When does it rain in Chamba?

Most in July–August (~170 mm a month) and March (~160 mm). October and November are the driest.

What is the weather like during the Minjar fair?

Minjar is in Shravan (July–August) — warm, humid and rainy. Bring an umbrella and good shoes.

How different are Chamba and Dalhousie?

Dalhousie is about 1,000 m higher and usually 5–7 °C colder; it gets snow in winter while Chamba mostly does not.
