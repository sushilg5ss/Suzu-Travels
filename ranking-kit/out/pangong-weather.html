Pangong Tso sits at about 4,250 m, roughly 160 km from Leh over the Chang La; Nubra Valley (Diskit, about 3,150 m) is about 120 km from Leh over the Khardung La. Pangong is far colder than Leh — summer nights are near freezing and winter nights fall well below −20 °C — and the lake usually freezes from about January to March. Nubra, lower and drier, is milder than Leh in summer. June to September is the comfortable season for both; the high passes can close for a while after any snowfall, even in early summer and autumn.

Roads and planning

Chang La, Khardung La and planning your days

Both trips cross passes well above 5,000 m — Chang La to Pangong and Khardung La to Nubra — so spend at least two nights in Leh to acclimatise first, start early, and carry warm layers even in July.

Snow can close a pass for hours or days, even in May–June and September–October. In winter, Pangong is visited mainly for the frozen lake by well-prepared travellers with a local winter driver.

Check the current permit and environment-fee rules on the Ladakh administration's portal before you go. More: Ladakh weather (Hindi) and Ladakh in winter.

Before you go

Pangong and Nubra trip: what the weather means for you

Clothing: a warm jacket, fleece, cap and gloves for every trip; a down jacket and thermals for Pangong nights even in summer; sunglasses and high-SPF sunscreen always.

Altitude: acclimatise in Leh for two nights, drink water, and avoid heavy exertion on the first day at the lake.

FAQ

Pangong and Nubra weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather at Pangong Lake today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Pangong, Nubra and Leh (MET Norway data).

When does Pangong Lake freeze?

Usually from about January to March; the edges start freezing in November–December.

How cold is Pangong at night?

Near freezing even in summer, and well below −20 °C in winter.

What is the best time to visit Pangong and Nubra?

June to September, when the passes are open and the days are mild.

Is Nubra warmer than Leh?

A little — Nubra (Diskit) is about 350 m lower, and summer days in the dunes can be hot.

How far are Pangong and Nubra from Leh?

Pangong is about 160 km over the Chang La; Nubra (Diskit) is about 120 km over the Khardung La.
