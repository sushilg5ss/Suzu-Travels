Mandi is an old temple town at about 760 m on the Beas, on the Kiratpur–Manali highway about 110 km before Manali. In town, see the Bhootnath temple and the stone Panchvaktra temple at the meeting of the Beas and Suketi — so many old temples that Mandi is called Chhoti Kashi. Nearby are Rewalsar lake (about 22–25 km), Prashar lake (about 49 km, 2,730 m), the Kamrunag lake and temple (a trek from Rohanda) and Janjehli and Shikari Devi (about 80 km). The International Shivratri fair is held in February–March. Mandi town does not get snow; Prashar and Kamrunag do in December–February.

Getting there

How to reach Mandi

Mandi is on the Delhi–Manali highway: about 430 km from Delhi, about 200 km from Chandigarh and about 145 km from Shimla. The nearest airport is Bhuntar (Kullu), about 60 km away. Every bus and taxi to Manali passes through Mandi — Delhi to Manali taxi (sedan from ₹6,899) and Chandigarh to Manali taxi (sedan from ₹4,199). Route details: Chandigarh to Manali and Manali to Dharamshala, which also runs through Mandi.

Plan

A 2-day Mandi plan

Day 1: the Bhootnath and Panchvaktra temples and the old market in the morning, then Rewalsar lake for the afternoon and sunset. Day 2: an early drive to Prashar lake, back in Mandi by evening — or continue up the Beas to Kullu and Manali. With two more days, go south to Janjehli for Shikari Devi.

FAQ

Mandi tourist places: common questions

What are the best places to visit in Mandi?

The Bhootnath and Panchvaktra temples in town, Rewalsar lake, Prashar lake, Kamrunag and Janjehli–Shikari Devi.

How far is Prashar lake from Mandi?

About 49 km by road.

How far is Rewalsar from Mandi?

About 22–25 km.

Why is Mandi called Chhoti Kashi?

Because of its many old temples along the Beas, much like Varanasi (Kashi).

Does it snow in Mandi?

Not in the town; Prashar lake and Kamrunag get snow from December to February.

When is the Mandi Shivratri fair?

It begins on Mahashivratri and runs for about a week, in February–March.

How far is Mandi from Manali?

About 110 km up the Beas via Kullu.
