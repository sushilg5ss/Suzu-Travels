January is Gulmarg's coldest month and one of its snowiest. Days average about 0.7 °C and nights about −7.7 °C, with about 161 mm of precipitation, almost all of it as snow (IMD 1991–2020 normals). The meadow is deep in snow most years, the ski season is in full swing at Kongdoori and Apharwat, and the first part of the month falls in Chillai Kalan, Kashmir's 40 coldest days, which run to about 30 January. The road from Tangmarg needs snow chains after heavy falls, and Gondola Phase 2 often stops in wind or heavy snow — book the Gondola early and keep a spare day.

January weather

What is the weather like in Gulmarg in January?

January brings Gulmarg's lowest temperatures: about 0.7 °C by day and −7.7 °C at night on average, colder at Kongdoori and far colder at Apharwat, and colder still on clear nights after fresh snow. Western disturbances bring heavy snow every few days in a normal winter, followed by bright, freezing days.

The snow is usually dry and powdery in January, which is why it is a favourite month for skiers. For non-skiers it means snow play on the meadow, sledging, and the Gondola to Kongdoori for views over the Pir Panjal.

Gulmarg in January: road, Gondola and packing

Road: Gulmarg is about 50 km from Srinagar. After heavy snow, chains are fitted at Tangmarg and the last stretch is sometimes open only to 4x4s for a few hours until it is cleared — travel up in daylight and keep a spare day.

Gondola: tickets sell out quickly in January, so book on the official J&K Cable Car Corporation portal as soon as your dates are fixed. Plan Phase 2 for the morning; it often stops in wind or heavy snow.

Flights: winter fog and snow can delay flights into Srinagar — avoid tight connections on the way out.

Packing: thermals, a down jacket, waterproof gloves, a woollen cap, sunglasses and snow boots with grip. Nights fall well below freezing.

FAQ

Gulmarg in January: frequently asked questions

Is there snow in Gulmarg in January?

Yes, in a normal winter the meadow is deep in snow in January, and the ski slopes at Kongdoori and Apharwat are in season.

How cold is Gulmarg in January?

About 0.7 °C by day and −7.7 °C at night on average (IMD 1991–2020 normals), colder higher up and after fresh snow.

Is the Gulmarg Gondola open in January?

Yes, in season; Phase 2 to Apharwat often stops in wind or heavy snow, so plan it for the morning and keep a spare day.

What is Chillai Kalan?

The 40 coldest days of the Kashmir winter, from 21 December to about 30 January.

Can the road to Gulmarg close in January?

After heavy snow, chains are needed from Tangmarg and the road may be open only to 4x4s for a few hours until cleared.

How far is Gulmarg from Srinagar?

About 50 km by road.
