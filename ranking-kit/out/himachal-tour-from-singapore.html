From Singapore, fly to Delhi (about 5½–6 hours) and drive or take a short flight into Himachal. Singapore's two long school breaks match Himachal's two best seasons: in the year-end holidays (late November–December), Shimla, Kufri, Narkanda and Manali–Solang turn cold and, usually from late December, snowy; in the June holidays, the hills are a cool 15–25 °C and the high valleys of Lahaul and Spiti open up. A first trip of 7 days covers Shimla and Manali; allow 10 days to add Spiti or Dharamshala. Singapore citizens can usually apply for an Indian e-Visa online; check the official site before booking.

When to go

December or June? Choosing your Himachal season

December: the first half is cold and clear; snow usually arrives in the hills from late December, and Christmas–New Year is busy. Plan a buffer day for weather and book hotels early. June: the plains are hot, so Shimla and Manali are busy with Indian families — but it is the best month to go higher, to Sissu, Keylong and Spiti, where the roads are open and the skies clear.

Getting there

Flights and transfers

There are daily direct flights from Singapore to Delhi. From Delhi it is about 345 km to Shimla and 540 km to Manali by road — we meet you at the airport with a private car and a hill driver. You can also connect to Shimla, Kullu (Bhuntar) or Dharamshala (Gaggal) by short domestic flights, which are weather-dependent. See Delhi to Shimla, Delhi to Manali and the Himachal distance chart.

Singapore passport holders can usually apply for an Indian e-Visa online before travel. Foreign nationals also need an Inner Line Permit for the stretch of the Kinnaur–Spiti road near the border; we arrange it as part of a Spiti trip. Read Spiti for international travellers.

Sample plans

Two sample itineraries

7 days, December snow: Delhi → Shimla (2 nights; Kufri, Mashobra) → Narkanda (1 night; Hatu Peak) → Manali (3 nights; Solang, Sissu, Old Manali) → Delhi. 10 days, June mountains: Delhi → Manali (3 nights) → Keylong or Jispa (1 night) → Kaza via Kunzum (3 nights; Key, Kibber, Langza, Dhankar) → back via Kinnaur and Shimla (3 nights).

Packing for December: a padded jacket, thermals, gloves, cap and waterproof boots. For June: layers — warm days, cold nights in Spiti — and strong sun protection.

FAQ

Himachal from Singapore: frequently asked questions

Will there be snow in Himachal in the December school holidays?

Usually from late December at Kufri, Narkanda, Solang and Sissu, though it varies year to year; early December is cold but often dry.

How long is the flight from Singapore to Delhi?

About 5½–6 hours direct.

Do Singapore citizens need a visa for India?

Yes; most can apply for an Indian e-Visa online. Check the official Indian visa website before booking.

Is June a good time for Himachal?

Yes, the hills are cool and Spiti and Lahaul are open, but Shimla and Manali are busy with Indian holidaymakers.

How many days do we need?

7 days for Shimla and Manali; 10 days or more to add Spiti or Dharamshala.

Do foreigners need a permit for Spiti?

Foreign nationals need an Inner Line Permit for the Kinnaur–Spiti border stretch; we arrange it with your trip.
