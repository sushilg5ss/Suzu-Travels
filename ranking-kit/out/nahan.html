Nahan: the quiet old capital of Sirmaur

A small hill town on a Shivalik ridge, founded in 1621 as the seat of the Sirmaur rajas — tanks, temples and old palace gates in town, and Renuka Lake, Jaitak Fort and a fossil park within an hour's drive. Mild in winter, about 80 km from Chandigarh.

Nahan is the headquarters of Sirmaur district in south-eastern Himachal, a small town at about 930 m on a ridge of the Shivalik hills, founded in 1621 by Raja Karam Prakash as the capital of the Sirmaur state. In town, walk around Rani Tal and its temple, the open Chaugan ground, the old palace gates and the temple lanes. Within an hour's drive are Renuka Lake (about 38 km), Jaitak Fort (about 18 km), the Trilokpur temple (about 16 km) and the Suketi Fossil Park. Nahan is about 80 km from Chandigarh, rarely gets snow, and is pleasant from October to March.

Getting there

How to reach Nahan

By road: Nahan is on the highway from Ambala and Kala Amb to Paonta Sahib and Dehradun, about 80 km from Chandigarh (2–2.5 hours). Buses run from Chandigarh, Ambala, Delhi and Shimla. By rail: the nearest large stations are Ambala (about 70 km) and Chandigarh (about 80 km). By air: Chandigarh airport is about 82 km away. From Shimla, the road via Solan and Sarahan (Sirmaur) is slower — plan 4–5 hours.

2-day plan

A 2-day Nahan and Renuka plan

Day 1: drive from Chandigarh in the morning, visit Trilokpur on the way, check in, then walk Rani Tal, the Chaugan, the palace gates and the old town lanes, ending on Villa Round at sunset. Day 2: drive to Renuka Lake early for the quiet morning, walk around the lake, stop at Jaitak Fort or the Suketi Fossil Park on the way back, and return to Chandigarh by evening.

FAQ

Nahan: common questions

Where is Nahan?

In Sirmaur district in south-eastern Himachal Pradesh, on a Shivalik ridge about 80 km from Chandigarh.

What is Nahan famous for?

It was the capital of the Sirmaur state from 1621, and is known for Rani Tal, its old palace and temples, and as the base for Renuka Lake.

How far is Renuka Lake from Nahan?

About 38 km by road, roughly 1–1.5 hours.

Does it snow in Nahan?

Rarely; at about 930 m Nahan has mild winters. For snow, go to Shimla, Kufri or Narkanda.

What is the best time to visit Nahan?

October to March for clear, mild weather; summers are hot.

How do I reach Nahan from Delhi?

By road via Ambala and Kala Amb (about 5–6 hours), or by train to Ambala and then about 70 km by road.
