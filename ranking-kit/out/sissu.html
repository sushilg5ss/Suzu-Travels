Sissu: the snow on the other side of the mountain

Since the Atal Tunnel opened, Sissu has become Manali's winter snow trip — a wide white valley, a frozen lake and a frozen waterfall about an hour from the hotel, even when Rohtang is closed.

Sissu is a village at about 3,100 m in the Chandra valley of Lahaul, about 13 km beyond the north portal of the Atal Tunnel and roughly 40 km (1.5 hours) from Manali. It usually has snow from December to March, when the Sissu lake and the tall Palden Lhamo waterfall freeze and the valley turns white — which makes it the easiest place near Manali to see deep snow in winter while Rohtang Pass is closed. No Rohtang permit is needed to drive through the Atal Tunnel, but after heavy snowfall the authorities may close the tunnel or limit tourist vehicles for a day or more. Plan Sissu as a day trip from Manali, keep a spare day, and go early to avoid traffic at the tunnel.

What to see

Things to do at Sissu

Sissu lake lies in the flat valley floor near the helipad; in summer it reflects the peaks, and in winter it freezes and disappears under snow. Across the Chandra river, the Palden Lhamo waterfall drops down a cliff and freezes into an ice column in the coldest weeks.

Above the village rises Ghepan Goh, the sacred peak of Lahaul, and the small Ghepan monastery with carved prayer stones. In winter the slopes near the road are used for snow play, with snow scooters and sledges available locally in season — see Atal Tunnel and Sissu snow trip.

Winter

Sissu in snow: what to expect

Lahaul is in the rain shadow but gets heavy winter snow from western disturbances. After a big spell, the Atal Tunnel approaches and the road to Sissu are cleared first; until then, the police may stop tourist vehicles at the tunnel or at Solang. Check the forecast before you go and leave Manali early.

Getting there

How to reach Sissu from Manali

From Manali, drive up to Solang and the Atal Tunnel south portal (about 25 km), through the 9 km tunnel, and down to the Chandra valley; Sissu is about 13 km beyond the north portal. In winter, take a car with a driver used to snow and ice, and carry chains. From Delhi, Manali is about 540 km — see our Delhi to Manali taxi and Atal Tunnel guide (Hindi).

FAQ

Sissu: frequently asked questions

How far is Sissu from Manali?

About 40 km, roughly 1.5 hours through the Atal Tunnel, longer in snow or traffic.

Does Sissu have snow in December?

Usually from mid- or late December; January and February have the most snow.

Do I need a permit to go to Sissu?

No Rohtang permit is needed for the Atal Tunnel route, but local restrictions can apply after heavy snowfall.

Is the Atal Tunnel open in winter?

On most days, yes; it can close for hours or days after heavy snow while roads are cleared.

Can I stay at Sissu?

Yes, there are a few homestays and hotels; most visitors come on a day trip from Manali.

Is Sissu better than Solang for snow?

Sissu is higher and usually has deeper, longer-lasting snow; Solang is closer and has more activities.
