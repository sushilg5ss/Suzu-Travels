January is the most reliable month for snow in Auli, and the ski season usually starts once the base is deep enough. The slopes, at about 2,500–3,050 m above Joshimath, are normally white from late December; nights fall well below freezing, and days hover around freezing to a few degrees above. Last winter Auli had heavy snowfall on 23 January 2026, which closed the Joshimath–Auli road for a time. The Joshimath–Auli cable car has been closed since January 2023, so you reach Auli by the 16 km road from Joshimath. Book early for weekends and keep a spare day for the road.

January weather

What is the weather like in Auli in January?

There are no published IMD averages for Auli. Joshimath, 16 km below at about 1,875 m, averages 11.0 °C by day and 2.0 °C at night in January (IMD 1971–2000); Auli, roughly 1,000 m higher, is usually about 6 °C colder — so expect days around 3–6 °C and nights of −4 °C or lower, colder in wind and after fresh snow. These are estimates; use the live forecast above for your dates.

Western disturbances bring the heavy falls: a single spell can add a good depth of snow in a day or two, followed by clear, very cold days with sharp views of Nanda Devi, Dronagiri and the Garhwal peaks.

Auli in January: getting there, roads and packing

Getting there: most travellers drive from Delhi, Dehradun, Haridwar or Rishikesh to Joshimath along the Alaknanda valley — a long mountain day of roughly 250 km from Rishikesh — then up the 16 km road to Auli. Break the journey in Rudraprayag or Joshimath.

Roads: after heavy snow the Joshimath–Auli road can close until it is cleared, and the highway below can be slow; travel in daylight with a driver used to winter roads, and keep a spare day.

Cable car: the Joshimath–Auli cable car has been closed since January 2023 and remained closed in 2026, so plan on the road.

Packing: thermals, a down jacket, waterproof gloves, a cap, sunglasses and snow boots. Nights at Auli fall well below freezing in January.

FAQ

Auli in January: frequently asked questions

Is there snow in Auli in January?

Yes, in a normal winter — January is the most reliable month for snow on the Auli slopes.

Can I ski in Auli in January?

Usually yes, once the snow base is deep enough; lessons and equipment hire are available in season.

How cold is Auli in January?

Nights fall well below freezing and days hover around 3–6 °C (an estimate from Joshimath's IMD normals); colder after fresh snow.

Is the Auli cable car open?

No — the Joshimath–Auli cable car has been closed since January 2023 and remained closed in 2026. The 16 km road from Joshimath is the way up.

Can the road to Auli close in January?

Yes, after heavy snow, until it is cleared — keep a spare day.

When did Auli last get heavy snow?

On 23 January 2026, when heavy snowfall closed the Joshimath–Auli road for a time.
