Bilaspur is a lake town at about 670 m on the Kiratpur–Manali highway, about 110 km (2.5–3 hours) from Chandigarh. The old town was submerged when the Bhakra dam created the Gobind Sagar reservoir in the early 1960s, and the present town was rebuilt on the hillside above. When the water level drops, usually from about March to June, the stone shikhara temples of old Bilaspur rise out of the lake. Other things to do: boating and water sports on Gobind Sagar, paragliding from Bandla hill, and the hilltop Naina Devi temple in the same district. Bilaspur is warm for most of the year and makes an easy meal or overnight break on the way to Mandi, Kullu and Manali.

What to see

Things to do in Bilaspur

The submerged temples. Old Bilaspur's stone temples — a group of tall shikhara shrines — were left under the water when the reservoir filled. As the level falls in spring and early summer they appear again, standing in the shallows and on dry banks; it is a striking, quiet sight. Ask locally for the best viewpoint in the season.

Gobind Sagar. The reservoir, named after Guru Gobind Singh, stretches for many kilometres up the Sutlej valley. Boating, kayaking and other water sports are usually available near Bilaspur town in season. Bandla hill above the town is a take-off point for paragliding with views over the lake.

Naina Devi, one of Himachal's best-known hilltop shrines, is in the same district — see Naina Devi and the Naina Devi ropeway. Further down the Sutlej, the Bhakra dam is one of India's great dams; access to the dam itself is restricted, so check before planning a visit.

Getting there

How to reach Bilaspur

Bilaspur is on the four-lane Kiratpur–Manali highway: about 110 km from Chandigarh, 330 km from Delhi, 70 km to Mandi and 180 km to Manali. It is also on the Shimla–Mandi road (about 90 km from Shimla). Buses on the Delhi/Chandigarh–Manali route stop here. FAQ

Bilaspur: frequently asked questions

When can you see the submerged temples in Bilaspur?

When the Gobind Sagar water level is low, usually from about March to June.

How far is Bilaspur from Chandigarh?

About 110 km, 2.5–3 hours by road on the Kiratpur–Manali highway.

What is Gobind Sagar?

The reservoir on the Sutlej created by the Bhakra dam, named after Guru Gobind Singh.

Is there paragliding in Bilaspur?

Yes, from Bandla hill above the town, in season and weather permitting.

Can I visit the Bhakra dam?

Access to the dam itself is restricted; check current rules before planning a visit.

How far is Manali from Bilaspur?

About 180 km, roughly 4.5–5 hours via Mandi and Kullu.
