Delhi to Kasauli is about 300 km by road and takes 6–7 hours by car — NH-44 to Chandigarh, then the Shimla four-lane to Dharampur and the turn-off up to Kasauli. From Chandigarh it is about 60 km, 1.5–2 hours; a one-way taxi with us starts at ₹2,499 for a sedan (Ertiga ₹3,299, Innova Crysta ₹4,499). By train, go to Kalka or Chandigarh and drive up; the nearest toy-train stop is Dharampur. Buses to Shimla drop you at Dharampur, from where local buses and taxis climb to Kasauli. Snow in Kasauli is occasional — mostly a light fall in January.

Winter

Kasauli in winter

Kasauli is cold and clear from December to February, with a light snowfall in some years, usually in January — see Kasauli snowfall. Roads rarely close for long. Weekends around Christmas and New Year are busy with visitors from Chandigarh and Delhi; weekdays are quiet.

On arrival

What to see when you get there

Walk the Upper and Lower Mall, the old church and the pine-forest roads; see Kasauli tourist places for the full list. Nearby, Barog and Dagshai are a short drive away, and Solan and Chail make easy day trips.

FAQ

Delhi to Kasauli: common questions

What is the distance from Delhi to Kasauli?

About 300 km by road via Chandigarh, Parwanoo and Dharampur.

How far is Kasauli from Chandigarh?

About 60 km, 1.5–2 hours by road.

What is the taxi fare from Chandigarh to Kasauli?

With us, one-way from ₹2,499 for a sedan, ₹3,299 for an Ertiga and ₹4,499 for an Innova Crysta.

Which is the nearest railway station to Kasauli?

Kalka on the broad-gauge line, or Dharampur on the Kalka–Shimla toy train.

Does it snow in Kasauli?

Sometimes — usually a light fall in January, not every year.

Is Kasauli good for a weekend from Delhi?

Yes; it is about 6–7 hours by road, quieter than Shimla, and close to Barog, Dagshai and Solan.
