When it snows

Does it snow in Dharamshala?

Dharamshala climbs a steep slope: the lower town sits around 1,450 m, McLeod Ganj above it at about 2,080 m, and Naddi and Dharamkot a little higher still. Directly behind them, the Dhauladhar range rises to over 4,000 m. That height difference is the whole story of snow here.

When a strong western disturbance crosses Himachal in winter, the Dhauladhar and the Triund ridge get snow first. Only the colder spells push the snow line down to McLeod Ganj, Naddi and Dharamkot — usually in January or February, and not every year. The lower town mostly gets cold rain.

Last winter is a good example. On 23 January 2026, the Triund trekking site received fresh snow, while McLeod Ganj, Naddi, Dharamkot and Bhagsunag dropped to near-freezing temperatures. That same spell brought the season's first snow to Shimla and to Mussoorie in Uttarakhand.

Plan safely

Visiting Dharamshala in snow season

Getting there: Kangra (Gaggal) airport is about 15 km from Dharamshala; Pathankot is the nearest big railway station, about 90 km away. A Delhi to Dharamshala taxi takes most of a day.

Roads: the road up to McLeod Ganj and Naddi is steep and can ice over after snow — travel up in daylight with a hill driver, and expect short closures after a heavy spell.

Treks in snow: Triund and the trails above it change completely in snow, and local authorities sometimes restrict high treks in bad weather. Go with a local guide, start early, and turn back if the weather turns. We check current conditions the evening before.

Clothes: warm layers, a down or padded jacket, gloves, a cap and shoes with grip. Evenings in McLeod Ganj are freezing in January.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Does it snow in Dharamshala?

The lower town rarely gets snow. McLeod Ganj, Naddi and Dharamkot can get snow in January and February in a cold spell, and the Triund ridge and the Dhauladhar peaks above town are snowy for most of the winter.

Which month is best to see snow in Dharamshala?

January, then February. Late December is possible in a cold year. Even without snow in town, the Dhauladhar behind Dharamshala is snow-capped through the winter.

When did Triund get snow last winter?

Triund received fresh snow on 23 January 2026, while McLeod Ganj, Naddi and Bhagsunag dropped to near-freezing temperatures.

Can I trek to Triund in snow?

Sometimes, with a local guide and in good weather. Snow makes the upper trail slippery, and authorities can restrict high treks in bad weather, so check current conditions before you go.

Is Dharamshala or Manali better for snow?

Manali has surer snow nearby — Solang and Sissu are a short drive away. Dharamshala is better for snow views, monasteries and a quieter winter break, with snow on the Triund ridge above town.

How far is Dharamshala from the airport?

Kangra (Gaggal) airport is about 15 km from Dharamshala; McLeod Ganj is a little farther up the hill.
