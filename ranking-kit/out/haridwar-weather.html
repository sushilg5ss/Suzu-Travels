Haridwar lies on the Ganga at about 315 m, where the river enters the plains. By India Meteorological Department averages for nearby Roorkee (1991–2020), January is about 19 °C by day and 6 °C at night, often with dense morning fog, while May is the hottest month at about 38 °C. The monsoon (July–September) is wet, with the Ganga running high. For the Ardh Kumbh, January–April 2027, expect cold, foggy mornings for the January and February snans, mild days in March and hot afternoons by mid-April. The most comfortable months are October to March.

Ardh Kumbh 2027

Haridwar Ardh Kumbh 2027: weather on the bathing dates

The Ardh Kumbh in Haridwar runs from 14 January to 20 April 2027, with these main bathing dates announced in April 2026: 14 January (Makar Sankranti), 6 February (Mauni Amavasya), 11 February (Basant Panchami), 20 February (Magh Purnima), 6 March (Mahashivratri, Amrit Snan), 8 March (Phalguna Amavasya, Amrit Snan), 7 April (Nav Samvatsar), 14 April (Mesh Sankranti, Amrit Snan) and 20 April (Chaitra Purnima).

January and February snans: cold, often foggy mornings around 6–9 °C and cold river water — warm layers for the early-morning bath and the walk back. March snans: mild, pleasant days (about 30 °C) with cool mornings. April snans: hot afternoons (about 35 °C) — bathe early, carry water and keep out of the midday sun. Fog can delay trains and early-morning drives in January.

Before you go

Haridwar trip: what the weather means for you

Clothing: warm layers for winter mornings and evenings at the ghats; light cotton and sun protection from April to June; an umbrella in the monsoon.

Getting there: Haridwar is about 220 km from Delhi, 25 km from Rishikesh and about 40 km from Dehradun's Jolly Grant airport. In December and January, morning fog on the plains can slow trains and road travel. Cabs: Delhi to Haridwar taxi · Rishikesh taxi service.

How cold is Haridwar in January?

About 19 °C by day and 6 °C at night on average (Roorkee IMD reference), with dense fog on many mornings.

When is the Haridwar Ardh Kumbh 2027?

From 14 January to 20 April 2027. The Amrit Snans are on 6 March (Mahashivratri), 8 March (Phalguna Amavasya) and 14 April (Mesh Sankranti).

What will the weather be like for the Kumbh snans?

Cold, foggy mornings in January–February; mild in March; hot afternoons by mid-April. Check the live forecast a few days before.

Which is the hottest month in Haridwar?

May — about 38 °C by day on average.

When is the best time to visit Haridwar?

October to March for pleasant weather.
