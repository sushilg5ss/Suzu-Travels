When it snows

When does it snow in Pahalgam?

Pahalgam lies where the East and West Lidder rivers meet, at about 2,700 m, with Betaab Valley and Aru further up the valley. Winter snow comes with western disturbances from the west: the peaks above the town catch the first snow in autumn, and the town and forests are usually white from late December.

Last season began early. On 5 November 2025, the Srinagar weather office recorded 2.1 cm of snow at Pahalgam and 7.6 cm at Gulmarg in 24 hours, and the Srinagar–Leh highway was closed by snow. Chillai Kalan, the 40 coldest days of the Kashmir winter, then ran from 21 December 2025 to about 30 January.

In most winters, January is when Pahalgam is deepest in snow and coldest — nights well below freezing — while February keeps the snow with slightly milder days.

What's open

What is open in Pahalgam in winter?

After April 2025, several tourist spots around Pahalgam were closed while security was reviewed. Pahalgam town and the Lidder river reopened in June 2025, Betaab Valley reopened in June 2025 and Aru Valley in late September 2025. Baisaran, Tulian Lake and some trekking routes remained on the Anantnag district's closed list through 2026.

Orders can change at short notice, and in winter snow can block the road to Betaab or Aru even when they are open. We check the current district notice and the road before every Pahalgam day we plan.

Plan safely

Visiting Pahalgam in snow season

Getting there: Srinagar to Pahalgam is about 95 km, 2.5–3 hours by road via Anantnag. Two nights is ideal in winter, so a snow day is not lost to one bad-weather morning. See our Srinagar distance guide.

Roads: the main road to Pahalgam is usually kept open, but the side roads to Betaab, Aru and Chandanwari can stay blocked after heavy snow. Travel in daylight with a driver who knows the valley.

Rules and status: some spots and trek routes around Pahalgam have stayed closed under district orders since 2025. We follow the current notice and will tell you before you travel what is open.

Clothes: thermals, a down jacket, gloves, a cap and snow boots — nights in January are well below freezing.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Which month is best to see snow in Pahalgam?

January, during Chillai Kalan, is usually the snowiest and coldest. Late December and February also have snow in most winters.

Is Pahalgam open for tourists in winter?

Yes. Pahalgam town and the Lidder river are open, and Betaab Valley and Aru Valley reopened in 2025. Some spots, including Baisaran, stayed on the Anantnag district's closed list in 2026 — check the latest notice before you travel.

When did Pahalgam get snow last season?

Pahalgam recorded 2.1 cm of snow on 5 November 2025, an early spell, with deeper snow later in the winter.

Can I visit Betaab Valley and Aru in snow?

Usually, when the roads are cleared. Heavy snow can block the side roads for a day or more, so keep a buffer day.

How far is Pahalgam from Srinagar?

About 95 km, which takes 2.5–3 hours by road, longer after fresh snow.

Pahalgam or Gulmarg for snow?

Gulmarg is higher, has the Gondola and skiing, and the most reliable snow. Pahalgam is quieter, with snowy pine forest and river views. Many winter trips include both.
