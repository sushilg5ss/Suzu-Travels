Dhanaulti is a forested hill village at about 2,250 m, 24 km beyond Mussoorie on the road to Chamba. It is a little higher and colder than Mussoorie — roughly 1–2 °C cooler — and gets more snow: most winters bring several snowy days between late December and February, and Surkanda Devi (about 2,750 m) above it is often white when Mussoorie is not. Summers are mild, around 20 °C by day. The monsoon (July–August) is very wet. The best months are March–June, October–November, and January–February for snow.

Snow at Dhanaulti and Surkanda Devi

When does it snow in Dhanaulti?

Dhanaulti is one of the most reliable places for snow near Delhi. Snow usually comes with western disturbances between late December and February, and the deodar and pine forests can stay white for days afterwards. After fresh snow, the road from Mussoorie can be closed or slow at Buranskhanda and Kaddukhal — check before you set out.

Surkanda Devi, reached by a short, steep walk from Kaddukhal, about 8 km from Dhanaulti, stands at about 2,750 m and holds snow longer. On clear days from October to February its top looks out over the Garhwal Himalaya.

FAQ

Dhanaulti weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather in Dhanaulti today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Dhanaulti, Mussoorie and Dehradun (MET Norway data).

Does it snow in Dhanaulti?

Yes — usually on several days between late December and February, more often than in Mussoorie.

When is the best time to see snow in Dhanaulti?

January and the first half of February are the most likely; watch the live forecast for a western disturbance.

Is Dhanaulti colder than Mussoorie?

Yes, by about 1–2 °C, and it gets more snow.

How far is Dhanaulti from Mussoorie?

About 24 km; Dehradun is about 60 km away.

How high is Surkanda Devi?

About 2,750 m — a short, steep walk from Kaddukhal, about 8 km from Dhanaulti.
