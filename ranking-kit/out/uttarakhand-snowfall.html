In Uttarakhand, snow usually reaches the hill stations between late December and February, with January the most likely month. The high places — Kedarnath, Badrinath, Auli, Chopta–Tungnath, Harsil and Kedarkantha — get snow earlier and more reliably; this season the shrines had their first heavy snow in late October and early November 2026. Mussoorie, Dhanaulti, Chakrata, Mukteshwar and Munsiyari (about 2,000–2,300 m) get snow on some days most winters, but not every year: last season the first snow came only on 23 January 2026. For a sure snow trip, go to Auli, Chopta or the Kedarkantha trek between late December and March; for a short trip from Delhi, try Dhanaulti or Mussoorie's Lal Tibba in January.

When it snows

When does it snow in Uttarakhand?

Winter snow in Uttarakhand comes with western disturbances — weather systems from the west that bring a few days of cloud, rain in the valleys and snow on the heights. Height decides almost everything: above about 2,600 m (Auli, Chopta, Harsil, the Char Dham shrines) snow is regular from December to March; at 2,000–2,300 m (Mussoorie, Dhanaulti, Chakrata, Mukteshwar, Munsiyari) it comes on some days in a normal winter; below about 1,800 m it is rare.

This season started early on the heights: Kedarnath had its first heavy snow at the end of October 2026 and Badrinath had fresh snow on the night of 1–2 November. Last season shows how uncertain the hill stations are: after a long dry spell, the first snow reached Mussoorie, Dhanaulti, Chakrata, Auli and Munsiyari together on 23 January 2026.

So plan for a window, not a date — late December to February for the hill stations, December to March for the high places — and check the forecast above a week before you go.

Plan safely

Travelling in Uttarakhand in snow season

Roads: after a heavy spell, roads to Auli, Chopta, Harsil, Dhanaulti and Munsiyari can close for hours or days. Travel in daylight with a driver used to hill roads, keep a spare day, and expect chains or a smaller vehicle on the last stretch.

Crowds: the first snow brings big weekend crowds to Mussoorie and Dhanaulti, with long traffic queues from Dehradun. Midweek is calmer.

Clothes: a padded jacket, thermals, gloves, a cap and shoes with grip; for Auli, Chopta and Kedarkantha, proper winter boots and layers for well below freezing.

Shrines: Kedarnath, Badrinath, Gangotri and Yamunotri are closed in winter — see Char Dham closing dates 2026.

FAQ

Uttarakhand snowfall: frequently asked questions

When does it snow in Uttarakhand?

In the hill stations, usually between late December and February, with January the most likely month. The high places — Auli, Chopta, Harsil, Kedarkantha — get snow from December to March.

Has it snowed in Uttarakhand this season?

Yes, on the heights: Kedarnath had its first heavy snow at the end of October 2026 and Badrinath had fresh snow on 1–2 November. The hill stations usually get their first snow later, in December or January.

Which is the best place to see snow in Uttarakhand?

Auli is the surest, with its ski slopes 16 km by road above Joshimath; Chopta–Tungnath and the Kedarkantha trek are also reliable from late December to March.

Where can I see snow near Delhi in Uttarakhand?

Dhanaulti and Mussoorie's Landour and Lal Tibba, about 7–8 hours' drive from Delhi, give the best chance in January–February.

Does it snow in Nainital?

Only on a few days in some winters, usually in January; Mukteshwar, about 50 km away, gets snow more often.

When did it last snow in Mussoorie?

Last season the first snow reached Mussoorie, Dhanaulti, Chakrata, Auli and Munsiyari on 23 January 2026.
