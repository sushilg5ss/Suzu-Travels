Almora is an old Kumaon hill town on a ridge at about 1,640 m, roughly 60 km beyond Nainital. It is milder than Nainital or Mukteshwar: winter days are about 15–17 °C and nights a few degrees above freezing, with frost on clear mornings and snow only on rare days. Summer days reach the high 20s. July and August are the wettest months.October to February brings the clearest air and the best views of the Himalayan peaks from Kasar Devi and Binsar; March–June and September–November are the most comfortable months to visit.

  • WarmestMay–June — days in the high 20s.

Peaks and day trips

Almora weather and the Himalayan views

On clear days the snow peaks of the central Himalaya — Trishul, Nanda Devi and Panchachuli among them — line the northern horizon. The air is clearest from October to February, especially after rain; from April to June the views are often lost in haze, and in the monsoon in cloud.

The best viewpoints are on the ridges around the town: Kasar Devi, about 8 km away, and Binsar, about 30 km. Jageshwar's temple complex is about 35 km, in a deodar valley that is noticeably cooler in winter. In the monsoon, landslides can close the hill roads for hours.

Before you go

Almora trip: what the weather means for you

Clothing: a warm jacket and layers from November to February, especially for early-morning views; light layers in summer; a rain jacket in the monsoon.

Getting there: the nearest railway station is Kathgodam, about 85 km by road; Almora is about 60 km beyond Nainital. Keep a spare day in the monsoon. Cab: Nainital taxi service.

FAQ

Almora weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather in Almora today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Almora, Mukteshwar and Nainital (MET Norway data).

Does it snow in Almora?

Only rarely, on a few cold days in some winters. Mukteshwar and the higher ridges get snow more often.

How cold is Almora in winter?

January days are about 16 °C and nights about 5 °C (an estimate), with frosty mornings.

When can you see the Himalaya from Almora?

Most clearly from October to February, especially after rain; Kasar Devi and Binsar have the best views.

Which are the wettest months in Almora?

July and August.

When is the best time to visit Almora?

March–June and September–November; October–February for the clearest mountain views.
