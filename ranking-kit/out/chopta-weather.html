Chopta is a meadow and forest camp at about 2,680 m on the road between Ukhimath and Gopeshwar; from it a trail of about 3.5 km climbs to Tungnath (about 3,680 m) and on to Chandrashila (about 4,000 m). Summer days are cool, around 15–20 °C. Winters are snowy: from about December to March, Chopta and the trail lie under snow and nights fall well below freezing. Tungnath temple closed for the winter on 6 November 2026. The best months are April–June for rhododendrons and October–November for clear Himalayan views; January–February are for snow treks.

Tungnath and Chandrashila

Tungnath and Chandrashila: when to go

Tungnath, at about 3,680 m, is often called the highest Shiva temple in the world and is one of the Panch Kedar. It is open from about May to early November; in 2026 it closed on 6 November, and the deity spends the winter at Makkumath. Above it, Chandrashila (about 4,000 m) looks out over Nanda Devi, Trishul, Chaukhamba and the Kedarnath peaks.

The trail can be walked in winter, but from about December to March it is under snow, the temple is closed, and the road to Chopta can close after heavy falls. October–November give the clearest views; April–May bring rhododendrons in bloom across the slopes.

Before you go

Chopta trip: what the weather means for you

Clothing: a warm jacket and layers in every month; full winter wear, gaiters and microspikes for the trail from December to March; a rain jacket from late June to September.

Start early — mornings are clearest on Chandrashila; cloud often builds by midday.

Getting there: Chopta is reached by road from Rishikesh via Rudraprayag and Ukhimath — most of a day's drive. Cab: Rishikesh taxi service.

FAQ

Chopta weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather in Chopta today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Chopta, Tungnath and Joshimath (MET Norway data).

Does it snow in Chopta?

Yes — from about December to March Chopta and the Tungnath trail are usually under snow.

Is Tungnath open in winter?

No. In 2026 the temple closed on 6 November; it reopens around May.

How cold is Chopta in December?

Days around 8 °C and nights below freezing (an estimate); Tungnath is much colder.

What is the best time to visit Chopta?

April–June for rhododendrons and pleasant weather, October–November for clear views, January–February for snow.

How long is the trek from Chopta to Tungnath?

About 3.5 km, with Chandrashila about 1.5 km further up.
