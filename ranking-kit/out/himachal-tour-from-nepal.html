Nepali citizens do not need a visa to visit India, so a Himachal trip from Nepal is simple: fly Kathmandu → Delhi (about 1 hour 45 minutes) or cross overland at a border point such as Sunauli–Bhairahawa or Banbasa–Mahendranagar, then go by road or a short flight to the hills. Carry your passport or citizenship certificate — airlines and hotels ask for photo ID. From Delhi, Shimla is about 345 km (7–8 hours) and Manali about 540 km (11–13 hours). For snow, go between late December and February; for pleasant weather, March–June or September–November. A comfortable first trip is 7 days for Shimla and Manali.

Getting there

How to reach Himachal from Nepal

Most travellers fly from Kathmandu to Delhi; there are several daily flights. From Delhi, drive to Shimla or Manali, or take a domestic flight to Shimla or Kullu (Bhuntar) when the weather allows. Travellers from western Nepal often cross at Banbasa–Mahendranagar or Sunauli–Bhairahawa and continue to Delhi by bus or train.

When to go

Best time to visit Himachal from Nepal

Dashain and Tihar fall in autumn, which is a lovely, clear season in Himachal; the winter break brings snow; and spring and early summer are pleasant everywhere.

Documents

What ID do Nepali travellers need?

Under the India–Nepal treaty arrangements, Nepali citizens can enter and stay in India without a visa. For flights, airlines generally ask for a valid passport or citizenship certificate, and hotels in India ask for photo ID at check-in. Children travelling by air need their own ID. Rules can change — check with your airline before you travel.

FAQ

Himachal from Nepal: frequently asked questions

Do Nepali citizens need a visa for India?

No. Nepali citizens can travel to India without a visa; carry a passport or citizenship certificate as photo ID.

How long is the flight from Kathmandu to Delhi?

About 1 hour 45 minutes.

What is the best way to reach Manali from Kathmandu?

Fly to Delhi, then drive about 540 km (11–13 hours) or fly on to Kullu (Bhuntar) when weather allows.

When can we see snow in Himachal?

Usually from late December to February at Kufri, Narkanda, Solang and Sissu.

How many days do we need for Shimla and Manali?

About 7 days from Delhi; add 2–3 days for Dharamshala.

Can we pay in Nepali rupees?

Payments in India are in Indian rupees; exchange money before you travel or on arrival.
