Chillai Kalan is the 40-day core of the Kashmir winter, from 21 December to 29 January — so for this season, 21 December 2026 to 29 January 2027. It is followed by Chillai Khurd ("the small cold", 30 January–18 February) and Chillai Bachha ("the baby cold", 19–28 February). Nights stay below freezing, parts of Dal Lake can freeze, water pipes freeze, and snow that falls in these weeks lasts and feeds the glaciers. Srinagar averages about 7 °C by day and −2 °C at night in January (IMD 1991–2020); Gulmarg about 1 °C and −8 °C. It is also the best time for snow at Gulmarg, Sonamarg and Pahalgam — if you plan for the cold and for road and flight delays.

Life in the cold

What happens in Kashmir during Chillai Kalan

Freezing: water pipes in homes and hotels often freeze overnight, and parts of Dal Lake and other water bodies can freeze in the coldest spells. Electricity demand for heating rises sharply, and power cuts are common.

Keeping warm: Kashmiris wear the pheran, a long woollen cloak (21 December is observed as Pheran Day), and carry a kangri — an earthen pot of embers in a wicker basket — under it. Hearty foods such as harissa, a slow-cooked meat dish sold from early-morning shops in Srinagar, are a winter staple.

Travel: snow, ice and poor visibility can close the Jammu–Srinagar highway (NH-44) and delay or cancel flights at Srinagar airport. Keep a buffer day at the end of your trip, and book hotels with proper heating.

Planning a trip

Should you visit Kashmir in Chillai Kalan?

Yes, if you want snow. These weeks bring the most reliable snow at Gulmarg, Sonamarg and Pahalgam, and Srinagar's houseboats and Mughal gardens look very different under snow.

Plan for the cold: thermals, a down jacket, waterproof boots and gloves; confirm room heating (electric blankets or central heating) when you book. Put Gulmarg on the clearest forecast day; after snow, only 4×4s or cars with chains are allowed on the Tangmarg–Gulmarg road.

Be flexible: the highway and flights can be disrupted for a day or two after heavy snow — avoid tight connections on your last day. More: Kashmir in December and Kashmir in January.

Before you go

Chillai Kalan travel checklist

FAQ

Chillai Kalan: frequently asked questions

When is Chillai Kalan 2026–27?

From 21 December 2026 to 29 January 2027 — 40 days.

What comes after Chillai Kalan?

Chillai Khurd (30 January–18 February) and then Chillai Bachha (19–28 February).

How cold is Srinagar in Chillai Kalan?

IMD averages for January are about 7 °C by day and −2 °C at night; cold snaps go lower.

Does Dal Lake freeze?

Parts of Dal Lake can freeze during the coldest spells of Chillai Kalan, though not every year.

Is it a good time to visit Kashmir?

Yes for snow at Gulmarg, Sonamarg and Pahalgam — if you pack for the cold, book heated rooms and keep a buffer day for road and flight delays.

What are pheran and kangri?

The pheran is a long woollen cloak; the kangri is an earthen pot of embers in a wicker basket carried under it for warmth.
