The Kangra valley lies right below the Dhauladhar — Kangra town at about 733 m, Palampur at about 1,470 m and McLeod Ganj at about 2,080 m. India Meteorological Department averages for Kangra airport (Gaggal, 1991–2020) make January the coldest month (about 18.5 °C by day, 5.2 °C at night) and June the warmest (about 34.7 °C by day). It is one of the wettest valleys in Himachal — about 612 mm in July and 818 mm in August. Kangra and Palampur do not get snow, but from December to March the Dhauladhar peaks stand white above the tea gardens.

  • ColdestDecember–January — Kangra nights ~5 °C; Palampur a few degrees colder.
  • WarmestMay–June — Kangra ~34–35 °C by day.
  • WettestJuly–August, ~612 and ~818 mm — a very heavy monsoon.
  • ClearestOctober–November, about one rainy day a month.
  • SnowNot in the valley; on the Dhauladhar and Triund, December–March.

Dhauladhar

Snow in Kangra and Palampur: where you see it, where it falls

Kangra town and Palampur do not get snow, but the Dhauladhar peaks just above the valley (over 4,000 m) stay white from December to March — on a clear day, the best view in the valley is the snow line behind the tea gardens.

McLeod Ganj (~2,080 m): light snow in some winters, mostly January–February; Triund (~2,850 m) almost every winter — Dharamshala weather by month and Dharamshala snowfall.

Towards Bir–Billing and Bara Bhangal snow increases with height; the Billing road can close after snow from December to February.

Monsoon

Kangra's heavy monsoon: what to know

The monsoon is fierce here — about 20 rainy days in August. Landslides are common on the Dharamshala–McLeod Ganj road, Palampur–Bir and the village roads above, and cloud often hides the Dhauladhar.

In July–August keep dates flexible, carry a rain jacket and shoes with grip, and trek (Triund, Indrahar) only after checking the weather. The tea gardens are at their greenest in this season.

FAQ

Kangra and Palampur weather: questions

What is the weather in Kangra today?

The live forecast at the top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days for Kangra, Palampur and McLeod Ganj, using MET Norway data.

What is Palampur's weather like?

Palampur (~1,470 m) is about 4 °C cooler than Kangra town — pleasant in summer and cold in winter, but without snow. The Dhauladhar behind it is snow-covered from December to March.

Does it snow in Kangra?

Not in Kangra town or Palampur. Snow falls on the Dhauladhar peaks, at Triund and, in some winters, in McLeod Ganj.

When does it rain in Kangra?

A lot — about 612 mm in July and 818 mm in August (IMD averages), and about 2,270 mm a year. October and November are the driest.

What is the best time to visit Kangra?

October–November for clear skies and March–April for pleasant weather with snow on the Dhauladhar. April–May for the tea gardens.

How cold is Kangra in December?

IMD averages for Kangra airport are about 20.7 °C by day and 5.4 °C at night; Palampur and McLeod Ganj are colder at night.
