Renuka lake: a sacred lake in the green hills of Sirmaur

Himachal's largest natural lake lies low in the forested Sirmaur hills, ringed by temples and a wildlife park — an easy, warm-weather trip from Chandigarh, Dehradun or Delhi, and the scene of a big fair every November.

Renuka lake is the largest natural lake in Himachal Pradesh, at about 670 m in Sirmaur district, roughly 38 km from Nahan. Its long, curving shape is said to resemble a reclining woman, and it is named after Renuka, mother of Lord Parshuram; the Renuka Ji and Parshuram temples stand on its banks beside the smaller Parshuram tal. A big Renuka Ji fair is held here each year in November, a few days after Diwali. You can walk round the lake, take a boat and visit the small wildlife park. As it is low and warm, the best time is October to March; summers are hot. Renuka is about 130 km from Chandigarh and about 100 km from Dehradun.

What to see

Things to do at Renuka lake

The Renuka Ji temple and the Parshuram temple are the heart of the site; legend says Parshuram's mother Renuka took the form of the lake, and that Parshuram comes to meet her each year at the time of the fair. A path runs round the lake through the trees — about an hour's easy walk — and pedal boats are usually available.

The lake is surrounded by the Renuka Ji wildlife sanctuary, with a small zoo and lion safari area near the lake. Birdwatchers will find kingfishers, herons and many forest birds, and lotus covers parts of the water in late summer.

Getting there

How to reach Renuka lake

From Chandigarh, drive via Panchkula, Kala Amb and Nahan (about 130 km, 3.5–4 hours). From Dehradun, go via Paonta Sahib (about 100 km). From Delhi it is about 280 km via Yamunanagar and Nahan. The nearest railway stations are Kala Amb, Ambala and Dehradun; the nearest airports are Chandigarh and Dehradun. Distances to the rest of Himachal: Himachal distance chart.

Nahan, Sirmaur's old capital on a ridge at about 930 m, makes a good overnight stop with its bazaar, old tanks and the Jaitak fort viewpoint nearby. Paonta Sahib, on the Yamuna, has an important gurdwara linked with Guru Gobind Singh.

FAQ

Renuka lake: frequently asked questions

Is Renuka the largest lake in Himachal?

It is the largest natural lake in Himachal Pradesh; larger reservoirs such as Gobind Sagar and Pong are man-made.

How far is Renuka lake from Nahan?

About 38 km, roughly an hour by road.

When is the Renuka Ji fair?

In November, around Kartik Ekadashi a few days after Diwali; the exact dates change each year.

What is the best time to visit Renuka lake?

October to March; summers are hot.

Is boating allowed at Renuka lake?

Pedal boating is usually available; check timings locally.

How far is Renuka lake from Chandigarh?

About 130 km, 3.5–4 hours via Nahan.
