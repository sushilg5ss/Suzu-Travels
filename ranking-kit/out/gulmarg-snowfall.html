When it snows

When does it snow in Gulmarg?

Gulmarg sits in a cup-shaped meadow on the Pir Panjal range. Snow comes with western disturbances — winter weather systems from the west — and because the resort is high and the slopes above it climb steeply, snow arrives early up top and stays late.

The pattern is easy to see each autumn. Apharwat Peak, at the top of the Gondola, usually catches the first flakes in September or October; this season it got a fresh coat on 14 October 2026, with tourists out in it the same day. The meadow in town normally turns white in December, and by January the whole bowl is deep in snow.

Last winter, Gulmarg had about two inches of fresh snow on 21 December 2025, the first day of Chillai Kalan — the 40 coldest days of the Kashmir winter, which run until about 30 January. Snow in the valley floor around Srinagar is much less certain, which is why Gulmarg is where most snow trips to Kashmir are built.

Plan safely

Travelling to Gulmarg in snow season

Roads: Srinagar to Gulmarg is about 50 km, 1.5–2 hours. After fresh snow, the climb above Tangmarg needs snow chains and is sometimes open only to 4x4s for a few hours until it is cleared — keep a buffer day and travel up in daylight. Distances to Pahalgam and Sonmarg are in our Srinagar distance guide.

Gondola: tickets sell out quickly in the Christmas–New Year weeks and on winter weekends, so book on the official J&K Cable Car Corporation portal as soon as your dates are fixed. Phase 2 to Apharwat often stops in wind or heavy snow — plan it for the morning and keep a second day free.

Flights: Srinagar airport sometimes sees delays in heavy snow or fog; leave a gap before onward flights. A Srinagar taxi with a driver who knows the hill road is the easiest way up.

Clothes: a proper down or padded jacket, thermals, waterproof gloves, a cap and boots with grip. Snow boots and long coats can be hired in Tangmarg and Gulmarg.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Which month is best for snowfall in Gulmarg?

January and February are the most reliable months for deep snow. Late December usually has snow too, and early March is good for spring snow. October and November bring snow mainly to the upper slopes at Kongdoori and Apharwat.

When did Gulmarg get snow this season?

Apharwat Peak, at the top of the Gulmarg Gondola, got fresh snow on 14 October 2026. Last winter, the Gulmarg resort had about two inches of fresh snow on 21 December 2025, the first day of Chillai Kalan.

Does it snow in Gulmarg in December?

Usually yes — the meadow normally turns white in December, though a dry year can delay it. The upper slopes reached by the Gondola are almost always snowy by then.

When is the skiing season in Gulmarg?

Usually from January to early March, once the snow base is deep enough. Beginners can take lessons at Kongdoori; Apharwat is for experienced skiers.

Is it safe to drive to Gulmarg in snow?

Yes with care: chains are fitted at Tangmarg, the road is sometimes limited to 4x4s for a few hours after heavy snow, and it is best driven in daylight with a driver who knows it. Keep a buffer day.

How far is Gulmarg from Srinagar?

About 50 km, which takes 1.5–2 hours by road, longer after fresh snow.
