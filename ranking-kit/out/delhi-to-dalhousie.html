Delhi to Dalhousie is about 560 km by road and takes 10–11 hours by car via Ambala, Jalandhar and Pathankot. The easiest way is an overnight train from Delhi to Pathankot, then a taxi for the last 80 km (2.5–3 hours) — with us from ₹2,499 for a sedan (Ertiga ₹3,299, Innova Crysta ₹4,499). From Amritsar it is about 195 km, 4.5–5 hours (sedan from ₹2,999), and from Chandigarh about 300 km. Overnight Volvos also run from Delhi. In winter, the Dalhousie–Khajjiar road can close for a while after snow.

Winter

Getting to Dalhousie in winter

Dalhousie is one of the more reliable snow towns in lower Himachal in January–February. After a fall, the main road up from Pathankot is usually cleared first; the Dalhousie–Khajjiar road and the Dainkund side can stay closed for a while. Travel in daylight, carry warm layers, and keep a spare day. See Dalhousie snowfall, Dalhousie in December and Dalhousie in January.

FAQ

Delhi to Dalhousie: common questions

What is the distance from Delhi to Dalhousie?

About 560 km by road via Jalandhar and Pathankot.

Which is the nearest railway station to Dalhousie?

Pathankot, about 80 km away; the drive up takes 2.5–3 hours.

How far is Dalhousie from Amritsar?

About 195 km, 4.5–5 hours by road.

What is the taxi fare from Pathankot to Dalhousie?

With us, one-way from ₹2,499 for a sedan, ₹3,299 for an Ertiga and ₹4,499 for an Innova Crysta.

Is the road to Dalhousie open in winter?

Yes; the main road from Pathankot is usually cleared first after snow, while the Khajjiar road can stay closed for a while.

How far is Khajjiar from Dalhousie?

About 24 km by road.
