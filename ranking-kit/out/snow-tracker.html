In a normal Indian winter, snow is most likely from late December to February at the hill stations people can reach by road — Manali, Shimla, Kufri, Narkanda, Gulmarg and Auli. Higher points get it earlier and keep it longer: Sissu and the Atal Tunnel (about 3,130 m) and the upper slopes of Gulmarg often see snow from October, while Manali (about 2,050 m) and Shimla (about 2,205 m) town may wait until late December or January. Why height matters

Eight snow points, from lowest to highest

Snow in the Indian Himalaya depends mostly on height. When a winter weather system arrives, the snow line can sit at 2,500 m one week and 2,000 m the next — so the higher points on this list get snow first, more often, and keep it longest.

How it works

How this snow tracker works

Data: the board reads the 7-day Locationforecast from MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute, shared under CC BY 4.0) for each place's exact coordinates and height, refreshed through the day.

Snow amounts: when the forecast says precipitation will fall as snow, we show it as centimetres using the simple rule 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow. Real snow depth varies with temperature and wind, so read it as a guide.

How far ahead: snow forecasts are useful about a week out and get better each day. We check the board, the roads and local reports again the evening before every snow day we plan.

Coming from abroad?

Seeing snow in India as an overseas traveller

India's snow season runs mainly from late December to February. Most international flights land in Delhi; from there, Srinagar is a short flight for Gulmarg, Chandigarh and Kullu-Bhuntar serve Shimla and Manali, and Dehradun is the nearest airport for Auli.

A typical first snow trip is 5–7 days: a city night in Delhi or Chandigarh, then three or four nights in the hills with one buffer day for weather. See Kashmir for overseas travellers or ask us for a Himachal plan.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Where can I see snow in India right now?

Check the live board at the top of this page — it shows which of eight popular snow points (Manali, Solang, Sissu, Shimla, Kufri, Narkanda, Gulmarg and Auli) have snow in the 7-day forecast. In most winters, Gulmarg and Sissu have snow on the ground for the longest stretch.

Which place gets snow first in India?

Among places tourists can reach by road, the high points usually get it first — Sissu and the Atal Tunnel in Lahaul, and Apharwat above Gulmarg, often in October. Apharwat got fresh snow on 14 October 2026 this season.

Where does it snow in December in India?

Gulmarg, Sissu and Solang usually have snow in December; Kufri and Narkanda often do; Shimla and Manali town are possible in the second half of the month but not certain.

Which is better for snow, Manali or Gulmarg?

Gulmarg is higher and more reliably snowy, and has the Gondola and the best skiing. Manali is easier to reach by road from Delhi and Chandigarh, has more hotels, and puts Solang and Sissu within a short drive. Many travellers choose by starting city and dates.

What is the nearest snowfall place to Delhi?

Kufri and Narkanda near Shimla are the nearest regular snow points by road — Shimla is about 350 km from Delhi, Kufri 16 km beyond it. Manali, Solang and Sissu are farther but snowier.

How accurate is this snow forecast?

It is a good guide for the next few days and less certain towards day seven. Snow amounts are estimated from forecast precipitation; actual depth depends on temperature and wind.
