Shimla Airport is at Jubbarhatti, about 22 km from Shimla town (IATA code SLV). It is served only by Alliance Air with small ATR turboprops: when it resumed flights in May 2026 after a seven-month gap, it flew Delhi–Shimla about six times a week and Shimla–Dharamshala about five times a week. Seats are few and services are often suspended, so check the airline before you plan. The reliable alternative is to fly to Chandigarh and drive about 115 km (3–3.5 hours) — our Chandigarh to Shimla taxi starts at ₹2,499 — or take the Kalka–Shimla toy train when it is running.

Flights

Flights to Shimla airport

Alliance Air is the only airline at Shimla. In May 2026 it restarted Delhi–Shimla (about six flights a week) and Shimla–Dharamshala (about five a week) with ATR 42 turboprops, after a seven-month suspension. The route has stopped and restarted several times in recent years, and flights depend on good visibility on the hilltop runway. Book only after checking the current schedule on the airline's website, and keep a road plan as a back-up.

The Shimla–Dharamshala flight is the quickest link between Shimla and the Kangra valley — about an hour instead of 6–7 hours by road. At the other end you land at Kangra (Gaggal) airport, 13 km from Dharamshala.

Taxi

Taxi from the airport

There is no regular bus link worth planning around, so book a taxi with your flight number. Our Shimla taxi service does pickups at Jubbarhatti as well as Kalka station and Chandigarh airport; one-way drops from Shimla start around ₹4,899 to Manali and ₹5,199 to Delhi in a sedan. If your hotel is near the Mall, remember that the Mall and Ridge are closed to most traffic — the car stops at the nearest point and you walk the last stretch.

Tips

Before you fly

Weather: low cloud and winter snow can cancel flights; if you have an international connection from Delhi, keep a buffer day. Luggage: small aircraft can have tighter baggage limits than big jets — check your fare. Arriving from abroad: land in Delhi and connect, or fly on to Chandigarh. FAQ

Shimla airport: common questions

Where is Shimla airport?

At Jubbarhatti, about 22 km from Shimla town; the code is SLV.

Which airlines fly to Shimla?

Only Alliance Air, with small ATR turboprops; it resumed Delhi and Dharamshala flights in May 2026.

Is there a direct flight from Delhi to Shimla?

Yes, on most days of the week when the route is operating; check Alliance Air's current schedule.

Which is the nearest major airport to Shimla?

Chandigarh, about 115 km away (3–3.5 hours by road), with many flights a day.

How far is Shimla airport from the Mall Road?

About 22 km; the car stops near the Mall and you walk the last part, as the Mall is closed to most traffic.

Can I fly from Shimla to Dharamshala?

Yes, Alliance Air has flown Shimla–Dharamshala about five times a week since May 2026.
