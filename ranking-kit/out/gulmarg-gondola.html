The Gulmarg Gondola is a two-stage cable car run by the Jammu & Kashmir Cable Car Corporation, among the highest in the world.Phase 1 rises from Gulmarg (about 2,650 m) to Kongdoori (about 3,080 m) in roughly 10 minutes — snow play, sledging and beginner slopes. Phase 2 continues to a shoulder of Apharwat (about 3,950 m), with big Himalayan views and the expert ski terrain. It usually runs from about 10 am to 5 pm, but wind and snow often close Phase 2, especially in winter afternoons. Tickets are sold online on the official portal; they sell out in the Christmas–New Year weeks, on winter weekends and in May–June, so book as soon as your dates are fixed.

The two phases

Gulmarg Gondola Phase 1 vs Phase 2

Most visitors ride Phase 1; Phase 2 is higher, colder and more often closed. If you can, book both, and ride as early in the day as possible.

Weather and seasons

When to ride the Gulmarg Gondola

Winter (late December to March) is snow season: Kongdoori is deep in snow and Phase 2 serves skiers on Apharwat. It is also when wind and fresh snow most often stop Phase 2, so ride early and keep a spare morning. For skiing, see skiing in Gulmarg; for snow, Gulmarg snowfall.

Spring and summer (April to June) bring the longest queues — May is the busiest month for Gondola searches — with patches of snow still at the top in April and May. July to October is green and quieter; Apharwat may have little or no snow.

Whatever the season, it can be 10–15 °C colder at the top than in Gulmarg, with strong wind. Take a warm jacket even in June.

Getting there

How to reach the Gulmarg Gondola

The Gondola base station is a short walk or pony ride from the Gulmarg bus stand. Gulmarg is about 50 km from Srinagar — roughly 1.5 to 2 hours by road via Tangmarg. In heavy snow, vehicles may need chains above Tangmarg, and the road can be slow; leave Srinagar early.

Most travellers do Gulmarg as a day trip from Srinagar or stay a night in Gulmarg to get an early Gondola slot. We plan the day around the forecast and your Gondola booking. Cab: Srinagar taxi.

FAQ

Gulmarg Gondola: frequently asked questions

How high does the Gulmarg Gondola go?

Phase 1 reaches Kongdoori at about 3,080 m; Phase 2 reaches a shoulder of Apharwat at about 3,950 m.

How do I book Gulmarg Gondola tickets?

Online, on the official Jammu & Kashmir Cable Car Corporation portal. Book as soon as your dates are fixed, especially for Christmas–New Year, winter weekends and May–June.

What are the Gulmarg Gondola timings?

Usually about 10 am to 5 pm, but the Gondola runs only when the weather allows.

Is Phase 2 worth it?

On a clear, calm day, yes — the views from Apharwat are the highlight. It is colder and higher, and it closes more often in wind or fresh snow.

Is the Gulmarg Gondola open in winter?

Yes, it is busiest in winter for snow and skiing, but wind and fresh snow often stop Phase 2. Ride early and keep a spare morning.

Which is the best month for the Gulmarg Gondola?

January–February for deep snow and skiing; April–May for snow at the top with milder weather; September–October for quieter, clear days.

Can children and elderly people ride Phase 2?

Phase 2 reaches about 3,950 m. Phase 1 suits everyone; for infants, elderly travellers or anyone with heart or breathing problems, Phase 1 is the safer choice.
