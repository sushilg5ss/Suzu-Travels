Solang Valley is a side valley about 13 km north-west of Manali, at about 2,500 m — roughly 450 m higher and 3 °C colder than Manali. Using Manali's IMD 1991–2020 averages adjusted for height, January is about 7 °C by day and −4 °C at night, and June about 23 °C. Snow for skiing and sledging usually lies from late December to February; the gondola runs up to about 3,200 m; paragliding flies in the drier months. July and August are the wettest. After fresh snow, police allow only 4×4s with chains beyond Solang Nala on the road to the Atal Tunnel.

Snow and activities

Skiing, sledging, the gondola and paragliding: which months

Skiing and sledging on the beginner slopes need settled snow — usually late December to February. In years when Solang is bare, Sissu, through the Atal Tunnel, often still has snow.

The gondola climbs to about 3,200 m and runs most of the year, stopping in high wind or bad weather. Paragliding flies in the drier months and stops in rain and strong wind.

After fresh snow police allow only 4×4s with chains beyond Solang Nala towards the Atal Tunnel, or close the road until it is cleared. More: Solang Valley guide and Solang Valley activities.

When is there snow in Solang Valley?

Usually from late December to February. In bare years, Sissu beyond the Atal Tunnel often has snow.

When can you ski in Solang?

From late December to February, when there is settled snow on the beginner slopes.
