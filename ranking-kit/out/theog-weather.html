Theog is a ridge town about 30 km beyond Shimla on the highway to Narkanda and Kinnaur, at roughly Shimla's height, so its weather follows Shimla's closely. By India Meteorological Department averages for Shimla (1991–2020), January is about 9 °C by day and 2 °C at night, and July–August are the wettest months. Snow is likely from December to February, and heavy falls can briefly block the Shimla–Theog–Narkanda road; Narkanda and Hatu peak, higher up, get more. March–June and September–November are the most pleasant months.

  • ColdestJanuary — about 9° / 2 °C (Shimla reference).
  • SnowDecember–February; more at Narkanda and Hatu.
  • WettestJuly–August, with landslide risk.
  • Best monthsMarch–June, September–November.
  • Apple seasonAugust–October in the Theog–Kotkhai belt.

Snow on the highway

Theog, Narkanda and Hatu: snow and the road

Theog is on the Shimla–Narkanda–Rampur highway towards Kinnaur. After heavy snow the stretch from Kufri through Theog to Narkanda can be slippery or briefly blocked until it is cleared — check local updates and start early.

Narkanda (2,708 m) and Hatu peak above it are colder and snowier; the road up to Hatu closes in heavy snow. Theog is also the turn-off for the apple belt of Kotkhai and Jubbal.

Before you go

Theog trip: what the weather means for you

Clothing: a warm jacket all year for the evenings; full winter wear, gloves and shoes with grip from December to February; rain gear in July–August.

Driving in snow: after fresh snow, travel in daylight with a driver used to icy hill roads, and allow extra time between Kufri and Narkanda.

FAQ

Theog weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather in Theog today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Theog, Narkanda and Shimla (MET Norway data).

Does it snow in Theog?

Yes — snow is likely from about late December to February; Narkanda and Hatu peak, higher up, get more.

How cold is Theog in January?

About 9 °C by day and 2 °C at night (Shimla's IMD averages, which Theog follows closely); colder after snow.

How far is Theog from Shimla?

About 30 km on the highway towards Narkanda.

Is the road to Narkanda open after snow?

Usually within a day or so of clearing; heavy snow can block it briefly. Check local updates and start early.

What is the best time to visit Theog?

March–June and September–November; December–February for snow.
