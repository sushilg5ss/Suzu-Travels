Delhi to Dharamshala is about 475 km by road and takes 9–10 hours by car via Ambala, Kharar, Ropar, Una, Dehra and Kangra. From Chandigarh it is about 240 km, 5.5–6.5 hours, and from Pathankot about 90 km, 2.5–3 hours. The quickest way is a flight to Gaggal (Kangra) airport, about 13 km from Dharamshala; the classic budget way is an overnight AC Volvo to Dharamshala or McLeodganj (10–12 hours), or a night train to Pathankot and a taxi up. A one-way taxi from Delhi with us starts at ₹7,199 for a sedan (Ertiga ₹9,099, Innova Crysta ₹12,399).

Airport and station

Gaggal airport and Pathankot station

Gaggal (Kangra) airport is about 13 km from Dharamshala and 22 km from McLeodganj. There are direct flights from Delhi, but seats are few and bad weather can cancel flights in winter.

Pathankot (Pathankot Junction and Chakki Bank) is the nearest broad-gauge railhead; overnight trains from Delhi arrive in the morning. A narrow-gauge Kangra Valley train also runs from Pathankot, but it is slow — most travellers take a taxi or bus up.

Winter

Delhi to Dharamshala in winter

The road stays open through winter. Fog is common on the plains in December–January mornings, so start after first light. Dharamshala town rarely gets heavy snow, but McLeodganj, Naddi and the Dhauladhar above often do — see Dharamshala snowfall and Dharamshala in December. For snow trips from here, Triund is the classic winter walk.

Delhi to Dharamshala: common questions

What is the distance from Delhi to Dharamshala?

About 475 km by road via Ambala, Ropar, Una and Kangra.

How far is Dharamshala from Chandigarh?

About 240 km, 5.5–6.5 hours by road.

Which is the nearest airport to Dharamshala?

Gaggal (Kangra) airport, about 13 km from Dharamshala, with direct flights from Delhi.

Which is the nearest railway station to Dharamshala?

Pathankot, about 90 km away, is the nearest broad-gauge station; take a taxi or bus from there.
