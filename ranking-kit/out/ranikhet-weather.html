Ranikhet is a quiet Kumaon cantonment town on a pine-covered ridge at about 1,870 m, roughly 60 km from Nainital. Winters are cold and sunny: January days are around 14 °C and nights a few degrees above freezing, with light snow on a few days in some winters, usually January–February. Summer days stay mild, in the mid-20s. July and August are the wettest months. The snow peaks of the central Himalaya — Trishul and Nanda Devi among them — are clearest from October to February; March–June and September–November are the most comfortable months to visit.

Peaks, orchards and golf

Ranikhet weather and what to see

On clear mornings the Himalayan range — Trishul, Nanda Devi, Nanda Ghunti and their neighbours — stretches across the northern horizon. The air is clearest from October to February, especially after rain; from April to June haze often hides the peaks.

Around the town, the Chaubatia orchards (about 10 km) blossom in spring and fruit in late summer, the golf course at Upat (about 5 km) is green after the monsoon, and Bhalu Dam is an easy walk from Chaubatia. Kausani, with an even wider view of the range, is a few hours' drive away.

Before you go

Ranikhet trip: what the weather means for you

Clothing: a warm jacket and layers from November to February; a light jacket for summer evenings; rain gear in the monsoon.

Getting there: the nearest railway station is Kathgodam, about 80 km by road; Ranikhet is about 60 km from Nainital and 50 km from Almora. Cab: Nainital taxi service.

FAQ

Ranikhet weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather in Ranikhet today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Ranikhet, Kausani and Almora (MET Norway data).

Does it snow in Ranikhet?

Light snow falls on a few days in some winters, usually in January or February; it does not happen every year.

How cold is Ranikhet in winter?

January days are about 14 °C and nights about 4 °C (an estimate), colder after snow.

When can you see the Himalaya from Ranikhet?

Most clearly from October to February, especially after rain.

Which are the wettest months in Ranikhet?

July and August.

When is the best time to visit Ranikhet?

March–June and September–November; October–February for the clearest mountain views.
