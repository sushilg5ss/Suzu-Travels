Chakrata is a quiet cantonment town at about 2,118 m in the Jaunsar-Bawar hills, roughly 90 km from Dehradun via Kalsi. Its weather is close to Mussoorie's: by India Meteorological Department averages for Mussoorie, January is about 10 °C by day and 3 °C at night and June about 23 °C. Snow falls on a few days in most winters, usually in January and February, and more often on the higher ridges at Lokhandi and Deoban. July and August are very wet, when Tiger Falls is at its fullest but the roads are prone to landslides. The best months are March–June and October–November.

Snow, forests and waterfalls

Snow at Chakrata, Lokhandi and Deoban

Chakrata usually sees a few snowy days between late December and February. The higher forest ridges beyond the town — Lokhandi and Deoban, among deodar and oak — catch snow more often and hold it longer, and on clear winter mornings look out over the snow peaks of the Garhwal Himalaya.

Tiger Falls, a tall waterfall in the forest below Chakrata, is fullest in the monsoon and after it, in August–October; the path down can be slippery in the rain. The meadow of Moila top, with its small wooden temple, is a pleasant walk from April to November.

Before you go

Chakrata trip: what the weather means for you

Clothing: warm layers in every season; full winter wear from December to February; a rain jacket and good shoes in the monsoon.

Plan ahead: Chakrata is a quiet cantonment town with few shops and fuel stops; carry what you need and start the drive from Dehradun early, especially in winter and the monsoon.

FAQ

Chakrata weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather in Chakrata today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Chakrata, Mussoorie and Dehradun (MET Norway data).

Does it snow in Chakrata?

Yes — on a few days in most winters, usually January–February. Lokhandi and Deoban, higher up, get more.

How cold is Chakrata in December?

Days around 12–13 °C and nights near 4 °C on average, colder on the ridges (Mussoorie IMD reference).

When is the best time to visit Chakrata?

March to June and October to November; January–February for a chance of snow.

How far is Chakrata from Dehradun?

About 90 km via Kalsi — roughly 3 hours by road.

When is Tiger Falls best?

August to October, when it is fullest after the monsoon.
