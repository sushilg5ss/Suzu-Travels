Rohtang Pass (about 3,978 m) is around 51 km above Manali, nearly 2,000 m higher, and far colder and windier. It is usually open to tourist vehicles from about May to November — in 2026 the road reopened up to Marhi in early May — and normally closes to tourists for the winter around mid-November. Snow can fall in any month; this season around four inches fell at Rohtang on 17–18 September 2026. A rationed online permit is needed, and the pass stays closed to tourist vehicles on Tuesdays. In winter, use the Atal Tunnel to Sissu instead — no permit needed.

Permit and planning

Rohtang permit and planning your day

The permit is applied for online on the Tourism Development Council Manali portal (rohtangpermits.nic.in). For a car, jeep or MUV it is ₹500 plus a ₹50 congestion charge, valid for one day up and back; the portal opens only for the next six days. The vehicle must be under ten years old with a valid PUC certificate. Daily vehicle numbers are capped.

Leave Manali early: on clear days cloud and wind build by afternoon, and the road gets busy. Even in June the pass can be close to freezing in the wind — carry a warm jacket, gloves and sunglasses.

No permit is needed for the Atal Tunnel and Sissu, which stay open in winter except after heavy snow. More: Rohtang permit and road status and Atal Tunnel and Sissu snow trip.

FAQ

Rohtang Pass weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather at Rohtang Pass today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Rohtang, Solang and Manali (MET Norway data).

Is Rohtang Pass open now?

It is usually open to tourist vehicles from about May to mid-November and closed in winter. It is also closed to tourist vehicles every Tuesday. Check the permit portal for your date.

When does it snow at Rohtang?

Snow can fall in any month; it is deep from November to May. This season about four inches fell on 17–18 September 2026.

Do I need a permit for Rohtang?

Yes — an online, one-day permit from rohtangpermits.nic.in: ₹500 plus a ₹50 congestion charge for a car.

How cold is Rohtang?

Much colder than Manali — roughly 12 °C colder, and windy. Carry a warm jacket even in summer.

Where can I see snow near Manali in winter?

Take the Atal Tunnel to Sissu in Lahaul, or go to Solang. Neither needs the Rohtang permit.
