When it snows

When does it snow in Shimla?

Snow in Shimla comes with western disturbances — weather systems that cross north India in winter. When one is strong and the air is cold enough, the Ridge, Mall Road and Jakhu turn white; when the winter is dry, the town sees frost and clear blue days instead.

Recent seasons show how much it varies. On 8 December 2024, Shimla, Kufri and Fagu got the season's first snow — so early that long-time residents said they could not remember a December start like it. A year later the pattern flipped: after roughly three months without rain or snow, the first snow of the 2025-26 winter reached Shimla only on 23 January 2026.

So the honest answer is a window, not a date: late December to February, with January the safest bet and early March possible in a cold year.

Plan safely

Travelling to Shimla in snow season

Roads: Chandigarh to Shimla is about 115 km and usually stays open; after a heavy spell, the roads above Dhalli towards Kufri and Narkanda can be closed for hours until they are cleared. Snow often freezes into black ice early in the morning — travel in daylight and keep a buffer day.

Cars: hill drivers fit chains when needed; small hatchbacks struggle on icy climbs, so we use sedans or SUVs with drivers who know the road. A Chandigarh to Shimla taxi is the easiest start.

Toy train: the Kalka–Shimla line runs in winter, but services change — check our toy train status page before you plan around it.

Clothes: layers, a down or padded jacket, gloves, a cap, woollen socks and shoes with grip. Snow play in Kufri is easier in rented boots.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

Which month does it snow in Shimla?

January is the most likely month, then February; snow can also come in late December. Some winters bring several spells to Shimla town and others almost none — in 2025-26 the first snow came only on 23 January 2026.

When did Shimla get its first snowfall last season?

On 23 January 2026, after a dry spell of about three months. The season before, Shimla, Kufri and Fagu got their first snow on 8 December 2024.

Does it snow in Shimla in December?

Sometimes. It is possible, mostly in the second half of the month, but far from certain. Kufri and Narkanda, higher up, are more likely to have snow in December than the town.

Where can I see snow near Shimla if there is none in town?

Try Kufri (about 16 km), Fagu (about 22 km) or Narkanda and Hatu Peak (about 60–65 km). If the weather does not cooperate, Snow Kingdom in Kufri has indoor snow all year.

Is it safe to travel to Shimla during snowfall?

Yes with care: travel in daylight, use an experienced hill driver, expect short road closures above Dhalli and towards Narkanda after heavy spells, and keep a buffer day. Black ice is the main risk early in the morning.

How far is Shimla from Chandigarh and Delhi?

About 115 km (3–3.5 hours) from Chandigarh and about 350 km (8–9 hours) from Delhi by road, longer in snow or holiday traffic.
