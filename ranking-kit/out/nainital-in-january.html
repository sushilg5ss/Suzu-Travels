January is Nainital's coldest month. Days average about 11 °C and nights about 2 °C (IMD normals), with frost on clear mornings. The town gets light snow on a few days in most winters, usually in January–February, but not on dates you can plan around; the higher points — Snow View, Naina (China) Peak and the road to Kilbury — catch more, and Mukteshwar, about 50 km away, gets snow more often. Clear January days also bring some of the year's best views of the Himalayan peaks. Outside the New Year week, January is quiet and good value.

January weather

What is the weather like in Nainital in January?

January brings Nainital's lowest temperatures: about 10.9 °C by day and 1.7 °C at night on average, colder on clear nights. Most days are sunny and crisp, with the Himalayan peaks often visible from Snow View; western disturbances bring a few grey days with rain in the bowl and snow on the ridges.

Snow in Nainital itself is usually light and short-lived. For a better chance, stay higher — near Snow View or on the Kilbury road — or plan a night in Mukteshwar, which is a little higher and more open to the cold.

Nainital in January: roads, crowds and packing

Getting there: Kathgodam, the nearest railway station, is about 35 km (roughly 1 hour 15 minutes) below Nainital. Morning fog on the plains can delay trains in January. Cab: Kathgodam to Nainital taxi · Nainital taxi service.

Roads: the Kathgodam–Nainital road usually stays open; after snow, the higher roads to Kilbury, Snow View and Mukteshwar can be icy or closed for a few hours. Travel in daylight with a driver used to hill roads.

Crowds: busy over New Year and on weekends after a snowfall; quiet the rest of the month.

Packing: a padded jacket, thermals, gloves, a woollen cap and shoes with grip for icy lanes.

FAQ

Nainital in January: frequently asked questions

Does it snow in Nainital in January?

Lightly, on a few days in most winters, usually in January–February. Snow View, Naina Peak and Mukteshwar get more.

How cold is Nainital in January?

About 10.9 °C by day and 1.7 °C at night on average (IMD normals), with frosty mornings.

Is January a good time to visit Nainital?

Yes, for cold, clear days, Himalayan views and a chance of snow. Pack warm clothes and keep some time in hand.

Where can I see snow near Nainital?

On the higher points above the town — Snow View, Naina Peak, the Kilbury road — and at Mukteshwar, about 50 km away.

How far is Nainital from Kathgodam?

About 35 km, roughly 1 hour 15 minutes by road.

Is Nainital crowded in January?

Over New Year and on weekends after snow; the rest of January is quiet.
