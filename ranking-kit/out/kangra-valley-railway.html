The Kangra Valley Railway: the slow train under the Dhauladhar

The Kangra Valley Railway is the narrow-gauge “toy train” line from Pathankot in Punjab to Jogindernagar in Mandi district, built by the British in the late 1920s. It runs through the Kangra valley past Nurpur Road, Jawalamukhi Road, Kangra, Nagrota, Palampur and Baijnath Paprola, with views of the snowy Dhauladhar. Services stopped in August 2022, when floods washed away the bridge over the Chakki river near Pathankot. A new bridge has since been built, and from March 2026 a few trains have run again, mainly between Pathankot and Baijnath; by mid-2026 only some of the old services were back, and through trains to Jogindernagar had not fully returned. Check the current timetable on the official railway enquiry site before you plan, and keep a car as a back-up.

Riding it

Tips for riding the toy train

The trains are slow, simple and cheap — the point is the scenery, not speed. Sit on the side facing the mountains (the north side as the train heads east) for Dhauladhar views. Most seats are unreserved: arrive early, carry small change, and bring water and snacks, as stations are basic. A short ride — for example Kangra to Palampur, or Palampur to Baijnath — gives the feel of the line in a couple of hours; then continue by road. For the other famous hill railway, see the Kalka–Shimla toy train.

Back-up

If the train is not running

The highway follows much of the same valley. A full day by car from Dharamshala (up to 250 km — for example Palampur, Baijnath and Bir, or Kangra Fort and Masroor) costs from ₹3,000 in a sedan, ₹3,500 in an Ertiga and ₹4,750 in an Innova Crysta with our Dharamshala taxi service. Arriving by air? Land at Kangra (Gaggal) airport.

FAQ

Kangra Valley Railway: common questions

Is the Pathankot–Jogindernagar train running now?

Partly. After the Chakki bridge was rebuilt, some trains restarted from March 2026, mainly between Pathankot and Baijnath; check the official enquiry site for current trains.

Why did the Kangra valley train stop?

Floods washed away the railway bridge over the Chakki river near Pathankot in August 2022.

Where does the Kangra Valley Railway start and end?

At Pathankot in Punjab and Jogindernagar in Mandi district, Himachal Pradesh.

Which station is nearest to Dharamshala?

Kangra is the closest station on the line, about 20 km from Dharamshala; Pathankot is the nearest broad-gauge station, about 90 km away.

Can I book the Kangra toy train online?

Most travel is in unreserved coaches with tickets bought at the station; check IRCTC for any reserved options.

Is the Kangra Valley Railway the same as the Shimla toy train?

No — the Shimla toy train is the separate Kalka–Shimla railway.
