Munsiyari is a mountain town at about 2,200 m in Pithoragarh district, facing the five summits of the Panchachuli across the Gori Ganga valley. Winters are cold — days around 10–12 °C, nights at or below freezing — and snow falls in most winters between December and February, much more on Khaliya Top above the town. Summers are mild, around 20–23 °C. The monsoon (July–September) is wet and cloudy, with frequent landslides on the long road in. October–November gives the clearest Panchachuli views; March–June is the main season; January–February is for snow.

Panchachuli and Khaliya Top

Panchachuli views, Khaliya Top and snow

The Panchachuli peaks rise straight across the valley from Munsiyari, and their sunrise and sunset glow is the reason most people come. They are clearest from October to early December and on crisp mornings in spring; from July to September they are usually hidden in cloud.

Khaliya Top, a high meadow reached by a day trek from near Munsiyari, is snow-covered from about December to March and is a favourite winter snow walk; in spring its slopes turn red with rhododendrons. Munsiyari is also the starting point for the trek to the Milam glacier, which is a summer trek.

Before you go

Munsiyari trip: what the weather means for you

Clothing: warm layers in every season; full winter wear from December to February; rain gear in the monsoon.

Getting there: Munsiyari is a long drive — roughly 275 km from Kathgodam, and about 127 km from Pithoragarh. Break the journey at Chaukori or Birthi, and keep a buffer day: snow on the Kalamuni pass in winter and landslides in the monsoon can close the road.

FAQ

Munsiyari weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather in Munsiyari today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Munsiyari, Almora and Nainital (MET Norway data).

Does it snow in Munsiyari?

Yes — in most winters between December and February; Khaliya Top above the town gets heavy snow.

When can you see the Panchachuli peaks clearly?

October to early December is best; they are usually hidden by cloud from July to September.

How cold is Munsiyari in December?

Days around 12–14 °C and nights near freezing (Mukteshwar IMD reference).

What is the best time to visit Munsiyari?

March–June and October–November; December–February for snow.

How far is Munsiyari from Kathgodam?

Roughly 275 km — a long day's drive; most people break the journey.
