Chandigarh to Shimla is about 115 km by road and takes 3–3.5 hours by car on the four-lane highway (NH-5) via Pinjore, Parwanoo, Dharampur and Solan. HRTC and private buses from ISBT Sector 43 take about 4 hours. A one-way taxi with us starts at ₹2,499 for a sedan (Ertiga ₹3,299, Innova ₹4,499). The Kalka–Shimla toy train starts from Kalka, about 25 km from Chandigarh — but in late 2026 no trains run on the Kalka section until 31 December, so check the status first. The road stays open all year; after snow, the last stretch from Shoghi into Shimla can be slippery in the early morning.

Winter

Chandigarh to Shimla in winter

The highway stays open through the winter. Snow is rare below Solan; it falls more often from Shoghi upwards and in Shimla itself in January–February. After a snowfall, leave after sunrise, expect slow traffic for the last 15 km, and allow extra time if you plan to continue to Kufri or Narkanda. Christmas and New Year weekends bring long queues at the Parwanoo and Shoghi stretches — leave Chandigarh before 7 am.

Stops

Good stops and detours on the way

Kasauli — a quiet colonial hill station 30–40 minutes off the highway from Dharampur. Barog — the railway station by the famous long tunnel. Solan — temples, the Bon monastery at Dolanji and the bazaar. Chail — the Patiala palace and high cricket ground, a 1-hour detour from Kandaghat. Many travellers do Kasauli or Chail as an overnight stop and reach Shimla the next morning.

FAQ

Chandigarh to Shimla: common questions

What is the distance from Chandigarh to Shimla?

About 115 km by road on NH-5 via Parwanoo and Solan.

How long does Chandigarh to Shimla take?

3–3.5 hours by car and about 4 hours by bus; longer on holiday weekends.

What is the taxi fare from Chandigarh to Shimla?

With us, one-way from ₹2,499 for a sedan, ₹3,299 for an Ertiga and ₹4,499 for an Innova Crysta.

Is the Kalka–Shimla toy train running?

In late 2026 no trains run on the Kalka section until 31 December; check the latest status before planning.

Is the Chandigarh–Shimla road open in winter?

Yes; snow is rare below Solan, but the last stretch from Shoghi into Shimla can be icy after a snowfall.

What is the distance from Shimla to Chandigarh?

The same road, about 115 km; the drive down takes about 3 hours, longer on Sunday evenings when weekend traffic returns to the plains.

Which places can I visit on the way?

Kasauli from Dharampur, Barog and Dagshai near Solan, Solan itself, and Chail from Kandaghat.
