Bandipora (about 1,580 m) sits on the shore of Wular lake in north Kashmir; the Gurez valley, about 86 km away over the Razdan Pass, is at about 2,400 m. Bandipora's weather is close to Srinagar's: by India Meteorological Department averages for Srinagar (1991–2020), January is about 7 °C by day and −2 °C at night and July about 30 °C, with March the wettest month. Gurez is far colder and gets heavy winter snow — the Razdan Pass closes in winter and Gurez is cut off for about six months a year. May–October is the window for Gurez; April–June and September–October are best for Wular lake.

Gurez and Wular

Gurez valley and Wular lake: when to go

Gurez lies on the Kishanganga below the pyramid-shaped Habba Khatoon peak, about 123 km from Srinagar. The road crosses the Razdan Pass, which closes with heavy snow in winter — the valley is cut off for about six months a year, so plan Gurez between roughly May and October and check the J&K Traffic Police advisory first.

Wular lake, one of the largest freshwater lakes in South Asia, spreads beside Bandipora at about 1,580 m; its area changes a lot with the seasons. Spring and autumn are the clearest times for the lake.

FAQ

Bandipora and Gurez weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather in Bandipora today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Bandipora, Gurez and Srinagar (MET Norway data).

Is Gurez open in winter?

No — the Razdan Pass closes with heavy snow and Gurez is cut off for about six months a year.

When is the best time to visit Gurez?

Roughly June to September; May and October are possible when the pass is open.

How far is Gurez from Srinagar?

About 123 km; it is about 86 km from Bandipora.

Where is Wular lake?

Beside Bandipora town in north Kashmir, at about 1,580 m — one of the largest freshwater lakes in South Asia, its area changing a lot with the seasons.

Is Bandipora colder than Srinagar?

Only slightly — it is at about the same height. Gurez is much colder.
