Manali to Kasol is about 80 km by road and takes 2.5–3.5 hours by car. The route follows the Beas down to Kullu (about 40 km) and Bhuntar (about 50 km), then turns up the Parvati valley past Jari to Kasol (about 30 km from Bhuntar). By bus, take an HRTC bus from Manali to Bhuntar or Kullu and change to a Kasol/Manikaran bus. The road is open all year; in winter, watch for ice on shaded bends in the Parvati valley, and in the monsoon for landslides. From Kasol, Manikaran is about 4 km further, Tosh is at the end of the road, and Malana is a side trip from Jari.

Winter

Manali to Kasol in winter

The Manali–Kasol road stays open in winter. Kasol itself rarely gets heavy snow, but after a snowfall the shaded bends between Jari and Kasol, and the road beyond Manikaran towards Barshaini, can stay icy for days — leave after the sun is up and avoid driving after dark. If you want snow, it is usually higher up, around Tosh and on the Manali side at Solang and Sissu. See Kasol in December for the valley in winter.

Plan

A 3-day Manali–Kasol plan

Day 1: leave Manali after breakfast, stop at Kullu for shawls, reach Kasol by early afternoon and walk across the bridge to Chalal. Day 2: morning at Manikaran, afternoon to Barshaini and up to Tosh. Day 3: day trip to Malana from Jari — or start the drive back to Manali or on to Delhi. More ideas: Kasol trip plan · Kasol ghumne ki jagah.

FAQ

Manali to Kasol: common questions

What is the distance from Manali to Kasol?

About 80 km by road via Kullu and Bhuntar.

How long does it take from Manali to Kasol?

2.5–3.5 hours by car; 4–5 hours by bus including the change at Bhuntar or Kullu.

Is there a direct bus from Manali to Kasol?

Most travellers take an HRTC bus to Bhuntar or Kullu and change to a Kasol or Manikaran bus, which run through the day.

Is the Manali–Kasol road open in winter?

Yes, it stays open, but shaded bends in the Parvati valley can be icy after snow; drive in daylight.

Which is better, Manali or Kasol?

Manali has snow points, more hotels and activities; Kasol is smaller and quieter, a base for Malana, Manikaran, Tosh and treks. Many trips do both.

How far is Manikaran from Kasol?

About 4 km further up the Parvati valley.
