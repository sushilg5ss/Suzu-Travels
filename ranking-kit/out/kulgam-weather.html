Kulgam (about 1,739 m) lies in south Kashmir, about 67 km from Srinagar; Aharbal and its waterfall are about 25 km further up the Veshaw valley. Kulgam's climate is close to neighbouring Qazigund's: by India Meteorological Department averages for Qazigund (1991–2020), January is about 6.6 °C by day and −3.1 °C at night, July about 28 °C by day, and February–March are the wettest months, with snow in winter. Aharbal, about 500 m higher, is colder and snowier. April–June and September–October are the best months; the Kausar Nag trek is a summer trip.

Aharbal and Kausar Nag

Aharbal waterfall and Kausar Nag: when to go

Aharbal waterfall on the Veshaw river is at its most powerful in late spring and early summer (May–June), when snowmelt swells the river; in autumn the flow is gentler and the valley turns gold. In winter the area is snowbound and the road can close after heavy snow.

Kausar Nag (about 4,000 m) is a high lake reached by a multi-day trek from Aharbal — a summer trip, roughly July to September, when the snow has melted from the route.

Before you go

Kulgam trip: what the weather means for you

Clothing: a warm jacket and layers from October to April; full winter wear for Aharbal in winter; a light jacket for summer evenings and rain gear in spring.

At the falls: the rocks around Aharbal are wet and slippery — keep to the railings, especially in May–June when the water is high.

FAQ

Kulgam weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather in Kulgam today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Kulgam, Aharbal and Qazigund (MET Norway data).

Does it snow in Kulgam?

Yes, in winter (December–February); Aharbal, higher up, gets more and is often snowbound.

When is Aharbal waterfall best?

May–June, when snowmelt makes the falls most powerful; autumn is quieter and colourful.

How far is Aharbal from Kulgam?

About 25 km; Kulgam itself is about 67 km from Srinagar.

How cold is Kulgam in January?

About 6–7 °C by day and around −3 °C at night (Qazigund's IMD averages as a reference).

When can you trek to Kausar Nag?

In summer, roughly July to September, when the snow has cleared from the route.
