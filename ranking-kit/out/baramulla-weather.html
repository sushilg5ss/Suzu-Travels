Baramulla (about 1,590 m) is the main town of north Kashmir on the Jhelum, at almost the same height as Srinagar, so its weather follows Srinagar's closely. Using Srinagar's IMD 1991–2020 averages, January is the coldest month (about 7 °C by day and −2 °C at night) and July the warmest (about 30 °C). March is the wettest month and October the driest. Winter nights are below freezing from December to February, hardest during Chillai Kalan from 21 December. Gulmarg, in Baramulla district at about 2,650 m, is far colder and the snowiest resort in Kashmir.

Gulmarg

What Baramulla's weather means for Gulmarg

Gulmarg sits in Baramulla district at about 2,650 m — IMD averages there are about 0.7 °C by day and −7.7 °C at night in January, and February–March bring the most snow. Snow usually covers the meadow from late December to March; this season the upper slopes had their first snow on 13–15 October 2026.

After snowfall the SDM Gulmarg advisory allows only 4×4s or cars with anti-skid chains on the Tangmarg–Gulmarg road; chains are fitted at Tangmarg. Gondola phase 2 often stops for high wind or heavy snow — keep a spare half-day. Full guide: Gulmarg weather (Hindi).

On the way, the Baba Reshi shrine near Tangmarg is a short detour. Check the J&K Traffic Police advisory on the morning you travel.

FAQ

Baramulla weather: frequently asked questions

What is the weather in Baramulla today?

The top of this page shows the current temperature and the next days' forecast for Baramulla, Gulmarg and Srinagar (MET Norway data).

Does it snow in Baramulla?

Yes, in most winters between December and February; Gulmarg, in the same district, gets far more.

What is the coldest month in Baramulla?

January — about 7 °C by day and −2 °C at night (Srinagar's IMD averages).

Is Gulmarg in Baramulla district?

Yes. Gulmarg (about 2,650 m) is in Baramulla district, reached via Tangmarg.

When is Chillai Kalan?

The 40 coldest days of the Kashmir winter, from 21 December to about 30 January.

What is the road rule for Gulmarg after snow?

Only 4×4s or vehicles with anti-skid chains may use the Tangmarg–Gulmarg road after snowfall.
