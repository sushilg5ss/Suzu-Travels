Day by day

11-Day Char Dham Yatra Itinerary from Delhi

The classic road yatra in the traditional order. We add a buffer day for weather or rest whenever your dates allow.

DAY1

Arrive in Delhi

Pickup at Delhi airport and a quiet hotel to recover from the long flight. We brief the family on the yatra plan, registration and what to pack.

Stay Delhi
DAY2

Delhi → Haridwar (about 5 hrs)

Drive to Haridwar. Evening Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri, the traditional start of the yatra.

Stay Haridwar · Breakfast & dinner
DAY3

Haridwar → Barkot (about 7 hrs)

Scenic drive via Dehradun and Mussoorie to Barkot, the base for Yamunotri.

Stay Barkot · Breakfast & dinner
DAY4

Yamunotri Darshan

Drive to Janki Chatti, then the 6 km path to Yamunotri on foot, by pony or palki. Holy dip at Surya Kund and darshan of Maa Yamuna.

Stay Barkot · Breakfast & dinner
DAY5

Barkot → Uttarkashi

Drive along the Yamuna and Bhagirathi valleys. Evening at the Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Uttarkashi.

Stay Uttarkashi · Breakfast & dinner
DAY6

Gangotri Darshan

Drive to Gangotri, darshan of Maa Ganga and a holy dip in the Bhagirathi. Return to Uttarkashi.

Stay Uttarkashi · Breakfast & dinner
DAY7

Uttarkashi → Guptkashi / Sitapur

A long mountain drive via Tehri and Rudraprayag, the confluence of the Alaknanda and Mandakini.

Stay Guptkashi · Breakfast & dinner
DAY8

Kedarnath Darshan

By helicopter (booked on the official IRCTC HeliYatra portal) or the 16 km trek, pony or palki from Gaurikund. Darshan of Baba Kedarnath.

Stay Kedarnath or Guptkashi
DAY9

Guptkashi → Badrinath

Drive via Joshimath to Badrinath. Tapt Kund bath and evening aarti at Badrinath Temple.

Stay Badrinath · Breakfast & dinner
DAY10

Badrinath → Rishikesh

Morning darshan and Mana, India’s last village before Tibet. Drive down through Devprayag, where the Ganga is born.

Stay Rishikesh · Breakfast & dinner
DAY11

Rishikesh → Delhi airport

Laxman Jhula and the ghats, then the drive to Delhi for your onward flight.

Trek, Pony, Palki or Helicopter?

Option How it works Best for
Trek About 16 km uphill from Gaurikund, usually 6–8 hours, to about 3,580 m. Fit pilgrims who want the traditional yatra on foot.
Pony Ride most of the way from Gaurikund; book at the official counters. Most adults who find the full trek too long.
Palki (doli) Carried by a team of porters. Elders and pilgrims with limited mobility.
Helicopter shuttle A short flight from Phata, Sersi or Guptkashi. Tickets only on heliyatra.irctc.co.in. Short trips, seniors, and families with limited days.
Before you fly

Documents & Rules for NRI Pilgrims

What What you need to know
Visa Indian passport or OCI card: no visa needed. Other foreign passports: India e-Tourist Visa from indianvisaonline.gov.in, applied for at least 4 days before arrival.
Char Dham registration Mandatory for every pilgrim, on registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in. We do it for you with your passport details.
Kedarnath helicopter Official tickets only on heliyatra.irctc.co.in. Beware of fake websites and “guaranteed ticket” offers.
Health Kedarnath (about 3,580 m) and Yamunotri are high. Get a check-up before travel, especially for heart, lung or blood-pressure conditions, and carry your medicines.
Insurance Cover for high-altitude medical care and emergency evacuation, including helicopter evacuation.
Packing Warm layers (nights are cold even in June), rain jacket, good walking shoes, a torch and some cash — card payments and ATMs are limited in the mountains.

Char Dham Yatra for NRIs: Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Char Dham Yatra cost for NRIs in US dollars?

Our private Char Dham Yatra by road starts from about US$799 per person for 11 days from Delhi, based on two pilgrims sharing a room, with a private car and driver, hotels and breakfast and dinner. The shorter Do Dham (Kedarnath and Badrinath) starts from about US$549, and the Char Dham by helicopter from about US$2,650. You get an itemised quote in USD within 24 hours.

Do NRIs need a visa for the Char Dham Yatra?

If you travel on an Indian passport, no visa is needed. Foreign citizens with an OCI card also need no visa. Foreign citizens of Indian origin without an OCI card, and other foreign nationals, need an Indian e-Tourist Visa from the official site indianvisaonline.gov.in.

How do NRIs reach Char Dham from abroad?

Most NRIs can fly to Delhi and begin the journey from there. Suzu Travels can arrange Delhi airport pickup, accommodation, private transportation and the onward journey to Haridwar and the Char Dham circuit. Depending on the package, the yatra can also start from Dehradun or Haridwar.

Is registration mandatory for the Char Dham Yatra?

Yes. Every pilgrim must register with the Uttarakhand Tourism Department before the yatra, on the official portal registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in. Foreign passport holders register with their passport details. We complete the registration for our guests and carry the printouts.

When is the best time for NRIs to do the Char Dham Yatra?

The temples usually open in late April or May and close in October or November. May–June and September–October are the best windows. July and August bring monsoon landslides. The 2027 opening dates are announced by the temple committees in early 2027, and we plan your trip around them.

Is the Char Dham Yatra suitable for elderly parents?

Yes, with the right plan. Gangotri and Badrinath are reachable by road. For Yamunotri (about 6 km) and Kedarnath (about 16 km) we arrange pony, palki or the Kedarnath helicopter. We add rest days, carry oxygen and a first-aid kit in the vehicle and recommend a medical check-up before travel, as Kedarnath is at about 3,580 m.

How much does a Char Dham Yatra package cost for NRIs?

Char Dham Yatra packages for NRIs start from around US$799 per person for an 11-day private road yatra from Delhi. Shorter Do Dham packages start from US$549, while Char Dham helicopter packages start from around US$2,650 per person. The final price depends on travel dates, hotel category, number of travellers and selected services.

How do we book the Kedarnath helicopter?

Kedarnath helicopter shuttle tickets are sold only through the official IRCTC HeliYatra website (heliyatra.irctc.co.in), and seats go fast. We plan your dates around the booking windows and help you book. Please avoid websites or agents promising ‘guaranteed’ helicopter tickets.

Can you arrange puja and special darshan?

Yes. Where the temple committees offer official puja and darshan bookings, we help you book them in advance and plan your day so you reach the temple at the right time.

Which airport should we fly into from the USA, UK or Australia?

Fly into Delhi (DEL). It has direct flights from the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, the Gulf and South-East Asia. We pick you up at the airport. For the helicopter yatra, the starting point is Dehradun, a short domestic flight or a 5–6 hour drive from Delhi.

What travel insurance do we need?

Choose a policy that covers medical treatment at high altitude and emergency evacuation, including by helicopter. Check that it covers trekking above 3,500 m and your whole itinerary in India.
