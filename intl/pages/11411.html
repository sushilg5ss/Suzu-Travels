Visa guide 2026

Do You Need a Visa for India?

Most visitors need a visa, but for most nationalities it is a simple online e-Visa. Here is how it works. Rules can change, so always confirm on the official Government of India website before you travel.

No visa needed

🇳🇵 Nepal · 🇧🇹 Bhutan · OCI card holders

Citizens of Nepal and Bhutan can visit India without a visa. NRIs and people of Indian origin with an OCI card also need no visa: the OCI card is a lifelong, multiple-entry visa. Maldivian citizens have special arrangements; check with the Indian High Commission in Malé.

Visa on arrival

🇯🇵 Japan · 🇰🇷 South Korea · 🇦🇪 UAE (conditions apply)

Available at selected international airports for up to 60 days. Not available to travellers whose parents or grandparents were born in, or lived permanently in, Pakistan. We still recommend applying for the e-Visa in advance to save time at the airport.

e-Visa online

🇺🇸 USA · 🇬🇧 UK · 🇨🇦 Canada · 🇦🇺 Australia · 🇲🇾 Malaysia · 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka · 🇸🇬 Singapore · 🇪🇺 EU + 160 more

Around 170 nationalities can apply online for an e-Tourist Visa: 30 days, 1 year or 5 years. With the 1-year and 5-year visas, citizens of the USA, UK, Canada and Japan can stay up to 180 days per visit; most other nationalities up to 90 days.

e-Visa checklist Details
Where to apply Only on the official site: indianvisaonline.gov.in. Avoid look-alike agent websites that charge extra.
When to apply At least 4 days before arrival (we suggest 2–4 weeks ahead, especially in peak season).
Passport Valid for at least 6 months from arrival, with at least two blank pages.
You will need A recent photo, a scan of your passport photo page, and a card to pay the fee. The fee depends on your nationality and the visa length.
Entry points e-Visa holders must arrive through a designated airport or seaport, including Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad and Kochi. You can leave from any authorised exit point.
Special permits Some border areas need an extra Protected Area Permit for foreigners, for example parts of Ladakh, Spiti–Kinnaur in Himachal and upper Uttarakhand. If your route needs one, our team arranges it.

Need help? Send us your nationality and travel dates and we will tell you which visa suits your trip and share a day-by-day itinerary for your application. Suzu Travels is not a visa agent and does not apply on your behalf.

Plan with confidence

Before You Travel to India

Travel insurance: buy a policy that covers medical care and, for the mountains, high-altitude activities. Check that it covers every region on your itinerary.

Money: cards work in cities and hotels; carry some rupees for markets, tips and the mountains. ATMs are easy to find in Delhi, Agra and Jaipur.

Dress: cover shoulders and knees at temples, mosques and the Golden Temple. Carry a scarf; shoes come off at religious sites.

Health: drink bottled or filtered water and start gently with spicy food. Your driver knows clean places to stop for lunch.

Connectivity: an eSIM or local SIM keeps you on WhatsApp with your driver and our team throughout the trip.

When is the best time to visit North India?

October to March is best for the Golden Triangle (Delhi, Agra, Jaipur), with pleasant days and cool nights. The Himalayas (Himachal, Kashmir) are best from March to June and September to November, with snow from December to February. The Char Dham temples are open roughly from late April or May until October or November.

Do I need a visa to visit India?

Most visitors do. Citizens of Nepal and Bhutan and holders of an OCI card do not. Japan, South Korea and (with conditions) UAE nationals can get a visa on arrival. Around 170 nationalities, including the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Malaysia, Sri Lanka, Singapore and the EU, can apply online for an e-Tourist Visa at indianvisaonline.gov.in, at least 4 days before arrival.

I am an NRI or of Indian origin. Do I need a visa?

If you hold an Indian passport you need no visa. If you are a foreign citizen with an OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) card, you also need no visa, as the OCI card works as a lifelong multiple-entry visa. Foreign citizens of Indian origin without an OCI card need an e-Visa or regular visa. We host many NRI families, especially for Char Dham, Kashmir and Himachal trips.

Which countries have you hosted travellers from?

We have hosted NRIs and international travellers from the USA, the UK, Malaysia, Sri Lanka, the Maldives, Australia and other countries. As an Indian company with our own local teams, we manage every part of the trip on the ground ourselves.

Is India safe for foreign tourists?

Millions of international visitors travel in India every year. On a private tour you have your own vetted driver, pre-booked hotels and our team on call 24/7, which removes most of the stress. We also brief you on scams to avoid, dress codes and food and water safety before you arrive.

Is it safe to visit Kashmir?

Srinagar, Gulmarg and Pahalgam welcome large numbers of visitors and we run trips there with our own local ground team. Some governments, including the US and UK, advise against travel to parts of Jammu & Kashmir, and this can affect travel insurance. Please check your government’s current advisory and your insurer before booking. We will always tell you honestly about current conditions.

Is the Taj Mahal open every day?

The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays. We plan your itinerary around this and try to schedule a sunrise visit, when the light is softest and the crowds are smaller.

Can you customise the tour?

Yes. Our customized India tour packages are private and can be adapted to your preferred destinations, travel dates, sightseeing interests, hotels and trip duration. You can also add or remove destinations depending on your schedule and budget.

How many days are enough for an India tour?

For a first trip to North India, 6 to 8 days can cover popular destinations such as Delhi, Agra and Jaipur. A 10 to 14 day itinerary gives you more time to combine the Golden Triangle with destinations such as Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh or Rishikesh. The ideal duration depends on your destinations, interests and flight schedule.
