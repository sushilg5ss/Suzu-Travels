Day by day

6-Day Golden Triangle Itinerary from Delhi

A 6-day Golden Triangle tour is a comfortable option for first-time visitors, giving you enough time to explore Delhi, visit the Taj Mahal in Agra at sunrise and discover Jaipur without rushing between the three cities.

DAY1

Arrive in Delhi

Our driver meets you at Delhi airport (DEL) and takes you to your hotel. The rest of the day is free to recover from the flight.

Stay Delhi
DAY2

Old & New Delhi

Old Delhi by cycle rickshaw: Chandni Chowk and Jama Masjid. Then New Delhi: Humayun’s Tomb, Qutub Minar, India Gate and the government quarter, with a licensed guide.

Stay Delhi · Breakfast
DAY3

Delhi → Agra (about 3–4 hrs)

Drive by the Yamuna Expressway. Afternoon at Agra Fort, then Mehtab Bagh across the river to watch the sun set behind the Taj Mahal.

Stay Agra · Breakfast
DAY4

Taj Mahal at sunrise → Jaipur

Early visit to the Taj Mahal when the light is soft and the crowds are small. Back for breakfast, then drive to Jaipur, stopping at Fatehpur Sikri, the abandoned Mughal capital.

Stay Jaipur · Breakfast
DAY5

Jaipur, the Pink City

Amber Fort on the hill, a photo stop at Jal Mahal, then the City Palace, Jantar Mantar and Hawa Mahal. Evening free for the bazaars.

Stay Jaipur · Breakfast
DAY6

Jaipur → Delhi airport (about 5 hrs)

Drive back to Delhi for your flight home, or continue to Rishikesh, Kashmir or the Himalayas with us.

Visiting the Taj Mahal

Taj Mahal Tips for Foreign Visitors

What What you need to know
Closed day Closed to visitors every Friday. We never plan your Agra day on a Friday.
Opening hours About 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset. Sunrise gives the best light and the smallest crowds.
Tickets Foreign nationals pay INR 1,100 (at the time of writing; set by the Archaeological Survey of India). Lower prices for SAARC and BIMSTEC citizens. Book online to skip the ticket queue.
Photos Allowed in the gardens and outside; not allowed inside the main tomb.
What to carry Passport, a small bag, water and your phone. Large bags, food, tripods and drones are not allowed.
Train option The Gatimaan Express runs Delhi (Hazrat Nizamuddin) to Agra Cantt in about 1 hour 40 minutes, a good choice for a day trip.
When to go

Best Time for a Golden Triangle Tour

Months Weather Our advice
October – March Pleasant days, cool nights; foggy mornings in Dec–Jan. The best season. Book hotels early for Christmas and New Year.
April – June Very hot, often above 40°C. Sightseeing early morning and late afternoon; or add the cool Himalayas.
July – September Monsoon: warm, humid, some heavy showers. Greener and quieter, with good hotel deals.

FAQs

Golden Triangle Tour: Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Golden Triangle tour cost in US dollars?

Our private Golden Triangle tours start from about US$299 per person for 4 days and US$449 per person for the classic 6 days, based on two people sharing a room. That includes a private air-conditioned car and driver, hotels with breakfast, airport pickup and drop, and local guides at the main monuments. You get an exact quote in USD within 24 hours.

How many days do I need for the Golden Triangle?

Six days (5 nights) is ideal: one day each for Delhi and Jaipur, a night in Agra so you can see the Taj Mahal at sunrise, and time to rest after the long flight. Four days is possible if you are short on time, but the days are longer.

Is the Taj Mahal open every day?

No. The Taj Mahal is closed to visitors on Fridays. It opens about 30 minutes before sunrise and closes about 30 minutes after sunset. We plan your route so your Agra day never falls on a Friday.

What is the Taj Mahal ticket price for foreigners?

At the time of writing, the entry ticket for foreign nationals is INR 1,100, with lower prices for SAARC and BIMSTEC citizens. Prices are set by the Archaeological Survey of India and can change. Monument tickets are not included in our package price, and we can book them for you.

Should we travel between the cities by car or train?

A private car is the most flexible: Delhi to Agra is about 3–4 hours on the Yamuna Expressway, Agra to Jaipur about 4–5 hours with a stop at Fatehpur Sikri, and Jaipur to Delhi about 5 hours. For Delhi–Agra you can also take the Gatimaan Express, which takes about 1 hour 40 minutes from Hazrat Nizamuddin to Agra Cantt.

What is the best time for a Golden Triangle tour?

October to March is the best time, with pleasant days and cool nights. April to June is very hot, often above 40°C. July to September is the monsoon: greener and quieter, with some rain.

Do I need a visa for India?

Most visitors do. Around 170 nationalities, including the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Malaysia, Singapore and the EU, can apply online for an e-Tourist Visa at indianvisaonline.gov.in, at least 4 days before arrival. Holders of an OCI card, and citizens of Nepal and Bhutan, need no visa.

Can we add Rishikesh, Kashmir or the Himalayas?

Yes, and this is what we are known for. We are a North India DMC with our own teams in Himachal, Kashmir and Uttarakhand, so we can add Rishikesh, Kashmir, Shimla-Manali or the Char Dham Yatra to your Golden Triangle tour.

Is the Golden Triangle safe for first-time visitors and solo women?

Yes, with a private car, a driver who stays with you for the whole trip, licensed guides and a 24/7 WhatsApp line to our team. We also brief you on dress codes, scams to avoid and tipping.
