Why Sri Lanka works so well for Indian travellers

It is close, it is affordable, and it gives you four completely different holidays inside one small island. In a single week you can stand on a colonial-era rampart in Galle, ride the hill train through tea country to Ella, climb a fifth-century rock fortress at Sigiriya and watch elephants at a waterhole in Udawalawe. Flights from Chennai take about an hour and a half; Delhi and Mumbai are direct too. The food will feel familiar, rice and curry is the national staple, and vegetarian eating is genuinely easy rather than a negotiation.

The four Sri Lankas — and how to combine them

The Cultural Triangle

Sigiriya rock fortress, the cave temples at Dambulla and the ruined capitals of Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa. This is the historical heart of the island and needs two to three days. Sigiriya is a genuine climb of around 1,200 steps, best attempted early morning before the heat.

Hill country and tea

Kandy, Nuwara Eliya and Ella. Cool air, waterfalls, tea estates you can walk through, and the train journey between Kandy and Ella that is regularly called one of the most scenic rail routes anywhere. Two to three days, and the single part of the trip most people say they would extend.

Beaches and the south coast

Bentota, Mirissa, Unawatuna and the Dutch fort town of Galle. Whale watching operates out of Mirissa in season. Two or three days here at the end of a trip gives you the wind-down that a temple-and-fortress itinerary needs.

Wildlife parks

Yala for the highest leopard density in the world, Udawalawe for reliable elephant sightings, Wilpattu for a quieter safari. One full day plus an overnight nearby. Yala is busier and more of a lottery; Udawalawe is the safer bet if you want to be certain of seeing something.

When to go

Sri Lanka has two monsoons hitting opposite coasts at opposite times, which means somewhere on the island is always in season. This is the single most useful thing to understand when planning.

December–March · West and southPeak season for Colombo, Bentota, Galle and Mirissa. Dry, sunny and best for beaches and whale watching. Book early — this is when everyone goes.
May–September · East coastWhile the west is wet, Trincomalee, Nilaveli and Arugam Bay are at their best. Good for surfing and quieter beaches.
Year-round · Cultural TriangleSigiriya, Dambulla and the ancient cities work in any month. Hot and humid, so plan climbs for early morning.
Hill country · Cool alwaysKandy, Nuwara Eliya and Ella stay pleasant all year. Carry a light jacket — Nuwara Eliya genuinely gets cold at night.

Common questions about Sri Lanka

Do Indians need a visa for Sri Lanka?

You need an Electronic Travel Authorisation applied for online before you fly. Sri Lanka has periodically waived the fee for Indian nationals, and these schemes get extended and revised. We confirm the position for your travel dates and handle the application.

How many days do I need in Sri Lanka?

Five days covers either the Cultural Triangle and Kandy, or the hill country and the coast. Seven to eight lets you combine all three properly without spending your holiday in the car. Ten days if you want wildlife and the east coast as well.

Is Sri Lanka safe for families and solo women travellers?

It is generally regarded as one of the easier destinations in the region, and the chauffeur-guide model means you are rarely navigating alone. Normal precautions apply as anywhere. We brief you honestly on anything specific to your route.

Is the Kandy to Ella train worth it?

Yes, and it is worth booking a reserved seat rather than gambling on the day. It takes six to seven hours, which some find long. If your group is not keen, we can do a shorter Nanu Oya to Ella leg instead and drive the rest.

Will I see leopards in Yala?

Yala has the highest leopard density in the world but sightings are never guaranteed and the park gets busy. Morning drives give better odds. If seeing animals reliably matters more than seeing leopards specifically, Udawalawe and its elephants are the better choice.

Is vegetarian food easy to find?

Very. Rice and curry is typically served with several vegetable dishes, and South Indian food is widely available. Jain requirements need notice, which we give the hotels in advance.
