Common questions

Do you handle visas?

We guide you through the documentation and process for each destination and tell you honestly what the current requirements and timelines are. Visa approval is always the decision of the issuing country, and we never promise an outcome we do not control.

Are flights included?

Your choice. We can quote land-only (hotels, transfers, sightseeing) or a full package including flights from your nearest airport. Say which you prefer when you enquire.

Why are there no prices on this page?

Because an honest international quote depends on your dates, group size, hotel category and current airfare. A fixed number on a web page is usually a starting figure that changes once you ask. We would rather send you a real one.

Can you handle groups and corporate trips?

Yes. We regularly run group departures and MICE movements, and we work with travel agents on a B2B basis. Tell us the group size and we will quote accordingly.

How far in advance should I plan?

For Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia, four to six weeks is comfortable. For Europe, the UK, Japan, Australia and the USA, start at least two to three months ahead because visa appointments drive the timeline.
