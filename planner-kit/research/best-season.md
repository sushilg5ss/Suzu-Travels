# Best season per ready idea (v2.6, 21 Nov 2026)

Used by the "Best for <month>" chip and the "Best in <Mon>" card badge (`BEST` in src/planner.js).
Rule of thumb, conservative (a month is "best" only when roads are open, weather is comfortable or the season is the trip's highlight, e.g. snow):

- Himachal hill towns (Shimla, Manali, Dalhousie, Mussoorie, Patnitop): Oct–Jun; Dec–Feb = snow season. Jul–Aug monsoon landslides left out (HP Tourism, himachaltourism.gov.in "best time"; our own monsoon warning).
- Kinnaur / Grand Himachal / Tirthan: spring + Sep–Oct/Nov; winter snow on high roads (Chitkul, Jalori) left out.
- Spiti circuit Jun–Oct (Kunzum open); Winter Spiti Dec–Mar (via Kinnaur only) — research/winter-passes.md.
- Ladakh May/Jun–Sep (Manali–Leh & Zojila open; Leh flights all year but winter is −15 °C) — research/winter-passes.md.
- Char Dham / Do Dham: May–Jun and Sep–Oct (yatra open May–Nov; Jul–Aug monsoon landslides) — Uttarakhand Tourism / BKTC.
- Amarnath: Jul–Aug (SASB yatra window).
- Kashmir: Mar–Jun (tulips, meadows), Sep–Oct (autumn), Dec–Feb (Gulmarg snow) — J&K Tourism.
- Rajasthan, Agra, Golden Triangle, Kashi–Ayodhya: Oct–Mar (Apr–Jun 40 °C+, matches our heat warning) — Rajasthan Tourism / UP Tourism.
- Mata temples & Vaishno Devi: Mar–Jun, Sep–Nov (Navratri), Vaishno also Dec; Jul–Aug left out for monsoon on the track/hill roads.
- Corbett (Dhikala zone 15 Nov–15 Jun) — Corbett Tiger Reserve official site.
- Auli–Chopta: Dec–Mar snow/skiing, Apr–May rhododendrons, Oct–Nov clear views.
