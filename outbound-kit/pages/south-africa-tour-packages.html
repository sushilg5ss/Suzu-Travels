South Africa

Why South Africa is worth it for Indian travellers

South Africa packs two very different holidays into one trip. Cape Town sits between Table Mountain and the ocean, with mountain drives, beaches, the penguins of Boulders Beach and the Winelands an hour away. In the north-east, Kruger National Park and the private reserves beside it are home to lions, elephants, leopards, rhinos and buffalo, and the Panorama Route looks down into the Blyde River Canyon. The Garden Route along the south coast adds lagoons, forests and the cliffs of Tsitsikamma — and there is a large Indian community, so vegetarian food is easy to find.

Best season

When to go to South Africa

South Africa is in the southern hemisphere, so its summer is our winter. Cape Town has dry summers and wet winters, while the Kruger area gets its rain in summer and is dry in winter. This is general guidance; we check the season, lodge availability and flight prices against your plan before we quote.

Visa & entry

South Africa visa for Indian passport holders

What the official sources say

  • A visa is needed. On the Department of Home Affairs list of visa-exempt countries, India is exempt only for diplomatic, official and service passports — ordinary Indian passports need a visa. Home Affairs also says visas are not issued at South African ports of entry, so there is no visa on arrival.
  • The new ETA: South Africa’s Electronic Travel Authorisation (eta.dha.gov.za) was officially launched on 12 August 2026, starting with China, India, Indonesia and Mexico. You apply online from home; for visa-required travellers it issues a port of entry visa. Most applications get an outcome within 24 hours.
  • Validity and stay: the ETA is typically valid for up to six months with multiple entries, and each stay is limited to up to 90 days. The immigration officer decides your length of stay on arrival.
  • Airports: the ETA is currently used at OR Tambo (Johannesburg), Cape Town, King Shaka (Durban) and Lanseria airports. Fees are non-refundable, whatever the outcome.
  • Passport and documents: a machine-readable passport valid for at least seven months from your travel date. At the airport you may be asked for the ETA approval, a return ticket, proof of accommodation, proof of funds and a yellow fever certificate if you come from a yellow fever risk country.
  • Children and the VFS route: minors also need an ETA, but ETA applications for minors will open in a future phase. If the ETA is not approved, you can apply through a South African mission or the eVisa system; the South African High Commission in New Delhi takes visa applications through the VFS Global centres.

Checked 28 November 2026 · sources: Exempt countries — Home Affairs · Types of visas — Home Affairs · ETA FAQ — Home Affairs · ETA launch — SAnews · Visas — South African High Commission, New Delhi

Please note: visa approval, and entry at the border, are always the decision of the South African authorities — the official ETA FAQ says an approved ETA does not guarantee entry, and final admission is decided by the immigration officer. We help you prepare clear, consistent documents — itinerary, hotel and lodge bookings and return tickets — but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

The ETA is new and is still being rolled out, so rules, fees and airports can change. We re-check the official pages when you book. Please ask your doctor about vaccinations and malaria precautions for the Kruger area, and check the current health rules for your return to India, before you travel.

FAQ

Common questions about a South Africa holiday

Do Indians need a visa for South Africa?

Yes. On the Department of Home Affairs list of visa-exempt countries, India is exempt only for diplomatic, official and service passports — ordinary Indian passports need a visa for a holiday (checked 28 November 2026). Home Affairs also states that visas are not issued at South African ports of entry, so you must have your approval before you fly. The decision is always made by the South African authorities.

What is the South African ETA?

The Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) is the online system of the Department of Home Affairs at eta.dha.gov.za, officially launched on 12 August 2026. It started with travellers from China, India, Indonesia and Mexico. For Indian passport holders it issues a port of entry visa before you travel. The official FAQ says most applications get an outcome within 24 hours, the ETA is typically valid for up to six months with multiple entries, and each stay is limited to up to 90 days (checked 28 November 2026).

Which airports accept the South African ETA?

The official ETA FAQ lists OR Tambo International (Johannesburg), Cape Town International, King Shaka International (Durban) and Lanseria International, with more ports of entry to follow. We plan your flights so you land at one of the listed airports, and we re-check the list when you book.

Can children travel on the South African ETA?

The official ETA FAQ says minors also need an ETA, but applications for minors will be introduced in a future phase. For families with children we check the current route for each child when you book — the ETA FAQ names a South African mission or the eVisa system as the other channels, and the South African High Commission in New Delhi takes visa applications through the VFS Global application centres.

What documents are needed for South Africa?

The ETA FAQ asks for a machine-readable passport valid for at least seven months from your travel date, and lists what you may be asked to show at the airport: your passport, the ETA approval, a return or onward ticket, proof of accommodation, proof of sufficient funds and a yellow fever certificate if you travel from a yellow fever risk country. We give you a day-by-day itinerary and hotel confirmations so your papers are clear and consistent.

When is the best time to visit South Africa?

It depends on what you want to see. Cape Town and the Garden Route are at their warmest and driest from about November to March. The Kruger area is best for game drives in the dry winter, roughly May to September, when the bush is thinner. For one trip that combines Cape Town and a safari, the in-between months — about March to May and September to November — work well.

How many days are enough for South Africa?

Seven to ten nights covers Cape Town, the Cape Peninsula and a Kruger safari. Add three or four nights to drive the Garden Route between Cape Town and Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha), or a few days in the Winelands.

A South Africa trip cost depends on your dates, the season, the hotels and safari lodge, private or shared game drives, self-drive or chauffeur-driven travel, internal flights between Cape Town and the Kruger area, and the airfare from your city.
