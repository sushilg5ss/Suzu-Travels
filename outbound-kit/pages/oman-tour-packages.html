Oman

Why Oman is worth it for Indian travellers

Oman sits on the south-east corner of the Arabian Peninsula, close to India across the Arabian Sea. It is a calm, friendly country of old forts, rocky mountains, green wadis with natural pools, golden dunes and a long coast. Muscat, the capital, mixes the Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque and the Royal Opera House with the old harbour and souq of Muttrah. A week is enough to see the city, swim in a wadi, spend a night at a desert camp and visit Nizwa — and it pairs well with Dubai.

Visa & entry

Oman visa for Indian passport holders

What the official sources say

  • Apply online before you fly. Tourist visas for Oman are issued through the official eVisa portal of the Royal Oman Police. Its FAQ lists unsponsored and sponsored tourist visas and recommends applying at least four days before travel; the e-visa is sent by email.
  • Visa types depend on nationality: the visa types page lists unsponsored tourist visit visas of 10 days and 30 days for eligible nationalities, and 10- or 30-day types for travellers who hold a valid Schengen visa or a valid US, UK, Canada, Australia or Japan visa or residency. The portal shows which types you can apply for when you apply — we check this with you before booking.
  • Fees on the portal: OMR 5 for the 10-day and OMR 20 for the 30-day unsponsored tourist visa. Paying the fee does not guarantee that a visa will be issued.
  • Documents for the 30-day tourist visa: a digital photograph and a copy of your passport, valid for at least six months on entry; a valid hotel booking in Oman and a return ticket. You apply from outside Oman, and the visa is for a single entry.
  • Stay: keep to the length of stay on your visa — the portal lists a fine of OMR 10 for each day of overstay.

Checked 24 November 2026 · sources: eVisa — Royal Oman Police · Types of visa — Royal Oman Police eVisa · eVisa FAQ — Royal Oman Police

Please note: visa approval, and entry at the border, are always the decision of the Omani authorities — the portal itself says a valid visa does not guarantee entry, which is at the discretion of the Royal Oman Police officer. We help you prepare clear, consistent documents — itinerary, hotel bookings and return tickets — but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

Visa rules and fees can change. We re-check the official portal when you book and tell you which visa type fits your passport and plan.

FAQ

Common questions about an Oman tour

Do Indians need a visa for Oman?

Yes, plan on a visa before you travel. Tourist visas for Oman are issued online on the official eVisa portal of the Royal Oman Police. The portal lists unsponsored tourist visit visas of 10 days and 30 days for eligible nationalities, extra types for travellers who hold a valid Schengen visa or a valid US, UK, Canada, Australia or Japan visa or residency, and sponsored tourist visas. Which types you can apply for depends on your nationality and documents and is shown on the portal when you apply (checked 24 November 2026). Approval is always the decision of the Omani authorities.

How much is the Oman tourist visa fee?

On the Royal Oman Police eVisa portal, the 10-day unsponsored tourist visit visa is listed at OMR 5 and the 30-day one at OMR 20 (checked 24 November 2026). Fees can change, and the portal shows the amount for your visa type when you apply. Paying the fee does not guarantee that a visa will be issued.

What documents do I need for an Oman tourist e-visa?

For the 30-day unsponsored tourist visa the portal asks for a digital photograph and a copy of your passport, which must be valid for at least six months on entry, plus a valid hotel booking in Oman and a return ticket. You must apply from outside Oman, and the visa is for a single entry. We give you a clear itinerary and hotel confirmations to upload.

How early should I apply for the Oman visa?

The Royal Oman Police eVisa FAQ recommends applying at least four days before travel, as processing times vary. The e-visa is sent to the email address you registered with. We suggest applying once flights and hotels are confirmed, and well before your departure date.

Which is the best time to visit Oman?

October to March is the most comfortable time for Muscat, the mountains, the wadis and a desert night, with warm days and cooler evenings. April, May and September are hot, and June to August is very hot in Muscat and the interior. Salalah in the south is different: its khareef season, roughly July to September, brings mist, rain and green hills.

How many days are enough for Oman?

Five to seven nights covers Muscat, the coast road with Bimmah Sinkhole and Wadi Shab, a desert camp in the Wahiba Sands and Nizwa with the mountains. Add two or three nights for the fjords of Musandam or for Salalah, or combine Oman with a few days in Dubai.

Is Oman good for families and vegetarian travellers?

Yes. Oman suits families with its easy road trips, beaches, wadis to splash in and short desert camp stays. Muscat has many Indian restaurants and vegetarian food is easy to find; we note vegetarian and Jain meals with hotels and camps in advance. Muscat also has old Hindu temples, such as the Shiva temple in Old Muscat.

An Oman trip cost depends on your dates, hotels and desert camp, the car and driver you choose, add-ons such as Musandam or Salalah and the airfare from your city. Instead of a teaser price that changes when you ask, we send a costed, day-by-day quote for your dates — usually within 24 hours.
