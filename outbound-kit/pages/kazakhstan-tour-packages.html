Why Almaty and Kazakhstan work so well for Indian travellers

Almaty sits right below the snowy Tian Shan mountains: in under an hour you are at the Medeu skating rink, the Shymbulak ski resort or Big Almaty Lake, and day trips reach Charyn Canyon and the Kolsay and Kaindy lakes. There is no visa for short trips on an Indian passport, the city is modern and easy to get around, and Kyrgyzstan’s lakes and valleys are a short drive away.

Visa & entry

Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan visa for Indian passport holders

Kazakhstan — what the official rules say

  • Under Government Decree No. 464 of 7 July 2022, Indian citizens can enter Kazakhstan without a visa.
  • You can stay up to 14 days continuously, and no more than 42 days within every 180 days.
  • The visa-free regime applies to business, private and tourist trips.

Checked 31 October 2026 · source: Embassy of the Republic of Kazakhstan in India

Kyrgyzstan add-on — what the official rules say

  • Indian ordinary passport holders need a visa for Kyrgyzstan; it is applied for online on the official e-Visa portal.
  • A tourist e-visa is valid for up to 30 days, and the application review takes about 5 working days. Each traveller, children included, needs a separate e-visa; the fee is non-refundable.
  • Your passport should be valid for at least 6 months after the visa ends, with at least 2 blank pages. The portal advises not buying air tickets before the approval notice.
  • Kyrgyzstan adopted a new visa procedure (Cabinet Decree No. 312 of 30 April 2026), and overstays need a paid exit visa — we re-check the rules, including registration of stay, for your dates.

Checked 30–31 October 2026 · sources: Kyrgyz Republic e-Visa portal · Embassy of India, Bishkek — advisory on new visa regulations

Please note: admission at the border is always the decision of the Kazakh authorities, and whether a visa is issued is always the decision of the authorities of the Kyrgyz Republic. We help you prepare a complete, honest application, but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

FAQ

Common questions about a Kazakhstan tour

Do Indians need a visa for Kazakhstan?

No visa is needed for a short holiday. The Embassy of Kazakhstan in India says that under Government Decree No. 464 of 7 July 2022, Indian citizens can stay in Kazakhstan without a visa for up to 14 days at a time, and for a maximum of 42 days within every 180 days, for business, private and tourist trips. Entry is always the decision of the Kazakh border authorities. We checked this on 31 October 2026 and re-check it before every booking.

Do Indians need a visa for Kyrgyzstan?

Yes. The official Kyrgyz e-Visa portal (evisa.e-gov.kg) shows that Indian ordinary passport holders need a visa. A tourist e-visa is valid for up to 30 days, review takes about 5 working days, each traveller (children included) needs a separate e-visa, and the portal advises not buying air tickets before the approval notice. Your passport should be valid for at least 6 months after the visa ends, with at least 2 blank pages. Kyrgyzstan changed its visa procedure in 2026 (Cabinet Decree No. 312 of 30 April 2026), so we re-check the rules for your dates. Issuing a visa is always the decision of the Kyrgyz authorities.

How many days are enough for Almaty and Kazakhstan?

Five to six nights covers Almaty city, Medeu and Shymbulak, Big Almaty Lake, and a two-day trip to Charyn Canyon, Kolsay and Kaindy lakes. With three or four nights, keep to Almaty and the mountains just above the city. Add three or four nights for Bishkek, Ala-Archa and Lake Issyk-Kul in Kyrgyzstan.

Which is the best time to visit Almaty from India?

May to September is best for the lakes, canyons and mountain walks, when the roads to Kolsay and Kaindy are open and the hills are green. December to March is snow season, when Shymbulak is a ski resort and Medeu is used for skating. April and October are shoulder months with changeable mountain weather.

Can we combine Kazakhstan with Kyrgyzstan?

Yes. Almaty and Bishkek are close, and a road transfer between them is a common way to combine the two. In Kyrgyzstan we usually add Ala-Archa National Park, the Burana Tower and a stay on Lake Issyk-Kul. Remember that Kyrgyzstan needs a separate e-visa for Indian passport holders, so we plan the order of the trip around it.

Is vegetarian and Indian food available in Almaty?

Kazakh food is meat-heavy, but Almaty is a large city with Indian restaurants, cafes, salads, bread, fruit and vegetable dishes. On mountain and lake days, choices are smaller, so we note vegetarian and Jain needs with hotels and drivers in advance and suggest where to eat.
