Why Georgia works so well for Indian travellers

Old Tbilisi

Visa & entry

Visa and entry for Indian passport holders

What the official rules say

  • Indian ordinary passport holders need a visa to enter Georgia. (Only Indian diplomatic and official/service passport holders are exempt.)
  • e-Visa: India is on the list of countries whose citizens can apply on the official Georgian e-Visa portal, evisa.gov.ge. Tourists are asked for a photo, a copy of the passport, proof of accommodation, a return ticket, travel insurance and proof of funds.
  • Visa-free with another visa or residence permit: if you hold a valid visa or residence permit of a country on Georgia's official list — including the USA, the UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, EU member states, Switzerland, Norway, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait and Bahrain — you may enter and stay without a Georgian visa for 90 days in any 180-day period. The visa or permit must be valid on the day you enter Georgia.
  • Georgia's National Tourism Administration says a decision on a visa is made within 5 to 30 calendar days depending on the category — so apply well before your travel date.

Checked 20 October 2026 · sources: Georgia e-Visa portal — eligible countries · Government of Georgia Ordinance No 256 (list of countries whose visa/residence permit allows visa-free entry), Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Georgia · Georgian National Tourism Administration — visa information

Please note: visa approval and entry at the border are always the decision of the Georgian authorities. We guide you through the documents and timelines honestly, but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

Apply only on the official portal, and keep printed copies of your hotel bookings, return ticket and insurance with you when you travel.

FAQ

Common questions about Georgia

Do Indians need a visa for Georgia?

Yes, Indian ordinary passport holders need a visa for Georgia. India is on the list of countries that can apply for a Georgian e-Visa on the official portal evisa.gov.ge. If you hold a valid visa or residence permit of a country on Georgia's official list — such as the USA, the UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, EU member states, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait or Bahrain — you may enter without a Georgian visa for up to 90 days in any 180-day period, as long as that visa or permit is valid on the day you enter. Entry is always the decision of the Georgian authorities.

How many days are enough for Georgia?

Five to six nights covers Tbilisi, the mountain drive to Kazbegi (with Mtskheta, Ananuri and Gudauri on the way) and a day in the Kakheti wine region. With seven or eight nights you can add Batumi on the Black Sea coast or slow the pace down.

When is the best time to visit Georgia from India?

May to June and September to October suit most Indian travellers: pleasant days in Tbilisi and green, open mountain roads. July and August are warm in Tbilisi and pleasant in the mountains. December to March brings snow — Gudauri is a ski resort — but mountain roads can close for a while after heavy snowfall.

Can we see snow in Georgia?

Yes. In winter the Gudauri and Kazbegi area is usually snow-covered, and Gudauri has ski slopes and chairlifts. The high mountain road can close temporarily after heavy snow, so we keep a flexible day in winter plans and check road conditions before the drive.

Is vegetarian food easy to find in Georgia?

Yes. Georgian food has many vegetarian dishes — cheese-filled khachapuri breads, bean stew (lobio), vegetable and walnut pkhali, aubergine rolls and fresh salads — and Tbilisi has Indian restaurants. Jain meals need advance notice, which we arrange with hotels where possible.
