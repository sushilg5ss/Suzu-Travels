Kenya

Why Kenya is worth it for Indian travellers

Kenya, on the east coast of Africa across the Indian Ocean from India, is one of the easiest places in the world to go on safari. The Masai Mara has big cats and huge herds of wildebeest and zebra, Amboseli has elephants with Mount Kilimanjaro behind them, and the Rift Valley lakes of Naivasha and Nakuru add boat rides, birds and rhinos. Nairobi has its own national park on the edge of the city, and the coast at Diani has white beaches for a few slow days after the safari.

The classic road safari with a driver-guide. We rebuild it around you — fly into the Mara to save time, add more nights in the migration season, or finish on the beach at Diani.

Day 1Arrive in Nairobi

Airport pick-up and transfer to your hotel. If time allows, visit the Giraffe Centre or Nairobi National Park.

Day 2Into the Masai Mara

Drive to the Masai Mara (or take a short flight), check in at your lodge or tented camp and head out on an afternoon game drive.

Game drive in Lake Nakuru National Park, known for its rhinos and birdlife, then overnight at Nakuru or Naivasha.

Early game drive, when the mountain is often clearest, to see elephant herds, then free time at the lodge and another drive.

Day 8Fly home

Drive back to Nairobi for your flight home — or carry on to the beach at Diani for three or four more nights.

Visa & entry

Kenya eTA for Indian passport holders

What the official sources say

  • eTA before you travel. The official Kenya eTA portal of the Directorate of Immigration Services says all visitors, including infants and children, must have an approved Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA) before the start of their journey.
  • Indian passports: on the exemption list, India appears only for diplomatic, official and service passport holders. Travellers on an ordinary Indian passport apply for an eTA.
  • Fees on the portal: the standard eTA, for one entry, is listed at USD 30; the expedited service costs an additional USD 100. Each traveller needs their own eTA, even in a group.
  • Documents: a passport valid for at least six months with at least one blank page, a selfie or passport-type photo, email and phone number, a travel itinerary with arrival and departure, an accommodation booking confirmation and a card to pay.
  • Timing: usually about three working days, but it can take longer; the portal recommends applying at least two weeks before travel. Apply only on etakenya.go.ke or the official Kenya Travel Authorisation app (eTA FAQ).

Checked 25 November 2026 · sources: How to apply — Kenya eTA · General information — Kenya eTA · eTA types and fees — Kenya eTA · FAQs — Kenya eTA

Please note: eTA approval, and entry at the border, are always the decision of the Kenyan authorities — the portal itself says an eTA is not the final authority to enter and admissibility is decided at the point of entry. We help you prepare clear, consistent documents — itinerary, lodge bookings and return tickets — but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

Rules and fees can change. We re-check the official portal when you book. Please also ask your doctor about vaccinations and malaria precautions, and check the current health rules for your return to India, before you travel.

FAQ

Common questions about a Kenya safari

Do Indians need a visa for Kenya?

Kenya has replaced the visa with an Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA) for most visitors. The official eTA portal says every visitor, including infants and children, must have an approved eTA before starting the journey. For India, only diplomatic, official and service passport holders are on the exemption list, so travellers on an ordinary Indian passport need an eTA (checked 25 November 2026). The eTA is not the final authority to enter — admission is decided at the point of entry by the Kenyan authorities.

How much does the Kenya eTA cost?

On the official portal etakenya.go.ke, the standard eTA, which allows one entry into Kenya, is listed at USD 30, and the expedited service costs an additional USD 100 (checked 25 November 2026). Fees can change, and the portal shows the amount for your eTA type when you apply. Each traveller needs their own eTA, even in a group.

What documents do I need for the Kenya eTA?

The official portal asks for a passport valid for at least six months with at least one blank page, a selfie or passport-type photo, your email and phone number, a travel itinerary with arrival and departure, an accommodation booking confirmation and a card to pay. We give you a clear day-by-day itinerary and lodge confirmations to upload.

How early should I apply for the Kenya eTA?

The official portal says applications are usually processed in about three working days, but can take longer, and recommends applying at least two weeks before travel. Apply only on etakenya.go.ke or the official Kenya Travel Authorisation app. The approved eTA arrives by email; carry it with you for departure and arrival.

When is the best time for a Masai Mara safari?

The drier months from late June to October are the classic safari season in the Masai Mara, and the great wildebeest migration is usually in the Mara from about July to October — the herds follow the rains, so the timing changes a little each year. January and February are also dry and good for game drives. The long rains, roughly March to May, and the short rains around November make some roads muddy, but the parks are greener and quieter.

How many days are enough for a Kenya safari?

Six to eight nights covers Nairobi, two or three nights in the Masai Mara, a night at Lake Naivasha or Lake Nakuru and two nights in Amboseli with views of Mount Kilimanjaro. Add three or four nights on the Indian Ocean coast at Diani for a safari-and-beach holiday.

Is Kenya good for families and vegetarian travellers?

Yes. Safari lodges and tented camps welcome families, many have pools, and game drives can be kept shorter for young children and seniors. Nairobi has many Indian restaurants, and lodges and camps can prepare vegetarian and Jain meals when we tell them in advance — we note your needs at every stay.

Why are there no prices on this page?

A Kenya safari cost depends on your dates, the season, the lodges or camps you choose, park fees, the vehicle, whether you drive or fly into the Masai Mara, and the airfare from your city. Instead of a teaser price that changes when you ask, we send a costed, day-by-day quote for your dates — usually within 24 hours.
