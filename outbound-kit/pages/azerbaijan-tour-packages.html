Azerbaijan · Land of Fire

Why Azerbaijan works so well for Indian travellers

Azerbaijan packs a modern capital and old mountain country into a short trip. Baku has a walled Old City with the Maiden Tower and the Palace of the Shirvanshahs, a long seafront Boulevard on the Caspian Sea, the Flame Towers and the Heydar Aliyev Center. A day on the Absheron peninsula takes in the Gobustan rock art and the Ateshgah fire temple, whose old inscriptions include Sanskrit and Punjabi. A few hours' drive away, Gabala and Qusar offer green Caucasus valleys in summer and snow and cable cars in winter.

Where to stay — it shapes your whole trip

Old City & Fountain Square, Baku

Stay inside or next to the walled Old City — the Maiden Tower, cafés and the Boulevard are all on foot. The easiest base for a first visit.

Seafront & Boulevard, Baku

Larger city hotels along the Caspian, with sea views and easy taxis. Good for families who want space and a pool.

Gabala

Mountain resorts below the Greater Caucasus, with the Tufandag cable car and Nohur lake nearby. Green in summer, snowy in winter.

Shahdag (Qusar) or Sheki

Shahdag is a mountain resort with ski slopes in winter; Sheki is an old silk-road town known for the Khan's Palace, for a longer trip.

Visa & entry

Visa and entry for Indian passport holders

What the official rules say

  • Indian passport holders need a visa for Azerbaijan. India is on the list of countries whose citizens can apply for the electronic visa (ASAN Visa) on the official portal evisa.gov.az.
  • The e-visa is single entry and valid for 90 days, with permission to stay up to 30 days.
  • The standard e-visa is issued within 3 working days and the urgent e-visa within 3 hours, so apply at least that long before you arrive — we suggest well before your flight.
  • Your passport must be valid for at least 3 months beyond the e-visa's expiry date. The e-visa is not stamped in the passport — print it and carry it with your passport.
  • India is not on the official visa-upon-arrival list, so do not plan to get a visa at the airport. Payments are not refunded if an e-visa is refused.

Checked 21 October 2026 · sources: ASAN Visa portal (evisa.gov.az) — conditions for issuing electronic visas · Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Azerbaijan, Embassy page — ASAN Visa (e-Visa) and eligible countries · Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Azerbaijan, Embassy page — visa upon arrival

Please note: visa approval and entry at the border are always the decision of the Azerbaijani authorities. We guide you through the documents and timelines honestly, but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

Apply only on the official portal evisa.gov.az — not on look-alike sites — and keep printed copies of your e-visa, hotel bookings and return ticket with you.

FAQ

Common questions about Azerbaijan

Do Indians need a visa for Azerbaijan?

Yes. Indian citizens need a visa, and India is on the list of countries whose citizens can apply for Azerbaijan's electronic visa (ASAN Visa) on the official portal evisa.gov.az. The e-visa is single entry, valid for 90 days, and allows a stay of up to 30 days. The standard e-visa is issued within 3 working days and an urgent one within 3 hours. India is not on the official visa-upon-arrival list, so apply online before you fly. Approval is always the decision of the Azerbaijani authorities.

How many days are enough for Azerbaijan?

Four to five nights covers Baku — the Old City, the Flame Towers, the seafront Boulevard and a day trip on the Absheron peninsula — plus one or two nights in the mountains at Gabala or Shahdag. With six to eight nights you can add Sheki or combine Azerbaijan with Georgia.

When is the best time to visit Azerbaijan from India?

April to June and September to October suit most Indian travellers: comfortable weather in Baku and green mountain valleys. July and August are hot in Baku but pleasant in Gabala and Qusar. December to March is the snow season in the mountains, when Shahdag and Tufandag operate as ski resorts.

Can we see snow in Azerbaijan?

Yes. In winter the Greater Caucasus around Gabala and Qusar is usually snow-covered, and the Shahdag Mountain Resort (Qusar) and the Tufandag resort (Gabala) have ski slopes and cable cars. Snow depends on the year, so we check conditions against your dates before we confirm a snow day.

Is vegetarian food easy to find in Azerbaijan?

Azerbaijani food leans towards meat, but vegetarian options are easy to arrange: herb- and pumpkin-filled qutab flatbreads, vegetable dolma, rice dishes, fresh salads, bread, cheese and fruit. Baku also has Indian restaurants. Jain meals need advance notice, which we arrange with hotels where possible.

Can we combine Azerbaijan with Georgia?

Yes. Baku and Tbilisi are well connected, and Azerbaijan plus Georgia is a popular 8 to 10-night trip. Each country has its own visa rules for Indian passport holders, so we check both and plan the documents in the right order.
