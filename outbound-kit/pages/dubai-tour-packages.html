Dubai & UAE

Why Dubai works so well for Indian travellers

Dubai packs a lot into a short trip: record-breaking skyscrapers and malls, real desert a short drive from the city, beaches, theme parks and Abu Dhabi's landmarks within easy reach. For Indian families it is also one of the most comfortable places to travel abroad — Hindi and English are widely understood, Indian vegetarian food is everywhere, and the flight is shorter than many domestic connections.

Where to stay — it shapes your whole trip

Downtown & Business Bay

Next to the Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall. Central for sightseeing and the fountain show; the most convenient base for a first visit.

Dubai Marina & JBR

Waterfront towers, the beach walk and dhow cruises at the door. Livelier in the evenings and good for couples and friends.

Bur Dubai & Deira

Old Dubai by the Creek — souks, abra boats and many Indian vegetarian restaurants. Better value, a little further from the new landmarks.

Palm Jumeirah

Resort hotels with private beaches and pools. Best for a relaxed honeymoon or a family who wants a resort day or two.

Visa & entry

Visa and entry for Indian passport holders

What applies to most Indian travellers

  • Most Indian passport holders need a UAE visit visa issued before they fly. We tell you the documents needed and guide the application through an authorised channel.
  • Visa on arrival — only for some Indian citizens. GDRFA Dubai lists a 14-day visa on arrival for Indian citizens with an ordinary passport who hold a US permanent resident card (green card) or a UK or EU residence visa, valid for at least 6 months. It can be extended once for a similar period.
  • Your passport should be valid for at least 6 months.
  • The UAE has widened visa-on-arrival eligibility in recent years, so we check your exact case against the current official rules before you book.

Checked 18 October 2026 · source: GDRFA Dubai — Visa on arrival for citizens of the Republic of India

Please note: visa approval is always the decision of the UAE authorities. We guide you through the documentation and timelines honestly, but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

Apply with enough lead time — allow extra time around school holidays and festivals.

FAQ

Common questions about Dubai

Do Indians need a visa for Dubai?

Most Indian passport holders need a UAE visit visa issued before they fly, and we guide you through the documents and the application. Indian citizens who hold certain US, UK or EU visas or residence permits may qualify for a visa on arrival instead — we check your exact case against the official UAE rules before you book. Approval is always the decision of the UAE authorities.

How many days are enough for Dubai?

Four nights covers Dubai's highlights at a comfortable pace. Five nights lets you add a full day in Abu Dhabi for the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and Yas Island, which we recommend for a first visit.

What is the best time to visit Dubai from India?

November to March, when days are warm rather than hot and evenings are pleasant outdoors. June to September is very hot, so plans shift to indoor attractions, mornings and evenings — hotel rates are usually lower then.

Is Dubai good for families with kids and senior citizens?

Yes. Distances are short, roads and hotels are excellent, and Indian vegetarian food is easy to find. We pace the days with rest in the afternoon heat and use private transfers so nobody waits around.

Is vegetarian and Jain food easy in Dubai?

Very. Dubai has a large Indian community and many pure-vegetarian restaurants, especially around Bur Dubai and Karama. Jain meals need advance notice, which we arrange with hotels and the desert camp.

Why are there no prices on this page?

Dubai costs move a lot with dates, school holidays, events and airfare. Instead of a teaser price that changes when you ask, we send a costed, day-by-day quote for your dates — usually within 24 hours.
