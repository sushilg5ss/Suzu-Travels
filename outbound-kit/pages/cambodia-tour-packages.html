Cambodia

Why Cambodia is worth it for Indian travellers

The temples of Angkor are among the great sights of Asia — Angkor Wat, the faces of the Bayon, Ta Prohm in the forest and the fine carvings of Banteay Srei, all within reach of Siem Reap. Phnom Penh adds the Royal Palace, the Silver Pagoda and riverside evenings, and the south coast has Kampot’s river and the islands of Koh Rong. It is a compact country that pairs naturally with Vietnam or Thailand, and Indian travellers often feel at home among its Hindu and Buddhist temples.

When to go to Cambodia

Cambodia is warm all year, with a dry season and a rainy season. November to March suits most travellers; the green season has its own charm. We check the season, holidays and flight prices against your plan before we quote.

Visa & entry

Cambodia visa for Indian passport holders

What the official sources say

  • A visa is needed. India is not on the visa-exemption list of Cambodia’s official e-Visa portal (that list covers nine ASEAN countries), so Indian travellers need a visa.
  • Official e-Visa: apply online on the Cambodia e-Visa portal (evisa.gov.kh) of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation — fill in the form, upload a recent digital photo and pay by Visa or MasterCard; the approved e-Visa is emailed to you or downloaded from the portal.
  • Tourist e-Visa (Visa T): the portal’s visa information lists it as single entry, valid 3 months from the date of issue, for a stay of up to 1 month; fee USD 30; processing 3 business days (the portal says processing times are estimates); passport valid for more than six months at entry.
  • Where you can enter on an e-Visa: the airports at Techo International (Phnom Penh), Siem Reap Angkor International and Sihanoukville, and the land crossings at Bavet and Trapeang Kreal. The portal says Cham Yeam and Poipet are temporarily closed for e-Visa entry — we plan Thailand combinations around this.
  • Cambodia e-Arrival: every traveller, including visa holders, completes the free online e-Arrival form (arrival.gov.kh), ideally within 7 days before arrival. After arrival you get a v-Pass by email instead of an ink stamp.

Checked 19 November 2026 · sources: Cambodia e-Visa — Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation · e-Visa visa type information · Cambodia e-Arrival (official)

Please note: whether a visa is issued is always the decision of the Cambodian authorities — the portal itself says that submitting an application is not an approval — and admission is decided by Cambodian immigration on arrival. We help you prepare a clear, consistent application, but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

Fees, rules and open checkpoints can change. We re-check the official portal when you book and remind you about the e-Visa and e-Arrival before you fly. Use only the official government sites, not look-alike agencies.

FAQ

Common questions about a Cambodia tour

Do Indians need a visa for Cambodia?

Yes. When we checked the official Cambodia e-Visa portal of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation on 19 November 2026, India was not on its visa-exemption list (that list covers Laos, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia, Brunei and Myanmar). Indian travellers therefore need a visa. Whether a visa is issued is always the decision of the Cambodian authorities.

How does the Cambodia e-Visa work?

The official portal is evisa.gov.kh. You fill in the online form, upload a recent digital photo and pay by Visa or MasterCard; the approved e-Visa is emailed to you or can be downloaded from the portal. On 19 November 2026 the portal listed the tourist e-Visa (Visa T) as single entry, valid for 3 months from the date of issue for a stay of up to 1 month, with a passport valid for more than six months at entry and a processing time of 3 business days. The portal states that processing times are estimates.

What is the Cambodia e-Visa fee?

On 19 November 2026 the official portal listed USD 30 for the tourist e-Visa (Visa T), a fee in force since 1 January 2025. Fees are set by the Cambodian government and can change, so we re-check the portal when you book. Use only the official portal evisa.gov.kh and ignore look-alike websites that charge extra.

Can I enter Cambodia by land from Thailand or Vietnam on an e-Visa?

Only at the checkpoints the portal lists for e-Visa entry. On 19 November 2026 these were the airports at Techo International (Phnom Penh), Siem Reap Angkor International and Sihanoukville, and the land crossings at Bavet (from Vietnam) and Trapeang Kreal (from Laos). The portal also said Cham Yeam and Poipet (the main crossings from Thailand) were temporarily closed for e-Visa entry. We plan your route around the open checkpoints.

What is the Cambodia e-Arrival?

It is Cambodia's online arrival form at arrival.gov.kh. The official e-Visa portal says every traveller must complete it, including visa holders, and recommends submitting it within 7 days before arrival; the e-Arrival site says submission is free. After arrival you receive a v-Pass by email instead of an ink stamp.

How many days are enough for a Cambodia trip?

Five to six nights is a comfortable first trip: three nights in Siem Reap for Angkor Wat, the Bayon, Ta Prohm and Banteay Srei, and two nights in Phnom Penh. With nine to eleven nights you can combine Cambodia with Vietnam, or add the south coast at Kampot or the islands of Koh Rong.

Which is the best time to visit Cambodia?

The cooler, dry months from about November to March are the most comfortable for temple visits. April and May are the hottest. From about June to October the rainy season brings short heavy showers, green landscapes and fewer visitors, and the Tonle Sap lake is at its fullest.
