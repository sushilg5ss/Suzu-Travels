Visa & entry

Russia e-visa for Indian passport holders

What the official rules say

  • A visa is needed. India is on the official list of countries whose nationals can apply for the Russian e-visa — for tourism, guest and business visits and events. No invitation is needed; you fill in the application form online and pay the fee.
  • Apply only on the official site of the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs: evisa.kdmid.ru. The Ministry says the e-visa is issued within 4 calendar days, not counting the day you submit; if the application is returned for corrections, the time starts again from resubmission. The fee is not refundable and is paid by a bank card issued outside Russia.
  • Validity and stay: a single-entry e-visa is valid for 120 days from issue, with a stay of up to 30 days from entry; you must also leave within the 120 days. Each child needs a separate e-visa application.
  • Approved checkpoints only: enter and leave through the border points on the official list — it includes the Moscow airports (Sheremetyevo, Domodedovo, Vnukovo, Zhukovsky), Saint Petersburg Pulkovo and Kazan. Show the e-visa notification to the airline and at the border.
  • Registration: foreign visitors must be registered at their place of stay within 7 working days; when you stay in a hotel, the hotel does this for you.

Checked 15 November 2026 · sources: MFA of Russia — e-visa portal · e-visa FAQ · list of eligible countries · list of approved checkpoints

Please note: whether a visa is issued, and admission at the border, is always the decision of the Russian authorities. We help you prepare a correct application, but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

We help you fill in the form with the hotel details from your itinerary, check your flights against the approved checkpoints, and confirm non-refundable bookings only once your e-visa is issued. If you need a stay or purpose the e-visa does not cover, we guide you to a regular visa through a Russian embassy or consulate.

FAQ

Common questions about a Russia tour

Do Indians need a visa for Russia?

Yes. When we checked the official e-visa portal of the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs on 15 November 2026, India was on the list of countries whose nationals can apply for the Russian e-visa, and a visa was still needed for a holiday. The e-visa is applied for online, with no invitation needed, and the Ministry says it is issued within 4 calendar days (not counting the day you submit). Reports about a possible visa-free arrangement for Indian tour groups are not a rule until Russia officially brings one into force — we will update this page if that happens. Whether a visa is issued and whether you are admitted is always the decision of the Russian authorities.

How long can I stay in Russia on an e-visa?

According to the official e-visa portal, since 23 August 2025 a single-entry e-visa is valid for 120 days from the date it is issued, and you can stay in Russia for up to 30 days from the date of entry. You must also leave Russia within the 120 days. For longer stays or purposes the e-visa does not cover, you apply for a regular (paper) visa at a Russian embassy or consulate.

Can I fly into any Russian airport on an e-visa?

No — the e-visa is valid only for entry and exit through the border crossing points approved by the Russian Government, and you cannot leave through a checkpoint that is not on that list. The official list includes the Moscow airports (Sheremetyevo, Domodedovo, Vnukovo and Zhukovsky), Saint Petersburg (Pulkovo) and Kazan. We check your flights against the current list before you book.

How many days are enough for Moscow and Saint Petersburg?

Seven or eight days is a comfortable first trip: three or four days in Moscow, a morning on the high-speed train, and three days in Saint Petersburg including Peterhof and Pushkin. Add two days for Kazan, or a day trip from Moscow to Sergiev Posad.

Which is the best time to visit Russia?

Late May to early September is the most comfortable season for Moscow and Saint Petersburg, with long days — around midsummer Saint Petersburg has its famous White Nights. Winter, from December to February, is cold and snowy but beautiful, with New Year lights in Moscow; you need proper winter clothing. Spring and autumn are cooler and quieter.

Can I use my Indian debit or credit card in Russia?

Do not count on it. The official e-visa portal itself says the visa fee can only be paid with cards issued outside Russia, and inside Russia many international cards do not work. We tell you the current, practical options for money before you travel, and keep your hotels, transfers and tours prepaid where possible.

Is vegetarian and Indian food available in Russia?

Yes, in Moscow and Saint Petersburg there are Indian restaurants and places with vegetarian dishes; in smaller towns it takes more planning. We note vegetarian and Jain needs with hotels in advance and suggest restaurants on your route.

Why are there no prices on this page?

Russia costs depend on your dates, hotel category, the train or flight between cities, palace and museum tickets, guides and the airfare from your city. Instead of a teaser price that changes when you ask, we send a costed, day-by-day quote for your dates — usually within 24 hours.
