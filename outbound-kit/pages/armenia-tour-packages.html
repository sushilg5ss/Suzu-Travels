Why Armenia works so well for Indian travellers

Armenia is small, so the big sights are close together: from a base in Yerevan you reach Garni and Geghard, Khor Virap below Mount Ararat, and Lake Sevan as day trips, and a two-day loop adds Noravank and Tatev in the south. Distances are short, the pace is relaxed, and it pairs naturally with Georgia next door.

Sample plan

A sample 6-day Yerevan, Garni, Sevan & Tatev itinerary

The classic first trip to Armenia. We rebuild it around you — a shorter Yerevan-and-day-trips holiday, a winter snow trip, or three or four extra nights in Georgia.

Day 1Arrive in Yerevan

Airport pickup and hotel. An easy evening walk to Republic Square and up the Cascade steps for views over the city — and Ararat on a clear day.

Day 2Garni & Geghard

Drive into the hills to the Garni Temple above its gorge, then Geghard Monastery, partly carved into the rock of the Azat valley. Back to Yerevan.

Day 3Lake Sevan & Dilijan

North to Lake Sevan and the Sevanavank churches on the peninsula, then the forest town of Dilijan and its old quarter. Return to Yerevan.

Day 4Khor Virap & Noravank

Khor Virap with Mount Ararat behind it, then the red cliffs of Noravank, and on south to Goris for the night.

Day 5Wings of Tatev

Ride the Wings of Tatev cable car across the gorge to Tatev Monastery, then the long scenic drive back to Yerevan.

Day 6Fly home

Free morning for shopping and a last coffee in Yerevan, then transfer to the airport — or carry on north to Georgia.

Visa & entry

Armenia visa for Indian passport holders

What the official rules say

  • Indian ordinary passport holders need a visa for Armenia. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs recommends the e-visa, applied for on the official portal evisa.mfa.am, with a stated processing time of three working days.
  • For the e-visa you need travel (medical) insurance plus either a valid visa or residence permit of the EU/Schengen states, the USA, the UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, the Republic of Korea, Russia or Japan (or a residence permit of the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain or Oman), or a return air ticket reservation, an invitation from a party in Armenia (for example a local tourist agency) and proof of funds such as a 3-month bank statement.
  • With such a valid visa or residence permit, a visa can also be obtained at the Armenian border; otherwise you can apply at an Armenian embassy.
  • Temporary exemption: from 1 July 2026 to 1 July 2027, Indians holding a US, EU, Schengen, UAE, Bahrain, Qatar, Saudi, Kuwait or Oman residence permit valid for at least six months from entry do not need a visa (up to 180 days in a year).
  • Your passport must be valid for the entire stay in Armenia.

Checked 1 November 2026 · sources: Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Armenia — visa requirements (India, ordinary passport) · Armenia e-visa portal · MFA list of countries for the temporary visa exemption

Please note: whether a visa is issued, and admission at the border, is always the decision of the authorities of the Republic of Armenia. We help you prepare a complete, honest application, but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

FAQ

Common questions about an Armenia tour

Do Indians need a visa for Armenia?

Yes. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Armenia shows that Indian ordinary passport holders need a visa. Most travellers apply for an e-visa on the official portal (evisa.mfa.am); the Ministry recommends the e-visa and gives a processing time of three working days. With an Indian passport, the e-visa needs travel (medical) insurance plus either a valid visa or residence permit of the EU/Schengen states, the USA, the UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, the Republic of Korea, Russia or Japan (or a residence permit of a Gulf country), or a return air ticket reservation, an invitation from a party in Armenia such as a local tourist agency, and proof of funds. We checked this on 1 November 2026. Issuing a visa is always the decision of the Armenian authorities.

Can I get an Armenian visa on arrival with a US, UK or Schengen visa?

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Armenia says Indian citizens can get a visa at the border if they show a valid visa or residence permit of the EU/Schengen states, the USA, the UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, the Republic of Korea, Russia or Japan, or a valid residence permit of the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain or Oman. Separately, from 1 July 2026 to 1 July 2027, Indians who hold a US, EU, Schengen, UAE, Bahrain, Qatar, Saudi, Kuwait or Oman residence permit valid for at least six more months are exempt from the visa. Entry is always the decision of the Armenian border authorities, so we confirm your case before booking.

How many days are enough for Armenia?

Five to six nights covers Yerevan, Garni and Geghard, Khor Virap and Noravank, Lake Sevan and Dilijan, and the long drive south to Tatev. With three or four nights, keep to Yerevan and the day trips around it. Add three or four nights to combine Armenia with Georgia.

Which is the best time to visit Armenia from India?

May, June, September and October are the most comfortable months, with mild days for sightseeing and road trips. July and August are hot in Yerevan but fine for Lake Sevan and the hills. December to March is snow season — Garni and the mountains turn white, and the ski slopes at Tsaghkadzor open.

Can we combine Armenia with Georgia?

Yes. Armenia and Georgia share a border, and a road transfer between Yerevan and Tbilisi via the north (Dilijan or Haghpat side) is a common way to combine them. Georgia has its own entry rules for Indian passport holders (usually an e-visa, unless you hold a qualifying visa or residence permit), so we plan both countries' paperwork together. See our Georgia page for that part of the trip.

Is vegetarian and Indian food available in Armenia?

Armenian food has plenty of vegetarian dishes — fresh bread (lavash), salads, cheese, grilled vegetables, dried fruit and herbs — and Yerevan has Indian restaurants too. Outside Yerevan the choice is smaller, so we note vegetarian and Jain needs with hotels and drivers in advance and suggest where to eat.
