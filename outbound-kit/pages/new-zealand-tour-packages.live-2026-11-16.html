Why New Zealand is worth the long flight

Two islands with very different moods: the North Island with Auckland’s harbour, the green hills of Hobbiton and the geysers and Māori culture of Rotorua, and the South Island with Queenstown, the fiords of Milford Sound, the turquoise lakes of Tekapo and Pukaki and the peaks around Aoraki / Mount Cook. Distances are manageable with one domestic flight, and the visa is an online Visitor Visa — we walk you through the official steps.

Visa & entry

New Zealand visitor visa for Indian passport holders

What the official rules say

  • A visa is needed. India is not on New Zealand’s visa waiver list, so Indian passport holders apply for a Visitor Visa before travelling (the NZeTA travel authority is only for visa-waiver nationalities). You apply online on the official Immigration New Zealand website.
  • Processing: Immigration New Zealand showed 80% of Visitor Visa applications processed within 2 weeks when we checked, and says it is busiest between September and March. It recommends not booking non-refundable travel until the visa is approved.
  • What you show: genuine plans to visit and leave; enough money for your stay — at least NZD 1,000 a month, or NZD 400 a month if your accommodation is prepaid; a ticket out of New Zealand or funds to buy one; and a passport valid for at least 3 months after the date you plan to leave New Zealand. Immigration New Zealand has a Visitor Visa checklist for applicants from India.
  • Length of stay: a Visitor Visa can allow stays of up to 6 or 9 months; Immigration New Zealand decides whether to issue a single-entry or multiple-entry visa. Partners and dependent children can be included in one application.
  • On arrival: complete the New Zealand Traveller Declaration and apply for entry permission at the border; officers may ask to see your onward ticket and how you will support yourself.

Checked 16 November 2026 · sources: Immigration New Zealand — Visitor Visa · visa waiver countries · Visitor Visa checklist — India · arriving in New Zealand

Please note: whether a visa is granted, and permission to enter at the border, is always the decision of Immigration New Zealand. We help you prepare a complete application, but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

We give you a day-by-day itinerary with hotel details for your application, help you follow the official India checklist, and confirm non-refundable bookings only once your visa is approved. Visa fees are paid by you directly to Immigration New Zealand.

FAQ

Common questions about a New Zealand tour

Do Indians need a visa for New Zealand?

Yes. India is not on New Zealand's visa waiver list, so Indian passport holders apply for a New Zealand Visitor Visa before travelling — the NZeTA travel authority is only for visa-waiver nationalities. When we checked Immigration New Zealand's official pages on 16 November 2026, the Visitor Visa was applied for online and Immigration New Zealand recommended not booking non-refundable travel until the visa is approved. Whether a visa is granted, and permission to enter at the border, is always the decision of Immigration New Zealand.

How long does a New Zealand visitor visa take?

Immigration New Zealand's Visitor Visa page showed 80% of applications processed within 2 weeks when we checked on 16 November 2026, and says processing is busiest between September and March. Apply early, include all the documents on its India checklist, and keep your flights and hotels flexible until the visa is approved.

How much money do I need to show for a New Zealand visitor visa?

Immigration New Zealand asks visitors who pay for themselves to show at least NZD 1,000 a month of stay, or NZD 400 a month if the accommodation is already paid for, with bank or credit card statements in the applicant's name. You also need an onward or return ticket, or funds to buy one, in addition to that money. We give you a day-by-day itinerary with hotel confirmations to support the application.

How many days are enough for New Zealand?

Eight to ten days is a good first trip that covers both islands: Auckland, Hobbiton and Rotorua in the North Island, then Queenstown, Milford Sound and Aoraki / Mount Cook in the South Island. Twelve to fourteen days lets you add Wanaka, the glaciers on the West Coast or the Coromandel at a relaxed pace.

Which is the best time to visit New Zealand?

December to February is summer, with long days and the busiest season. March to May brings autumn colours and fewer crowds, and September to November is spring. June to August is winter: snow on the Southern Alps and skiing around Queenstown, with some mountain roads and walks affected by weather. New Zealand's seasons are the opposite of India's.

Is it easy to get around New Zealand?

Yes. Most trips combine a short domestic flight between the North and South Islands with private transfers, coach tours or a self-drive car. New Zealand drives on the left, like India; if you want to drive, we tell you the official licence rules for your stay before you book the car.

Is vegetarian and Indian food available in New Zealand?

Yes — Auckland, Queenstown, Rotorua and Christchurch all have Indian restaurants and plenty of vegetarian options. In small towns and on long scenic drives it takes a little planning, so we note vegetarian and Jain needs with hotels and tours in advance.

Why are there no prices on this page?

New Zealand costs depend on your dates, hotel category, the domestic flight, self-drive or private transfers, activities like Milford Sound cruises and Hobbiton tours, and the airfare from your city. Instead of a teaser price that changes when you ask, we send a costed, day-by-day quote for your dates — usually within 24 hours.
