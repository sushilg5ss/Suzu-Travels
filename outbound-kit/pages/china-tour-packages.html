Visa & entry

China tourist visa for Indian passport holders

What the official rules say

  • A visa is needed. For a holiday in mainland China, Indian passport holders apply for a tourist (L) visa at the Chinese Embassy in New Delhi or the Chinese Consulate General in Mumbai (for residents of Maharashtra and Karnataka). Applicants from Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh and West Bengal currently apply in New Delhi.
  • Online first: fill in the visa application form and upload the documents on the official Chinese consular website for online review (no fee at this step). After approval you get an email and must submit your passport and documents in person within 30 days; the fee is paid when you collect the visa.
  • Documents include a passport valid for more than six months with at least two blank visa pages (and any previous passport from the last five years), two recent 48 mm × 33 mm colour photos on a white background and, for a tourist visa, six months of personal savings bank statements with an average monthly balance of at least INR 1 lakh, or a bank deposit certificate of a similar amount made at least three months before applying.
  • Processing time: 7 working days regular, 4 working days express, counted from the day after the passport is submitted. Until 31 December 2026, fingerprints are not collected for short-term visas of up to 180 days.
  • Hong Kong and Macau have their own entry rules, separate from the mainland China visa — see our Hong Kong & Macau page.

Checked 14 November 2026 · sources: Embassy of China in India — Notice on Chinese Visa Application · Embassy notice on direct acceptance of visa applications (from 26 Oct 2026)

Please note: whether a visa is issued, and admission at the border, is always the decision of the Chinese authorities. We help you prepare a complete application, but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

We send you the current official checklist, a day-by-day itinerary with hotel bookings for your application, and confirm non-refundable bookings only once your visa is issued. Apply well ahead of your travel dates — we suggest at least a month.

FAQ

Common questions about a China tour

Do Indians need a visa for China?

Yes. Indian passport holders need a Chinese visa for a holiday in mainland China — for tourism this is the L (tourist) visa, issued by the Chinese Embassy in New Delhi or the Chinese Consulate General in Mumbai. You first fill in and submit the visa application form online for review on the official Chinese consular website; once the online review is approved you receive an email and, within 30 days, take your passport and documents to the Embassy or Consulate in person. The Embassy lists regular processing as 7 working days and express as 4 working days, counted from the day after you hand in your passport. Approval is always the decision of the Chinese authorities — we help you prepare, but we never promise an outcome.

Which documents does a tourist (L) visa need?

The Embassy's notice lists the online application form, a passport valid for more than six months with at least two blank visa pages (plus any previous passport issued in the past five years), two recent colour photos (48 mm × 33 mm, white background) and, for tourist visas, personal savings bank statements for the last six months with a consistent average monthly balance of at least INR 1 lakh, or a bank deposit certificate of a similar amount made at least three months before you apply. Children under 18 need extra documents. Requirements can change, so we always send you the current official checklist.

Where do I submit my China visa application?

Applicants living in Maharashtra and Karnataka submit at the Chinese Consulate General in Mumbai. Applicants from Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh and West Bengal currently submit at the Chinese Embassy in New Delhi, as do applicants from all other states. Since 26 October 2026 the Embassy and the Mumbai Consulate accept applications directly. Until 31 December 2026, fingerprints are not collected for short-term visas of up to 180 days.

How many days are enough for a China trip?

Eight days is a comfortable first trip for Beijing, Xi'an and Shanghai, travelling between the cities by high-speed train or short flight. Add three or four days for Zhangjiajie's sandstone pillars, or for Guilin and the Li River to Yangshuo.

Which is the best time to visit China?

April to May and September to October usually bring the most comfortable weather for Beijing, Xi'an and Shanghai. Summer is hot and humid with more rain, and northern winters are cold and dry (the Great Wall can be beautiful in snow). China's national holidays in early May and the first week of October are very crowded, so we plan around them.

Is vegetarian and Indian food available in China?

In Beijing and Shanghai there are Indian restaurants and vegetarian Buddhist restaurants; in smaller places it takes more planning. We note vegetarian and Jain needs with hotels in advance and give you a written food card in Chinese to show at restaurants.

Will my phone, WhatsApp and Google work in China?

Many apps Indian travellers use every day, including Google services and WhatsApp, do not work normally on mainland China networks. We share your full plan in advance (offline), keep phone contact with you and suggest how to stay connected and pay — mobile payment apps are widely used in China.

Why are there no prices on this page?

China costs depend on your dates, cities, hotel category, train or flight connections between cities, entry tickets and the airfare from your city. Instead of a teaser price that changes when you ask, we send a costed, day-by-day quote for your dates — usually within 24 hours.
