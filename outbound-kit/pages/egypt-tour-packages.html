Egypt

Why Egypt works so well for Indian travellers

Few places pack so much history into one trip: the Pyramids and Sphinx at Giza, Islamic Cairo and the Khan el-Khalili bazaar, the Grand Egyptian Museum near the pyramids, then the Nile valley — Philae and Abu Simbel near Aswan, Kom Ombo and Edfu on a cruise, and the temples of Karnak and Luxor, the Valley of the Kings and the Temple of Hatshepsut at Luxor. Distances are covered by short internal flights and the cruise itself, licensed Egyptologist guides explain every site, and many trips end with a few easy days on the Red Sea.

Visa & entry

Egypt visa for Indian passport holders

What the official rules say

  • The Government of Egypt’s official e-Visa Portal says citizens of a foreign country generally need a visa before entering Egypt, and India is on its list of nationalities that may be issued an e-Visa on application.
  • The tourist e-Visa is issued for single or multiple entry. You apply and pay online, and once approved a link to download your e-Visa is sent to you.
  • Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months from your arrival date.
  • On arrival the portal lists: a printout of your e-Visa, your travel itinerary, and hotel bookings or details of the places you will visit.
  • The portal also states that Egyptian port authorities have the right to refuse entry to an e-Visa holder without explanation — an e-Visa is permission to travel, not a guarantee of entry.
  • Apply only on the official portal. Each traveller, including children, needs their own visa. We check the latest rules again for your exact dates before you apply.

Checked 27 October 2026 · source: Egypt e-Visa Portal (Government of Egypt) — FAQ and eligible nationalities

Please note: whether a visa is granted, and whether you are admitted at the border, is always the decision of the Egyptian authorities. We help you prepare a complete, honest application, but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

FAQ

Common questions about an Egypt tour

Do Indians need a visa for Egypt?

Yes. Indian nationals need a visa for Egypt. India is on the list of nationalities that can apply for a tourist e-Visa on the Government of Egypt's official e-Visa Portal (visa2egypt.gov.eg), for single or multiple entry. Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months from your arrival date. The decision is always made by the Egyptian authorities, and the portal says port authorities can refuse entry even to an e-Visa holder — we help you prepare a complete, honest application but cannot promise an outcome. We checked these rules on 27 October 2026 and re-check them before every booking.

What documents do we carry on arrival in Egypt?

The official e-Visa Portal lists a printout of your e-Visa, your travel itinerary and your hotel bookings (or details of the places you will visit), together with a passport valid for at least 6 months from arrival. We give you a printed day-by-day itinerary with all hotel and cruise details for exactly this reason.

How many days are enough for Egypt?

Seven to eight nights covers the classic trip: Cairo and the Pyramids of Giza, a flight to Aswan, Abu Simbel, a three- or four-night Nile cruise to Luxor and the Valley of the Kings. With ten to twelve nights you can add three or four relaxed beach days at Hurghada on the Red Sea. A short Cairo-and-Giza break of four nights also works if you are combining Egypt with another country.

Which is the best time to visit Egypt from India?

October to April is the most comfortable time, and December to February is the coolest and busiest season, including Christmas and New Year. From May to September it is very hot in Luxor and Aswan, so sightseeing is planned for early mornings and evenings — the Red Sea coast is still good for a beach holiday. We also check Ramadan and Eid dates, which move every year, against your plan.

Is a Nile cruise better than staying in hotels?

For most first-time visitors, yes. A cruise between Aswan and Luxor is your hotel, restaurant and transport in one, and it stops at the temples of Kom Ombo and Edfu on the way. If you prefer more space or fewer fixed timings, we can plan hotels in Aswan and Luxor with a private car instead. We compare both for your dates.

Is vegetarian and Indian food easy to find in Egypt?

Yes, with a little planning. Many Egyptian staples are vegetarian — koshari, ful medames, ta'meya (Egyptian falafel), lentil soup and fresh breads and salads — and Indian restaurants can be found in Cairo and the main resort towns. We note vegetarian and Jain needs with your hotels and Nile cruise in advance.

Why are there no prices on this page?

Egypt costs depend on your dates, the hotel and cruise category, internal flights to Aswan or Abu Simbel, entry tickets, guides and the airfare from your city. Instead of a teaser price that changes when you ask, we send a costed, day-by-day quote for your dates — usually within 24 hours.
