Switzerland

Why Switzerland is worth a trip of its own

On a multi-country Europe tour Switzerland gets two or three days. On its own, you have time for the places that make it special: Lucerne and its lake with Mount Titlis above Engelberg; Interlaken, Grindelwald, the Lauterbrunnen valley and the railway to the Jungfraujoch; car-free Zermatt under the Matterhorn with the Gornergrat railway; and panoramic trains such as the Glacier Express and the Bernina Express. Trains, buses and lake boats link it all, so there are no long drives — and one Schengen visa also lets you add Paris or Italy.

Visa & entry

Swiss (Schengen) visa for Indian passport holders

What the official rules say

  • Switzerland is part of the Schengen area. Indian passport holders need a Schengen short-stay visa for a holiday, which allows up to 90 days in any 180-day period across the Schengen countries.
  • If Switzerland is your main destination (where you spend the longest time), you apply to Switzerland. In India, Swiss Schengen visa applications are submitted at VFS Global centres (including Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata and Pune) and processed by the Embassy of Switzerland in New Delhi.
  • The Embassy says to apply not earlier than 6 months before entering the Schengen area, and that processing normally takes 10 to 15 working days after the application reaches the Embassy. Applicants should be legally residing in India for at least the last six months to apply with the Swiss representation in India.
  • Under the common Schengen rules you apply at least 15 days before travel, give fingerprints, and carry travel medical insurance covering emergency medical care, hospitalisation and repatriation, with a passport valid for at least 3 months after you leave the Schengen area.
  • Appointments drive the timeline, so we suggest starting two to three months before travel — earlier for May–June school-holiday dates.

Checked 24 October 2026 · sources: Embassy of Switzerland in India — Visa, entry and residence · Embassy of Switzerland in India — Schengen visa · Schengen-wide rules checked 23 October 2026: European Commission — Applying for a Schengen visa

Please note: visa approval and entry at the border are always the decision of the Swiss authorities and the border officers of the country you enter. We guide you through the documents and timelines honestly, but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

Apply only through the official channel, keep your hotel bookings, insurance and return ticket consistent with your application, and carry copies for the immigration desk.

FAQ

Common questions about a Switzerland tour

Do Indians need a visa for Switzerland?

Yes. Switzerland is part of the Schengen area, so Indian passport holders need a Schengen short-stay visa for a holiday. If Switzerland is your main destination, you apply to Switzerland: in India, applications are submitted at VFS Global centres and processed by the Embassy of Switzerland in New Delhi, normally in 10 to 15 working days after they reach the Embassy. You can apply no earlier than six months before your trip. Approval is always the decision of the Swiss authorities.

How many days are enough for Switzerland?

Seven to eight nights covers the classic first trip: Lucerne and Mount Titlis, Interlaken and the Jungfraujoch, then Zermatt and the Matterhorn. Five nights works for Lucerne and Interlaken alone. With ten nights or more you can add the Glacier Express or Bernina Express, Lake Geneva and Montreux, or a few days in Paris or Italy on the same Schengen visa.

Which is the best time to visit Switzerland from India?

June to September is the classic season — green valleys, long days and every mountain railway and cable car running. December to March is for snow, skiing and winter villages. April–May and October are quieter shoulder months with lower demand, but some mountain lifts close for maintenance between seasons, so we check each excursion against your dates.

What is the Swiss Travel Pass and do I need it?

It is a rail pass for visitors that covers most trains, buses and lake boats in Switzerland for a set number of days and gives discounts on many mountain railways and cable cars. Whether it saves money depends on your route, so we compare the pass with point-to-point tickets for your exact itinerary before we book.

Is Switzerland good for a honeymoon?

It is one of the most popular honeymoon destinations for Indian couples: lake-view hotels in Lucerne or Interlaken, the car-free mountain village of Zermatt under the Matterhorn, panoramic train journeys and snow at the Jungfraujoch even in summer. We keep hotel changes few and add special touches when you tell us the occasion.

Is vegetarian food easy to find in Switzerland?

Yes. Lucerne, Interlaken, Zurich, Geneva and even Zermatt have Indian restaurants, and Swiss cheese fondue, rösti and Italian dishes have vegetarian options. We note vegetarian and Jain needs with hotels and suggest restaurants for every stop.

Switzerland costs depend heavily on your dates, hotel category and location, the rail pass or tickets, mountain excursions such as the Jungfraujoch and Mount Titlis, and the airfare from India.
