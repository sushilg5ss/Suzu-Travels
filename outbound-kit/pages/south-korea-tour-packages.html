Why South Korea is worth it for Indian travellers

Seoul packs royal palaces, hanok lanes, street-food markets and a skyline view from Namsan into one easy, safe city with excellent metro and trains. The KTX high-speed train links it to Busan’s beaches and Gyeongju’s temples, and Jeju Island adds volcanic coast and Hallasan. Spring blossom and autumn colour make it a strong pick for honeymoons and family trips alike.

Visa & entry

South Korea visa for Indian passport holders

What the official sources say

  • A visa is needed before you travel. The Korean consulate’s visa guide for Indian applicants states that Indian nationals must apply for a Korean visa and obtain it through the official Korea Visa Portal and a Korea Visa Application Centre (KVAC).
  • How it works: fill in and print the visa e-Form on the Korea Visa Portal (visa.go.kr), submit it in person at a Korea Visa Application Centre, then check the status of your application on the Visa Portal.
  • Documents: the exact list for your visa type is shown by the Visa Portal and the application centre. We prepare matching hotel confirmations, a flight itinerary and a day-by-day plan for your file.
  • K-ETA is not a visa. The official K-ETA site says K-ETA is for nationals who can enter Korea without a visa, so it does not replace a visa for Indian passports. Use only official sites and ignore look-alike agencies.
  • e-Arrival Card: Korea’s Ministry of Justice runs the official e-Arrival Card site; it can be filed online with no fee, from three days before arrival.

Checked 18 November 2026 · sources: Consulate General of the Republic of Korea in Chennai — Korean Visa Application Guide · Korea Visa Portal · K-ETA (official) · Korea e-Arrival Card (official)

FAQ

Common questions about a South Korea tour

Do Indians need a visa for South Korea?

Yes. Indian passport holders need a Korean visa before they travel. When we checked on 18 November 2026, the Consulate General of the Republic of Korea in Chennai stated that Indian nationals must apply for a Korean visa and obtain it through the official Korea Visa Portal (visa.go.kr) and the Korea Visa Application Centre (KVAC). Whether a visa is issued is always the decision of the Korean authorities, and admission is decided by Korean immigration on arrival.

How do I apply for a South Korea tourist visa from India?

According to the Korean consulate's visa guide for Indian applicants, you fill in and print the visa e-Form on the Korea Visa Portal, submit your application in person at a Korea Visa Application Centre (KVAC), and then track its status on the Visa Portal. The exact documents for your visa type are listed by the Visa Portal and the application centre. We prepare your hotel confirmations, flight itinerary and a day-by-day plan so that your trip papers are consistent.

Can Indians use K-ETA instead of a visa?

No. The official K-ETA website says the electronic travel authorisation is for nationals who can enter Korea without a visa. Indian nationals need a visa, so K-ETA does not replace it. Apply only on official government sites, and ignore look-alike websites that charge extra fees.

What is the Korea e-Arrival Card?

It is Korea's online arrival card, run by the Ministry of Justice on the official site e-arrivalcard.go.kr. The official site says it can be filed online with no fee, from three days before you arrive in Korea. We remind you about it before you fly and tell you whether your airline also hands out a paper card.

How many days are enough for a South Korea trip?

Six to eight nights is a comfortable first trip: four nights in Seoul with a day trip to Nami Island, then the KTX train to Busan for two or three nights with an optional day in Gyeongju. With nine or ten nights you can add Jeju Island for its volcanic coast and Hallasan.

Which is the best time to visit South Korea?

Spring (about March to May) and autumn (about September to November) are the most pleasant. Cherry blossoms usually appear in late March and early April, and the autumn colours peak around October and early November. Summer is hot and humid with a rainy spell, and winter is cold with snow — good for ski resorts and snowy scenery such as Nami Island.

Is vegetarian and Indian food available in South Korea?

Yes, especially in Seoul and Busan, where you will find Indian restaurants and vegetarian or vegan places; many Korean dishes contain meat, fish sauce or seafood, so it takes some planning outside the big cities. We note vegetarian and Jain needs with hotels in advance and suggest restaurants along your route.
