Seychelles

Why Seychelles is worth it for Indian travellers

Seychelles is a group of islands in the Indian Ocean, known for white-sand coves between smooth granite boulders, clear turquoise water and green hills. Most trips combine three islands: Mahé, with the capital Victoria and the international airport; Praslin, with Anse Lazio and the Vallée de Mai nature reserve; and La Digue, a small island best explored by bicycle. There is no visa to apply for in advance — you complete an online Travel Authorisation before you fly — which makes it an easy choice for a honeymoon or a short beach holiday.

Visa & entry

Seychelles entry rules for Indian passport holders

What the official sources say

  • No visa is needed in advance. Seychelles’ Immigration and Civil Status department says a visa is not required to enter Seychelles. A Visitor’s Permit is issued on arrival to travellers coming for a holiday, business or to visit friends or family who meet the criteria below.
  • Travel Authorisation before you fly: according to the Visitor’s Permit page, all visitors must hold a valid Electronic Travel Authorisation before departing from their country of origin — applied for on the official Seychelles Electronic Border System or its Seychelles e-Border app. Any fee is shown there when you apply.
  • Criteria for the Visitor’s Permit: a valid return or onward ticket, confirmed accommodation and sufficient funds for the stay (the immigration page mentions a minimum of US$150 or equivalent per day), and you must not be a prohibited immigrant.
  • Passport: valid for the period of your intended stay until you are back in your country of origin or residence.
  • Length of stay: the Visitor’s Permit is first granted for up to three months, free of charge; extensions of up to three months at a time, to a maximum of twelve months, carry a fee. Give your correct accommodation address and tell immigration if it changes.

Checked 23 November 2026 · sources: Visiting Seychelles — Immigration and Civil Status · Visitor’s Permit — Immigration and Civil Status · Seychelles Electronic Border System (official)

Please note: whether you are admitted, and for how long, is always the decision of the Seychelles immigration authorities at the border. We help you prepare clear, consistent documents — itinerary, hotel bookings and return tickets — but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

Entry rules and fees can change. We re-check the official sources when you book, and remind you to complete the Travel Authorisation before your flight.

FAQ

Common questions about a Seychelles tour

Do Indians need a visa for Seychelles?

No visa is needed. Seychelles' Immigration and Civil Status department says a visa is not required to enter Seychelles, and that a Visitor's Permit is issued on arrival to travellers coming for a holiday, business or to visit friends or family who meet its criteria (checked 23 November 2026). Admission is always the decision of the Seychelles immigration officers at the border.

What is the Seychelles Travel Authorisation?

The Immigration and Civil Status department says all visitors must hold a valid Electronic Travel Authorisation before departing from their country of origin. You apply on the official Seychelles Electronic Border System website (seychelles.govtas.com) or its Seychelles e-Border app. Any fee is shown on the official site when you apply; we help you fill it in correctly with your hotel addresses.

What documents do I need to enter Seychelles?

The official criteria for a Visitor's Permit are: a passport valid for the period of your stay until you are back in India, a valid return or onward ticket, confirmed accommodation, and sufficient funds for the stay — the immigration page mentions a minimum of US$150 or equivalent per day. You must not be a prohibited immigrant. We give you a clear itinerary and hotel confirmations to carry.

How long can I stay in Seychelles as a tourist?

The Visitor's Permit is first granted for up to three months and is free of charge for that period, according to the Immigration and Civil Status department. Extensions are possible for further periods of up to three months each, to a maximum of twelve months, for a fee — each is decided by Seychelles immigration.

Which is the best time to visit Seychelles?

Seychelles is warm all year. The calmer months between the two trade-wind seasons, roughly April to May and October to November, are often the most comfortable for beaches, boat trips and snorkelling. December to March is usually more humid with more rain showers; June to September is drier but breezier, with rougher seas on some coasts. We pick beaches and boat days to suit your month.

How many days are enough for Seychelles?

Six to seven nights works well for Mahé, Praslin and La Digue: two or three nights on Mahé, then Praslin and La Digue by ferry. For a honeymoon at a slower pace, eight to nine nights with one resort stay is a good plan. Shorter trips of four to five nights can cover Mahé with a day trip to Praslin and La Digue.

Is Seychelles good for a honeymoon or a family trip?

Yes. Couples come for quiet beaches, granite-boulder coves, sunsets and boat days around the islands. Families do well with calm-water beaches, the giant tortoises of Curieuse and easy cycling on La Digue. We choose hotels for your group, plan vegetarian meals in advance, and note that Victoria on Mahé has a Hindu temple, the Arul Mihu Navashakti Vinayagar Temple.
