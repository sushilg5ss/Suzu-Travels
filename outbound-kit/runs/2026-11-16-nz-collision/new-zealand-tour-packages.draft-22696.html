Visa & entry

New Zealand visitor visa for Indian passport holders

What the official rules say

  • A visa is needed. India is not on New Zealand’s visa waiver list, so Indian passport holders apply for a Visitor Visa before travel. The NZeTA (electronic travel authority) is only for passports from visa waiver countries.
  • Apply online with Immigration New Zealand on its official website: immigration.govt.nz. Immigration New Zealand shows a processing time of 80% of applications within 2 weeks, says it is busiest between September and March, and recommends not booking non-refundable travel until the visa is approved.
  • What you must show: genuine plans for a visit and to leave at the end of it, enough money for your stay (or an acceptable sponsor), and good health and character. A holiday visit does not normally need a chest X-ray or medical certificate.
  • Length of stay: Immigration New Zealand decides whether to issue a multiple-entry or a single-entry visa; a multiple-entry Visitor Visa allows stays of up to 6 months in each 12-month period. Partners and dependent children can be included in the same application.
  • Groups and arrival: tour groups visiting for 1 month or less may be able to apply for a Group Visitor Visa. On arrival you apply for entry permission and answer biosecurity and customs questions.

Checked 16 November 2026 · sources: Immigration New Zealand — Visitor Visa · visa waiver countries and territories · what you need to visit New Zealand

Please note: whether a visa is granted, and on what terms, is always the decision of Immigration New Zealand, and admission at the border is decided on arrival. We help you prepare a complete application, but we never promise an outcome we do not control.

We give you a day-by-day itinerary and hotel details for your application, a checklist of the documents to gather, and we confirm non-refundable bookings only once your visa is approved. Combining with Australia? That is a separate Australian visa — we plan both timelines together.

FAQ

Common questions about a New Zealand tour

Do Indians need a visa for New Zealand?

Yes. When we checked Immigration New Zealand's website on 16 November 2026, India was not on New Zealand's visa waiver list, so Indian passport holders apply online for a New Zealand Visitor Visa before they travel. Whether a visa is granted, and on what terms, is always the decision of Immigration New Zealand.

Can Indian passport holders use the NZeTA instead of a visa?

No. The NZeTA (New Zealand Electronic Travel Authority) is for passport holders of the countries on New Zealand's visa waiver list, and India is not on that list. Indian travellers need a Visitor Visa. If you also hold another passport, we check which rule applies to that passport.

How long does a New Zealand visitor visa take?

Immigration New Zealand shows a processing time of 80% of Visitor Visa applications within 2 weeks, and says processing is busiest between September and March, so it asks travellers to apply early with complete information. It also recommends not booking non-refundable travel until the visa is approved — we plan your bookings around that.

How many days are enough for New Zealand?

Ten days is a good first trip covering both islands: Auckland and Rotorua in the North Island, then Queenstown, Milford Sound, Wanaka, Mount Cook and Lake Tekapo in the South Island. With 7 to 8 days, focus on the South Island; with 12 to 14 days, add Waitomo, Hobbiton, the West Coast glaciers or a Bay of Islands stay.

Which is the best time to visit New Zealand from India?

December to April — New Zealand's summer and early autumn — brings the longest, warmest days and the easiest driving, and fits Indian winter and spring holidays. September to November is spring, with fresh green landscapes. June to August is winter: the ski season around Queenstown and Wanaka, with snow on the mountains and some mountain roads that can close in bad weather.

Can I combine New Zealand with Australia?

Yes, many Indian travellers combine the two. They are separate countries with separate visas, so you apply for an Australian visa and a New Zealand Visitor Visa. We plan the order of cities and the flight across the Tasman Sea so the trip flows — see our Australia page for the Australian side.

Is vegetarian and Indian food available in New Zealand?

Yes. Auckland, Queenstown, Christchurch and Rotorua have Indian restaurants and many cafés with vegetarian food; in small towns we plan meals in advance. We note vegetarian and Jain needs with your hotels and suggest places on your route.

Why are there no prices on this page?

New Zealand costs depend on your dates, the flights from your city, hotel or apartment category, self-drive or chauffeur-driven travel, domestic flights, cruises and activities. Instead of a teaser price that changes when you ask, we send a costed, day-by-day quote for your dates — usually within 24 hours.
