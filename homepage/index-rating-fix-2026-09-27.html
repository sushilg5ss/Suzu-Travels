Season guide

Where should you go this month?

Weather decides half your trip. Pick a month to see where the mountains, valleys and beaches are at their best.

Best in January

Manali & Shimla snowPeak snow season in the Himachal hills; Solang and Kufri are usually white.Gulmarg, KashmirDeep snow, the Gondola and skiing slopes at their best.Winter SpitiFrozen rivers and empty roads for the brave, via Kinnaur. Very cold.Kerala & GoaDry, warm and sunny: the best beach and backwater weather.

Best in February

Gulmarg snowStill full winter in Kashmir, with slightly longer days.Manali snowSnow around Solang, Atal Tunnel side trips when the road is open.RajasthanPleasant days and cool nights, ideal for forts and deserts.GoaGreat beach weather before the heat builds.

Best in March

Kashmir in springSnow melts in the valley; the tulip garden usually opens late March.Dharamshala & DalhousieSpring in the Dhauladhars, clear views and mild days.Golden TriangleLast comfortable weeks for Delhi, Agra and Jaipur.KeralaWarm but pleasant in Munnar’s tea hills.

Best in April

Kashmir tulipsTulip season in Srinagar and green meadows in Pahalgam.Shimla & ManaliSpring weather, apple blossoms and easy road trips.Dharamshala & DalhousieCool escape from the plains before the summer rush.Char Dham opensTemples usually open late April or early May; book early.

Best in May

Char Dham YatraMain Yatra season; hotels and helicopter slots fill fast.Shimla & ManaliSummer holiday favourite: cool hills, Rohtang when permits open.KashmirGreen valleys, Sonamarg and Gulmarg meadows in full bloom.Leh by flightLadakh season starts; roads from Manali usually open later.

Best in June

Leh LadakhRoad season: Nubra, Pangong and the classic bike routes.Spiti via ManaliKunzum Pass usually opens in June, completing the Spiti circuit.KashmirPleasant summer in Srinagar, Pahalgam and Gulmarg.Char DhamYatra continues until the monsoon sets in.

Best in July

Leh LadakhRain-shadow region: dry and clear while the plains get monsoon.Spiti ValleyAlso rain-shadow; Chandratal and Kaza at their best.Amarnath YatraYatra season, with helicopter options from Baltal and Neelgrath.KashmirCool green summer, good for families.

Best in August

Leh LadakhPeak Ladakh month: all high passes open.Spiti & KinnaurRain-shadow valleys stay dry; check road updates for Kinnaur.KashmirLush meadows; Amarnath Yatra usually ends this month.Monsoon tipLower Himachal can see landslides; we plan buffer days.

Best in September

Spiti ValleyClear skies after the rains, golden fields and quiet roads.Leh LadakhLast of the season with fewer crowds.KashmirCrisp weather and the start of the harvest season.Kinnaur apple seasonOrchards heavy with apples through September and October.

Best in October

Shimla & ManaliClear autumn skies, mountain views and fewer crowds.Kashmir autumnChinar trees start turning red and gold.Char Dham, last weeksTemples usually close around Diwali; go early in the month.RajasthanSeason opens with cooler evenings.

Best in November

Manali in NovemberCrisp, quiet and often the first snowfall late in the month.Kashmir chinarsPeak autumn colours in Srinagar’s gardens.Golden TriangleComfortable weather for Delhi, Agra and Jaipur.Goa & KeralaBeach season begins after the monsoon.

Best in December

Snow in HimachalChristmas and New Year in the hills; book early for snow points.Gulmarg, KashmirWinter wonderland with houseboat evenings in Srinagar.Goa New YearPeak party season; hotels sell out early.KeralaWarm backwaters and hill stations, ideal for honeymoons.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your trip with Suzu Travels.

Which is the best travel agency for Himachal Pradesh tours? +

Suzu Travels is one of the top-rated travel agencies for Himachal Pradesh tours, with 7+ years of experience, 5000+ happy customers, 4.7-star Google rating from 370+ reviews. What tour packages does Suzu Travels offer? +

We offer fully customized domestic tour packages across India – Himachal Pradesh (Shimla, Manali, Spiti, Dharamshala, Kasol, Kinnaur), Kashmir, Leh Ladakh, Goa, Kerala, Rajasthan, and Char Dham Yatra pilgrimage packages. Does Suzu Travels provide customized tour itineraries? +

Yes – customization is our specialty. We tailor every itinerary to your budget, travel dates, group size, and interests. We handle hotel bookings, transport, sightseeing entries, and assign a dedicated tour manager to accompany your group throughout the journey.

Is Suzu Travels approved by Himachal Pradesh Tourism? +

Yes. Suzu Travels is officially approved by Himachal Pradesh Tourism, making us a certified and trusted Destination Management Company (DMC) for Himachal Pradesh tour packages. Our certification is displayed in our office and on our website footer.

What is the best time to visit Kashmir? +

The best time to visit Kashmir is March to May (spring tulip season) and September to November (golden autumn foliage). Winters (December – February) are ideal for snow experiences at Gulmarg. Suzu Travels runs Kashmir tours year-round with packages suited to each season and your travel goals.
