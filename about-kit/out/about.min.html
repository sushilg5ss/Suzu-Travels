Our story

Born in the mountains of Himachal

Who is Suzu Travels?

Suzu Travels is a Himachal-based travel agency and destination management company (DMC), founded in 2019 in Ghumarwin, District Bilaspur, and registered with HP Tourism (Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022). It plans and runs complete tours — hotels, cabs, sightseeing and meals — across Himachal and the rest of India, for travellers and for travel-agent partners.

We are not an aggregator sitting in a city office. We live in Himachal. We know the roads, the hotels, the passes and the seasons — when Rohtang opens, which Spiti road is shut by snow, which hotel in Manali has the view you were promised. That local knowledge is what makes a Suzu itinerary work on the ground, not just on paper.

From one desk in Ghumarwin we have grown into a full-service team with departments for sales, reservations, operations, marketing, accounts and customer experience. Himachal is still our home ground; today our planners also run trips to Kashmir, Ladakh, Uttarakhand, Rajasthan, Goa, Kerala, the Andamans and India's great pilgrimages.

Every traveller should get a genuine, safe and unforgettable experience of the Himalayas and of India.— Our mission since day one

Who owns Suzu Travels?

Suzu Travels is owned and run by Sushil Kumar, its Managing Director, who leads sales, marketing and business growth. Sujata Rai is Director of Finance & Operations. The business is registered for GST (GSTIN 02BLPPK1401E1ZR) as a proprietorship of Sushil Kumar.

Is Suzu Travels a registered and genuine travel agency?

Yes. Suzu Travels is a registered Travel Agent with the Department of Tourism & Civil Aviation, Government of Himachal Pradesh — Reg. No. DTO-MND-11-243/2022, valid to December 2028 — and has been GST-registered since January 2021. You can see both on our certificates page. Travellers rate us 4.7★ on Google from 390 reviews.

Where is the Suzu Travels office?

Our head office is on NH-103 Roadside, Kulahru, Ghumarwin, District Bilaspur, Himachal Pradesh 174021 — on the Shimla–Dharamshala highway. It is open every day from 9 am to 8 pm, and on-trip support runs around the clock on +91 70874 88961.

What is a DMC, and what does Suzu Travels do?

A DMC (destination management company) is a local company that plans and runs everything on the ground — hotels, cabs, sightseeing, guides, permits and activities. Suzu Travels does this for travellers who book with us directly and for travel agents who send us their guests.

Which destinations does Suzu Travels cover?

Himachal Pradesh is our home ground — Shimla, Manali, Spiti, Dharamshala, Dalhousie and beyond. We also run trips to Kashmir, Ladakh, Uttarakhand and the Char Dham, Rajasthan, Goa, Kerala, the Andamans and India's major pilgrimages, plus holidays abroad.

How do I book a trip, and how do payments work?

Tell us your dates and plans on WhatsApp, phone or email and a planner sends you an itinerary with hotels named. A small token advance confirms the booking and locks today's rate; the balance is paid before the trip starts. You can pay online (Razorpay), by bank transfer or by UPI. The advance is non-refundable, but you can move your travel date once, to any date within six months.

Can travel agents work with Suzu Travels?

Yes. More than 200 travel agents across India work with us for net rates, confirmations within about two hours, white-label itineraries, a dedicated B2B manager and credit terms for established partners. Write to b2b@suzutravels.com or WhatsApp +91 70874 88961.

Which languages does the Suzu team speak?

Our planners speak Hindi and English, and Sujata Rai and Shashi Rai also speak Nepali. Shashi Rai is the single point of contact for travellers from Nepal planning an India tour.

How quickly will Suzu Travels reply to me?

Our sales team replies to enquiries within 30 minutes on WhatsApp, phone and email, every day. Complaints are resolved within 24 hours.
